# 🔁 THE VAULT WEEKLY // 2026-W31
*Week of July 27 – August 2, 2026*

## 📡 The Week's Signal
The AI capex verdict arrived. After months of uncertainty, Big Tech earnings delivered a clean split: Microsoft (Azure $100B quarterly revenue, +43% YoY) and Amazon (AWS +37%, fastest growth in 18 quarters) surged on evidence that AI infrastructure spending converts to enterprise revenue; Meta and Apple were punished for capex without visible payoff. The same week produced the Dow's worst session in 15 months (Fed hold + Iran missiles + chip selloff) and Microsoft's best day since 2008 — a narrow breadth rally where two-thirds of S&P 500 stocks still fell. The momentum factor swung 54 percentage points in five weeks. The unified signal across technology, finance, and productivity notes: **organizational infrastructure to convert AI access into output is now the decisive variable**, and markets, researchers, and Gallup are all measuring the same gap.
