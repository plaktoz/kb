## 🧱 One Lesson to Keep

When AI makes execution abundant, the scarce and irreplaceable resource is evaluation — the capacity to tell whether output actually solved the right problem — and automation bias, the fluency effect, and Goodhart's Law all erode that capacity by design if left unchecked.
