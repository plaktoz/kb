# 🔁 THE VAULT WEEKLY // 2026-W46
*Week of 2026-11-09 – 2026-11-15*

## 📡 The Week's Signal
**AI is being adopted faster than organizations can absorb it, and the cost of that gap is showing up in both workplaces and markets.** Productivity notes found individual gains that stall at the team level ([Workstyle Monitor](../../wiki/productivity/workstyle-monitor-2026-ai-individual-gains-team-productivity-gap.md)), and workers report that reviewing AI output adds hours ([AI mandates](../../wiki/productivity/ai-mandates-added-hours-workday-review-burden-workers.md)). McKinsey's banking review puts numbers on it: 80% of banks report individual AI gains but only 6% see significant EBIT impact ([McKinsey](../../wiki/finance/mckinsey-global-banking-annual-review-2026-ai-disruption-dividend.md)). Finance notes show the same pressure from the funding side. Nvidia's CEO resigned, hot CPI and PPI prints pushed the 10-year Treasury yield past 5%, and Alphabet's $51.4B quarterly capex drew more scrutiny than its 103% cloud growth ([Alphabet](../../wiki/finance/goog-reverses-300-recovery-gemini-hydrogen-anthropic-10bn-capex-fxleaders-nov13-2026.md)). Technology notes meanwhile showed agents getting wallets, orchestrators and air-traffic roles, so deployment is outrunning oversight in every category.

## 🗄️ From the Vault
- **[AI makes workers faster and reviewers busier](../../wiki/productivity/ai-makes-workers-faster-reviewers-busier-measure-handoffs.md)**: This October note argues enterprises measure the person using AI instead of the system absorbing its output, so review and verification costs move downstream. It anticipates this week's workplace findings and McKinsey's process-redesign prescription.
- **[Jevons Paradox: the perils of efficiency](../../wiki/strategy/jevons-paradox-the-perils-of-efficiency.md)**: Efficiency tends to increase total consumption, and the note's remedy is to pre-assign saved time on purpose. It supplies the mechanism behind why freed AI capacity does not turn into rest.
- **[Wharton survey: managers' AI excitement fades as verification shifts](../../wiki/productivity/wharton-survey-managers-ai-excitement-fades-verification-shift.md)**: Daily use among managers rose from 46% to 62% while excitement fell to 51%, and the note ties the cooling to verification and workflow redesign. This week's notes read as the next chapter.

## 🧱 One Lesson to Keep
**Speeding up one step of work rarely banks the savings: freed capacity is absorbed by more demand, and the bottleneck moves downstream to review, so decide in advance where saved time goes and measure the next handoff instead of the task** (principles recurring in [AI workplace adoption](../../topics/ai-workplace-adoption.md) and [AI agents](../../topics/ai-agents.md), and in [AI mandates added hours](../../wiki/productivity/ai-mandates-added-hours-workday-review-burden-workers.md) and [the efficiency paradox](../../wiki/productivity/ai-planned-week-efficiency-paradox-priorities-energy-boundaries.md)).
