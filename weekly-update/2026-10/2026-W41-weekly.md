The productivity notes show the same gap at work: [71% of employees feel more productive with AI while the share calling their workload reasonable barely moves](../../wiki/productivity/culture-amp-ai-productivity-workloads-not-falling.md), [70% of workers say visibility beats results](../../wiki/productivity/visibility-rewarded-over-results-myperfectresume-survey.md), and [faster AI users push the work onto reviewers](../../wiki/productivity/ai-makes-workers-faster-reviewers-busier-measure-handoffs.md). This week's compound run made the pattern explicit: Goodhart's Law now anchors a Core Principle in eleven topic files, from [[ai-agents]] to [[workplace-wellbeing]], and "Map Is Not the Territory" anchors one in five more, including [[medical-diagnostics]] and [[labor-market]].

## 🔗 Cross-Category Connections
- **Index level and risk score are the same kind of blind spot**: A cap-weighted S&P record [hides a median stock in a drawdown](../../wiki/finance/hidden-warning-record-highs-breadth-bond-divergence.md), just as a cardiovascular [risk score estimated from age, blood pressure and cholesterol hides plaque that imaging finds](../../wiki/health/silent-atherosclerosis-young-adults-react-study.md). In both cases the fix is to measure the underlying thing (breadth, the artery) directly.
- **The revenue reality check lands beside the free-tier squeeze**: In the same week that [OpenAI's net run rate came in about $20B below the headline](../../wiki/finance/ai-stocks-sink-openai-50-billion-annualized-revenue-oct2026.md), OpenAI said it would test [display ads next to generated images in ChatGPT's free tier](../../wiki/technology/openai-chatgpt-visual-display-ads-image-generation.md), and Google [cut free Gemini users to Flash-Lite only](../../wiki/technology/google-gemini-free-tier-flash-lite-only-oct-2026.md). Finance and product notes both show labs under pressure to make free usage pay.
- **AI helps when the human keeps doing the work**: Students who practised with [unguarded GPT-4 scored 17% lower once it was removed](../../wiki/learning/ai-us-schools-learning-evidence-student-privacy.md), and a [22-study review](../../wiki/learning/ai-impact-student-metacognition-systematic-review.md) found direct-answer chatbots breed "metacognitive laziness." At work, [framing the same AI tool as job enrichment rather than a productivity boost drove 58% more usage](../../wiki/productivity/ai-job-enrichment-framing-vs-productivity-tool-karunakaran.md). In both settings, how the interaction is designed decides whether skill grows.

## 🗄️ From the Vault
- **[Goldman Sachs: hyperscalers need ~$300B of AI revenue to break even](../../wiki/finance/goldman-sachs-ai-revenue-break-even-threshold-2026.md)**: In late September, Goldman put the break-even bar at roughly $300B of AI revenue over the next few years. This week the market learned that the sector's flagship lab books about $50B on a net basis, so the gap between spend and revenue is wider than the headline figures implied.
- **[Modifiable risk factors drive vulnerable coronary plaque](../../wiki/health/modifiable-risk-factors-coronary-plaque-heart-attack.md)**: The August imaging study linked dyslipidemia, hypertension, diabetes, obesity and smoking to high-risk thin-cap plaques. REACT now shows that plaque is common in "healthy" adults that risk scores clear, which shifts attention from estimated risk to what the arteries actually look like.

## 🧱 One Lesson to Keep
Before you act on a number, check what it actually counts (gross or net revenue, the index or the median stock, the risk score or the artery, hours on screen or what's done there), because a proxy that becomes the target stops tracking what it stands for ([[ai-workplace-adoption]] T5, [[medical-diagnostics]] T3, [[remote-work]] T1, [[sp500]] T1).
