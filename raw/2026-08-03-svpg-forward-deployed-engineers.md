# Forward Deployed Engineers

**Author:** Marty Cagan
**Date:** September 17, 2025
**Source:** SVPG (Silicon Valley Product Group)
**URL:** https://www.svpg.com/forward-deployed-engineers/

---

## Summary

This article is part of Cagan's *Product Creator* series and explores the concept of **Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)** — technical people who embed directly with customers to understand their environment, problems, and what's genuinely needed to deliver outcomes.

---

## Key Points

**What is an FDE?**
An FDE is typically an engineer (though increasingly PMs and designers qualify too) who embeds with target customers to deeply understand their context and problems. The goal is discovering solutions that deliver real outcomes.

**The Palantir Model**
Palantir is often cited as the origin of the FDE concept. Rather than acting like a traditional consulting firm (building what clients specify), Palantir applies the *product model* to complex client problems — essentially betting on their own ability to solve the problem. Their FDEs build prototypes using platform services, and a central platform team generalizes those learnings into reusable product capabilities.

**Two Traditional Software Company Types**
- Companies building products for many customers
- Companies building custom solutions for specific clients (e.g., Accenture)

Palantir bridges both: it operates as a **platform product company** where FDE learnings feed back into a continuously evolving platform.

**Why FDEs Matter Beyond Palantir**
The core value of an FDE doesn't require a Palantir-scale platform strategy. At its simplest, sending engineers to visit multiple customers enables them to observe similarities and differences firsthand — the essence of customer discovery. This is particularly relevant today for **AI agent product discovery**, where many startups are already adopting the model.

**The Recommendation**
> "If you do nothing else, please consider having your best engineers visit the offices of your customers..."

Cagan argues that combining embedded engineers with modern prototyping tools creates a faster, more effective path to building valuable products.

**Career Impact**
Product creators who have worked in this model have gone on to outsized careers in product leadership and startup founding.

---

## Core Insight

The FDE model accelerates product discovery by replacing assumptions with direct, immersive customer knowledge — and it's applicable far beyond the complex enterprise scenarios where it originated.
