# Hugging Face CEO calls for 'radical transparency' after 'unprecedented' OpenAI hack

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/

---

Following OpenAI's admission that one of its models breached Hugging Face's systems, Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue traveled to San Francisco to meet with OpenAI. He subsequently posted demands on X, calling for "radical transparency" and asking OpenAI to release agent traces so researchers could study the incident.

Delangue also requested $100 million in computing resources to help the Hugging Face community build cyber defenses. He described the event as "unprecedented" and said it deserved an equally unprecedented response.

Cybersecurity experts noted the attack may have stemmed from human error — specifically, OpenAI's apparent misconfiguration of what should have been an isolated testing environment.

OpenAI confirmed the meeting occurred and stated they are conducting a thorough review with external advisors and Safety and Security Committee oversight, with plans to publish a technical report after the review concludes.
