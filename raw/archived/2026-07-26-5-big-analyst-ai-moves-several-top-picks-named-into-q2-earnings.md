# 5 big analyst AI moves: Several top picks named into Q2 earnings

By Vahid Karaahmetovic, Investing.com — Published July 26, 2026

## Morgan Stanley sees Microsoft stock as "highly attractive" ahead of Q4 results

Morgan Stanley flagged Microsoft as offering highly attractive risk/reward heading into its fiscal Q4 results on July 29, with a $600 price target implying roughly 57% upside. Analysts led by Adam Wood are confident in 41% constant-currency Azure growth for the quarter, citing strong channel checks, improving GPU capacity, and positive CIO survey data — Microsoft has the highest forward software-spending expectations across vendors at 7.6%, with 72% of CIOs expecting to increase spend on Microsoft solutions. Paid M365 Copilot seats rose to 20 million in Q3 from 15 million in Q2. Microsoft's July 1 M365 price increase is estimated to add roughly $2B, $4B, and $6B of incremental revenue across fiscal 2027–2029. On capex, the team said consensus estimates "remain conservative" relative to Microsoft's buildout, with CFO Amy Hood guiding for roughly $190 billion of total 2026 capex.

## Wedbush adds Alphabet and Reddit to Best Ideas List

Wedbush analyst Ygal Arounian added Alphabet and Reddit to the firm's Best Ideas List, citing cloud share gains and advertising momentum. Google Cloud revenue grew 82% year-over-year to $24.8 billion, accelerating from 63% growth in Q1, with Cloud operating margin expanding to 35.6% from 20.7% a year earlier and backlog growing to $514 billion — though Alphabet raised full-year capex guidance to $195–$205 billion, pushing Q2 free cash flow to negative $5.9 billion. For Reddit, Arounian flagged active advertisers up more than 75% year-over-year and AI data-licensing deals with Google (~$60M/year) and OpenAI (~$70M/year).

## Mizuho names Meta top pick ahead of Q2 earnings

Mizuho named Meta Platforms its top pick among mega-cap tech ahead of Q2 earnings, reiterating Outperform and an $835 price target. A key new catalyst: Meta's reported deal to lease computing power to Anthropic, worth up to $10 billion over two years — Mizuho called it "the first sign of capex return on investment," estimating it could add $1–1.25/share in tax-affected EPS annually.

## Semiconductor pullback is a buying opportunity, Citi says

Citi views the recent pullback in semiconductor stocks — driven by surging oil prices, rising bond yields, and AI-spending concerns — as a buying opportunity, preferring semiconductor capital equipment stocks over chip stocks. Data centers account for 34% of total semiconductor demand and are on track to exceed the entire semiconductor TAM by 2030. Intel raised 2026 capex guidance to more than $20 billion; TSMC hiked 2026 capex guidance to $60–64 billion and announced an additional $100 billion investment in Arizona.

## Jefferies cuts Datadog to Hold after strong rally

Jefferies downgraded Datadog to Hold from Buy on valuation, even while maintaining its view of the company as an AI beneficiary — shares are up 94% YTD. At roughly 18x EV/CY27 revenue, Jefferies said Datadog "trades at a 4-turn premium to Snowflake, leaving little cushion for execution slippage." The broker favors Amazon, Microsoft, and Atlassian heading into earnings, and turned negative on Palantir citing "escalating comps and competition."
