# Microsoft logs $3.2B from Anthropic investment, but OpenAI was a mixed bag

In its fiscal Q4 2026 earnings (ending June 30), Microsoft disclosed contrasting results from its two major AI investments.

**Anthropic:** Microsoft recorded a $3.2 billion gain, boosting diluted EPS by $0.33. This stems from a $5B investment made in November 2025, tied to a deal where Anthropic committed to purchasing $30B in Azure services.

**OpenAI:** The investment was marked down roughly $600 million for the quarter, reducing EPS by ~$0.07. Microsoft holds about 27% of OpenAI.

**Full-year view:** OpenAI's investment generated a $5 billion gain for the fiscal year, adding $0.67 to EPS — but notably, Microsoft nearly matched that in a single quarter with Anthropic.

Microsoft's overall quarter was strong: $90B revenue, $35.8B net income. For the full year: $331.8B revenue, $133.7B net income, with diluted EPS of $17.95.
