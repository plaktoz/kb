# Dow Soars 600 Points After Sharp Selloff

source_url: https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/stock-market-news-today-073026

---

By Naomi Buchanan, Barron's. Last updated July 30, 2026 at 4:44 PM ET.

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq also rallied Thursday, with tech stocks doing most of the heavy lifting. All three major indexes closed the session higher, but under the surface Wall Street's rebound rally was more complicated than the headline numbers suggest.

The Nasdaq Composite led gains, up 2.8%, while the S&P 500 rose 1.7%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed 1.2%, or 614 points.

"Typical snap back day after an exaggerated Fed Day move," said Carson Group's chief strategist Ryan Detrick, nodding to tech leading gains.

Tech stocks were more than pulling their weight, leading the climb higher. In fact, the S&P 500 Ex-Information Technology was down for the day. The strength in tech stocks offset what was otherwise a rough day for the broader market: nearly two-thirds of the S&P 500's components were trading lower, and the equal-weight S&P 500 was in the red. Looking beyond the index level, it wasn't much of a bounce back for most of the market.

Other key events from the session: memory stocks surged, with Sandisk topping S&P 500 gainers; Microsoft had its best day since 2008, offsetting laggards elsewhere in the Dow; a sharp spike in the Japanese yen stoked currency-intervention worries; and Amazon shares rose ahead of its earnings report, with investors watching whether it would follow Microsoft's or Meta's lead.
