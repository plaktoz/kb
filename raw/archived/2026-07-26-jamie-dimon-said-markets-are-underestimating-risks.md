# Jamie Dimon Said Markets Are Underestimating Risks Shifting "Like Tectonic Plates"

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/jamie-dimon-said-markets-are-underestimating-risks/

---

JPMorgan Chase reported Q2 2026 earnings of $7.70/share — up 47% year-over-year — marking its strongest quarter on record. However, a $1.27/share one-time gain from a Visa securities conversion inflated results; adjusted earnings were $6.43/share.

Despite the strong performance, CEO Jamie Dimon cautioned that risks are building beneath the surface. He warned of "geopolitical tensions and wars, sticky inflation, large global fiscal deficits and elevated asset prices," comparing these forces to tectonic plates that "could also cause meaningful disruptions when they shift or collide."

Dimon isn't urging panic — he acknowledged conditions remain favorable and the bank continues performing well. His message is one of tempered optimism: investors may be too focused on present strength while underestimating compounding global risks. If those risks materialize into recession or a bear market, JPMorgan's results could deteriorate alongside its customers' financial health.
