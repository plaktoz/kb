# AT&T Beat on Earnings, Announced a $10 Billion Buyback, and Still Trades at 8 Times Earnings With a 4.6% Yield

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/att-beat-on-earnings-announced-a-10-billion-buybac/

---

AT&T's Q2 2026 results showed modest 2.3% revenue growth to $31.6 billion, but profit metrics moved faster. Adjusted EPS climbed to $0.65 from $0.54 — roughly 20% year-over-year growth. Free cash flow reached $4.7 billion, up from $4.4 billion. The company added 432,000 postpaid phone subscribers and 646,000 internet customers, with fiber now passing 38.6 million locations.

Management raised its 2026 buyback plan to ~$10 billion (from $8 billion). Combined with ~$7.6 billion in annual dividends, shareholder returns nearly match the guided "at least $18 billion" in free cash flow — leaving little cushion. Longer-term, management projects FCF above $19 billion in 2027 and $21 billion in 2028, with $45 billion total returns planned over three years.

Net debt stands at $126.4 billion (2.68x EBITDA), expected to rise to ~3.2x after the EchoStar deal closes, then decline toward 2.5x over three years.

The stock trades near 10x adjusted EPS guidance of "$2.25 to $2.35" for 2026. The author views it as reasonable for income investors — covered yield, fiber-driven margin expansion — but notes revenue growing only ~2% annually is "just a few percentage points from flipping to a decline."
