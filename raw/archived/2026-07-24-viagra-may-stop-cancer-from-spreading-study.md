# Viagra May Stop Cancer from Spreading, Study Suggests

source_url: https://www.healthline.com/health-news/viagra-may-stop-cancer-from-spreading-study

---

Author: David Mills
Published: July 24, 2026

A new study suggests sildenafil (Viagra) may inhibit cancer metastasis through a mechanism involving cholesterol regulation. Researchers found the drug appears to block a pathway that cancer cells use to acquire cholesterol, a nutrient they need to spread.

Key findings:
- Sildenafil inhibited metastatic activity in lab models of multiple cancer types
- The mechanism involves PDE5 inhibition, which affects cholesterol uptake pathways in tumor cells
- Researchers are cautiously optimistic but note this is preclinical research, not yet tested in human trials

Context and caveats:
- This is not a recommendation for patients to self-administer sildenafil as a cancer treatment
- Prior studies have explored sildenafil's potential anti-cancer properties; this adds specificity around the cholesterol mechanism
- Human clinical trials would be necessary to establish safety and efficacy
- Cardiovascular contraindications for sildenafil remain unchanged

The finding is notable because sildenafil is a widely available, well-understood drug with a long safety record — making it a lower-barrier candidate for cancer repurposing research if human trials confirm the mechanism.
