# Cloudflare to Post Q2 Earnings: What's in Store for the Stock?

Cloudflare (NET) is scheduled to report second-quarter 2026 results on August 6. The company anticipates revenue between $664-665 million, with the Zacks Consensus Estimate at $665.4 million, implying 30% year-over-year growth. Non-GAAP EPS is expected around 27 cents, up 28.6% year-over-year. Cloudflare has beaten the Zacks Consensus Estimate in each of the trailing four quarters, with an average surprise of 11.62%.

## Growth drivers

Results are expected to benefit from rapid AI adoption and the emergence of the "agentic Internet," as enterprises deploy AI agents and autonomous workflows that drive demand for Cloudflare's network, application, and security offerings. Management views AI-driven traffic as a structural growth opportunity.

Cloudflare's Workers developer platform is another expected growth driver, as enterprises build AI-native applications and developers embrace AI-assisted coding. Rising demand for AI Gateway, Agent Cloud, and AI Crawl Control is likely to support the top line. The ongoing shift toward Zero Trust cybersecurity architectures — with strong adoption of Cloudflare One, SASE, Browser Isolation, Gateway, and Access — is also expected to be a tailwind, as enterprises consolidate networking and security vendors onto unified platforms.

## Headwinds

Higher network utilization and a growing mix of developer-focused products are likely to keep pressuring gross margins, as rising AI and application traffic drives up infrastructure costs. Persistent macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty is also expected to weigh on enterprise spending decisions.
