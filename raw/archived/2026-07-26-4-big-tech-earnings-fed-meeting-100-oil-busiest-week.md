# 4 Big Tech Earnings Reports, a Fed Meeting, and $100 Oil: It's the Busiest Week of the Quarter

---

Author: Jake Conley, Breaking Business News Reporter
Published: July 26, 2026

Investors face a packed week with four Magnificent Seven earnings reports, a Federal Reserve decision, and oil crossing $100/barrel.

Markets: The S&P 500 finished the prior week down 0.6%, the Dow off 0.4%, and the Nasdaq down 2.1%.

Big Tech Earnings: Microsoft and Meta report Wednesday; Apple and Amazon on Thursday. The central question mirrors Alphabet's recent disappointment — spending is surging, but returns remain unclear. Alphabet's stock sold off despite beating estimates after announcing ~$200B in 2026 capex and its first-ever negative free cash flow as a public company. Deutsche Bank forecasts Microsoft will raise capex guidance to $238B, with free cash flow near breakeven, down from over $70B just years ago.

The Fed: A rate hold is widely expected, but markets are fully pricing one hike in September. Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack noted she's hearing from businesses urging action: "inflation isn't coming from only one source—it's broad based."

Oil: Brent futures crossed $100/barrel after Houthi attacks on Saudi vessels threatened the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Rystad Energy warns that "the risk of a significant rebound in oil prices would be substantial" without a ceasefire, as fewer shipping routes, strained refineries, and depleted buffers compound the supply crunch.
