# GLP-1s Like Ozempic, Wegovy Can Help Maintain Lower Calorie Intake for Over 1 Year

source_url: https://www.healthline.com/health-news/semaglutide-sustains-lower-calorie-intake-over-one-year

---

A 60-week study published in the American Journal of Clinical Nutrition (July 16, 2026) examined 120 adults with overweight or obesity. Participants received either weekly 2.4mg semaglutide or a placebo, plus lifestyle counseling. Calorie intake was measured at weeks 20, 40, and 60 using standardized meals eaten to comfortable fullness.

Key findings:
- Semaglutide users consumed 24–30% fewer calories than the placebo group at every assessment point
- Semaglutide participants lost an average 15.1% of body weight vs. 3.4% for placebo
- Hunger and food preoccupation improvements were significant early on but became statistically insignificant by weeks 40–60, yet calorie reduction persisted

Dr. Mir Ali noted that "the benefits of the medication can persist over time, although some patients may become slightly less sensitive to its effects."

Dr. Jonathan Jennings explained that semaglutide reduces insulin levels, which in turn lowers appetite stimulation.

Both experts emphasized medication works best alongside sustainable dietary and lifestyle changes, not as a standalone solution.
