# Markets News, Aug. 5, 2026: Dow Extends Rally, Sets Record High as Major Indexes Finish Mixed; SpaceX, AMD Shares Weigh on Nasdaq

The Dow Jones Industrial Average finished at a record high Wednesday, up 0.5% for a fifth straight winning session and third straight record close, while the S&P 500 (-0.2%) and Nasdaq (-0.8%) ended mixed, dragged down by SpaceX and AMD. SpaceX shares retreated nearly 14% and AMD dropped 6.5% despite results topping estimates. Other post-earnings movers: Shopify soared 17% on a "monster quarter" (34% revenue growth, gross merchandise volume up 32% to $115.57 billion), Booking Holdings rose 6.5%, Eli Lilly gained nearly 5%, Disney gained almost 4% after beating profit estimates and unveiling a TikTok content partnership, while Pinterest, Uber, and CVS Health each fell.

**Alphabet shares fell 3.5% to lead Dow decliners** after CEO Sundar Pichai announced a major AI leadership reshuffle: Chief Scientist Jeff Dean (a 27-year Google employee) and Senior Fellow Sanjay Ghemawat are leaving to launch an independent public-benefit corporation focused on ML, science, and engineering discovery, with Google investing in the new venture. Demis Hassabis is stepping back from day-to-day Google DeepMind operations to become the unit's Chair and Alphabet's Chief Scientist, while Koray Kavukcuoglu — DeepMind's current CTO and Google's Chief AI Architect — becomes SVP overseeing Gemini model development, frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. The reshuffle comes ahead of Google's still-unreleased Gemini 3.5 Pro model; Alphabet shares remain up about 16% for the year despite the pullback.

**Nvidia rose about 3.5%** to lead the Magnificent Seven after Elon Musk said on SpaceX's first public-company earnings call that SpaceX will buy Nvidia chips "exclusively" for its AI products going forward, citing the Vera Rubin architecture as "the best AI computer." Nvidia shares are up 17% year-to-date but still about 7% off their May highs.

Oil prices were little changed as investors awaited word on a possible US-Iran deal over the Strait of Hormuz (WTI slipped 0.8% to $75.15/barrel). The 10-year Treasury yield was near 4.63%, and gold gained 3.9% to $4,315/oz. Insulet shares dropped 20% after cutting full-year Omnipod revenue guidance, and Sandisk/Western Digital fell ahead of earnings after the bell, despite both remaining among 2026's top S&P 500 performers (up roughly 500% and tripled respectively) amid the AI-driven data-storage boom.
