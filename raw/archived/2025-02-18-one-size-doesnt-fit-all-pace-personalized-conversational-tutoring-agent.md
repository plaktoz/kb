# One Size doesn't Fit All: A Personalized Conversational Tutoring Agent for Mathematics Instruction

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12633

---

**Authors:** Ben Liu, Jihan Zhang, Fangquan Lin, Xu Jia, Min Peng

**Submitted:** February 18, 2025 (v1); revised February 19, 2025 (v2)

**Abstract:**

The paper introduces PACE (Personalized Conversational Tutoring Agent), an LLM-based system designed for mathematics education. Key aspects include:

- Simulates student learning styles using the Felder and Silverman learning style model to align with individual learner personas
- Employs the Socratic teaching method to deliver real-time feedback and encourage deeper reasoning
- Addresses a gap in prior work: most existing systems overlook individual learner differences, which are critical for engagement and comprehension in math
- Builds personalized training datasets and trains models to adapt to each student's needs
- Establishes multi-aspect evaluation criteria to assess personalized teaching performance

The authors report that PACE outperforms existing approaches in personalizing the educational experience and boosting student motivation.
