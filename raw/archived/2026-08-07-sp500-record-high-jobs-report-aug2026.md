Stocks rose Friday, led by tech sector gains, as investors digested a surprising report on the labor market that eased concerns about possible rate hikes.

The S&P 500 advanced 0.6% to close at a record high for the second time this week, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite added 1.3% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average tacked on 0.3%. The major indexes posted their [biggest weekly gains since April](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-nasdaq-08072026-12036199#toc-2026-08-07t203250461z).

The [July jobs report released this morning](https://www.investopedia.com/july-jobs-unemployment-report-12036335) showed that U.S. employers shed 23,000 jobs in July, compared with economists’ expectations for an 83,000 increase, while the unemployment rate ticked lower to 4.1% as labor force participation shrunk.

Investors are hoping that the surprisingly soft hiring numbers—the report also included a downward revision of the June employment figure—could keep the Federal Reserve from raising interest rates soon.
