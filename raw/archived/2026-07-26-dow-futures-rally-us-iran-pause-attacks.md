# Dow Futures Rally 500 Points as Oil Prices Fall After U.S. and Iran Pause Attacks

source_url: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html

---

Published: July 26–27, 2026 (live updates)

Stock futures rallied early Monday as oil prices declined, amid a pause in fighting between the U.S. and Iran over the weekend. Traders were also looking ahead to a demanding week of megacap earnings and a potentially surprising Federal Reserve meeting.

Dow Jones Industrial Average futures rose by 550 points, or 1.1%. S&P 500 futures gained 1%, while Nasdaq-100 futures advanced 1.7%.

Cooling of tensions in the Middle East sent Brent crude futures down 9.2% to around $87.89 a barrel. WTI crude dropped over 7% to $82.46 a barrel.

The week ahead will have no shortage of challenges for the major averages. Quarterly reports from Amazon, Apple, Meta Platforms and Microsoft will either soothe investor fears of rampant artificial intelligence spending, or add to them, after Alphabet's disappointing results last week. The results could directly affect semiconductor companies that are the chief beneficiaries of the AI spending spree.

"The biggest risk is the continuation of the spend," said Ken Mahoney, CEO of Mahoney Asset Management. "And then the problem is, if they do listen to shareholders and wind down a little bit of that spend, or reduce the growth of that spend, then the rest of the market is not going to like it."

The Fed will make its latest decision on interest rates on Wednesday. The consensus view is that the central bank will hike rates in September, but markets are pricing in the meaningful possibility of a quarter-point hike as soon as this week.

U.S. equities are coming off yet another losing week — the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq slid 0.6% and 2.1% respectively — posting back-to-back weekly losses.

Singapore tightened its monetary policy for a second consecutive time, moving preemptively against renewed oil price risk even as domestic inflation stays subdued. The MAS increased the rate of appreciation of the Singapore dollar's NEER policy band "very slightly."

China's industrial profits rose 15.1% in June, slowing for a second straight month from May's 21.1% gain, as AI-fueled chip and equipment manufacturing gains moderated.

China memory chipmaker CXMT debuted on Shanghai's STAR Market surging ~470%, raising $8.6 billion — Asia's largest IPO so far this year.
