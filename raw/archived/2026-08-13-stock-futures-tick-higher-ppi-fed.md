# Stock Market Today: Stock Futures Tick Higher

## Asian markets climb on AI enthusiasm, yields dip

U.S. stock futures are ticking higher this morning and government bond yields are retreating slightly as traders adjust to predictions that the Federal Reserve won't hike rates imminently.

Investors now see a 66% chance that officials hold rates steady when they meet next month, according to CME Group data, compared to 45% a week ago. Bets that the Fed may not hike at all this year are edging higher, too. Traders will get another look at inflation data this morning, with the release of the producer-price index at 8:30 a.m. ET.

Enthusiasm for the AI trade continues to build overseas, with South Korea's Kospi index gaining more than 3% for a second straight day, thanks to gains in Samsung and SK Hynix. Meanwhile, shares of Lenovo surged 19% in Hong Kong after it recorded record-high revenue thanks in part to robust AI infrastructure demand.

Otherwise, the market's quiet stretch rolls on, with trading volumes staying low as many investors are away from their desks for summer vacations. With few new developments in the Iran war to sway the oil market, Brent crude futures retreated slightly to trade below $88 a barrel.
