## Key IBM news this week

- **Positive:** IBM and partners including Algorithmiq, Qedma, RIKEN, and the University of Chicago reported multiple quantum-advantage results, using IBM quantum systems and error-reduction software to simulate quantum materials and perform calculations the companies say are beyond practical classical-computing capability — strengthening IBM's long-term quantum positioning, though revenue and commercialization remain uncertain.
- **Positive:** IBM's 2026 Cost of a Data Breach research found AI-enabled attacks are rising and more expensive than average breaches, potentially supporting demand for IBM's cybersecurity and AI-security consulting offerings.
- **Neutral:** Circle acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio (nearly 1,000 issued patents, 680+ patent families); the financial terms were not disclosed.
- **Negative:** The quantum announcements haven't offset concerns about IBM's growth profile. Recent quarterly results matched EPS expectations but revenue of $17.16 billion fell short of the $17.46 billion consensus, with year-over-year revenue growth of just 1.1%. Coverage also noted less visibility into generative-AI progress in the latest earnings discussion.
- **Negative:** Erste Group Bank slightly reduced its fiscal 2026 and 2027 IBM EPS forecasts.
- **Negative:** Pomerantz and Bleichmar Fonti & Auld announced investigations into potential securities-law violations related to IBM's performance and an alleged slowdown in its IBM Z product business; the investigations don't establish wrongdoing but raise headline and litigation risk.
