# Microsoft and Meta Platforms Are Negative in 2026. Here's My Favorite One to Buy Now.

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/microsoft-and-meta-platforms-are-negative-in-2026/

---

Both Microsoft (MSFT) and Meta Platforms (META) are down in 2026 — Meta roughly 10%, Microsoft over 20% — despite being major AI hyperscalers.

Meta's struggles stem from market skepticism about its massive AI spending, drawing comparisons to its failed metaverse bet. Its Llama model lacks broad adoption, and Meta has no clear monetization path. Its long-term bet centers on a superintelligence model paired with AR glasses.

Microsoft's decline is less logical. Its AI annual revenue run rate hit $37 billion, up 123% year-over-year, with cloud revenue growing 40%. The author describes it as executing well with "little execution risk."

Both stocks trade below the S&P 500's ~21.5x forward earnings multiple.

Meta catalyst: Rumors of a cloud computing launch could provide investors a clearer monetization path for its data center spending.

Conclusion: The author favors Microsoft as the better buy, calling it "about as no-brainer a buy in the stock market as it gets," given its proven AI and cloud execution.
