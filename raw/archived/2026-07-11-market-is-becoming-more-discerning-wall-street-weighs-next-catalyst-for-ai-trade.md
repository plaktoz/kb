# 'Market is becoming more discerning': Wall Street Weighs Next Catalyst for AI Trade

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/market-is-becoming-more-discerning-wall-street-weighs-next-catalyst-for-ai-trade-114052280.html

---

Wall Street is seeking follow-through on the AI trade heading into earnings season. The S&P 500 gained under 1% last week, while the Nasdaq whipsawed with semiconductors at the center of volatility.

T. Rowe Price's Tony Wang described recent market moves as "pretty frenetic," adding that earnings season should clarify the outlook for coming quarters.

SK Hynix's Nasdaq debut — shares surged 14% — signaled continued market enthusiasm. Kevin Mahn of Hennion & Walsh noted that when Micron signals it can't meet demand, SK Hynix stands to benefit, adding: "I see more opportunities in memory."

Since late June, the Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF rebounded ~8%, while the PHLX Semiconductor Index fell 12%. Wang observed the market has shifted from rewarding all AI spending to becoming "more discerning," with margins, revenue, and free cash flow now under scrutiny.

UBS strategists noted investors are shifting focus toward sustainability of AI spending beyond 2027, favoring semiconductor equipment, foundries, memory, payment networks, and data center REITs. Yardeni Research characterized recent semiconductor weakness as "a buying opportunity."
