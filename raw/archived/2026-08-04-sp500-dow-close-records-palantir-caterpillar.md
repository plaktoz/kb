# Markets News, Aug. 4, 2026: S&P 500, Dow Close at Records as Palantir, Caterpillar Shares Jump; Oil Drops on US-Iran Deal Optimism

Major stock indexes soared Tuesday, with the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average setting new records, as Palantir Technologies and Caterpillar shares jumped and oil prices sank further. The Nasdaq Composite, S&P 500, and Dow surged 2.6%, 1.8%, and 1.7% respectively. The Dow added more than 900 points to notch its second closing high this week.

Palantir (PLTR) reported better-than-expected results and lifted its outlook after markets closed Monday, and shares soared nearly 30% Tuesday to lead the S&P 500 and Nasdaq — its best day in over a year. William Blair analysts wrote that Palantir's "stellar performance defies concerns" about competition from OpenAI and Anthropic for business customers. Jefferies analysts were more cautious, preferring other AI and software stocks like Microsoft, Amazon, and Snowflake, citing Palantir's international growth and valuation.

Caterpillar (CAT) shares surged about 10% after the company reported profit and revenue far above estimates, driven by demand for construction and power-generation equipment as big tech companies build AI data centers. CEO Joe Creed cited "broadening momentum across all three of our primary segments."

The Information Technology sector led S&P 500 gains, up 3.7% in early afternoon trading. The Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) and iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) jumped 7.3% and 6.8% respectively as AI-tied chip stocks soared.

Amazon (AMZN) declined more than 2% after a regulatory filing showed founder Jeff Bezos intended to sell 15 million shares worth over $4 billion — even after Amazon joined the $3 trillion market-cap club a day earlier following strong AWS growth.

Oil prices extended declines after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the U.S. and Iran "may have a deal today or tomorrow" to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. WTI crude fell 5.8% to $75.70 a barrel. Energy stocks NRG Energy and Diamondback Energy were among the day's biggest S&P 500 decliners.

The 10-year Treasury yield eased to near 4.63%. Bitcoin traded around $64,300, and gold rose 1.2% to $4,140 an ounce.
