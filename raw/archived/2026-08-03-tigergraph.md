# TigerGraph

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TigerGraph
Scraped: 2026-08-03

## Overview

TigerGraph is a private American software company based in Redwood City, California, offering graph database and analytics software.

---

## History

- **Founded:** 2012 by Ruoming Jin, Li Chen, Mingxi Wu, Like Gao, and Yu Xu, originally under the name **GraphSQL**
- **2017:** Emerged from stealth mode rebranded as TigerGraph, securing $33M in funding
- **2019:** Raised an additional $32M
- **2021:** Completed a $105M Series C round; cumulative funding reached $170M by March 2021

---

## Products & Features

The platform is a **hybrid transactional/analytical processing** database capable of scaling to hundreds of terabytes with trillions of edges.

**Use cases include:**
- Fraud detection
- Customer 360 analytics
- IoT
- AI and machine learning

**Technical highlights:**
- C++-based analytics engine with parallel processing
- Available as a cloud-delivered service
- Includes a software development kit for graph visualization
- Current version: **4.2.0** (as of March 2024)
- Free **Community Edition** available for developers, researchers, and educators

---

## Query Language: GSQL

GSQL is a "SQL-like Turing complete query language" designed by Mingxi Wu and Alin Deutsch in 2015. It includes extensions for compliance with the **Graph Query Language (GQL)** standard.
