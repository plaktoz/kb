# Meta Slipped Back Below $600 Before Wednesday's Report. It Now Trades at About 22 Times Earnings. Here's What Investors Should Know.

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/meta-slipped-back-below-600-before-wednesdays-repo/

---

Author: Daniel Sparks
Published: July 26, 2026

Meta closed at $595.19 — roughly 25% below its 52-week high — trading at ~22x earnings, below the S&P 500's ~28.5 multiple. The selloff isn't driven by advertising weakness; Q1 revenue surged 33% YoY to $56.31B, with ad impressions up 19% and average ad price up 12%.

The concern centers on capital expenditure. Meta raised its 2026 capex range to "$125 billion to $145 billion," and spent nearly $20B in Q1 alone. Investors worry that range could climb further, and that AI infrastructure spending may not yield returns on any predictable timeline.

Despite heavy spending, Meta generated $12.39B in free cash flow in Q1 and held $81.18B in cash and securities. Full-year expense guidance remained unchanged.

For Q2, management guided revenue of $58–$61B (22–28% growth), a deceleration from Q1 but one already "sitting in guidance." Wednesday's report will reveal whether the ad engine remains strong and whether capex guidance expands again — the central anxiety behind the drawdown.
