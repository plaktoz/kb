# The latest AI news we announced in July 2026

July brought a wave of AI advances to support developers, simplify everyday life, and protect local communities. Google gave developers faster, more efficient Gemini models for scaling production agents and advancing robotics, expanded its creative suite with next-generation music and video tools, and made Search and Gemini Spark more connected to everyday apps.

## AI to help developers and advance frontier AI

Google introduced three new Gemini models — Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber — built for the efficiency and quality needed to scale agentic workflows. It also launched Gemini Robotics ER 2, its most capable "embodied reasoning" model yet, designed to let robots converse naturally with people, make sense of their surroundings, and work through complex, multi-step tasks.

## AI on devices and daily life

With the Galaxy Unpacked 2026 launch of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Fold8, and Flip8, Google rolled out the first capabilities of Gemini Intelligence on Android, along with a native migration experience in Android 17 for moving data from iPhone without a separate app.

## AI for learning and the economy

Gemini Notebook (built on NotebookLM) now works across the Gemini app and Google Search with a secure cloud computer. Google also published the first iteration of its AI & Economy ATLAS, a large-scale, de-identified study of how people use AI at work and in daily life, and made AlphaEvolve — its Gemini-powered AI code-optimization agent — generally available to Google Cloud customers.

## Creative tools and community initiatives

New Gemini Omni and personal-avatar features in Google Vids make video creation easier, and the Lyria 3.5 model powers Google Flow Music for AI-assisted songwriting. Google also joined BlackRock, Carhartt, and Ford to launch the Alliance for America's Skilled Trades, and worked with NOAA to run atmospheric weather models on Google Cloud's H4D virtual machines. Three new FireSat satellites launched to expand global wildfire detection.
