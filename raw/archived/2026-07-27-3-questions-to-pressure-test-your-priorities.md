# 3 Questions to Pressure-Test Your Priorities

Walker opens with a workshop with 10 leaders at a billion-dollar tech company undergoing a SaaS-to-AI transformation, where she asked participants: "What has the last 90 days really required of you?"

The piece addresses what Walker terms **"ambient urgency"** — a condition where constant change and competing demands cause everything to feel equally critical, undermining effective prioritization.

Traditional prioritization approaches are insufficient for today's leadership environment. Leaders need a more rigorous framework to distinguish what truly matters from reactive busyness.

Walker, who coaches CEOs and senior leadership teams at high-growth companies, frames the article around three diagnostic questions designed to help leaders stress-test whether their stated priorities reflect genuine strategic choices or simply reactive busyness.
