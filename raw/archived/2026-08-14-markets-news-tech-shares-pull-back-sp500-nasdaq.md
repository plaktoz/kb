# Markets News, Aug. 14, 2026: Tech Shares Pull Back as Major Indexes Slip; S&P 500, Nasdaq Log 3rd Straight Week of Gains

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite finished higher for a third straight week, even after a Friday session where all three leading indexes lost ground. The Nasdaq, S&P 500, and Dow finished the day down 0.4%, 0.2%, and 0.1% respectively; only the Dow finished the week lower, snapping a two-week winning streak.

Indexes slipped Friday partly because U.S. retail sales data declined 0.6% in July when a 0.1% rise was expected, and the University of Michigan consumer sentiment survey came in below expectations. "Unexpected weakness in consumer spending isn't good news for the wider economy, but markets may embrace the data in the near term because it strengthens the case for avoiding rate hikes," said Ellen Zentner, Chief Economic Strategist for Morgan Stanley Wealth Management.

The 10-year Treasury yield was above 4.69%, up nearly five basis points from Thursday. The Magnificent Seven finished mostly lower; Broadcom (AVGO) fell almost 6%, the worst performer in the S&P 500, while SanDisk (SNDK) rose more than 7%.

**Reddit (RDDT)** shares soared almost 13% after S&P Dow Jones Indices announced Reddit will join the S&P 500 ahead of the opening bell Aug 18, replacing AvalonBay Communities (which Equity Residential is acquiring and renaming Vivmark Residential). Reddit CFO Drew Vollero: "We're proud to be added to the S&P 500... Our focus remains on executing against the many opportunities ahead." Reddit shares had been down more than 30% this year.

**Applied Materials (AMAT)** fell 5% despite beating estimates: adjusted EPS of $3.50 on revenue up 25% YoY to $9.12 billion, with Q4 guidance also ahead of forecasts — investors were unimpressed after a strong run (shares had more than doubled YTD through Thursday).

**Chip stocks**: Thursday's rally (SanDisk +14%, Micron +4%, Arm +3%) brought the PHLX Semiconductor Sector to the cusp of a new bull market, up nearly 20% from recent lows though still 15% off June highs. Bank of America named AMD its top pick for AI server chip growth, alongside Nvidia.

**Other notes**: Elon Musk disclosed a 48.4% stake in SpaceX (6.42 billion shares, valuing his holding at over $900 billion). Bitcoin fell to around $62,500 amid dashed regulatory hopes after the SEC canceled a planned meeting on crypto-startup fundraising rules and the Senate recessed without advancing the Clarity Act. Workday (WDAY) shares gave back gains after soaring 18% on acquisition talk with Silver Lake. Crude oil (WTI) rose 1.3% to $82.25/barrel amid Iran sanctions rhetoric from Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent.
