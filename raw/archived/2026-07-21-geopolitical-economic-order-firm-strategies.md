# New Geopolitical-Economic Order Framework Shows Firm Strategies Vary Under Two Types of State-Market Order

source_url: https://phys.org/news/2026-07-geopolitical-economic-framework-firm-strategies.html

---

Published: July 21, 2026 — Phys.org / Strategic Management Society

A new research framework in the Global Strategy Journal shows that firms operating internationally face fundamentally different strategic environments depending on whether they operate under a state-based or market-based geopolitical-economic order.

## First Principles Foundation

The research team returned to the first principles of international relations: anarchy is what makes international politics different from domestic politics. Because there is no world government to safeguard countries' interests, governments prioritize geopolitics. "This creates an incentive to cooperate at times and compete at others, which, in turn, shapes the state-market relationship that global companies encounter."

## Two Types of Order

**State-based order:** Firms' competitive advantage comes from their bargaining power and corporate nationality. They should highlight their relevance to the host country's national goals and mitigate security concerns. Large firms or those from more powerful home countries are most likely to succeed.

**Market-based order:** MNEs focus heavily on exploiting global efficiencies. Competitive advantage comes from operational excellence and cost optimization rather than geopolitical positioning.

## Strategic Implications

The framework argues that global strategy must account for which type of order governs a given market or region — the same operational strategy that succeeds in a market-based order may fail in a state-based one. Companies that misread the order type risk misallocating resources and facing unexpected regulatory or political friction.

## Citation
