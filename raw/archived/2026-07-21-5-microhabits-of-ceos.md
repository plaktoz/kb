# 5 Microhabits of CEOs

source_url: https://www.kornferry.com/insights/this-week-in-leadership/5-microhabits-of-ceos

---

Korn Ferry, This Week in Leadership — July 21, 2026

Science shows repetitive daily habits can help leaders reduce decision fatigue, improve execution, and relieve stress. Korn Ferry's leadership-development experts shared five microhabits they see among CEO clients:

**Take a "forest bath."** One industrial-firm CEO takes a walk in the woods each morning to set intentions and impact for the day, says Kevin Cashman of Korn Ferry's CEO and Enterprise Leadership practice. Not everyone has access to a forest, but solo time each morning to focus and set priorities is a portable habit.

**Have a 30-minute rule.** Two CEOs Alan Guarino works with spend only a half hour on work email each Saturday and Sunday, protecting the rest of the weekend to recharge and pace themselves — a guardrail many leaders adopted more seriously after the pandemic.

**Find the skeptic.** After a big meeting, some leaders follow a "one last question" rule: intentionally giving the final word to the most skeptical person in the room, to challenge groupthink and surface overlooked risks.

**Create a priority list.** Many CEOs end the day by writing down the top three priorities for tomorrow, helping them focus time and energy on what matters most while delegating the rest.

**Give yourself a daily pep talk.** One CEO spends under five minutes each morning on positive self-talk, countering a tendency toward self-criticism and setting a positive tone for the day, says Jamen Graves, Korn Ferry's global leader of CEO and enterprise leadership development.

"Every CEO has a unique personality, style, and set of circumstances that require a bespoke set of habits and routines to help them, their team, and the enterprise operate at an optimal level," Graves said.
