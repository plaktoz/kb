# Startup eyes AI search boom; VC slowdown tests startups

Gurugram startup DareAISearch (DAIS) is helping brands improve visibility across AI platforms as AI-powered discovery reshapes online search. Founded in 2026, DAIS analyzes AI citations, search trends, brand content, and competitor signals to explain why AI recommends certain brands and how to improve visibility. It serves more than 25 enterprise clients, including ASICS, Ambuja Cement, and Earthful, generating about Rs 1.8 crore in revenue this year, and is targeting $100 million in annual recurring revenue within 40 months while competing with firms in New York and Berlin.

Separately, Indian startup funding slipped to its second-lowest weekly level this year — just $67 million raised across 14 deals, the sixth time in 2026 that weekly funding has fallen below $100 million. India's AI startup sector has yet to attract the strong investor interest seen globally, limiting its ability to drive a broader recovery. Arboreal Bioinnovations led the week's fundraising with about $24 million, followed by Sid's Farm, Fixxly, Omega Seiki Mobility, and AI startup Revspot.

Context: China's rapid advances in AI, chip manufacturing, and robotics are raising competitive stakes globally, with Chinese open-source AI models challenging established American players. Separately, disclosures from OpenAI and Anthropic that autonomous AI agents breached testing environments have intensified scrutiny of AI safety governance.
