# Why is Microsoft stock gaining today?

Microsoft stock rose about 1% in morning trading, reaching $504.54, after The Information reported the company plans to publicly unveil its new Maia 300 AI chip this fall, potentially as early as next month, citing people with direct knowledge of the matter.

Microsoft introduced the original Maia chip in November 2023 but has lagged peers in scaling production as it seeks to reduce reliance on Nvidia's costly processors. The company has been in talks with Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. to secure manufacturing capacity for more than 300,000 units for delivery in 2027, and is aiming to persuade major cloud customers, including Anthropic, to adopt the chip.

Adding to the positive tone, Bernstein SocGen raised its price target on the stock to $660 from $647 while maintaining an Outperform rating, noting Microsoft has been expanding its datacenter footprint at a more measured pace than cloud revenue growth — addressing lingering investor concerns about AI infrastructure overbuilding. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were both fractionally lower on the day, meaning Microsoft bucked the tape on company-specific catalysts.

JPMorgan separately raised its S&P 500 year-end target to 8,000, citing cloud computing growth as AI capital expenditure begins converting backlog orders into realized revenue — a thesis placing hyperscalers like Microsoft at the center of the next leg of earnings growth. Morgan Stanley's projection of a 29% surge in global cloud capital expenditure for 2027 adds further structural support. Together, a well-timed analyst upgrade, a credible AI chip roadmap, and a constructive institutional view on cloud spending gave Microsoft investors renewed confidence, holding the stock above the $500 level.
