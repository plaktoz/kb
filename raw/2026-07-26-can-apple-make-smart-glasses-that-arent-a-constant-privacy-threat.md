# Can Apple make smart glasses that aren't a constant privacy threat?

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/can-apple-make-smart-glasses-that-arent-a-constant-privacy-threat/

---

Apple is preparing its first smart glasses launch, but is also grappling with privacy concerns, per Bloomberg's Mark Gurman. The unveiling has been pushed to WWDC in June 2027, with availability expected by year's end.

The delay stems partly from lessons observed around Meta's smart glasses, which drew controversy for being used in "non-consensual video recordings." The issue carries more weight for Apple, given the company's strong marketing emphasis on user privacy.

Apple's planned approach reportedly includes on-device processing, no facial recognition, avoiding use of customer recordings for AI training, and distancing itself from Meta's reported practice of using "contractors to review customer footage."
