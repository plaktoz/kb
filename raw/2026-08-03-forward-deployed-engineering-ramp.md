# Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE): Lessons from Ramp

Source: https://builders.ramp.com/post/forward-deployed-engineering
Scraped: 2026-08-03

## What Is FDE?

Palantir invented the role post-2003, placing engineers physically at customer sites to iterate rapidly on real problems. For a decade it stayed niche, then Scale AI, C3.ai, Databricks, and Snowflake adopted similar models. Now it's standard at OpenAI, Anthropic, Ramp, and many AI startups.

The core purpose remains constant: deliver customer value at maximum speed and quality.

---

## Why FDE Is Exploding

Enterprise B2B companies dramatically outgrow their SMB peers — the non-enterprise CAGR averages ~15%, while enterprise averages ~30%. Winning enterprise requires deep customization, legacy system integration, and genuine outcome delivery. As Foundation Capital notes, "how you integrate, embed, and operate becomes the moat."

## FDE at Ramp

Launched in late 2023 with 2 engineers, the team grew ~10x to 16 FDEs. Early days involved constant firefighting; over time the team progressed from reactive survival to proactive innovation — building generalized features, platforms, and AI tooling for enterprise segments including healthcare and government.

The team is heavy on AI tooling, using Cursor and Claude Code extensively.

---

## Operating Model: Follow the Customer Lifecycle

FDEs stay with customers from pre-sales through long-tail support. Benefits include:

- **Continuity** — retained context on customer environments and preferences
- **Early scoping** — reducing complexity before commitments solidify
- **Long-term accountability** — success measured beyond close or go-live

### Prioritization Framework

1. Serve existing customers well (retention before growth)
2. Accelerate onboarding and activation (implementation is the bottleneck)
3. Expand product capabilities to grow TAM

## Core Principles

### 1. Always Be Scoping

Before FDE existed, a common failure pattern emerged: customer request → AE relays it → engineering scopes a multi-month project → delays, shifting requirements, frustration all around.

FDEs break this by talking directly to customers and questioning every requirement. One example: a feature gap estimated at 3 engineer-days was resolved in a single call via a workaround. Scoping prevents wasted effort and accelerates delivery.

### 2. Generalize the Work

Even when solving one customer's problem, FDEs ask: *is this a one-off hack, or something hundreds of customers could use?* Generalized solutions scale; ad-hoc customizations create technical debt. But this principle creates tension with scoping — knowing when to generalize versus MVP is where FDE judgment matters most.

### 3. Do More

Speed is a competitive advantage. The team embraces high iteration pace, recognizing that "perfect is the enemy of good." Equally important is extreme ownership — FDEs treat customer outcomes as their personal responsibility, not something to hand off.

### 4. We Win When Customers Win

FDE metrics are tied directly to customer and business outcomes. The team sees itself as a conduit bringing customer-first thinking back into engineering, especially valuable as organizations grow and engineers naturally distance from end users.

## How Ramp Hires FDEs

| Dimension | What They Look For |
|---|---|
| **Drive & work ethic** | Single strongest predictor of performance; hustle matters more than polish |
| **Engineering fundamentals** | Must pass a competency bar, but perfection is not the goal — AI tools close gaps |
| **Customer empathy** | Emotional intelligence for tense negotiations, delays, and de-scoping conversations |
| **Communication** | Clear, compelling, comfortable leading customer meetings |

Previous customer-facing experience helps but isn't required. Teaching, TA roles, or internal platform work (with close user contact) are strong proxies for empathy. Notably, 7 of 16 FDEs are former founders — entrepreneurial backgrounds translate well.

---

## Key Takeaways

- FDE is a strategic unlock for enterprise B2B, not just a support function
- Customer proximity throughout the lifecycle produces better scoping, faster delivery, and lower churn risk
- The best FDEs combine technical competence with ownership mentality and empathy
- The art of the role lives in the judgment call: quick fix or scalable solution?
- "The next generation of B2B companies will be won by engineering teams that are as customer-focused as they are technically strong"
