# Everything new coming to Meta's AI agent Muse

At Meta's annual Connect event, CEO Mark Zuckerberg unveiled major expansions for Muse, the company's personal AI agent launched in early September 2026. Powered by the **Muse Spark** multimodal model, Muse is designed to automate everyday tasks across apps, devices, and digital accounts.

**Key announcements:**

- **Digital Avatar:** A new "Muse Realtime Avatar" model enables users to create a personalized visual avatar and video chat with it in real time. Zuckerberg named the default avatar "Jolly."
- **Smart Glasses Integration:** Muse will work hands-free through Meta's smart glasses via wake word, handling tasks like logging meals and booking appointments — arriving "in the coming months."
- **Mac Computer Control:** Muse will operate desktop apps autonomously, similar to offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic, letting users "walk away" while it works.
- **Dedicated Email Address:** Muse will receive its own email address, allowing it to be added to threads and handle correspondence independently.
- **Shopping & Retail Partnerships:** Integrations with Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Walmart, Wayfair, Expedia, Instacart, GitHub, Granola, and Notion were announced.

On monetization, Zuckerberg said Meta plans to keep Muse free for a large volume of usage, eventually earning revenue through small transaction fees.
