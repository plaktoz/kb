# 1 Top Warren Buffett Stock for Dividend Investors

Apple (AAPL) deserves consideration as a dividend stock despite its modest 0.33% yield. Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway holds Apple as its largest position, drawn by the company's powerful brand loyalty and sticky hardware-plus-services ecosystem.

While Apple's yield is low, the payout represents only 12% of 2026 earnings estimates, leaving substantial room for dividend growth. Analysts project roughly 13% average annual earnings growth over the next three to five years.

Apple's aggressive buyback program — over $82 billion spent in the past four quarters, reducing share count by 31.5% over a decade — provides an indirect dividend benefit. Fewer shares mean higher per-share earnings, which allows Apple to grow its dividend faster than the raw payout ratio would suggest.

Apple is also broadening its consumer base through an entry-level laptop and new leasing programs, positioning it for continued ecosystem expansion.

Dismissing Apple as a dividend stock due to its low yield overlooks the compounding effect of buybacks on per-share dividend growth potential.
