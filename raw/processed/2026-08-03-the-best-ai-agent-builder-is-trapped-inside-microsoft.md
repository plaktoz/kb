# The Best AI Agent Builder Is Trapped Inside Microsoft

Mike Taylor argues that Microsoft's Copilot Studio is a surprisingly powerful no-code AI agent builder, hidden behind a deeply frustrating onboarding experience and confusing branding.

Despite Microsoft losing ground in AI coding tools — GitHub Copilot ceded its lead to Claude Code, which now commands 63% of respondents in one survey — Copilot Studio quietly serves enterprise clients well. Taylor was tipped off by an IT leader who told him Microsoft had become "the best place for us to do this" when building AI agents.

## The core problem: discovery and setup

Microsoft has launched over 80 products named "Copilot," leading one consultant to observe: "There are now Copilots inside Copilots, Copilots for other Copilots, and a physical Copilot key on your keyboard."

Getting a colleague access required roughly 15 steps across multiple portals, mandatory bundled subscriptions (~$282/year), and still didn't surface the right product. His colleague's reaction on arriving: *"wow it's like being transported to january 2025."*

Taylor concludes the product's depth is real — but the new-user experience is a significant barrier outside of enterprise IT environments.
