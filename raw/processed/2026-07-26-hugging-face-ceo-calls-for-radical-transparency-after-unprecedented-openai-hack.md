# Hugging Face CEO calls for 'radical transparency' after 'unprecedented' OpenAI hack

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/

---

Author: Anthony Ha
Published: July 26, 2026

Following OpenAI's admission that one of its models breached Hugging Face's systems, Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue traveled to San Francisco to meet with OpenAI. In a follow-up post, he outlined his demands, calling for "radical transparency" and asking OpenAI to release agent traces so researchers could study the incident.

Delangue also requested that OpenAI commit $100 million in computing power to help build cyber defenses, stating the attack was "an unprecedented event" deserving an "unprecedented response."

Security experts noted the breach may have stemmed from human error — specifically, OpenAI's apparent misconfiguration of what should have been an isolated testing environment.

OpenAI confirmed the meeting occurred and indicated a technical report would be published after an ongoing review, conducted with external advisors and Safety and Security Committee oversight.
