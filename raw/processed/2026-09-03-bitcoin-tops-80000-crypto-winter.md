# Bitcoin Tops $80,000: 'Crypto Winter Is Close to Being Over'

**Author:** Ines Ferré, Senior Business Reporter
**Date:** Friday, September 4, 2026
**Source:** Yahoo Finance
**source_url:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/bitcoin-tops-80000-crypto-winter-is-close-to-being-over-173224980.html

---

## Summary

Bitcoin surged over 5% on Thursday, breaching $81,000, driven by easing Fed rate hike concerns and falling Treasury yields.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin historically shows negative September returns in 9 of the past 15 years, though Fundstrat's Sean Farrell noted it has "bucked" that trend recently
- The token rallied ~25% in August, aided by Treasury intervention in bond markets and assistance to Japan
- Noelle Acheson (author, *Crypto Is Macro Now*) suggested "the crypto winter is close to being over"
- Fed Governor Christopher Waller signaled support for holding rates if inflation eases
- Finality Capital's David Grider sees a potential post-FOMC rally taking bitcoin higher into late September/early October
- Bernstein analyst Gautam Chhugani maintains a **$150,000 year-end price target**
- Bitcoin remains ~7% down year-to-date and ~35% below its all-time high of **$126,000**, reached in early October 2025

## Market Context

Oil price surges and hawkish remarks from Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh had trimmed some August gains prior to Thursday's rally.
