# Anthropic is operating a lab that conducts biology experiments

Anthropic has confirmed to TechCrunch that it operates a wet biology lab in the Bay Area, using its AI models to run physical experiments. The lab focuses on fundamental biology rather than drug discovery, partly to avoid appearing competitive with pharmaceutical industry partners like Novo Nordisk.

Head of life sciences Eric Kauderer-Abrams told Reuters: "We believe that to do biology, the final test is still...in real lab work. We absolutely are doing that today."

The lab's existence follows Anthropic's April acquisition of stealth AI biotech Coefficient Bio. Alongside the lab revelation, Anthropic launched a Life Sciences Verification Program giving vetted researchers access to its most powerful models, and published research on protein design and biomolecular modeling.

The announcement arrives amid significant internal tension — a recently resigned Anthropic researcher warned that staff "earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade," and Anthropic's alignment lead placed odds above 10% on AI posing an extinction-level risk within ten years. CEO Dario Amodei has separately called for industry self-regulation and cited bioterrorism as a top AI risk.

Investor Chamath Palihapitiya noted the irony on X, quipping that the company behind warnings about human extinction is now building a wet lab in San Francisco.
