# Apple Shares 'Shocking Evidence' Against Former Employee Accused of Stealing Data for OpenAI

**Author:** Amanda Silberling
**Date:** August 31, 2026
**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/apple-shares-shocking-evidence-against-former-employee-accused-of-stealing-company-data-for-openai/

---

In its lawsuit against OpenAI, Apple filed what it describes as "shocking evidence" against former employee **Chang Liu**, who now works at OpenAI.

Key allegations from Apple's latest filing:

- Liu used a confidential Apple circuit schematic in his OpenAI work
- A tool he used shares a name with an internal Apple engineering application
- OpenAI was allegedly "well-aware" of Liu's access to Apple data
- Liu allegedly enlisted OpenAI colleague **Yu-Ting Peng** to help destroy evidence in June after learning he was under investigation

The evidence came from Liu's old Apple work laptop, which his legal counsel handed over earlier in August.

OpenAI has defended Liu, arguing he only accessed Apple files post-employment to assist former colleagues, and blamed Apple for poor access management when employees depart.

Apple counters that Liu maintained access by exploiting "a rare, previously unknown authentication bug."

Apple is seeking:
- A **preliminary injunction** blocking OpenAI from working on hardware based on Apple's technology
- **Expedited discovery**, citing concerns that additional former employees may be implicated

Over 400 former Apple employees now reportedly work at OpenAI.
