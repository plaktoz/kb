# Alphabet Stocks Jump 6.97% as AI Rebound Spotlights Google Cloud's 103% Growth

Alphabet (NASDAQ:GOOGL) shares rallied 6.97% to $283.78 at 10:40 a.m. ET in regular trading on November 23, outpacing chip partner Broadcom by more than 4 percentage points. A report from TF International's Ming-Chi Kuo, cited by TradingKey, said Google trimmed 2027 orders for its Broadcom-linked TPU 8i and plans to ship more of a MediaTek-designed TPU 8t. That reads as supplier pressure for Broadcom and more bargaining power for Google, though the details are not final.

The TPU push already shows up in Alphabet's numbers. The company reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $127.6 billion, up 25%, while Google Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.7 billion, up 103%, with TPU system sales cited as a meaningful contributor. Cloud operating income jumped to $11.1 billion from $3.6 billion. The cost is steep: purchases of property and equipment hit $51.4 billion, more than double the prior year.

That spending pushed quarterly free cash flow to about negative $9.1 billion, since capex exceeded the $42.3 billion of operating cash flow. Cloud now earns a 36% operating margin, which helps justify the outlay if growth holds. The GuruFocus chart shows a GF Score of 98/100, with top-tier profitability and a strong balance sheet. The GF Value of $276.83 leaves the price 2.5% above GuruFocus's estimate, a "Fairly Valued" label, so the rally has used up the model's discount. The next test is whether Cloud growth stays above capex growth in the fourth quarter.
