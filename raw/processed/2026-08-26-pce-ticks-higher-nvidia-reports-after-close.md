# PCE Ticks Higher in July, NVIDIA Reports After the Close

## Key Takeaways
- Q2 GDP remained at 1.5% on its first revision
- PCE inflation reached 3.7% in July, 3.3% on core
- Durable goods orders doubled expectations to 1.1%
- NVIDIA reports Q2 earnings after the close

Pre-market futures were mixed following a truckload of economic data, including a first update on Q2 GDP. GDP stayed at a still-low +1.5%, while year-over-year consumption rose 20bps to +3.4% and the price index grew +20bps to +6.4%, the highest read on that metric in four years.

Personal Income for July doubled expectations to +0.4% month over month; Consumer Spending also doubled expectations to +0.2%, slightly cooler than June's +0.3%. Real Spending (inflation-adjusted) came in flat at 0.0%. The PCE Price Index reached +0.2% versus expectations of +0.1%, following a negative -0.1% for June; year-over-year matched +3.7%. Core PCE was +0.2% for the month and +3.3% for the year, in line with expectations.

Durable Goods Orders more than doubled expectations — +1.1% versus +0.5% — the second-strongest month of the year so far after April's +8.5%. Ex-Transportation this figure was +0.4%. Non-defense, ex-aircraft orders (a proxy for "normal" business spending) pulled back to +0.2%, though June's revision grew to +1.7%.

## What to Expect from Today's Stock Market

Focus is on NVIDIA, which reports Q2 earnings after today's closing bell. Expectations are for another terrific quarter: +99% earnings growth, +96.5% on revenues, against steep growth comps since the AI trade took off at the start of 2024. The stock is up +14% year to date (+850% over five years), but questions remain about "circular financing" — NVIDIA lending money to AI companies to purchase its own chips. NVIDIA gave $30 billion to OpenAI in February with no milestones for chip deployment; OpenAI then developed its own Jalapeno chip with NVIDIA competitor Broadcom, which is more or less a direct competitor to NVIDIA's Blackwell processors. Whether CEO Jensen Huang can lay out a long-term strategic vision today, given these developments, is a key question for the market.
