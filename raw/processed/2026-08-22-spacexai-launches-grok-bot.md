# SpaceXAI launches Grok Bot, an always-on AI agent

Elon Musk's SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) has introduced Grok Bot, a team of always-on AI agents that can accomplish tasks in the background while you work on other stuff.

The feature is called "Grok Bot" but is described in the plural — it should resemble a "highly capable teammate" to which you can give tasks simply by chatting with it. You can run several bots in parallel and even have one manage a team of bots, which can independently message each other as they work to accomplish a task. Bots can also be placed in a group chat where they can work and communicate together as a team of actual humans would. "They pass work, assign ownership, and only pull you in for judgment calls," says SpaceXAI.

SpaceXAI provides examples of how its own teams use Grok Bot: one bot sends invites and follow-ups to collect NDA signatures for beta access to a feature; another finds open invoices across portals and email, sends follow-ups in the same voice, logs replies, and only escalates the ones that are stuck or disputed.

Grok Bot is in beta and is now available to SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium subscribers on desktop and iOS. Enterprise users can join a waitlist for future access.

SpaceXAI's Grok Bot follows in the footsteps of other companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic, which offer similar, persistent AI agents that can do work in the background and as a team.
