# Is Wall Street Bullish or Bearish on Alphabet Stock?

With a market cap of $3.4 trillion, Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) is a global technology conglomerate operating through Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets segments across regions including the U.S., Europe, Asia-Pacific, and more. Evolving from a search engine leader with over 94% market share, Alphabet now spans cloud computing, video and music streaming, autonomous vehicles via Waymo, and healthcare innovations through Verily.

Shares of the Mountain View, California-based company have underperformed the broader market over the past 52 weeks. GOOGL stock has fallen marginally over this time frame, while the broader S&P 500 Index ($SPX) has rallied 11.1%. Moreover, shares of the company are down 10.6% on a YTD basis, compared to SPX's 10.7% rise.

Focusing more closely, shares of the internet search leader have shown a less pronounced decline than the State Street Communication Services Select Sector SPDR ETF's (XLC) 12.6% drop over the past 52 weeks.

Alphabet shares tumbled 12.6% following its Q3 2026 results on Oct. 28, due to weaker-than-expected revenue of $127.6 billion, adjusted EPS of $2.67, and advertising revenue of $82.01 billion. Investor concerns deepened as search advertising growth slowed amid increased use of AI Mode, negative free cash flow reached $9.07 billion, compared with expectations of near break-even, and net income fell 25% to $26.17 billion due to heavy AI and data center spending.

Despite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling to $30.71 billion and its backlog reaching $561 billion, investors were unsettled by plans to increase capital expenditure beyond the already massive $195 billion - $205 billion forecast for 2026, raising fears of further cash burn and margin pressure.

For the fiscal year ending in December 2026, analysts expect GOOGL's EPS to grow 85.5% year-over-year to $20.05. The company's earnings surprise history is promising. It beat the consensus estimates in three of the last four quarters while missing on another occasion.

Among the 53 analysts covering the stock, the consensus rating is a "Strong Buy." That's based on 45 "Strong Buy" ratings, three "Moderate Buys," and five "Holds."

This configuration is slightly less bullish than three months ago, with 46 analysts suggesting a "Strong Buy."

On Oct 29, Barclays analyst Ross Sandler cut its price target on Alphabet to $405 while maintaining an "Overweight" rating.

The mean price target of $406.04 represents a 45.1% premium to GOOGL's current price levels. The Street-high price target of $515 suggests a 84% potential upside.
