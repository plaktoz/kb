# OpenAI narrows Anthropic's business lead as Ramp data shows faster Q3 growth

New data from corporate-spending platform Ramp shows OpenAI closing the gap with Anthropic among U.S. businesses, even as Anthropic retains the lead in the latest monthly reading. Anthropic was used by 43.5% of businesses in Ramp's July sample (up 1.1 points from June); OpenAI reached 39.7% (up 0.23 points). Ramp economist Ara Kharazian said OpenAI's growth rate was higher early in Q3.

Anthropic first moved ahead of OpenAI in Ramp's data in spring 2026: 34.4% vs 32.3% in April, widening to 41% vs 39% by May. The July figures put Anthropic 3.8 points ahead on customer adoption, even as OpenAI grew faster quarter-to-date. The percentages aren't mutually exclusive — many companies pay for both.

Ramp's AI Index is built from aggregated, anonymized transactions on Ramp cards and Bill Pay; its sample covers 70,000+ American businesses, skewed somewhat more tech-oriented than the general market.

OpenAI's July release of the GPT-5.6 family (flagship "Sol," with "Terra" and "Luna" at lower tiers, later discounted 80% and 20% respectively) is a likely factor in its Q3 momentum. Kharazian also cited weaker adoption of Anthropic's higher-end "Fable 5" model, tied to its price and a 30-day customer-data retention requirement for safety monitoring — data that Anthropic says is not used for training and is deleted after 30 days in nearly all cases. Fable 5 accounted for 6% of tokens and 11.4% of dollars spent on Anthropic models in its first month in Ramp's token-spend sample.

Overall business AI adoption keeps rising: the share of surveyed businesses paying for any AI product grew from just over 50% in March to nearly 56% in July, meaning both OpenAI and Anthropic can add customers while competing for relative share.
