# Jensen Huang explains why Nvidia will grow an astounding 70% next year

At the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology conference, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reiterated his confidence that the company can achieve roughly 70% year-over-year revenue growth, which — based on analyst expectations of ~$400B for the current fiscal year — would put next year's revenue near $680 billion.

Huang pushed back on the perception that Nvidia merely makes chips, noting that a single modern GPU system costs "$8.5 million dollars" and involves millions of components. One product line — combining Grace CPUs with Blackwell GPUs — is seeing 27% month-over-month sales growth.

His confidence stems from Nvidia's deep integration across the AI ecosystem: "We are a foundational platform of the AI ecosystem," he said, noting they run models for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and open-weight providers alike.

Huang also described tracking global data center development: "We're tracking every single gigawatt of land, power, shell around the world."

On questions about circular investing — where Nvidia invests in companies that buy its products — he offered a candid quip: "We put a little bit of money in, and a lot of money comes back." He insisted investments are only made after verifying real customer contracts, claiming he's reviewed $100 billion worth.

Long-term dominance isn't guaranteed, as AI infrastructure efficiency will likely improve over time.
