# Nvidia closes in on Hugging Face acquisition

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $12.9 billion, per The Information. Business Insider noted talks had not yet produced a signed agreement and could still fall apart.

Neither company responded to TechCrunch's request for comment.

Nvidia's key motivations include protecting its chip market dominance as major AI labs (OpenAI, Google, Amazon, Anthropic) develop their own chips, gaining a foothold in open-source AI to maintain hardware demand, re-entering cloud computing after scaling back its DGX Cloud business, and offloading unused computing capacity from existing cloud commitments to Hugging Face's developer base.

Hugging Face was last valued at $4.5 billion in a 2023 funding round. It previously declined a Nvidia investment offer that would have valued it at $7 billion. The company was generating roughly $150 million annually in revenue, described as "close to profitability." Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue has been publicly aligned with Nvidia's open-source advocacy.

The deal would represent nearly a 3x premium over Hugging Face's last known valuation.
