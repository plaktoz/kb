# Why Arm Stock Popped Today

Arm Holdings (ARM) surged ~8.6% after CEO Rene Haas expressed confidence in hitting $2 billion in revenue from its new AGI CPU data center chip — up from prior guidance of $1 billion, which had been constrained by supply shortages.

In a CNBC interview, Haas indicated supply constraints were easing, saying: "We're feeling good about it. We're feeling really good about it."

The AGI CPU represents Arm's first fully developed chip — a departure from its traditional architecture-licensing model. The AI CPU market is projected to grow roughly fivefold to $170 billion by 2030, per Bank of America, making successful execution on this chip a significant milestone for the company.
