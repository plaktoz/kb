# Nvidia Is Weighing a $10 Billion Stake in Anthropic's IPO. It Would Be Buying Its Own Demand.

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to serve as an anchor investor in Anthropic's planned IPO, potentially committing up to $10 billion — matching a prior investment made in November 2025, when Nvidia pledged up to $10 billion tied to Anthropic purchasing Nvidia-powered computing capacity.

At a reported ~$2 trillion valuation, $10 billion would represent roughly "half of 1% of the company" — a tenth of the anticipated $100 billion raise, but a tiny ownership slice.

The author argues Nvidia would essentially be "buying its own demand," since IPO proceeds could flow back to Nvidia through compute purchases. However, Anthropic has diversified its infrastructure, securing up to 5 gigawatts each from Amazon and Google's TPUs, while also developing its own chips.

Nvidia's private equity stakes have grown dramatically — from $3.8 billion a year prior to $47.9 billion as of late July 2026. The author notes this creates a dual risk: if AI spending slows, Nvidia faces both reduced orders and potential investment markdowns simultaneously.

Trading near $225 at ~14x fiscal 2028 earnings, the author views shares as fairly priced but advises watching the expanding investment portfolio.
