# 3 Great Quality Stocks To Own In September 2026

The article identifies three quality stocks suited for environments where borrowing costs may remain elevated, filtering for strong balance sheets, high returns on equity, and resilient business performance.

## The Three Stocks

### Microsoft (MSFT) — Market Cap: ~$3.8T

Azure, Microsoft 365, and Dynamics 365 drive high-margin recurring revenue. Revenue segments include Intelligent Cloud (~$138B), Productivity/Business Processes (~$140B), and Personal Computing (~$54B). The article notes heavy AI/data center investment is manageable given strong free cash flow, though regulatory risk and capex scale remain concerns.

### Micron Technology (MU) — Market Cap: ~$1.1T

Highlighted for a 50.1% ROE and 55.9% net margin, driven by HBM, DRAM, and SSD products critical to AI workloads. Revenue leaders include Cloud Memory (~$31.3B) and Mobile/Client (~$27.2B). Long-term customer agreements aim to reduce cyclical volatility, though Chinese competition and peak-cycle risk are flagged.

### Palantir Technologies (PLTR) — Market Cap: ~$438.6B

Reports ~31% ROE and ~49% net margins. Government revenue is ~$3.2B; commercial ~$2.9B. The balance sheet carries no debt. The article cautions that its premium valuation leaves little room for growth disappointments.
