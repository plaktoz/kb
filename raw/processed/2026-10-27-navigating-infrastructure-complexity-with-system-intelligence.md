# Navigating infrastructure complexity with system intelligence

Announcing IBM Z Atlas, the industry-first Z Software offering that is intended to address the need for a total Z system intelligence and change orchestration layer across the z/OS environment.

IBM Z environments are more interconnected than ever. Applications, middleware, infrastructure, security controls, and operational processes all depend on one another, making even routine changes difficult to assess. As organizations accelerate AI adoption, application modernization, hybrid cloud integration, and regulatory transformation, understanding how the entire IBM Z environment is interconnected has become increasingly important. Many mainframe teams still rely on manual analysis, fragmented tooling, and institutional knowledge to understand dependencies and evaluate risk across teams, creating a manual process that is slow and ineffective.

As experienced professionals retire and modernization initiatives accelerate, the siloed knowledge problem is magnified with mainframe teams having trouble measuring the full impact of their changes before they reach production.

Today, most Z teams can see pieces of their environment, but the next evolution of IBM Z management requires an end-to-end view of how the entire stack works together and turning that data into actionable intelligence. Without a unified view spanning infrastructure, operations, and applications, Z teams are forced to manually assemble context from multiple tools and sources. Organizations looking to modernize their mission-critical systems, while maintaining the reliability of IBM Z, need a faster, simpler, and more intelligent approach to understanding and validating their environment without inviting the possibility of system downtime.

## The only total system intelligence and change orchestration layer for the Z stack

Today, we’re announcing IBM Z Atlas, the industry-first Z Software offering that is intended to address the need for a total Z system intelligence and change orchestration layer across the z/OS environment.

IBM Z Atlas is designed to create a unified understanding of the IBM Z environment by continuously discovering relationships across infrastructure, software, middleware, applications, and operational processes. This system intelligence is visualized into a dynamic topology that gives teams a common view of their Z environment and dependencies. IBM Z Atlas uses that context to reduce the manual effort of tasks like patch management, regulatory response, and sharing system knowledge across every team, from systems programmers to application developers.

IBM Z Atlas eliminates the leading cause of unplanned z/OS outages — changes that aren’t proven to be safe — by mapping how a change impacts the z/OS stack by testing and validating it. This design accelerates clients’ modernization efforts by keeping experts in control and giving every practitioner, in operations or development, the same shared Z system visibility to reduce risk.

As IBM Z environments continue to evolve, teams need more than visibility into individual domains. They need the tools and institutional knowledge to change quickly when the business demands it. That’s why we’re excited to introduce IBM Z Atlas, so mainframe teams can modernize with confidence and speed.

IBM Z Atlas’ system intelligence is built on a foundation of automated discovery, topology modeling, dependency analysis, natural-language interactions, and AI-powered workflows to create impact assessments and change evidence. Together, these capabilities are intended to create a continuously updated understanding of the IBM Z environment and the relationships between its components.

For example, z/OS system programmers can use this solution to evaluate how a middleware upgrade might affect dependent applications, identify related infrastructure and operational processes that may require validation, determine which systems should be included in a test plan, or coordinate validation activities across application, infrastructure, and operations teams.

Whether planning a software update, validating a modernization initiative, preparing change artifacts for an audit, responding to regulatory requirements, or analyzing environment drift, IBM Z Atlas intends to provide the trusted context Z clients need so they can make informed decisions faster while reducing by risk and keeping humans in control.

While change orchestration is an important first focus, the broader vision for IBM Z Atlas is to establish the shared intelligence layer that will enable Z teams to understand, manage, govern, and optimize their IBM Z environments through natural-language experiences and AI-assisted workflows.

## Accelerating modernization efforts from weeks to minutes

IBM Z Atlas goes beyond individual applications and specific domains. It elevates the broader IBM Z Software portfolio through shared Z system intelligence that sits across development and operational workflows with a common understanding of the environment and the changes being made within it.

This solution is intended to bolster organizations by providing unified system intelligence across multiple domains to help teams ship faster and with less risk. The early results include:

- Reducing system context gathering time by 60-80%
- Decreasing detailed impact analysis and comprehensive test planning activities from hours to minutes
- Accelerating provisioning and test generation from weeks to hours
- Generating change evidence that reduces manual tracking effort by hours.

These efficiencies can help Z teams spend less time collecting information and coordinating activities so they can focus on modernizing and innovating their platforms.
