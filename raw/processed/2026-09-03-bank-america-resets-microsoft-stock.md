# Bank of America Resets Microsoft Stock Price Target for 2026

**Author:** Moz Farooque (TheStreet, republished via Yahoo Finance)
**Date:** September 3, 2026

---

Microsoft's 2026 performance has been mixed — shares gained roughly 3.6% year-to-date and ~13% over three months, masking a painful spring selloff followed by an August recovery.

## BofA's New Target

Bank of America analyst Tal Liani raised his MSFT price target to **$600** (from $500), maintaining a Buy rating. Against a September 1 reference price of $507.29, that implies ~18.3% upside. The key change: BofA now applies a **28x multiple** on forecasted 2027 earnings, up from 24x, reflecting reduced execution risk around Microsoft's AI investments.

## Why the Upgrade

- Azure accelerated from 39% growth (Q3) to 43% (Q4), with management guiding **45% for Q1 2027**
- Microsoft 365 Copilot surpassed **30 million paid seats**, with quarterly net additions more than doubling
- Remaining performance obligations surged 84% to **$678 billion**
- BofA models Azure constant-currency growth rising to 41.8% in fiscal 2027

## AI Stack Advantage

Microsoft's Copilot functions as an orchestration layer — routing tasks across models based on cost, speed, and quality. Proprietary models like MAI-Code-1-Flash reportedly match GPT-5.6 on Excel tasks "at a lower cost," while MAI-Cyber-1-Flash could handle "up to 90% of tasks" in Microsoft's security operations at half the cost of leading models.

## Risks Noted

BofA forecasts fiscal 2027 EPS growth of only 8.9%, with CapEx hitting $205.6 billion and free cash flow dropping ~51.7% to $32.4 billion. The $600 target depends on Azure hitting its 45% guidance, continued Copilot adoption, and eventual FCF recovery.

## Peer Comparison

BofA's $600 target matches Morgan Stanley and Citi, but trails JPMorgan ($625), Goldman Sachs ($655), Bernstein ($660), and Wells Fargo ($700). The Wall Street consensus sits at ~$568.
