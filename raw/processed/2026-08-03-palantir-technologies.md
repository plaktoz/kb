# Palantir Technologies

Palantir Technologies Inc. is an American publicly traded data integration and analytics software company, headquartered in Miami, Florida. Founded May 6, 2003, by Peter Thiel, Stephen Cohen, Joe Lonsdale, Alex Karp, and Nathan Gettings.

**Ticker:** NASDAQ: PLTR | **CEO:** Alex Karp | **Chairman:** Peter Thiel

**2025 Financials:**
- Revenue: $4.48B
- Net income: $1.63B
- Employees: 4,429

## History Highlights

- **2003–2004:** Thiel bankrolled early development; CIA-affiliated In-Q-Tel invested ~$2M
- **2010:** VP Biden credited Palantir for stimulus fraud detection
- **2013:** Valued at $9B; served 12+ U.S. government groups including CIA, NSA, FBI
- **2020:** Went public via direct listing on NYSE (September 30)
- **2023:** First profitable GAAP quarter ($31M net income)
- **2024:** Added to S&P 500; moved listing to Nasdaq
- **2025:** Market cap exceeded $400B; *The Economist* called it possibly "the most over-valued firm of all time"

## Core Products

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| **Gotham** | Defense/intelligence platform (released 2008); used by U.S. intelligence community, military, some police |
| **Foundry** | Commercial/civil government platform; used in healthcare, NHS, COVID response |
| **Apollo** | CI/CD delivery platform across all environments |
| **AIP** | Launched April 2023; integrates large language models into private networks |
| **TITAN** | Mobile AI command vehicle for military field intelligence |
| **ELITE** | ICE deportation targeting tool drawing on HHS/Medicaid data |
| **MetaConstellation** | AI-enabled satellite network |
| **Skykit** | Portable intelligence toolkit including ruggedized laptop and quadcopter |

## Key Customers

**U.S. Government:** CIA, NSA, FBI, DHS, DOD, ICE, CDC, Marine Corps, Air Force, SOCOM, FDA, NIH

**International:** NATO, Ukrainian military, NHS England, UK Ministry of Defence, Danish police, Norwegian Customs, Europol, IDF (Israel)

**Corporate:** Morgan Stanley, Airbus, Merck KGaA, Ferrari, PG&E, Fiat Chrysler

## Ukraine

Palantir provides Ukraine with battlefield intelligence tools. Gotham processes drone video, satellite imagery, and civilian tips to shorten the "kill chain." Other platforms used include AIP, Foundry, MetaConstellation, and a Maven variant. Zelensky described the relationship as "mutually beneficial," though analysts note strategic dependency concerns.

## Controversies

- **Predictive policing:** Civil liberties groups criticize AI-based crime prediction tools; Karp disputes the "predictive policing" label
- **ICE partnership (since 2014):** Built Investigative Case Management system and later ELITE for deportation targeting
- **NHS data contracts:** £330M Federated Data Platform contract drew protests over privacy and IDF ties
- **HHS Protect Now (2020):** Privacy concerns over COVID-era data aggregation
- **Second Trump administration:** ~$908M in contracts as of May 2025 across DHS, Pentagon, and DOD
- **Racial discrimination lawsuit:** Filed 2016
- **AI targeting tools:** Used in Israel/Gaza conflict; prompted employee departures and protests

## Corporate Structure

- **Voting:** Multi-class share structure preserving founder control
- **Headquarters history:** Palo Alto → Denver (2020) → Miami (announced February 2026)
- The company was unprofitable for approximately 20 years before achieving GAAP profitability in Q1 2023
