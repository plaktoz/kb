# States Have Passed 14 New Laws This Year Regulating the Use of AI in Healthcare

source_url: https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/state-lawmakers-have-passed-15-new-laws-regulating-the-use-of-ai-in-health-care

---

Published: July 27, 2026 — Transparency Coalition

State legislators have heard concerns from both patients and medical professionals about the inappropriate use of AI tools, and so far in 2026 they've responded with more than a dozen new laws adopted in 11 different states. The TCAI Mid-Year Report documents 84 new AI laws enacted in 27 states so far in 2026.

## Limiting AI Use in Medical Authorizations

Seven states limited the use of AI in medical authorization decisions made by healthcare insurance companies. Key legislation includes:

- **Alabama SB 63**: Insurers must disclose AI use; AI-determined decisions must be based on patient's medical history. Denials must be made by a licensed physician. Enacted April 17, 2026.
- **Colorado HB 1139**: Requires medical authorization decisions to be based on patient's individual clinical circumstances, not solely on group data. Denial must be reviewed by a licensed clinician. Enacted June 2, 2026.
- **Georgia SB 444**: Prohibits insurance coverage decisions from being based solely on AI systems or software tools. Enacted May 5, 2026.
- **Illinois SB 3114**: Prohibits AI from bypassing evaluation of all billing information; all downcoding determinations must be made or reviewed by a natural person.
- **Washington SB 5395**: AI shall not be the sole means used to deny, delay, or modify health care services. Enacted March 23, 2026.

## Therapy Chatbot Bans

Five states banned the commercial offering of AI chatbot therapy services, following Illinois and Nevada's 2025 laws:

- **Colorado HB 1195**: Prohibits AI therapy chatbots and limits AI use by licensed mental health professionals. Enacted June 3, 2026.
- **Maine LD 2082**: Prohibits AI therapy; violation is an unfair trade practice. Enacted April 13, 2026.
- **Tennessee SB 1580**: Prohibits AI systems from advertising as qualified mental health professionals. Enacted April 1, 2026.
- **Vermont H 816**: Ensures mental health services are delivered by professionals, not independently by AI systems. Enacted June 17, 2026.

## Patient Consent and Scope of Practice

- **Iowa HB 475**: Licensed healthcare professionals must verbally disclose AI recording use before any appointment is transcribed by AI.
- **Utah SB 150**: Specifies that AI does not qualify as an "innovation" within a medical clinic's scope of practice.
