# IBM Stock Gains as z17 Enters an AI Classroom

IBM installed a z17 mainframe at Marist University's new Innovation Incubator, continuing a partnership spanning over five decades. Shares edged up roughly 0.4% to $227.97 Friday morning, though the stock remained about 6% below its GF Value estimate.

Students and faculty will use the system to investigate AI marketing agents, portfolio allocation, and polling methods — all proposed research with no disclosed budgets or revenue targets.

The author cautions investors: campus hardware placement isn't evidence of new commercial sales. IBM gains potential future advocates, but as the piece notes, "the case grows stronger when research turns into software, customers or measurable demand."
