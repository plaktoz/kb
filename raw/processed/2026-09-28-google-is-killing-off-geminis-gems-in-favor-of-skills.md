# Google is killing off Gemini's Gems in favor of 'skills'

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/google-is-killing-off-geminis-gems-in-favor-of-skills/

---

By Sarah Perez | September 28, 2026

Google is retiring Gems, the Gemini feature that let people build custom AI assistants for specific tasks, as competitors like Meta roll out their own all-in-one AI agents. Users' existing work will carry over, since Gems will be automatically converted into "skills" usable across various AI tasks.

According to notifications in the Gemini app, "Gems will become skills starting on November 17, 2026," and the company says it will handle the migration so users don't need to take any action. Gems will continue functioning normally until that date.

Gems debuted in 2024 as a way to let "users teach their AI to perform certain tasks" without repeating instructions each time. Google's own premade examples included a learning coach, brainstorming assistant, career guide, coding partner, and editor, while users could also build personalized versions like a running coach or vacation planner. People could share their custom Gems with others too, a feature Google hoped would boost the app's popularity.

The piece notes this shutdown reflects a broader pattern of Google frequently rebranding and reorganizing AI features rather than keeping them stable — something the author says has been "a failing point of Google's strategy long before the AI era," citing the company's past habit of running multiple overlapping messaging apps simultaneously.

Even in their new "skills" format, the article argues these tools remain less approachable than simply chatting with something like Meta's Muse, since users must type a forward slash in a task thread to select a skill — an interface the author describes as appealing more to engineers than everyday users.

The report notes that 9to5Google first covered the Gems phase-out over the preceding weekend.
