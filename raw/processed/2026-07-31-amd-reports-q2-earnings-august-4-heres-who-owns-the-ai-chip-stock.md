# AMD Reports Q2 Earnings August 4 — Here's Who Owns the AI Chip Stock

Story Highlights:
- AMD will announce its second-quarter results on August 4.
- Let's explore AMD's shareholder structure through TipRanks' ownership tool.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) is set to report its Q2 2026 earnings on August 4. The stock has surged more than 120% year-to-date, driven by major customer wins and rapid growth in its data center business. However, AI stocks have turned volatile in recent sessions amid concerns over higher AI spending. In this article, we break down who owns AMD stock ahead of its Q2 earnings report.

For context, AMD designs high-performance processors, graphics cards, and AI chips used in personal computers, data centers, gaming consoles, and cloud computing systems. Overall, Wall Street expects AMD to report earnings per share (EPS) of $1.62 for Q2, reflecting more than 235% year-over-year growth. Meanwhile, revenue is projected to rise 47% to $11.3 billion.

## Who Owns AMD Stock?

According to TipRanks' Ownership Tool, public companies and individual investors own 52.01% of AMD. They are followed by ETFs, mutual funds, other institutional investors, and insiders at 28.63%, 16.77%, 2.37%, and 0.22%, respectively.

Overall, AMD's ownership structure is heavily dominated by public and individual investors, reflecting the stock's popularity among both retail investors. Meanwhile, insiders own just 0.22% of AMD shares, indicating that company executives and directors have a relatively small direct ownership stake.

## Breaking Down AMD's Ownership Structure

Looking closely at the top shareholders, Vanguard holds the largest stake in AMD at 8.56%. Next up is Vanguard Index Funds, which holds a 6.90% stake in the company.

Among the top ETF holders, the Vanguard Total Stock Market ETF (VTI) owns a 3.18% stake in Advanced Micro Devices stock, followed by the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO), with a 2.60% stake.

Moving to mutual funds, Vanguard Index Funds holds about 6.90% of AMD. Meanwhile, Fidelity Concord Street Trust owns 1.76% of the company.

## Is AMD a Buy or Sell Now?

According to TipRanks, AMD stock has a Strong Buy consensus rating based on 28 Buys and seven Holds. The average AMD stock price target of $582.65 indicates an upside of 20.04% from current levels.
