# Is the AI Industry Really Ready to Slow Down?

The article covers a TechCrunch Equity podcast debate on whether AI executives genuinely intend to slow development. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a "pace the frontier" plan, while Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang aligned with President Trump in dismissing AI safety concerns as unnecessary.

Podcast hosts Anthony Ha, Sean O'Kane, and Kirsten Korosec were skeptical. O'Kane noted that despite apparent industry consensus around Amodei's proposal, it was "still missing a lot of specifics" on both claimed dangers and what slowing down actually means.

Amodei's plan involves three broad ideas: independent third-party safety evaluators embedded at AI companies, coordination on safety standards among major AI firms in democratic nations, and international coordination.

Korosec flagged that industry consensus could signal "the beginnings of forming a cartel." She also noted Jensen Huang's pushback felt "a little staged" and that Nvidia clearly benefits from unimpeded AI progress.

O'Kane argued free-market accountability is weakened because the current federal government shows little appetite for enforcement, enterprise clients won't switch providers over ethical disagreements, and companies are insulated by massive investment capital — making consumer pressure largely ineffective.
