# A Group Funded by Andreessen, Horowitz, and Brockman Plans Data Center Ads to Sway Midterms

A new pro-AI advocacy group called **Build American AI** — affiliated with the super PAC *Leading the Future* (LTF) — plans to run millions of dollars in ads targeting voters in Kansas, Ohio, and Wisconsin, promoting the benefits of data centers ahead of the midterm elections.

LTF is heavily funded by venture capitalists Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, as well as OpenAI President Greg Brockman. The super PAC launched with over $50 million and has directed funds toward pro-AI candidates while working to oppose AI skeptics.

OpenAI publicly distanced itself from LTF, stating it does not direct the group's activities and condemning tactics like "astroturfing" — describing it as making organized campaigns appear to be grassroots efforts.

Andreessen Horowitz has emerged as the largest disclosed donor to the midterm elections, with over **$115 million** in federal contributions. The firm is also heavily backing pro-crypto efforts through the Fairshake super PAC.

Separately, Anthropic is a known supporter of Public First Action, a group that backs AI regulation.
