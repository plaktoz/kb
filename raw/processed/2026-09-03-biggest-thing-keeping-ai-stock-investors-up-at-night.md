# Here's the biggest thing keeping AI stock investors up at night

**Author:** Brian Sozzi, Executive Editor
**Date:** September 2, 2026

The central concern among AI stock investors is whether demand has peaked. Deutsche Bank analyst Melissa Weathers noted "a notable discrepancy between management teams' outlooks for fundamentals and investors' outlooks for share prices."

## Conference Signals

Tech executives at a recent Deutsche Bank conference — including those from Intel and Sandisk — signaled "steady and strong trends," while investor sentiment had soured due to "peak cycle" fears around DRAM pricing and memory wall challenges.

## Countering the Pessimism

Recent data counters that pessimism:

- Dell reported fiscal Q2 sales surging 58% year-over-year to a record $47 billion, with EPS up 273%, driven by enterprise and hyperscaler AI server demand. FY2027 guidance came in roughly $25 billion above estimates.
- Nvidia projected ~70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028, well above the ~45% analysts expected, with potential for over 100% growth absent memory shortages.
- PWC projected global data center spending could hit $31.6 trillion through 2050, possibly reaching $50 trillion if the US AI buildout surpasses prior infrastructure cycles.

## Key Concern

The overarching fear: are we at peak AI infrastructure investment, or is the current slowdown in stock prices just a disconnect between cautious investors and fundamentally optimistic executives?
