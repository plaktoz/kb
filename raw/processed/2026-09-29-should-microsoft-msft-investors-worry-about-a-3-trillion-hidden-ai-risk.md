# Should Microsoft (MSFT) Investors Worry About A $3 Trillion Hidden AI Risk?

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/microsoft-msft-investors-worry-3-000824730.html

---

By Bailey Pemberton (Simply Wall St). Published Tuesday, September 29, 2026.

The piece reports that Michael Burry flagged roughly $3 trillion in obscured investment exposure tied to Microsoft and other AI companies. According to the article, Burry's concern centers on how massive AI data center projects get financed — namely, that they rely on "off balance sheet structures that do not appear as conventional debt." He reportedly warned that if AI demand comes in softer than anticipated, this could lead to major writedowns and strain on tech firms and the lenders behind them.

For Microsoft specifically, the article explains that Burry's warning applies to "AI infrastructure commitments, long term energy deals and capacity contracts" — obligations that may not be reflected as traditional liabilities. The piece frames this not as a brand-new worry but as reinforcement of an existing risk already noted in Microsoft's investment narrative, which flags "heavy AI and cloud investment" as a concern if spending remains elevated while margins face pressure.

The article suggests the next meaningful indicator will be Microsoft's upcoming capital expenditure and Azure revenue disclosures under its revamped FY27 reporting format, which would show whether infrastructure spending is being matched by actual usage and cash flow. It also notes, as a broader consideration, that leadership decisions and executive compensation structures can shape long-term outcomes for the company, separate from balance sheet questions.

The article includes a standard disclaimer that Simply Wall St's commentary is general, historically and analyst-forecast based, not financial advice, and that the firm holds no position in the stocks discussed.
