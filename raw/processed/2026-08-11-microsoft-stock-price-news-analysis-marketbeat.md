# Microsoft (MSFT) Stock Price, News & Analysis

Microsoft scored higher than 96% of companies evaluated by MarketBeat, ranking 40th of 595 technology-sector stocks. Consensus rating is Moderate Buy (average score 2.89), based on 42 buy ratings, 5 hold ratings, no strong buy or sell ratings, from 30 research reports in the past 90 days. Consensus price target is $559.16, about 10.4% upside from the current price of $506.48.

Earnings are expected to grow 18.59% in the coming year, from $19.58 to $23.22 per share. Microsoft's P/E of 28.20 is cheaper than both the broader market average (~39.45) and the technology sector average (~75.02). Its PEG ratio is 1.61 (above 1 can indicate overvaluation) and its P/B ratio is 8.50 (above 3 can indicate overvaluation relative to assets).

Dividend yield is 0.73% (bottom 25% of dividend payers), but Microsoft has raised its dividend for 23 consecutive years, with a healthy 20.27% payout ratio (projected to fall to 15.68% next year), indicating strong sustainability. In the past three months, insiders sold $17.78 million in stock and bought none. Institutions hold 71.13% of shares, a signal of strong market trust; insiders hold just 0.03%.

Microsoft's stock has risen 4.6% year-to-date in 2026, from $483.62 to $505.65. Last quarterly earnings (reported Wednesday, July 29) beat expectations: $4.74 EPS versus a $4.24 consensus estimate, and revenue up 17.7% year-over-year. The company's board authorized a $60 billion share buyback program in September 2024, allowing repurchase of up to 1.9% of shares. Recent headlines flagged include reports that Microsoft could unveil a "Maia 300" AI chip as early as September, and commentary on the case for the stock to rally further after its recent recovery.
