# Should You Worry That IBM Has Gone Quiet On Its AI Total?

IBM previously publicized a running total of generative-AI business won — climbing from over $3 billion (October 2024) to over $7.5 billion (July 2025). That cumulative figure disappeared from the July 2026 earnings call, coinciding with a quarter where performance "fell short of expectations."

IBM now describes AI progress through proportional metrics: generative AI represented roughly half of Consulting signings and over 30% of its backlog in Q2 2026. Consulting is approximately $21.1B annually (~30% of a $69.1B top line), with signings growing 6% and revenue growing 1% (constant currency).

The company's narrative has shifted toward **watsonx Orchestrate** as its agent-building platform, alongside a recurring software base (~80% of software revenue) generating $24.6B in annual recurring revenue, up 8% year-over-year.

The key warning: watch whether Consulting revenue achieves the guided "low to mid-single-digit" constant-currency growth. IBM shares sat at $234.71, roughly 28% below their 52-week high, though up 24.5% over two years.
