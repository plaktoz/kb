# 15 management tools leaders rely on daily

Daily tasks and leadership demands can take on a life of their own. Without the right tools, the workday can slip by with critical items still left undone. Management tools and systems can help wrangle the many demands of leadership. Fast Company Executive Board members shared what they rely on daily:

1. **Own dashboards** — build custom reporting so leaders have direct visibility into the business without a reporting layer.
2. **AI meeting summaries** — AI-assisted summarization captures decisions and action items in real time, reducing time spent recapping.
3. **A written list** — get everything out of your head and onto a written list plus one trusted calendar-holder.
4. **ClickUp** — centralize every task, deadline, and dependency into one source of truth so teams look at the system, not each other, when something is missed.
5. **Claude AI** — used daily to think faster, brief better, and pressure-test ideas before they go to a team or clients.
6. **A simple notebook** — capture daily leadership noise to distinguish the urgent from the important.
7. **Prioritization framework** — a simple framework to separate urgent from important and align time to business outcomes.
8. **Teams and Copilot Studio** — consolidate communication and build AI-driven automations around day-to-day loose ends.
9. **Daily recap note** — a three-line end-of-day note from each direct report: what got done, what's stuck, what they need.
10. **Three daily questions** — top three priorities, who owns what, and what "done" looks like.
11. **Connection building** — understanding people's priorities and energy levels to sense where support is needed before issues escalate.
12. **Priority alignment** — staying disciplined about what not to pursue as AI increases the pace of incoming opportunities.
13. **Structured project management system** — calendar, task platform, and documented SOPs for consistency and accountability.
14. **Pick-one prioritization** — every morning, pick the one thing that would have the most impact if done today.
15. **The follow-up** — the habit of circling back on what you and your people said you'd do, more valuable than any dashboard or tool.
