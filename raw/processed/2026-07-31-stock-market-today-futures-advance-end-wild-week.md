# Stock Market Today: Futures Advance to End Wild Week After Indexes Bounce Back Sharply; Amazon Soars, Apple Sinks

source_url: https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-07312026-12031593

byline: Aaron Rennie, Aaron McDade, Polo Rocha

date: 2026-07-31

---

Stock futures pointed to a higher open at the end of a volatile week for major indexes. Tech shares led the charge, with Amazon soaring after the Magnificent Seven company reported better-than-expected results. Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones Industrial Average, and S&P 500 futures were up 1.1%, 0.5%, and 0.4%, respectively, in recent trading.

The prior day, the tech-focused Nasdaq Composite, benchmark S&P 500, and blue-chip Dow Jones Industrial Average rebounded sharply, closing up a respective 2.8%, 1.7%, and 1.2% higher, with the Dow adding 600 points. The Nasdaq, Dow, and S&P 500 entered Friday up a respective 0.6%, 0.5%, and 0.3% for the week, which included a sell-off Wednesday after the Federal Reserve elected to stand pat on interest rates despite lingering inflation concerns.

Indexes were boosted by an 11% premarket jump in shares of Amazon, whose results were aided by its cloud-computing business. However, shares of fellow Magnificent Seven tech giant Apple sank 7.5% after weaker-than-expected services revenue.

Microsoft shares surged 16% to pace the Dow the day before after results beat analysts' expectations, adding $450 billion to the company's market capitalization — a one-day record for any firm, surpassing Nvidia's $440 billion gain from last April. Shares of Meta Platforms pointed 1.5% higher after sinking 8% the prior day.

The Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) and broader iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) were up roughly 5% and 4%, respectively, before the bell after soaring 16% and 8.5% the day before. Memory stocks Sandisk, Micron Technology, Seagate Technology, and Western Digital pointed up 4.5% to 6% after jumping between 11% and 26% the prior session.

Oil prices were modestly higher as traders assessed the latest Middle East developments. US benchmark West Texas Intermediate futures rose 1.6% to near $85 a barrel, while global benchmark Brent crude futures advanced 1.5% to $90.30. The 10-year Treasury yield was above 4.68% in recent trading, up slightly from the prior close.

## Novo Nordisk shares sink on disappointing heart drug trial results

Novo Nordisk's US-listed shares sank 11% premarket after the Danish pharmaceutical firm said its heart drug ziltivekimab did not meet expectations in a Phase 3 trial, failing to translate target engagement and inflammation marker reductions into major adverse cardiovascular event risk reduction versus placebo. The outcome will not impact 2026 adjusted operating profit outlook but will result in a non-cash impairment charge in Q3 2026.

## Microsoft coming off record $450B rise in market cap

Microsoft shares moved slightly lower after a record-breaking prior session. After earnings topped estimates and capital expenditures came in lighter than expected, investors rewarded Microsoft with a 15.5% jump, adding $450 billion to its market cap — the largest single-day gain in market cap for any US company, surpassing Nvidia's 19% and $440 billion gain from last April.

## Bond market is testing Warsh's "noncommittal" rate outlook

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh says he's committed to bringing inflation down, but the bond market isn't buying it unless he shows a willingness to raise interest rates, analysts say. At his second press conference as Fed chair, Warsh said the central bank "remains resolute" and "will not waver" on its 2% inflation target, but did not raise short-term rates or signal upcoming hikes. Bank of America analysts called the press conference "all hat, no cattle," and bond markets are questioning the Fed's inflation-fighting resolve.
