# 3 Historical Reasons I'll Be Completely Avoiding Anthropic's Upcoming and Potentially Record-Breaking IPO

Anthropic, developer of the Claude large language model, has confidentially filed to go public and is targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation — potentially surpassing SpaceX's record-breaking $1.77 trillion IPO. The author outlines three reasons for skepticism:

## 1. Tech IPOs Often Stumble Early

Truist Financial analyzed the last 30 major tech IPOs since mid-2012, finding an "average year-one maximum drawdown of 55%." Anthropic's infrastructure costs will also pressure near-term profitability despite a small Q2 adjusted profit on over $11.5 billion in sales.

## 2. Valuation Looks Stretched

Three decades of data suggest companies can't sustain price-to-sales ratios above 30 for long. At a $2 trillion valuation, Anthropic would debut well above that threshold relative to its trailing sales.

## 3. Game-Changing Tech Historically Triggers Bubbles

Every major technology since the mid-1990s internet boom has gone through an "early innings bubble-bursting event," driven by investors overestimating adoption speed. The author warns AI is unlikely to be an exception.
