# Synopsys Announces Advancements to Agentic AI in Collaboration with NVIDIA

source_url: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/synopsys-announces-advancements-to-agentic-ai-in-collaboration-with-nvidia-thefly-news

---

Published: July 2026 — TipRanks / The Fly

At the 2026 DAC Chips to Systems Conference, Synopsys (SNPS) announced advancements to agentic AI for engineering in collaboration with NVIDIA (NVDA). Synopsys has developed fully autonomous, long-running agentic capabilities for chip design and electronics system design enabled with NVIDIA Nemotron on NVIDIA's accelerated computing platform, secured by the NVIDIA OpenShell runtime.

## Key Quote

"AI is fundamentally reshaping engineering, and Synopsys is at the forefront of this transformation, enabling fully autonomous agents across every stage of silicon and systems development," said Ravi Subramanian, Chief Product Management Officer at Synopsys.

## Significance

- **Fully autonomous chip design agents:** Long-running agentic AI can handle complex engineering workflows that previously required teams of specialized engineers.
- **NVIDIA Nemotron integration:** Domain-specific AI models purpose-built for engineering tasks enable more reliable agentic behavior than general-purpose LLMs.
- **IBM Quantum connection:** Separately, IBM and NVIDIA are also accelerating quantum investment — CXMT chipmaker's IPO debut (470% gain on Shanghai STAR Market) signals how AI-driven chip demand is reshaping the semiconductor landscape.
