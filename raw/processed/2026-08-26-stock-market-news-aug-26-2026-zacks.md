# Stock Market News for Aug 26, 2026

U.S. stocks ended higher on Tuesday as tech stocks rebounded following a sell-off earlier in the session, while a drop in bond yields brought some relief to investors. All three major indexes ended in positive territory.

## How Did the Benchmarks Perform?

The Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 0.3%, or 160.24 points, to close at 53,577.40 points, its third straight day of gains. The S&P 500 rose 0.3%, or 24.38 points, to finish at 7,677.24 points, with tech and communication services the biggest gainers. The Information Technology Select Sector SPDR (XLK) gained 0.9%; the Communication Services Select Sector SPDR (XLC) rose 0.8%. The tech-heavy Nasdaq jumped 0.7%, or 171.11 points, to 26,151.30. The VIX fell 2.52% to 15.45.

## Bond Yields Drop

Bond yields fell for a second straight day as oil prices eased, following a report that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent could use the department's nearly $1 trillion General Account to help finance bond buybacks. The 10-year Treasury yield slid more than 7 basis points to 4.625%. WTI crude fell over 3%.

## Chip Stocks Rally

Tuesday's rebound was primarily led by a chip rally ahead of NVIDIA's quarterly results, due Wednesday. NVIDIA rose 2.2%; Micron gained 2.5% and AMD gained 4.9%. Meta ended 2% higher and Netflix gained 2.8%. Investor sentiment remained low overall as the US-Canada trade war appeared to escalate, with Canada slapping retaliatory tariffs on American imports. Investors are also awaiting a speech from Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh at the Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium this week.

## Economic Data

The Conference Board's consumer confidence report fell to a seven-month low of 89.4 in August, down 0.8% from the prior month and below the consensus estimate of 90.2. The S&P Cotality Case-Shiller Index showed U.S. home prices rose 0.4% sequentially in June and 1.5% year over year.
