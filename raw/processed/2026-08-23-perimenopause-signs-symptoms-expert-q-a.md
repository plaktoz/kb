# Are You In Perimenopause? Signs, Symptoms, and What to Ask Your Doctor

Perimenopause affects roughly 2 million American women annually, typically beginning in the mid-40s and lasting 4–8 years. Despite this prevalence, widespread confusion persists — a recent survey found women correctly identified their menopause stage only about half the time.

**Common symptoms include:**
- Hot flashes and night sweats
- Sleep and mood disturbances
- Irregular periods
- Vaginal dryness
- Reduced sexual desire
- Joint aches

Dr. Stephanie Faubion (Mayo Clinic) cautions against attributing every midlife symptom to perimenopause, noting that symptoms like itchy skin or heart palpitations could signal unrelated — sometimes serious — conditions.

## Emotional Impact

Anxiety and irritability are the most common emotional symptoms. Women with prior PMS, postpartum depression, or mood disorders face elevated risk.

## What to Ask Your Doctor

If you suspect perimenopause, Faubion recommends asking your doctor directly: "Do you think I'm in perimenopause?" and describing your symptoms.

## Lifestyle Recommendations

- Monitor blood pressure, blood sugar, and cholesterol
- Begin or maintain exercise
- Adjust diet proactively, since weight gain occurs even without behavioral changes
- Stop smoking

Treatment varies by symptom — hormones, birth control, or antidepressants may all be appropriate depending on presentation.
