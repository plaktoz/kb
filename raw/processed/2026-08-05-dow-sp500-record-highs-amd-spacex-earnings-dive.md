# Stock Market Today: Dow, S&P 500 Build On Record Highs With Hopes Of Iran Deal; AMD, SpaceX Dive

The Dow Jones Industrial Average and other major stock indexes pushed higher Wednesday as President Donald Trump indicated a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz is imminent. Arista Networks (ANET) climbed after beating second-quarter expectations. But Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and Elon Musk-led Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX), known as SpaceX, dropped following their financial updates.

Just after Wednesday's open, the Dow rose 0.8%, aided by gains for Nvidia (NVDA) and Disney, with the entertainment giant beating on quarterly earnings views. The S&P 500 rose 0.6%. The tech-heavy Nasdaq composite climbed 0.4% in morning trading. The Dow and the S&P 500 finished Tuesday's session at record highs.

President Trump told reporters Wednesday there's been progress in talks about reopening the Strait of Hormuz and an agreement could be announced Wednesday or Thursday. Iran and Oman are discussing reopening prospects. West Texas Intermediate crude futures fell modestly to trade around $75.60 a barrel. The 10-year Treasury yield ticked lower to 4.61%. Bitcoin rose near $64,600.

Early Wednesday, ADP said private payrolls rose 44,000 in July, below estimates of 75,000 and down significantly from a 98,000 increase in June.

AMD stock plunged 8% even after the chipmaker edged above Wall Street's targets for the second quarter and with its sales outlook. AMD predicted total revenue of $13 billion for the current quarter, versus Wall Street's model of $12.56 billion for the third quarter.

SpaceX stock tumbled 12% after the company reported a loss of 9 cents per share, narrowing from 34 cents per share last year. Revenue spiked 92% to $7.8 billion. Analysts expected a loss of 23 cents per share on nearly $6.83 billion in revenue, according to FactSet. But capital spending was very high with rising costs for artificial intelligence and space development continuing to weigh on the balance sheet.

Arista shares soared more than 11% after the networking leader reported second-quarter earnings and revenue that handily beat Wall Street targets amid strong demand for AI cloud-computing networking gear. Management increased its 2026 revenue growth outlook to 40% from 28%.

Other earnings movers included Astera Labs, Amgen, Circle Internet, CVS Health, Eli Lilly, Match Group, SharkNinja, Shopify, and Uber Technologies. Astera Labs stock declined 1%, while Amgen shares rose modestly. Circle stock rallied 2%. Shares of CVS Health climbed more than 3%, as Eli Lilly stock jumped almost 5%. Match shares dived 10%. SharkNinja stock was up slightly but Shopify shares soared 28%. Uber stock skidded 3% in premarket trading.
