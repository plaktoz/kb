# Microsoft CFO Touts AI Stack, Azure Efficiency and Agent Growth

Microsoft CFO Amy Hood spoke at the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, outlining Microsoft's competitive positioning in AI and cloud computing.

## Enterprise AI Strategy

Hood described Microsoft as a full-stack platform company, emphasizing that "trust, security, sovereignty, the protection of IP" are foundational priorities. The platform supports over 11,000 AI models, spanning frontier, open-source, and specialized models.

## Azure Infrastructure

Microsoft cut "dock-to-live" hardware deployment times by more than 50% over one year, accelerating conversion of capital spending into revenue. The company has secured long-term supply chain agreements for complete equipment sets. Its Cobalt CPU is slated for roughly 25 data centers annually, with Maia chip benchmarks drawing positive internal response.

## Pricing and Monetization

Microsoft is blending user-based licenses (valued for budget predictability) with consumption-based pricing for measurable AI workloads. GitHub pricing changes demonstrated users will pay more for productivity-improving tools.

## Agentic Workflows

Hood indicated that Microsoft 365 is positioned as a secure environment where, as she stated, "agents and apps and humans will work together."
