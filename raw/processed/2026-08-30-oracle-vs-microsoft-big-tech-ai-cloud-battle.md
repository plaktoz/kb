# Oracle vs. Microsoft: Big Tech's AI Cloud Battle Has a New Underdog

The piece examines whether Microsoft or Oracle represents the better AI cloud investment, framing Oracle as an emerging challenger to Microsoft's established dominance.

## Microsoft's Position

- Fiscal 2026 cloud revenue exceeded $214B; Azure surpassed $100B
- Total revenue grew 18% YoY to $331.8B; adjusted earnings rose 22%
- Expanded to 88 data centers across five continents
- Offers access to 11,000+ AI models on Azure
- Q4 free cash flow: $19.6B; ended quarter with $76.8B in cash/equivalents
- Wall Street consensus: "Strong Buy" — mean target of $554.76 (~10% upside)

## Oracle's Position

- Cloud infrastructure revenue surged 93% YoY; total Q4 revenue up 21% to $19.2B
- Remaining performance obligations jumped 363% YoY to $638B
- Signed $67B in AI infrastructure contracts in Q4 alone
- GPU utilization running at 97.5%
- Capital expenditures hit $48B in fiscal 2026; ~$70B budgeted for next year
- Free cash flow turned negative; stock down 23% YTD
- Wall Street consensus: "Strong Buy" — mean target of $251.50 (~67% upside)

## Conclusion

Microsoft leads in scale, ecosystem, and financial stability. Oracle's rapid infrastructure growth and massive contracted backlog make it a compelling but riskier long-term play that still must "prove that it can turn the demand into the scale and profitability needed."
