# Alpha School to open new K-8 Campus in The Woodlands with innovative AI-powered education

The Woodlands®, a Howard Hughes® community, today announced that Alpha School will open a new K–8 campus for the 2026–2027 school year, bringing an innovative, AI-powered educational model to one of the most academically accomplished communities in Texas. The addition of Alpha School expands educational choice for families in The Woodlands and reinforces the community's longstanding commitment to high-quality academic programs.

"The Woodlands is one of the most dynamic, forward-thinking communities in Texas, and Alpha is proud to bring an innovative educational model that complements its strong academic foundation," said Rachel Goodlad, Head of Expansion for Alpha School. "We are excited to partner with Howard Hughes Communities and to serve families who value both academic excellence and real-world preparation."

Located at 2000 Woodlands Parkway, adjacent to The Woodlands Waterway, Alpha The Woodlands will initially serve students in kindergarten through eighth grade. The approximately 8,000-square-foot campus is designed to feature open workshop spaces and innovative classrooms that support personalized instruction, core academics, leadership development, and real-world life skills.

"Education has always been central to The Woodlands' success," said Jim Carman, President of the Texas Region for Howard Hughes Communities. "Our residents consistently prioritize academic excellence and innovation. Alpha School adds a forward-thinking option that complements our outstanding public and private schools while reinforcing The Woodlands as a place where families can thrive for generations."

Founded in 2014, Alpha School combines adaptive technology-driven academic instruction with immersive life skills workshops. Its model emphasizes mastery-based learning in core subjects alongside development of communication, critical thinking, financial literacy, and leadership skills.

The new campus further strengthens The Woodlands' reputation as a premier destination for families seeking access to top-tier schools, vibrant neighborhoods, and a high quality of life. As a master planned community designed for long-term growth, The Woodlands continues to attract institutions that support its evolving population and workforce.

Families interested in enrolling can apply at alpha.school/the-woodlands or via email at admissions.thewoodlands@alpha.school. Early applications are recommended, as space is limited.
