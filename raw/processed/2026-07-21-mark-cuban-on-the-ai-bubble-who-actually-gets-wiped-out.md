# Mark Cuban on the AI Bubble: Who Actually Gets Wiped Out?

**Publication:** All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg

## AI Bubble vs. Dot-Com

Cuban distinguishes today from the dot-com era — no retail frenzy, but warns that VCs and PE funds going "all in" face serious risk if things collapse.

## Data Center Overbuilding

He draws a fiber-optic parallel: just as "dark fiber" sat unused after bandwidth improvements, AI efficiency breakthroughs could leave data centers stranded. "There's going to be a lot of data centers that are going to be turned into pickleball courts."

## Enterprise AI Is Harder Than Expected

Cuban notes that while basic AI tasks are easy, enterprise deployment is genuinely difficult. CEOs largely don't understand what's happening, and even simple automation — like a recurring weekly report — remains clunky and requires programming knowledge.

## Entrepreneurial Opportunity

Despite limitations, Cuban is enthusiastic about tools like Lovable (an investment), citing 770,000 apps built per week. He argues AI lowers startup barriers dramatically — "12 minutes" for a patent, business plan, and bill of materials.

## Go Public Advice

Cuban urges portfolio companies to IPO so they have stock as acquisition currency: "Go public, motherfuckers."

## AI & Politics

He argues LLMs are truth-seeking by necessity — their credibility depends on accuracy — unlike social media algorithms optimized for engagement. He expects people to increasingly consult LLMs for political guidance.

## Health & AI

Cuban uses Open Evidence (a medical AI investment) for personal health decisions, and advocates blood testing every 3–6 months combined with wearables.

## Texas vs. California

Praises Texas's regulatory flexibility and lower cost of living; criticizes wealth tax proposals as economically naive and politically theatrical.

## NBA/Knicks

Celebrates the Knicks' championship, discusses league parity from the second apron rule, and predicts three-peats are effectively over.
