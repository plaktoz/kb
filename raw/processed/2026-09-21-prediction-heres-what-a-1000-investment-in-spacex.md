# Prediction: Here's What a $1,000 Investment in SpaceX Stock Could Be Worth in 2031

SpaceX (NASDAQ: SPCX) had a turbulent debut, surging to $225 after a $135 IPO before dropping over 50%, now trading around $150. Insider lock-up expirations loom, though Musk has pledged not to sell his stake.

Management projects reaching **$1 trillion in annual revenue by 2030**, up from roughly $20 billion recently — growth driven largely by its AI compute-rental business. SpaceX has signed deals with Anthropic, Alphabet, and others to rent out data center capacity. CFO Bret Johnsen noted "a less than one-year payback on our new capital deployments for compute" — an exceptional return rate compared to peers like Alphabet, whose average server carries a two-year payback.

Wall Street is more conservative, with analyst consensus projecting around **$530 billion in AI-related revenue by 2031**, potentially reaching $600–650 billion when Starlink and launch services are included.

Using CoreWeave and Oracle as comparables (both trade ~6x sales), a $600B revenue figure implies a **$3.6 trillion valuation** — about 77% above current levels. That translates a $1,000 investment into roughly **$1,770** by 2031, or ~12% annually.

The author cautions that significant uncertainty remains, particularly around orbital data centers and whether the AI compute supply shortage persists, and suggests better risk-adjusted opportunities exist elsewhere.
