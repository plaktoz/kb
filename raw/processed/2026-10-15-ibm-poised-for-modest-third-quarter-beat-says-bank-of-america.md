# IBM poised for modest third-quarter beat, says Bank of America

Bank of America expects International Business Machines Corp (NYSE:IBM), the technology and consulting group, to deliver a modest third-quarter earnings beat on October 21 while keeping its financial 2026 outlook unchanged.

The bank reiterated its 'buy' rating and $280 price target, citing IBM's shift towards higher-margin software, improving profitability and strong free cash flow.

Bank of America forecasts third-quarter revenue of $16.8 billion and earnings per share of $2.82, with the potential for a slightly stronger performance from favourable currency movements and profitability.

The bank expects investors to focus on progress in converting delayed deals, signs of a recovery in customer spending and improving growth in Transaction Processing software.

Software growth remains below ideal levels, although Bank of America expects strength in Red Hat (NYSE:RHT)'s OpenShift and Ansible products, alongside continued expansion in the data business following IBM's acquisition of Confluent.

The bank forecasts 4.5% reported software growth for financial 2026, with organic growth, excluding acquisitions, expected to reach 1% for the year as Transaction Processing recovers gradually.

Infrastructure revenue is expected to grow 3% at constant exchange rates in the third quarter, while consulting revenue is forecast to rise 3.6% as growth improves in the second half.

Bank of America describes 2026 as a transitional year for IBM, with a stronger operating performance expected in 2027 as software growth and margins improve.

The bank trimmed its financial 2026 revenue forecast to $70.3 billion from $70.5 billion and its earnings-per-share estimate to $12.40 from $12.45, leaving its price target unchanged at $280.

Its valuation assumes IBM will trade at 19 times the estimated 2027 enterprise value to free cash flow, a measure comparing the company's overall value with the cash it generates after operating costs and capital expenditure.
