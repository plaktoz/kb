# Stock Market Today: Nasdaq, S&P 500 Close Lower as Oil Drops and Yields Ease on New Iran Sanctions

Crude oil prices remained lower as investors mulled the Treasury Department's new sanctions on Iran and countries that trade with it. West Texas Intermediate futures traded down 2.2% at $85.15 a barrel at 4 p.m. ET, while Brent crude was 2.3% lower at around $92.20.

Treasury yields declined modestly but remained elevated despite the government's recent efforts to calm markets. The 10-year Treasury yield, which influences rates on mortgages and other consumer loans, was around 4.71% late Monday afternoon, down roughly three basis points from Friday's close. The 30-year yield, which hit a nearly two-decade high of 5.34% last week, was recently at 5.23%, down four basis points.

On the corporate side, XPeng's CEO He Xiaopeng touted expectations for rapid scaling of humanoid robots in China and overseas following mass production, supporting the company's physical-AI R&D investment; US-listed XPeng shares are down 45% since the start of the year. The S&P 500 Energy sector was the worst performer among the 11 tracked industries around midday as crude prices pulled back.
