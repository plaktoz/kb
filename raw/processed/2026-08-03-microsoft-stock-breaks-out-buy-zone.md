# Microsoft Stock Breaks Out Into Buy Zone. Here's Why.

Microsoft (MSFT) stock broke out on Monday following its beat-and-raise fiscal Q4 earnings report last week, which topped estimates and guided to sales above views for the current quarter. On Thursday, the day after its earnings report, Microsoft surged 15.5% to close at 451.10, forming a cup base with a buy point of 466.32 per IBD MarketSurge charts. On Friday the stock flirted with that buy point, climbing as high as 466.84 intraday before closing at 464.72, up 3%. In Monday trading it jumped nearly 4% to 482.43 amid a rotation into hyperscale cloud-computing providers, with hyperscaler peers Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta also rising.

Investors were enthused by Microsoft's cloud and AI business performance in fiscal Q4 (ended June 30): Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 27% year-over-year to $59.3 billion, and commercial remaining performance obligations jumped 84% to $678 billion. Azure revenue increased 43%, topping the 40.4% consensus estimate, with the company predicting 45% Azure growth in the current quarter. Microsoft 365 Copilot has reached over 30 million paid seats.

Total fiscal Q4 earnings rose 30% to an adjusted $4.74 a share, while sales increased 18% to $90 billion. At least 10 Wall Street analysts raised their price targets on Microsoft following the earnings report.
