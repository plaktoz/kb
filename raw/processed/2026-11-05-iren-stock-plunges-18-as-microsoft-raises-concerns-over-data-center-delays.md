# IREN stock plunges 18% as Microsoft raises concerns over data center delays

Investing.com -- IREN Ltd. shares plunged 18% Thursday amid a broad technology selloff, with a Bloomberg report highlighting growing concerns at Microsoft over delays and execution risks among its neocloud partners.

Microsoft is tightening oversight of data center projects involving IREN, Nebius Group and Nscale while reconsidering whether to sign additional agreements with third-party infrastructure providers, Bloomberg reported.

Some of the problems involve IREN's Texas campus, where cabling issues have disrupted construction timelines. The company also experienced a generator fire at the site in April and disclosed hardware delivery delays in August.

Microsoft and IREN officials met last week and negotiated a revised timeline that could push completion back by several weeks or months, according to the report.

Microsoft has also walked away from several Nscale projects, including a roughly $3 billion UK data center deal delayed by difficulties securing grid power. The company is increasingly prioritizing its own data center expansion rather than relying on additional neocloud agreements.

The developments highlight growing execution risks for AI infrastructure providers, even as demand for computing capacity remains far above available supply. Gartner estimates that 80% of neocloud providers could be consolidated or shut down by the end of the decade.
