# Nasdaq futures rise as Nvidia Q2 earnings beat expectations

Nasdaq-100 futures climbed 1% Thursday, driven by Nvidia's ~7% premarket surge after the chipmaker posted a blowout earnings report that lifted semiconductor stocks across the US, Europe, and Asia.

Nvidia's fiscal Q2 revenue more than doubled, beating analyst forecasts by the widest margin in two years. The company projected 70% revenue growth for fiscal year 2028. S&P 500 futures gained 0.4%, while Dow futures were marginally negative.

Other chip stocks followed Nvidia higher — Marvell rose 5%+, while Micron and Arm each gained 4%+. Beyond semiconductors, Salesforce surged 12% premarket on strong Q2 results, and Okta jumped 19%+ after beating estimates and citing growing customer interest in agentic AI.

European stocks were broadly lower, though tech gained 1.5%. In Asia, South Korea's Kospi rose 1.53% and China's CSI 300 closed up 0.86%, while Japan's Nikkei fell 0.20%.

Investors also monitored the Federal Reserve's Jackson Hole symposium. UBS's Mark Haefele noted that "the second-quarter earnings season has shown that the fundamentals remain robust."
