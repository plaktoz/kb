# Jensen Huang Just Said a 1-Gigawatt Facility Is Worth $50 Billion to $60 Billion

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, speaking at the G20 Summit, estimated that constructing a 1-gigawatt AI data center facility costs between $50B and $60B. This has significant implications for neocloud providers like Nebius (NBIS).

## Valuing Data Centers Beyond Annual Recurring Revenue

Nebius reported Q2 revenue growth of 454% year-over-year, reaching $582M. Annual recurring revenue hit $3B, with a target of $7B–$9B by year-end.

Huang's per-gigawatt valuation offers an alternative lens: Nebius' projected five gigawatts of contracted power by end of 2026 could represent up to $300B in total facility value — well beyond what ARR alone suggests.

## Prepayments as a Capital Engine

Per-megawatt annual contract values rose sharply — from $12M at the start of the year, to over $20M in Q2, to short-term Q3 deals "above $40 million per megawatt." As a result, prepayments now self-finance 50–60% of Nebius' capital expenditures, contributing to an $8B cash position.

## Data Center Financing as a Safety Net

Iren CEO Dan Roberts noted the company's data center portfolio remains "unencumbered," meaning both Iren and Nebius could theoretically borrow against those assets if needed. GPU-backed financing has been the primary route so far, with data center financing serving as an additional fallback.
