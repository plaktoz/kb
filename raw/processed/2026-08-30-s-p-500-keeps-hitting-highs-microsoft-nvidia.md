# The S&P 500 Keeps Hitting Highs — But It's Just Microsoft and Nvidia Carrying the Entire Market

Since Q2 earnings season began July 13, the S&P 500 gained $1.75 trillion in market value — but the gains are highly concentrated. Microsoft and Nvidia together added $1.42 trillion in market cap, while the remaining 71 tech sector stocks collectively lost $22.3 billion.

Technology accounted for 79% of the index's total gains. Meanwhile, several sectors declined significantly:

| Sector | Change |
|---|---|
| Technology | +$1.392T |
| Health Care | +$345.2B |
| Financials | +$192.7B |
| Energy | +$174.7B |
| Communication Services | -$299.6B |
| Utilities | -$88.5B |
| Industrials | -$67.3B |

The S&P 500's market-cap weighting means two stocks now largely "determine its direction," creating a false sense of broad market health. If either giant disappoints, their outsized weight could drag the entire index lower even if hundreds of other companies perform well.
