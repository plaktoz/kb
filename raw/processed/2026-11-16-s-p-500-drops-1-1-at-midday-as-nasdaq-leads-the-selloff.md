# S&P 500 Drops 1.1% at Midday as Nasdaq Leads the Selloff

As seen on the 24/7 Wall St. homepage on November 16, 2026.

Tech is carrying the damage at midday, with the Nasdaq down 1.72% while the Dow holds up better. The afternoon session decides whether this stays contained to AI names or spreads.

The S&P 500 is sitting at 7,475.80 at midday, below Friday's close. The move lower has been fairly consistent since the opening bell, with selling pressure picking up through the late morning before settling into the current range.

Technology is absorbing most of the pain. The Nasdaq Composite is off 1.72% at midday, a notably steeper decline than the broader index, which points to concentrated weakness in the higher-growth, higher-valuation names that dominate that benchmark.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average is holding up considerably better, down just 0.83% at the same point in the session. The gap between the Dow and the Nasdaq shows the selling concentrated in the most rate-sensitive and momentum-driven corners of the market rather than across the board.

The afternoon decides whether this turns into a fresh leg lower. If the damage stays confined to tech-heavy names, the broader index can recover some ground before the close. A spread into cyclicals and financials would signal something more serious is developing.
