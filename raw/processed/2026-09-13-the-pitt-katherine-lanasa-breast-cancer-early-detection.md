# 'The Pitt' Star Katherine LaNasa Says Early Breast Cancer Detection Saved Her Life

Emmy-winning actress Katherine LaNasa, known for playing Nurse Dana on HBO Max's *The Pitt*, was diagnosed with stage 1 breast cancer in February 2023 at age 59, discovered through a routine mammogram. She had no symptoms and felt healthy at the time.

LaNasa underwent surgery followed by roughly three weeks of daily radiation, experiencing treatment complications for about six months afterward. She is now cancer-free and partnered with Novartis' *Your Attention, Please* campaign to promote regular screening and awareness of personal breast cancer risk.

Dr. Francisco Esteva of Northwell's Lenox Hill Hospital noted that only half of eligible women get annual mammograms, citing barriers like cost, time, transportation, and fear. He emphasized that women should discuss personal risk factors with their doctors, as "family history matters. Your own history matters."

LaNasa credited her breast cancer experience with shaping her portrayal of Nurse Dana, giving her insight into patient fear and the impact of compassionate care. She also said the experience taught her to prioritize wellness and stress reduction.

She noted that when caught early, the five-year relative survival rate is 99%, adding that a friend she encouraged to get screened was subsequently found to have a large mass — and survived.
