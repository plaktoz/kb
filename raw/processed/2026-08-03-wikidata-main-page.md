# Wikidata Main Page

## Overview

Wikidata is a free, open knowledge base with over 122 million data entities, readable and editable by both humans and machines. It serves as "central storage for the **structured data**" of Wikimedia sister projects including Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wiktionary, and Wikisource.

Content is available under a Creative Commons CC0 license and can be exported in standard formats or interlinked with other open data sets.

## Recent News

- **WikiProject Days 2026** ran June 19–21 online, focusing on WikiProjects as spaces for community connection and data modelling decisions.
- Mobile editors can now edit all datatypes directly in mobile view; Wikimedia Deutschland seeks UX testers (especially RTL language editors).
- **2025-11-12:** Temporary accounts deployed — unregistered editors now receive temporary accounts instead of IP-based identification.
