# Siri AI could come with a paywall for power users

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/siri-ai-could-come-with-a-paywall-for-power-users/

---

By Amanda Silberling — July 31, 2026

In Tim Cook's final earnings call as Apple CEO, he indicated that the upgraded Siri AI may include tiered paid access through iCloud+ subscriptions — mirroring how competitors like Anthropic and OpenAI handle usage limits.

Cook noted: "We do believe there will be people that want to use [Siri AI] a lot, and so we will have some kind of upgrade possibilities on iCloud+."

Plans remain tentative. The revamped Siri AI is currently in iOS 27 beta, with a broader fall rollout expected.

Incoming CEO John Ternus inherits a challenging situation: Apple fell behind in AI development, licensed a custom Gemini model from Google to bolster Siri, and settled a $250 million class action lawsuit over delayed AI features promised with the iPhone 16.

Apple also faces hardware supply pressures driven by an industry-wide RAM shortage, which has pushed up device prices across Meta, Samsung, Microsoft, and Sony. Apple recently raised Mac and iPad prices but has not yet adjusted iPhone pricing.
