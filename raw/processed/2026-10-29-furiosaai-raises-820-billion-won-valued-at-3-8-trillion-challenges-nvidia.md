# FuriosaAI Raises 820 Billion Won, Valued at 3.8 Trillion, Challenges NVIDIA

AI semiconductor startup plans global commercialization of RNGD chip and third-gen development with Broadcom

Domestic artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductor startup FuriosaAI has successfully secured an investment of 820 billion Korean won. The company was valued at approximately 3.8 trillion Korean won. It plans to accelerate mass production of its self-developed inference-focused semiconductors and expand into the global market, challenging NVIDIA's dominance in the AI semiconductor sector.

FuriosaAI announced on the 29th that it completed a Series D funding round of 820 billion Korean won. Initially targeting 450–750 billion Korean won in investments this January, the final amount expanded due to surging interest from domestic and international investors.

The National Growth Fund participated as the largest investor, injecting 370 billion Korean won. Overseas investors included U.S.-based Infinitum and Cisco Investments, as well as Singapore's QUDA Capital Partners. Existing shareholders such as Naver, DSC Investment, and Korea Development Bank collectively added 170 billion Korean won. The Export-Import Bank of Korea also selected FuriosaAI as its first direct investment target among venture companies since its establishment.

FuriosaAI plans to allocate the secured funds toward global commercialization of its second-generation AI semiconductor, 'RNGD,' and the development of next-generation products. RNGD is specialized for "inference," a process where AI models generate answers or perform tasks based on learned data.

The semiconductor focuses on reducing power consumption and costs required for AI services. FuriosaAI is also collaborating with U.S. semiconductor company Broadcom to develop a third-generation AI accelerator. With this investment, the company aims to transition from R&D to full-scale global supply expansion.

FuriosaAI CEO June Paik stated, "We have now reached the stage to actively disseminate our technology into the global AI infrastructure market. By accelerating the global commercialization of RNGD and developing next-generation products, we will present a new option in the AI infrastructure market based on high-performance, energy-efficient semiconductors."
