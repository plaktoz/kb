# Viral 'Menopause Cocktail' of Allergy Meds and Antacids: What Experts Say

source_url: https://www.healthline.com/health-news/viral-menopause-cocktail-allergy-meds-antacids-risks

---

Author: Mandy French
Published: July 26, 2026

A social media trend dubbed the "menopause cocktail" involves combining over-the-counter allergy medication (such as famotidine/Pepcid) with antihistamines (such as cetirizine/Zyrtec) to relieve menopausal symptoms including hot flashes, night sweats, and sleep disruption.

Proponents cite anecdotal relief. However, medical experts are urging caution:

- No clinical trials have evaluated this specific combination for menopause symptom management
- Antihistamines can increase dementia risk with long-term use in older adults
- Famotidine and antihistamines combined may cause additive sedation and anticholinergic effects
- Menopausal symptoms vary widely; what helps one person may not be appropriate for another

Experts recommend that anyone considering this approach consult a healthcare provider first. FDA-approved options for menopause management, including hormone therapy and low-dose antidepressants, have established safety and efficacy profiles.

The trend illustrates how health misinformation spreads rapidly via social platforms when conventional healthcare feels inaccessible or unresponsive.
