# Vocci's ring adds a new form factor to meeting note-taking

Vocci has released a ring-form-factor device aimed at meeting note-taking. The ring weighs under 6 grams, uses titanium surfaces, and features a button for double-tap recording control, a haptic motor, and an indicator light. Battery life is claimed at 8 hours, with a charging case providing three additional charges.

The reviewer found audio capture impressive — handling hour-long conversations in noisy environments — but criticized the software. The app felt cluttered, with AI-generated summaries often longer than the recordings themselves, and limited integration with reminder or productivity tools. MCP support for connecting to other AI assistants only recently appeared in the app.

Privacy is a notable concern. The ring resembles ordinary jewelry, and its recording indicator faces the wearer rather than others nearby. Vocci says it encourages disclosure, but the design makes covert recording easy.

At $249, it's priced higher than competing notetakers like Plaud or Pocket, justified by its compact form. The reviewer sees it as one of many hardware formats competing for user preference, with the market still maturing.
