# 10,000 Literacy Educators Worldwide Empowered Through Digital Skills Training

As education systems evolve, literacy today means more than reading and writing — it also means using digital tools effectively, critically evaluating online content, and participating in a society increasingly mediated by technology.

To support educators through this shift, the Secretariat of the Global Alliance for Literacy (GAL) at the UNESCO Institute for Lifelong Learning (UIL), in collaboration with Huawei, launched the multilingual online course "Improving the Digital Competencies of Literacy Educators." Available in Arabic, English, French and Spanish, the course equips educators with practical skills they can immediately apply, as part of UNESCO's Global Education Coalition.

Since its December 2025 launch, around 10,000 educators worldwide have benefited. Delivered through 11 self-paced sessions, the course connects strongly to real teaching practice, letting educators reflect on their work while developing and testing new skills. A literacy educator from Togo said the course reshaped his thinking around integrating theory, digital tools, and reflective practice; an educator from Pakistan emphasized that "reflective practice and digital integration can directly improve teaching effectiveness."

Rather than focusing solely on tools, participants develop a deeper understanding of how digital technologies shape learning and information — helping learners not just access content but question its intent and use it responsibly. National and municipal adult education agencies are now key partners in expanding the course across countries and UNESCO learning cities.
