# Why Evals Are So Hot Right Now

## Grok 4.7 Review

xAI's Grok 4.7 launched to mixed results. Early internal testing showed it scoring 42% on one benchmark versus Grok 4.6's 84%. However, performance improved before public release — one power user now calls it his daily driver, though he finds it weak at multi-agent coordination. Writing tests were harsh: one staffer described it as having "big Elon Musk energy" with no empathy for readers.

## Evals Go Mainstream

Nearly half of 25 PM job listings flagged by Lenny Rachitsky required eval experience. One company ran ~1,800 eval checks in a month, up from near zero in May. The shift reflects a maturing AI market where multiple models can handle common tasks, making speed, cost, and personal fit more important than raw intelligence.
