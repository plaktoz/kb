# Teaching According to Students' Aptitude: Personalized Mathematics Tutoring via Persona-, Memory-, and Forgetting-Aware LLMs

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15163

---

**Authors:** Yang Wu, Rujing Yao, Tong Zhang, Yufei Shi, Zhuoren Jiang, Zhushan Li, Xiaozhong Liu

**Submitted:** November 19, 2025

**Venue:** AAAI 2026 Workshop

**Abstract:**

The paper introduces TASA (Teaching According to Students' Aptitude), a tutoring framework addressing a core limitation in LLM-based education: most systems fail to track how student knowledge shifts over time across proficiency levels, conceptual gaps, and memory decay.

TASA combines three components:
- Persona modeling — structured proficiency profiles per student
- Event memory — logs of prior learning interactions
- Forgetting dynamics — a continuous forgetting curve integrated with knowledge tracing

The system dynamically updates each student's mastery state to generate appropriately scaled questions and explanations. Results show TASA outperforms baseline approaches, highlighting that modeling "temporal forgetting and learner profiles" is critical for effective LLM tutoring in mathematics.
