# IBM Stock Trades Around $234 as Quantum Advances and Q2 2026 Earnings Support Valuation

IBM (NYSE: IBM) stock is hovering near $234 as of September 4, 2026, with Q2 2026 earnings and fresh quantum-computing progress underpinning the technology group's valuation in the broader AI and cloud market.

## Q2 2026 financial results

- **EPS**: $2.93 (matched consensus estimate)
- **Revenue**: $17.16 billion (+1.1% YoY from $16.97 billion)
- **Quarterly dividend**: $1.69 per share (annualized $6.76; ~2.9% yield)
- **Payout ratio**: ~60% of current earnings

## Stock price context

- Current price: $234.00 USD (NYSE, September 4, 2026)
- 52-week range: $199.19 (low) to $332.46 (high)
- Market cap: ~$218 billion
- Index: Dow Jones Industrial Average
- Sector: Information Technology / IT Services and Software

## Quantum computing developments

IBM's positioning in quantum computing is emerging as a strategic differentiator:
- **25-fold throughput improvement** in certain quantum computing workloads (recent research update)
- Latest quantum processor: **120 programmable qubits** with high-speed qubit reset architecture handling over 100,000 circuits per second
- Moving quantum systems from experimental setups toward practical high-throughput applications

## Strategic context

IBM's hybrid cloud and AI software portfolio combines infrastructure and data platforms with ML tools to help enterprises modernize legacy systems while integrating new AI-driven workflows. The modest top-line growth (+1.1%) contrasts with stronger growth rates at cloud-native peers, but the dividend yield and steady earnings support income-oriented shareholders.
