# Ahead of Microsoft Earnings, Here's What Barchart Data Says Comes Next for MSFT Stock

source_url: https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3505511/ahead-of-microsoft-earnings-here-s-what-barchart-data-says-comes-next-for-msft-stock

---

Microsoft (MSFT) stock is extending gains ahead of the company's fiscal Q2 earnings scheduled to be released after the market close on Wednesday, July 29. Consensus is for the multinational to post $87.42 billion in revenue on $4.21 in earnings per share (EPS), representing a 15.34% year-over-year increase. Heading into the quarterly print, Microsoft shares are down more than 15% versus their June high.

## Options Pricing Suggests MSFT Stock Will Rally After Earnings

Despite recent underperformance amid a broader tech rout, the derivatives market believes MSFT shares will push higher after earnings. According to Barchart, the put-to-call ratio on options contracts expiring July 31 sits at 0.45x, indicating a strong bullish skew. The upper price on those contracts is set at $413, signaling Microsoft could gain more than 6% from current levels through the end of the week. The Nasdaq-listed firm also has a history of closing both July and August in the green, a seasonal pattern that makes it attractive to own near-term.

## Technicals and Valuation Favor Buying

Microsoft shares have recently ripped through their 20-day moving average and now look poised to break above the 50-day moving average as well; a decisive break above $399 could help sustain upward trajectory. MSFT is trading at a forward price-to-earnings multiple of under 20x, described as a compelling valuation for a major AI beneficiary. Microsoft also pays a small dividend yield of 0.94%. That said, insiders have predominantly sold MSFT shares in the trailing three months.

## Wall Street Recommendation

The consensus rating on MSFT stock sits at "Strong Buy," with a mean price target of about $545 — indicating potential upside of nearly 40% from current levels over the next 12 months.
