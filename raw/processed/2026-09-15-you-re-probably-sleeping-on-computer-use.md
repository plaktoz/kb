# You're Probably Sleeping On Computer Use

Mike Taylor, Every's head of evals, initially rated the AI model Astra as merely "okay" but reversed course after experiencing its computer use capabilities. The feature — allowing the model to click, type, and scroll through apps — transformed it into his daily driver.

He started with small tasks (filling out school forms), then escalated to updating Google Slides decks, editing video, and checking every hyperlink in a book manuscript. Astra opened each URL, verified it loaded correctly, and compiled a problem spreadsheet autonomously.

## What Every staffers are delegating via computer use

- Calendar management and school event entry
- Selling items on marketplaces by browsing phone photos
- Negotiating phone plans through live support chat
- Fixing mysterious computer slowdowns
- Transferring internet service between apartments

## Slide deck workflow (Douglas Brundage, head of marketing)

1. Design a few reference slides manually
2. Draft copy with Codex
3. Have Codex build the full deck in Google Slides using your templates
4. Review, edit, then feed corrections back so Codex saves them as rules

The throughline: these aren't complex tasks — they're the annoying ones humans perpetually defer. Computer use converts procrastination items into delegatable ones.
