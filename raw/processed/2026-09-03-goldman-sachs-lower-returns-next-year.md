# Goldman Sachs warns investors to expect lower returns over the next year

Goldman Sachs chief global equity strategist Peter Oppenheimer cautioned investors that after strong recent gains, future returns will be more modest. He projected "mid- to high-single-digit [percentage] returns over the next 12 months" across regions, contingent on continued economic growth.

The S&P 500 has already gained 12% in 2026, setting a high bar. Several headwinds are emerging:

## Rising Bond Yields Globally

The 10-year US Treasury recently hit its highest level since 2023. Japan's 10-year yield crossed 3% for the first time since 1996, while UK and German yields are also at multi-year highs.

## Oil Above $90/Barrel

Driven by Iran-related geopolitical tensions and Strait of Hormuz disruptions, energy costs are pushing up prices across transportation, agriculture, and manufacturing.

## Market Risk

Strategists note stocks have so far absorbed yield increases, but Miller Tabak's Matt Maley warned that "higher yields don't matter for stocks…until they do."

The convergence of these pressures suggests a more cautious environment ahead.
