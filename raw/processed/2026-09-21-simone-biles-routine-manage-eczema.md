# Simone Biles Thought Her Itchy, Flaky Scalp Was Dandruff. It Was Eczema

Olympic gymnast Simone Biles has opened up about her diagnosis of both atopic dermatitis and seborrheic dermatitis. She initially mistook scalp flaking and itching for dandruff, and felt embarrassed enough that she delayed seeking medical care. After posting about her symptoms during the Paris Olympics, she finally consulted a dermatologist.

Her doctor, Angela Lamb, MD, confirmed that stress, weather extremes, and individual triggers can all worsen eczema flares. Biles found relief using Zoryve (roflumilast), a steroid-free topical PDE4 inhibitor, in both foam (scalp) and cream (skin) forms. She partnered with Zoryve's manufacturer, Arcutis Biotherapeutics, for their "Skin to Believe In" campaign.

For stress management — a known flare contributor — Biles relies on social time with friends, massages, and evening baths. She noted that stepping back from intense daily training also significantly reduced her stress levels.

Biles encouraged others not to "silently suffer" and to seek professional care.
