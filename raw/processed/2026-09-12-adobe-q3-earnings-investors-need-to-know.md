## Overview

Adobe (ADBE) reported Q3 earnings on Sept. 10, prioritizing user acquisition over paid product conversion. Fourth-quarter guidance missed analyst expectations, sending shares down ~5% in after-hours trading before recovering.

## Competitive Pressure

Adobe's Creative Cloud suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro) now competes with generative AI tools from ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini. Adobe's response is **Firefly**, its generative AI model integrated into existing products via text prompts, alongside a freemium tier.

## Earnings Highlights

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Q3 Revenue | $6.76B (+13% YoY) |
| Diluted EPS | $4.62 |
| ARR | $27.50B |
| AI-first ARR growth | +150% YoY |
| Freemium MAUs | 100M+ (+70% YoY) |

**Q4 Guidance:** Revenue of $6.8B–$6.85B (midpoint below consensus)

## Strategy

Incoming CEO Anil Chakravarthy described a funnel approach: acquire users via freemium, deepen engagement, then "convert them into ARR" at the right time.

The article compares this to Zuckerberg's early Facebook strategy — offer free products, build habit, then monetize.

## Outlook

Adobe stock is likely to remain range-bound until it demonstrates meaningful freemium-to-paid conversion rates, as today's market favors tangible AI results over future potential.
