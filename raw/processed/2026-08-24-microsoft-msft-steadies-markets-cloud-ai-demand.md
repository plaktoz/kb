# Microsoft (NASDAQ:MSFT) Steadies Markets As Cloud And AI Demand Stay Hot

Microsoft returned to the center of market conversation as cloud strength across big technology helped reassure a market that has been leaning heavily on the artificial intelligence trade, with US benchmarks hovering near record highs and the market-fear gauge easing into a calmer stretch. The tone across large-cap software firmed as rate-cut expectations built. Microsoft shares were last quoted at $487.79, up 0.94% on the day.

The piece frames Microsoft's cloud and AI demand as a stabilizing force for the broader market during a period when investors have been on edge about the durability of AI-driven spending.
