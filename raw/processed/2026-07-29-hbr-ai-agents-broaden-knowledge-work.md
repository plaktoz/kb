# Research: How AI Agents Broaden the Scope of Knowledge Work

AI agents don't just speed up knowledge work; they change its nature. In a field study of Perplexity's conversational assistant and autonomous agent, researchers found meaningful differences in what each tool actually delivers to users.

You ask two different AI tools the same question—say, to pull together a comprehensive competitive landscape for a new product line. The first comes back in seconds with a tidy summary and a list of links. You read it, open a dozen tabs, copy numbers into a spreadsheet, build the chart, and write the memo yourself. The second comes back twenty-some minutes later with the spreadsheet built, the chart drawn, and a draft memo attached. One handed you information to act on. The other handed you completed work.
