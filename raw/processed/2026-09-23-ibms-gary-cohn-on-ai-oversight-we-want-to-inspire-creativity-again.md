# IBM's Gary Cohn on AI oversight: 'We want to inspire creativity again'

IBM Vice Chair Gary Cohn spoke with Yahoo Finance's Brian Sozzi about how the U.S. government should approach AI regulation.

Cohn's central concern was fragmentation: having 50 states craft independent AI rules would be damaging, particularly given that the U.S. is in direct competition with China on AI development.

He argued against regulating the technology itself, instead favoring oversight of end products and outcomes. As he put it, the goal is to "inspire creativity" rather than stifle it, keeping the playing field open for startups and large companies alike.

On the question of jurisdiction, Cohn pointed to existing regulatory frameworks as the natural home for AI oversight — financial AI products would fall under financial regulators, medical AI under the FDA, and so on. In his words: "You're not going to regulate AI, you're going to inspire creativity. You're going to regulate the outcomes."

No new dedicated AI oversight body was endorsed; rather, Cohn favored routing AI accountability through whichever sector-specific regulators already govern a given product domain.
