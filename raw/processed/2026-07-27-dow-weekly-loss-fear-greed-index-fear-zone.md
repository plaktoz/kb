# Dow Gains Over 200 Points But Records Weekly Loss: Greed Index Remains in Fear Zone

source_url: https://www.benzinga.com/markets/market-summary/26/07/60687939/dow-gains-over-200-points-but-records-weekly-loss-investor-sentiment-edges-higher-but-greed-index-remains-in-fear-zone

---

Published: July 27, 2026 — Benzinga

U.S. stocks settled mixed on Friday (July 25), with the Dow Jones gaining more than 200 points during the session. However, the Nasdaq settled lower amid a decline in chip stocks.

Major indices on Wall Street settled lower last week:
- S&P 500: -0.6%
- Nasdaq: -2.1%
- Dow Jones: -0.4% (third straight week of declines)

On the economic data front, the S&P Global composite PMI climbed to 53.6 in July from 51.9 in the previous month — its highest reading since November. The S&P Global manufacturing PMI slipped slightly to 53.8 in July from 53.9 in June.

Most sectors on the S&P 500 closed on a positive note, with consumer staples, real estate and materials stocks recording the biggest gains on Friday. However, information technology stocks closed the session lower.

The Dow Jones closed higher by around 236 points to 51,947.25 on Friday. The S&P 500 rose 0.05% to 7,411.98, while the Nasdaq Composite declined 0.64% to 24,975.82.

## CNN Business Fear & Greed Index

At a current reading of 39.4, the index remained in the "Fear" zone on Friday, versus a prior reading of 38.9. The index ranges from 0 (maximum fear) to 100 (maximum greed) and is based on seven equal-weighted indicators.

Higher fear exerts pressure on stock prices; higher greed has the opposite effect.

## Earnings Watch

Investors are watching upcoming earnings from Nucor Corp, Celestica Inc, and F5 Inc. Verizon reported better-than-expected Q2 earnings; American Express reported mixed results and narrowed its FY26 sales guidance slightly below estimates.
