# When There's More Work Than Your Team Can Handle

The article opens with a coaching client named Chris, a newly appointed chief legal counsel at a financial services firm. After his company completed three rapid acquisitions, his team faced overwhelming demands — contract reviews, compliance work, and regulatory filings piled up quickly.

When staff reported being "at capacity," Chris responded by absorbing the excess work personally, working nights and weekends. This approach ultimately undermined his ability to focus on the high-level strategic leadership his role required.

When demands increase while resources shrink, leaders face a genuine dilemma: absorbing extra work themselves or pushing an already-stretched team harder — and "both approaches carry costs."
