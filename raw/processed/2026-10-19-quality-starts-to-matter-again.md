# Quality Starts to Matter Again

Inflation came in hot and crude oil climbed back over $100,1 yet bonds rallied anyway. September Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 3.7% from a year earlier, and core CPI rose 2.6%, both above forecasts.2 Producer prices followed with their biggest monthly gain since March 2022.3 The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to 5.33% after the CPI release, then reversed as investors sold artificial intelligence (AI) stocks and bought Treasuries, finishing the week at 5.08%, down from 5.24% the prior week.4 Hotter-than-expected inflation would normally push yields higher. This time, the flight to safety won out. Bond investors appreciated the bid, which has been elusive for much of the year.

Credit spreads are decompressing, with investors demanding even more yield from weaker borrowers. Concerns about how the AI buildout is being financed hit the most indebted names hardest. CoreWeave fell more than 15% in a single day, and Oracle slid nearly 9%.5 Chip stocks had their worst week since April 2025, while consumer staples rallied.6 Credit is showing the same split. Investment-grade spreads widened from 81 to 84 basis points over the past week, and the lowest-rated high-yield bonds are widening fastest.7 Spreads remain tight by historical standards, but the market is starting to differentiate between credits and reward stronger ones.

The week ahead is light on economic data, with flash purchasing managers' index (PMI) readings due Friday.8 Treasury supply is the main rates event: a 20-year bond reopening Wednesday and a 5-year Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) auction Thursday, a timely test of demand for inflation protection.9 Federal Reserve (Fed) officials entered their pre-meeting quiet period Saturday; therefore, markets will hear little Fed commentary ahead of the Oct. 28 decision.10 Earnings take center stage, with SpaceX, Netflix and Capital One reporting Tuesday, and Tesla, Meta and AT&T on Wednesday.11,12 Last week, the market rewarded quality, favoring defensive sectors and stronger balance sheets over the most leveraged corners of the AI trade.13 This week's earnings will test whether last week's flight to quality has staying power.
