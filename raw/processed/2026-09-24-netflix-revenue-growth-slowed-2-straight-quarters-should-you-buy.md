# Netflix's Revenue Growth Has Slowed for 2 Straight Quarters. Should You Buy the Stock Anyway?

Netflix's revenue growth has decelerated from a 17.6% peak in Q4 2025 to 13.4% in Q2 2026, with management forecasting roughly 12% growth in Q3 — a potential third consecutive slowdown.

The culprit is largely tough year-over-year comparisons rather than weakening demand. Supporting evidence includes:

- Over 97 billion viewing hours in H1 2026, with engagement growing 2% YoY
- Advertising revenue on pace to roughly double to ~$3 billion
- Operating income up 11% YoY to $4.2 billion in Q2
- Management maintained — not cut — its 2026 revenue forecast of $51.0–$51.4 billion

The stock, trading near $72, sits about 40% below its 52-week high at roughly 19x forward earnings. This is "arguably fair if growth settles near 12%," but not an obvious bargain.

**Verdict:** Hold for existing shareholders; new buyers should wait for a reported quarter confirming the growth rate has stabilized.
