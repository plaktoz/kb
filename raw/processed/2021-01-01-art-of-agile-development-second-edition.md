# The Art of Agile Development, Second Edition

A comprehensive guide to Agile software development, this second edition covers adoption, planning, development, delivery, and management drawn from over two decades of experience. It synthesizes ideas from Extreme Programming, Scrum, Lean, DevOps, and related methodologies.

The book addresses a core problem: most companies claim to practice Agile, but there's "widespread misunderstanding of what Agile is and how to use it."

## Key Topics

- Improving organizational agility and creating conditions for Agile to succeed
- Team focus on value, priorities, and continuous improvement
- Reliable software delivery through shared ownership and continuous deployment
- Product planning, budgeting, and experimentation

## What's New vs. First Edition

A complete rewrite (not just an update), adding:
- Agile adoption guidance based on the **Agile Fluency Model** (co-created with Diana Larsen)
- A new chapter on **scaling Agile**
- A new chapter on **DevOps**
- Guidance for **remote teams**

## Structure

Four parts: *Improving Agility*, *Focusing on Value*, *Delivering Reliably*, and *Optimizing Outcomes*
