# Is It Too Late to Buy International Business Machines Corp (IBM) After 3.9% Rally? GF Value Says Undervalued

International Business Machines Corp (IBM) shares rose 3.9% on August 27, 2026, reaching $238.79, within a 52-week range of $199.19 to $332.46. GF Value places fair value at $242.26, putting the current price 1.4% below that estimate — a slight undervaluation that suggests a margin of safety for investors.

## How does IBM's valuation compare to its history?

IBM's current P/E (TTM) of 21.2x is notably lower than its 5-year median of 25.2x, indicating the stock trades at a discount to its historical valuation, consistent with the GF Value assessment.

## What does IBM's GF Score tell us?

IBM's GF Score is 81/100, reflecting a strong overall market position, with a valuation rank of 10/10 and profitability rank of 8/10. Financial strength (5/10) and growth (6/10) are more moderate, suggesting some caution alongside the attractive valuation.

## What are gurus and insiders doing with IBM?

Thirteen gurus currently hold IBM stock, with 6 adding to positions and 5 trimming in recent quarters — a mixed but generally positive institutional sentiment. Insiders purchased $0.4M in shares over the past 12 months with no selling observed, a pattern of insider buying that can signal confidence in the company's prospects.

The rally was primarily driven by IBM's completed acquisition of HRL Laboratories to expand quantum hardware capabilities and management's reaffirmed guidance on robust annual free cash flow growth.
