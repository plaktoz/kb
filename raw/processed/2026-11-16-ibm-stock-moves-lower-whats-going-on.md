# IBM Stock Moves Lower: What's Going On?

International Business Machines Corp. (NYSE:IBM) shares are falling Monday despite announcing a quantum computing collaboration with NVIDIA and Fermilab, as the stock slips below an important multi-month trend line.

- IBM shares are consolidating.

## IBM Stock Falls Despite Quantum Computing News

IBM revealed a plan Monday to work with NVIDIA Corp. (NASDAQ:NVDA) and Fermilab on blending classical and quantum computing. The aim is to learn how the two can grow together inside the modular systems that fault-tolerant machines are expected to use.

The partners want to open a shared lab platform that places NVIDIA DGX B200 servers beside IBM's quantum computers, among them models built on the Nighthawk chip. That chip supports as many as 7,500 two-qubit operations across 120 qubits.

Scientists would aim it at particle and nuclear physics questions that classical computers cannot answer alone, and IBM's Jerry Chow said the effort "will open new frontiers."

IBM also worked with NVIDIA and Pasqal on open-source software that lets supercomputing centers schedule quantum processors through Slurm, a widely used job manager that never treated them as built-in resources. Hybrid quantum-classical tasks now run as ordinary Slurm jobs.

IBM also reported new work with RIKEN and NVIDIA on an iron-sulfur molecule, where 72 qubits and 5 million samples sharpened accuracy 79-fold compared with earlier attempts and landed 4.3 millihartree from a leading classical technique.

## IBM Stock Technical Read: Break Below 200-Day Average

Technical weakness seemingly explains the slide better. Shares fell beneath the 200-day average, near $246.38, a multi-month trend line that is itself sloping down. After climbing from about $217 in late May to a peak near $332 on June 2, the stock tumbled to roughly $199 by July 23 and has won back only part of that loss.

It now trades under its 20-day line as well, and the MACD momentum gauge has dipped beneath the signal line it tracks, a sign that buying interest has cooled.

A close above $246.38 would wipe out Monday's drop, while the 20-day line near $249.82 is the next hurdle for buyers.

## IBM Shares Are Dipping

**IBM Price Action:** IBM shares were down 1.22% at $244.12 at the time of publication on Monday, according to Benzinga Pro.
