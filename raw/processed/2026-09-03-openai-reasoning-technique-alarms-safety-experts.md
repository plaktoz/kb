# OpenAI's new reasoning technique alarms AI safety experts

**Author:** Russell Brandom

**Published:** September 2, 2026

OpenAI's upcoming Astra model employs a technique called "recurrent depth" (also termed "opaque recurrence"), which allows the model to process queries in loops rather than sequentially. This reduces the legibility of its chain-of-thought reasoning, raising alarms among AI safety researchers.

## Expert Reactions

Redwood CEO Buck Shlegeris warned: "I am extremely concerned by the reporting that Astra uses opaque recurrence." He cautioned that scaling the technique could "totally destroys CoT monitorability."

Safety advocate Zvi Mowshowitz suggested legislation may be needed to prevent competitive pressure from eroding transparency standards, calling the approach "playing with fire."

Redwood Research's Ryan Greenblatt expressed concern about a natural progression toward models reasoning "entirely or almost entirely in latent space."

## OpenAI's Response

OpenAI pushed back, with chief scientist Jakub Pachocki affirming the lab's commitment to legible chains of thought. The company noted Astra's use of the technique remains limited.

## Broader Industry Context

A follow-up report indicated Anthropic and Google DeepMind were also exploring the technique, suggesting competitive pressure may drive wider adoption across the industry.
