# A Formula for Calculating Your Company's Geopolitical Exposure

**Authors:** Cindy Levy, Matt Watters, and Shubham Singhal (McKinsey & Company — senior partners in London, New Jersey, and Detroit offices)
**Published:** August 26, 2026, *Harvard Business Review*

The piece opens with a contrast: "Two companies. One geopolitical moment. Two very different outcomes."

Geopolitical volatility has shifted from being an occasional disruption to becoming the default operating environment for businesses. In response, McKinsey developed a formula to help companies assess the enterprise value at risk from geopolitical exposure.

The framework is designed to quantify what's at stake, moving beyond qualitative risk awareness toward a more structured, financial assessment of geopolitical risk.

Key topics: risk management, business and society, globalization, strategy, and trade.
