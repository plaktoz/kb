# Y Combinator's Garry Tan Wants US Open-Weight AI Labs to 'Distill' Frontier Models, Too

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/y-combinators-garry-tan-wants-u-s-open-weight-ai-labs-to-distill-frontier-models-too/
author: Julie Bort
date: 2026-09-11

---

## Summary

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan is pushing back against calls for regulatory intervention on AI distillation, and is instead advocating for American open-weight labs to freely use the same techniques on U.S. frontier models.

Speaking to CNBC, Tan said: "I would do nothing. We could argue that there should be an American distillation regime."

He clarified to TechCrunch that his vision involves smaller U.S. open-weight labs applying distillation training techniques to American frontier models, broadening the domestic open-weight ecosystem beyond Chinese options.

**What is distillation?** It involves extensively prompting a model to understand how it reasons and works — a common, legitimate training technique in AI development.

## Context

Anthropic this week released its second threat intelligence report alleging Chinese labs are conducting "illicit distillation attacks" using stolen credentials and fraud. CEO Dario Amodei had previously called on regulators to crack down on the practice.

Tan's position differs sharply. His argument rests on two points:

- AI labs shouldn't dictate what customers do with information their models provide
- Frontier labs themselves trained on vast amounts of public and copyrighted data without permission — referencing Anthropic's recently settled $1.5B copyright case

He told TechCrunch: "Controlling what users and customers do with API calls to closed weight models feels constraining..."

## Bigger Picture

Tan frames his concern around AI concentration risk. "The nightmare scenario...is that there's just one company," he said, warning against a single dominant, proprietary AI provider controlling the frontier.

He supports frontier labs remaining viable businesses while ensuring open-weight models preserve broader public access — viewing both as necessary for a healthy AI ecosystem.
