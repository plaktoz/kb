# IBM Slips as Its $5 Billion AI Security Bet Finds a Builder

IBM added LTM as a deployment partner for its Lightwell platform on Wednesday, though shares dipped roughly 0.2% to $231.48 — indicating investor skepticism that an additional distribution channel will quickly generate meaningful revenue.

IBM committed $5 billion to Lightwell and related open-source security initiatives earlier this year. The platform uses AI agents to detect vulnerabilities, develop patches, and validate fixes, producing "7,500 patches during its first two weeks." LTM brings integration, validation, and DevSecOps support for enterprise customers — addressing the gap between capable technology and reliable deployment that actually moves corporate budgets.

Financially, IBM closed Q2 with $8.2 billion in cash and $62 billion in debt. The $5 billion commitment represents roughly 61% of its cash balance, though spending will occur over time. Software revenue rose 5% to $7.8 billion quarterly, while infrastructure revenue fell 7%.

At $231.48, IBM shares trade about 4.56% below GuruFocus's estimated fair value of $242.54 — offering modest valuation support as Lightwell attempts to offset declining infrastructure revenue with recurring software growth.
