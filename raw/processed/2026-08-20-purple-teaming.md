# Purple Teaming Explained

**By Janani Nagarajan | February 22, 2023**

Source: https://www.crowdstrike.com/cybersecurity-101/purple-teaming/

## What is a Purple Team?

A purple team combines offensive and defensive cybersecurity professionals to simulate attacks, identify vulnerabilities, and recommend remediation strategies. The name reflects the blending of red (offensive) and blue (defensive) teams.

Unlike separate red/blue team structures, purple teams operate in close coordination, sharing insights to address weaknesses and improve overall security posture.

---

## Purple Team vs. Red Team vs. Blue Team

| | Red Team | Blue Team | Purple Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Offensive/ethical hackers acting as adversaries | Incident responders and analysts | Combined offensive + defensive members |
| **What** | Attack defenses using real-world TTPs | Identify and respond to attacks | Simultaneously test and defend |
| **Why** | Find exploitable gaps | Test defenses and IR playbooks | Improve security posture long-term |

---

## Advantages of Purple Teaming

Standard red/blue benefits include:
- Identifying misconfigurations and coverage gaps
- Strengthening network security and detection capabilities
- Fostering cooperation across IT and security teams
- Raising awareness of human vulnerabilities
- Building security skills in a low-risk environment

Additional purple team benefits:
- Enhanced protection through continuous feedback and knowledge sharing
- Greater consistency through ongoing engagement
- Shared goals between offensive and defensive personnel
