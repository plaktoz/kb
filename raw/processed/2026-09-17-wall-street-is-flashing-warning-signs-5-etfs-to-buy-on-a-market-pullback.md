# Wall Street Is Flashing Warning Signs: 5 ETFs to Buy on a Market Pullback

Several market warning signals are pointing to potential overvaluation — the S&P 500 Shiller CAPE ratio has exceeded 40x and the Buffett indicator has surpassed 230%. Historically, the September-October midterm election window has been volatile, with the S&P 500 dropping 5%+ in 15 of 24 such cycles.

Rather than panic, any pullback should be treated as a buying opportunity. Five ETFs stand out:

1. **VOO** (Vanguard S&P 500 ETF) — broad large-cap exposure; 15.3% average 10-year annual return
2. **VUG** (Vanguard Morningstar Growth ETF) — growth-oriented S&P 500 slice; 17.8% 10-year average return
3. **QQQ** (Invesco QQQ Trust) — Nasdaq-100 tracker; 20.8% 10-year average return
4. **AIQ** (Global X AI & Technology ETF) — global AI/tech exposure; 33% average return over the past three years
5. **SCHD** (Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF) — dividend-focused value fund; 13.2% 10-year average return, up ~29% year-to-date in 2026

AI-driven productivity gains and the tech-heavy S&P 500 composition differentiate today's market from historical precedents where such valuation levels signaled danger.
