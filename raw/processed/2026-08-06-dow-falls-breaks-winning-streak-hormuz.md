# Stock Market Today: Dow Falls 460 Points, Breaks Winning Streak

A sixth consecutive day of gains proved out of reach for the Dow Jones Industrial Average. The blue-chip index opened Thursday's session higher, on track for its longest winning streak of the year, but declines in shares of Salesforce, Boeing and UnitedHealth and others dragged it lower. The Dow finished the day 464 points, or 0.85% lower.

The Nasdaq traded near flat for much of the day, finishing 0.1% lower amid declines for Sandisk and Western Digital. Both companies reported results that showed strong growth but failed to live up to investors' lofty expectations.

Investors have been bracing for potential volatility in SpaceX shares. A post-IPO lockup on more than 900 million shares held by some employees and early investors is ending, meaning the stock can now be sold. The rocket maker's stock showed resilience, rising 6%.

Oil climbed as investors await updates from the Middle East. Brent crude, the international benchmark, rose 3.8% to $82.49 a barrel. Iran and Oman on Wednesday were finalizing a draft agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
