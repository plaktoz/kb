# How Much Data Center Revenue Do AI Companies Earn?

## Chip & Hardware Suppliers

**Nvidia** posted data center revenue of $89.0B for Q2 2026 (ended July 26), up 117% YoY. Full-year 2025 data center revenue was $193.7B. CEO Jensen Huang stated visibility to "$1 trillion in Blackwell and Vera Rubin sales from 2025 through calendar 2027."

**Broadcom** reported $10.8B in AI semiconductor revenue for Q2 2026, up 143% YoY, with Q3 guidance of $16B — over 200% growth. Full fiscal-year guidance raised to $56B.

**AMD** earned $6.7B in data center revenue for Q2 2026, up 107% YoY.

**Intel** reported $6.3B in DCAI revenue for Q2 2026, up 59% YoY — its strongest growth in years.

**IBM** reported Infrastructure revenue of $3.84B in Q2 2026, down 7% overall, as mainframe declines offset 37% growth in AI-relevant servers.

## Cloud Operators

- **AWS:** $42.2B in Q2 2026, up 37% YoY; full-year 2025 revenue ~$128.7B
- **Microsoft Azure:** $39.3B (Intelligent Cloud segment) in Q2 2026, up 32%; Azure alone surpassed $100B annually for the first time
- **Google Cloud:** $24.8B in Q2 2026, up 82% YoY — fastest growth among major providers

The four largest hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta) collectively guide to roughly **$745B in combined 2026 capex**, up from $700B earlier in the year.

## Outlook

Goldman Sachs estimated 2027 hyperscaler capex could reach "roughly $1.1 trillion in its base case, and as much as $1.4 trillion in a bullish scenario." Nvidia guided for $108B in total revenue for the quarter ending late October 2026.

Risks include efficiency gains potentially moderating infrastructure demand, and hyperscalers spending at historically high levels relative to operating cash flows.
