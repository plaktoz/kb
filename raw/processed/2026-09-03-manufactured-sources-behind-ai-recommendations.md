# Three sites made 215,128 "best software" pages for AI. Perplexity cites them

**Author:** Trellner Research

**Published:** September 2, 2026

Trellner Research queried two Perplexity models across 380 software categories, generating 7,534 citations. The study reveals a pattern of manufactured, AI-targeted content dominating AI-powered search recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Nearly 60% of citations pointed to domains ranked outside the top 100,000 most-visited websites
- Over 23% came from domains not in the top 1 million at all
- The third-most-cited source was **guideflow.com**, a demo software vendor cited 194 times despite not being a review platform

## The Three-Site Network

Three sites — **wifitalents.com**, **worldmetrics.org**, and **gitnux.org** — appear to share common infrastructure and together published 215,128 machine-generated "best [x] software" pages.

Two of those sites title their homepages "Facts & Grounding Page" — language directed at AI retrieval systems rather than human readers.

The same category query returned contradictory rankings across all three sites, yet each credited named editorial staff and claimed an editorial process. One carried an unrendered template variable reading "Within the next 26 days" in its byline.

## Anomalies

Among vendor links found in citations:
- One redirected to an Indonesian gambling portal
- Another redirected to a Monaco casino hotel

## Limitations

The study notes it only measured Perplexity, used a single prompt run, and did not test whether removing these sources would alter recommendations.
