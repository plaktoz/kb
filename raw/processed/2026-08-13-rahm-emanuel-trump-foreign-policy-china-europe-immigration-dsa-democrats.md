# Rahm Emanuel on All-In Podcast: Trump's Foreign Policy, China, Europe's Decline, Immigration, DSA vs Democrats

Former Chicago Mayor and Obama Chief of Staff Rahm Emanuel joined Jason Calacanis and David Friedberg for a wide-ranging discussion on geopolitics, domestic policy, and the Democratic Party's future.

Duration: 1:11:35

## Clinton & Obama Legacies

Emanuel credited Clinton for integrating "policy, politics, and press" and noted Obama inherited near-depression conditions, transitioning America "from losing 800,000 jobs to creating 200,000 jobs."

## China Strategy

Emanuel argued China's primary export is economic dysfunction — deliberately crushing other nations' manufacturing bases. He advocates forming an economic bloc with Australia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, and EU allies to "isolate the isolator."

On Taiwan, he outlined three Chinese strategies: economic quarantine, rapid invasion, or seizing small offshore islands incrementally. He warned deterrence is currently weakened.

## Europe

Emanuel pushed back on framing Europe's decline as purely a "leftist" problem, noting Angela Merkel (CDU/conservative) made the nuclear shutdown and open-border decisions. He acknowledged immigration disorder fueled political backlash but cited economic stagnation as equally responsible.

## Immigration

Emanuel endorsed the bipartisan Dignity Act (23 Republicans, 23 Democrats), calling it an imperfect but workable compromise. He expressed support for border enforcement alongside legal pathways, referencing his own design of Operation Gatekeeper under Clinton. He criticized his own party for promising "free healthcare to people that cross illegally" in 2020 debates.

## Education

Emanuel detailed a comprehensive plan: universal pre-K, teacher retraining, requiring high school graduates to show acceptance letters for college/military/vocational programs, making community college free for B-average students, and allowing up to 20% foreign students at universities paying full tuition to subsidize Americans.

## Fiscal Policy

He attributed deficit explosion to fighting two wars while cutting taxes — "never been done in American history." He called for a tax code shift from wealth preservation to wealth creation, criticizing step-up basis provisions.

## DSA vs. Democrats

Emanuel was blunt: "You are not a Democrat... I'll see you in November." He argued Justice Democrats aren't interested in beating Republicans, calling that a "fundamental strategic difference."
