# Alibaba shares rally after unveiling its 'most powerful' AI model as U.S.-China competition heats up

Alibaba unveiled its latest and "most powerful" AI model, Qwen3.8-Max, on Monday as Chinese companies race to close the AI gap with the U.S. Qwen3.8-Max, scheduled for release next week, boasts 2.4 trillion parameters, making it one of the most powerful models in the Qwen family to date. Parameters refer to the numerical settings that shape how AI processes information and generates responses.

The model supports a context window of up to 1 million tokens, meaning it can understand and work with thousands of pages of information at once. Alibaba's New York-listed shares were up 4.5% in premarket trading, while its Hong Kong-listed shares rose 7%.

The model's capabilities span coding, real-life work, research, long-horizon tasks, and visual intelligence. Alibaba shared results showing it delivering comparable or sometimes better scores than Anthropic's Fable 5, ranking second in the Vision Arena and fifth in the Text Arena.

The release comes after domestic rival Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 earlier this month, which has 2.8 trillion parameters, making it China's largest AI model. Qwen3.8-Max can reportedly code highly autonomously for weeks with little human input — in one internal test it spent 16 days building and improving an AI coding tool by writing code, testing it, fixing errors, and refining its own work.

It can also manage real-world workloads such as reviewing legal documents, conducting financial research, or architectural 3D modeling. Its visual intelligence spans understanding hundred-page documents, television series, or 100-hour livestreams and turning them into searchable, interactive knowledge hubs.
