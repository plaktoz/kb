# IBM Stocks Fall Over 2% While Agent Identity Becomes Infrastructure

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ibm-stocks-fall-over-2-191613367.html

---

By Khac Phu Nguyen | Published: Tuesday, September 29, 2026

IBM is integrating its agent identity and credential management tools into Nvidia OpenShell. The company's shares dropped roughly 2.0%, trading at $220.89 around 9:52 AM ET on Monday, September 28. The core idea behind the move is that as AI agents get more autonomy within organizations, businesses need visibility into "which agent is acting and what it can reach."

The newly previewed IBM Agent Identity tool works alongside other systems: HashiCorp Vault manages credential access, IBM Identity Protection helps flag agents acting beyond expected boundaries, and Red Hat OpenShift allows these agents to run across varied computing setups. Combined, these tools aim to give large enterprises better oversight of autonomous AI systems.

Despite the potential business opportunity this represents for IBM's software division, the announcement did not include specifics on "contract value, pricing or date for a full Agent Identity release." According to the referenced valuation chart, IBM's share price sits "9.08% below its $242.94 GF Value estimate," suggesting room for growth if the company can convert its agent security offerings into substantial revenue.
