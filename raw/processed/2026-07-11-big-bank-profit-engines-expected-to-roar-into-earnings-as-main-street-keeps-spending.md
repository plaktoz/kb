# Big bank profit engines expected to roar into earnings as Main Street keeps spending

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/big-bank-profit-engines-expected-to-roar-into-earnings-as-main-street-keeps-spending-133238691.html

---

Jamie Dimon's special retention award has grown to over $280 million, symbolizing the broader resurgence of big banks heading into earnings season.

JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citigroup, and Goldman Sachs will all report second quarter results Tuesday before the opening bell, with Morgan Stanley following Wednesday. Analysts expect each firm to show year-over-year profit growth.

UBS analyst Erica Najarian noted that investors are "naturally skeptical," observing it's been a while since conditions felt like peak bank performance.

HSBC analyst Saul Martinez described the fundamental backdrop as good but cautioned the quarter may not "recalibrate people's expectations materially higher."

Tailwinds include AI deals, loan growth, consumer spending, and SpaceX's IPO generating a $500 million windfall for participating banks. Credit markets show no stress — Martinez called the overall picture "benign."

Bank of America's Institute reported card spending jumped 6.3% year-over-year in June, the strongest growth in four years, driven by discretionary purchases.

Bank of America analyst Ebrahim Poonawala said Wall Street operations are "literally firing on all cylinders right now," with hopes of a multiyear AI-driven capital markets supercycle.

Dimon himself acknowledged his bank is "overearning," adding: "So far, so good this year. Hold on, you really don't know."
