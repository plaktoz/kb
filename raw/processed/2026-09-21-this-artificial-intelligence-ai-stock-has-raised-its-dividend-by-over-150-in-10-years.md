# This Artificial Intelligence (AI) Stock Has Raised Its Dividend by Over 150% in 10 Years

Microsoft (MSFT) has become a notable dividend growth stock despite being primarily known as a tech giant. Its quarterly dividend now stands at $0.98/share after an 8% increase — up from $0.39 a decade ago, representing roughly 151% growth.

The current yield of ~0.8% appears modest, but this is largely because the stock itself has surged ~65% over five years. At the prior price of ~$300, the yield would approach 1.3%, exceeding the S&P 500 average of ~1.1%.

The low yield is a trade-off investors are likely more than happy to make, given capital appreciation alongside dividend growth.

Microsoft trades at 27x trailing earnings — described as "not an overly high valuation" for a company with significant AI-driven growth prospects. The combination of stability, reasonable valuation, rising dividends, and AI upside makes it an excellent fit in any portfolio.
