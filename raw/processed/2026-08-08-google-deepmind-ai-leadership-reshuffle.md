# The next chapter of our AI momentum

*Editor's note: Google and Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai shared changes with Google DeepMind teams, including new roles for Demis Hassabis and Koray Kavukcuoglu. Below are the messages Sundar and Demis sent to employees.*

## Message from Sundar Pichai

We've made extraordinary progress to deliver on our full AI stack. We've got amazing talent, world-class compute, and products that bring AI to more people than any other company. Our Gemini models are in high demand among developers and businesses, and the Gemini app reached 950M+ monthly users. Meanwhile, our AI research continues to drive field-defining breakthroughs, like recent Gemini Robotics advances.

**AGI and science:** Demis has described Google as standing in the foothills of the singularity and has been spending a lot of time engaging externally. Moving forward, Demis will become Chair of Google DeepMind (GDM) and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, while continuing to lead Isomorphic Labs. He will remain closely connected to Koray, Josh, and the GDM teams, advising across models and research.

**Google DeepMind:** Flash is in high demand, the Cyber model is live, and Gemma models have surpassed 900M+ downloads. Koray Kavukcuoglu, the current Chief Technology Officer of GDM and Chief AI Architect, will step up as SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting to Pichai. He will oversee Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. Koray has been at DeepMind since its early days, over 13 years, and started the deep learning team and led breakthroughs like WaveNet and DQN.

After a 27-year run at Google, Jeff Dean is launching an independent public benefit corporation with Google Senior Fellow Sanjay Ghemawat to accelerate discoveries in ML, science, and engineering. Google will continue working with them as a founding investor and Cloud partner.

## Message from Demis Hassabis

Hassabis said he has decided now is the right time to hand over day-to-day operational responsibilities at GDM to focus on the bigger picture of AGI's trajectory. He is taking on a new strategic role as Chair of GDM and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, with Koray stepping up to lead GDM as SVP.

Hassabis and Koray have worked together for over 13 years since DeepMind's early days. Hassabis said he has "total confidence" in Koray, Josh, and the rest of the GDM executive team as they continue AI development across Google, including progress on new models such as Gemini 4.

In his new role, Hassabis will continue working closely with Pichai on strategic and global AGI matters and will lean further into his role at Isomorphic Labs, where he said the company is making "extremely rapid and promising progress" toward curing diseases like cancer. He described AI's improving human health as what should be its number one application.

Hassabis noted GDM has become "the AI engine room of Google," with Gemini powering AI Mode and AI Overviews and the Gemini App reaching over 950 million monthly users.
