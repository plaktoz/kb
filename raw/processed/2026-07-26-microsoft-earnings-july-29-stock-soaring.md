# Microsoft's Next Earnings Report on July 29 Could Send the Stock Soaring

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/microsofts-next-earnings-report-on-july-29-could-send-the-stock-soaring-heres-why/

---

Author: Bram Berkowitz — The Motley Fool
Published: July 26, 2026

Microsoft (NASDAQ: MSFT) has lost nearly a fifth of its value in 2026. The stock — typically viewed as one of the safest plays in the entire stock market — has been weighed down by concerns about elevated capital expenditures on AI infrastructure and fears that AI will erode traditional software solutions.

Microsoft reports its 2026 fiscal fourth-quarter earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, July 29.

## Why the Stock Could Soar

**The AI capex concern:** The company has guided for $190 billion in capital expenditures in calendar year 2026, largely for AI infrastructure. Investors are concerned this is overspending.

**Copilot adoption gap:** Copilot had about 20 million paid enterprise seats on Microsoft's last earnings call, despite 450 million-plus Microsoft 365 subscribers — only ~4% penetration.

**Morgan Stanley bull case:** Analyst Adam Wood expects Azure (Microsoft's cloud business) to show accelerated growth over the next few quarters and Copilot adoption to pick up steam. Wood has a $795 price target as his bull case, implying more than a double from current levels (~$381).

**Azure growth:** Azure revenue has been growing at an annual rate of around 40% in recent quarters, indicating high AI investment is paying off on the cloud front.

## Valuation

Microsoft currently trades around 23x trailing earnings — well below its five-year average of roughly 32.5x. This represents an undemanding multiple for a company with Azure growing at ~40% annually.

## Key Risks

- Higher-than-expected capex guidance for next quarter
- Disappointing Azure revenue
- Copilot adoption failing to impress
- Microsoft 365 subscriber growth coming in weak

## Bottom Line

The article argues that long-term investors can buy the stock even if near-term earnings are volatile. Microsoft trades at a historically low multiple, Azure growth is strong, and the company is well-positioned to survive any AI correction.
