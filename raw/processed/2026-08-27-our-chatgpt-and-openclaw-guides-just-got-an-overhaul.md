# Our ChatGPT and OpenClaw Guides Just Got an Overhaul

The piece announces rewrites of two AI workflow guides — not because models changed, but because the surrounding infrastructure did.

## ChatGPT for Knowledge Work

The guide was retitled and restructured around ChatGPT's now-distinct interfaces: Chat for quick questions, Work for longer assignments, and Codex for software tasks. New features covered include:

- `/goal` command for persistent objectives
- Cloud vs. local project types
- Scheduled tasks and thread automations
- Browser access with signed-in profiles

## OpenClaw Guide Update

The updated guide reflects real-world maintenance challenges: expired credentials, broken integrations, and stale memory. As the article notes, "a stronger model still can't log in when a credential expires." Every is now experimenting with a shared company agent living in Slack instead. The guide helps readers decide whether maintaining a personal Claw is worth the tradeoff.

## Central Theme

The model version is only one variable. Apps, tools, permissions, and ongoing maintenance shape outcomes just as significantly.
