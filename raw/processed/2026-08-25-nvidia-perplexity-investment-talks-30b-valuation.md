# Nvidia in Talks to Invest Billions in Perplexity at Over $30B Valuation

Nvidia is negotiating a multi-billion dollar equity investment in Perplexity, the AI search startup. If the deal closes, Perplexity's valuation would top $30 billion, more than 50 percent above its funding round from a year ago. Perplexity's annual recurring revenue has also roughly tripled this year, from under $250 million to over $750 million.

Nvidia has already backed Groq, Poolside, and Enfabrica in similar deals. On the same day, Hugging Face was reported to be in acquisition talks at a $13 billion valuation — a company that once turned down a $500 million investment offer from Nvidia itself.

Perplexity is already part of Nvidia's Nemotron Coalition, a group co-developing hardware and software. Beyond simply selling chips, Nvidia is extending its reach by directly bankrolling the companies that use them.

Separately, companies are splitting on who should own the underlying AI model. Thomson Reuters spent $40 million over two years building its own legal AI on top of Alibaba's open Qwen weights rather than paying OpenAI or Anthropic by the month. OpenAI, meanwhile, is trying to push its coding tool Codex out to accountants and doctors, though usage among individual subscribers still sits under 1 percent versus 98 percent internally.
