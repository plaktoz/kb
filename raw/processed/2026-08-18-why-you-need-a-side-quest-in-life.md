# Why You Need a Side Quest in Life

**Author:** Sahil Bloom
**Date:** August 18, 2026
**Source:** Sahil Bloom Newsletter

Bloom opens with a professor's observation that students keep the same seat all term — a metaphor for how early choices lock us into life paths.

He introduces **"life gravity"** in two forms:

- **External gravity**: visible sunk costs — salary, reputation, identity — that make leaving feel impossible
- **Internal gravity**: the more insidious force; you stop *seeing* alternatives altogether

He cites a 2013 Harvard/Brigham study where 83% of radiologists missed a gorilla on a CT scan — illustrating **inattentional blindness**: prolonged focus on one thing blinds you to everything else.

His solution: **side quests** — pursuits outside your two main quests (career and personal/family life). Examples: hobbies, art, physical challenges, learning. These are *atelic* — done without a goal.

Bloom shares his own story: starting to write during the 2020 pandemic as a casual exercise, which eventually became his newsletter and career.

His closing argument: side quests matter not because they'll change your life — *"though they might"* — but because they restore awareness that *"being trapped by life gravity is optional."*
