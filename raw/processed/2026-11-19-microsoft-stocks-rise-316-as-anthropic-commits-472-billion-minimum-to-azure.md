# Microsoft Stocks Rise 3.16% as Anthropic Commits $47.2 Billion Minimum to Azure

Microsoft (NASDAQ:MSFT) shares rose 3.16% to $524.36 at 9:45 a.m. ET on November 19 in regular trading, as Anthropic's final prospectus confirmed that Microsoft will buy 12,048,192 Class N shares for about $1.0 billion at the $83 IPO price. The same filing details Anthropic's non-cancelable Azure deal, amended between February and November 2026.

Under that agreement, Anthropic committed to spend at least $47.2 billion and up to $49.2 billion with Microsoft between November 2026 and June 2033, regardless of actual usage. That compares with floors of about $121.3 billion for Google and $110.0 billion for Amazon. Claude is also sold through Azure, so Microsoft earns from both compute and distribution.

Over roughly six and a half years, the Azure floor averages about $7.2 billion to $7.5 billion a year, real revenue but a fraction of Microsoft's cloud base. The equity stake itself is tiny next to a $3.89 trillion market value. GuruFocus awards a perfect GF Score of 100/100, combining top profitability, strong finances, growth and momentum. Shares trade about 13% below the GF Value of $603.85, a modest cushion for a business of this quality, though a model value is not a promise of upside. The next marker is Microsoft's January quarterly report and whether Azure growth reflects the new floor.
