# STI Edges Higher At Open With Banks In Focus

Singapore stocks opened slightly higher on Tuesday, with the Straits Times Index (STI) rising 2.91 points or 0.05% to 5,731.93 as of 9.06am.

Market breadth was positive, with 84 gainers against 78 decliners. Trading volume stood at 48.73 million shares worth S$102.48 million.

Among STI constituents, DBS rose 0.22% to S$78.28, while UOB and OCBC Bank were also among the active counters in early trade. Yangzijiang Shipbuilding traded at S$5.18 while City Developments was at S$7.52.

Hongkong Land was at US$8.30 while Geo Energy Resources traded at S$0.615.

In the REIT segment, CapitaLand Ascendas REIT fell 0.44% to S$2.28. CapitaLand Integrated Commercial Trust traded at S$2.23, while Keppel DC REIT stood at S$2.11.

In derivatives, FTSE China A50 Index futures stood at 14,022 as of 8.47am, while Nikkei 225 Index futures traded at 65,485.

SGX FTSE Taiwan Index futures stood at 64,250, while MSCI Singapore Index futures were at 532.35.

The STI tracks the performance of the 30 largest companies listed on the Singapore Exchange by market capitalisation.
