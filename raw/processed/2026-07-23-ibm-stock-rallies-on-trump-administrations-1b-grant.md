# IBM Stock Rallies On Trump Administration's $1B Grant: Analysts Say 'Big Blue' Could Become First US Quantum Foundry

Stocktwits — July 2026

International Business Machines Corp. shares jumped 12.4% on Thursday — its best day in over a year — and gained another 4.6% in overnight trading, after the U.S. government announced a proposed $1 billion grant to support IBM's new quantum computing venture, expected to become a major domestic manufacturer of quantum chips for IBM and other customers.

## IBM's quantum chip foundry

IBM announced the launch of Anderon, its quantum chip foundry venture, and said it would match the government funding with an additional $1 billion investment plus intellectual property, assets, and a skilled workforce. Headquartered in Albany, New York, Anderon will operate as a 300-millimeter quantum wafer foundry that IBM says will "help the nation solidify its leadership at the center of a thriving new quantum industry... estimated to generate up to $850 billion in economic value by 2040."

The Department of Commerce announced a $2 billion package overall to support the quantum computing industry, which President Trump has described as critical to national security and to maintaining the U.S.'s technological edge over China. IBM was the biggest recipient; smaller grants went to Rigetti Computing, D-Wave Quantum, and Infleqtion, whose shares also jumped.

## Analysts see a big quantum opportunity for IBM

"IBM wants to turn its captive quantum fab into an industry fab," said Patrick Moorhead, Chief Analyst at Moor Insights, noting Anderon isn't greenfield — IBM has been fabricating its Loon and Nighthawk quantum processors on 300mm at NY CREATES Albany since November 2025, and Anderon is "the corporate spinout, capitalized for merchant scale."

Wedbush said the government funding "will act as a new catalyst to the industry with IBM at the helm," even though the long-term timeline for quantum computing capabilities hasn't changed. The firm reiterated a Buy rating with a $225 price target — about 11% below the stock's last close at the time.

On Stocktwits, retail sentiment on IBM shifted to "extremely bullish" from "bearish" the previous day. Despite the rally, IBM shares remained down 14% year-to-date.
