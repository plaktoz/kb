# Major updates for Apple's software platforms are now available

Apple released iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, and tvOS 27 on September 14, 2026, introducing several major feature categories.

## Siri AI

A rebuilt assistant with personal context awareness, onscreen understanding, and broad web knowledge. It launches as an English beta, with five additional languages arriving in October. A dedicated Siri app syncs conversation history across devices via iCloud. New writing assistance lets users generate or refine drafts by description.

## Apple Intelligence Enhancements

Photos gains Spatial Reframing, Extend, and an upgraded Clean Up tool. Image Playground now supports photorealistic image generation. Safari adds automatic tab organization, website change alerts ("Notify Me"), and AI-described custom extensions. Call Context surfaces relevant details during business calls.

## Child Safety & Screen Time

Parents gain streamlined child-account setup with age-based safeguards. "Ask to Browse" requires parental approval before kids visit new Safari sites. Communication Safety now blocks gore and violent content in shared media. Screen Time was redesigned with usage summaries, time-category allowances, and daily app schedules.

## Performance & Platform Improvements

- Apps launch "up to 30 percent faster" on iPhone/iPad
- Photos load "up to 70 percent faster" after capture
- AirDrop transfers "up to 80 percent faster"
- Apple Vision Pro connects to Wi-Fi "up to 3x faster"
- Liquid Glass UI now features a user-adjustable tint slider

## Additional Features

iCloud Shared Albums now support full-resolution sharing. Health gains perimenopause/menopause cycle tracking. AirPods add custom EQ. Apple Watch gains single-handed widget opening and offline Workout Buddy. Apple TV adds lossless audio for Apple Music.
