# Microsoft President Brad Smith: AI's Power Is Advancing Faster Than Its Safety

Microsoft Vice Chair and President Brad Smith, speaking with WSJ's Alan Murray, warned that AI's capabilities are outpacing safety development.

**Core concern:** "The science that is creating more powerful AI models [is] advancing much faster than the science that aligns those models with human values."

## On Safety & Regulation

- Smith called for mandatory "emergency brakes" in AI systems, especially for critical infrastructure
- Compared AI governance to airline and food safety regulations developed over decades
- Supports independent safety evaluations before frontier model releases
- Acknowledged product liability law already obligates companies not to knowingly ship unsafe products

## On Human Extinction Risk

Smith said he doesn't believe there's a 10% extinction risk, but added: "I'd rather have us worry too much than do too little."

## On Business Applications

Smith highlighted positive uses including faster wildfire detection and AI-assisted medical documentation, while warning the technology gap between global North (29% usage) and South (16%) could worsen inequality.

## On Workforce

Smith advocates for AI as a tool that elevates workers from repetitive tasks, potentially reviving the "generalist" professional — someone empowered by AI to tackle broader problems rather than remaining narrowly specialized.
