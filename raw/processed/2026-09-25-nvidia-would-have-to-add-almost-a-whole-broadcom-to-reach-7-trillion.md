# Nvidia Would Have to Add Almost a Whole Broadcom to Reach $7 Trillion

Nvidia's market cap sits around $5.45 trillion, leaving roughly a $1.55 trillion gap to reach $7 trillion — comparable to nearly Broadcom's entire market value of roughly $1.69 trillion.

At roughly $225 per share, reaching $7 trillion requires roughly $290 per share, a roughly 28% gain — unremarkable given the stock already climbed 37% from its 52-week low.

Recent results for Q2 FY2027 showed revenue hitting $96.2 billion, up 106% year-over-year, with net income of $59.7 billion, up 126%. Q3 guidance is $108 billion. CFO Colette Kress indicated roughly 70% revenue growth expected in FY2028, describing it as "a supply-constrained outlook."

Analyst consensus puts FY2027 revenue near $412 billion; applying roughly 70% growth yields roughly $700 billion for FY2028. Gross margins are expected to settle around 72–73%.

At current roughly 14x forward earnings, hitting $290 per share would imply roughly 18x FY2028 earnings — roughly what Broadcom trades at today. The article notes the earnings forecast is already in place; the unknown variable is what multiple the market will assign.
