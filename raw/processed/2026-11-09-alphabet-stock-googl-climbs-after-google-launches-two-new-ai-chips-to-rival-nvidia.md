# Alphabet Stock (GOOGL) Climbs after Google Launches Two New AI Chips to Rival Nvidia

Alphabet's stock (GOOGL) climbed in early trading on Monday after Google introduced two new versions of its custom AI chips. The launch marks a direct step up in its fight against Nvidia (NVDA), the market leader in AI chips. The new chips, called the TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, are updated versions of older chips built for specific tasks.

## Google Creates Two New Chips for Data Security and Complex AI Tasks

Google's chief technologist for AI infrastructure, Amin Vahdat, told *Bloomberg* that the 8tx and 8i Max are reconfigured setups made to handle jobs that cloud customers have been requesting.

The 8tx chip is made for government agencies and companies in strict industries like banking and healthcare. These buyers need to keep private data locked down while AI models work on it. The system pairs Google's custom chip tech with processors from AMD (AMD) to lock data directly on the hardware. Google said the 8tx runs up to twice as fast as the prior version without losing speed from the added security.

The 8i Max is built for AI tools that need to read and handle huge amounts of information all at once. Google combined two chips into a single package to give users more memory and computing power. This makes the 8i Max a strong fit for math problems and drug research.

## Google's Cloud Sales Double as Buyers Rent Custom Chips

For years, Google mostly kept its tensor processing units in-house to run its own services like Search, YouTube, and Gemini. Now, the company rents those chips to outside businesses through Google Cloud to bring in more AI revenue.

That shift is already paying off. In Alphabet's third-quarter earnings report on October 28, total company revenue rose 25% to $127.6 billion. Google Cloud revenue more than doubled, jumping 103% to $30.71 billion, with cloud operating income reaching $11.12 billion.

Alphabet noted that strong customer demand for its custom TPU systems played a key role in that third-quarter cloud growth. To keep up with enterprise demand, Alphabet spent $51.4 billion on capital projects in the third quarter alone and expects full-year 2026 spending to land between $195 billion and $205 billion.

## Is Google Stock a Strong Buy Right Now?

Turning to the Street, Alphabet's stock (GOOGL) carries a Strong Buy consensus based on 29 Buy and four Hold ratings assigned in the past three months. The average 12-month GOOGL stock price target is $398.30, which implies 35.6% upside from the current price.
