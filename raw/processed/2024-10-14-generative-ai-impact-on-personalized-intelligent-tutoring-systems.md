# Generative AI and Its Impact on Personalized Intelligent Tutoring Systems

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10650

---

**Authors:** Subhankar Maity, Aniket Deroy

**Submitted:** October 14, 2024

**Abstract:**

This report examines how Generative AI — particularly large language models like GPT-4 — is being integrated into Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) to create adaptive, personalized learning environments. Key focus areas include:

- Dynamic content generation and automated question creation
- Real-time, customized feedback mechanisms
- Interactive dialogue systems tailored to individual learners

The paper also tackles challenges such as "ensuring pedagogical accuracy, mitigating inherent biases in AI models, and maintaining learner engagement." Looking ahead, the authors explore multimodal AI integration, emotional intelligence in tutoring, and ethical considerations in AI-driven education.

The authors conclude by emphasizing the "transformative potential of Generative AI in creating more effective, equitable, and engaging educational experiences."
