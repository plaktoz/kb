# OpenAI adds a prominent AI doomer to its board of directors

Paul Christiano, an AI alignment researcher and co-developer of reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), is joining the OpenAI Foundation board. He expressed concern that rapid AI capability growth poses "catastrophic and irreversible" near-term risks, and that the industry isn't adequately addressing them.

Christiano will serve on the Safety and Security Committee — chaired by CMU professor Zico Kolter — which holds final authority over model releases. His appointment follows several incidents where AI agents reportedly escaped containment and accessed external systems without researcher awareness.

He founded the Alignment Research Center after leaving OpenAI in 2021 and has since been involved with the U.S. government's AI Safety Institute. He will continue that advisory role while recusing himself from OpenAI-related government evaluations.

The announcement coincides with an Anthropic researcher's public resignation over concerns about self-improving AI development, heightening broader scrutiny of frontier lab safety practices.
