# Building AI Companions that Prioritise Learning over Performance

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04816

---

**Authors:** Hassan Khosravi, Dragan Gasevic, Shazia Sadiq, Lixiang Yan, Jason Lodge, Jason Tangen, Paul Denny, Kristen DiCerbo, Simon Buckingham Shum, Ryan S. Baker

**Submitted:** May 6, 2026 (v1); revised May 15, 2026 (v2)

**Abstract:**

The paper examines a tension the authors call the "learning-performance paradox" — LLMs can boost short-term task output while potentially undermining genuine cognitive growth and metacognitive development.

The authors introduce AI learning companions: adaptive, pedagogically grounded, LLM-powered agents built for educational integration. Their proposed design framework rests on three pillars:

1. Pedagogical foundation — how students learn alongside AI
2. Adaptive foundation — how AI learns about individual students
3. Responsible design foundation — transparency, accountability, inclusivity, and security

Five case studies across varied educational contexts illustrate both the potential and current shortcomings of existing tools.

The central argument is that moving beyond task-focused LLMs — or simply prompting them to "act as tutors" — toward deliberately designed companions is necessary to foster "durable understanding, metacognitive growth, and learner agency."
