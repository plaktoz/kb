# Veeam founder backs Worker.ai, which buys voice AI startup DeltaGen

What's the deal? Worker.ai has acquired DeltaGen, a Prague-based startup that builds real-time voice AI, for an undisclosed sum. The deal was announced on November 12, 2026, alongside Worker's launch of its company operating system for small businesses, backed by Veeam founder Ratmir Timashev.

What's the endgame? Worker puts a company's plan, responsibilities, key numbers, and processes in one place, where employees and AI workers share the day-to-day work. DeltaGen's technology listens to conversations as they happen and picks out decisions, commitments, and next steps, which Worker then turns into assigned tasks.

Why now? The acquisition grew from a personal connection: Worker co-founder and chief executive officer John Roberts, a former private equity operator, had used DeltaGen's technology at a previous cybersecurity job. All three DeltaGen founders are joining — René Bystroň as co-founder and chief operating officer, with Michal Studnička on product and Matúš Ondreička leading technical development.

What about the expansion? DeltaGen's team of around 10 people stays in Prague, and Worker plans to grow its product and engineering team in the city, one of the region's most active startup hubs. DeltaGen, launched in 2024, previously raised a $1.2 million pre-seed round and had backing from Dutch firm Forward Venture Capital and Techstars.

What's driving the bet? Timashev is backing Worker to help small businesses such as manufacturers, service companies, and family firms grow. "AI can now give small businesses more capacity without asking already stretched owners to work longer days," he said. "Worker is my bet on that shift."

The signal: The deal lands as larger software platforms absorb conversational AI — Salesforce recently agreed to buy voice-AI company Cresta. Unlike Cresta's enterprise customer-service focus, DeltaGen captures decisions from everyday business conversations, and with terms undisclosed, the significance here is strategic: a new entrant buying technology and talent to build out a small-business platform.
