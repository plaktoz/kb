# Fed Minutes; U.S. Debt Hits $40 Trillion - What's Moving Markets

Futures linked to the main U.S. stock indices point slightly higher after a move by the U.S. Treasury to shore up debt markets bolstered equities in the prior session. Minutes from the Federal Reserve's July meeting show that many officials backed an interest rate hike. Walmart was set to report earnings this week, while President Trump warned of economic consequences for supporting Iran, as the U.S. debt pile surpassed an ominous milestone.

## Futures inch higher

U.S. stock futures edged up on Thursday following an upbeat session for equities fueled by a rally in government bonds. The main averages on Wall Street rose on Wednesday, buoyed in part by a move by the U.S. Treasury to stem a rout in the debt market that had pushed the U.S. 30-year bond yield up to its highest level in nearly two decades. Shortly before the start of trading, the department said it would double buyback sizes for long-term debt.

Global bond yields sank, dragging down the dollar and boosting gold. Earlier this week, a debt market selloff had weighed on stocks, as concerns mounted around runaway artificial intelligence spending, fiscal deficits and elevated oil prices.

## Fed minutes in focus

Minutes from the Federal Reserve's latest meeting showed that more officials backed an interest rate hike last month. The Fed stood pat on borrowing costs at the July gathering, although three of the 12 voting members of the rate-setting Federal Open Market Committee supported a quarter-point increase. "Many" of the 19 total FOMC members also felt that policy tightening would likely be warranted should inflation not decline.

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has previously stressed a desire to corral inflation, but left investors flummoxed after he suggested that a rise in market interest rates since the Fed's June meeting had effectively tightened financial conditions without a formal hike.

## Walmart earnings ahead

Big-box retail giant Walmart was due to highlight Thursday's batch of quarterly results, with analysts watching its advertising unit, Walmart Connect, which grew 44% in the quarter ended April 30 — its fastest-ever recorded pace.

## Trump warns of economic consequences for Iran support

President Trump warned of "TREMENDOUS Economic Consequences" for any country engaging in business with Iran. "I am announcing the MOST CRUSHING ECONOMIC OPERATION EVER TAKEN AGAINST ANY COUNTRY!" Trump said in a social media post. Brent crude prices have risen by more than 5% this week amid fears of prolonged supply constraints.

## U.S. debt crosses $40 trillion

The U.S. gross national debt has exceeded $40 trillion for the first time, roughly doubling under the tenures of Trump and former President Joe Biden, the Treasury Department said. The latest daily cash and debt balances statement showed total public debt outstanding at $40.047 trillion. "While White House officials pay lip service to addressing fiscal imbalances, their actions suggest a complete disregard for any type of soberness on this issue," analysts at Vital Knowledge said in a note.
