# 10-Year Treasuries Yield About 4.8%. Here Are 3 High-Yielding Dividend Stocks That Actually Beat That.

With 10-year Treasury yields hovering near 4.8% — recently hitting a high not seen since November 2023 — income investors may want to consider dividend stocks offering even greater returns. Three candidates stand out:

## 1. AGNC Investment (NASDAQ: AGNC)

**Yield: ~13.5%**

AGNC is a mortgage REIT holding a leveraged portfolio of agency-backed mortgage-backed securities. Because these are government-backed, default risk is minimal, though interest rate movements and spread changes affect portfolio value. MBS spreads currently sit around 2 percentage points — historically elevated — and conditions (Fed buybacks, reduced net supply) could push spreads narrower, benefiting AGNC.

## 2. Energy Transfer (NYSE: ET)

**Yield: ~6.3%**

Energy Transfer operates one of the largest midstream pipeline networks in the U.S. Growth drivers include AI data center energy demand, rising electricity needs, and NGL export demand. The distribution is covered at a 2.2x ratio, roughly 90% of adjusted EBITDA comes from fee-based contracts, and the company plans 3–5% annual distribution growth.

## 3. Verizon Communications (NYSE: VZ)

**Yield: ~5.6%**

Verizon's customer-focused strategy has reduced churn and boosted subscriber additions — 184,000 postpaid phone net adds last quarter, its best in five years. The Frontier acquisition adds significant fiber network capacity. Trading at a forward P/E of roughly 9.5x on 2027 estimates, the dividend is well-supported by free cash flow.
