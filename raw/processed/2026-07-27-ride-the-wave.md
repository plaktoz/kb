# Ride the Wave

source_url: https://fs.blog/ride-wave/

---

Drawing on Charlie Munger's concept of "competitive destruction," this piece argues that industries are repeatedly disrupted by new waves of innovation. Kodak's failure to embrace digital photography — despite inventing it internally in 1975 — illustrates the cost of clinging to outdated models.

Munger describes the pattern: established industries collapse when new technology arrives, and "when these new businesses come in, there are huge advantages for the early birds." Those who catch the wave early can sustain momentum for a long time; those who don't get "mired in shallows."

Success requires three things:
- **Vision** to spot emerging waves early
- **Courage** to move before others see the opportunity
- **Skill** to time your entry correctly

Missing or abandoning waves doesn't just mean falling behind — it means watching others compound advantages while you stagnate on the sidelines.
