# Microsoft Stocks Move Lower 0.80% as AI Rotation Swings Back to Chipmakers

Microsoft (NASDAQ:MSFT) shares eased 0.80% to $531.64 at 10:10 a.m. ET on Friday, November 6, as money flowed back into the chipmakers that were crushed on Thursday. Earlier in the week, the trade ran the other way. On November 4, Microsoft jumped 6.6% to a record close of $555.80 as investors bought AI spenders and sold AI suppliers.

The fundamentals behind that rally are strong. Microsoft's fiscal first-quarter revenue rose 19% to $92.3 billion, Azure growth accelerated to 48%, and diluted EPS reached $4.92, up 32%, according to RuntimeWire's summary of the October 28 release. Revenue and EPS both beat Wall Street expectations.

The GuruFocus chart shows a GF Score of 99/100, with Profitability and Momentum at 10/10, GF Value at 9/10 and Financial Strength at 8/10. Shares trade about 11% below a GF Value of $599.97 at roughly 28 times earnings, so the model sees room, not a guaranteed gain. The test is whether Azure growth holds in the high 40s if data center construction slows.
