# Stock futures are little changed ahead of Wednesday's big inflation report

U.S. stock futures were little changed Tuesday night, as traders looked ahead to an important inflation report that will likely determine the near-term direction for interest rates. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures rose 16 points (0.03%), while S&P 500 futures and Nasdaq 100 futures climbed 0.07% and 0.1% respectively.

Super Micro Computer shares rallied more than 6% after the data center infrastructure company issued upbeat guidance for first-quarter earnings and revenue. CoreWeave shares surged 14% after second-quarter adjusted operating income margin of 5% exceeded expectations.

These moves come ahead of the July consumer price index, due out at 8:30 a.m. ET Wednesday, expected to show only a slight monthly inflation increase — 0.1% headline, 0.2% core — according to economists polled by Dow Jones. The estimated 12-month rates of 3.4% and 2.5% remain above the Fed's 2% target.

A surprisingly hot inflation report would likely alarm investors who expect it to shape the Federal Reserve's September meeting decision, with three dissenters at the last meeting having voted to raise rates. Concerns persist as U.S. oil prices push above $83 a barrel amid dwindling hopes for a Strait of Hormuz reopening.

"All the Fed seems to be focused on is inflation, which means that tomorrow's report is very important, especially because right now the odds for a September hike are 50-50," said Liz Thomas, head of investment strategy at SoFi. "Tomorrow pushes that in one direction or the other, and that gets baked in."

The bond market will also be closely watched, with the 10-year Treasury yield hovering around 4.7% and the 2-year yield around 4.2%, both already reflecting fears of a more restrictive rate regime. Wednesday's inflation report won't be the market's last hurdle this week — Thursday's July producer price index will need to confirm the prior month's softer reading can be repeated.
