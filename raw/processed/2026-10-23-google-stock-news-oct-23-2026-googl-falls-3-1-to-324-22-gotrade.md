## How GOOGL Traded in the Session

GOOGL opened at $330.46, below the previous close of $334.76, and traded between $322.00 and $331.43 before finishing just $2.22 above the day's low. Volume reached 27.7 million shares, 9.27% above its average of 25.4 million shares.

The drop was 0.55 percentage points steeper than the Nasdaq Composite, which fell 2.60% to 26,008.86. The S&P 500 lost 1.21% to 7,663.43, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average edged up 0.02% to 52,867.98.

Large tech peers fell alongside it. Meta Platforms (META) dropped 6.98%, Amazon (AMZN) lost 4.57% and Microsoft (MSFT) slipped 2.23%. After the session, GOOGL sits 20.65% below its 52-week high of $408.61.

## Why Google Stock Fell

No source we reviewed tied Thursday's decline to an Alphabet-specific announcement; the stock moved with the wider tech sell-off. According to Yahoo Finance, worries over AI spending and the cost of capital resurfaced on October 22 and hit semiconductor stocks and hyperscaler shares, the large cloud-computing providers.

Yahoo Finance also reported that Meta's drop followed its quarterly results and a 2-for-1 stock split announcement, while CoreWeave sank as worries over debt-heavy balance sheets returned.

Investors moved into bonds and sent Treasury yields lower, per Yahoo Finance, as Middle East tensions lifted oil. The US 10-year Treasury yield ended the session at 4.95%, down 19.3 basis points, and front-month Brent futures settled at $113.17 a barrel.

Two Alphabet stories published the same day dealt with compute capacity. According to TIKR, CEO Sundar Pichai said in an October 20 interview on the Big Technology podcast that Google Cloud is giving up short-term revenue so frontier AI research gets first claim on computing power, calling Google DeepMind's compute needs "non-negotiable." Separately, The Motley Fool's Leo Sun wrote on October 22 that Alphabet reportedly began paying SpaceX $920 million a month, or $11 billion a year, to lease xAI's cloud infrastructure through 2029, covering a cluster of roughly 110,000 GPUs.

## GOOGL Stock Analysis: Supporting Factors and Risks

## Supporting Factors

Google Cloud grew 82% in the second quarter, a figure that included TPU system sales, according to TIKR.

Per The Motley Fool, Google Cloud's annual revenue rose from $19.2 billion in 2021 to $58.7 billion in 2025, and Synergy Research Group put Google's share of the global cloud infrastructure market at 15% in the second quarter of 2026.

## Risks

Pichai said serving new customers asking for 2027 capacity would be "incredibly difficult," and TIKR noted he did not say how much Cloud revenue is being given up.

Leo Sun flagged concentration risk in the reported SpaceX deal: the cluster sits at xAI's centralized facilities, including its Colossus clusters in Tennessee, and he warned that "any power failures or disruptions could impact a large portion of its cloud-based tenants." In his view, the deal also helps fund xAI into a future cloud competitor.

Yahoo Finance reported that worries over AI spending and the cost of capital weighed on chip and hyperscaler shares on October 22.

Alphabet's next scheduled event is its third-quarter earnings report, which TIKR said is due after the close on October 28, 2026.
