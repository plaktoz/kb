# Discouraged? Here's How to Stay Motivated

source_url: https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2026/07/30/discouraged-heres-how-to-stay-motivated/

---

Author: Scott H. Young
Published: July 30, 2026

Young responds to a reader struggling to maintain motivation after repeated setbacks — failed exams, rejected job applications, persistent failure.

## Quitting is sometimes rational

Humans often persist too long due to sunk-cost bias. Before trying to boost motivation, honestly assess: should you keep going?

## The cost-benefit framework

Evaluate three factors when deciding to persist:

- Realistic probability of success
- Required time/effort investment
- Quality of alternative options

## Rebuilding motivation

Young rejects affirmations and hype. His core insight: "the key to rebuilding motivation is to have a clear plan that you honestly believe will work."

## Belief in the plan matters

Motivation erodes when plans contain steps requiring miracles. A credible, step-by-step path — even one with imperfect odds — sustains effort better than willpower alone.

## Exploratory fallback

When no clear plan exists, adopt an experimental mindset: test approaches specifically to discover a viable path forward.

## Core thesis

Persistence is less a willpower problem than a strategic one — rational assessment plus a believable plan generates the emotional fuel needed to continue.
