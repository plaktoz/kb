# Phil Schiller's App Store Exit Reportedly Driven by Wariness Over Future Plans

Phil Schiller is stepping down from leading the App Store, and Bloomberg's Mark Gurman sheds light on the reasoning. While family time and philanthropy were factors, Schiller also had reservations about the direction new CEO John Ternus and services chief Eddy Cue intend to take the App Store — specifically, efforts to boost margins and generate more recurring revenue.

No major internal conflict occurred, but Schiller reportedly felt that pushing for greater profits would escalate tensions with developers and regulators, leading him to exit rather than participate in that strategy. He will stay on at Apple as a Fellow, working on unspecified projects.
