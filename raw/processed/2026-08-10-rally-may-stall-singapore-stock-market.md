# Rally May Stall For Singapore Stock Market

Ahead of Monday's holiday for National Day, the Singapore stock market had moved higher in back-to-back sessions, gathering almost 120 points (about 2 percent) along the way. The Straits Times Index (tracked by the SPDR STI ETF, SGX: ES3) sits just beneath the 5,700-point plateau, though it may run out of steam on Tuesday.

The global forecast for Asian markets is weak on continuing concerns over the Middle East conflict and the resulting spike in crude oil prices. European markets were mixed and flat, and U.S. bourses were slightly soft, with the Asian markets figuring to split the difference.

The STI finished sharply higher on Friday on gains from financial shares and properties, while industrials were mixed. For the day, the index climbed 59.44 points (1.05 percent) to close at 5,698.43, trading between 5,644.56 and 5,700.07. The lead from Wall Street was soft, as major U.S. averages hugged the line for much of the day before fading midday and ending in the red.
