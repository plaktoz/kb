# Stock Market: Will S&P 500 Open Up or Down Today?

U.S. stock futures are sliding early Wednesday as Wall Street braces for a packed pre-Thanksgiving economic data dump—headlined by the Fed's preferred PCE inflation gauge—alongside intensifying political and regulatory scrutiny surrounding Nvidia Corp. (NASDAQ:NVDA) CEO Jensen Huang's reinstatement.

Polymarket traders, a Polygon (CRYPTO: POL)– based prediction platform, have turned distinctly bearish for the Nov. 25 trading session. The "S&P 500 (SPX) Up or Down on November 25?" contract currently reflects a 38% chance of a higher open.

## Why That Number Matters

Traders are balancing broad pre-market equity weakness against sliding energy prices and major macroeconomic releases:

Negative Index Futures: Equity futures were in the red across all major benchmarks, led by tech. Nasdaq 100 futures dropped 1.20%, and S&P 500 futures fell 0.73%. Russell 2000 futures slipped 0.50%, while Dow Jones futures declined 0.44%.

Nvidia & Political Scrutiny: Nvidia shares fell 5.86% on Tuesday and shed another 2.21% in after-hours trading as political heat escalates over Huang's return. Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer (D-N.Y.) demanded congressional investigations into whether Chinese President Xi Jinping pressured President Donald Trump during talks last week and questioned potential conflicts of interest regarding Trump's Nvidia holdings. Meanwhile, Trump said a pardon for Huang is "always an option" ahead of his upcoming congressional testimony, while the SEC is reportedly scrutinizing Nvidia's investor disclosures.

Sliding Oil Prices: Energy markets continue to pull back sharply ahead of the holiday weekend; Brent crude fell 1.57% to $87.39 a barrel, while WTI crude dropped 1.76% to $80.61 a barrel.

PCE Inflation, GDP & Earnings: Wednesday's massive 8:30 a.m. ET economic slate includes the third-quarter second estimate GDP, weekly initial jobless claims, October durable goods orders, personal income, consumer spending, and both headline and core PCE price index data. The light pre-holiday corporate calendar is headlined by Deere & Co. (NYSE:DE), Li Auto Inc. (NASDAQ:LI), and Kanzhun Ltd. (NASDAQ:BZ).

## The Bull Case and Market Outlook

Wharton Emeritus Professor and WisdomTree Senior Economist Jeremy Siegel argues that the market is overlooking a major tailwind: sharply falling energy prices. Siegel notes consumers are gaining significant purchasing power heading into the holiday shopping season.

While markets were spooked last week by Fed Governor Christopher Waller floating a 50-basis-point rate hike in December, Siegel does not expect the Fed to follow through, contending that declining oil prices will cool headline inflation and ease tightening pressure.

Addressing the tech selloff, Siegel acknowledges investors are reassessing whether massive AI infrastructure spending will justify lofty valuations as token prices fall, drawing parallels to the early internet's fiber-optic boom.

Still, with resilient economic fundamentals, he expects value stocks to regain relative strength and remains constructive on equities heading into 2027.
