# Satya Nadella Has Delivered a More Than 10X Return for Microsoft Shareholders Since Becoming CEO in 2014, a 30% Annualized Growth Rate. Is That Pace Still Achievable From Here?

Since taking over from Steve Ballmer in February 2014, Nadella has overseen a dividend-adjusted total return of roughly 1,570%, representing a ~30% compound annual growth rate.

## Context at the time of appointment

When Nadella assumed leadership, Microsoft faced real headwinds:

- The company had largely missed the mobile revolution
- Windows and Office were stagnating
- Growth bets were failing
- Market cap was around $300 billion
- Stock had fallen out of favor

## The cloud-first pivot

Nadella's cloud-first pivot — particularly Azure's expansion and AI positioning — reinvigorated the business. Today Microsoft's market cap sits near $3.7 trillion, ranking it among the world's largest companies.

## Can the pace be replicated?

The article argues that while Microsoft remains strong, replicating that ~30% annualized return is unlikely:

- Part of the earlier gains reflected a depressed starting valuation
- At current scale, the law of large numbers makes sustained rapid growth much harder
- The author suggests Microsoft can likely remain a "market-beating investment," but investors should temper expectations accordingly
