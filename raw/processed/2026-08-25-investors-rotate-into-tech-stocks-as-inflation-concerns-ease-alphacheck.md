# Investors rotate into tech stocks as inflation concerns ease: AlphaCheck

Markets opened on a positive note Tuesday, with declining oil prices and Treasury yields boosting sentiment amid ongoing concerns about US-Iran and US-Canada tensions.

Tech (XLK) stocks rebounded after Monday losses, with broad gains across semiconductors, software, and hardware. Nvidia (NVDA) climbed roughly 2%, potentially ending "a seven-day losing streak" ahead of its earnings report.

Meanwhile, Consumer Staples (XLP) saw selling pressure — a reversal of Monday's pattern. Easing inflation jitters are driving a rotation toward growth and technology.

Notable stocks drawing reader interest: Sandisk (SNDK), Marvell (MRVL), Intel (INTC), SK Hynix (SKHY), AMD, Bloom Energy (BE), and Lam Research (LRCX).
