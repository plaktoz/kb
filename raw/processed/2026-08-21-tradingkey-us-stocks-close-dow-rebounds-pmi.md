# US Stocks Close: Dow Strongly Rebounds 500 Points; Financial and Gold Stocks Lead; SpaceX Rises 5%

Despite rising US Treasury yields, the US August Composite PMI hit a four-year high, easing fears of an economic slowdown and driving a stock market rebound led by financial and gold shares. At the close: Dow Jones Industrial Average +0.98% to 53,277.01; Nasdaq Composite +0.43% to 26,180.45; S&P 500 +0.43% to 7,674.37.

**Tech stocks:** Tesla (TSLA) rose 5.14% to $362.86 after The Information reported Tesla plans to open public test rides for its steering-wheel-less, pedal-less Cybercab by month-end and connect it to the Austin Robotaxi network. Also higher: SpaceX (SPCX) +2.22%, Google (GOOGL) +1.22%, Broadcom (AVGO) +1.21%, Meta (META) +0.75%, Microsoft (MSFT) +0.43%. Decliners: Nvidia (NVDA) -0.97%, Apple (AAPL) -0.63%, Amazon (AMZN) -0.57%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 0.51%, with Marvell (MRVL) -5.57%, Arm (ARM) -2.95%, Intel (INTC) -2.24%.

**Company news:** Broadcom is reportedly in talks for $70–80 billion in debt financing (about $45B senior / $35B subordinated tranches) to support AI companies including Anthropic in procuring chips and infrastructure — a deal that could reach $100B total with other financing included, per Bloomberg. Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs across Siri and Vision Pro teams (about 100 each) to shift resources toward AI and new devices, largely shutting down a Vision Pro gaming team and downsizing immersive-video production. Nvidia is reportedly in advanced talks to invest hundreds of millions in Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a data-center power developer with 10+ gigawatts of pipeline capacity, to help secure future data-center capacity for its chips.

**Macro:** President Trump publicly backed AI data center construction, calling it a major source of construction jobs, capital, and tax revenue, while noting the industry "needs a little PR help" given public opposition to local data centers. Bridgewater's Ray Dalio warned of a potential US debt crisis within three years and recommended investors reduce bond holdings and allocate 10–15% of portfolios to gold to lower risk and enhance returns. Separately, Omani and Iranian foreign ministers discussed resuming talks on the Strait of Hormuz to help restore freedom of navigation and regional stability.
