# What's Going On With Microsoft Stock Today?

Microsoft Corp. MSFT stock fell more than 3% Wednesday as concerns over AI spending, U.S. inflation and President Donald Trump’s proposed tariffs pressured technology stocks, while a major cloud outage raised questions about Microsoft’s service reliability.

## Big Tech Stocks Slide Amid Market Sell-Off

Microsoft’s decline came amid a broader AI and semiconductor sell-off, with S&P 500 futures falling approximately 2.80%.

Fellow cloud computing and AI infrastructure companies Amazon.com Inc. AMZN, Alphabet Inc. GOOG GOOGL and Meta Platforms Inc. META declined between 3% and 6%.

Investors also faced uncertainty over the sustainability of substantial AI infrastructure spending and its implications for technology companies.

## Inflation And Trump’s Tariff Plans Pressure Tech Stocks

October’s Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) inflation rose 3.8% year over year, matching economists’ expectations.

Core PCE inflation, which excludes food and energy, increased 3.3%, exceeding the 3.2% forecast.

Futurum Group CEO Daniel Newman warned that Trump’s proposed tariffs could push markets lower, increase Treasury yields and fuel inflation, potentially prompting a more hawkish Federal Reserve.

Newman also questioned the administration’s objectives behind the proposed trade measures.

## Microsoft Launches ‘Code Red’ After Cloud Outage

Microsoft reportedly initiated a “Code Red” engineering overhaul after a 24-hour outage disrupted Outlook, Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 services.

The company attributed the disruption to an undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July.

Microsoft’s response includes improved traffic management, stronger system safeguards and faster recovery procedures.

Jason Henderson, president of Microsoft 365 Core, reportedly called the disruption the most customer-impacting incident in Exchange Online’s history.

The company aims to reduce the time needed to deploy critical security fixes worldwide to one hour, compared with the five to six days required for one previous production fix.

## Technical Analysis

Microsoft shares traded 6.1% below their 20-day simple moving average (SMA) of $516.90 and 6.6% below their 50-day SMA of $519.46.

The stock also remained 0.9% below its 100-day SMA of $489.83.

Trading below these moving averages indicates weakness in the stock’s short-term price trend.

## Analyst Ratings And Price Forecasts

Microsoft carries a Buy consensus rating with an average analyst price forecast of $644.46.

RBC Capital maintained Outperform with a $640 price forecast on Nov. 20.

TD Cowen reiterated Buy with a $650 forecast on Nov. 18.

Bernstein maintained Outperform and raised its forecast to $722 on Oct. 29.

## ETF Exposure

Microsoft accounts for 9.74% of the Vanguard Growth ETF (NYSE:VUG), 9.91% of the iShares Global Tech ETF (NYSE:IXN) and 9.80% of the Xtrackers S&P 500 Scored & Screened ETF (NYSE:SNPE).

These allocations expose the funds to Microsoft’s share-price fluctuations, while ETF inflows, outflows and portfolio rebalancing can influence trading activity in the stock.
