# Not Nvidia. Not Micron. This Dirt Cheap "Magnificent Seven" Stock Could Be the Biggest Winner of the Trillion-Dollar AI Race

**Author:** Adria Cimino
**Published:** September 12, 2026 | The Motley Fool

## Core Argument

The article identifies **Alphabet (GOOGL/GOOG)** as the cheapest and potentially most rewarding AI investment among the Magnificent Seven, trading at roughly 15x forward earnings.

## Key Points

- Nvidia and Micron have delivered ~900% and ~1,300% returns over five years, driven by GPU and memory chip demand
- Alphabet holds over 90% of the internet search market; recent ad revenue reached $81B within $119B total quarterly revenue
- Google Cloud, powered by the Gemini AI model family, is experiencing "explosive growth"
- Share price has dipped due to investor concerns about AI infrastructure spending pace
- The author argues Alphabet wins regardless of which chipmaker dominates, because it's both an AI *provider* and *user*

## Valuation Context

Alphabet is described as "the cheapest of the 'Magnificent Seven'" — its valuation dip is framed as a buying opportunity given strong long-term fundamentals.

*Disclosure: The author holds positions in Amazon and Tesla.*
