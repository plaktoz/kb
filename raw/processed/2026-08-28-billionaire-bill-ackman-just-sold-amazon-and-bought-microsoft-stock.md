# Billionaire Bill Ackman Just Sold Amazon and Bought Microsoft Stock. Which Cloud Computing Behemoth Is the Better Buy Today?

Ackman's Pershing Square reduced its Amazon position by over 25% in Q2 while boosting Microsoft holdings by nearly 10%. However, 13F filings lag by 45 days, so the moves may no longer reflect current positioning.

Since Q2 ended, Microsoft climbed roughly 30% while Amazon rose about 10%, significantly changing the relative valuation picture.

## Q2 Results Compared

- Amazon: revenue up 20%, operating income up 43%, with AWS delivering outsized results and operating income rising at a 64% year-over-year pace
- Microsoft: revenue and earnings each grew 18%

## Valuation Argument

Despite Amazon appearing more expensive on a trailing P/E basis, Amazon is actually the better value given AWS accounts for roughly 60% of Amazon's total operating income and is growing rapidly. Amazon also raised capital expenditure guidance, targeting $220 billion in data center spending for 2026, while Microsoft held guidance flat.

## Conclusion

The author favors Amazon as the stronger buy, citing superior growth trajectory and heavy infrastructure investment likely to fuel continued AWS expansion.
