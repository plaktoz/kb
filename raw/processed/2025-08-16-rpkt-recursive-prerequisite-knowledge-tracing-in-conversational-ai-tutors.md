# RPKT: Learning What You Don't Know — Recursive Prerequisite Knowledge Tracing in Conversational AI Tutors for Personalized Learning

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11892

---

**Authors:** Jinwen Tang, Qiming Guo, Zhicheng Tang, Yi Shang

**Submitted:** August 16, 2025 (v1); revised September 8, 2025 (v2)

**Abstract:**

The paper tackles what it calls the "unknown unknowns" problem — students often cannot identify gaps in their own knowledge. The proposed system, RPKT (Recursive Prerequisite Knowledge Tracing), uses LLMs to dynamically discover prerequisite concepts in real time, recursively tracing dependencies until reaching a learner's actual knowledge boundary.

Key features include:
- No reliance on pre-built knowledge graphs
- LLM-driven prerequisite extraction at runtime
- Binary assessment interfaces to reduce cognitive load
- Personalized learning path generation requiring no pre-constructed curricula

Testing in computer science domains demonstrated the system's ability to uncover "multiple nested levels of prerequisite dependencies" and identify cross-domain mathematical foundations, all without requiring pre-constructed curricula.
