# Implementing Learning Principles with a Personal AI Tutor: A Case Study

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13060

---

**Authors:** Ambroise Baillifard, Maxime Gabella, Pamela Banta Lavenex, Corinna S. Martarelli

**Submitted:** September 10, 2023

**Abstract:**

The study examines how AI tutors can support learning science principles like personalization, retrieval practice, and spaced repetition. Conducted over a semester at UniDistance Suisse, 51 psychology students in a neuroscience course used an AI tutor app. The system auto-generated microlearning questions from course materials via GPT-3, then built a dynamic neural-network model tracking each student's conceptual understanding.

Key findings:
- Students who actively engaged "achieved significantly higher grades"
- Active users showed "an average improvement of up to 15 percentile points" versus a parallel course without the AI tutor
- Student grasp scores "strongly correlated with the exam grade," validating the neural-network model

The authors conclude that personal AI tutors can effectively model human learning and boost academic performance, offering educators a pathway to deliver "personalized learning experiences grounded in the principles of learning sciences."
