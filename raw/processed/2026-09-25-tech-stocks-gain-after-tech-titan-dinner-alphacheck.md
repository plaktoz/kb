# Tech stocks gain after tech titan dinner with Trump and China's Xi Jinping: AlphaCheck

Stocks rose Friday as oil prices eased amid hopes of a potential deal to open the Strait of Hormuz. The gains followed a high-profile dinner involving President Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping, and several prominent tech executives, including Nvidia's Jensen Huang, AMD's Lisa Su, and Tesla's Elon Musk.

The 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.17%, while Brent crude fell toward $98/barrel. A softer US dollar index provided additional support for equities.

The Technology sector (XLK) posted gains, with Microsoft surging roughly 3%. Meta declined about 2% after a "16% rally over the past four days," driven by enthusiasm around its new Muse AI agent.

Other stocks drawing reader attention included Costco, Greenland Energy, and Moderna.
