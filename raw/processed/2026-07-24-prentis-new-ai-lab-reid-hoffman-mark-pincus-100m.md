# Prentis, New AI Lab Co-Founded by Reid Hoffman, Mark Pincus, in Talks to Raise $100M

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/prentis-new-ai-lab-co-founded-by-reid-hoffman-mark-pincus-in-talks-to-raise-100m/

---

Author: Marina Temkin
Published: July 24, 2026

Prentis, an AI research lab launched in April 2026, is seeking $100M at a $1B valuation. Co-founded by Ritankar Das, Reid Hoffman, and Mark Pincus, it focuses on computer-use models that automate routine office workflows — think insurance claims processing or customs paperwork — without human intervention.

The startup claims its Hive-32B model beats OpenAI's GPT-5.4 and Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 on two computer-use benchmarks, while running at roughly 10x lower cost per task than frontier APIs. TechCrunch noted it has not independently verified these claims.

Prentis reports contracts worth up to $50M with healthcare, manufacturing, and retail customers. Its pitch deck projects an estimated $75M annualized run rate by Q3 2026, though figures are "performance-dependent and subject to final execution."

CEO Das, 31, previously founded Titan, a self-funded holding company behind several AI health ventures. Hoffman and Pincus are involved as co-founders while maintaining other primary ventures. The company has 25+ employees with backgrounds from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Tencent, and Alibaba.
