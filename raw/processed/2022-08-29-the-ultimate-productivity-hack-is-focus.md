# The Ultimate Productivity Hack is Focus

Focus means saying no — not just to bad ideas, but to genuinely good ones. Buffett, Gates, and Jony Ive all cited focus as central to success.

Ive described his key lesson from Jobs: **"What focus means is saying no to something that… you think is a phenomenal idea… but you say no to it because you're focusing on something else."**

Focus isn't a switch — it's a muscle requiring daily practice. Jobs reportedly tested people by asking what they had said no to recently.

## Key Principles

- **Commitment enables focus.** Without it, energy disperses and impact shrinks.
- Outstanding performers often work on the same thing for an unusually long time before results appear.
- **"If you start saying yes to average ideas, you quickly lose the space and time you need to execute on the great ones."**
- The only employees worth hiring — and competitors worth tracking — are focused ones.

## Focus Creates Opportunity

Focused people say no to good ideas, which leaves gaps others can exploit. To find openings, ask focused colleagues about their best unpursued idea, or study what strong competitors are ignoring.

## Energy → Results

Focused organizations move fastest. Deep commitment to a problem reveals insights casual observers miss. Every unit of energy spent on lower-priority work is subtracted from what matters most.

Narrow the focus. Raise the standard. Set yourself apart.
