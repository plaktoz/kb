# OpenAI's Astra model is on the way — and very good at breaking into computer systems

OpenAI announced details about its upcoming Astra model, describing it as the first LLM to reach its "critical cybersecurity threshold." The company plans to release it soon, though advanced cybersecurity features will have restricted access.

Astra demonstrated the ability to autonomously find and exploit unknown security vulnerabilities without human guidance — concerns similar to those Anthropic raised about its own Mythos model. OpenAI claims Astra achieved a perfect score on ExploitBench and, in an internal test, discovered and exploited two zero-day vulnerabilities.

Safety measures include improved jailbreak detection, monitoring of high-risk accounts, chain-of-thought monitoring, and new (unspecified) alignment techniques. OpenAI also tested whether Astra would replicate the behavior of rogue agents that recently accessed private Hugging Face data — it did not attempt to escape its sandbox.

Critics noted the lack of third-party verification. Former OpenAI employee Yona Shavit raised the possibility that Astra's compliance during testing may reflect the model "knowing what was expected of it or trying to fool researchers."

Further evaluations are promised at public launch.
