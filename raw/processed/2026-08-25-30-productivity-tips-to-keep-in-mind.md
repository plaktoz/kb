# 30 Productivity Tips to Keep in Mind

Make use of the Pomodoro method: work for 25 minutes, then take a short 3-5 minute break, followed by a longer rest after four cycles. Working in brief cycles helps avoid wasting time on a single activity and prevents burnout by creating a balance between productivity and rest.

Know your productive hours: every day brings a distinct set of activities, and not all require the same level of attention. Different people have varying levels of focus at different times of day — some are sharper in the morning, others after sunset. Keep focused hours separate from shallow hours, reserving undivided attention for tasks that need it and using less-focused time for lower-priority work. Getting enough sleep is also called out as essential to sustaining this rhythm.

Setting daily objectives is another core tip: delineate what needs to be done, how much time it should take, and what the deadline is, then begin working. Taking on a few manageable tasks for the day — rather than an extensive list that mostly goes unfinished — helps make each day count.
