# Cyber Kill Chain® | Lockheed Martin

Lockheed Martin developed the Cyber Kill Chain® as part of its Intelligence Driven Defense® model. The framework is designed to identify and prevent cyber intrusions by mapping the steps adversaries must complete to reach their objectives.

## The Seven-Step Framework

The model provides seven phases that improve analyst visibility into attacks and illuminate adversary "tactics, techniques and procedures."

## APT Definition

The page breaks down the Advanced Persistent Threat concept:

- **A – Advanced:** Targeted, coordinated, purposeful
- **P – Persistent:** Targeted, coordinated, purposeful
- **T – Threat:** Person(s) with intent, opportunity, and capability
