# Microsoft kills off unsuccessful AI features while merging its separate Copilot apps

Microsoft is consolidating its consumer and business Copilot apps into a single unified product, while simultaneously retiring several features that failed to gain traction.

Features being cut by August 18, 2026 include:

- Group Chats
- AI-generated podcasts
- Copilot Labs experimental features
- Deep Research (consumer version)
- "Mico," an animated blob character described as a modern Clippy

Professional subscribers will retain access to a deep-research equivalent through a tool called **Researcher**.

The move reflects Microsoft's acknowledgment that its Copilot strategy had grown overly fragmented, hindering competition with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Microsoft EVP Jacob Andreou reportedly stated internally that Copilot needed to earn "the right to exist" in customers' lives.

The consolidation mirrors broader industry trends — Claude merged Cowork into Chat, and OpenAI folded its Operator agent into ChatGPT.

Files from the standalone Copilot app will migrate to OneDrive; some features may temporarily disappear during the transition.
