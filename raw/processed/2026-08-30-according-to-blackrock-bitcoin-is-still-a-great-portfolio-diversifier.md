# According to BlackRock, Bitcoin Is Still a Great Portfolio Diversifier. So How Much Bitcoin Should You Be Holding in Your Portfolio?

Bitcoin's price climbed over 22% in two weeks amid investor anxiety over inflation (3.3% in July), a national debt surpassing $40 trillion, and expanded Treasury bond buybacks.

BlackRock released a report calling Bitcoin "a potential strategic diversifier for long-term portfolio construction," recommending investors allocate just 1%–2% of a traditional 60/40 portfolio to the cryptocurrency.

The firm argues that Bitcoin's volatility has decreased due to greater institutional adoption and the rise of Bitcoin ETFs. A small allocation offers meaningful upside potential without dramatically increasing overall portfolio risk.

The renewed interest in Bitcoin mirrors a flight to safe havens — similar to gold — as investors worry about stagflationary pressures and fiscal instability.

A 1%–2% Bitcoin allocation is a conservative, reasonable approach: limiting downside exposure while still capturing potential gains — but only if the investor is comfortable with added volatility.
