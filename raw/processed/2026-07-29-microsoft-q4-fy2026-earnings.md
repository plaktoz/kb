# Microsoft Cloud and AI Strength Fuels Fourth Quarter Results

Microsoft reported results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026: revenue of $90.0 billion, up 18% year over year (17% in constant currency); operating income of $40.6 billion, up 18%; GAAP net income of $35.8 billion, up 31%; and GAAP diluted EPS of $4.81, up 32%. Non-GAAP figures (excluding OpenAI investment impacts) showed net income of $35.3 billion (up 22%) and diluted EPS of $4.74 (up 23%). Results included a $3.2 billion gain from Microsoft's investment in Anthropic.

CEO Satya Nadella: "This year, Azure revenue surpassed $100 billion for the first time, and Microsoft 365 Copilot reached over 30 million paid seats." CFO Amy Hood highlighted Microsoft Cloud revenue of $59.3 billion, up 27% year over year.

Segment detail: Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion (+14%), with Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue up 14–16%, LinkedIn revenue up 12%, and Dynamics 365 up 13%. Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion (+32%), driven by Azure and other cloud services revenue growth of 43%. More Personal Computing revenue was $12.9 billion (-4%), with Windows OEM/Devices down 7% and Xbox content/services down 10%, partly offset by 10% growth in search advertising revenue.

For fiscal year 2026 overall: revenue was $331.8 billion (+18%), operating income $155.2 billion (+21%), GAAP net income $133.7 billion (+31%), and GAAP diluted EPS $17.95 (+32%). Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders via dividends and buybacks in the quarter. Commercial remaining performance obligation — a forward revenue backlog indicator — rose 84% year over year to $678 billion.
