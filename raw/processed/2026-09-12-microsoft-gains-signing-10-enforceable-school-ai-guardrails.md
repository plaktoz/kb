# Microsoft Gains After Signing 10 Enforceable School-AI Guardrails

Microsoft committed to ten legally enforceable principles governing AI use in schools, reaching agreements with two major teachers unions. Shares rose roughly 0.7% to $495.93 on Friday.

The guardrails establish firm limits on how sensitive classroom data can be used. Key provisions include:

- Student and educator data will **not** be used to train AI models
- Data collection will be limited
- AI systems must be explained to families in plain language
- AI companions are prohibited
- Decisions with serious consequences require human review

These measures follow temporary restrictions on student-facing AI tools imposed by New York City and Los Angeles.

School districts may add the provisions to new or existing Microsoft contracts starting in November, without needing to renegotiate entire agreements. No adoption figures or revenue details were disclosed.

Microsoft's share price of $495.93 sits approximately 15% below its GF Value estimate of $583.64, indicating potential upside if broader trust translates into wider classroom adoption.
