# Microsoft Stock Is Flat So Far in 2026. Is a Rally Coming?

**Author:** Keithen Drury | **Source:** The Motley Fool | **Date:** September 11, 2026

---

## Summary

Despite a strong business performance, Microsoft (MSFT) has delivered minimal gains for 2026 investors — though those who bought during the spring sell-off have fared much better.

---

## Stock Performance

- **Year-to-date gain:** ~2%
- During April–June 2026, shares fell as much as **27%**
- Investors who bought at those lows are now up **30%+**
- Current price: **$495.63** (+0.65%)
- 52-week range: $349.20 – $553.72

---

## Business Highlights (Fiscal Q4 2026, ended June 30)

- **Copilot** reached 30 million paid seats
- **Azure** surpassed $100 billion in annual revenue (~1/3 of total revenue)
- Azure grew **43%** year-over-year
- Overall company revenue grew **18%**

---

## Valuation Analysis

Microsoft currently trades at **25x forward earnings** — described as average for a large-cap tech company. The article notes the stock was previously trading at a discounted P/E during its sell-off, which represented the real buying opportunity.

The author suggests a re-rating to **30x forward earnings** "wouldn't be unreasonable," but concludes the stock is now "fairly priced."

---

## Key Takeaway

"The time to buy was a few months ago" when shares traded at a discount. The author believes Microsoft will likely outperform the broader market over the next year given its **>10% growth rate**, but warns that the potential for "huge returns in the near term has come and gone."
