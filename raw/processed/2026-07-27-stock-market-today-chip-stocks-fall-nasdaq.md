# Stock Market Today: Chip Stocks Fall, Weighing on Nasdaq

---

By Chelsey Dulaney | July 27, 2026 at 4:21pm ET

## Oil prices tumble after Trump puts Mideast fighting on hold

Chip stocks weighed on Nasdaq to start the week, as a sharp fall in oil prices failed to give markets a broad reprieve.

Nvidia shares fell 5%, dragging its market value below that of Apple. Nvidia investors are concerned about the possibility that the chip giant could provide a roughly $250 billion backstop for a massive OpenAI data-center project.

Global oil prices meanwhile retreated below $89 a barrel after an 8.7% drop in front-month Brent crude futures. The decline followed the Trump administration pausing plans to escalate its war in Iran. While pressures on global energy supplies remain in place, travel stocks advanced Monday, while the S&P energy sector retreated 2%.

Treasury yields slipped while the Dow industrials moved 0.5% higher, lifted by a 6.1% rally in Salesforce shares. Nasdaq declined 0.2%, while the S&P 500 ended the day close to flat.

Memory-chip maker CXMT pulled off a blockbuster stock-market debut that made it the most valuable company listed in mainland China.

Investors will watch for a fresh batch of U.S. tech earnings this week, including reports from Microsoft and Meta Platforms on Wednesday.

The Fed's interest-rate decision Wednesday, one of the least predictable in years, is also in focus.
