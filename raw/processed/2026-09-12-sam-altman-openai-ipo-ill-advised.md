# OpenAI's Sam Altman Says It Would Be 'Ill-Advised' to Go Public in 2026

Despite filing confidentially for an IPO, OpenAI will not go public in 2026, per CEO Sam Altman. In a Fortune interview with editor in chief Alyson Shontell, Altman stated: "We're not rushing into an IPO" and called the current moment "ill-advised" for going public given AI safety concerns.

Altman indicated OpenAI will list "when we're ready" — contingent on business readiness and broader societal context around the technology. When pressed on 2026 timing, he confirmed: "I would say not 2026, yeah."

The NYT had previously reported that OpenAI, despite hiring bankers and lawyers targeting a Q3/Q4 2026 listing, was leaning toward 2027 due to tech stock volatility and internal financial challenges.
