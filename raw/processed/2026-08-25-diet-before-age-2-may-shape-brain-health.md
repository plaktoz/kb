# Your Diet Before Age 2 May Shape Your Brain Health for Decades

A new study links lower sugar intake during the first 1,000 days of life — from conception to about age 2 — with lower risks of dementia and Alzheimer's disease later in life. The findings point to an early developmental window where dietary patterns may have outsized, long-term effects on brain health outcomes decades later.
