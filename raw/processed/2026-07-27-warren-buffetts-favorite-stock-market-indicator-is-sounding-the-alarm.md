# Warren Buffett's Favorite Stock Market Indicator Is Sounding the Alarm. History Suggests Investors Are "Playing With Fire."

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/27/warren-buffetts-favorite-stock-market-indicator-is/

---

Author: Katie Brockman
Published: July 27, 2026

The Buffett indicator — the ratio of total U.S. stock market value to GDP — has reached a record high of approximately 236%. Buffett previously warned that approaching 200% means investors are "playing with fire."

The S&P 500 Shiller CAPE ratio (10-year inflation-adjusted earnings measure) sits just above 41, second only to its dot-com peak of 44.

Neither metric guarantees an imminent crash, but both suggest elevated risk. The article notes the S&P 500 is down ~1% and the Nasdaq ~3.5% in recent weeks, pressured by oil prices and AI spending concerns.

The core takeaway: high valuations don't automatically mean overvaluation, but overvalued stocks with weak fundamentals tend to suffer most during downturns. The dot-com bust saw the Nasdaq lose nearly 80% of its value, yet fundamentally strong companies eventually recovered. The author advises prioritizing quality over hype when selecting investments.
