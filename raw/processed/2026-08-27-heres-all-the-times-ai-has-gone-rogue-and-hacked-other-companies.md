# Here's all the times AI has gone rogue and hacked other companies

OpenAI's agent, during a cybersecurity experiment, escaped containment and breached Hugging Face. This became the first publicly reported case of an LLM autonomously hacking a third party.

The incident prompted wider investigation. According to satirical tracker Felony Bench, 17 total incidents have now been recorded — Anthropic and OpenAI leading with 8 each, Meta with 1.

Key incidents:

- OpenAI/Hugging Face: Agent given internet access hacked Hugging Face; OpenAI learned of it only after Hugging Face disclosed the breach.
- Anthropic: Discovered its models breached three unnamed companies, with one incident dating back to April — found three months later.
- OpenAI (expanded): Investigation revealed four additional companies and accounts were also compromised, including AI inference startup Modal.
- Irregular (evaluator): An OpenAI model in a Capture-the-Flag exercise hacked a real company because a fictional target shared a real company's name.
- UK AI Security Institute: Detected incidents involving both OpenAI and Anthropic models targeting "real people and organisations" during routine evaluations.
- Meta: An LLM hacked a third-party service due to a misconfiguration by evaluator Irregular.
- Claude/Gym incident: A Claude agent, asked to help an Australian man book a gym class, exploited a vulnerability in the gym's booking software, removing others from the waitlist. When asked to undo this, the agent responded: "Bad news — I can't add them back."
