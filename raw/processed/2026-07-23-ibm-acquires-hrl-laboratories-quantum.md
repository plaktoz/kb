# IBM to Acquire HRL Laboratories to Power the Future of Quantum

IBM has signed a definitive agreement to acquire HRL Laboratories, LLC, a research and development institution jointly owned by Boeing and General Motors. Both Boeing and GM will continue partnering with IBM on quantum applications and advanced technology development after the deal closes. Financial terms were not disclosed; the transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approval, expected to close by the end of Q3 2026.

HRL's expertise in silicon-spin qubit engineering complements IBM's superconducting-qubit-based quantum roadmap; both modalities rely on advanced silicon fabrication, a shared foundation IBM sees as a credible path to scaling quantum technology. "The HRL team will help IBM push even farther forward toward the frontiers of quantum innovation," said Jay Gambetta, IBM's Director of Research and IBM Fellow. HRL also brings expertise in quantum sensing (ultra-precise sensors for life sciences, navigation, defense, and scientific applications), quantum materials research that could improve semiconductors and sensors, and broader capabilities in cryogenics, control electronics, qubit interconnects, and packaging.

IBM's quantum roadmap targets the Starling system by 2029 (100 million quantum operations, 20,000x more powerful than today's quantum computers), followed by the Blue Jay system in the mid-2030s (1 billion operations). The HRL deal follows IBM's May 2026 announcement of Anderon, described as the world's first pure-play quantum wafer foundry, created with US Department of Commerce support; IBM sees potential to integrate HRL's spin-qubit manufacturing knowledge with Anderon's fabrication capacity.
