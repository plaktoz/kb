# How EdTech Startups Are Disrupting Traditional Learning Methods

Technology has changed the field of education. We’re seeing the adoption of EdTech on a mass scale. The market was valued at more than $140 billion in 2023, and it growing exponentially.

However, how have online learning platforms inspired digital transformation in education? How have adaptive learning solutions disrupted the traditional methods? That’s what we’re here for. We’re going to explore how EdTech startups have helped change the educational industry and the effect they have on traditional approaches.

Can we take a moment to appreciate the Internet which has made EdTech and many other advancements possible? The revolution is constantly evolving, bringing change at an incredible pace.

Many Internet providers strive to power the users with cutting edge technology to harness the full potential of digital Innovation.

Ultra-low lag connectivity initiative by Xfinity is one example. The high-speed internet provider aims to allow users to unlock a wealth of entertainment, sports, news and information.

So, let’s begin.

## The Role of EdTech Startups

EdTech refers to using technology to improve learning methodologies. Multiple tools and software help engage students and assist teachers. EdTech startups are continuously striving to create cutting-edge platforms to transform the conventional mode of teaching and learning.

Among many, the following EdTech startups have emerged as the market leaders:

- Sana Labs
- Riiid
- Udacity
- Amira Learning
- 360Learning
- Tuteria

Each platform serves various needs of educational institutes, providing them with the right tools and features that make teaching and getting education easy.

## How EdTech Startups Disrupt Traditional Learning Methods?

In this context, the word disrupt is inappropriate as it means ‘to break apart,’ literally!

Instead,

EdTech startups and technology are Changing, Transforming, and Innovating traditional learning practices. They do it by offering:

## Better Engagement

The foremost element that EdTech focuses on is engaging students better. Here, EdTech startups play a key role by providing the right tool for the institutes. While different tools work differently, the goal is the same– to engage the students.

Each tool assesses the learning abilities of the students. By determining the level of their engagement, the tools formulate teaching experiences to improve it. For instance, Carnegie Learning’s teaching methods include games and fun exercises for the students. This technique allows the tool and the teachers to engage students better, making it easier for them to grasp the concepts and apply them optimally.

## Personalized Learning Experiences

The purpose of EdTech is to provide personalized learning experiences to the students. For instance, Amira Learning, an AI-based tool, creates a personalized learning journey for students. It allows the students to learn at their own pace and improve where they lack. With regular tracking of their progress, the platform creates assessments.

This personalized experience makes EdTech a great choice for teachers as they can focus on individual students and help them improve without using many resources.

## Skill & Subject Based Learning

EdTech startups have also developed platforms that offer formal skill development. This kind of education focuses on developing the skills of the students who want to pursue a particular field.

For instance, Blackbullion provides financial education to students– money management in particular. It has engaging content that the students can use to learn financial management skills. Moreover, it facilitates the students by offering them insights related to scholarships and how to apply for them.

Udacity is another AI-based platform that guides a plethora of subjects. With its integrated AI system, it evaluates the skills of the students and recommends courses that are similar to theirs.

In this manner, several EdTech startups have made acquiring dedicated skills easier for students. This method also accelerates career persuasion from early school life, enabling the students to pick a career right away.

## Access to Remote Certifications

Apart from remote access to education, several EdTech startups have partnered with educational institutes to provide students access to online degrees and certifications. Udemy, Coursera, HubSpot, etc., are quite prominent platforms, offering learning materials as well as online certifications to students.

## Gamification

Another trendy element EdTech has added to traditional learning methods is gamification. Instead of traditional online methods where students had to watch and learn from the videos, gamification allows the students to learn by playing games and competing.

This mode improves engagement as the students don’t feel bored learning and the sense of competing keeps them enthusiastic. Various apps such as Lingawa, Kahoot!, and Secure Code Warrior offer gamified learning experiences.

It’s one of the popular strategies used by EdTech platforms, which has proven to be helpful for students in terms of learning. Research says that more than 33% of the students’ performance has been improved with gamification.

## Enhanced Student Analysis

Student analysis is one of the key requirements of any institute. Fortunately, EdTech startups have considered it and provided amazing data analysis of the student’s performances. Almost all EdTech platforms and software provide an in-depth analysis of how each student is performing.

This is how these platforms can create personalized teaching experiences for the students. By evaluating the student’s performance based on the initial assessments, the platforms quantify the level of skills for each student.

Once done, the tools present this data to the teachers, enabling them to know where every student stands. If these tools are used individually, the students can assess their level of skills and where they lack, and use key insights to improve.

## Affordability

With several EdTech startups partnering with institutes, it’s easier for students to enroll in online degrees and certifications, and that too at affordable rates. Unlike many institutes, where each program charges hefty fees, students can enroll in a degree for free or at a low price on EdTech platforms.

Moreover, it removes the hassle of relocation or paying for additional campus charges. It’s also a great choice for parents who want to homeschool their children.

## Flexible Teaching

Campuses have fixed programs, limited teachers, and rigid schedules. On the other hand, EdTech platforms make it easier to acquire education with flexibility in location, time, teachers, and subject. A student can easily enroll in subjects of his liking without having to follow a fixed course breakdown.

The EdTech platforms can also create a customized learning experience for the students, enabling them to learn what they want. The platforms can recommend the best options that go with their current course/ subject, and the students can easily choose if they want it or not.

This mitigates the need to add unnecessary subjects and shift focus on the preferred subjects of any degree. Someone interested in AI can easily choose from the core subjects without taking on minor courses.

## Challenges Faced by EdTech Startups

Even though they are promising, EdTech startups face numerous challenges along their journey:

## Funding Issues

The development of EdTech platforms is not easy and certainly not cheap. Numerous resources are required to create an operational and useful platform, which requires funding. The companies need investors to ensure the platform remains operational.

## User Adoption

Any EdTech startup planning to launch itself needs to sustain itself, and for that, it needs clients. However, the most important element is to keep those clients happy with the platform. Similar to many AI-based tools that have evolved into completely different platforms with promising growth, EdTech platforms need an ever-growing development.

This works when the platform engages in strategies to improve the platform while addressing queries and solving problems. Not only this, startups need to pitch their tools effectively, use marketing tactics and, convince the institutes and the students to use them.

## Regulatory Compliance

Almost every regulated educational sector requires adherence to compliance. EdTech startups need to ensure that their tools or platforms provide usefulness in learning experiences only.

Apart from this, the platforms must exhibit proficiency in mitigating cheating while ensuring the safety of those connected to it, directly or indirectly. Most importantly, it should safeguard the data used by it.

## Future of EdTech Startups

The future of EdTech startups and the industry as a whole is quite promising. Numerous institutes and millions of students have already opted for such platforms for their amazing features, great perks, and adaptive learning experiences.

Once the challenges mentioned above are overcome, any EdTech startups can prove to be vital for the educational sector. Moreover, with technology advancing, we can expect future EdTech platforms to host the best use of high-end tech for educational purposes, making learning as interactive as ever!
