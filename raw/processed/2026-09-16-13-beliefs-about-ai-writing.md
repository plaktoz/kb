# 13 Beliefs About AI Writing

Mike Taylor outlines the principles that moved him from skepticism to regular AI-assisted publishing.

## Key Beliefs

- **Input > Output:** "The prompt should be larger than the output" — load prompts with your own material to limit AI invention.
- **Originality matters:** Write from firsthand experience and experiments that aren't already in training data.
- **Idea ownership:** If you can't defend the ideas in conversation, there's no point publishing them.
- **Social posts stay human:** Short-form writing is where ideas begin; feel the risk personally.
- **Good ideas over good prose:** AI helps non-writers get valuable ideas into the world, and cuts writing time significantly.
- **Quality threshold:** He argues models have crossed into "worth publishing" territory, though AI writing remains controversial.

He notes his O'Reilly book was written without AI per contract — and calls it his last such project. His test: could he argue for every idea if a reader pushed back? If yes, it's publishable regardless of how it was drafted.
