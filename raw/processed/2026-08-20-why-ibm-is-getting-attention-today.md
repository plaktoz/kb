# Why International Business Machines (IBM) Is Getting Attention Today

IBM has drawn fresh investor attention after reporting a fiscal Q2 2026 revenue shortfall and cutting its 2026 revenue growth guidance, while keeping operating pretax margin and free-cash-flow commitments unchanged.

The guidance cut sits against a mixed price backdrop: the share price is up 11.34% over the past 30 days but down 18.64% year to date. The 5-year total shareholder return of 114.96% reflects a stronger longer-term picture. Per the most-followed valuation narrative on the platform, IBM's fair value sits at $256.08, above the last close of $237.16, framing the guidance cut against a valuation that still screens as roughly 7.4% undervalued — a case built on IBM's mix-shift toward software, strong margins, and durable cash flows as it transitions from a traditional IT services provider into a hybrid cloud and AI platform leader. That narrative could be challenged if IBM stumbles on AI execution or if quantum computing investments weigh more heavily on earnings than expected.
