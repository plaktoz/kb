# The Mindset of a Successful Practitioner

Lori Kennedy outlines 5 mindset elements she considers essential for health practitioners building a successful business.

## The 5 Mindset Elements

**1. Self-Belief Over External Validation**
Seeking reassurance from others about whether you can succeed reveals a deeper lack of inner conviction. When obstacles arise, only your own belief will carry you through — not others' opinions.

**2. Specificity of Purpose**
Cool ideas are plentiful, but a deep, driving purpose is different. Kennedy describes purpose as a "pit-of-your-stomach feeling" that sustains you through inevitable setbacks. Purpose may shift over time — her own path moved from candida treatment to weight loss to coaching practitioners — but following that internal drive is what matters.

**3. Work Smart, Hard & Long**
Building a business during startup requires significant sacrifice — long hours and missed family moments. Kennedy pushes back against claims that success comes from working only 10–20 hours weekly, arguing those outcomes typically follow years of intensive effort.

**4. Growth Mindset Over Fixed Mindset**
Resisting new approaches — "that's not how it's done" — is fighting for your own limitations. A growth mindset welcomes challenges as stepping stones rather than obstacles.

**5. Enjoy the Ride**
Entrepreneurship is a rollercoaster. The difficulties reshape your sense of identity and possibility. As Kennedy frames it, the transformation on the other side justifies the struggle.
