# IBM (IBM) Beats Stock Market Upswing: What Investors Need to Know

IBM closed the latest session up +1.13% at $234.31, outpacing the S&P 500's 1.06% gain. The Dow rose 1.18% and the Nasdaq gained 1.4% on the same day.

Over the past month, IBM shares declined 1.79%, lagging the Computer and Technology sector's 3.99% gain and the S&P 500's 2.46% rise.

Looking ahead, analysts project IBM's upcoming EPS at $2.90, a 9.43% year-over-year increase. Consensus revenue estimates stand at $16.88 billion, reflecting roughly 3.34% growth from the prior year's comparable quarter.

For the full year, estimates call for earnings of $12.33 per share and revenue of $70.43 billion, representing gains of approximately 6.38% and 4.28% respectively versus last year.

Analyst estimate revisions have been modest, with the consensus EPS projection moving 0.08% lower over the past 30 days. IBM currently holds a Zacks Rank of #3 (Hold).

On valuation, IBM trades at a Forward P/E of 18.79, a discount to the industry average of 20.27. Its PEG ratio stands at 2.21, compared to the Computer - Integrated Systems industry average of 0.61.

The Computer - Integrated Systems industry carries a Zacks Industry Rank of 40, placing it in the top 17% of more than 250 industries tracked.
