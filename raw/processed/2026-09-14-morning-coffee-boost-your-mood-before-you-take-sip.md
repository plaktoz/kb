# Why Your Morning Coffee May Boost Your Mood Before You Even Take a Sip

A small study published in *Nutrients* (September 9) found that coffee aroma may reduce stress and improve mood in healthy young adults — even before a single sip is taken.

## Study Details

Twenty healthy adults (ages 20–29) were exposed to freshly brewed coffee placed about 50 cm away for five minutes. Mood was assessed using the **Profile of Mood States (POMS)**, alongside EEG monitoring and heart rate tracking.

Key findings:
- Total mood disturbance decreased
- Reductions observed in tension-anxiety, fatigue, confusion, and depression-dejection
- The EEG relaxation index rose modestly from ~0.74 to 0.78
- No significant changes in heart rate

**Important caveat:** The study lacked a control group, so results could reflect the restful environment, expectations, or lab conditions rather than coffee aroma itself.

## Expert Perspective

Psychologist Rod Mitchell noted that smell connects more directly to emotional brain centers than other senses. The effect likely stems from **conditioned responses** — regular coffee drinkers have repeatedly paired the aroma with rest and comfort, so the scent alone can trigger that response.

Mitchell observed that the aroma seemed to *settle* rather than energize participants: "Tension and fatigue decreased while energy stayed flat."

On coffee-scented candles, he suggested a candle burning during work emails is unlikely to replicate the calming effect of a genuine mindful coffee break.

## Coffee Aroma vs. Other Aromatherapy

Lavender and orange essential oils currently have stronger research backing for anxiety and stress reduction than coffee aroma does.

## Practical Tips

- Pause and inhale deeply before your morning brew
- Keep coffee beans at your desk for stressful moments
- Brew coffee during focused work sessions
- Treat coffee breaks as genuine sensory pauses — phone down, full minute of mindful smelling before drinking
