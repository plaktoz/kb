# Google's AI Red Team: The Ethical Hackers Making AI Safer

*Published July 19, 2023 | By Daniel Fabian, Head of Google Red Teams*

Source: https://blog.google/technology/safety-security/googles-ai-red-team-the-ethical-hackers-making-ai-safer/

---

## Overview

Google published its first public report on the AI Red Team, exploring how red teaming supports the **Secure AI Framework (SAIF)** introduced the prior month.

---

## What Is Red Teaming?

Google's Red Team simulates adversaries — from nation-states and APT groups to individual criminals — to test defenses. The term originates from military practice. The AI Red Team combines traditional red team skills with specialized AI expertise, drawing on intelligence from **Mandiant**, the **Threat Analysis Group (TAG)**, and **Google DeepMind**.

---

## Common Attack Types Simulated

- Prompt attacks
- Training data extraction
- Model backdooring
- Adversarial examples
- Data poisoning
- Data exfiltration

---

## Key Lessons Learned

1. Traditional red teams are a starting point, but AI attacks grow complex quickly
2. Some findings lack simple fixes — red teaming should feed ongoing R&D
3. Locking down systems/models via traditional security controls meaningfully reduces risk
4. Many AI attacks are detectable using conventional methods

---

## Looking Forward

Google recommends all organizations run regular red team exercises on critical AI deployments and points to SAIF implementation resources and AI pipeline security guidance for further reading.
