# Microsoft Copilot Is Turning Into An 'AI Operating System' — Here's What It Means For MSFT Stock, According To Oppenheimer's Brian Schwartz

Oppenheimer analyst Brian Schwartz appeared on CNBC to discuss Microsoft's latest Copilot announcement, calling MSFT a "top pick" and describing the news as pushing Microsoft "deeper into that AI win category."

## Key Points

- **Copilot reframed:** Schwartz characterized the new Copilot developments as transforming it from a simple chatbot into an "AI operating system," arguing that platform/OS status yields higher engagement, greater monetization, and competitive defensibility.

- **Monetization path:** New features—Cowork, co-development, and Autopilot—could be monetized via consumption-based pricing, credits, and tokens, potentially benefiting both Microsoft 365 and Azure.

- **Azure growth:** Azure is currently growing in the low-40% range. Schwartz suggested that if growth approaches 50% "at a scale exceeding $100 billion," a stock rerating becomes possible.

- **Price target:** Oppenheimer raised its MSFT target to $570 (from $515), maintaining an Outperform rating—representing roughly 10%+ upside from Friday's close.

- **Stock performance:** MSFT closed up 4% Friday; shares are up 7% year-to-date but only 2% over the past 12 months, trailing QQQ (+21%) and XLK (+36%).
