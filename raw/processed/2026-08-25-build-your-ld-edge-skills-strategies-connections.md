# Build Your L&D Edge with Skills, Strategies, and Connections That Matter

Learning and development (L&D) professionals can strengthen their organizational influence by connecting learning initiatives directly to what the business cares about. That means understanding how the organization makes money, what its strategic priorities are, and which KPIs leadership tracks.

When proposing an L&D initiative, tie it explicitly to outcomes such as productivity, retention, sales performance, or customer satisfaction — the metrics leadership already cares about — rather than framing it purely in learning terms. The article points to DevLearn as one venue with 200+ learning activities related to the latest learning technology, with an Expo pass available for those unable to travel, and to the Everyday Tools in Learning Development Online Conference as a lower-cost alternative.
