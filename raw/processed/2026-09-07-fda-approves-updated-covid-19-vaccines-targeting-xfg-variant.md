# FDA Approves 4 Updated COVID-19 Vaccines for 2026–2027 Season

On August 27, the FDA approved four updated COVID-19 vaccines for the 2026–2027 season, each targeting the JN.1-lineage XFG variant — currently the most prevalent strain globally. All are approved for adults 65+ and younger people with qualifying underlying conditions.

## The Four Approved Vaccines

| Vaccine | Manufacturer | Type | Min. Age (with conditions) |
|---|---|---|---|
| COMIRNATY | Pfizer-BioNTech | mRNA | 5 years |
| SPIKEVAX | Moderna | mRNA | 6 months |
| MNEXSPIKE | Moderna | mRNA | 12 years |
| NUVAXOVID | Novavax | Protein-based | 12 years |

## Why Annual Updates Matter

COVID-19 vaccines are updated each fall to match dominant circulating strains. Dr. William Schaffner (Vanderbilt University) noted that mRNA technology "can respond rapidly to changing strains," enabling strong protection against serious illness.

Dr. Monica Gandhi (UCSF) explained that while SW2 is actually the most prevalent U.S. variant at 21%, the two strains are closely related, so the XFG-targeted vaccine should still provide meaningful protection.

## About the XFG Variant

XFG, also called "Stratus," is part of the Omicron family. It does not typically cause more severe illness than prior variants — its main distinguishing trait is high transmissibility. As of late August, the CDC estimated COVID-19 cases were growing in 47 states.

## Who Should Consider Vaccination

- Adults 65 and older
- People with underlying conditions including:
  - Chronic kidney disease
  - Chronic lung disease (COPD, pulmonary hypertension, etc.)
  - Heart conditions, diabetes, obesity, liver disease
  - Pregnancy
- Those in close contact with high-risk individuals

Anyone interested in prevention may also consider getting vaccinated, per Dr. Schaffner.
