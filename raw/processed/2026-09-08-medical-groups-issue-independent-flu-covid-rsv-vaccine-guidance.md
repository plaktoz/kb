# U.S. Medical Groups Issue Flu, COVID Vaccine Guidance Independent of CDC

With the CDC's ACIP failing to issue new COVID-19 or RSV recommendations for the 2026–2027 respiratory season, four major medical organizations stepped in on September 2 with their own guidance:

- AAP (children)
- AAFP (adults)
- ACOG (pregnant people)
- IDSA (immunocompromised individuals)

The AMA and University of Minnesota's Vaccine Integrity Project reviewed hundreds of studies, publishing findings in *JAMA* the same day, finding vaccines still protect against severe illness with no new safety concerns identified.

## Key Recommendations

**Flu:** Everyone 6 months+; enhanced formulation for adults 65+.

**COVID:** All adults 19+; adults 65+ get two seasonal doses; children 6–23 months; higher-risk youth 2–18.

**RSV:** Adults 75+; adults 50–74 at elevated risk; during pregnancy; infants under 8 months.

## Background

HHS dismissed ACIP members last year; a court challenge followed but expert leadership hasn't been restored. Emory epidemiologist Benjamin Lopman noted: "The science is as strong as ever. What's changed is that CDC and the ACIP have not been there."
