# Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years in cloud deal

Anthropic has committed $11.6 billion over seven years to Akamai's cloud infrastructure — more than six times a previously reported $1.8 billion arrangement from May. The deal is conditional, depending on Akamai meeting delivery and service requirements, with either party able to exit under certain conditions.

The agreement is Akamai's largest ever and focuses notably on CPUs rather than GPUs, reflecting growing demand as AI agents take on more computational tasks. Akamai doesn't expect revenue from the deal in 2026, projecting $150–300 million in the second half of 2027 and roughly $1.7 billion annually by end of 2028. To support the deal, Akamai plans to spend approximately $5.5 billion on capacity buildout, plus $1.7 billion in accelerated component purchases this year.

In an unusual arrangement, Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for nonvoting preferred stock convertible into 7.7 million common shares — up to roughly 5% of outstanding stock at $111.33 per share. About 2% vests upon first payment, with the remainder unlocking at approximately 1% per additional $3 billion Anthropic spends, potentially growing the total deal to around $20 billion.

This structure inverts the typical "circular AI deal" pattern, where suppliers invest in labs; here the supplier grants its customer a growing equity stake. The arrangement mirrors a similar structure AMD used with OpenAI in 2025.

Akamai shares rose as much as 17% in after-hours trading following the announcement.
