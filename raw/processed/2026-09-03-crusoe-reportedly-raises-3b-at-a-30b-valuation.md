# Crusoe reportedly raises $3B at a $30B valuation

Crusoe, a data center developer whose clients include Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI, has reportedly closed a $3 billion funding round at a $30 billion valuation, per Bloomberg.

The round is co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, with participation from Mubadala Capital, an Abu Dhabi sovereign wealth fund subsidiary.

A key catalyst: Crusoe recently secured a five-year, $13 billion cloud contract with quantitative trading firm Jane Street to provide GPU and AI infrastructure.

This raise follows a $1.38 billion Series E at a $10 billion valuation just ten months prior. Founded in 2018 as a flared natural gas-powered crypto mining company, Crusoe has since evolved into a major AI infrastructure and hyperscale data center provider.

The company has also reportedly held IPO discussions with investment banks including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.
