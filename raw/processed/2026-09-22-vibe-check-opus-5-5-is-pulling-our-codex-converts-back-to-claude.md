# Vibe Check: Opus 5.5 Is Pulling Our Codex Converts Back to Claude

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 is winning back users who had migrated to competing models. Priced at $4/million input tokens and $20/million output tokens — roughly 60% cheaper than Fable 5.1 — it delivers near-comparable performance at significantly lower cost.

## Key Findings by Domain

**Coding:** Kieran Klaassen rates it ~90% as capable as Fable 5.1, producing cleaner, more idiomatic code. It follows workflow instructions that Opus 5 ignored. However, it can deliver apps with broken core screens.

**Writing:** Best readability scores measured (Flesch-Kincaid ~6.95 grade level), and collaborative to work with. Persistent flaw: it buries the lead, often taking nearly twice as many sentences to make a point.

**Knowledge Work:** Strong analyst and planner, but struggles with timed deliverables — one test saw it build training materials and run out of time without producing the requested schedule.

**Agent Behavior:** Follows instructions reliably now, but will consume entire token budgets if unconstrained. Tyler Nishida noted it autonomously rejected 90%+ of its own UI outputs for reasons he couldn't trace to any rubric.

## Team Verdicts

- **Kieran Klaassen** — Replacing Fable 5.1 as daily driver
- **Tyler Nishida** — "My jaw dropped at least five or six times"
- **Mike Taylor** — Positive but not gold; prefers Fable for harder problems
- **Dan Shipper** — Calls it "a smaller Fable"; still 80/20 favoring Codex

## Bottom Line

Opus 5.5 is recommended for visual/interface work, creative collaboration, and analytical tasks requiring judgment. Stay with Fable 5.1 for high-stakes or large-scale problems. Use Astra/Sol when deadlines are firm or budget control matters.
