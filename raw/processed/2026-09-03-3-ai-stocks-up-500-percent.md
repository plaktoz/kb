# 3 AI Stocks Up 500% or More in the Past Year That Could Have More Room to Run

Three AI-related stocks that surged 500%+ over the prior year may have further upside.

## Sandisk (SNDK) — Up ~2,900%

The pure-play NAND flash memory maker benefited from a supply-demand imbalance as capacity was cut post-pandemic while demand for AI storage surged. The company signed long-term deals worth "a minimum of $42 billion" and projected "mid-to-high teens" revenue growth through 2030. Despite gains, it trades at a forward P/E of just 7.

## Micron (MU) — Up ~700%

Micron earns 76% of revenue from DRAM, driven heavily by high-bandwidth memory (HBM) demand tied to AI chips. Supply constraints are structural — HBM requires "upward of 3 times the wafer capacity of regular DRAM" — keeping prices elevated. It trades at a forward P/E of 6.

## Lumentum (LITE) — Up ~550%

Lumentum makes indium phosphide lasers for optical data transmission, holding up to 60% market share. Its 1,000+ patents and manufacturing scale create durable competitive advantages as AI data centers shift away from copper wiring.
