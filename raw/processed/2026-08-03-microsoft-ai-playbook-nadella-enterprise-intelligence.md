# Microsoft's new AI playbook: Satya Nadella wants enterprises to stop outsourcing their intelligence

Microsoft closed fiscal 2026 with annual revenue of $331 billion, Microsoft Cloud crossing $214 billion, and Azure surpassing $100 billion in annual revenue. But the real story of the earnings call was Satya Nadella's vision of a future in which every company runs its own AI-powered learning machine.

**"Every firm needs its own learning machine."** Nadella argued the future belongs not to companies that simply plug into external AI models, but to those that build systems preserving their own institutional knowledge. "The models are an input, not some extraction of the knowledge of the enterprise," he said — models become interchangeable commodities while data, context, and memory stay under the customer's control.

**Copilot becomes a "super app."** Copilot is evolving from an Office chatbot into a "super app" combining chat, coding, autonomous agents, and collaborative workflows — moving from chat to "Cowork" and eventually "Autopilots" (long-running autonomous agents). Microsoft now has more than 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, with net seat additions more than doubling quarter over quarter, and weekly engagement now comparable to Outlook and Teams.

**"Seat plus consumption" pricing.** Microsoft is shifting from pure per-seat software licensing to a model where companies also pay based on how much AI they consume — aligning pricing with business outcomes and expanding Microsoft's addressable market.

**Azure demand still exceeds supply.** CFO Amy Hood said "demand continues to exceed available supply." Azure revenue surged 43% during the quarter; Microsoft added 31 new data centers across five continents (88 for the year) plus another gigawatt of capacity, and expects capex to reach $175 billion in calendar 2026.

**No single-model dependency.** "You can't depend on any one model," Nadella said. Microsoft has built an ecosystem of more than 11,000 models spanning OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, xAI, and its own MAI family, with a five-fold increase since the start of the year in customers using models from multiple providers — citing the Hugging Face security incident as evidence enterprises need redundancy.

**GitHub as the agent economy's backbone.** GitHub Copilot now has 50 million users; GitHub itself has 225 million developers. One in three pull requests on GitHub now involves an AI agent, and GitHub Copilot revenue accelerated more than 60% quarter over quarter.

**Cybersecurity as an agent battle.** Microsoft unveiled Project Perception, a multi-agent security system with "red-team," "blue-team," and "green-team" AI agents that simulate attacks, investigate threats, and recommend fixes.

Microsoft also launched Frontier Co., a new engineering organization embedding 6,000 industry and engineering experts inside customer organizations to help redesign how they work — reflecting Microsoft's ambition to become the infrastructure layer on which the modern enterprise thinks, learns, and evolves.
