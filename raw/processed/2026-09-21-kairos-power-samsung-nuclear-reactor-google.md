# Kairos Power Gets Up to $100M from Samsung Group to Build Nuclear Reactor for Google

Kairos Power has selected Samsung C&T as its engineering partner for a 50-megawatt demonstration reactor being built for Google, targeting completion by 2030.

Samsung C&T will contribute an equity investment of $70 million plus "in-kind" engineering services, totaling up to $100 million. The firm brings experience having "built or helped build about a dozen nuclear reactors around the globe."

Google originally contracted with Kairos in fall 2024, seeking roughly half a gigawatt of nuclear capacity by 2035. Two reactors are currently under construction in Oak Ridge, Tennessee:

- **Hermes 1** – a low-power demonstrator for design refinement
- **Hermes 2** – the first commercial-scale unit, whose output counts as the initial 50 MW toward the Google agreement

Both received NRC construction approval in late 2024. Hermes 2 uses a fluoride salt-cooled high-temperature design — never before built at commercial scale — which keeps operating pressures low, reducing blowout risk. Fuel is TRISO-type, encapsulating uranium in ceramic and carbon layers within billiard ball-sized spheres to prevent meltdowns.
