# What's Going On With Microsoft Stock?

Microsoft Corp (NASDAQ:MSFT) shares rose Thursday after the company announced a new AI partnership with Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN this week, with broader strength across software stocks adding to the momentum.

## Microsoft, HUMAIN announce long-term AI partnership

Microsoft revealed it's joining forces with HUMAIN, an AI company backed by Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, to bring AI tools into wider use among businesses across the kingdom and elsewhere. The alliance connects HUMAIN's Arabic-language ALLAM models to Microsoft's Foundry platform and 365 Copilot, giving companies a way to build AI applications built specifically for Arabic speakers. Both sides described this as an opening move, with more collaboration planned across models, software tools, hardware and infrastructure. Microsoft president Brad Smith described the arrangement as bringing together Microsoft's AI systems with HUMAIN's command of Arabic-language technology, built to satisfy the stricter security and compliance demands large companies and governments require.

## Moonshot in talks with Microsoft on revenue sharing

Separately, Chinese startup Moonshot is reportedly in talks with Microsoft, Amazon and Google over deals that would put its Kimi K3 model on their cloud services in exchange for a cut of resulting revenue. Moonshot is said to be pushing for close to 30% of whatever revenue K3 generates, though negotiations haven't progressed far. Independent testing has been kind to K3, with Arena.ai naming it the top performer in a web-interface benchmark.

## Broader software strength follows Salesforce's strong quarter

Software stocks broadly caught a bid after Salesforce blew past Wall Street's revenue and profit targets and raised its forecast, with CEO Marc Benioff highlighting a four-year high in new order growth and Agentforce usage multiplying sixfold. Investors extended that optimism to technology names more broadly, including Microsoft, whose shares were up 1.05% at $501.49 at time of publication.
