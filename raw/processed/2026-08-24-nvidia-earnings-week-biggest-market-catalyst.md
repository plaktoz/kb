# Top Strategist Warns Nvidia's Earnings on Wednesday Could Hit the Entire Market

Seema Shah, chief global strategist at Principal Asset Management, told CNBC that Nvidia sits at the center of this week's market setup, with its upcoming quarterly report positioned as the critical test of whether the AI capital-spending cycle can start showing profitable returns. Nvidia reports fiscal Q2 2027 results on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, after market close.

Shah identifies NVDA's August 26 earnings as the week's top market catalyst. Prediction markets assign 96% odds of a headline earnings beat but only a 43% chance NVDA closes above $220 after the report. Nvidia's Q1 posted $82 billion in revenue, up 85% year-over-year, with Q2 guidance set at $91 billion, excluding China data center compute.

Guidance for the coming report calls for revenue of $91 billion +/- 2% and non-GAAP gross margin of 75% plus or minus 50 basis points, with no China data center compute revenue included in the outlook. CEO Jensen Huang has argued that "Compute is revenue. Compute is profit" and that "Tokens are now profitable," positioning Nvidia's platform as the monetization proof point Shah says investors now demand.
