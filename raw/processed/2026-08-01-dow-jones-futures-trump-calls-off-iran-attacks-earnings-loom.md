# Dow Jones Futures: Trump Calls Off Iran Attacks; SpaceX, AMD, Sandisk, Eli Lilly Earnings Loom

President Trump said Saturday night he is calling off major new strikes against Iran after requests from several Mideast countries, easing geopolitical tension heading into a heavy earnings week (SpaceX, AMD, Sandisk, Datadog, Eli Lilly). The stock market closed last week with solid gains across major indexes despite mixed Big Tech earnings, rising crude oil prices, and a Treasury yield spike tied to Fed doubts.

SpaceX reports earnings for the first time since its June IPO, with investors watching Starlink growth, AI infrastructure costs, and the Starship roadmap; its stock sits at record lows well below the $135 IPO price, and its IPO lockup expires two days after the earnings report, freeing insiders to sell. Sandisk, Western Digital, and AMD will offer insight into AI hardware demand.

Market recap: the Dow climbed 1% last week, the S&P 500 gained 1.05% (back above its 50-day line), the Nasdaq jumped 1.6% off a three-month low, and the small-cap Russell 2000 edged up less than 0.1%. Microsoft and Amazon helped spur an AI-led rebound after a hedge-fund-driven liquidation had intensified losses earlier in the week. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to a fresh 18-month high of 4.74%; the 30-year hit a 19-year high of 5.27%.

Among ETFs, the iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV) surged 7.5% while the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) slumped 3.7%. The Financial Select SPDR ETF (XLF) climbed 1.1%. The author cautions that a couple of good days don't confirm a durable rally given repeated failed win streaks in recent months, and that rising long-term yields remain a headwind.
