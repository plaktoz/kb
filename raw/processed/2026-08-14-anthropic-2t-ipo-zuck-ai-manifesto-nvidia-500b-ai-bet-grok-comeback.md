# Anthropic's $2T IPO, Zuck's AI Manifesto, Nvidia's $500B AI Bet, Grok's Comeback

All-In with Chamath, Jason, Sacks & Friedberg — podcast transcript via HappyScribe

## Anthropic IPO (~$2T target)

Investors reportedly expect an October IPO at a $2 trillion valuation, with annualized revenue projected between $100–$120B by year-end — roughly a 10x annual growth rate. Gavin Baker expressed some skepticism about the $2T figure, suggesting banker leaks often sandbag the number. He noted Anthropic appears profitable, calling token economics "overwhelmingly profitable for everyone in the chain."

## Zuckerberg's AI Manifesto

Zuckerberg published a ~6,500-word essay titled *"The Future Is for Everyone."* Sacks summarized its core argument: "the notion that AI is so dangerous that the only safe path is an extreme concentration of power seems inherently problematic." The hosts broadly agreed with its pro-decentralization, open-source stance, framing it as a counter to the EA/Anthropic worldview.

## Nvidia's $500B AI Financing Plan

Jensen Huang outlined a plan partnering with Goldman, BlackRock, and others to treat GPUs as financeable income-producing assets — similar to aircraft financing. Baker suggested Nvidia could become "a very large cloud with a capital-light business" through revenue-sharing arrangements. The main risk flagged: a potential compute glut analogous to post-dot-com dark fiber.

## Grok's Comeback

XAI's Grok 4.6 was highlighted as reaching near-frontier quality at significantly lower cost. Baker noted that most investors overlook Grok entirely when evaluating SpaceX, despite its rapid competitive progress in roughly six months.
