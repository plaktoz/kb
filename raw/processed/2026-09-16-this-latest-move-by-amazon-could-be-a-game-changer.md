# This Latest Move by Amazon Could be a Game Changer

Amazon is pursuing an aggressive retail expansion called **Project Mercury** — adding 1,000+ same-day fulfillment centers over five years, a tenfold increase over its current 85 locations. The goal: place a same-day facility "within 10 miles of 80% of Prime customers by 2031."

Currently, Walmart holds a geographic advantage, with stores near ~90% of the U.S. population. Amazon intends to close that gap by investing roughly $7 billion through 2027, betting that faster delivery drives more frequent purchases and higher margins.

On the cloud side, AWS grew 37% year-over-year in Q2 2026 — its fastest pace in 18 quarters — holding a 28% market share. North American e-commerce sales reached $426 billion in 2025, up 10%.

Despite strong fundamentals across both segments, Amazon trades near an all-time valuation low, "less than 20 times earnings," which the author views as a buying opportunity amid broader AI-related investor uncertainty.
