# WorkTime's 2026 productivity study featured on MSN

WorkTime Featured on MSN for [Research on Meeting Overload and Productivity](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/general/why-meetings-kill-productivity-and-what-to-do-about-it/ar-AA1X49Sw)

WorkTime has been cited in a recent article on MSN examining how excessive meetings and workplace distractions erode business productivity. According to WorkTime data referenced in the piece, U.S. employees spend at least 21.5% of their workweek in meetings, with up to 60% of their overall time consumed by "work about work" rather than core tasks. The article highlights these metrics to illustrate how constant meeting interruptions disrupt deep focus, increase cognitive overload, and lead to billions in lost productivity globally.
