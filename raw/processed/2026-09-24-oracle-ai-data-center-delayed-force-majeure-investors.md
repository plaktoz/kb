# Oracle Wants Protection If Its AI Data Center Is Delayed. Here's What That Changes for Investors.

Oracle issued a force majeure notice to Blue Owl Capital's subsidiary — the developer of its "Project Jupiter" AI data center in New Mexico — citing pipeline delays that could push the project past its 2028 deadline. The notice seeks to defer rent payments if the facility isn't delivered on time, though Oracle still owes certain fees regardless.

Despite the notice, Oracle publicly insisted "Project Jupiter remains on our planned schedule." However, investor anxiety is mounting: the $18 billion in project financing has fallen to roughly 89–91 cents on the dollar, signaling lender stress. Local opposition is significant, with 65% of respondents in an Albuquerque Journal poll opposing data center construction in the state. Two separate natural gas pipeline plans have been blocked by state regulators.

Oracle's cloud infrastructure unit (OCI) posted 121% revenue growth to $7.4 billion last quarter, with remaining performance obligations reaching $664 billion. However, free cash flow ran negative by $5.4 billion last quarter, and the company carries $125 billion in long-term debt — excluding Project Jupiter's financing and $288 billion in uncommenced lease commitments.

Oracle's expansion has been characterized as a "bet-the-farm approach" to AI infrastructure, with the force majeure development as further evidence of mounting financial risk.
