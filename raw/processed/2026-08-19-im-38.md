# I'm 38

Scott Young reflects on turning 38, noting continued progress from his "Foundations" project. He remains consistent with daily exercise, completing a mountain race (16 km, 1400 m elevation). He created an online course called *Everyday Energy*, covering motivation, productivity, health, and well-being across 75+ lessons.

**Plans for the coming year** center on YouTube video creation, acknowledging that "video production is a lot more time-consuming than writing," which previously prevented serious commitment to the medium. He cites channels like 3Blue1Brown and Veritasium as inspirations for rigorous yet engaging content.

**Personal goals** include:
- Possibly entering a half-marathon or triathlon
- Broader reading beyond project-focused research
- Prioritizing friendships, noting that "the emergence of AI has served as a reminder of how much value flows through real relationships"

He closes by expressing gratitude toward longtime readers, having written for the site for over two decades.
