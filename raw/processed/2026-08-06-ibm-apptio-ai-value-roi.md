# IBM Introduces Apptio AI Value & ROI to Close the Gap Between AI Spend and Business Results

Apptio, an IBM company, announced the public preview of IBM Apptio AI Value & ROI, giving technology and finance executives, AI governance teams, and business leaders a single view of AI spending — including token costs — and its connection to measurable business results. It centralizes AI initiatives and tracks metrics across revenue, cost, speed, productivity, and risk. IBM Cloudability provides visibility and attribution of token spend, while AI Value & ROI connects that spend to business outcomes.

The launch targets a widening gap between AI investment and accountability: Gartner estimates 84% of finance leaders have not been able to measure the ROI of AI initiatives, and among those who tried, roughly two in five succeeded in quantifying returns. "AI is facing a cost crisis, and organizations are under growing pressure to demonstrate business value," said Bill Lobig, VP, IBM Apptio, noting one client saw a 50% reduction in costs, freeing funding for new AI initiatives.

Key capabilities include Proof Metrics and Results (connecting each AI initiative to business outcomes and customer-selected proof metrics like cycle time, cost avoided, conversion rate, or incident volume, tracked over time), a flexible framework supporting multiple inventory/metric sources, and integration with IBM Apptio AI TCO & Usage and IBM Cloudability for cost and consumption inputs. IBM Apptio AI Value & ROI is in public preview for IBM Apptio Costing Standard and AI TCO & Usage customers, with general availability planned for Q3 2026.
