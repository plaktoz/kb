# Common Erectile Dysfunction Drug Linked to Glaucoma, Researchers Say

A new study published in the *British Journal of Ophthalmology* found that long-term daily use of tadalafil (Cialis) — prescribed for enlarged prostate/urinary symptoms — may elevate glaucoma risk in men.

The study analyzed 70,000+ males (ages 40+) with no prior glaucoma history, comparing tadalafil users vs. non-users over five years. Tadalafil users showed:
- 22% higher likelihood of developing glaucoma
- 32% higher risk of ocular hypertension
- 33% higher risk of primary open-angle glaucoma
- 33% more likely to begin glaucoma treatment

## Expert Context

Experts emphasized the absolute risk remains modest. Craig Comiter, MD (Stanford), noted that the increased absolute risk was "less than 1% greater with daily tadalafil." S. Adam Ramin, MD, stated the concern is relevant primarily for daily, long-term users — not occasional erectile dysfunction users.

## About Glaucoma

Glaucoma, typically caused by elevated intraocular pressure, damages the optic nerve and can cause permanent vision loss. Over 4 million Americans are affected; roughly half are undiagnosed. Treatments include medications, laser therapy, and surgery, though no cure exists.
