# Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

Google announced two new AI voice models designed to make AI conversations more natural and intelligent:

- **Gemini 3.8 Live** – Optimized for scale and cost efficiency, offering fluid dialogue, real-time visual grounding, and automatic language detection across 97 languages mid-conversation.
- **Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking** – Designed for complex, high-stakes tasks requiring multi-step reasoning while maintaining uninterrupted conversational flow.

## Key Capabilities

- Processes visual inputs in near real-time
- Executes background tool/API calls without pausing conversation
- Uses verbal cues like *"Let me check that…"* to signal active reasoning
- All audio output is watermarked via **SynthID** for AI-content transparency

## Performance Highlights

- Extended Thinking ranked **#1 on Artificial Analysis' Speech to Speech Quality Index** (82.6)
- Leads agentic benchmarks: **68.6% on τ-Voice**, **35.1% on Sierra's τ-Voice-banking**
- Scores **97.7% on Big Bench Audio**

## Availability

Both models are rolling out via the **Gemini API**, **Google AI Studio**, **Google Workspace** (Docs, Gmail, Keep), **Search Live**, and enterprise preview on **Gemini Enterprise**.
