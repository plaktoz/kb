# TED, Google Seek Educator Perspectives on AI

## An initiative from TED and Google will select five educators at elementary, secondary and postsecondary levels to present their ideas about artificial intelligence in teaching and learning at TED2027.

A new initiative from Google and nonprofit media company TED seeks input from educators worldwide about the role of artificial intelligence in teaching and learning, with plans to select five participants to present their ideas at TED's annual conference in San Diego next March, according to a recent news release shared with the Center for Digital Education via email.

The initiative is open to educators at the elementary, secondary and higher education levels who are using AI in instruction or curriculum development. It begins with a free, hourlong online professional development workshop, which launched Oct. 5, that helps participants identify and develop ideas for potential presentations.

Participants can submit proposals through Nov. 15 for consideration by TED's selection team, according to the news release.

"So much of the conversation around AI in education is being led by people operating outside of the field. That's backwards; educators should be leading that conversation," Logan Smalley, TED's head of learning and education, said in a public statement.

The initiative arrives against a backdrop of public discussions about whose perspectives should inform decisions about AI in education. District leaders and students at the Bridges conference in July emphasized the [importance of involving students in decisions](https://www.govtech.com/education/k-12/bridges-2026-ai-decisions-should-start-with-students) about AI policies, curriculum and classroom expectations, rather than relying exclusively on top-down approaches.

Other recent initiatives have focused on preparing educators to use AI in their professional practice. In July 2025, the American Federation of Teachers, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthology [announced a $23 million initiative](https://www.govtech.com/education/k-12/tech-giants-aft-launch-national-ai-training-academy-for-educators) to provide AI training and certification opportunities to 400,000 educators by 2030.

While that program focuses on developing educators' AI skills, the TED and Google initiative appears to emphasize sharing their experiences and ideas with TED's global audience, which extends well beyond the education sector and into broader public conversations about technology and society.

The content of TED's global platform was [viewed or listened to more than 1.7 billion times in 2025](https://impact.ted.com/), according to an internal impact report by TED.

According to the news release, the self-paced Idea Workshop, which participants can complete on their own schedules, includes three lessons designed to help educators identify a compelling topic, draw from their professional experiences and organize their ideas into a presentation. Participants who complete the workshop will also receive access to TED's public speaking course and educational resources from Google, including its AI Educator Series.

Five selected educators will develop and deliver talks during a community-stage session in March at TED2027 in San Diego and will subsequently be published on TED's global platform, the news release said.

"Educators at every level, from primary schools to universities, are experiencing firsthand the opportunities and challenges this profoundly powerful technology is creating in this profoundly important field," Smalley's statement read.
