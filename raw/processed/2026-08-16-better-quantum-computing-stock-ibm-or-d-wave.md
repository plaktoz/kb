# Better Quantum Computing Stock: IBM or D-Wave?

**Author:** Rick Orford
**Published:** August 16, 2026, *The Motley Fool*

The article compares IBM (NYSE: IBM) and D-Wave (NASDAQ: QBTS) as quantum computing investments. The core argument is that IBM's financial strength, enterprise reach, and staying power may matter more than which company achieves quantum breakthroughs first.

The piece notes that the "ultimate quantum winner may not be the company that arrives first, but the one that stays in the race the longest" — suggesting endurance over speed as the key investment thesis.

Stock prices referenced (from August 13, 2026):
- IBM: $235.59 (-1.34%)
- D-Wave: $16.99 (-5.08%)

*Note: The Motley Fool holds positions in IBM. Rick Orford held no positions in the mentioned stocks at publication.*
