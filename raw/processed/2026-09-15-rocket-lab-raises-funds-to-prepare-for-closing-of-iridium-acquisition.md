# Rocket Lab Raises Funds To Prepare For Closing Of Iridium Acquisition

Rocket Lab (RKLB) completed a $1.94 billion at-the-market equity offering, issuing 29.3 million shares. Combined with existing cash, this covers the full Iridium Communications (IRDM) acquisition cost plus partial debt repayment. The deal is expected to close mid-2027, pending regulatory approval.

Rocket Lab also amended its existing $1.77 billion credit facility and canceled a $3.6 billion bridge loan originally secured for the transaction.

CFO Adam Spice described Iridium as providing the "application piece," calling the deal "incredibly transformational" in fulfilling Rocket Lab's end-to-end space ambitions. He suggested future acquisitions remain possible, particularly in the constellation and applications segments.

Iridium shareholders vote on the deal September 24. Both boards have unanimously approved the combination.

Rocket Lab remains unprofitable while investing in its Neutron reusable rocket. Spice previously indicated EBITDA would turn positive following a successful Neutron test launch, with positive cash flow expected "within 18 months to two years thereafter."
