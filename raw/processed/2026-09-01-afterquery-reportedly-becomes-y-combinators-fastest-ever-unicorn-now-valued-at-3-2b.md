# AfterQuery reportedly becomes Y Combinator's fastest-ever unicorn, now valued at $3.2B

AI training-data startup AfterQuery has reportedly closed a round valuing it at $3.2 billion — a more than 10x jump from its $300 million valuation just five months prior, when it raised a $30M Series A in April.

YC partner Gustaf Alströmer called it the fastest launch-to-unicorn in the accelerator's history. The company's two founders, ages 22 and 23, participated in YC's Winter 2025 cohort roughly 18 months ago.

By April, AfterQuery had disclosed an annualized revenue run rate of $100 million, with customers including Nvidia, Legora, and Korean AI lab Motif Technologies.

The startup occupies a niche similar to Mercor and Scale, employing specialists — doctors, lawyers, and other professionals — for model training. Its distinct focus is teaching models and agents how to work like experts, described as "encoding the patterns, decisions, and reasoning of the world's best practitioners."

Forbes first reported the funding. AfterQuery did not respond to requests for comment.
