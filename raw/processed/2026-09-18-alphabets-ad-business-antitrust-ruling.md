# Alphabet's Ad Business Got a Lift From a Key Antitrust Ruling, Helping End Google's Worst Losing Streak in Over a Decade. Is Sundar Pichai's Alphabet Stock a Buy Now?

A federal judge rejected the Justice Department's attempt to force Alphabet to divest its ad exchange and related tools, yet investors largely shrugged off the ruling as GOOGL's downtrend continued.

Despite shifting investor focus toward AI and cloud, Google Advertising still represented 68% of Alphabet's Q2 2026 revenue — down from 74% a year prior and 89% a decade ago. Google Cloud accounted for 21%, and CEO Sundar Pichai suggested Waymo could "meaningfully" contribute to revenue as soon as next year.

The ruling matters more than investors appreciate. Alphabet's trailing 12-month free cash flow reached $53 billion as of Q2 2026, but the company spent $132 billion in capital expenditures over the same period. Advertising revenue may be the key force keeping free cash flow positive while Alphabet funds its massive AI investments.

Google Advertising's gradual decline — rather than a forced, abrupt divestiture — gives Alphabet a smoother transition toward cloud and autonomous vehicle revenue, which is a significant positive for shareholders.
