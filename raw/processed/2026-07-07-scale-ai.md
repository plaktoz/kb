# Scale AI

Scale AI, Inc. is an American AI infrastructure and software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. Originally a data annotation firm, it now provides RLHF services, LLM evaluation, and enterprise software for building and deploying AI applications.

Its research arm, the Safety, Evaluation and Alignment Lab, focuses on evaluating and aligning LLMs, and co-created the **Humanity's Last Exam** benchmark. Subsidiaries include **Remotasks** (computer vision/autonomous vehicles) and **Outlier** (LLM data annotation).

- **Founded:** 2016
- **Founders:** Alexandr Wang, Lucy Guo (via Y Combinator)
- **Current CEO:** Jason Droege
- **Revenue:** $870 million (2024)
- **Employees:** ~1,200 (2025)
- **Major owner:** Meta Platforms (49%)

## History

### Early Years (2016–2019)

Founded by Wang and Guo, who had previously worked together at Quora. Early investors included Dragoneer, Tiger Global, and Index Ventures. Co-founder Guo was fired in 2018.

In August 2019, Peter Thiel's Founders Fund invested $100 million, pushing Scale's valuation past $1 billion — achieving **unicorn status**.

### Growth (2019–2025)

Key milestones:

- **2020:** Contracted with the U.S. Department of Defense
- **2021:** Valuation reached $7 billion; former U.S. CTO Michael Kratsios joined as managing director
- **January 2022:** Won a $250 million federal contract
- **February 2022:** Launched Automated Damage Identification Service to assess destruction from Russia's invasion of Ukraine
- **January 2023:** Laid off 20% of workforce
- **May 2023:** Deployed LLM ("Donovan") on a classified U.S. Army network — first AI company to do so
- **August 2023:** Named OpenAI's "preferred partner" to fine-tune GPT-3.5; services contributed to ChatGPT's development
- **March 2024:** Valuation neared $13 billion (Accel-led round)
- **May 2024:** Raised $1 billion more (Amazon and Meta among investors); valuation hit $14 billion
- **January 2025:** Released *Humanity's Last Exam* benchmark with the Center for AI Safety
- **February 2025:** Five-year partnership with the Qatari government for AI-powered public services
- **March 2025:** Multimillion-dollar Pentagon deal for **Thunderforge**, aimed at using AI to coordinate military asset movements

### Meta Investment (2025)

On June 10, 2025, Meta agreed to purchase a **49% non-voting stake** in Scale AI for approximately $14.8 billion. Scale remains an independent entity. Alexandr Wang moved to a senior role at Meta; Jason Droege (former Uber executive and Scale's chief strategy officer) became CEO.

### Scale Labs (2026)

Launched in March 2026 as an expanded research division building on the earlier SEAL lab. Focus areas include AI capabilities research, post-training evaluation, enterprise deployment, and public benchmarks such as SWE-Atlas and Voice Showdown.

## Remotasks

Established in 2017 as a crowdworking platform for labeled data creation, particularly for computer vision and autonomous vehicles. Operating in Southeast Asia and Africa, it has faced significant criticism:

- Pay for some tasks dropped to under one cent due to competition after expansion to India and Venezuela
- Late payments reportedly common; some workers received only a fraction of promised pay
- A 2022 Oxford Internet Institute study found it met fair work standards in only **1 out of 10 criteria**
- Early 2024: Terminated operations in Kenya, Nigeria, and Pakistan without clear explanation

## Outlier

A separate platform for generative AI data work, focused on LLM fine-tuning. Contributors typically hold advanced degrees or have specialized expertise. Tasks involve content evaluation and RLHF.
