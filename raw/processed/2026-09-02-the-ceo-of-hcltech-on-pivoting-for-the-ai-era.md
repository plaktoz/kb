# The CEO of HCLTech on Pivoting for the AI Era

C. Vijayakumar reflects on how generative AI's emergence in late 2022 forced HCLTech to reassess its core business model. IT services had long operated on a linear principle — more revenue meant more staff. Each prior tech wave (internet, digitalization, cloud) simply expanded demand for engineers and consultants.

AI breaks that pattern entirely. It has changed "the economics of our business," enabling vast knowledge work to be completed "dramatically faster with much less human effort." This isn't marginal productivity improvement — it fundamentally severs the traditional link between workforce growth and revenue growth.

HCLTech's recognition is that surviving the AI era requires a genuine strategic pivot, not incremental adaptation.
