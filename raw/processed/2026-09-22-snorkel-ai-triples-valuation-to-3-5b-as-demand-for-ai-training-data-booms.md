# Snorkel AI triples valuation to $3.5B as demand for AI training data booms

Snorkel AI raised a $350M Series E led by Insight Partners and S32, reaching a $3.5B valuation — nearly triple its prior $1.3B valuation from 17 months ago. Existing backers including Addition, Lightspeed, Greylock, GV, and Wells Fargo also participated.

The company pivoted last year from data-labeling software to a "data-as-a-service" model, delivering completed datasets using a hybrid of synthetic generation and human subject-matter experts. It now sells reinforcement learning environments and full datasets rather than human labor directly.

Snorkel reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375M, representing an 18x increase over the prior 12 months, driven by AI labs' demand for high-quality training data.

Peer companies are seeing similar surges: Mercor reached $2B gross annualized revenue, Handshake hit $1B, and Micro1 scaled to $500M — though those figures are gross, with 60–70% paid out to domain specialists.

Snorkel was founded by CEO Alex Ratner, emerging from Stanford AI research and launching commercially in 2019.
