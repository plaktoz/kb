# Novo Nordisk Shares Plunge After a Key Trial Fails. Should Investors Buy the Dip?

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/31/novo-nordisk-shares-plunge-after-a-key-trial-fails-should-investors-buy-the-dip/

---

By John Bromels | July 31, 2026

Novo Nordisk (NVO) dropped ~9.4% after its experimental heart drug ziltivekimab failed a Phase 3 trial called "Zeus." The drug is a monthly IL-6 inhibitor tested in ~6,300 patients with cardiovascular disease, kidney disease, and inflammation.

While ziltivekimab successfully reduced IL-6 and C-reactive protein levels, it failed to lower patients' risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) like heart attacks and strokes. Additionally, treated patients showed higher rates of serious infections than the placebo group.

Two related trials — "Hermes" (heart failure) and "Artemis" (acute heart attack) — remain ongoing, with results expected in early 2027. The failure won't affect 2026 operating profit guidance, though a non-cash impairment charge will hit Q3.

This decline follows steeper drops earlier in 2026 tied to weak guidance and a disappointing head-to-head trial of CagriSema versus Eli Lilly's tirzepatide. Despite setbacks, Novo Nordisk's semaglutide franchise (Ozempic, Wegovy) remains strong, with the GLP-1 market projected to reach $120 billion by 2030. The stock trades at a P/E of roughly 11, near historic lows, which the author views as a potential buying opportunity.
