# Databricks buys Row Zero and is scouting for more startups to acquire

Databricks acquired Row Zero, a cloud spreadsheet startup capable of handling over 1 million live rows. The deal originated when Databricks' finance team began using Row Zero alongside Databricks' AI agent, Genie, which lets users query business data through natural language.

CEO Ali Ghodsi noted that spreadsheets are a beloved interface for business analysts, making it logical to combine BI, agents, and spreadsheets. The integration keeps data secure within Databricks' cloud storage while allowing familiar spreadsheet-style interaction, avoiding insecure file sharing.

Row Zero was founded by former AWS and Tableau engineers and raised $10 million in May 2025 at an estimated $40 million valuation. Deal terms were undisclosed.

## Databricks' 2026 Acquisition Push

This acquisition is part of Databricks' broader 2026 strategy. Earlier purchases include:

- **Quotient AI** — agent evaluation and reinforcement learning
- **SiftD.ai** — interactive notebooks
- **Panther** — AI security operations, previously valued at $1.4 billion
- **Electric** — makers of PGlite, a lightweight Postgres database

Databricks recently closed $5 billion in new funding and reached $7 billion in annualized revenue. CEO Ghodsi told TechCrunch they "intend to do many more acquisitions like this."
