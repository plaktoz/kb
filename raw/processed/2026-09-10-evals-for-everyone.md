# Evals for Everyone

Every is building personal AI benchmarks for each employee — custom evaluation sets that test how well models handle individual work tasks, graded against personal standards.

Rather than relying on public benchmarks (where top models score ~93–96% on graduate science tests), the approach captures idiosyncratic preferences: where to place a comma, how many ideas belong on a slide.

## How it works

- Staff identify 5–10 recurring AI tasks
- Models run those tasks; employees review outputs and explain what's wrong
- Feedback becomes specific pass/fail checks
- An AI judge grades outputs against those checks
- Disagreements prompt refinement of the checks themselves

Head of evals Mike Taylor found that after running his benchmark, a smaller model (GPT-5.6 Luna) outperformed larger ones on many daily tasks — prompting him to expand testing toward harder assignments.

## Five-step DIY workflow

1. Identify a recurring task where AI keeps making mistakes
2. Save prompt + source files as a fixed test
3. Convert your feedback into yes/no checklist items
4. Have AI grade the output; compare its grades to yours
5. Resolve disagreements by clarifying checks, then retest

The goal: turn one-off corrections into a reusable system that improves over time — what Dan Shipper calls "a compounding loop."
