# Anthropic Could Publicly File IPO Paperwork as Soon as This Month

Anthropic is preparing to publicly file paperwork for its initial public offering (IPO) as early as the end of the month. According to Bloomberg, the AI startup is angling to match or surpass SpaceX's record-setting $75 billion June IPO.

If Anthropic sticks to its timeline, it would beat rival OpenAI to market — OpenAI is widely expected to IPO later this year or in early 2027.

The filing will give the outside world its first look at Anthropic's financials, including quarterly revenue growth and its biggest business segments. Anthropic was cofounded by former OpenAI employees Dario Amodei, who serves as CEO, and his sister Daniela Amodei in 2021, after being seen at one point as an also-ran next to OpenAI.
