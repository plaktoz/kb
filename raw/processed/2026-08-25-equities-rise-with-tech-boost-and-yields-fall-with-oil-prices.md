# Equities rise with tech boost and yields fall with oil prices

Global equities climbed Tuesday as investors anticipated Nvidia's earnings and incoming economic data. The MSCI global gauge gained 0.42%, while the S&P 500 rose 0.32% to 7,677.28 and the Nasdaq added 0.66%.

Strategist Tim Ghriskey noted that "technology is stronger today after the recent weakness," attributing much of the momentum to Nvidia anticipation, adding that Nvidia's valuation decline had attracted buyers "looking for a strong earnings report."

Treasury yields fell for a second consecutive day — the 10-year dropped approximately 8 basis points to 4.625% — partly linked to Treasury Secretary Bessent's expanded buyback program.

Oil fell approximately 3% to $82.36/barrel despite U.S. sanctions threats against Iran, as traders saw economic pressure as less disruptive to supply than military action would be. Brent settled at $88.58.

Bitcoin briefly topped $80,000 for the first time since mid-May before retreating slightly. Spot gold edged up 0.31% to $4,665.86/oz, though its rally stalled near a key resistance level ahead of Wednesday's Fed inflation data release.
