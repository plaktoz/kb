# Anthropic continues compute-gobbling streak in $45B deal with Nscale

Anthropic has agreed to rent roughly $45 billion in AI compute from Nscale, a British AI infrastructure firm founded in 2024. The compute will be delivered via Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips — a six-chip system representing the current frontier of chip design — from Nscale's West Virginia data center. Service is expected to begin in late 2027, spanning a six-year term.

This deal is part of an aggressive compute expansion campaign Anthropic has pursued over the past eight months:

- August 2026: $10B deal with cloud startup Volta (Norway data center)
- July 2026: $5B deal with AMD
- May 2026: Partnership with SpaceX, reportedly yielding "$1.25 billion worth of capacity each month"
- April 2026: Expanded Amazon partnership adding 5 gigawatts of compute; also expanded ties with Google and Broadcom

Anthropic is not alone in this race — Google, OpenAI, and Meta are similarly racing to secure computing capacity.
