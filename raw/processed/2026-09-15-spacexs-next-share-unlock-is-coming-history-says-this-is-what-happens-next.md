# SpaceX's Next Share Unlock Is Coming. History Says This Is What Happens Next.

SpaceX (NASDAQ: SPCX) faces a series of lockup expirations that could pressure its share price. Up to 328.4 million shares unlock on Sept. 24, with identical tranches following on Oct. 9 and Oct. 24. A larger batch — roughly 1.3 billion shares — becomes eligible after Q3 earnings, then ~800 million more on Dec. 8.

The most consequential event arrives June 12, 2027 — one year post-IPO — when CEO Elon Musk's approximately 48.4% ownership stake becomes eligible for sale.

The prior lockup expiration on Sept. 9 coincided with a 3.9% single-day price decline. SpaceX's stock currently trades around $143, down ~34% from its all-time high but near its $150 IPO opening price.

On the business side, Q2 results beat expectations: revenue of $7.81B (vs. $6.93B estimated) grew 92% year-over-year, and net loss narrowed to $541M from ~$1B prior-year. The company's long-term growth strategy centers heavily on AI, beyond its Starlink and rocket operations.

The article notes that lockup expirations don't guarantee declines — strong business results and market demand can offset selling pressure.
