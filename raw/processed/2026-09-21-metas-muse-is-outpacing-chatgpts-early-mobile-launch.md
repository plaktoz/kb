# Meta's Muse is outpacing ChatGPT's early mobile launch

Meta's AI app Muse has outperformed ChatGPT's early mobile numbers, according to estimates from Apptopia. In a comparable iOS-only view of the U.S. and Canada during each app's first 12 days, Muse recorded 1.8 million downloads versus ChatGPT's 1.3 million.

To account for differing launch strategies — ChatGPT debuted globally on iOS only, while Muse launched on both iOS and Android but limited to the U.S. and Canada — Apptopia isolated iOS data for an equivalent comparison.

On daily active users, Muse also leads: 642,000 U.S. daily users versus ChatGPT's 231,000 at the equivalent point. Even on iOS alone, Muse's 359,000 daily users still exceeded ChatGPT's comparable figure. Total global installs reached 2.8 million within 12 days.

Muse climbed from No. 2 to No. 1 on the U.S. App Store, surpassing ChatGPT. Apptopia also noted that over 95% of Muse's users are Facebook users and 63% are Instagram users, reflecting Meta's cross-promotion strength — a strategy previously used to grow Threads to 500+ million monthly users.
