# Goldman Sachs Just Gave AI Stock Fans a Cold, Hard Reality Check

Goldman Sachs strategist Ryan Hammond warns that hyperscalers — Amazon, Oracle, and Microsoft — must generate roughly $300 billion in AI revenue over the next few years just to break even on their AI investments.

Currently, hyperscaler cloud revenues are running about $70 billion above pre-AI trends (annualized, as of Q2 2026), with announced backlogs topping $1.5 trillion — still far short of what's needed.

Hammond estimated that AI users would need to spend $1 trillion annually on AI applications for hyperscalers to achieve solid investment returns and for the application layer to reach strong profit margins.

Despite this gap, investor enthusiasm remains high. The Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF (MAGS) climbed approximately 8% within a month, outpacing modest S&P 500 gains.

The core message: meaningful returns on AI investment are still years away — and breaking even comes first.
