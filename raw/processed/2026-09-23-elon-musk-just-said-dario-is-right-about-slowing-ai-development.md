# Elon Musk Just Said "Dario Is Right" About Slowing AI Development. Here's What That Endorsement Could Mean for Tesla's and xAI's AI Road Maps.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay urging AI companies to slow development pace to prioritize safety. Musk publicly responded on X: "Dario is right."

However, analysts suggest this verbal endorsement is unlikely to meaningfully alter Tesla or SpaceX's AI trajectories. Musk has expressed skepticism toward government regulation as the solution, instead favoring peer review among leading AI firms.

Both companies remain heavily committed to AI expansion:

- **Tesla** is rolling out a semi-autonomous ride-share network and targeting 1 million Optimus humanoid robots by 2030, powered by Grok AI
- **SpaceX** plans orbital data center satellite demonstrations by late 2027 and spent roughly **$23.5 billion** on AI capital expenditures in the first half of this year

While Musk may support AI safety dialogue, financial commitments make an actual slowdown implausible. Any shortfalls in Tesla or SpaceX timelines would more likely stem from "overly ambitious" planning than deliberate deceleration.
