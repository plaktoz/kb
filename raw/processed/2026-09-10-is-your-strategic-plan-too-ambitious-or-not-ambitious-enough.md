# Is Your Strategic Plan Too Ambitious? Or Not Ambitious Enough?

The article poses a central strategic dilemma: how ambitious should a strategic plan be? Setting targets too low risks settling for incremental progress, while overreaching can overwhelm an organization.

The piece addresses leaders weighing decisions such as entering new markets, launching new offerings, or creating new business divisions — situations where benchmarks and rough cost estimates exist, but the right level of ambition remains unclear.

"How ambitious should your strategic plan be? Aim too low, and you may settle for incremental gains. Aim too high, and you risk overwhelming the organization."

The article covers the topics of Strategy, Change Management, Leadership, and Leadership and Managing People.
