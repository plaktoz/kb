# Waymo is scaling fast. Here's what the fleet data shows.

Waymo has expanded from 3 cities in September 2024 to 15 U.S. cities today, now averaging 500,000 paid rides weekly. Its ~4,000-vehicle fleet is heavily concentrated, with roughly 80% in California and Texas.

Texas is the current growth hotspot — fleet size jumped 49% in three weeks, reaching 1,102 registered vehicles as of September 24, driven largely by its new "Ojai" robotaxi. The Ojai is a modified Zeekr RT minivan (built by Geely-owned Zeekr in China), shipped to the U.S. without Chinese connected-car tech, then outfitted with Waymo's 6th-generation self-driving system at an Arizona facility. It features Google's Gemini AI as an in-car assistant.

The Ojai is intended to reduce costs and accelerate scale, though current tariffs on Chinese-built vehicles are offsetting savings. Research firm MoffettNathanson projects Waymo will import approximately 5,100 Ojai vehicles by year-end.

The remaining ~800 vehicles operate across Arizona, Florida, and other states. Florida and Las Vegas are cited as likely targets for future fleet expansion.
