# CFTC Chairman Michael Selig Predicts a Massive Wave of Asset Tokenization

CFTC Chairman Michael Selig (Sept. 22) warned regulators and investors to prepare for "mass tokenization" of assets, predicting it will reshape markets faster than prior decades' changes. McKinsey projects tokenized assets will reach $1.9 trillion by 2030, up from roughly $38.6 billion today.

## What is tokenization?

Tokenization means representing asset ownership as a digital token on a blockchain.

The analogy used: buying food in plastic wrap doesn't give you upside from plastic wrap sales. Value accrues to the *services* handling tokenization and the *blockchain networks* hosting the assets.

## Investment Options Discussed

**Solana (SOL)**
- Leading network for stock tokenization (~$491M in tokenized stocks as of Sept. 25)
- Fast and inexpensive, suits frequent institutional trading
- Drawback: burns only half of base fees; issues ~60,000 SOL daily to validators vs. ~648 SOL destroyed daily

**Robinhood Markets (HOOD)** — highlighted as the strongest play
- Launched "Robinhood Chain" for tokenized stock trading
- Chain fees: $35.2M in first 25 days of September vs. $6.7M for all of August
- Revenue flows directly to shareholders via traditional equity structure

**Pons (PONS)** — higher-risk option
- Robinhood Chain's largest meme coin launchpad
- Pairs meme coins with tokenized stocks; burns most revenue via buybacks
- $31.5M in August fees, but revenue is highly variable

## Conclusion

The author recommends Solana or Robinhood as the most accessible tokenization investments, noting that crypto launchpad projects carry significantly higher risk.
