# Arm Stock Is Down More Than 40%. Here's Why I'm Staying on the Sidelines.

Arm Holdings (ARM) dropped nearly 10% Monday amid an AI stock selloff triggered by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay urging slower AI model development, with OpenAI's Sam Altman echoing support. The decline puts ARM roughly 40% below its 52-week high of $452.70.

The author argues the selloff is somewhat disconnected from Arm's actual business. Arm earns licensing fees and per-chip royalties — revenue that follows physical chip shipments, not AI training schedules. Fiscal Q1 2027 revenue rose 22% YoY to $1.29 billion, with non-GAAP EPS up 29% to $0.45.

Smartphones remain the largest royalty segment (~43% of fiscal 2026 royalty revenue). Data center royalties are growing fastest — more than doubling YoY — but stem from general-purpose Neoverse server chips, not AI accelerators directly.

The CFO noted royalty growth guidance was trimmed to "high teens" due to higher memory prices dampening smartphone sales — unrelated to AI model timelines.

The concern? Valuation. At ~79x forward earnings, the stock prices in years of AI-driven growth. The author concludes the business is healthy, but the price warrants staying on the sidelines.
