# Anthropic's CEO is about to have dinner with President Trump

Dario Amodei, Anthropic's chief executive, has had a busy weekend, including being the subject of a comedic sketch on a late-night sketch show, and now a planned evening meeting with the president at the White House. This will mark their first one-on-one meeting, coming amid diverging views on AI oversight — Amodei has proposed slowing AI's development pace, while Trump has dismissed safety concerns as politically motivated and floated renaming the technology as "super intelligence."

The article recaps prior friction between Anthropic and the administration, including a Pentagon designation labeling the company a supply-chain risk, which Anthropic is contesting legally, while noting relations with some other officials have reportedly improved recently.

The dinner plans were first reported by Axios, which TechCrunch says it verified independently through a source familiar with the matter.
