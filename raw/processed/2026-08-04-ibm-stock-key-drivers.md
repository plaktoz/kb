# International Business Machines Corp Stock (IBM) Closed Up by 3.51% on Aug 4: Key Drivers Unveiled

IBM closed up 3.51% on August 4, outperforming the Software & IT Services sector's 3.15% gain. The move reflects a shift in investor perception toward IBM's enterprise AI capabilities, driven primarily by accelerating adoption of the watsonx platform as institutional investors rotate toward value-oriented AI plays with proven revenue streams. IBM's consulting division has built a substantial bookings backlog by guiding large enterprises through AI implementation, providing long-term cash flow visibility that appeals to portfolio managers seeking defensive growth. Analysts maintain bullish sentiment (average price target $243.32) citing improved margins and IBM's focus on sovereign AI and data privacy, which resonates with regulated industries.

## Offsetting risks

The rally sits alongside significant company-specific risk. On August 2, securities law firms announced a formal investigation into whether IBM management misled investors about demand for the IBM Z mainframe line, following a 25% single-day share collapse after preliminary Q2 results revealed deal slippage. Enterprise clients are diverting budget from IBM's legacy software and mainframe infrastructure toward AI-specific hardware and memory — "budget crowding" that produced a preliminary Q2 revenue miss of roughly $700 million. Infrastructure revenue declined 7% year-over-year amid rising semiconductor costs, and management admitted it "did not adapt and move quickly enough" to supply chain price increases. A separate analyst downgrade to "Reduce" (price target cut to $191) flagged IBM's projected 10.7% earnings growth as far below the sector median of 19.2%, with $69.8 billion in total debt raising questions about dividend sustainability if free cash flow in consulting and legacy mainframe segments continues to slow.
