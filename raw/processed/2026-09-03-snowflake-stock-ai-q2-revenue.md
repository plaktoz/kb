# Snowflake Stock Skyrockets on AI-Driven Q2 Revenue Jump

**Author:** Ines Ferré, Senior Business Reporter
**Date:** Thursday, September 3, 2026
**Source:** Yahoo Finance
**source_url:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/snowflake-stock-skyrockets-on-ai-driven-q2-revenue-jump-130537300.html

---

## Summary

Snowflake (SNOW) shares jumped as much as 20% in premarket trading after the cloud data company delivered a strong second quarter, with revenue climbing 35% to $1.55 billion.

CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy credited AI adoption as a structural driver, saying the "agentic enterprise has created a powerful flywheel across our business, and that flywheel is accelerating."

Adjusted EPS came in at **$0.62** for fiscal Q2 2027.

## Key Drivers

- Customers using AI on Snowflake consume more data platform resources, amplifying revenue
- AI products contributed roughly **half** of the quarter's growth acceleration
- **Cortex** (AI coding assistant) and **CoWork** (enterprise chatbot) saw rapid adoption

## Year-to-Date Performance

SNOW shares are up more than **40%** year-to-date.
