# After Shocking Quarter, IBM Insists That AI Isn't Killing the Mainframe

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/after-shocking-quarter-ibm-insists-that-ai-isnt-killing-the-mainframe/

---

Author: Julie Bort
Published: July 22, 2026

IBM reported a 42% year-over-year decline in mainframe revenue in its most recent quarter, sending the stock down approximately 25%. The shock was amplified because IBM had been signaling mainframe resilience to investors throughout 2025.

IBM's explanation: customers are deferring mainframe hardware purchases not because they're abandoning the platform, but because they're reallocating IT budgets toward AI infrastructure — GPUs, accelerators, and AI cloud spend. IBM characterized the declines as a "timing issue" rather than a structural shift.

Skeptics note:
- A 42% decline is historically severe and difficult to dismiss as a timing blip
- Major enterprise AI projects are consuming IT budgets that would previously have funded mainframe upgrades
- Competitors including AWS and Google Cloud continue to offer mainframe migration incentives

IBM's counter-argument centers on AI workloads running on mainframes themselves — IBM's z-series supports on-premises AI inference, and the company sees mainframe-resident AI as a long-term differentiator for regulated industries (banking, healthcare, government).

Investor verdict: the market did not accept the "timing" framing. The stock decline reflects concern about whether AI cannibalization of IT budgets represents a durable headwind for legacy infrastructure.
