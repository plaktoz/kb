# Bill Ackman Built a $2.4 Billion Stake in Microsoft, Calling It a "Core Holding"

Pershing Square's Bill Ackman disclosed a major Microsoft position earlier this year, initially purchasing over 5.65 million shares worth roughly $2.1 billion. He later sold his Alphabet holdings to increase the stake to approximately 6.2 million shares, valued at $2.3 billion by end of June.

Tech stocks have become central to Pershing Square's strategy — four of its equity holdings are tech-oriented, including Uber, Meta, and Amazon, collectively representing 40% of the portfolio.

Ackman's shareholder letter noted that just two S&P 500 industry groups — semiconductors and IT hardware — drove "85% of the index's year-to-date gains" in the first half of the year.

Pershing Square's investment criteria favor companies that are "simple, predictable, and free cash flow-generative, with strong competitive positions." Ackman argues Microsoft fits this profile well, given ongoing AI expansion and the enduring demand for enterprise tech solutions.
