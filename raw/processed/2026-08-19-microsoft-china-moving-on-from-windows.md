# Microsoft China Is Moving On From Windows

Microsoft Corp. (MSFT) is facing another China worry, with the software giant finding Beijing apparently speeding attempts to remove Windows from some state-linked computer systems. Chinese officials have reportedly instructed some Chinese government-linked organizations to delete a modified version of Windows 10 before the original deadline. The software was due to be retired in February 2027.

The move is said to be linked to data security issues and a broader desire by Beijing to increase its reliance on indigenous technology. In the short term, Microsoft may not feel much financial pain, since China has typically been a tiny contributor to the company's overall sales. But the larger issue is direction: for years, China has been working to wean itself from American hardware and software, and an accelerated phaseout of Windows shows that effort is still under way.

For investors, the event is another warning that even strong U.S. digital firms can lose access to strategically vital Chinese government customers.
