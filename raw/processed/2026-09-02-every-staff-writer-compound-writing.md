# How an Every Staff Writer Developed Compound Writing

**Author:** Kaushik Viswanath
**Date:** September 2, 2026
**Source:** Every

Katie Parrott, Every staff writer, turned a post-layoff ChatGPT subscription into a sophisticated AI writing system called Compound Writing. After losing her crypto-industry job in 2023, she used the $20/month tool for career coaching and discovered it helped her find clarity through self-directed questioning.

Her approach centers on feeding AI rich foundational context — audience personas, brand positioning, competitive data — rather than relying on clever prompts. This allowed her to handle massive freelance workloads efficiently.

She describes AI as reigniting her passion for writing by offloading mechanical tasks, freeing mental energy for deeper creative questions. The result is an open-source plugin ported from Kieran Klaassen's compound engineering system, incorporating editorial frameworks inspired by Vonnegut and Hitchcock.

Looking ahead to Every's Thesis conference, Katie emphasizes that equitable access to AI tools matters as much as capability itself — warning that gains could concentrate among those with time and resources to experiment, rather than benefiting everyone.
