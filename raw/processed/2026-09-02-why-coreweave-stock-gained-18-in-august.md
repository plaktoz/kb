# Why CoreWeave Stock Gained 18% in August

CoreWeave (CRWV) rose 18% in August, driven by improved AI sector sentiment and a strong Q2 earnings report.

The month opened positively when Piper Sandler initiated coverage with an overweight rating and a $151 price target, and CoreWeave announced expansion into Indonesia. Broader tailwinds came from hyperscaler earnings and the unwinding of the Situational Awareness hedge fund blowup.

Mid-month, CoreWeave reported Q2 revenue up 112% to $2.58 billion, beating estimates of $2.56 billion. The company disclosed a revenue backlog of $104 billion, plus an additional $25 billion in new commitments. Full-year guidance was raised to $12.4 billion–$13.2 billion from the prior $12–$13 billion range.

The stock surged approximately 19% on August 12 but retreated as rising interest rates pressured neocloud stocks. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaled inflation-fighting remained the priority, implying rates could climb further.

A key concern: CoreWeave posted a net loss of $626 million in Q2, largely due to $640 million in interest expense, highlighting significant leverage risk if AI demand weakens.
