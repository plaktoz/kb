# How to Develop a Game-Changing Worldview

To develop a company's long-term strategy, leaders need a strong point of view about the world, says former Best Buy CEO Hubert Joly. In this edition of the HBR Executive Agenda, Joly tells editor at large Adi Ignatius about the three essential components of an effective worldview:

- The personal: your own unique leadership philosophy.
- The micro: an understanding of your company and its customers.
- The macro: an analysis of the larger world.

Joly's framework argues that a credible long-term strategy cannot be built without first making these three components explicit — leaders who skip this step tend to react to events rather than shape a coherent direction through them.

Adi Ignatius is the editor at large at Harvard Business Review and its former editor in chief.
