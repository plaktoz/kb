# Meta Says Interest in Its New AI Agent Has 'Blown Way Past' Expectations. The Stock Is Rallying

Meta Platforms (META) shares rose approximately 6% following the announcement of its Muse AI agent. The stock was among the S&P 500's top performers on an otherwise weak market day.

Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang stated that usage had "blown way past our projections." Muse is designed to autonomously handle everyday tasks such as sending emails, booking reservations, and arranging flights.

The product will be free for standard use, with paid subscriptions available for more complex tasks — representing a potential new AI revenue stream for the company.

Despite AI developments boosting Meta's advertising business, investor concerns over heavy technology spending had pressured the stock in recent months. Wednesday's rally brought shares back to roughly their year-opening levels.
