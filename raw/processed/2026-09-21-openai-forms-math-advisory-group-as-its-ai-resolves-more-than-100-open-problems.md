# OpenAI forms math advisory group as its AI resolves more than 100 open problems

OpenAI announced a new independent advisory body — the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence — hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton. The group is designed to give mathematicians greater input into OpenAI's math research, acting as "a bridge to the mathematical community."

The announcement follows OpenAI's earlier publication of a solution to the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem. Alongside forming the group, OpenAI revealed its internal model has also resolved over 100 additional open mathematical problems across various fields.

The move comes amid significant pushback: 25 Fields Medal recipients signed an open letter arguing AI labs are endangering mathematicians' intellectual work through competitive problem-solving races.

The advisory group will assess results and coordinate releases, but members are unpaid and hold no decision-making authority. OpenAI was explicit that the group would not advise on pacing internal research progress. IAS reinforced this, noting responsibility for company decisions "will rest with that company."

Nine mathematicians were named as founding members; only one also signed the Fields Medalists' letter.
