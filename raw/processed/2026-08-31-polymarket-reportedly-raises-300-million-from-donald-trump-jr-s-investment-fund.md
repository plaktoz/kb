# Polymarket Reportedly Raises $300M from Donald Trump Jr.'s Investment Fund

Polymarket has secured $300 million from 1789 Capital as part of a broader funding round expected to reach approximately $1 billion, per Wall Street Journal reporting citing anonymous sources.

## Key Facts

- **1789 Capital** — a fund in which Donald Trump Jr. is a partner — led the investment, having previously put in $200 million
- The firm has backed other controversial ventures, including the Enhanced Games (dubbed the "steroid Olympics")
- TechCrunch reached out to Polymarket for comment but received no response at time of publication

## Regulatory Context

- At least **20 states** are engaged in litigation against prediction market sites over sports wagering
- The Trump administration has argued the **CFTC** should be the sole federal regulator of the industry
- The CFTC has sued **at least 9 states** attempting to regulate prediction markets
- A coalition of **44 state attorneys general** signed a letter contesting CFTC authority over sports-related wagers
- Trump Jr. recently described the industry as having "robust oversight" at a conservative attorneys general event
