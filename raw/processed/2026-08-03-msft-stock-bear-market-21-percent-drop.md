# MSFT Stock Enters Bear Market After 21% Drop From Peak: Retail Cautious But Analysts Stay Overwhelmingly Bullish

Microsoft Corp.'s shares fell 3.2% on Monday, pushing the stock more than 20% below its June 1 peak and into bear market territory, despite solid earnings and ongoing strategic momentum. MSFT stock is down 23% in 2026, the worst performance among the Magnificent Seven. The iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV), a proxy for the software sector, has declined 19% this year while the S&P 500 gained 9.2% — even as semiconductor stocks surge, with Intel and Marvell nearly tripling and Micron and SanDisk up 324% and 858% respectively in 2026.

Microsoft, an early AI leader through its OpenAI partnership, has been swept up in broader negative sentiment toward software stocks as competition intensifies: Alphabet's Gemini 3 helped drive a 12% gain in Google shares over six months, while Anthropic continues shipping new AI offerings aggressively. Microsoft has responded with strategic moves including reportedly exploring a spin-off or restructuring of its Xbox gaming division and considering integrating China's lower-cost DeepSeek models into Copilot.

CEO Satya Nadella publicly criticized the current direction of the AI race, telling the Wall Street Journal that a small group of companies concentrating too much control over frontier models "would not be accepted" by the public, especially amid job-loss and AI-safety fears. Nadella outlined Microsoft's push toward cheaper, more flexible AI models and model-switching choice for users — intensifying competition with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Separately, Chevron signed a 20-year deal with Microsoft to provide natural-gas power for a proposed West Texas data center ("Project Kilby"), expected to reach 2.67 gigawatts by full ramp.

Retail sentiment on Stocktwits dropped to "bearish" from "neutral," with 24-hour message volume up 740% amid debate over whether the AI-linked selloff will continue. Despite the retail unease, analysts remain overwhelmingly bullish: 53 of 56 analysts rate the stock "Buy" or higher, the remaining three rate it "Hold," and the average price target of $561.39 implies 53% upside from Monday's close.
