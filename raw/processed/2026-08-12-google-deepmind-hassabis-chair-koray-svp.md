# The next chapter of our AI momentum

Google and Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai announced leadership changes at Google DeepMind (GDM), including new roles for Demis Hassabis and Koray Kavukcuoglu.

## Message from Sundar Pichai

Google has made extraordinary progress delivering its full AI stack, with momentum across Search, YouTube, and Cloud, and the Gemini app reaching 950M+ monthly users. To accelerate further, Pichai announced: Demis Hassabis will become Chair of GDM and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, continuing to lead Isomorphic Labs, so he can focus on shaping the future of AGI. Koray Kavukcuoglu, currently CTO of GDM and Chief AI Architect, will step up as SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting to Pichai, overseeing Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. Koray has been at DeepMind for 13 years, having started its deep learning team and led breakthroughs like WaveNet and DQN.

Separately, after a 27-year run at Google, Jeff Dean and Senior Fellow Sanjay Ghemawat are launching an independent public benefit corporation to accelerate discoveries in ML, science, and engineering, with Google continuing as a founding investor and Cloud partner.

## Message from Demis Hassabis

Hassabis said he is handing over day-to-day operational responsibilities at GDM to focus on the broader picture of shaping AGI's development safely. He will take on the new Chair of GDM and Chief Scientist of Alphabet role, with Koray stepping up to lead GDM as SVP in addition to his Chief AI Architect role. Hassabis will continue advising Koray and the GDM leadership team from Google's new London Platform 37 offices, while leaning further into Isomorphic Labs, where he says the No. 1 application of AI should be improving human health, including curing diseases like cancer.

Hassabis credited GDM's culture for the Gemini app's growth to over 950M monthly users and for continued fundamental and scientific research leadership, and expressed confidence in Koray and the GDM executive team to continue leading AI development at Google.
