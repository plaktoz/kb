# Tesla's Free Cash Flow Went Negative. It Still Has a $43.5 Billion Cash Cushion.

Tesla's FCF turned negative in Q2, posting a $1.1 billion loss versus a $6.2 billion gain the prior year, as the company pours capital into self-driving, AI, and its Optimus robot program.

Despite this, Tesla holds $43.5 billion in cash and equivalents. After subtracting $9.3 billion in debt and finance leases, the net cushion stands at $34.2 billion — enough to sustain operations through projected cash burn until 2029, when robotaxi and Optimus revenues are expected to reverse the trend.

Analyst consensus (Visible Alpha) projects roughly $25 billion in robotaxi revenue by 2030, though the rollout has faced regulatory, software, and production hurdles. The article cautions that these forecasts carry significant uncertainty.

CFO Vaibhav Taneja noted the company has discussed borrowing capacity with banks, stating that "any time you have a consistent stream of cash flow, you can go and get money from the banks."

The investment thesis hinges heavily on Cybercab's success — the stock trades at 205 times estimated 2026 earnings, a valuation difficult to justify without robotaxi conviction.
