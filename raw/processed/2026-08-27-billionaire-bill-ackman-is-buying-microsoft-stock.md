# Billionaire Bill Ackman Is Buying Microsoft Stock. Is It Still the Best Artificial Intelligence (AI) Buy Today?

Microsoft (MSFT) has been largely flat in 2026 while the S&P 500 climbed over 10%, making it a relative underperformer for most of the year. However, investors who bought during dips — particularly at the end of June — saw roughly 30% gains.

Bill Ackman's Pershing Square increased its Microsoft stake by nearly 10% in Q2, making it roughly 12% of the fund's portfolio. That said, the data reflects positions as of June 30, before the stock's sharp rise.

Microsoft's late-July earnings impressed investors, showing 18% revenue and operating income growth, with Azure cloud up 43%. Notably, Microsoft did not raise its data center spending guidance — unlike peers — which the market viewed favorably.

Before earnings, the stock traded at under 20x forward earnings. It now sits around 25x — still below its historical ~30x level, but no longer a clear bargain. The author views current pricing as "fair," noting that while Microsoft remains a solid hold, other AI stocks may offer better entry points right now.
