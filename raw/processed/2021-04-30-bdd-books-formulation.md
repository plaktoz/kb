# The BDD Books – Formulation

This book covers the Formulation practice of Behaviour Driven Development (BDD), teaching readers how to translate concrete examples into business-readable Gherkin scenarios using Given/When/Then syntax.

Written by the creator of SpecFlow and the author of *The Cucumber for Java Book*, the guide follows a team building living documentation for a product under development. The book is intended for all stakeholders — product owners, analysts, developers, and testers alike.

It is the second volume in the BDD Books series.

**Note:** *"The content of this book will be included in our new book, Effective Behavior Driven Development, published by Manning."*

As a result, this edition has been withdrawn from sale. Translations remain available in Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese on Leanpub.
