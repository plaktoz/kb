# Matt Mullenweg Claims Return as Automattic CEO Days After Board Ouster

**Author:** Sarah Perez | **Date:** September 11, 2026

## Summary

Matt Mullenweg posted a message to Automattic's internal Slack claiming he had retaken control as CEO, just days after the company's board voted to place him on leave. Automattic had not officially confirmed the reversal at time of publication.

## Key Events

- **Wednesday:** Automattic's board voted to put Mullenweg on paid leave. CFO Mark Davies was named interim CEO — a move Mullenweg described as the board "conspiring" behind his back.
- **Friday morning:** Mullenweg posted to company Slack: *"(Don't call it a comeback…) Howdy everybody! Happy to announce the board is back in agreement, and I'm in control of Automattic."*
- He also linked to an LL Cool J music video in the same post.
- Mark Davies' Slack account was reportedly deactivated.

## Uncertainty & Odd Behavior

TechCrunch noted that prior to the Friday post, Mullenweg had removed all Slack admins and made an unusual "pirate"-themed post with profanity — notable because he is famously known for not cursing.

When asked if he was trolling staff, he replied: *"I'm not a troll I'm a pirate, obviously."*

His promised blog post turned out to be about purchasing a houseboat.

## External Signals

- WordPress.org Executive Director Mary Hubbard posted on X that she was *"relieved and pleased"* by Mullenweg's reported return.
- On X, Mullenweg posted *"Boards are never boring"* followed by a laughing/crying emoji, and earlier wrote *"Things are different than they appear."*
- Mullenweg publicly suggested Silver Lake — the PE firm majority-owning WP Engine, currently in litigation with Automattic — was involved in a plan to *"destroy"* his life.

## Status

The situation remained unresolved at publication, with no official board confirmation of Mullenweg's reinstatement.
