# Transcript: Vivek Ramaswamy with Elon Musk & David Sacks

*Generated via AWS Transcribe + GPT-4; may contain inaccuracies*

## Summary of Key Topics

### Introductions & American Identity

David Sacks introduced Vivek as founder of Strive Asset Management and biotech firm Roivant Sciences, Harvard/Yale Law graduate. Vivek framed his presidential run around restoring purpose and national identity, describing his generation as "starved for purpose, meaning, and identity."

### Anti-Woke Corporate Governance / ESG

The trio discussed how passive index funds (roughly half the stock market) outsource shareholder votes to ISS and Glass Lewis. Elon called this a fundamental governance failure, saying firms like BlackRock are "breaking the deal with their customers" by pursuing social agendas rather than maximizing returns. Vivek cited his founding of Strive as a market-based alternative.

### Ukraine

- Elon noted he had read extensively on the region's history and said the conflict "is going to get worse from here."
- Sacks recounted the Istanbul peace negotiations and argued NATO expansion was a root cause of the conflict.
- Vivek criticized Boris Johnson's reported role in pushing Zelensky away from early peace talks.
- All three expressed concern about Ukrainian casualties and called for realistic peace negotiations.

### FBI / Federal Bureaucracy

Vivek proposed dissolving the FBI and redistributing its 15,000 frontline agents to specialized agencies (US Marshals, DEA, etc.), arguing redundant bureaucracies breed institutional corruption regardless of individual integrity.

### Civil Rights & Meritocracy

Vivek pledged to rescind Executive Order 11246 on day one, calling for a "colorblind meritocracy." He referenced the recent Harvard Supreme Court case as a legal foundation. Richard Hanania's upcoming book *The Origins of Woke* was cited as influential.

### Government Pressure on Private Companies

Vivek described plans for "state action files" — publishing all documented cases of federal officials pressuring private companies to censor or adopt policies the government couldn't mandate directly. He argued this represents unconstitutional state action in disguise.

### China & Trade

Vivek favored rejoining the CPTPP on renegotiated terms as leverage against China, emphasizing the need for a level playing field: no forced IP transfers, no forced data sharing.

### Campaign Finance

Vivek noted he had contributed over $15 million of his own money, achieved 70,000 small-dollar donors (40% first-time Republican donors), and argued cost-per-polling-point efficiency — not raw fundraising — is the better electoral predictor, citing Trump's 2016 run as a model.

## Notable Exchanges

Elon on the government's role: "The government is really a corporation in the limit. It's got a monopoly on violence."

Vivek on American ideals: "Free speech, self-governance... that's extreme for most of human history. But that extreme set of ideals is what unites us."

Elon on Ukraine casualties: "Those people, those kids, are never coming back. Dying for a mile of territory is insane."

Elon on Vivek: "It's rare to meet politicians who aren't NPCs. Please take that as a high compliment."
