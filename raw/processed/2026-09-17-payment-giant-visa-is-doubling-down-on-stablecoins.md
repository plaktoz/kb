# Payment Giant Visa Is Doubling Down on Stablecoins

Visa (NYSE: V) has moved aggressively into stablecoins with three major developments:

**Key Points:**
- Visa launched a stablecoin platform giving banks ready-made tools for digital dollar management
- 160+ stablecoin-linked card programs are live globally, with volume up ~200% year over year
- Stablecoin settlement volume surpassed a $20 billion annualized run rate

**1. Visa Stablecoin Platform (VSP):** Introduced in July, VSP offers a "Wallet-as-a-Service" toolkit for banks and fintechs. It initially supports Open USD, with USDC integration planned later in the year.

**2. On-Chain Lending Rail:** Announced September 8th, this pairs VisaNet settlement data with smart contracts, enabling blockchain-based short-term credit. Partner Credit Coop has facilitated over $2.5 billion in settlement financing since 2023 with zero defaults.

**3. Scale:** Stablecoin settlement volume is now "more than 15 times higher in a single year," with organic stablecoin payment volume exceeding $350 billion monthly industry-wide.

For consumers, the practical impact is significant — stablecoin holders can now spend via standard Visa cards while merchants automatically receive local fiat currency, eliminating friction between crypto and traditional finance.
