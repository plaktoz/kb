# Why Isn't AI Taking Our Jobs?

source_url: https://calnewport.com/why-isnt-ai-taking-our-jobs/

---

Author: Cal Newport
Published: June 8, 2026

Newport challenges the industrial-automation analogy that AI leaders have used to predict mass job displacement. He notes that figures like Dario Amodei and Sam Altman have begun walking back their most dire predictions — Altman reportedly saying he was "delighted" to be wrong about an AI "jobs apocalypse."

Through interviews with small business owners, Newport found AI being used less like a transformative power loom and more like a resourceful intern — building quick, practical internal tools. He likens this to his own teenage job writing custom web apps for a consulting firm, which helped workers without eliminating them.

His conclusion is measured: "we don't seem to be hurtling toward the darker vision of a workforce hollowed out by this technology." Instead, AI appears to be reshaping parts of roles rather than eliminating positions wholesale — a phenomenon he ties to an emerging strategy he calls "freestyle work," detailed further in his New Yorker piece.
