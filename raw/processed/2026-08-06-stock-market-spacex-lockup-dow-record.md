# Stock Market Today: Futures Mixed After Dow Closes at Record High for 3rd Straight Day; Memory Chipmakers Sink

Stocks pointed to a mixed open Thursday, with Dow Jones Industrial Average futures heading higher after three consecutive record closes but Nasdaq 100 futures falling as shares of memory chipmakers sank. Dow and S&P 500 futures pointed up 0.3% and 0.1%, respectively, while Nasdaq 100 futures declined 0.7%.

The prior day, the blue-chip Dow finished up 0.5% to extend its winning streak to five sessions and three straight record-high closes. It hit a new intraday record along with the S&P 500, though the benchmark index ended 0.2% lower. The Nasdaq declined 0.8%, with the tech-focused index and the S&P 500 ending four-session winning streaks.

Weighing on the Nasdaq early Thursday were shares of Western Digital (WDC) and Sandisk (SNDK), which sank 15% and 11%, respectively, as investors mulled their earnings reports issued after markets closed the prior day. The Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) pulled back 6.5%. In other post-earnings moves, shares of Datadog (DDOG) plunged 22%; AppLovin (APP) dropped 19%; Honeywell Aerospace (HONA) retreated 15%; and DoorDash (DASH) rose 2%.

Shares of SpaceX (SPCX) rose 3% after plunging nearly 14% the day before as investors digested the Elon Musk-led company's first results as a public company. About 911 million shares of SpaceX stock — about $98 billion worth at recent prices — were released Thursday from trading restrictions, which expire two days after the company's first earnings report as a publicly traded entity. Such lock-up expirations tend to have a negative effect on stocks because they give early investors an opportunity to sell shares, often for the first time. SpaceX shares were, as of the prior close, off about 20% from their IPO price and 52% below their post-IPO peak.

All of the Magnificent Seven mega-cap tech firms pointed higher before the bell except for Microsoft (MSFT). The prior day, the group finished mixed, with Nvidia (NVDA) leading gains at about 3.5% and Alphabet (GOOGL) the biggest decliner at 4%.

Oil prices ticked higher as investors remained hopeful for an agreement between the U.S. and Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. U.S. benchmark West Texas Intermediate futures rose 0.8% to $75.80 a barrel, while global benchmark Brent crude futures advanced 1% to $80.25. The 10-year Treasury yield was at 4.64%, up two basis points from the prior close. The U.S. dollar index ticked 0.1% higher to 99.76. Bitcoin was trading around $64,500, down slightly over the past 24 hours. Gold futures rose 0.5% to $4,330 an ounce.
