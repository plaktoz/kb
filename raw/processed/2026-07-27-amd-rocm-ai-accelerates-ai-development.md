# AMD AAI 2026: AMD ROCm.ai Accelerates AI Development Across AMD Platforms

source_url: https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2026/07/27/amd-aai-2026-amd-rocm-ai-accelerates-ai-development-across-amd-platforms

---

AMD announced ROCm.ai, an AI-native software experience for AI development across AMD platforms. It combines AI-assisted development, intelligent deployment and AI-powered software optimization into a unified experience that helps developers move quickly from intent to production. Introduced at Advancing AI 2026, ROCm.ai includes ROCm CLI — a new command-line experience for installing, validating, serving and managing AI workloads — and AMD Skills, bringing AMD-authored expertise into leading AI coding assistants such as Claude, Cursor and Codex. It also includes Hyperloom, a new open-source, agentic system aimed at automating the time-consuming task of optimizing end-to-end inference workloads.

ROCm.ai delivers an average 3.3x inference improvement and 2.4x training improvement for the latest version of ROCm over ROCm 7 on the same hardware.

**ROCm CLI** provides a unified command-line experience for installing, validating, serving, updating and troubleshooting AI workloads on AMD platforms. Hardware-aware workflows, automated environment setup and support for secure, air-gapped deployments help simplify software installation.

**AMD Skills** integrates AMD-authored expertise into leading AI coding assistants including Claude, Cursor and Codex. Instead of relying on generic recommendations, developers receive AMD-specific guidance for installing, migrating, debugging and optimizing AI workloads.

**Hyperloom** automates the optimization of end-to-end inference workloads. It handles the full inference optimization loop — from profiling and analysis to kernel optimization and validation — helping accomplish in hours what previously required weeks of specialized engineering effort.

Availability for ROCm.ai will begin in August 2026.
