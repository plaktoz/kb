# What Does Human Work Look Like After Automation?

Shipper poses a central question: when intelligence becomes abundant and execution gets automated, how will humans spend their working hours?

Every has tracked this territory since GPT-3, previously arguing that AI would enable individuals to do team-scale work, turn knowledge workers into "managers of models," and paradoxically generate *more* demand for human expertise.

## Thesis Statements initiative

The new initiative, **Thesis Statements**, assembles specific, contestable predictions from 100 builders and thinkers with daily hands-on AI experience. The first 25 contributors include:

- Karri Saarinen (Linear CEO): design becomes AI's biggest bottleneck
- Chris Pedregal (Granola CEO): some hard problems will self-resolve
- Anne-Laure Le Cunff (Ness Labs): questions become scarce while answers grow cheap
- Yash Tekriwal (Clay): computational thinking will reshape jobs
- Tina He (Pace Capital): boring infrastructure wins
- Alex Komoroske (Common Tools): software serves users rather than conditioning them

## Goal

The project aims to create a public, revisitable record — tracking which predictions hold, which fail, and which evolve. A companion **Thesis conference** will bring contributors together to test ideas and "build a world after automation that is not only more productive but more human."
