# Simple Truths: Clear & Gentle Guidance on the Big Issues in Life

Kindle highlights from Kent Nerburn's *Simple Truths*.

## Education

Education is one of the great joys and solaces of life. It gives us a framework for understanding the world around us and our place in it.

True education goes beyond schooling — it is a mindset of curiosity and openness. As Confucius warned: "Study without thinking and you are blind; think without studying and you are in danger."

Knowledge is multiple; wisdom is singular. Knowledge is words; wisdom is silent.

## Work

Choose your work carefully. Your work makes you who you are, because it is where you put your time.

## Money & Wealth

Money's emotional meaning is self-constructed. Those who measure their wealth against their desires will never be satisfied. Those who measure it against their needs will find contentment.

"Debt defines your future, and when your future is defined, hope begins to die."

## Possessions

Objects hold no inherent value. Fewer possessions often mean deeper appreciation. Giving away what is unused restores perspective on what truly matters.

## Travel

Real travelers surrender to new experiences rather than recreating familiar comforts in new places.

## Relationships & Love

Compatibility matters deeply. Shared laughter signals a healthy connection. "If you can make each other laugh, you can always surprise each other."

## Solitude vs. Loneliness

Solitude is becoming one with the space around you. Loneliness is an awareness of separateness.

## Strength

True strength is not about force, but about conviction. Strength based in love is a strength people crave.
