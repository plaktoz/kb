# Microsoft Eases AI Spending Concerns, Giving MSFT Stock More Room to Rally

source_url: https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3577444/microsoft-eases-ai-spending-concerns-giving-msft-stock-more-room-to-rally

byline: Amit Singh

date: 2026-07-30

---

Microsoft (MSFT) stock jumped 15.51% on July 30 after the company delivered a stronger-than-expected fiscal fourth-quarter earnings report, easing investor concerns about its massive AI investments. While the software giant comfortably beat Wall Street's expectations for both revenue and earnings, the biggest catalyst was the accelerating growth of its Azure cloud business.

For much of this year, Microsoft shares faced pressure as investors questioned whether the company's aggressive AI-related capital expenditures would generate sufficient returns, weighing on margins and free cash flow. The latest results suggest the payoff may already be underway: Azure revenue growth accelerated during the fourth quarter, and management sees continued momentum ahead. Microsoft Copilot also continued gaining enterprise adoption, strengthening the company's long-term AI strategy.

## Azure and Copilot are supercharging Microsoft's growth

Fiscal 2026 revenue climbed to more than $331 billion, with growth accelerating to 18%. Fourth-quarter revenue increased 18% year-over-year to $90 billion. Cloud generated $59.3 billion in revenue, growing 27%; Azure and other cloud services revenue growth accelerated to 43%, up from 40% in the previous quarter. For the full fiscal year, Microsoft Cloud revenue exceeded $214 billion, with nearly 90% generated from customers outside Frontier Model companies — highlighting broad-based enterprise demand rather than reliance on a handful of AI developers.

Copilot paid seat additions more than doubled sequentially, pushing total paid seats above 30 million. Higher adoption of premium offerings — Copilot, Microsoft 365 E5, and the early rollout of E7 — also lifted average revenue per user. Commercial remaining performance obligation (RPO) rose 84% year-over-year to $678 billion, with all the sequential increase coming from customers outside Frontier Model companies (RPO excluding OpenAI still rose 25%).

Looking ahead, the Intelligent Cloud segment is projected to generate $40.95–$41.25 billion in first-quarter revenue (about 33–34% growth), with Azure revenue expected to increase roughly 45% in constant currency. Management expects Azure growth to accelerate further in the first half of fiscal 2027. Microsoft also plans to increase capital expenditures again in fiscal 2027 to expand AI infrastructure, which may continue to pressure gross margins even as accelerating revenue growth eases investor concern about the pace of spending.

## The bottom line

Accelerating Azure growth, expanding Copilot adoption, and a record commercial backlog suggest AI investments are evolving from a near-term margin headwind into a durable growth catalyst. Wall Street remains bullish: the vast majority of analysts rate MSFT a "Strong Buy," with an average price target of $543.08, implying over 20% upside from its recent closing price of $451.10.
