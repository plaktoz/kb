# How the Best Leaders Shape Conversations

Every strategy, decision, and transformation begins as a conversation. The highest-performing teams intentionally steward how those conversations unfold.

Meetings often "meander," with important concerns left unspoken and decisions either unresolved or repeatedly revisited.

The authors argue that the best leaders actively shape the arc of conversations — steering them toward clarity, inclusion, and resolution rather than letting them drift.
