# Nvidia Might Deliver Another 'Beat and Raise' Quarter, But Its Financing Deals Could Be the Bigger Driver of NVDA Stock

Nvidia releases fiscal Q2 2027 earnings on Aug. 26. The stock is down 3.33% over the last five days and trading 8.5% below the record highs it hit in May. Consensus estimates call for Q2 revenue to nearly double year-over-year to $92.01 billion, above Nvidia's own guidance of $91 billion (±2%). Analysts expect EPS to more than double to $2.01.

Wall Street is broadly bullish heading into earnings. Stifel's Ruben Roy and TD Cowen's Joshua Buchalter both expect another "beat and raise" quarter. UBS sees Nvidia exceeding its revenue guidance by up to $4 billion and forecasts an October-quarter guide well above Street estimates.

Bank of America's Vivek Arya finds NVDA's valuation "compelling" despite off-balance-sheet commitments — Nvidia has agreed to provide guarantees of up to $105 billion for OpenAI's Ohio data center, a risk not captured on the balance sheet. This echoes broader "circular financing" concerns across the AI ecosystem, where companies invest in their own customers.

Goldman Sachs' James Schneider flagged four things markets will watch on the earnings call: the $500 billion AI financing plan Nvidia has arranged with six asset managers, the Rubin platform ramp, gross-margin sustainability amid rising AI-chip competition, and CPU demand from agentic AI.

Nvidia trades at a forward P/E of 25.59x and a PEG ratio of just 0.44x. The article's author notes genuine concerns over the financing deals but sees room for the stock to move higher, while cautioning investors not to expect it to double every year.
