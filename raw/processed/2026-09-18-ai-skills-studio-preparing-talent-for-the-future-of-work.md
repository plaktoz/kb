# AI Skills Studio: Preparing talent for the future of work

AI is changing which skills matter for your workforce. AI Skills Studio helps job seekers build and prove real, relevant AI skills via hands-on projects—showing employers what they can actually do.

We are in the middle of the largest workforce transformation in history. AI is changing how work gets done, which skills matter, and how quickly people and companies need to adapt.

Today, we are introducing Handshake AI Skills Studio, a free, hands-on learning experience where job seekers build practical AI skills by completing real projects with the tools shaping work.

The need is clear. AI skills appear 9x more often on Class of 2026 profiles than on Class of 2022 profiles. At the same time, applications per job on Handshake are up 26% year over year. Employers have more candidates to consider, but fewer clear ways to identify who has the skills to succeed.

AI Skills Studio brings learning and hiring closer together. It helps job seekers prepare for the AI economy while giving employers stronger evidence of what candidates have learned and built.

## Bringing new skill signals into hiring

AI Skills Studio gives job seekers a way to learn by doing. Through short, project-based missions, they use real AI tools like OpenAI, Google, and Salesforce to create something tangible and build skills they can carry into the workplace.

These missions are developed with leading employers and AI labs, helping connect what people learn to the tools and skills companies actually need. Candidates who complete and publish an approved project through AI Skills Studio earn an AI Project Builder badge.

Employers will now have the chance to see two new badges on candidate profiles:

- **AI Project Builder:** The candidate completed and published an approved AI project through AI Skills Studio.
- **AI Fellow:** The candidate completed at least five hours of paid, hands-on AI work through Handshake AI.

Together, these badges give employers evidence of completed work and experience, not simply another self-reported skill. Employers will see them while sourcing candidates, reviewing applicants, and viewing profiles, along with an explanation of how each badge was earned.

When employers include AI-related skills in their Hiring Intelligence criteria, Handshake can also use these signals to help surface stronger potential matches.

## Connecting hiring and upskilling

Workforce transformation has two sides: helping employers hire for the capabilities they need and helping people build them.

Handshake sits at the intersection of both. We connect the AI labs building new technology, the educational institutions preparing people for work, the employers defining emerging needs, and the job seekers navigating what comes next. This gives us a front-row view into how jobs are changing, which skills are becoming more important, and how people are developing them.

AI Skills Studio helps us turn that insight into action. By making hands-on AI learning more accessible, we can help more people prepare for the future of work while creating stronger signals for the employers looking to hire them.

Over time, this connection could help employers address skill gaps more directly. Imagine identifying a capability your organization needs and introducing a relevant project earlier in the hiring process. Candidates could demonstrate what they can do before an interview, helping recruiters focus sooner on the right people. The same insight could help employers determine which capabilities to hire for and which to develop across their workforce.

Those employer experiences are not part of today's launch, but they reflect the future we are building toward: a more connected system where the skills employers need inform how people learn, and what people build becomes a stronger signal for hiring.

AI Skills Studio is an important first step toward preparing more people for the AI economy and helping employers build the workforce they need.
