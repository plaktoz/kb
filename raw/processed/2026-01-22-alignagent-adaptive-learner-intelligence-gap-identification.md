# ALIGNAgent: Adaptive Learner Intelligence for Gap Identification and Next-step Guidance

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15551

---

**Authors:** Bismack Tokoli, Luis Jaimes, Ayesha S. Dina

**Submitted:** January 22, 2026

**Abstract:**

The paper introduces ALIGNAgent, a multi-agent educational framework addressing a core limitation in personalized learning: most existing systems specialize in only one function (knowledge tracing, diagnostics, or recommendations) rather than combining them cohesively.

The system works in three integrated stages:
1. Processes student quiz results, gradebook data, and learner preferences
2. Uses a Skill Gap Agent to identify misconceptions and knowledge deficiencies at the concept level
3. Deploys a Recommender Agent to surface targeted learning materials matched to diagnosed gaps, with a continuous feedback loop before topic progression

Evaluation used real data from two undergraduate computer science courses. GPT-4o-based agents achieved:
- Precision: 0.87–0.90
- F1 scores: 0.84–0.87

Results validated against actual exam performance, confirming the proficiency estimates are meaningful.
