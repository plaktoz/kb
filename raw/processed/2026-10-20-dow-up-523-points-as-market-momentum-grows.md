# Dow Up 523 Points as Market Momentum Grows

## The Russell 2000 is headed for its best single-session gain in over two months

Stocks are gaining steam midday, with all three major indexes confidently higher thanks to the oil price pivot and a slew of upbeat earnings. The Nasdaq Composite (IXIC) and S&P 500 Index (SPX) have pared modest morning losses, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJI) leads the way with a 523-point pop.

Small caps are also notable winners today, with the Russell 2000 Index (RUT) eyeing its best day since Aug. 4. Front-month crude, after nearing $110/barrel in the morning, is now 2.5% lower today.

3M Co (NYSE:MMM) stock is seeing an uptick in options volume today. At last look, 28,000 options have changed hands, volume that's triple the average intraday amount. The November 175 call leads the way by a wide margin. MMM is up 6% to trade at $171.91 after the industrial conglomerate reported an earnings beat-and-raise for the third quarter. Year to date, the shares are now 6.3% higher, with recent support stemming from the $160 floor.

Eli Lilly And Co (NYSE:LLY) is near the top of the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) today, last seen up 5.4% to trade at $1,304.31. Goldman Sachs (GS) maintained its "buy" rating and hiked its price target to $1,413 this morning, a 7% premium from its current perch. LLY hit a record high of $1,311.75 out of the gate this morning, and is now up 20% in 2026 with support in place at its 100-day moving average.

Nuscale Power Corp (NYSE:SMR) is sitting out the rally today, down 0.2% to trade at $5.69. Barclays downgraded the nuclear energy concern to "underweight" from "equal-weight along with a price-target cut to $5 from $11. The analyst in coverage is wary of slower commercial milestones and execution risks. Nuscale stock earlier traded at a more than two-year low of $5.33, and is now down 60% year to date.
