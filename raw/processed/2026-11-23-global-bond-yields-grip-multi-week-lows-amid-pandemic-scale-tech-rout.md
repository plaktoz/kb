# Global bond yields grip multi-week lows amid pandemic-scale tech rout

Investing.com - Global sovereign bond yields consolidated near multi-week lows on Monday, cementing a fierce safe-haven rally as a historic, pandemic-scale wipeout across the artificial intelligence and technology sectors sent spooked investors seeking refuge in government debt.

The benchmark U.S. 10-year note yield was below the psychologically critical 5% threshold during European trade, hovering near two-week lows at 4.96% after logging its biggest single-session rally in over a month on Friday. Bond yields move inversely to prices, meaning falling yields reflect surging investor demand for the underlying paper.

Across the Atlantic, the benchmark German 10-year Bund yield held largely steady at 3.3167%, retaining the structural momentum from Friday's session where it touched its lowest levels in over a week and recorded its largest rally since May 2026.

At the front end of the curve, the policy-sensitive German two-year yield hovered near two-week lows at 3.096%, following its biggest surge in nearly two months.

The flight to safety was equally pronounced in the Asia-Pacific region, where the fallout from the tech contagion has been particularly severe.

The Australian 10-year note yield settled at 4.914% - its lowest level since August - fresh off logging its best weekly performance since April of last year. Regional authorities are now reportedly readying measures to prop up sliding stock markets across Asia in an attempt to stem the bleeding, though broader market volumes were thinned with Japanese dealing rooms closed for a national holiday on Monday.

## The AI wipeout

The urgent rush into fixed income is a direct reaction to a catastrophic unwinding of the AI trade. Traders are aggressively questioning whether the sums of mostly borrowed capital spent building massive data centers to power AI tools will ever generate profits large and fast enough to repay lenders.

The sheer scale of the equity destruction forcing capital into bonds is staggering. Last week's 10% plunge in the tech-heavy Nasdaq was its largest weekly decline since pandemic panic gripped markets in March 2020.

The structural foundation of the AI sector has been severely destabilized by the abrupt exit of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang - a shock departure that has actively called the chipmaker's strategy of financing some of its own customers into question.

Furthermore, the faltering public debut of AI developer Anthropic confirmed that traders are decisively abandoning the tech euphoria, leaving sovereign bonds as the undisputed asset of choice for portfolio ballast.
