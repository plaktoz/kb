# Microsoft Stock (MSFT) Climbs Despite a Major Service Outage

Microsoft (MSFT) stock was up slightly on Thursday, even as a major outage affects some of the tech company's services. Users in the Americas, including the U.S. and Canada, are having trouble accessing the company's services. Reports suggest that affected services include Microsoft Outlook, Exchange Online mailboxes, Microsoft 365, Copilot, Teams, Microsoft Store, and OneDrive.

Microsoft is a leading provider of services to private users and businesses. With this outage, companies across the Americas will experience problems connecting employees and clients, which could hamper their business. While it's not a good look for Microsoft, outages do happen from time to time.

The real question is how long this outage will last. If Microsoft can take care of it quickly, it's not likely to have a large impact on businesses or the company's reputation. However, the longer users are unable to access its services, the more they potentially lose profits and faith in the tech giant's capabilities.

Of course, Microsoft is aware of the issue. The company has an official incident ID for it on its admin center. The company attempted an initial fix of the issue, but appears to have run into complications. This could imply that users will have to wait a little bit longer than normal before they can resume using the company's services.

Microsoft is also experiencing problems with a new patch that was released today. The latest patch from the company was designed to fix a large number of bugs, close to 1,200, but it can't even be installed. A bug in a bug-fixing patch just adds to Microsoft's woes today.

## Not All Microsoft Services Are Affected by Today's Outage

While Microsoft may be having trouble with its services, not all of the company's businesses are. For example, Xbox is having no trouble today, despite the issue at its parent company. The video game company's online services and storefront are still completely operational. That's interesting to note, as the main Microsoft Store is included in the outage.

## Is Microsoft Stock a Buy, Sell, or Hold?

Turning to Wall Street, the analysts' consensus rating for Microsoft is Strong Buy, based on 35 Buy and four Hold ratings over the past three months. With that comes an average MSFT stock price target of $632.48, suggesting a possible 20.42% upside for the shares. (See MSFT Stock Forecast)
