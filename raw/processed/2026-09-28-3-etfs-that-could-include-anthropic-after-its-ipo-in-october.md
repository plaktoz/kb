# 3 ETFs That Could Include Anthropic After Its IPO in October

The article discusses Anthropic's anticipated October IPO, noting it could rival SpaceX's June offering as one of the largest ever, with a potential valuation around $2 trillion. It explains that new "fast-track" rules from major index providers—put in place after SpaceX's IPO—could let Anthropic enter certain ETFs quickly after going public, rather than waiting the typical weeks or months.

Three ETFs are highlighted as likely candidates:

1. **Invesco QQQ ETF (QQQ)** – Tracks the Nasdaq-100. A new Nasdaq rule allows a newly public company that would rank among the 40 largest Nasdaq-100 components to join the index after only 15 trading days.

2. **iShares Russell 1000 Growth ETF (IWF)** – Tracks a Russell growth index. FTSE Russell's updated policy permits a qualifying new IPO that would land within the Russell Top 500 to enter Russell U.S. Indexes after the fifth trading day, assuming it's classified as a growth stock.

3. **First Trust U.S. Equity Opportunities ETF (FPX)** – Tracks an index of roughly 100 large, liquid newly public U.S. companies, with eligibility lasting for their first 1,000 trading days, making it a direct vehicle for IPO exposure.

The piece concludes that while investors shouldn't chase these funds solely for Anthropic exposure, they offer a more measured way to gain indirect access to the company without buying shares directly on IPO day.
