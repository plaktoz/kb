# A Script for Mark Zuckerberg

Thompson writes a fictional earnings call speech for Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg, set in August 2026. The piece is framed as what Thompson *wishes* Zuckerberg would say publicly.

## Key Themes

### Facebook's Origins & Growth

The feed transformed engagement by surfacing content passively. Mobile forced Meta away from platform ambitions and toward pure content — which Thompson argues was a blessing.

### Zuckerberg's Admitted Mistakes (per Thompson's script)

1. **Platform obsession** — prioritizing third-party developers over Meta's own content hurt early mobile performance
2. **Reality Labs** — massive losses driven partly by a desire to own hardware platforms
3. **Underestimating entertainment** — TikTok recognized before Meta did that the feed was primarily an entertainment product

### The "Ad Blindspot"

Thompson argues Zuckerberg never sufficiently championed Meta's ad business or made the affirmative case that targeted advertising connects entrepreneurs with consumers in uniquely valuable ways.

### Why AI Matters for Meta (per the script)

- All digital businesses face existential AI risk; Meta must invest defensively
- AI improves ad targeting and content recommendation directly
- "AI makes every pixel monetizable" — the largest inventory expansion in Meta's history is approaching
- Meta's consumer focus (connection, entertainment, commerce) is an *advantage* over enterprise AI competitors

### The Compute Strategy

Thompson's script proposes Meta rent spare compute capacity on the spot market, creating a market-based hurdle rate that disciplines internal AI spending — forcing Meta to use compute only when it can outperform rental revenue.

### Core Argument

Meta's competitive moat is celebrating and connecting humans — not building productivity tools or competing with OpenAI. The script closes: "We are not out here to make chatbots... Our goal is to celebrate humans, to connect them, to entertain them, and to enable commerce among them."
