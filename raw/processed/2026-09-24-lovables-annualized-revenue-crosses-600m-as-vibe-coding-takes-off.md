# Lovable's annualized revenue crosses $600M as vibe coding takes off

Lovable co-founder Fabian Hedin announced at the HumanX summit in Amsterdam that the vibe-coding platform has surpassed $600M in annualized run-rate revenue, up from $500M reported in June.

Hedin highlighted enterprise growth, stating that two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies now use the platform, with notable customers including Microsoft, Nvidia, and Deutsche Telekom.

He distinguished Lovable from other coding tools: "Lovable does not output code. The output is a product, and increasingly so, a business." Apps built on the platform now draw nearly a billion monthly views combined.

The company has raised over $700M across two rounds in roughly eight months — $300M in December at a $6.6B valuation, followed by $400M in August at a $13.3B valuation, with Menlo Ventures participating in both.
