# Red Team vs Blue Team in Cybersecurity

## Summary

This article explains the roles of red and blue teams in cybersecurity simulations modeled after military training exercises.

## Key Concepts

**Red Team** — Offensive security experts who simulate real-world adversary attacks, attempting to breach defenses using techniques like credential theft, social engineering, and lateral movement.

**Blue Team** — Defensive incident responders who identify, assess, and counter intrusions while working to improve detection and remediation capabilities.

**Purple Team** — A collaborative model where red and blue teams share insights openly, rather than operating in isolation.

## Notable Points

- CrowdStrike recommends a **"1-10-60 rule"**: detect intrusions under 1 minute, assess within 10 minutes, eject the adversary within 1 hour.
- Research indicates adversaries dwell an average of **197 days** inside a network before detection.
- Red team activities commonly follow the **MITRE ATT&CK Framework**.
- Red teams focus on penetration testing, social engineering, communication interception, and access cloning.
- Blue teams employ DNS hardening, microsegmentation, least-privilege access, and security monitoring.
