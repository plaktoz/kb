# Why Fortinet Stock Zoomed Almost 9% Higher This Week

Fortinet (FTNT) shares rose nearly 9% over five trading days, driven by two factors:

1. **AI security fears** boosted cybersecurity stocks broadly, as prominent AI developers raised alarms about the technology's rapid advancement.

2. **Analyst upgrade:** Bernstein SocGen's Peter Weed raised his price target to $145 from $102, though he maintained a hold-equivalent rating.

The author notes Fortinet's competitive advantages include "proprietary custom silicon" and its unified FortiOS operating system, positioning it well as demand for cybersecurity grows alongside AI concerns.
