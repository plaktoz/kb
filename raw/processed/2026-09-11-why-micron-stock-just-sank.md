# Why Micron Stock Just Sank

Micron (MU) dropped ~4.7% on Thursday, significantly underperforming the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, which each fell around 0.6%.

**Macro pressures:** Rising 10-year Treasury yields reached 4.84% — the highest since 2023 — despite Treasury buyback efforts. Combined with climbing oil prices and upcoming CPI inflation data, investors shifted toward risk-off positioning.

**AI regulation concerns:** Growing discussion around guardrails for artificial intelligence created uncertainty, though major demand shifts for Micron's chips seem unlikely in the near term.

**Employee compensation:** Micron announced substantial bonuses for workers at its Taiwan facilities to head off potential labor disputes. While viewed as appropriate given the company's strong performance, the move raises modest margin concerns.

The stock had been off as much as 5.3% intraday before recovering slightly by session's end.
