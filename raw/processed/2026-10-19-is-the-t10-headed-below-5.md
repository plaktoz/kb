The Inflation Sky is Falling!

Core CPI, both month over month as well as annually, came in hotter than expected. This is a problem because “core” strips out oil and yet inflation is accelerating. Rates jumped immediately.

Then stocks were like, “Wait, higher rates might actually be bad for the economy…especially for leveraged AI plays” and equities tanked. That, in turn, pulled rates down.

If the best cure for inflation is inflation, maybe the best cure for higher rates is higher rates. They can’t go up forever because they actually do restrict growth. And higher Treasury yields are doing some of the heavy lifting for the FOMC.

I also think that under the hood, the inflation story isn’t quite as concerning as the initial CPI print felt. Owners’ equivalent rent was more than half of the monthly Core CPI increase.

Bloomberg’s Anna Wong broke down 170 of the CPI components to identify how many were rising more than 3%. The historical average is 35% and the post-covid peak was 80%.

Rates

For the first time in an eternity, the 10T actually dropped. The Bloomberg rates team thinks the peak is behind us for now, with their technical analysis implying a move back below 5%.

The Fed will be quiet this week ahead of next week’s meeting. Markets have an 86% probability of a hold, so it would take something pretty dramatic for them to hike at this point.

The general consensus as we look ahead is 50bps more between now and end of March 2027. I have the under.
