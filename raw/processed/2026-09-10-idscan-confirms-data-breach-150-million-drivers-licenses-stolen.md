# ID verification giant IDScan confirms data breach with more than 150 million driver's licenses stolen

IDScan, a Louisiana-based ID verification company serving entertainment venues and cannabis dispensaries, confirmed hackers stole driver's license data from its cloud systems. The breach included full names, driver's license numbers, and identity numbers from other government documents like passports.

The company stated it "received information" around September 1 about the hack claim — the same day journalist Brian Krebs reported a dark web site offering searchable access to over 150 million U.S. and Canadian driver's license records, including photos. Krebs verified the data using his own record, and the database reportedly included high-profile individuals such as U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth.

IDScan acknowledged that "full access to the information required payment," suggesting a ransom-style monetization by the attackers. The Pentagon and FBI both confirmed awareness of the incident. IDScan has not disclosed the exact number of affected individuals, though its own website notes it holds over 150 million driver's license records. The company did not respond to questions regarding ransom demands.
