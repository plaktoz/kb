# Closing the Gap Between AI Investment and Financial Return

The piece addresses a growing disconnect in corporate AI strategy: despite heavy investment in pilots, workforce training, data infrastructure, and model adoption, many companies struggle to demonstrate concrete financial returns.

The authors note that firms frequently pursue low-value AI opportunities — adding features customers don't truly value, or giving employees time-saving tools that don't translate into measurable business outcomes. This leaves CFOs unable to connect AI spending to bottom-line impact.

Rogers brings perspective from advising major corporations on digital transformation, while Sankaranarayanan draws on his AI transformation leadership at Eastman and 24 years of industry experience across ExxonMobil, SABIC, and Eastman.

The core argument: companies are "abuzz with AI" but the "returns have been difficult to quantify."
