# What to Make of the Anthropic Warning

## The AI Slowdown Goes Mainstream

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay calling for deliberate slowdowns in AI development, arguing advances are outpacing researchers' ability to control them. Sam Altman and Elon Musk both endorsed the idea. This followed the resignation of Jacob Coxon, a former Anthropic engineer, who warned AI could threaten humanity — a warning Anthropic's alignment science lead seconded, placing odds above 10%.

## Every Team Reactions

The internal discussion was measured rather than panicked. Key perspectives:

- **Mike Taylor:** Historical pattern of overhyped AI dangers; labs recruit worriers by design
- **Kieran Klaassen:** Anthropic's responsible scaling policy expects public resignations — it's the system functioning correctly
- **Brian Dell:** A decade without catastrophe isn't reassuring if odds are ~10%; the real danger may already be happening undetected

## The Navier–Stokes Breakthrough

OpenAI proposed a solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem. The proof reveals conditions where the equations predict physically impossible infinite fluid speeds — exposing a fundamental limit in fluid-motion modeling. Contributor Ashwin Sharma notes that skeptics are now reconsidering AGI timelines, and warns: "believing in it doesn't make me much better at anticipating it."
