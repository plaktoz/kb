# Wall Street Closes Lower as Oil Prices Jump, Indexes Notch Monthly Gains

U.S. stocks fell on Monday as surging crude oil prices reignited inflation fears, though all three major indexes still posted gains for the month of August.

## Key Facts

- **Dow Jones** fell 374.09 points (-0.70%) to **53,185.90**
- **S&P 500** lost 25.62 points (-0.33%) to **7,686.14**
- **Nasdaq** dropped 31.53 points (-0.12%) to **26,370.89**
- Despite the sell-off, all three indexes posted **monthly gains**; Nasdaq led August percentage growth; Dow recorded its **fifth consecutive monthly advance**

## Drivers

- **Oil prices surged** due to ongoing Middle East hostilities and closure of the **Strait of Hormuz**
- Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh** delivered a hawkish tone at the **Jackson Hole Symposium** the prior Friday
- Markets pricing in **>65% probability** of a 25-basis-point rate hike in September, per CME FedWatch
- **Iran's President Pezeshkian** indicated Tehran still seeks a negotiated resolution to the conflict

## Notable Quotes

> "The odds now favor a rate hike in September...Throw that into the mix of continued hostilities in the Middle East." — Peter Tuz, Chase Investment Counsel

> "If they do not hike rates in September, I think you will see a dramatic reaction in the markets." — Paul Nolte, Murphy & Sylvest
