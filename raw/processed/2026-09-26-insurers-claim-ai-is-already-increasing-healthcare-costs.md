# Insurers claim AI is already increasing healthcare costs

Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analysis found hospital AI tools used in insurance claims led to $942 million in additional healthcare spending over two years. The BCBSA identified a spike in patients documented with complex conditions, but noted "no evidence of corresponding change in care delivered" — suggesting AI-driven coding inflation without clinical justification.

The New York Times framed this as part of a broader AI-fueled cost escalation, with both hospitals and insurers deploying AI tools, worsening longstanding billing disputes.

Abridge founder Dr. Shiv Rao warned of a potential "horrible dystopic future" of automated adversarial systems, while also acknowledging AI could reduce friction and costs.

BCBSA's Luke Chalker rejected the "war" framing, instead calling it "a completely one-sided blood bath," positioning insurers as the losing party.
