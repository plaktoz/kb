# AMD Is Up 187% This Year and Nvidia Is Up 22%. Prediction: Nvidia Wins the Next 12 Months.

AMD reached a $1 trillion market cap for the first time on Sept. 21, 2026, surging ~10% in a single day to cap a 187% year-to-date gain. Nvidia, by contrast, is up roughly 22% in 2026.

**Nvidia's case:** In its fiscal Q2 2027, Nvidia posted revenue of $96.2B (+106% YoY) and net income of $59.7B (+126% YoY). Management guided Q3 at $108B and projected ~70% revenue growth in fiscal 2028, describing the outlook as "supply-constrained." At ~$227/share, Nvidia trades around 15x fiscal 2028 earnings estimates.

**AMD's case:** AMD posted record Q2 2026 revenue of $11.5B (+50% YoY), with data center revenue more than doubling. CEO Lisa Su indicated data center revenue could "more than double year-over-year in 2027." However, at ~$615/share, AMD trades around 40x projected 2027 earnings — after a 22% jump in just four trading days.

**Conclusion:** The author favors Nvidia over AMD for the next 12 months. AMD's valuation demands continued execution and sustained market enthusiasm, while Nvidia's price, at ~15x guided earnings, leaves more room for ordinary disappointment.
