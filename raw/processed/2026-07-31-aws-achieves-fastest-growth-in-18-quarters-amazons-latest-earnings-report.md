# AWS Achieves Fastest Growth in 18 Quarters. And That May Not Even Be the Best Part of Amazon's Latest Earnings Report.

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/31/aws-achieves-fastest-growth-in-18-quarters-and-that-may-not-even-be-the-best-part-of-amazons-latest-earnings-report/

---

By Jeremy Bowman | Published July 31, 2026

Amazon posted blockbuster Q2 results, with total revenue climbing 20% to $200.6 billion, beating consensus estimates of $196.4 billion. Operating income rose 43% to $27.5 billion.

AWS led headlines with 37% revenue growth — its fastest pace in roughly five years — matching cloud acceleration seen at Alphabet and Microsoft. Amazon noted that both its AI and chips businesses within AWS surpassed a "$25 billion annual revenue run rate," each growing triple digits year-over-year.

Beyond cloud, e-commerce results were equally strong. North America revenue rose 16% to $116.2 billion, and International revenue grew 15% to $42.2 billion — both representing five-year highs for those segments. Advertising revenue surged 26% to $19.8 billion, its fastest growth since 2023.

Key growth drivers included same-day/overnight delivery (exceeding 40% of Prime orders), grocery expansion to 2,300 U.S. cities, and the Amazon Now ultra-fast delivery rollout to 80 new cities. CEO Andy Jassy described grocery as a "needle mover," with monthly active perishable customers up over 50% since January.

Prime Day's move from July to June added an estimated ~500 basis points to e-commerce growth. Shares rose roughly 15% on the news.
