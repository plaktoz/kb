# International Business Machines Corp Stock (IBM) Closed Up by 3.51% on Aug 4: Key Drivers Unveiled

IBM closed up 3.51% on August 4, outperforming a Software & IT Services sector that rose 3.15% (Palantir +29.52%, Microsoft +0.99%, Alphabet +1.18%). Drivers: accelerating enterprise adoption of IBM's watsonx AI platform, which has become central to large enterprises' digital transformation strategies, drawing institutional investors seeking value-oriented AI plays with proven revenue. The consulting division's bookings backlog also gives long-term cash-flow visibility, appealing to portfolio managers seeking defensive growth amid market volatility. Expectations of a more stable Fed rate environment are supporting high-yield tech names with strong balance sheets like IBM.

Analysts have an average price target of $243.32 (range $174–$350) with a consensus Buy rating over the past month. IBM's latest annual revenue is $67.53B (7th in its industry) with $10.59B net profit (11th in industry).

Company-specific risks flagged: a securities fraud investigation announced August 2 into whether IBM misled investors about IBM Z mainframe demand, following a 25% single-day share collapse after Q2 results revealed deal slippage; "AI capex reprioritization," with enterprise clients diverting budgets from IBM's legacy mainframe/software toward AI-specific hardware, contributing to a roughly $700 million preliminary Q2 revenue miss; infrastructure margin compression, with infrastructure revenue down 7% year-over-year amid rising semiconductor component costs, and management admitting it "did not adapt and move quickly enough" to supply chain price increases; and a recent downgrade to "Reduce" with a $191 price target, with analysts flagging IBM's projected 10.7% earnings growth as far below the 19.2% sector median, and $69.8 billion in total debt raising questions about dividend sustainability.
