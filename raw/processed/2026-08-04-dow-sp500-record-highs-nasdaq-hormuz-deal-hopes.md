# Stock market today: Dow and S&P 500 soar to record highs, Nasdaq rallies on hopes for a Hormuz deal

Stocks touched record highs on Tuesday as markets eyed Middle East talks and earnings rolled in, with SpaceX's after-hours earnings in focus. The Nasdaq Composite surged over 2.6%, the S&P 500 added 1.8% to close at a record high, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 1.8% (about 900 points), building on Monday's record close.

Caterpillar, the Dow's second-largest component by weight and an industrial beneficiary of the AI build-out, rallied after sales and revenue topped $20 billion for the first time. Palantir stock soared nearly 30% after what CEO Alex Karp called an "otherworldly" quarter, with commercial revenue from the US government surging 90% year over year. That helped propel a rally in chip and tech stocks — the PHLX Semiconductor Index jumped 6%, with Intel, Micron, and Nvidia gaining momentum.

Other companies reporting earnings Tuesday included AMD, McDonald's, and Spotify, but the standout was SpaceX's first quarterly earnings report since going public — its stock has been in free-fall since its June IPO, a warning sign for other megacap companies eyeing public markets.

Crude oil prices continued to slide after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC there's "a chance we may have a deal today or tomorrow" to open the Strait of Hormuz. Investors received mixed signals on Middle East talks: President Trump called off a large-scale attack on Iran to restart negotiations, but Iran disputed that talks were happening, prompting Trump to call the country "unbelievably duplicitous" on Truth Social.

On the economic calendar, the JOLTS report was the first key labor-market data release of the week, ahead of Friday's July jobs report.
