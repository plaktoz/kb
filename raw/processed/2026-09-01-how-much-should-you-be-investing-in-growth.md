# How Much Should You Be Investing in Growth?

**Authors:** Paul Blase and Paul Leinwand (both principals at PwC U.S.)
**Publication:** Harvard Business Review, September–October 2026

The piece addresses a fundamental strategic challenge: determining the right level of growth investment for corporations.

The authors open by identifying a troubling paradox — despite U.S. GDP growing at roughly 3% annually from 2014–2023 (largely technology-driven), medium-to-large American corporations actually reduced their median annual investment rate by 16% over that same period.

This underinvestment trend is not uniquely American. A 2025 OECD study spanning 17 advanced economies found that "real business investment is roughly 23% below its pre-financial-crisis level on a weighted average basis."

The article's core argument is that most firms get capital allocation wrong, and draws on a decade of performance data covering 2,900 U.S. public companies to build its framework for doing it right.

**Paul Blase** leads PwC's growth platform. **Paul Leinwand** also heads the PwC Leadership Center and teaches at Northwestern's Kellogg School.
