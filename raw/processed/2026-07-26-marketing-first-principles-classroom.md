# Marketing First Principles: What a Classroom Reminded Me

source_url: https://www.amasf.org/blog/marketing-first-principles-what-a-classroom-reminded-me

---

Published: July 26, 2026 — American Marketing Association San Francisco

Marketing first principles outlast every platform shift. The article reflects on what teaching a marketing fundamentals class revealed about the limits of AI-driven marketing and why foundational thinking remains the durable competitive advantage.

## Core Argument

The author — returning to a classroom setting — was reminded that the basics of marketing (understanding your customer, crafting a clear value proposition, and communicating it effectively) are the same principles that have always driven success. AI tools change the tactics and channels but cannot substitute for the strategic thinking that comes from first principles.

## Key Insight

Marketing practitioners who focus purely on tools and platforms are vulnerable to disruption every time technology shifts. Those who reason from first principles — what does the customer need, what problem are we solving, what is the simplest path to communicating value — are resilient across cycles.

## Practical Application

First-principles marketing asks:
- Who exactly is the customer and what are their core motivations?
- What is the one thing this product does better than any alternative?
- What is the simplest, most honest way to communicate that?

These questions never become obsolete, while specific channel tactics (SEO, paid social, email automation) are constantly being disrupted.
