# Plan Mode is Dead

Nadeem reflects on building Nuanced, a desktop coding app centered on structured AI planning workflows. He initially believed planning would become critical to AI-assisted development, but ultimately concluded that formalized "plan modes" have become obsolete.

## Why he built it

AI models generate code faster than developers can understand it, creating a mental disconnect. He wanted a persistent, collaborative plan to bridge intent and implementation.

## Why it failed

**Planning is not the same as a Plan:** He conflated the act of thinking with a document artifact. Users had little appetite for lengthy specs.

**Models improved rapidly:** Better models make reasonable assumptions autonomously, reducing the need for explicit human-specified plans.

**AI text is hard to read:** Specs were "long" and "overly structured," causing eyes to glaze over. A secondary "Spec Tour" feature only added complexity.

**Sequential workflow was unnatural:** Their pipeline forced users to "finish thinking" before building. Real understanding is iterative, not waterfall.

## The new loop

The loop he now describes: understand → act → inspect → clarify → adjust → act again.

Planning still happens within this loop — it just doesn't manifest as a named document.

## Unsolved problem

As parallel agents multiply, helping humans maintain a coherent mental model of a rapidly changing system remains an open challenge. Surfacing where human attention matters most is the next frontier.
