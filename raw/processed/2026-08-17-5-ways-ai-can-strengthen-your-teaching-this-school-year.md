# 5 ways AI can strengthen your teaching this school year

The conversation around AI in education has shifted from "can AI write a lesson plan?" to "how can AI help me become a more effective teacher?" The best educators use AI to amplify their expertise and, more importantly, to protect time for the human work of teaching — greeting students, conferring about learning, noticing when something feels off, and building trust.

Five ways AI can strengthen teaching for 2026–27:

1. **Build an AI workflow, not just an AI toolbox** — use different platforms for different purposes (brainstorming, differentiated materials, formative assessments, family communication, visuals, analyzing student work) rather than seeking one all-purpose tool.
2. **Differentiate faster — without lowering expectations** — AI can quickly generate multiple reading levels, vocabulary supports, sentence stems, graphic organizers, and multilingual resources, freeing time for individual student support rather than making learning easier.
3. **Let AI support student thinking — not replace it** — students should use AI to explain concepts differently, identify gaps in reasoning, practice academic conversation, and generate practice questions, turning it into a learning partner rather than a shortcut.
4. **Make AI literacy part of every classroom** — students need instruction in writing thoughtful prompts, recognizing fabricated information, identifying bias, verifying sources, and deciding when independent thinking is the better choice.
5. **Use AI to create more time for belonging before behavior** — AI can draft family communication, rubrics, and meeting summaries, but the real question is what teachers do with the reclaimed time: building the relationships that make learning possible.

The piece closes by framing AI's value not by how much more work educators produce, but by whether the time it saves lets them know students more deeply and build classrooms where every learner feels seen.
