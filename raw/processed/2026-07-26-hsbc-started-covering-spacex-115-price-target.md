# HSBC Just Started Covering SpaceX With a $115 Price Target. The Stock Closed Friday at $115.07.

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/hsbc-just-started-covering-spacex-with-a-115-price/

---

HSBC initiated SpaceX (NASDAQ: SPCX) coverage with a Hold rating and $115 price target — below the $135 IPO price. Shares fell ~6% Friday, closing at $115.07, roughly 50% off their post-IPO peak of $225.64.

Notably, HSBC's valuation applied a "2x premium" to account for Musk's history of commercializing disruptive technologies. Even so, the sum-of-parts analysis only justified $115. A bull-case scenario assuming Starship commercial viability by 2027 and doubled launch capacity yields $293, but that's not the base expectation.

Wall Street remains sharply divided: targets range from $62 to $800, with a consensus near $237. SpaceX trades at roughly 75x trailing revenue (~$19B), remains unprofitable, and has yet to report quarterly results as a public company. Its first earnings report is expected August 4, 2026.
