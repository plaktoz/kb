# Stock market today: Nasdaq soars, Dow and S&P 500 rebound as Microsoft leads tech gains, bond yields rise

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-july-30-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-treasury-yields-microsoft-082255995.html

---

By Grace O'Donnell and Jake Conley, Yahoo Finance. Thursday, July 30, 2026.

US stocks staged a major comeback on Thursday morning after a Fed-fueled sell-off, as Microsoft (MSFT) stock led tech gains and more earnings were on deck. Fresh US attacks on Iran, a bond rout, and worries about AI spending kept investors on guard.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 0.8%, while the S&P 500 rose 1.2%. The Nasdaq Composite led gains, jumping 2.4% after the Nasdaq-100 entered a correction on Wednesday, led by heavy declines in chip stocks.

The fortunes of Meta and Microsoft diverged after the two "Magnificent Seven" stalwarts reported earnings Wednesday afternoon. Meta shares fell about 9%, extending a historic losing streak, after the company's earnings miss added to worries about its ability to recoup AI investments. Microsoft's stock jumped more than 16% after its Azure cloud business topped $100 billion in revenue for the first time — on track for its best day since 2008.

Amazon and Apple were both due to report earnings after the bell. Traders continued to digest the Fed's decision to hold rates steady Wednesday as the bond market revolted, sending the 30-year Treasury yield to a multidecade high near 5.24%. US strikes on a dozen Iranian targets overnight threatened to spiral into another escalation in the war.

PCE data Thursday showed inflation advancing at a slower pace in June than in May, welcome news for the Fed, even as GDP data from the Commerce Department showed the economy grew at a slower pace than expected in the second quarter (1.5% annualized versus roughly 2% expected).

## Selected live updates

**Microsoft's best day since 2008:** The stock soared 15% intraday, adding roughly $439 billion in value to its $3.43 trillion market cap.

**Ex-OpenAI star's hedge fund sells out of AI positions:** Situational Awareness LP, the $24 billion fund run by 25-year-old former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, sustained large losses on chip-stock bets (SK Hynix, Nebius) amid the recent semiconductor rout, and reportedly engaged its investors about a potential capital raise. CNBC's David Faber reported the fund has sold all of its public equity holdings and engaged a buyer for remaining assets — a stunning turnaround for a fund that was up 439% year to date on a net basis at the end of June.

**AI lowers wages but doesn't cut jobs, Apollo economists say:** Workers in occupations with high exposure to AI disruption saw wages grow 6.7 percentage points slower since 2023 than those in low-exposure fields, per Apollo Global economists Torsten Sløk and Sania Edlich, though AI exposure had little measurable effect on employment itself. About 5.8 million US workers (3.7% of the workforce) are currently in "high exposure" occupations.

**ESG funds revive on AI data-center boom:** US sustainable fund inflows posted a net gain for the first time since 2022 in Q2, per Morningstar, driven by investor interest in electric infrastructure tied to the AI data-center buildout and energy-security concerns from the war in Iran.

**Jersey Mike's IPO stumbles:** Shares fell 8.7% in initial trading after the $1 billion IPO, following a trend of weak retail-IPO performance.

**Macro data:** Initial jobless claims rose to 197,000 (week ended July 25), below the 200,000 expected. Core PCE rose 3.3% year-on-year in June, in line with expectations and down from 3.4% in May.
