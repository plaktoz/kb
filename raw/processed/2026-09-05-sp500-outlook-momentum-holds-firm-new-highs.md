# S&P 500 outlook: momentum holds firm as index hits new highs

In the last three weeks, we have seen a major recovery for US — and indeed global — markets. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq both hit new highs after a massive and sudden recovery. Risk appetite continued to hold up well, as European markets closed the gap with their US counterparts with oil prices easing lower.

Traders are pricing in the possibility of a permanent ceasefire in the Middle East and expect the flows of oil through the Strait of Hormuz to re-open as a result. Rate cut expectations from the Fed are coming back and earnings have so far been good. Investors have refocused on the broader macro picture and underlying fundamentals and away from geopolitics.

## Technical picture still constructive

The S&P 500 closed above the key 7,000 level for the second consecutive session, marking a fresh all-time high and clearing the previous January 2026 peak of 7,013.

From a technical standpoint, the S&P 500 outlook remains bullish, even if momentum has driven some oscillators to overbought levels. The RSI on the daily chart has edged past the 70 mark, which may leave the market vulnerable to near-term profit-taking. However, overbought does not automatically mean selling pressure — at these levels, the RSI reflects the strong bullish trend. Only a confirmed reversal signal would validate the RSI warning.

## Key levels to watch

**Upside targets:**
- 7,100 and 7,200 (round handles)
- 7,205 (127.2% Fibonacci extension of the move down from the January high)

**Downside support:**
- 7,000–7,013 (initial support, former resistance-turned-support)
- 6,970
- 6,890 and 6,847 (deeper pullback levels)

## What's driving the rally

- Earnings: major banks delivered strong trading revenues; tech sector earnings expectations nudged higher
- Geopolitics: absence of further escalation in Persian Gulf taken as green light
- AI narrative remains intact despite the geopolitical backdrop
- Brent crude still hovering above $95/barrel, but absence of escalation allowing equities to grind higher

The path of least resistance still appears to be higher, and the broader tone remains constructive heading into the major tech earnings still to come.
