# The Fed Voted 12-0 to Raise Interest Rates for the First Time in 3 Years. Here's How a 3.75%-4% Fed Funds Rate Impacts Housing Stocks.

The FOMC unanimously raised the federal funds rate by a quarter point to 3.75%–4%, the first hike in three years. Most committee members anticipate at least one additional hike in 2026, and the majority don't foresee rate cuts until 2028 or later.

The housing sector has been under significant pressure. Mortgage applications were down 19% year-over-year, and rates have been approaching 7%. Higher borrowing costs suppress both home purchases and refinancing activity, hurting mortgage originators and home improvement companies alike.

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaled a hawkish stance, stating "inflation is too high, and has been for too long," noting that summer inflation data showed no meaningful improvement.

The author notes that conditions could shift — easing geopolitical tensions or economic slowdown could prompt rate reductions. Among housing-related stocks, the recommendation leans toward higher-quality names like Rocket Companies and Home Depot (HD) for investors seeking limited exposure ahead of a potential rate reversal.
