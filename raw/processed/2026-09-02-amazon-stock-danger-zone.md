# Amazon stock just entered the danger zone

Amazon (AMZN) shares have dropped roughly 11% from August's all-time high, falling below the 100-day moving average. The stock has underperformed the S&P 500 in 2026, gaining 10% versus the index's 12%.

## FTC Lawsuit

The FTC and 22 states filed suit alleging Amazon's ad platform overcharged ~1.2 million advertisers by $20 billion (2019–present) via undisclosed reserve-pricing mechanisms. Amazon disputes the claims. Citi analyst Ronald Josey called any share dislocation a buying opportunity, citing AI demand at AWS and retail tailwinds.

## AI Capital Spending Concerns

Amazon expanded its 2026 capex to ~$220 billion. Evercore ISI's Mark Mahaney warned: "We now estimate Amazon's 2027 capex to be $320 billion, and its 2028 capex to be $370 billion," projecting roughly **-$50 billion** in free cash flow in both years.
