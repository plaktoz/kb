# Amazon Blocked Meta's AI Shopping Agent. Shopify Welcomed It -- and Gets Paid on Every Checkout.

Amazon began blocking Meta's two-week-old AI shopping agent, Muse, citing violations of its conditions of use. Amazon alleges Muse doesn't identify itself while browsing and may capture customer credentials. The company also has financial motivation — its $68B+ annual ad business depends on shoppers browsing its pages directly.

Shopify took the opposite approach. CEO Tobi Lütke announced a deep partnership with Muse, enabling agentic checkout via Shop Pay. President Harley Finkelstein confirmed that "agentic transactions carry the exact economics as an online store transaction." Every Muse checkout runs through Shop Pay, meaning Shopify captures payment processing fees automatically.

Shopify's stock surged roughly 15% over two days following the announcement. The company's Q2 results were already strong — operating income climbed 68% year-over-year, free cash flow hit $654M, and AI-driven orders to Shopify stores tripled year-over-year.

However, the author cautions against buying at current valuations. The two-day rally pushed the forward P/E from ~52 to ~60, and no concrete revenue figures from agent-driven orders have been disclosed yet. The author concludes investors bid up the stock "on a mechanism, not on new revenue."
