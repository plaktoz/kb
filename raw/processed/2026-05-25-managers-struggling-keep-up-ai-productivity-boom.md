# Managers Are Struggling to Keep Up with the AI Productivity Boom

source_url: https://hbr.org/2026/05/managers-are-struggling-to-keep-up-with-the-ai-productivity-boom

---

Authors: Liz Fosslien, Mollie West Duffy
Published: May 25, 2026

AI has dramatically accelerated the pace of work, allowing employees to execute ideas, produce deliverables, and launch projects far faster than traditional management systems were designed to handle.

One manager is quoted as saying: "Every 30 minutes, someone creates something I have to look at."

The article argues that managers were not trained to operate at this pace. Traditional management cadences — weekly one-on-ones, monthly reviews, quarterly roadmaps — were designed around human-speed production. AI collapses that timeline.

The piece covers frameworks for evaluating AI-generated output quickly, redefining what "done" means when an AI produces a draft in 30 seconds, and managing the psychological toll on managers who feel perpetually behind. The authors argue the bottleneck has shifted: it is no longer the speed of production but the speed of review, judgment, and strategic direction.

Key themes: managing people, time management, generative AI, personal productivity, adaptive management frameworks.
