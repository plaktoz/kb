# If a Stock Market Crash Is Coming, History Says You'll Survive If You Make This Move

## Core Argument

Rather than fleeing to cash during market uncertainty, the author argues investors should consider long-term bonds as a hedge against downturns.

## Key Points

**Why cash is problematic:**
- No upside potential
- Triggers capital gains taxes when selling winners
- Earns less than long-term bonds

**Why bonds work better:**
- The 30-year Treasury currently yields 5.25%
- Bond prices tend to rise when interest rates fall — which happens during economic contractions
- Bonds are largely uncorrelated with stocks, providing portfolio balance

**Practical access:** The iShares 20+ Year Treasury Bond ETF (TLT) offers easy exposure through standard brokerage accounts.

**Historical context:** Since 1900, the market has dropped 20%+ roughly 33 times — meaning bear markets are inevitable, even if unpredictable in timing.

## Bottom Line

The author doesn't advocate dramatic portfolio shifts, but suggests investors concentrated in tech or AI names consider "mixing in some fixed income to create a portfolio of uncorrelated returns."
