# Samsung to Double HBM4 Output Next Year, Sources Say

Samsung Electronics plans to more than double production of its HBM4 and HBM4E high-bandwidth memory chips next year, with glass carrier outsourcing volumes set to rise 2.5-fold — from 20,000 sheets/month this year to 50,000 sheets/month in 2027.

Glass carriers are temporary supports attached to wafer undersides during the thinning and drilling process, preventing warping. Their importance grows as stack counts increase; Samsung's upcoming HBM4/HBM4E products center on 12-layer and higher configurations.

Overall HBM capacity is expected to grow roughly 40%, reaching ~250,000 wafers/month (from ~180,000 currently). The HBM4 family's share of shipments is projected to climb from ~40% to ~80% as HBM4E mass production scales up.

Samsung began mass-producing HBM4 in February 2026, using 10nm-class 1c DRAM with a 4nm base die. In May, 12-layer HBM4E samples were provided to customers including Nvidia.

An industry official noted: "As Samsung expands HBM production, it appears to be placing HBM4, a high-value product, at the center."
