# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang tells Trump 'we're not going to let [an AI slowdown] happen'

At the All-In Summit in Los Angeles, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang received a live call from President Trump during his onstage appearance. Huang was speaking with the All-In podcast hosts — including White House advisers David Friedberg and David Sacks — when the call came in and was put on speaker for the audience.

The discussion centered on Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's recent call to slow AI capability development, a position that Elon Musk and Sam Altman have publicly endorsed. Huang's stance diverged from theirs.

Trump dismissed concerns about AI development, calling opposition a "hoax" and suggesting it plays into the hands of political adversaries or China. Huang agreed: "You're right. We're not going to let that happen, sir."

The article notes that public concern about data centers is largely rooted in practical issues — a Gallup poll found roughly 70% of Americans oppose local data center construction, with over half citing environmental resource concerns and about 20% worried about cost-of-living impacts.
