# Specification by Example (Book)

## Overview

This book presents case studies from over 50 projects examining how successful Lean and Agile teams design, develop, test, and deliver software efficiently. Drawing on research across teams worldwide, it explores how organizations specify, develop, and deliver software without defects in short iterative cycles.

The book covers how teams implement specification by example, agile acceptance testing, and behaviour-driven development to bridge communication gaps between stakeholders and implementation teams. Projects range from high-traffic websites to internal back-office systems, spanning small startups to globally distributed teams using XP, Scrum, Kanban, and similar methodologies.

**Audience:** Testers, business analysts, developers, and project managers on Agile/Lean projects who want to improve quality, reduce rework, and collaborate more effectively with business stakeholders.
