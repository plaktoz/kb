# Amazon Nears $7.5 Billion Deal to Acquire Israeli AI Startup Decart

## What happened

1. Amazon is in advanced talks to acquire Israeli AI startup Decart for $7 billion to $7.5 billion.
2. The acquisition is expected to be finalized before Thanksgiving and announced at the AWS re:Invent event in Las Vegas.
3. Decart's three founders, who are Unit 8200 graduates, must commit to a five-year tenure at Amazon as part of the deal.
4. Nafaa Bishara, the head of Amazon's Annapurna Labs chip division, is leading the acquisition negotiations.
5. Decart's software optimizes AI model training across diverse hardware, helping Amazon compete against Nvidia.
6. The startup was founded in September 2023, employs over 100 people, and was valued at $4 billion in May 2026.
7. Previous acquisition talks between Decart and Anthropic for a $6 billion deal fell through in September 2026.

Decart was founded in September 2023 by three graduates of the elite Unit 8200, including CEO Dean, Orian, and Moshe Shalev. The startup, which employs over 100 people primarily in Israel, was valued at approximately $4 billion in May 2026. Its investors include Sequoia Capital and Nvidia. As part of the agreement, the founders must commit to a five-year tenure at Amazon, refrain from starting competing businesses, and defer a significant portion of their payments until after this period.

Decart develops software that optimizes the training and running of AI models on chips from various manufacturers, improving performance and reducing computing costs. This technology is highly strategic for Amazon as it seeks to compete with Nvidia. The acquisition is being led on Amazon's behalf by Nafaa Bishara, the head of Amazon's Israeli chip division Annapurna Labs. The deal is expected to be announced at the AWS re:Invent event in Las Vegas.
