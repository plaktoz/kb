# Monday.com Is the Latest Tech Company to Blame AI for Layoffs — Here Are 20 Others

source_url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/

---

Authors: Rebecca Bellan, Connie Loizos
Published: July 25, 2026

Monday.com announced layoffs of ~600 employees (~20% of workforce) tied to an "AI-driven growth strategy," though its co-founder insisted the move was "not made to reduce costs or replace people with AI."

The article compiles 20 other major tech companies citing AI in 2026 layoffs:

Amazon (Jan): 16,000 corporate cuts; Jassy had predicted AI would reduce headcount "in the next few years"
Salesforce (Feb): ~5,000 support roles cut; AI agents reduced case volume
Block (Feb): ~4,000 jobs cut (~half its workforce); Dorsey cited AI-enabled smaller teams
Oracle (Mar/Jun): 21,000 total cuts over 12 months; cited AI adoption directly
Dell (Jan/Mar): ~11,000 jobs; $569M in severance
Atlassian (Mar): 1,600 cuts; CEO acknowledged AI "changes the mix of skills we need"
IBM (rolling): 15,000+ cumulative cuts; ~200 HR roles replaced by AI agents
Snap (Apr): ~1,000 cuts; CEO cited AI reducing "repetitive work"
Microsoft (Apr–Jul): Buyouts plus 4,800 Xbox-area cuts; acknowledged "AI is changing how work gets done"
PayPal (May): 4,500+ planned cuts; CEO vowed to "aggressively adopt AI"
Coinbase (May): ~700 cuts; CEO said engineers now "ship in days what used to take a team weeks"
Cloudflare (May): 1,100 cuts despite record revenue; CEO said most were "measurers" — middle management and compliance roles
General Motors (May): 500–600 IT cuts; AI cited as a contributing factor
Cisco (May): ~4,000 cuts; CFO called it a realignment around "silicon, optics, security and AI"
Meta (May): 8,000 laid off; 7,000 moved into AI roles
Intuit (May): ~3,000 cuts (~17%); restructuring toward AI
Google (ongoing): Estimated 1,500–3,000+ engineers cut via rolling reviews and buyouts
GitLab (Jun): ~350 cuts (14%); funding AI infrastructure for "100x growth requirements"

The Financial Times found U.S. tech firms have eliminated nearly 140,000 jobs in 2026, with Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft accounting for ~50,000. Notably, companies citing AI in layoffs have underperformed the Nasdaq by ~10% in the 30 days following announcements.
