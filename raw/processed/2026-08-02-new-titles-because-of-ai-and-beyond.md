# New Titles Because of AI and Beyond

Corporate and startup organizations are rapidly restructuring around AI, reflected in a wave of new job titles. Examples already appearing: Chief AI Officer (CAIO), who owns enterprise AI strategy, governance, investment priorities, and adoption; Chief AI Transformation Officer / Head of AI Strategy; Director of AI Operations (AIOps), who manages the reliability of AI systems in production; AI Program Management Office (AI PMO) Lead; and Head of Agent Operations, who oversees the deployment and performance of AI agents operating within the business (governance for this role isn't yet standard, but is likely coming soon).

Roles likely to become commonplace within 3–5 years, based on early market signals: Head of Agent Operations (managing hundreds or thousands of AI agents), AI Governance Officer, AI Workforce Manager (planning work allocation across humans and AI), AI FinOps Manager (controlling token/inference spending), AI Evaluation Lead, AI Knowledge Engineer, AI Identity & Trust Manager, and Model Portfolio Manager (managing a multi-model vendor strategy).

AI-native startups are introducing a different set of titles that read more like a specialized engineering stack than a governance structure: Founder of AI Systems, Head of Model Strategy, Chief Prompt Architect, AI Product Architect, Agent Platform Lead, AI Research/Infrastructure/Runtime/Evaluation/Reliability Engineer, LLM Ops Engineer, Agent Orchestration Engineer, AI Cost Optimization Lead, and AI Safety/Security Engineer.

The throughline: organizations are building the same scaffolding they once built for human employees — operations, governance, cost control, QA, knowledge management, identity, and vendor strategy — but re-specified for AI systems and agents rather than people. This reflects AI's shift from tool to workforce.
