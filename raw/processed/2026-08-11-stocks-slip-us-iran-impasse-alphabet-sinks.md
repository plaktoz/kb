# Stock market today: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq slip amid US-Iran impasse, Alphabet stock sinks

US stocks slipped Tuesday as the standoff between the US and Iran intensified, raising doubts about progress toward reopening the Strait of Hormuz ahead of a key inflation report. The Dow fell roughly 0.3%, the S&P 500 dropped 0.3%, and the Nasdaq Composite declined about 0.6%, following Monday's losses. The Dow closed at 53,791.85, down 184.13 points (-0.34%).

President Trump rebuked Iran's demand for war reparations and said he would wait for economic pressure to build on the country; Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said there was "no possibility of restarting negotiations" under current conditions. Oil prices rose even after Pakistan's defense minister said the US and Iran were "close to some sort of arrangement" — Brent crude hovered around $89/barrel.

Persistently high oil prices and an unclear path to ending the standoff built anticipation toward Wednesday's CPI report. After Friday's softer-than-expected jobs report, bets shifted toward a possible September Fed rate hike, with Cleveland Fed president Beth Hammack telling Yahoo Finance it may take more than one rate hike to rein in inflation.

New fundraising efforts by Intel and Nvidia renewed scrutiny of the capital-intensive AI build-out. Big Tech posted losses, with Alphabet (GOOGL/GOOG, down ~3.6%), Apple, and Amazon all declining. Earnings from CoreWeave and Super Micro Computer after the close were expected to offer insight into AI hardware demand health.
