# Put it to bed: Decade-old sleep recommendations still sound, experts say

Researchers from the National Sleep Foundation recently examined more than 3,200 sleep-related studies published since the organization released updated sleep recommendations in 2015. Their finding: "A decade of new science continues to support the original recommendations."

NSF affirms the suggested nightly sleep ranges for nine age groups: newborns (0-3 months) 14-17 hours; infants (4-11 months) 12-15 hours; toddlers (1-2 years) 11-14 hours; preschoolers (3-5) 10-13 hours; school-age children (6-13) 9-11 hours; teens (14-17) 8-10 hours; young adults (18-25) 7-9 hours; adults (26-64) 7-9 hours; older adults (65+) 7-8 hours.

"This review shows the core message remains unchanged: Getting the right amount of sleep is essential for health," said Joseph Dzierzewski, lead study author and NSF senior vice president of research and scientific affairs. The study was published in the journal Sleep Health.
