# Transformation Should Be a Learning Journey

**Authors:** Evgeny Kaganer & Christoph Loch (professors at IESE Business School, Barcelona)
**Published:** September–October 2026, *Harvard Business Review*

Most corporate transformations fail because organizations treat them as rigid, top-down programs with fixed goals and timelines. When the external environment shifts, these programs become destabilized, causing initiative proliferation, employee fatigue, and shareholder frustration.

The core problem: a CEO sets a "bold, monolithic transformation goal" and launches multiple initiatives to achieve it — but environmental volatility constantly undermines that goal.

The alternative proposed is an **experimental, adaptive approach** — treating transformation as a learning journey rather than a predetermined roadmap. This approach builds momentum incrementally and improves the likelihood of success by responding to real-world feedback rather than clinging to predefined targets.

Key themes covered include organizational transformation, change management, organizational learning, and business management.
