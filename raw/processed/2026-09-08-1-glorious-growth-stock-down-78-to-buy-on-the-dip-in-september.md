# 1 Glorious Growth Stock Down 78% to Buy on the Dip in September

DocuSign (DOCU) peaked at $310 in late 2021 — roughly 10x its $29 IPO price — before falling 78% to ~$68 as pandemic-era demand faded.

## The IAM Platform

DocuSign launched its Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) platform to address inefficiencies in contract workflows. Deloitte research cited in the article found businesses waste ~55 billion hours annually on poor contract management, representing "$2 trillion in lost economic value."

IAM features:
- **Agreement Manager** — a repository storing 300M+ contracts with AI-powered search
- **Iris AI assistant** — answers questions about any contract in an organization's ecosystem
- **Agent Studio** — lets businesses build custom AI agents for drafting and negotiating agreements

Adoption has grown from 2.3% of ARR to a projected ~18.5% by end of fiscal 2027, though only ~40,000 of 1.9 million customers have adopted it so far.

## Financials

- Q2 FY2027 revenue: $875.7M (beat guidance of $865–$869M), up 9% year-over-year
- GAAP net income rose 23% to $77.7M
- Operating expenses grew just 2%, showing cost discipline
- Non-GAAP net income: $224.4M (includes $148.6M stock-based compensation exclusion)

## Valuation

The stock trades at a price-to-sales ratio of ~4.1, well below its historical average of 11.8 since its 2018 IPO. Management targets $3.55B in annual recurring revenue by end of FY2027.

## Bottom Line

DocuSign looks "attractively valued," with IAM's rapid adoption potentially reigniting top-line growth for a more profitable, sustainable business.
