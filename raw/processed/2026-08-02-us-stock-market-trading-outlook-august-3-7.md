# US Stock Market Trading Outlook For August 3-7: Is August Bearish Or Bullish For Nasdaq, Dow Jones, S&P 500?

The US stock market is entering a new month on August 3rd, and the overall weekly outlook is cautious. Wall Street ended the month of July on a mixed note with the Dow Jones and S&P 500 marginally up but the Nasdaq Composite and Nasdaq 100 indices crashing severely on a month-on-month basis. A host of macroeconomic data and stock-specific actions due to quarterly earnings is expected. The much-awaited report will be SpaceX's first quarterly report since its debut on Wall Street. Treasury yields and oil prices will continue to trend amidst geopolitical risks in West Asia.

There are no special holidays in August 2026, so Wall Street trades normally throughout the month.

## Outlook

Last week, on July 31st, the DJIA closed at 52,485.03 (+0.53%), while the S&P 500 rose 0.70% to 7,489.72, and the Nasdaq Composite surged 1% to 25,373.85. For the month of July overall, the Dow ended higher by 0.34% and the S&P 500 gained a marginal 0.09%. But the Nasdaq Composite recorded a monthly decline of 2.6%, and the Nasdaq 100 crashed 5.15% to end the month at 28,274.20.

August is expected to be more bearish for Wall Street. "We are entering a more bearish seasonal period for stocks, historically speaking, as we enter the August/September timeframe," said Nathan Peterson, Director of Derivatives Research and Strategy for Charles Schwab. He cited risks from a lack of progress in U.S.-Iran negotiations and rising Treasury yields — the 3-year yield is at fresh highs of 5.273%, and the 10-year yield rose to 4.741%, the highest since January 2025.

"Higher Treasury yields are economically restrictive, they compress the equity risk premium... which can divert money flow away from stocks and toward higher-yielding, safer investments," Peterson said. On the bullish side, the S&P 500 Equal Weight Index continues to climb steadily, and strong earnings from Amazon and Microsoft have helped calm concerns around AI economics.

Peterson issued an overall "Cautious" forecast for the week of August 3-7, noting that lower oil prices and yields could trigger dip-buying in the still technically oversold tech/AI complex.
