# Not Sandisk. Not Micron. This AI Semiconductor Powerhouse Could Be the Biggest Winner of the Memory Boom

While Sandisk and Micron have benefited from AI-driven memory demand, ASML Holding (NASDAQ: ASML) may be an even stronger play on the memory boom.

ASML supplies chipmaking equipment — notably its monopoly-position EUV lithography machines — to memory manufacturers. Management reported that memory represented 49% of system sales in Q2 2026, with a projected 75% increase in memory-related net system sales for the full year, driven by surging demand for HBM and DRAM chips powering AI data centers.

Micron has reportedly sold out its 2026 HBM capacity, pushing customers into multi-year supply agreements. Deloitte estimates memory manufacturer capex could jump ~67% this year to $97 billion, then another ~50% to $146 billion in 2027 — a sustained tailwind for ASML's equipment sales.

Because ASML holds a monopoly on EUV machines, it can command premium pricing. Its profit margins, currently below those of Sandisk and Micron, have more room to expand, whereas the memory makers' margins may already be near peak levels. Analysts have grown more bullish on ASML's long-term earnings growth following its most recent quarterly results.
