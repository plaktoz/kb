# Berkshire Hathaway Is Set to Report Earnings Saturday. Here's What You Need to Know

Berkshire Hathaway is set to post earnings at 8 a.m. ET Saturday, offering fresh insight into performance during new CEO Greg Abel's second quarter at the helm. During the period, Berkshire closed a $6.8 billion deal to buy homebuilder Taylor Morrison, a major bet on the US housing market. Former CEO Warren Buffett publicly praised the move, telling CNBC that "Greg did that faster than I could have done it, smoother than I could have done it, and I never talked to the CEO."

Abel has continued repurchasing Berkshire shares, keeping with Buffett's longtime buyback playbook, saying earlier this year he plans to keep doing it "as long as our intrinsic value exceeds the market value."

UBS analysts estimate Berkshire repurchased $8.5 billion of shares between mid-April and mid-July and would view continued buybacks as a bullish signal. UBS raised its price target for Berkshire's Class B shares to $585 from $570, suggesting a full recovery to new highs within 12 months. Berkshire shares have gained more than 10% since the start of June, though they remain off the roughly $540 high set in May 2025, just before Buffett announced his planned step-down at the end of 2025. Shares were down 1% in midday trading Friday at around $519.

UBS expects "solid results" from Berkshire's businesses, with higher earnings from the BNSF railroad unit and a decline in insurance-arm catastrophe losses. Investors will also watch for changes in Berkshire's cash level and investments. In June, Berkshire agreed to significantly expand its investment in Alphabet, a decision Buffett said in a CNBC interview that he personally initiated. Berkshire's cash and cash equivalents hit a record $374 billion in the first quarter, a figure likely to have declined following recent purchases.
