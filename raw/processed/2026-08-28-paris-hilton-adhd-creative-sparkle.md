# Why Paris Hilton Believes Living with ADHD Gives Her a 'Creative Sparkle'

Paris Hilton opened up about receiving an adult ADHD diagnosis, describing it as "a huge relief" that helped explain childhood struggles with focus and school performance. She recalled feeling misunderstood and constantly punished simply due to how her brain functioned.

Hilton partnered with Collegium Pharmaceutical to discuss JORNAY PM, a stimulant taken at night that activates roughly 10 hours later, helping her manage mornings. She credits the medication with enabling her to be more present as a mother and entrepreneur.

Psychologist Justin Barterian, PhD, noted that stimulant medications — especially extended-release formulations — provide more consistent symptom control throughout the day. He emphasized that cognitive behavioral therapy and executive functioning skills training complement medication by building organizational and time-management strategies.

Hilton wants to reframe how people think about ADHD, saying it fuels her creativity and innovation. Her message to others struggling: "There's nothing wrong with you, [it's] just the way that your brain works."

She hopes her visibility encourages others to embrace their strengths rather than focus solely on the condition's challenges.
