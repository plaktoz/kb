# Case Study: Is Our Subscription Program Really Working?

The case study centers on **Hohenbruck Motoren**, a fictional German luxury automaker, and its car subscription service called **Hohenbruck Access**, launched in 2021.

The subscription's monthly fee exceeds a typical lease cost, but offers greater flexibility and bundled services in return.

The narrative opens with **Jon Weber**, SVP of Customer Strategy for Hohenbruck Motoren North America, commuting in the company's HX7 SUV while reflecting on the program's performance.

The piece explores whether the subscription model is genuinely delivering value — for both the company and its customers — touching on themes of business model viability, customer strategy, and growth strategy.
