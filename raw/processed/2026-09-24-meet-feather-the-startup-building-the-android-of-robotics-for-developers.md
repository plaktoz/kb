# Meet Feather, the startup building the 'Android of robotics' for developers

Feather Robotics is a humanoid robot startup positioning itself as a developer platform — the "Android of robotics" — rather than pursuing a closed, vertically integrated approach like Tesla or Figure.

Co-founder Hoa Mai, whose previous humanoid startup was acquired by 1X, partnered with Parsa Bakhtiari, a former Tesla Model 3 engineer, to build a modular, customizable robot priced at $30,000 — roughly half the cost of Unitree's H2 Edu. The platform supports AI models from Nvidia, Skild, and Physical Intelligence.

Gradient Ventures led a $7.6M pre-seed round. The startup has exceeded $1M in revenue, with robots currently deployed in restaurant kitchens and science lab cleanup roles.

Mai argues that successful hardware companies built deployable products first: "You can't buy a Tesla robot today and develop on top of it."

Gradient GP Darian Shirazi sees Feather as the only U.S. startup in the modular humanoid platform category, noting the economic case: a robot at $30K compares favorably to a $50–60K annual laborer plus overhead.

Feather's long-term bet is that thousands of physical AI application companies will emerge, and it aims to be the underlying platform powering them.
