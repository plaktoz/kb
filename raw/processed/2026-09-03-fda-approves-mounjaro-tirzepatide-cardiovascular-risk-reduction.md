# FDA Approves Mounjaro for Cardiovascular Risk Reduction in Type 2 Diabetes

**Author:** Mandy French
**Date:** September 2, 2026
**Fact-Checked By:** Jill Seladi-Schulman, Ph.D.

## Key Approval Details

The FDA approved Mounjaro (tirzepatide), manufactured by Eli Lilly, on August 28, 2026, to reduce major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) — including non-fatal heart attack, non-fatal stroke, and cardiovascular death — in high-risk type 2 diabetes patients.

## Clinical Trial (SURPASS-CVOT)

- First head-to-head GLP-1 cardiovascular outcomes trial (vs. Trulicity/dulaglutide)
- 13,000+ participants across 30 countries; ~4-year median follow-up
- Mounjaro: 12.2% MACE rate vs. Trulicity: 13.1% — an **8% lower relative risk**

## Expert Perspectives

- Dr. Mir Ali noted the findings suggest benefits "beyond blood sugar control and weight management"
- Dr. Randy Gould explained Mounjaro's dual GIP/GLP-1 mechanism reduces inflammation and "helps stabilize arterial plaques"
- Dr. Rigved Tadwalkar cautioned that "the population studied was very high risk" and results shouldn't be broadly generalized

## Clinical Significance

The approval may improve insurance coverage access and encourages physicians to consider Mounjaro's cardiovascular benefits when selecting diabetes therapies.
