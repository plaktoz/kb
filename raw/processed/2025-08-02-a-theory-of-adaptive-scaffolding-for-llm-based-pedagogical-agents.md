# A Theory of Adaptive Scaffolding for LLM-Based Pedagogical Agents

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01503

---

**Authors:** Clayton Cohn, Surya Rayala, Namrata Srivastava, Joyce Horn Fonteles, Shruti Jain, Xinying Luo, Divya Mereddy, Naveeduddin Mohammed, Gautam Biswas

**Submitted:** August 2, 2025 (v1); revised March 23, 2026 (v2)

**Published in:** AAAI 2026 main technical track (Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 40(3), 1757–1765)

**Abstract:**

The paper addresses a gap where classroom LLM systems lack the theoretical grounding present in earlier intelligent tutoring systems. The authors propose a framework combining Evidence-Centered Design, Social Cognitive Theory, and Zone of Proximal Development to enable adaptive scaffolding in LLM-based agents for STEM+C learning.

They implement this through Inquizzitor, a formative assessment agent using human-AI hybrid intelligence. Results show it delivers "high-quality assessment and interaction aligned with core learning theories, offering effective guidance that students value," demonstrating the viability of "theory-driven LLM integration in education."
