# A Split Verdict on Fable vs. Astra

Two major AI models launched the same week: Anthropic's Fable 5.1 (Tuesday) and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (Thursday). Every's team compared them at a subscriber camp and came away divided.

**Key findings:**

- **Daily driver:** Split — Kieran preferred Fable for buildability; Katie found Astra easier to steer in back-and-forth writing tasks
- **App simplicity:** Fable won — Jack Cheng's journal-digitizing app required fewer clicks with Fable
- **Diagrams:** Fable won — Astra added meaningless decorative flourishes
- **Prose quality:** Fable's draft had more "warmth and specific detail," though Astra was cleaner in shorter tasks

**Other coverage that week:**
- Katie's *Compound Writing* guide went open source — a repeatable loop for writing with AI
- 10 Every staffers completed Anthropic's certification; the training focused more on shared vocabulary than workflows
- Kieran's "folder as agent" concept was republished ahead of a new piece

**Notable detail on Fable 5.1:** It "blows past prompted limits" — when asked for 8–12 quotes, it returned 43.
