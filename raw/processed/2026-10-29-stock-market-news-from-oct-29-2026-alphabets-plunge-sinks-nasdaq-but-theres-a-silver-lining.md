# Stock Market News From Oct. 29, 2026: Alphabet's Plunge Sinks Nasdaq, but There's a Silver Lining

Oct. 29, 2026 at 7:25pm ET

It was a tough day on Wall Street with megacap tech losses dragging all three major indexes lower. Underneath the surface, most stocks were actually holding up all right.

The Nasdaq Composite dove 1.4%. The S&P 500 dropped 0.3%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 0.3%, or 132 points.

Alphabet, which is one of the market's largest companies by market cap, had its worst trading day in its 22-year history on the public market.

"Strong AI demand no longer guarantees strong stock performance," says Will McGough from Prime Capital Financial. "We're in a period again where the market’s re-underwriting the headline risk of all the capex."

Despite the index-level weakness, most stocks in the S&P 500 actually ended the day higher and most sectors were green. The communications sector stood out because of its dramatic decline.

Small-caps and mid-caps gained. The Russell 2000 and the S&P 400 ended the day higher.

The sharp drop in some of the market's biggest tech names drove indexes lower, but things aren't quite as bad as they seem.
