# Straits Times Index Keeps Constituents Unchanged in September Review

The Straits Times Index (STI), Singapore's main equity benchmark jointly calculated by FTSE Russell, SPH Media Trust and SGX Group, will see no changes to its constituents or reserve list following the September 2026 quarterly review.

All outcomes from this review take effect on 21 September 2026.

## Reserve list (unchanged)

- First Resources
- Keppel REIT
- Olam Group
- Sheng Siong Group
- Suntec REIT

## Significance

The unchanged composition signals stability in the current market leaders represented in the STI, providing continuity for investors and product issuers who benchmark against or invest through STI-based instruments — including ETFs, warrants, futures, and other derivatives.

The quarterly review process is governed by FTSE ST index rules, designed to ensure the STI accurately reflects Singapore's investable universe and can be efficiently replicated for index-linked products.

FTSE Russell (part of London Stock Exchange Group) covers 98% of the investable market globally. Its STI indices are widely used by asset owners, asset managers, and ETF providers to benchmark performance.

*Relevant holding: ES3 (STI ETF) tracks the Straits Times Index.*
