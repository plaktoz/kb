# President Trump's Strait of Hormuz Blockade Pushed the S&P 500 Down 0.79%

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/president-trumps-strait-of-hormuz-blockade-pushed/

---

Author: Lawrence Rothman, CFA
Published: July 26, 2026

The Iran conflict reignited in July, rattling equity and oil markets. The S&P 500 dropped roughly 0.8% in mid-July after Trump announced renewed blockage of Iranian ships through the Strait of Hormuz.

The CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) sat at 18.7, above June's average of 17.91, though still well below the ~25.6 average seen during the war's early days in March.

Compounding uncertainty, the administration announced additional tariffs — including a 50% levy on certain Canadian goods — and broader tariffs of 10–25% on 60 trading partners after prior attempts were struck down by the Supreme Court.

Despite the turbulence, the author argues long-term investors shouldn't panic. Historical context supports patience: an S&P 500 investment made before the 2008 recession would have returned 659% return, including dividends, through today; a pre-pandemic 2020 investment would have returned 153%.

For those without a long horizon, short-term fixed-income ETFs are suggested as more appropriate. For patient investors, volatility is framed as a potential buying opportunity.
