# AI Answers. You Decide: UNESCO launches a new Media and Information Literacy curriculum

As artificial intelligence transforms how information is created, accessed and shared, UNESCO is launching a new educational resource to help educators and learners critically engage with AI and make informed decisions.

*AI Answers. You Decide. – Teaching Media and Information Literacy in AI-Mediated Information Environments* is designed to address the growing influence of artificial intelligence on information, knowledge and society.

Introduced during Global Media and Information Literacy Week 2026 (24–31 October), the publication is part of UNESCO's efforts to support education systems in adapting to the rapidly evolving digital environment.

> Artificial intelligence is transforming how we learn, communicate and access information. We must, therefore, ensure that people retain the ability to think critically, question what they see and make informed choices. With this new publication, UNESCO is equipping learners with the skills they need to navigate the age of AI with confidence and independence.
>
> Mariya Gabriel, Assistant-Director General for Communication and Information, UNESCO

### Why Media and Information Literacy matters in the age of AI

Generative AI can create convincing texts, images, audios and videos, while algorithms increasingly influence what content people encounter online. These technologies offer new opportunities for learning, creativity and access to information, but also raise important questions about reliability, authenticity, privacy, algorithmic bias and transparency.

Media and Information Literacy provides a framework for addressing these challenges. Beyond learning how to use AI tools, it helps people understand the systems and interests behind these technologies, recognize their limitations and critically assess their impact on society.

### A new resource for educators and learners

The publication builds on UNESCO's flagship Media and Information Literacy (MIL) Curriculum, *Media and Information Literate Citizens: Think Critically, Click Wisely!*, launched in 2021 and subsequently piloted and adapted in more than 40 countries.

Developed in collaboration with eight international experts and peer-reviewed by ten additional specialists, the new companion guide is designed primarily for educators, teacher-training institutions and curriculum developers. It provides a framework for teaching MIL in an information environment increasingly shaped by AI.

Rather than focusing on specific technologies or technical skills, the publication examines how AI influences information, knowledge, human behaviour and society. It addresses five interconnected areas:

* **AI in everyday life:** Understanding AI systems, their applications and the role of personal data.
* **AI and the media:** Exploring how AI transforms journalism, content creation and access to information.
* **AI, the human mind and knowledge:** Strengthening critical thinking, informed trust and the ability to evaluate AI-generated information.
* **AI, power and human rights:** Examining algorithmic bias, inequalities, sustainability and the broader societal implications of AI.
* **AI and the future:** Encouraging learners to participate in shaping inclusive and human-centred AI futures.

The guide combines conceptual explanations with discussion questions, practical activities and examples that educators can adapt to different educational contexts.

> AI does not change the foundations of media and information literacy, but the environment in which we apply them. The goal is not to keep up with every technological change, but to equip learners with the critical thinking skills to navigate an ever-evolving information environment.
>
> Anne Leppäjärvi, Author of the guide
