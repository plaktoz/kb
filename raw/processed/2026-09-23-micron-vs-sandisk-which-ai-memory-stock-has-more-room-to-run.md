# Micron vs. Sandisk: Which AI Memory Stock Has More Room to Run?

Both Micron (MU) and Sandisk (SNDK) have delivered massive gains over the past year driven by AI data center memory demand. Analysts favor Micron for larger future upside (~53% projected) versus Sandisk (~24%), largely because Micron operates in both DRAM and NAND flash markets while Sandisk focuses solely on NAND.

Micron holds a 15% NAND market share versus Sandisk's 11%, and its DRAM share grew from 22% to 24% in early 2025. Deloitte projects DRAM prices could quadruple by 2026, with tight supply persisting through 2029–2030 — a dynamic that favors Micron's broader portfolio.

Long-term EPS growth estimates also favor Micron significantly. The two stocks trade at similar valuations, making Micron the preferred pick given its diversification, stronger growth outlook, and slightly cheaper price-to-earnings ratio.

The author holds no position in either stock; The Motley Fool holds positions in Micron.
