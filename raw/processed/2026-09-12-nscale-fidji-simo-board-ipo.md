# Nscale Adds Former OpenAI Exec Fidji Simo to Its Board Ahead of Potential IPO

**Author:** Kirsten Korosec | **Source:** TechCrunch | **Date:** September 11, 2026

---

## Board Appointment

UK-based AI data center startup Nscale has brought on former OpenAI, Meta, and Instacart executive Fidji Simo as a new board member.

Simo joins an already high-profile board that includes Sheryl Sandberg, Susan Decker, and Nick Clegg.

---

## About Fidji Simo

- Previously served as CEO of AGI Deployment at OpenAI — effectively the company's second-in-command
- Departed OpenAI in July 2026 for health reasons; continues in a part-time advisory capacity
- Led Instacart as Chair and CEO through its 2023 IPO
- Spent over a decade at Meta, including as head of the Facebook app
- Currently sits on Shopify's board

---

## What Nscale's CEO Said

Josh Payne described Simo as one of few leaders who grasp what it takes to scale products to billions of users and the infrastructure demands involved: "That's what we're building for at Nscale."

---

## IPO and Funding Plans

- Founded roughly two years ago
- Designs, builds, and operates AI data centers
- Reportedly seeking up to **$3.5 billion** in pre-IPO financing (per Bloomberg)
- IPO anticipated for fall 2026
