# Are Oklo and NuScale Power Still a Buy After Data Center Backlash?

Public opposition to data centers is growing, with Gallup polling showing ~70% of Americans oppose local construction, citing excessive energy and water use. This sentiment threatens nuclear SMR stocks like Oklo (OKLO) and NuScale (SMR), which have positioned themselves as power solutions for AI infrastructure.

McKinsey projects global data center spending could reach $7 trillion by 2030, but backlash may slow construction timelines — weakening the urgent demand that makes SMRs attractive. Their key advantage is speed (potentially ~2 years to deploy), but that premium matters less if AI companies face less pressure to scale quickly.

Natural gas presents another headwind. Elon Musk's SpaceX is investing heavily in natural gas for rockets and compute power, even planning its own pipeline. If natural gas bridges near-term demand until conventional nuclear comes online, the SMR case weakens further.

Additional context: Alphabet plans three conventional nuclear plants (600+ MW each). Only two SMR systems have been built globally; NuScale hasn't commercialized its design, and Oklo still lacks U.S. regulatory construction approval.
