# Open-weight AI companies are the Valley's hottest acquisition targets

Major tech acquisitions are reshaping the open-weight AI landscape. Nvidia is reportedly nearing a $13B acquisition of Hugging Face, following its $6B deal with Poolside and Stripe's $7B+ purchase of OpenRouter.

Nvidia's motivation stems partly from needing to reduce reliance on hyperscalers and frontier labs — especially as companies like OpenAI develop their own inference chips (e.g., "Jalapeño"). Acquiring Hugging Face would give Nvidia access to a large developer base to channel toward its own chips and standards.

Currently, open-weight model adoption remains modest — just 6% of companies use them per Ramp spending data. They're most practical for high-volume, repetitive tasks like customer service. For complex agentic/coding work, proprietary frontier models still dominate.

Fireworks CEO Lin Qiao, whose platform processes 40 trillion tokens daily, argues the future lies in model specialization: "Every single app company should consider hiring an in-house researcher."

The broader takeaway: as frontier lab dominance faces uncertainty, tech giants are hedging by investing heavily in open-weight infrastructure.
