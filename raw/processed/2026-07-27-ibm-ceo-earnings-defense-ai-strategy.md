# IBM CEO addresses earnings shortfall and historic stock plunge: 'Our AI strategy is the right one'

**Source:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/ibm-ceo-addresses-earnings-shortfall-and-historic-stock-plunge-our-ai-strategy-is-the-right-one-201452259.html  
**Author:** Brian Sozzi (Yahoo Finance)  
**Date:** 2026-07-27

---

IBM CEO Arvind Krishna opened the Q2 earnings call by acknowledging the shock warning a week prior: "Our AI strategy is the right one for IBM, and aligns to what we are known for: hybrid sovereignty and trust. With the portfolio, we have opportunities ahead. It comes down to execution. That is where we fell short in the second quarter."

IBM stock rose 2% following the earnings release.

**Q2 results:**
- Net sales: $17.2 billion (+1%), meeting estimates
- Diluted EPS: $2.93 (+5%), meeting estimates
- Infrastructure: -7%; Consulting: unchanged; Software: +5% (slowing from 11% in Q1)
- 2026 full-year guidance: 4%–5% constant currency revenue growth (cut from "more than 5%")

On July 14, IBM preannounced results significantly below the then-consensus of $17.85 billion, triggering IBM's worst single-day stock decline ever (-25%).

**CFO Jim Kavanaugh on the earnings warning:** "What changed is the level of magnitude given the extreme price increases that hardware providers and memory providers went to the market with in the last 30 days. Companies are building out the infrastructure portfolio to support the future of AI realization. Our inventory is up $600 million year over year because I went out consciously, made economically prudent buy-aheads for server storage parts, so I can protect against future price increases. This is just them filling out the infrastructure layer as they move forward."

**On cost cuts:** IBM has driven $5.5 billion in annual exit run rate savings to date and is raising that target above $5.5 billion. This 300 basis points of operating leverage allowed the company to grow profit 5% and operating margins 30 bps despite only 1% revenue growth.

**On breakup rumors:** "We remain confident IBM is strongest as an integrated company. Our strategy is built around hybrid cloud, AI, and quantum. All of that plays to the integrated portfolio of having a software, infrastructure, consulting, and ecosystem differentiated value proposition."

**Bottom line:** IBM will remain in the "penalty box" with investors until it demonstrates improved quarters. "This is one that you're going to have to wait," said Globalt Investments senior portfolio manager Thomas Martin.
