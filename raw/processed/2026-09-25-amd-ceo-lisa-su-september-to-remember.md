# AMD CEO Lisa Su is Having a September to Remember

AMD CEO Lisa Su has emerged as one of tech's most respected leaders after years of lower visibility.

## Key September Highlights

Su was photographed at President Trump's table during the US-China state dinner with Xi Jinping (Sept. 24), seated next to Elon Musk and two seats from Trump. Nvidia's Jensen Huang sat across from Trump.

On Wednesday, Su joined First Lady Melania Trump at the NYSE to ring the opening bell and participate in a fireside chat.

AMD's market cap crossed $1 trillion for the first time on Monday, with shares surging 9.9%. AMD stock is up 193% year to date, far outpacing Nvidia's 22% gain.

## Q2 2026 Financial Results

- Record non-GAAP EPS of $1.66 (vs. $1.61 estimate)
- Revenue up 50% YoY to a record $11.5 billion
- Data center revenue more than doubled YoY to $6.7 billion

## Forward Outlook

- Second-half 2026 server CPU revenue projected to grow 80% YoY
- Data center revenues expected to more than double in 2027
- AI GPUs projected to grow "well over 100%"

## New Products and Deals

- Unveiled Instinct MI450 Series GPUs and 6th Gen EPYC Venice CPUs
- Launched Helios rack-scale AI systems
- Microsoft will deploy Helios across Azure AI services (H2 2026)
- Anthropic deal: up to 2 gigawatts of MI450 GPUs deployed starting H1 2027; AMD committed to invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic
