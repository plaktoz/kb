# Stock Market News for Aug 28, 2026

U.S. stocks ended higher on Thursday, led by a tech rally after semiconductor giant NVIDIA issued a robust revenue forecast, reassuring investors about the potential of artificial intelligence (AI). All three major indexes ended in positive territory.

## How Did the Benchmarks Perform?

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJI) gained 0.2%, or 105.56 points, to close at 53,569.44 points.

The S&P 500 jumped 0.7%, or 55.29 points, to finish at 7,730.99 points. Tech stocks were the only gainer.

The Information Technology Select Sector SPDR (XLK) gained 3.2%. Ten of the 11 sectors of the benchmark index ended in negative territory.

The tech-heavy Nasdaq rose 1.6%, or 411.16 points, to end at 26,541.35 points.

The fear gauge, the CBOE Volatility Index (VIX), was down 4.60% to 14.51. Decliners outnumbered advancers on the NYSE by a 1.02-to-1 ratio. On the Nasdaq, a 1.11-to-1 ratio favored advancing issues. A total of 14.9 billion shares were traded on Thursday, lower than the last 20-session average of 16.3 billion.

On the NYSE, there were 192 new highs and 82 new lows. On the Nasdaq, there were 73 new 52-week highs and 85 new lows. On the S&P 500, there were 15 new 52-week highs and eight new lows.

## NVIDIA Drives Broader Market Rally

Stocks rallied on Thursday, led by an 8.7% jump in shares of NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) after the semiconductor giant reported blowout earnings and forecast solid revenue growth.

NVIDIA reported second-quarter 2026 earnings of $2.22 per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $2.09 per share. The company posted revenues of $96.22 billion for the quarter ended July 2026, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 4.82%.

The tech behemoth also projected revenue growth of 70% for fiscal 2028, sharply higher than analysts' earlier projections. NVIDIA has a Zacks Rank #2 (Buy).

NVIDIA's revenue forecast reassured investors that the AI boom has a lot of potential and isn't just a bubble. The robust quarterly results from NVIDIA gave a boost to other tech stocks, especially chipmakers. Shares of Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) gained 4.5%, while Intel Corporation (INTC) rose 4.4%.

Besides chipmakers, Salesforce, Inc. (CRM) gained big on Thursday, with the company's shares surging 22.6% after the company beat on both earnings and revenue estimates. Salesforce reported second-quarter earnings of $5.9 per share, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $3.27 per share.

The company reported revenues of $11.35 billion for the quarter ended July 2026, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 0.25%.

Investors also waited for the speech from Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh at the Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium on Friday, which could give them a clear picture of the central bank's future monetary path.

## Economic Data

The Labor Department reported that jobless claims totaled 203,000 for the week ending Aug. 22, a decrease of 4,000 from the previous week's revised level of 207,000. The four-week moving average was 205,500, an increase of 1,250 from the previous week's revised average of 204,250.

Continuing claims came in at 1,778,000, a decrease of 18,000 from the previous week's revised level of 1,799,000. The 4-week moving average was 1,788,500, an increase of 250 from the previous week's revised average of 1,788,250.
