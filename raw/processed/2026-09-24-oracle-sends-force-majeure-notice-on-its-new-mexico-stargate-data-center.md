# Oracle sends force majeure notice on its New Mexico Stargate data center

Oracle issued a force majeure notice to Blue Owl Capital, developer of Project Jupiter — a Stargate AI data center campus in New Mexico. The notice would permit Oracle to delay payments if the facility misses its 2028 launch target, though Oracle is not seeking to exit as the campus's primary tenant.

Oracle stated: "Project Jupiter remains on our planned schedule," adding it remains "fully committed to New Mexico." Blue Owl responded that "this notice does not change the financial commitments to this multi-year project."

The 2.45-gigawatt campus faces several energy-related setbacks:

- An Energy Transfer gas pipeline has been delayed ~6 months (now targeting Feb. 1, 2027) after repeated permit denials
- The pipeline route was rerouted following regulatory rejections
- A pending air-quality permit for Bloom Energy fuel cells faces a November 23 state deadline

Project Jupiter is a flagship site of the Stargate initiative — announced by Oracle, OpenAI, and SoftBank alongside President Trump — and has drawn opposition from residents and environmental groups ahead of midterm elections.
