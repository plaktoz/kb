# Here's How Much Oracle Stock Is Expected to Move After Earnings

Oracle reports earnings Thursday afternoon, with options pricing indicating traders expect shares to swing up to 11% in either direction by week's end. That range could push the stock as high as $179 or below $145 from Wednesday's close.

ORCL has dropped nearly 20% in 2026 and shed more than half its value since its record high last September, weighed down by concerns over AI spending plans and backlog concentration among a few large customers.

Morgan Stanley (neutral rating) cautioned that winning back skeptical investors may require "several quarters of solid results." Citi analysts noted they "see opportunity after one of the most extreme dislocations and drawdowns in the stock's history."

Consensus estimates call for fiscal Q1 revenue of $19.06 billion — a 28% year-over-year increase — and adjusted EPS of $1.74, up from $1.47 a year prior. Backlog is expected to reach roughly $640 billion, a ~40% annual gain per Visible Alpha.

Of 11 analysts tracked by Visible Alpha, nine rate the stock a "buy," with an average price target of $239 — implying nearly 50% upside from Wednesday's close.
