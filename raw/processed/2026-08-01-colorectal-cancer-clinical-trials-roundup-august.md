# On Our Radar: August 2026 Clinical Trials Roundup

**BRAF V600E-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), Phase 2, Pfizer-sponsored, multinational:** This trial evaluates the combination of encorafenib, cetuximab, and FOLFIRI in patients with previously treated, BRAF V600E-mutated metastatic CRC. Results presented at ASCO GI 2026 showed a significant increase in overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival compared to historical controls, offering a promising targeted therapy option earlier in the treatment journey for BRAF-mutant patients. Patients whose tumors are BRAF V600E-positive and have been previously treated are encouraged to ask their care team about this combination. (ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04607421)

**Expanded-access BRAF V600E cohort:** A related expanded-access cohort evaluated encorafenib, cetuximab, and chemotherapy in patients not eligible for the original BEACON trial. Preliminary real-world data shows efficacy in older patients and those with comorbidities, supporting more inclusive eligibility criteria for accessing promising triplet regimens. Patients previously excluded from BRAF-targeted trials are encouraged to ask whether real-world data may now support this approach. (ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT02928224)

**BREAKWATER trial — triplet therapy sets new standard for BRAF V600E mCRC (January 2026 roundup, still relevant):** A Phase 3, multi-center trial evaluated mFOLFOX6 with or without atezolizumab after surgery in stage III colon cancer with mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR/MSI-H). Three-year disease-free survival improved to 86.4% versus 76.6%, which may change adjuvant therapy for early-stage dMMR CRC. Patients with MSI-H/dMMR tumors receiving adjuvant chemo are encouraged to ask whether immunotherapy was considered.

**IVX037 + sintilimab in MSS CRC:** An RNA virus therapy trial targeting immunotherapy-resistant (microsatellite stable) tumors, aimed at patients whose cancers don't typically respond to checkpoint inhibitors.
