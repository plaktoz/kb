# Arista Networks vs. IBM: Comparing Quarterly Revenue Trends Between These Artificial Intelligence Giants

Arista Networks (NYSE: ANET) builds cloud networking solutions, high-performance switching hardware, and technical support services for major internet and financial firms. It posted a 45% operating margin for Q2 2026 while launching new data center hardware and expanding its enterprise security portfolio.

IBM (NYSE: IBM) derives revenue from hybrid cloud software, enterprise servers, and consulting services. It reported a 15% operating margin for Q2 2026, faced securities fraud investigations, and acquired HRL Laboratories to advance quantum computing work.

| Quarter | Arista Networks | IBM |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 2024 | $1.8B | $15.0B |
| Q4 2024 | $1.9B | $17.6B |
| Q1 2025 | $2.0B | $14.5B |
| Q2 2025 | $2.2B | $17.0B |

| Q4 2025 | $2.5B | $19.7B |
| Q1 2026 | $2.7B | $15.9B |
| Q2 2026 | $3.0B | $17.2B |

Arista has shown consistent sequential revenue growth, benefiting from AI data center buildouts. The company forecasts Q3 2026 revenue of approximately $3.3 billion.

IBM's revenue has been volatile, reflecting its mixed business model. Its infrastructure segment saw a 7% year-over-year Q2 sales decline, and consulting revenue was flat year-over-year. IBM reduced its 2026 full-year sales forecast as customer spending shifted toward AI infrastructure priorities.
