# AI in Higher Education Survey 2026: Student AI Use Hits 88%, Faculty Lag

The Digital Education Council's 2026 global survey (45,398 respondents across 35 countries) reveals near-universal student AI adoption, while institutional readiness trails far behind.

## Key findings

- 88% of students now use AI in their learning; 77% of faculty use it in teaching (up 16 points from 2025)
- 57% of students report inadequate AI guidance in assessments
- Only 29% of students believe instructors can effectively guide them on AI use
- 64% of faculty claim AI literacy training, yet fewer than 3-in-10 students see the results

## Dependence concerns

- 22% of students find it harder to work without AI
- 66% of students worry AI could make learning too shallow; that figure climbs to 81% in the US/Canada

## Regional divide

Faculty intent to teach with AI stands at 92–94% across APAC and Latin America, but has dropped from 76% to 67% in the US and Canada — the lowest globally.

## Assessment gap

Only 28% of students feel their assessments reflect skills needed in an AI-enabled workplace. The DEC's 2025 workplace report found "80 percent of employers said higher education is not keeping up with industry change."
