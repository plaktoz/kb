# Advancing Education through Tutoring Systems: A Systematic Literature Review

source_url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09748

---

**Authors:** Vincent Liu, Ehsan Latif, Xiaoming Zhai

**Submitted:** March 12, 2025

**Abstract:**

This systematic review examines how Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) and Robot Tutoring Systems (RTS) address global educational challenges. ITS employs AI techniques — including Bayesian Knowledge Tracing and Large Language Models — to deliver cognitive support, while RTS fosters social and emotional engagement via human-like interaction.

Following the PRISMA framework, the authors analyzed 86 studies and applied Latent Class Analysis, identifying three system categories:
- Computer-based ITS
- Robot-based RTS
- Multimodal systems

Key findings highlight meaningful progress in AI-driven adaptability and learning outcomes, though the authors note that "challenges such as ethical concerns, scalability issues, and gaps in cognitive adaptability persist."

The paper advocates for hybrid ITS/RTS integration and calls for future research addressing scalability, ethics, and support for diverse learners.
