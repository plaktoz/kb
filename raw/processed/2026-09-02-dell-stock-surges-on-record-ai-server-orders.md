# Dell Stock Surges on Record AI Server Orders

Dell Technologies (DELL) shares jumped as much as 10% Wednesday after reporting record quarterly results driven by explosive AI server demand.

The company posted record revenue of $47 billion for fiscal Q2 2027, up 58% year-over-year. Adjusted EPS reached $7.04, a 203% increase. Dell recorded $60.9 billion in AI server orders and exited the quarter holding a $95 billion backlog — both record figures.

Growth extended beyond AI hardware, with traditional servers, networking, and storage also improving.

CFO David Kennedy noted: "The momentum we've seen continues, and we are raising our expectations across every line..."

Dell lifted its full-year FY2027 revenue outlook by $25 billion to $192 billion, roughly 70% growth year-over-year.

Enterprise adoption of agentic AI workloads run locally on Dell servers has been cited as a key demand driver. DELL shares are up over 230% year-to-date. Peer Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), up ~110% since January, also gained on the news.
