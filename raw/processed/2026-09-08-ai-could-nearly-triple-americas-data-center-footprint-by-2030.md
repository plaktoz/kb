# AI Could Nearly Triple America's Data Center Footprint by 2030

Big Tech companies can acquire AI chips, but locating power to run them is increasingly difficult. Large-scale U.S. data centers may nearly triple by 2030 as AI infrastructure investment surges.

Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, and Meta are constructing massive facilities to train and operate AI models. Each hardware generation demands greater electricity, potentially making power the primary constraint.

Building data centers isn't straightforward — they require reliable power, grid connections, cooling systems, and years of construction. As the article notes, infrastructure is "starting to matter almost as much as the chips themselves."

For Nvidia, more data centers translate to more processor sales. For hyperscalers like Alphabet, it means billions in capital expenditure before AI revenues catch up. The article concludes that the next AI phase may hinge less on model sophistication and more on "who can secure enough power to run it."
