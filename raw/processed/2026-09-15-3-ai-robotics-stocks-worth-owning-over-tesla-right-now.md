# 3 AI Robotics Stocks Worth Owning Over Tesla Right Now

The article argues that Tesla's high valuation and uncertainty around its Optimus humanoid robot program make it a riskier bet than three alternatives.

## 1. Nvidia (NVDA)

Described as the ultimate "pick-and-shovels" play — whichever robotics companies succeed, they'll need Nvidia chips. The company posted 106% year-over-year revenue growth and a 62% net profit margin in its fiscal Q2 2027, with a PEG ratio of 0.46 versus Tesla's 4.37.

## 2. Alphabet (GOOG/GOOGL)

Google Cloud revenue surged 82% year-over-year, and Waymo leads the autonomous vehicle space. Alphabet trades at a 1.20 PEG ratio, well below Tesla's.

## 3. Zebra Technologies (ZBRA)

The smallest pick at ~$17B market cap, Zebra provides data capture technology and software that serves as intelligence for robots across retail, manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare. It delivered 20.4% revenue growth and more than doubled net income year-over-year, with a 0.59 PEG ratio and a 16 forward P/E.

The author concludes that all three offer stronger near-term fundamentals and lower valuations than Tesla, without requiring humanoid robots to become commercially viable.
