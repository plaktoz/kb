# Workday Completes Acquisition of C1.ai

With C1.ai, Workday Becomes the Identity Governance Layer for People and Agents

PLEASANTON, Calif., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Workday, Inc. (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced it has completed its acquisition of C1.ai, a leading agentic identity platform for people and AI agents.

Every new hire, job change, and exit is already a Workday event. With C1.ai, Workday extends its role as the identity governance layer for people and AI agents, helping organizations manage access to business systems from applications to cloud infrastructure. The acquisition strengthens Workday's ability to help customers govern access for both human workers and autonomous AI agents from permissioning, to monitoring to real-time security enforcement. This will enable Workday customers to manage their hybrid workforce across its full lifecycle with confidence.

As organizations adopt AI agents at an accelerating pace, they need a dynamic way to manage who and what has access to critical systems and data with the ability to apply security when AI agents perform tasks. By combining Workday's trusted employee data with C1.ai's identity governance capabilities, organizations can simplify access management, reduce manual processes, and apply policy-driven controls across employees and agents.

"Companies need trusted AI," said Gerrit Kazmaier, president, product and technology, Workday. "Today's combined workforce of people and AI agents requires a new approach to identity governance and security. Our acquisition of C1.ai is a deliberate investment in that future, bringing together the capabilities to govern access, monitor and enforce policies, to help ensure every person and agent operates securely and within the right enterprise guardrails."

"Joining Workday takes C1.ai's agentic identity platform to global scale on day one," said Alex Bovee, CEO and co-founder, C1.ai. "Embedded at the operational heart of the enterprise, we're positioned to help secure access for every person and AI agent across the enterprise in real time, at the speed modern business demands."
