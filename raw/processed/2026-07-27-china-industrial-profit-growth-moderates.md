# China's Industrial Profit Growth Moderates as Exports Cushion Uneven Recovery

source_url: https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/chinas-industrial-profit-growth-moderates-exports-cushion-uneven-recovery-4233721

---

Published: July 27, 2026 — The Daily Star / Reuters

Profits at China's industrial firms grew at a solid, though slower, pace as resilient exports helped cushion sluggish domestic demand, highlighting the economy's uneven recovery despite policymakers' efforts to spur consumption.

## Key Data

- Industrial profit growth eased to **15.1%** in June from **21.1%** in May
- First-half profits rose **18.7%** year-on-year, slowing from 18.8% in the January-May period
- Automobile manufacturing profits fell **19.5%** in the first half of the year; car sales declined for a ninth consecutive month in June

## Two-Speed Recovery

The figures add to evidence of a two-speed recovery in the world's second-largest economy:
- **Manufacturers** have benefited from robust overseas demand (particularly AI-fueled chip and equipment manufacturing)
- **Domestic sectors** continue to struggle — consumption and property market weakness dragged Q2 growth to its slowest pace in more than three years

"If this recovery can be sustained, it will be a good sign for the rest of the economy, as a return of profits growth could give companies room to resume wage growth," said Lynn Song, chief economist of Greater China at ING.

"The external environment remains complex and international commodity prices uncertain. Industrial firms also face weak demand and cash flow pressures." — NBS statistician Yu Weining.

## Policy Outlook

Attention is turning to the Communist Party's Politburo meeting at the end of July, where investors will look for signals on additional support measures. Expectations for broad-based stimulus have been tempered by resilient exports and Beijing's preference for targeted easing.
