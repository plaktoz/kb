## Does International Business Machines Look Undervalued on Cash Flow?

The Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) approach here projects what International Business Machines might return to shareholders in pure cash terms, then discounts that stream back to today. The current share price of $225.62 is meaningfully below the value implied by those long range cash assumptions.
