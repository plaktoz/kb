# Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta Will Spend Over $500 Billion on AI in 2026. Nvidia Collects a Huge Share.

source_url: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/alphabet-amazon-and-meta-will-spend-over-500-billi/

---

Author: Daniel Sparks
Published: July 26, 2026

Three major tech companies have committed massive 2026 AI infrastructure budgets: Alphabet ($195–$205B), Amazon (~$200B), and Meta ($125–$145B) — totaling over $500 billion, with Microsoft adding ~$190B more.

Nvidia is the primary beneficiary, with its most recent quarter showing 85% year-over-year revenue growth to $81.6B, driven by 92% data center revenue growth to $75.2B. The company guided for ~$91B in its next quarter at ~75% gross margins. CEO Jensen Huang described the AI buildout as "the largest infrastructure expansion in human history."

Despite surging customer spending, Nvidia stock slid Thursday alongside other megacaps, sitting ~12% below its 52-week high. The author attributes the dip partly to investor concern over whether massive AI spending will generate adequate returns.

Key risks noted: Nvidia's revenue depends heavily on a few large customers; Meta is deploying custom silicon alongside Broadcom and AMD chips, reducing some Nvidia dependency; and semiconductor cycles historically don't last indefinitely.

Valuation context: Nvidia trades at ~32x trailing earnings and ~21x forward earnings — relatively modest given its growth rate. The author views it as the most direct way to own the AI buildout at current prices, while recommending position sizing that reflects customer-concentration risk.
