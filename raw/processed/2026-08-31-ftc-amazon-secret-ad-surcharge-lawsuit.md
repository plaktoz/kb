# FTC Accuses Amazon of Running a 'Secret Ad Surcharge Scheme'

**Author:** Lauren Forristal
**Date:** August 31, 2026
**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/ftc-accuses-amazon-of-running-a-secret-ad-surcharge-scheme-in-new-lawsuit/

---

The FTC, joined by 22 states, has sued Amazon for allegedly inflating advertising costs secretly over seven years, affecting over 1 million brands and potentially generating tens of billions in extra revenue.

The complaint focuses on Amazon's Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Display ads. Amazon reportedly told 500,000+ small businesses it ran a **"second-price" auction** — where winners pay just one cent above the next-highest bid. This encouraged high bidding, as advertisers trusted the system to cap their actual costs.

Starting in 2019, the FTC alleges Amazon quietly introduced a hidden surcharge it internally called a **"soft reserve price,"** using what internal documents described as an *"invented auction participant"* — essentially a fake bidder — to drive prices higher than genuine competition would produce.

The result, per the FTC: advertisers were charged their full winning bid roughly **80% of the time**, converting a marketed second-price auction into a first-price one.

Amazon, which earned over $68 billion in ad revenue last year, called the lawsuit "misguided," arguing it "fundamentally misunderstands how advertisers operate."

**The 22 participating states:** Alaska, Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Nebraska, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Vermont, and Washington.
