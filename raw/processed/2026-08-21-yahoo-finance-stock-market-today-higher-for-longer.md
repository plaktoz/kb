# US Stock Market Today: S&P 500 Futures Edge Lower As Higher For Longer Worries Build

US stock futures were slightly softer on the morning of Friday, August 21, with E-mini S&P 500 contracts down about 0.1% as investors weighed a mix of interest-rate and bond-market signals. The US Treasury announced it would double its buybacks of long-term bonds, pulling 30-year yields lower in an attempt to ease pressure from higher borrowing costs. At the same time, Federal Reserve minutes showed some officials were open to raising rates again to keep inflation in check. The key question for investors: how much would "higher for longer" rates hit rate-sensitive areas such as banks, real estate, and smaller US companies.

**Top movers:** Strategy/MicroStrategy (MSTR) jumped 7.81% on renewed interest in listed bitcoin proxies; Coinbase Global (COIN) gained 7.58% after President Trump pushed Congress to advance the Clarity Act for crypto; Deere (DE) rose 6.94% on quarterly results showing higher sales, revenue, and EPS.

**Top losers:** Moderna (MRNA) fell 23.55% as traders reassessed the stock after a prior sharp rally driven by cancer-vaccine data; Walmart (WMT) declined 9.15% after mixed earnings and several analyst target/rating cuts; FTAI Aviation (FTAI) fell 6.21%.

**On the radar:** the Chicago Fed National Activity Index (Monday) for a broad read on July growth trends; South Korea Consumer Confidence (Monday) as a global demand gauge; the BCB Focus Market Readout (Monday) on Brazil's rates/inflation outlook; and continued market focus on Fed minutes and Treasury buybacks as investors assess future funding costs and bond volatility — all directly relevant to broad index holdings like the S&P 500 and global/world-index ETFs.
