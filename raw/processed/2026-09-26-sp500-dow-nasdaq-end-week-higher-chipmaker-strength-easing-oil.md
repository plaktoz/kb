# S&P 500, Dow, Nasdaq End Week Higher On Chipmaker Strength, Easing Oil Amid Signs Of Easing US-Iran Conflict — META, COST, MSFT, CRWD, SKHY In Focus

US stock indices closed Friday higher as oil prices declined following reports of diplomatic overtures between the US and Iran, while semiconductor stocks provided additional upward momentum.

## Index Performance

- S&P 500: +0.5% (closed at 7,743.41)
- Nasdaq 100: +0.4%
- Dow Jones: +0.9% (closed at 51,828.62)
- Russell 2000: +0.1%

## Key Drivers

Iran's Foreign Minister indicated Tehran submitted "a week-long proposal to Washington to resolve hostilities, unblock the Strait of Hormuz," per the NYT. WTI crude dropped ~2.3% to $92.41/barrel; Brent fell to $104.32.

Semiconductor ETFs (SMH, SOXX) each rose ~1.4%, tracking gains in Nvidia, Broadcom, and Micron. Treasury yields remained elevated, with the 10-year hitting its highest since 2007.

## Notable Stocks

- **META:** Addressing a security flaw in its Muse AI assistant
- **CRWD:** Federal prosecutors closed a distributor-arrangement investigation without action
- **SKHY:** Considering an IPO of its Solidigm unit, potentially valued at up to $150 billion
- **MSFT:** Unveiled a major Copilot overhaul with new AI agent and Office integration
- **COST:** Quarterly profits beat estimates, aided by tariff refund benefits
