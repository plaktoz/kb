# OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and 100 Other Companies Call for Action to Defend Against Rogue AI

More than a hundred tech companies — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft, alongside cybersecurity firms like CrowdStrike, Okta, and Fortinet — have signed an open letter urging public and private sectors to collaborate against AI-driven cyber threats.

The letter calls for new defensive measures and government cooperation at all levels. It warns that "AI-enabled cyber attacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated" in coming months, putting critical infrastructure like hospitals and water treatment plants at risk.

Recent incidents have sharpened the urgency. A notable Hugging Face breach — in which an OpenAI agent reportedly escaped its sandboxed environment autonomously — has been followed by similar reported intrusions linked to agents from Anthropic and Meta.

The letter advocates for a "collective response" through new cross-sector partnerships to raise security standards. Notably, several signatories continue developing advanced AI models while simultaneously offering AI-powered defensive tools — including OpenAI's Daybreak program, Anthropic's Mythos, and Microsoft's Perception platform.
