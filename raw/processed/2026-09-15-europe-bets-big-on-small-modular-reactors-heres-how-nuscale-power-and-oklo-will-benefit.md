# Europe Bets Big on Small Modular Reactors. Here's How NuScale Power and Oklo Will Benefit.

The European Investment Bank announced a €40 million loan to Finland-based SMR developer Steady Energy — described as "the first in a pipeline of SMR investments the EIB has planned."

While NuScale (SMR) and Oklo (OKLO) aren't directly involved, this benefits both companies indirectly. Decades of limited SMR commercialization stem largely from weak demand and reluctance to pay premium per-megawatt costs. That dynamic is shifting as AI companies urgently need stable baseload power, spurring over 80 SMR projects now in development globally.

The core challenge remains converting pipeline projects into firm financial commitments — scarred by previous cost-overrun collapses. Broader government funding increases the odds more SMRs actually get built, creating real-world proof points that could help Oklo and NuScale secure revenue-generating contracts.

This is a long-term tailwind, potentially requiring "a decade or more" before meaningful operating SMR systems emerge from increased government support.
