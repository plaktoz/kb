# The Most Active Stocks Today Include Tesla, Nvidia, and Apple: Here's My Contrarian Take on All Three

James Brumley takes a bearish minority stance on three heavily traded mega-cap stocks:

**Nvidia:** Despite leading AI chip sales — data center revenue up 117% YoY — competition is growing from Alphabet's Tensor Processing Units and Amazon's Graviton processors. The stock is priced for "84% top-line growth this year" with no margin for error, and poor AI ROIs may push institutions toward alternatives.

**Tesla:** The Optimus humanoid robot remains a key valuation driver, but Brumley warns investors underestimate rivals like 1X Technologies (NEO), Figure AI, Unitree, and others nearing market entry. Tesla is already "down nearly 30% from its December peak" yet still trades at elevated multiples.

**Apple:** iPhone revenue jumped 22% in the June quarter, and Siri has improved. However, the stock trades near analysts' consensus price target of $333.10, leaving little upside. A new CEO (John Ternus, effective Sept. 1) faces a maturing competitive landscape, and Apple's once-proprietary AI development silo is eroding — Siri now runs on Google's Gemini, with ChatGPT also integrated into iOS.
