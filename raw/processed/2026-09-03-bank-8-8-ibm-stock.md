# How To Bank 8.8% A Year On IBM Stock Before Buying A Single Share

**Author:** Trefis Team
**Date:** September 3, 2026

## Summary

The article outlines a cash-secured put options strategy on IBM stock, which has fallen roughly 30% from its 52-week high following a disappointing Q2.

## The Trade Setup

- Sell a put on IBM expiring 9/17/2027 at a $165 strike price
- Collect ~$843 premium per contract (100 shares)
- ~4.9% annualized yield on $16,500 set aside as collateral
- Pair with T-bills yielding ~3.9% for a combined ~8.8% total annualized return
- If assigned, effective entry is ~$157/share — roughly a 33% discount to the then-current ~$231 price

## Two Outcomes

1. IBM stays above $165 → put expires worthless, investor keeps full premium
2. IBM falls below $165 → investor buys shares at an effective ~$157 cost basis

## Bull Case for IBM

Software segment drives ~80% of annual revenue, with annualized recurring revenue of $24.6 billion, up 8% year-over-year. The z17 mainframe refresh is described as running at "nearly 130% program to program" versus its predecessor.

## Key Risk

Q2 transaction processing revenue declined 9%, raising the question of whether customer spending shifts are "deferred or destroyed."

## Conclusion

The article recommends this trade as a complement to a diversified core portfolio rather than a standalone strategy.
