# Wolfe Research sees strong S&P 500 earnings momentum

Wolfe Research reported that 465 S&P 500 companies had released Q2 2026 results through Wednesday, with 69% beating consensus revenue estimates by a dollar-weighted surprise of 3.8%.

Of 86 companies that provided Q3 2026 guidance, 64% offered a midpoint above consensus estimates — the highest percentage since the COVID period.

S&P 500 operating EPS is expected to grow 31% in 2026; Wolfe Research estimates roughly 27% growth on an adjusted basis after excluding large one-time gains from mega-cap technology companies. The firm called this the strongest fundamental outlook outside of a post-recession recovery in over 50 years.

Wolfe Research flagged the sustainability of this growth into 2027 as the key open question, particularly given rising AI capital spending.
