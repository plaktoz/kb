# Goldman Sachs doubles down on Applied Materials stock target

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/goldman-sachs-doubles-down-applied-133300462.html

---

By Peace Longe | Published July 11, 2026

Goldman Sachs maintained its buy rating on Applied Materials (AMAT) and raised its 12-month price target to $645 from $520, applying roughly 32x a normalized EPS of ~$20/share — a valuation reflecting confidence in long-term durability rather than a single strong quarter.

Key driver: DRAM and HBM demand. Applied Materials manufactures deposition, etch, and packaging equipment used across chipmaking, making it a bet on industry-wide capital expenditure. The company reported record Q2 revenue of $7.91 billion (+~20% YoY), beating EPS estimates ($2.86 vs. $2.68 expected).

Goldman projects non-GAAP EPS of $14.15 for 2026 — about 6% above consensus.

CEO signal: Gary Dickerson told Nikkei Asia that customers are providing equipment demand forecasts extending two years or more, with some plans reaching 2030.

Stock performance (2026 YTD):
- AMAT: ~+127%
- S&P 500: ~+10%

Risks cited: Export restrictions on advanced tools and growing competition from domestic Chinese equipment suppliers.

Next catalyst: August 13 earnings report (consensus: $3.39/share, $8.94B revenue).

The average analyst target across 29 analysts sits at ~$617, meaning AMAT has already surpassed the consensus fair value estimate.
