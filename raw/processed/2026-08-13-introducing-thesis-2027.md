# Introducing Thesis: 2027

Every is launching **Thesis**, a one-day conference on November 5, 2026, at Pioneer Works in Brooklyn, focused on the future of human work in an era of AI automation.

The event targets a specific audience: people already using AI to pursue "more ambitious, more creative, and more interesting human work" — builders, operators, and frontier AI lab leaders who are living the future rather than speculating about it.

## Key Details

- **Format:** Talks, demos, working sessions, office hours, small-group conversations
- **Attendance:** Application-based; limited in-person capacity; free livestream available
- **Location:** Pioneer Works, Red Hook, Brooklyn

## Notable Speakers Include

- Ivan Zhao (Notion CEO)
- Andrew Ambrosino (OpenAI Codex)
- Cat de Jong (Anthropic)
- Nick Thompson (The Atlantic)
- Josh Miller (The Browser Company)
- Cristobal Valenzuela (Runway)
- Sahil Lavingia (Gumroad)

## Core Thesis

The conference centers on one question: what does excellent human work look like once automation becomes widespread? The organizers believe New York is uniquely positioned as the place where "new model capabilities meet real-world work," making it ideal for observing AI's practical impact.

Applications available at every.to/thesis-2027.
