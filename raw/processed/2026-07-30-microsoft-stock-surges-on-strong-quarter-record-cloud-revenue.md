# Microsoft stock surges on strong quarter, record cloud revenue

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/microsoft-stock-surges-on-strong-quarter-record-cloud-revenue-123002444.html

---

By Ines Ferré and Daniel Howley, Yahoo Finance. July 30, 2026.

**What happened:** Microsoft (MSFT) surged more than 15% on Thursday.

**What's behind the move:** The software and cloud giant reported fourth-quarter earnings Wednesday that beat Wall Street's estimates on the top and bottom lines, as its Azure service topped $100 billion in revenue for the first time. Microsoft said it spent $41 billion on capital expenditures, including leases, in the quarter — below the anticipated $42 billion. The Intelligent Cloud segment saw revenue of $39.3 billion versus expectations of $38.1 billion. Shares closed the day at $451.10, up $60.56 (+15.51%).

**What else you need to know:** Microsoft, like much of the tech industry, has faced concerns about aggressive capital expenditures and their impact on free cash flow, and whether AI investments are paying off in revenue terms. Investors appear relieved that capex came in below expectations and that 2026 spending plans were unchanged. By contrast, Google parent Alphabet said last week it plans to spend $195–205 billion on capex for the year, up from a prior $180–190 billion estimate and well ahead of analyst expectations of $186.4 billion — sending Alphabet stock tumbling more than 6% that day. Microsoft stock is down 5% year to date despite Thursday's surge.
