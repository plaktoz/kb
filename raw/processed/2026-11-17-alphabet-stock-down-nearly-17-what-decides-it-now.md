# Alphabet Stock Down Nearly 17%: What Decides It Now?

If you own Alphabet (GOOGL), the past month has been hard: the stock lost 16.7% between October 16 and November 16, 2026, while the S&P 500 lost 3.1%. Management also expects capital spending to increase significantly in 2027 and will give the details on its fiscal Q4 2026 call. Below are the four events most likely to move Alphabet stock over the next six months, soonest first.

## Alphabet's Spending Plan For 2027

Alphabet is expected to report its fiscal Q4 2026 results in February 2027. Management has said the call will set out a 2027 capital spending plan it already expects to rise significantly. The company left its full-year 2026 guide for capital spending unchanged at $195 billion to $205 billion on the fiscal Q3 2026 call. That spending drove negative free cash flow in fiscal Q3 2026, management said. Management expects the build-out and the inventory of TPU systems to keep pressure on free cash flow. The number to watch on the February call is the size of the 2027 spending plan.

## TPU System Sales In Google Cloud

Management expects sales of TPU systems, which it describes as complete systems of hardware, compute, storage and networking, not just a chip, to keep growing in fiscal Q4 2026 and then to bring a meaningful rise in revenue in 2027. These sales run through Google Cloud, whose revenue rose 103% to $30.7 billion in fiscal Q3 2026, and management named TPU system sales as a meaningful contributor. Alphabet has recently entered partnerships with Crux AI and Oracle to deliver TPU systems, with more in the works, management said. The measure to watch is Google Cloud revenue growth in the February 2027 report.

## Growth In Google Search

Management expects Search growth to slow in fiscal Q4 2026, because Alphabet is now up against the pickup in Search that began in fiscal Q3 2025, and because currency moves should weigh slightly more than in fiscal Q3 2026, mainly on Search and YouTube ads. Search and other advertising revenue grew 12% to $63.3 billion in fiscal Q3 2026. Management also said fast growth in AI Mode queries added to the slowdown from fiscal Q2 to fiscal Q3 2026, because AI Mode was earning less from ads. Alphabet is now scaling ads in AI Mode and taking them to international markets. Search revenue growth in the February 2027 report is the figure to watch.

## Can You Sit Through These Dates With Alphabet?

Alphabet stock fell 30.7% from its close on May 13, 2026 to its close on November 2, 2026. Its largest one-day move of the past year came inside that fall: a 12.6% drop on October 29, 2026, the session after the fiscal Q3 2026 call. The 2027 spending plan, Google Cloud revenue and Search revenue all arrive in one report, in February 2027. If you could watch your Alphabet holding go through its May-to-November fall again and still keep it, you can hold through these dates; if a repeat would push you to sell, the holding is larger than these dates allow. For the risk to be worth taking, Google Cloud's growth has to keep pace with the spending, and the February 2027 report will show both.

Every one of these events hits a single company, so how much of your money is in Alphabet, and how you would step out of it, is worth settling before the February report.
