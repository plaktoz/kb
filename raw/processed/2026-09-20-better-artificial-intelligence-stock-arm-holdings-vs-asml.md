# Better Artificial Intelligence Stock: Arm Holdings vs. ASML

The article compares Arm Holdings and ASML as AI investment options, examining each company's business model, financials, risks, and valuation.

**Arm Holdings** licenses energy-efficient CPU architecture used in over 350 billion chips, powering most smartphones globally. Its FY2026 revenue hit $4.9B (up ~23% YoY), with an 18.4% net margin. The balance sheet shows minimal debt (0.1x D/E) and strong liquidity (6.0x current ratio), though stock-based compensation consumed roughly 69% of operating cash flow.

**ASML** holds a monopoly on EUV lithography machines essential for advanced chip manufacturing. FY2025 revenue reached ~€32.7B (up from €28.3B), with a 29.4% net margin and €11.1B in free cash flow. Its D/E ratio also stands at 0.1x, though its current ratio is a more modest 1.3x.

**Key risks:** Arm faces RISC-V open-source competition and smartphone market concentration; ASML contends with geopolitical export restrictions (notably China) and semiconductor industry cyclicality.

**Valuation gap is stark** — Arm trades at a 125x forward P/E versus ASML's 27.9x, and a 57.3x P/S versus 16.5x.

ASML suits income-oriented investors (it pays a dividend; Arm does not) while Arm appeals to growth investors, given its faster revenue growth and expansion into physical AI and data center markets.
