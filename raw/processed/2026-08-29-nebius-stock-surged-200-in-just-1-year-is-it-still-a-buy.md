# Nebius Stock Surged 200% in Just 1 Year. Is It Still a Buy?

Nebius Group (NBIS) provides AI computing infrastructure — renting GPU-powered data centers to businesses building AI models. Its Q2 results showed revenue jumping 454% year-over-year to $582 million, with adjusted EBITDA of $236 million and four contracts exceeding $1 billion each.

Management projects 2026 full-year revenue of $3–$3.4 billion and year-end annual recurring revenue of $7–$9 billion. The company reports over $40 billion in customer commitments and says its 2027 capacity could be sold at current terms.

A notable improvement: infrastructure payback periods have shortened to roughly 22 months, down from the historical two-to-three-year range — a meaningful gain in capital efficiency.

However, risks remain. Overcapacity across the industry could pressure pricing. The stock trades at ~18x forward sales on a ~$60 billion market cap. Shares also dropped over 20% recently after a $5 billion convertible notes offering raised dilution concerns.

The author's takeaway: the business is strong, but "a great company isn't always a great stock at any price." Existing holders needn't sell; new investors might consider a smaller initial position rather than a large commitment at current valuations.
