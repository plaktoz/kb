# ScrollEd Wants to Turn Textbooks into TikTok

**Author:** Rebecca Bellan
**Date:** September 20, 2026

---

## Summary

Palo Alto startup **ScrollEd** is pitching at TechCrunch Disrupt Startup Battlefield 200 with an app that converts textbooks and PDFs into a scrollable, TikTok-style feed featuring AI-generated video, audio, text, and interactive quizzes.

---

## How It Works

- Upload any text file or PDF
- Content is transformed into a vertical, swipeable feed
- Swipe **up** → new topic
- Swipe **sideways** → go deeper on current topic, ending in a quiz

---

## Founders

Co-founders and spouses **Utsav Gupta** (AI & human purpose, Stanford) and **Rebecca Neff** (computer science, UPenn) bootstrapped ScrollEd in 2026 after feeling dissatisfied with their own doomscrolling habits.

Gupta noted the core tension: younger users are gravitating toward short-form, vertical feeds, which "poses challenges for any kind of media that requires depth."

---

## Business Model

| Tier | Details |
|------|---------|
| Free | Consumer feed |
| Pro | Paid subscription |
| Institutional | Annual licenses with engagement/progress reporting |

Target markets include educational institutions, corporate training, and individual consumers.

---

## What's Next

- Consumer launch at Disrupt (October 13–15, San Francisco)
- Expanding lesson library and source-checking workflows
- Institution pilot programs
- Future: personalized, adaptive learning infrastructure

The broader vision, per Gupta: *"a social network that's better for you."*
