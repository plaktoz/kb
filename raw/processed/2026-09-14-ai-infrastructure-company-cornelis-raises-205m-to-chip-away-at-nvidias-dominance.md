# AI infrastructure company Cornelis raises $205M to chip away at Nvidia's dominance

Cornelis, a networking technology firm that helps AI chips communicate more efficiently, announced a $205 million funding round led by IAG Capital Partners.

The company unveiled its "Active Compute Fabric" product — a networking solution addressing the problem of GPU idle time spent waiting for data, enabling chips to process and transmit information simultaneously.

Spun out of Intel in 2020, Cornelis differentiates itself from Nvidia through an open architecture approach, allowing customers to pair various GPU and accelerator hardware with its networking fabric. By contrast, while Nvidia chips can technically run on competing fabrics, they are heavily optimized for Nvidia's own software ecosystem, creating strong lock-in incentives.

The company is already shipping its current product and is developing a next-generation version slated for release later in 2026. Cornelis represents part of a broader wave of AI infrastructure startups seeking to erode Nvidia's market dominance across different layers of the stack.
