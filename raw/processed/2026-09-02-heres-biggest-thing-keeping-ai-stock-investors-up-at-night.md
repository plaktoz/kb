# Here's the biggest thing keeping AI stock investors up at night

**Author:** Brian Sozzi, Executive Editor
**Date:** September 2, 2026

## Summary

The central investor concern is whether AI demand has peaked. Deutsche Bank analyst Melissa Weathers noted a gap between management optimism and investor skepticism, writing about "a notable discrepancy between management teams' outlooks for fundamentals and investors' outlooks for share prices."

## The Core Fear: Peak AI Cycle

Tech executives at a Deutsche Bank conference signaled "steady and strong trends," while investors expressed "peak cycle" fears, particularly around DRAM pricing and memory wall constraints.

## Data Pushing Back on Pessimism

- **Dell** reported fiscal Q2 sales surging 58% year-over-year to a record $47B, with EPS up 273%, and issued FY2027 guidance ~$25B above estimates
- **Nvidia** projected 70% revenue growth for FY2028, topping analyst forecasts of 45%
- **PWC** projected global data center spending could reach $31.6–$50 trillion through 2050

## Analyst Views

- Evercore ISI's Amit Daryanani said Dell appears "well positioned to sustain outsized revenue/EPS growth"
- BofA's Vivek Arya said Nvidia "raised the bar further in the AI arms race"
