# Nvidia Just Reshuffled Its $99 Billion Portfolio, and 1 Stock Moved Up the Ranks

Nvidia's quarterly 13F filing (for fiscal Q2 ending July 26) revealed a ~$99 billion investment portfolio. The notable change: SpaceX became Nvidia's second-largest holding, valued at roughly $21 billion — a newcomer that immediately claimed that rank.

Intel remained Nvidia's largest holding at ~$30 billion, while CoreWeave came in at ~$4.7 billion.

Nvidia's SpaceX position originated from an initial $10 billion investment in xAI, which merged with SpaceX in February. When SpaceX went public in June, Nvidia's private shares converted to publicly traded ones.

The relationship is strategic as well as financial: Elon Musk committed to using Nvidia GPUs as the exclusive primary hardware for SpaceX's data centers — notably excluding AMD from competition. With SpaceX centering data centers in its growth strategy and Nvidia expanding into CPUs for AI inference, analysts see the partnership deepening further.
