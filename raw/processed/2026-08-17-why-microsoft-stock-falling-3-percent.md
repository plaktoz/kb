# Why is Microsoft stock falling 3% today?

Microsoft (MSFT) shares fell about 3.2% on Monday after a Guardian investigation raised questions about whether the company has enough advanced AI chips installed to support its rapid infrastructure expansion. The Guardian reported that Microsoft had 2.2 million AI chips installed in its global data centers, based on internal documents reviewed by the publication — compared with an earlier target of 1.8 million chips by the end of 2024, and lower than some estimates based on Microsoft's stated data-center capacity.

Microsoft disputed the calculations, saying they drew "the wrong conclusions from incorrect assumptions" and that it does not disclose the volume of specific chips used in its AI infrastructure. The company says it added 5 gigawatts of data-center capacity over the past two years and now operates hundreds of data centers across five continents, though estimating chip counts is difficult because Microsoft reports capacity largely in terms of power rather than individual chips. Shaolei Ren, a UC Riverside professor, said Microsoft's reported capacity figures give "insufficient context."

The Guardian report suggested the issue may not be a chip shortage at all. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella previously said the company could have chips sitting in inventory that can't be used due to a lack of completed data-center facilities and available electricity: "It's not a supply issue of chips," Nadella said. The report also highlighted that parts of Microsoft's Fairwater data-center projects in Wisconsin and Georgia appeared unfinished despite earlier comments that the Wisconsin facility was going live.

The concerns come shortly after JPMorgan raised its Microsoft price target to $625 (from $550) for December 2027, maintaining an Overweight rating. Analyst Samik Chatterjee cited accelerating growth in Azure and Microsoft 365 Commercial Cloud tied to AI infrastructure investment, and estimated stronger Copilot adoption could generate $24-41 billion in additional revenue before potential AI-credit monetization.
