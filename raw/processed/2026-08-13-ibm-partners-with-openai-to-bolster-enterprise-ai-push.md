# IBM partners with OpenAI to bolster enterprise AI push

IBM announced a partnership with OpenAI to deliver AI models and tools to enterprise customers through IBM's global consulting arm. The deal follows a similar Anthropic alliance struck less than a year prior.

IBM will create a dedicated OpenAI practice within IBM Consulting, with tens of thousands of consultants trained and certified on OpenAI technologies. Training covers Codex, API, cybersecurity, and consultative credentials. A specialized "Forward Deployed Experts" group will be established via OpenAI's Partner Network.

OpenAI models — including GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work — will be integrated into IBM Consulting Advantage. Target sectors include financial services, government, telecom, and retail.

The partnership extends a prior cybersecurity-focused collaboration from June 2026. It reflects OpenAI's broader strategy of working with large systems integrators — including Infosys and Tata Consultancy Services — to capture corporate customers. For IBM, it reinforces a model-agnostic approach combining its own Granite models with third-party offerings via the watsonx platform, amid pressure following a lowered 2026 revenue forecast.
