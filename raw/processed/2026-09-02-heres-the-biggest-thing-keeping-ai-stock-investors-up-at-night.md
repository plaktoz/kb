# Here's the biggest thing keeping AI stock investors up at night

**Author:** Brian Sozzi, Executive Editor
**Date:** Wednesday, September 2, 2026

The core investor anxiety around AI stocks centers on fears that demand may be peaking. Deutsche Bank analyst Melissa Weathers noted a disconnect: tech executives (from Intel, Sandisk, and others) expressed confidence in "steady and strong trends," while investors had grown increasingly bearish, citing "peak cycle" fears and concerns about DRAM pricing.

However, the article argues this worry is misplaced. Key evidence:

- **Dell** reported Q2 sales up 58% YoY to a record $47B, with EPS up 273%. FY2027 guidance came in ~$25B above analyst estimates, driven by AI server demand from enterprises and cloud hyperscalers.
- **Nvidia** projected 70% revenue growth for FY2028, above analyst forecasts of 45%, though memory shortages remain a constraint.
- **PwC** projected global data center spending reaching "$31.6 trillion through 2050," potentially hitting $50T if the AI buildout surpasses historical infrastructure cycles.

Sozzi's conclusion: the data suggests AI demand is nowhere near its peak.
