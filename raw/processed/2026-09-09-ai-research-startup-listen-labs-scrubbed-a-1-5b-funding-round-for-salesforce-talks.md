# AI research startup Listen Labs scrubbed a $1.5B funding round for Salesforce talks

Listen Labs, a voice AI market research startup, signed a $125M Series C term sheet at a $1.5B valuation with Menlo Ventures set to lead — then walked away from it. The likely cause: acquisition discussions with Salesforce reportedly valued at approximately $2 billion.

The three-year-old company generates roughly $30M in annualized revenue and counts Microsoft, Canva, Anthropic, and Sweetgreen among its customers. Its AI conducts audio/video customer interviews and converts them into reports and presentations.

A competing startup, Simile, recently closed a $200M Series B at a $2B valuation — which sources say influenced Listen Labs' own valuation thinking. If the Salesforce deal falls through, VCs expect Listen Labs to seek a $2B+ valuation in a renewed fundraise.

One source noted Salesforce may balk at a "67 times revenue multiple." The deal remains unfinalized.

Listen Labs was co-founded in 2023 by Florian Jüngermann and Alfred Wahlforss, who met at Harvard. Its most recent prior round was a $69M Series B led by Ribbit Capital at a $500M valuation.
