# New Highs Hit Early Despite SpaceX, AMD Weakness

The earnings-driven boom that lifted stocks to record highs Tuesday continued Wednesday, sidestepping disappointment from SpaceX and AMD, both of which fell sharply despite beating analyst estimates. Crude oil ticked up after fresh attacks on Middle East shipping by Iran's allies, even as President Trump said a short-term deal to open the Strait of Hormuz may be near.

Walt Disney and Eli Lilly both impressed in early trading. Eli Lilly climbed nearly 5% after EPS beat consensus by $2.37 and revenue rose 47.7% year-over-year to $22.97 billion, with Mounjaro sales up 91% and Zepbound U.S. revenue up 44%; Lilly raised full-year guidance above consensus. Disney added almost 3% after EPS topped consensus and it reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance. Private-sector ADP jobs growth for July missed expectations at 44,000 versus 75,000 expected, ahead of Friday's nonfarm payrolls report.

Tuesday was among the year's strongest days, with about 71% of S&P 500 stocks trading above their 200-day moving average — up from 55% in June — suggesting broader market participation beyond chip stocks.

## Other movers

AMD fell more than 8% and SpaceX lost about 10% in early trading despite beating consensus, as investors focused on "whisper numbers" and, in SpaceX's case, heavy AI capital spending forecasts. Nvidia climbed 1.5% after Elon Musk said SpaceX will exclusively use Nvidia chips for AI services. Arista Networks climbed 9% on an earnings and revenue beat. Shopify surged 27% on an earnings beat and strong forward revenue guidance. Uber dropped more than 3% despite in-line results.

## Week ahead

August 6: preliminary Q2 productivity data and earnings from ConocoPhillips, Cloudflare, and Airbnb. August 7: July nonfarm payrolls. August 12: July CPI, with earnings from Nebius, Cisco, and Cerebras.
