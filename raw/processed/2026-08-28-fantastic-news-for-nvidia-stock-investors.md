# Fantastic News for Nvidia Stock Investors!

Nvidia reported Q2 fiscal year 2027 results (quarter ending July 26), delivering exceptional performance that impressed even skeptical investors.

## Key Financial Highlights

- Revenue: $96.2B — up 106% year-over-year
- Gross margin: 75% (vs. 72.4% prior year)
- Operating income: $63.7B — up 124% YoY
- Adjusted EPS: $2.22 — up 120% YoY
- Next quarter revenue guidance: ~$108B midpoint (~89.5% YoY growth)

## Demand Outlook

Hyperscale revenue reached $48.7B, up 102% YoY. Despite fears that major cloud providers would shift to custom AI chips, both Amazon's Andy Jassy and Alphabet's Sundar Pichai reaffirmed continued Nvidia purchases. AWS announced deployment of an additional 2 million Nvidia GPUs.

## Growth Drivers

- Hyperscale capex projected near $800B this year, potentially ~$1.3T next year
- CPU segment targeting $20B in standalone revenue this fiscal year, doubling next year
- Commitment to return at least 50% of free cash flow to shareholders

## Valuation

The stock trades at approximately 23.9x forward earnings, modestly above the sector average of 20.9x — considered reasonable given growth rates. The article concludes Nvidia remains a strong buy.
