# AMD Expands Global AI Footprint With New Saudi Platform

**Author:** Anusuya Lahiri
**Date:** August 31, 2026
**Source:** https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amd-expands-global-ai-footprint-223104559.html

---

## Summary

AMD announced expansion of its AI infrastructure in Saudi Arabia, with shares little changed in Monday premarket trading.

## Key Developments

**Platform Launch:** AMD, Cisco Systems, and HUMAIN launched an AI platform in Saudi Arabia using AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs and EPYC CPUs. Cisco provides networking infrastructure including Silicon One technology and 800G optics. The platform enables HUMAIN to offer GPU-as-a-service for training and inference workloads.

**Expansion Plans:** Partners intend to deploy "up to 250 MW of additional AI infrastructure" beginning in 2027, powered by AMD Instinct MI400 Series GPUs. The broader joint venture targets up to 1 GW of AI infrastructure by 2030.

**Leadership Comments:** Lisa Su called the launch "an important milestone," while HUMAIN CEO Tareq Amin cited growing demand for sovereign, high-performance AI infrastructure. Cisco's Chuck Robbins noted the region is shifting "from AI investment toward deployment."
