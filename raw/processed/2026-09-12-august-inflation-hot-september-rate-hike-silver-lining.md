# August Inflation Came in Hot, and the Odds of a September Rate Hike Are Now 85%

author: Bram Berkowitz
date: 2026-09-11

---

## Key Points

- Core inflation came in slightly above expectations in August, pushing rate hike odds higher
- Rate hikes are typically negative for equities
- Bond market dynamics may explain the paradoxical stock rally on Sept. 11

---

## Summary

August CPI data released Sept. 11 showed headline inflation rising 0.4% month-over-month, landing at 3.4% year-over-year — in line with estimates. However, core CPI beat forecasts by 0.1%, coming in at 0.3% for the month.

Following the report, Fed rate hike probabilities jumped from roughly 72% to nearly 87%, per CME Group's FedWatch tool. Despite this, the Dow surged over 500 points.

The author notes the FOMC had grown more hawkish, with three voting members already dissenting in favor of a hike at the prior meeting. The 10-year Treasury yield sat near 4.95%, with the 30-year at approximately 5.34%.

The silver lining: bond investors may view a rate hike as the Fed finally "doing its job," potentially easing long-term yield pressures and supporting equities.
