# AI hallucination nearly triggers US military operation

US military aircraft were airborne before officials discovered that intelligence behind an armed operation targeting a Chinese vessel had been fabricated by an AI chatbot. The mission was called off at the last moment, narrowly avoiding a potential conflict with China, according to CNN.

A Special Operations Command analyst had used an AI chatbot to combine open-source data with classified signals intelligence. The tool incorrectly identified the ship's cargo as components for a nuclear weapons program. The analyst then used the same chatbot to format the false findings into an official-looking report, which spread through command channels during the war with Iran.

The incident highlights concerns that AI errors can travel up the chain of command before anyone questions them — a risk amplified by the military's push to integrate AI for faster decision-making.

Jake Steckler, a GovAI research scholar and Army veteran, stressed that personnel must grasp "the uncertainty inherent to LLMs," especially in targeting and operational planning where decisions carry life-or-death weight. He argued the episode calls for better safeguards rather than abandoning AI, warning that rushing adoption without them risks eroding service members' trust in these tools.
