# History Says This Chip ETF Keeps Beating the S&P 500. It Has Trailed the Index Only Twice Since 2016.

The VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) has outperformed the S&P 500 in 8 of the 10 calendar years from 2016–2025, and is on pace for a ninth win in 2026, up ~59% versus the index's ~12%.

## Track Record

- Outperformed the S&P 500 in 8 of 10 calendar years from 2016–2025
- Beat the index by at least ~13 percentage points in every winning year
- A $1,000 investment at end-2015 grew to ~$15,000 by end-2025, versus ~$4,000 in an S&P 500 index fund

## Downside Risk

In its two losing years (2018 and 2022), the fund fell roughly twice as deep as the market. In 2022, it lost about a third of its value. Notably, "the fund's two best years of the decade, 2019 and 2023, came directly after its two losing years."

## Concentration Risk

Nvidia alone represents ~23% of assets, with the top five holdings comprising ~half the fund's ~$72B in assets.

## Recommendation

Hold SMH *alongside* a broad index fund rather than as a replacement, sized so a ~33% drawdown wouldn't trigger a panic sell.
