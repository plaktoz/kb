# Semaglutide (Wegovy/Ozempic) Sustains Lower Calorie Intake Over One Year

source_url: https://www.healthline.com/health-news/glp-1s-like-ozempic-and-wegovy-may-help-you-eat-less-for-at-least-a-year-heres-how

---

Author: Mandy French
Published: July 25, 2026

A 60-week clinical study on semaglutide (the active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy) found that participants maintained a 24–30% reduction in caloric intake over the full study period — longer than many previous studies had tracked.

Key findings:
- Participants consistently consumed fewer calories without conscious dietary restriction
- The effect persisted across the entire 60-week observation window
- Researchers attribute the sustained effect to GLP-1 receptor agonism reducing appetite signaling in the brain

The mechanism differs from traditional diets: rather than willpower-based calorie counting, semaglutide modulates hunger hormones, making satiety feel natural rather than forced.

Caveats noted by researchers:
- The study did not directly measure long-term weight maintenance after discontinuation
- Side effects including nausea, vomiting, and gastrointestinal discomfort remain common
- Long-term cardiovascular and metabolic outcomes are still under active study

GLP-1 drugs have become one of the most commercially significant drug classes globally, with Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly dominating the market.
