# Sundar Pichai Says Alphabet Can't Build AI Capacity Fast Enough, and Anthropic Has Secured 5 Gigawatts of It

Despite committing up to $205 billion in capital spending in 2026, Alphabet remains unable to build AI computing capacity fast enough to meet demand. CEO Sundar Pichai noted the company is "supply constrained -- a sign of momentum and rapid adoption." Yet Alphabet has agreed to sell multi-gigawatt blocks of that scarce capacity to Anthropic, maker of the Claude AI models.

## Google Cloud Performance

- Q2 revenue: **$24.8 billion** (up 82% year-over-year, versus 63% growth in Q1)
- Q2 operating income: **$8.8 billion** (more than tripled YoY from $2.8B)
- Operating margin: ~**36%** (vs. ~21% a year prior)
- Cloud backlog: **$514 billion** (up from $462B at end of Q1)

## Anthropic Agreements

- **October deal:** Access to up to 1 million Google TPUs, with 1+ gigawatt of capacity coming online in 2026; valued at "tens of billions of dollars"
- **April 2026 expansion:** ~**5 gigawatts** of next-gen TPU capacity via Google and Broadcom, starting 2027
- **Investment:** Google agreed to invest up to **$40 billion** in Anthropic ($10B immediately, up to $30B more tied to milestones)
- Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate surpassed **$30 billion** in April 2026, up from ~$9B at end of 2025

## Capital Expenditures & Financing

- 2026 capex guidance: **$195–$205 billion**
- Q2 capex: **$44.9 billion** (exceeding $39.1B in operating cash flow)
- Raised **$49.6 billion** from stock sales in June; issued **$20.3 billion** in senior notes

## Stock Valuation

- GOOGL trading near **$339**, at roughly 23x forward earnings
- Total revenue growing approximately **24%** year-over-year

## Notable Risk

Per a Broadcom securities filing, Anthropic's use of the expanded capacity "is dependent on Anthropic's continued commercial success" — meaning a significant portion of Alphabet's contracted future revenue relies on a single young AI company growing into its commitments.
