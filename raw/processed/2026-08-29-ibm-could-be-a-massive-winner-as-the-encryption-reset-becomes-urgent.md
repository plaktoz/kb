# IBM Could Be a Massive Winner as the Encryption Reset Becomes Urgent

IBM may have a significant multi-year opportunity tied to the transition toward post-quantum cryptography security. The core thesis is that IBM could benefit if it successfully converts its technical credibility and large enterprise customer base into recurring revenue across software, consulting, and infrastructure.

IBM "may have a multiyear opportunity hiding inside the shift to post-quantum security," with the payoff hinging on whether IBM can monetize its position "before the revenue becomes obvious."

IBM stock price referenced: $235.59 (NYSE: IBM) as of August 17, 2026.

**Disclosure:** The Motley Fool holds positions in IBM. Rick Orford held no position in mentioned stocks at time of publication.
