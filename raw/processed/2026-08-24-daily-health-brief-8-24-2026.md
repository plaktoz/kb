# The Daily Health Brief - 8.24.2026

Two small trials released this week make an interesting pair: a fortified orange juice study didn't hold up under continuous monitoring, raising questions about how well short-term supplementation trials generalize to sustained, real-world use. The brief frames this as a reminder to weigh single small studies cautiously against longer-run evidence before changing daily habits.
