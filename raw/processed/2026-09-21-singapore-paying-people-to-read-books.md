# Singapore Is Paying People to Put Down Their Phones and Read Books

**Author:** Rex Edison
**Date:** September 17, 2026
**Source:** Gadget Review

---

## Summary

Singapore's National Library Board launched **ReadSG** on September 6, 2026 — a five-year pilot converting logged reading time into small cash rewards.

---

## How It Works

- Log **15 minutes** of reading daily on GovTech's CrowdTaskSG platform
- Earn **20 virtual coins** per session (one session per day counts)
- Conversion rate: 1,000 coins = S$1 (~S$0.02 per session)
- 50 consecutive days yields approximately **S$1**

The NLB describes the payout as "a behavioral nudge rather than an income stream."

---

## Communal Goal: Read for Good

A parallel track targets **7.5 million cumulative reading minutes** across all participants, unlocking up to **S$150,000** in charitable donations.

---

## Background

ReadSG builds on Singapore's 2016 National Reading Movement. The program remains in pilot phase, with refinements planned based on user feedback.

---

## Broader Significance

Governments worldwide are watching whether **gamification + micropayments** can shift screen habits at population scale. The behavioral data collected may inform future civic design efforts globally.
