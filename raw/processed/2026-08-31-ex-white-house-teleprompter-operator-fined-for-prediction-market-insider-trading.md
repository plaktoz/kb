# Ex-White House Teleprompter Operator Fined for Prediction Market Insider Trading

Gabriel Perez, a former White House teleprompter operator, settled CFTC charges for $172,000 after trading on advance knowledge of presidential speeches. He placed bets on "presidential mention market" contracts — event contracts paying out based on specific words Trump used in speeches.

Between December 2025 and February 2026, Perez generated over $107,500 in profits using nonpublic speech content. The settlement requires disgorgement of those gains, a $65,000 civil penalty, a three-year trading ban, and compliance with the Commodity Exchange Act.

The CFTC noted the penalty was reduced under a new cooperation policy, crediting both Perez's assistance and Kalshi's help with the investigation.

The case highlights growing insider-trading risks on prediction platforms. Similar incidents include a U.S. soldier charged over alleged Polymarket trading tied to the Maduro ouster, and a MrBeast video editor fired amid a Kalshi insider-trading probe. Kalshi has since introduced new safeguards and worked through a backlog of suspicious-activity reviews.
