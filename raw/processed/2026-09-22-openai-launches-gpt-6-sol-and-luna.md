# OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna, boasting lower cost and fewer mistakes

OpenAI expanded its GPT-6 lineup by releasing updated Sol and Luna models, following the earlier launch of GPT-6 Astra. The company describes the new releases as making Astra's intelligence "more efficient and accessible."

Sol targets complex tasks like coding; Luna handles high-volume clerical work such as summarization and quick Q&A. Both models are priced at half the cost of their 5.6 series predecessors, thanks to caching and inference improvements.

On accuracy, OpenAI states that GPT-6 Sol makes "about half as many mistakes as its predecessor, reaching Astra-level reliability at much lower cost."

OpenAI also claims the models outperform Anthropic's top offerings — notably Fable and Opus — though Anthropic released an updated Opus 5.5 just 90 minutes prior, underscoring fierce rivalry between the two labs.

GPT-6 Sol and Luna are rolling out to ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API for most paid users, with Luna also accessible to Free and Go users via the desktop app.
