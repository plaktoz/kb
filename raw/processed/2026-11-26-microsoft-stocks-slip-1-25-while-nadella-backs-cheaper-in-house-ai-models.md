# Microsoft Stocks Slip 1.25% While Nadella Backs Cheaper In-House AI Models

Microsoft (NASDAQ:MSFT) shares slipped 1.25% to $489.93 at the 4:00 p.m. ET close on November 25, while comments from Satya Nadella on Microsoft's AI model strategy drew attention. Speaking at the New York Times DealBook Summit, Nadella said Microsoft's in-house MAI models trailing OpenAI and Anthropic by three to six months is a great position if they use 5% to 20% of the compute. Shares had opened down 3.53% before recovering most of the loss.

Copilot already chooses among OpenAI, Anthropic and MAI models for each task, and Nadella said most users simply select the automatic option. He later wrote on X that "Outcome per dollar is the frontier," and argued AI should become a commodity like cloud computing. Microsoft announced seven MAI models at its Ignite conference on November 17. Its OpenAI agreement gives it royalty-free access to a frontier model until 2032, Windows Latest noted in its report.

The strategy speaks directly to the market's fear of runaway AI capex. If Copilot can run largely on cheaper in-house models, gross margins on AI products could hold up even as token prices fall. The GuruFocus chart shows a perfect GF Score of 100/100, with elite profitability, a strong balance sheet and top-ranked momentum. GF Value of $606.01 implies a 19.2% discount and a "modestly undervalued" label, an unusual opening for a company of this quality after the AI selloff. The next test is fiscal second-quarter results in January, where Copilot margins and Azure growth will show whether the cost-first approach is working.
