# Meta Earnings On Deck. How High Will Zuckerberg's AI Spending Go?

source_url: https://www.investors.com/news/technology/meta-stock-q2-2026-earnings-preview-big-tech

---

Meta Platforms (META) is set to publish its second-quarter results late Wednesday, July 29. For Meta stock, all eyes will be on the tech giant's capital expenditures and CEO Mark Zuckerberg's strategy for that AI spending.

"In the wake of Google's results, the market will be expecting Meta to raise its 2026 capital expenditures outlook from the prior $125 billion-$145 billion, and is also expecting Meta to preview a 'significant' increase in capex spend for 2027," Evercore ISI analyst Mark Mahaney said in a client note.

Meta stock slid late last week in response to earnings from Google parent Alphabet (GOOGL). Google hiked its 2026 capex guidance to a range of $195 billion to $205 billion, up from $180 billion to $190 billion. The results raised expectations that fellow cloud hyperscalers Meta, Microsoft (MSFT) and Amazon (AMZN) would raise their AI spending forecasts.

Mahaney, however, told clients he expects Meta to maintain its capex and total expenses guide, since Meta already raised 2026 capex guidance with its Q1 results in late April. He reiterated an outperform rating for Meta stock and said investors will be listening for updates on Meta's plan to sell excess cloud capacity to other companies. Zuckerberg earlier this month told Bloomberg the company is at least considering the option. "Confirmation of this would be perceived positively by the market, the key question is whether this marks a tactical or a strategic shift by the company," Mahaney wrote.

## Meta Q2 Earnings Preview: By The Numbers

Overall, analysts see Meta's earnings rising less than 1% to $7.19 per share, while revenue jumps 26.7% to $60.22 billion, according to FactSet.

Wedbush Securities analyst Ygal Arounian said he expects another strong quarter for Meta's advertising business, but excitement about that could be offset by concerns about AI spending. The Meta AI assistant is showing "real usage growth," while the company's AI business offerings have positive early indications, Arounian added. "However, outside of the ad stack, initiatives are still early: Meta AI/Muse Spark, Business AI, subscription tiers, a potential Meta cloud offering, and custom silicon are all early-stage or potential initiatives. The gap between capex intensity and diversified monetization remains the central debate for the stock." Arounian initiated coverage of Meta stock earlier this month with a neutral rating.

Another number to watch is the daily active user count for Meta's Family of Apps, which includes Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger and Threads. In its March quarter, Meta's "daily active people" metric declined quarter on quarter for the first time, from 3.58 billion to 3.56 billion. The company blamed the drop on outages caused by the wars in Iran and Ukraine.

## Meta Stock: Rebound Resistance

Meta stock traded sideways ahead of earnings, last up a fraction at 594.03. On Tuesday the company announced a $14 billion joint venture with BlackRock to build a data center in Texas.

Meta stock has lagged this year, down 10% year to date, but seemed to be rounding into form earlier this month on positive reviews for its new Muse Spark AI model and reported plans to sell cloud capacity. Shares have since been punished in recent trading days as investors seem anxious once more about AI spending — Meta stock has lost 13% since July 15.
