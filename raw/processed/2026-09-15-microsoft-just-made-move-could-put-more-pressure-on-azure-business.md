# Microsoft Just Made a Move That Could Put More Pressure on Its Azure Business. Here's Why It Could Turn Out Great for the Stock

Microsoft announced a significant reporting restructure — moving from three segments to two. The new segments are **"Agents and Infra"** (AI-focused, projected >$75B in Q1 FY2027) and **"Devices and Consumer"** (~$15B).

The key change: Microsoft will now report **actual Azure revenue figures** rather than just growth rates. The author interprets this transparency as a confidence signal — companies typically don't highlight numbers they're ashamed of.

Azure's performance has historically driven MSFT's stock movement. When Q4 results (reported July) beat expectations, the stock rose sharply.

On valuation, the author notes MSFT trades at roughly 28x trailing earnings, comparing favorably to Apple at ~38x — characterizing it as "not all that expensive" among large-cap tech peers.

The overall thesis: Microsoft's AI investment strategy appears to be gaining momentum, the reporting change signals management confidence, and the stock remains an attractive long-term hold despite recent gains.

*Disclosure: The Motley Fool holds positions in Apple and Microsoft.*
