# OpenAI buys smartphone camera maker Glass Imaging for $300 million, report says

OpenAI acquired Glass Imaging, a smartphone camera startup, for over $300 million, per a Wall Street Journal report. The Los Altos-based company was founded in 2019 by former Apple engineers Ziv Attar and Tom Bishop — the duo who originally built Apple's Portrait Mode feature.

Glass Imaging's approach uses neural networks trained on specific camera systems to improve image quality at the moment of capture, rather than applying AI edits after the fact. The startup had raised roughly $30 million before the acquisition.

The deal fits into OpenAI's broader hardware ambitions. The company has been rumored to be developing smartphones, earbuds, and screenless AI companion devices. In 2025, OpenAI also acquired Jony Ive's device startup "io" for $6.5 billion, with Ive taking on a design leadership role.
