# Nvidia stock falls after epic rally on Jensen Huang's return as CEO

Investors are digesting a historic day for the markets — and for AI chip champion Nvidia (NVDA). Shares of Nvidia fell over 7% in afternoon trading on Tuesday after a 31.2% surge on news that founder Jensen Huang had been reinstated as CEO. That came just 12 days after he resigned amid a personal scandal and federal chip-export probes.

The dramatic reversal followed intense lobbying from President Trump and Vice President JD Vance, who intervened directly with directors and said Huang had been cleared of national security concerns.

Upon returning to Nvidia's Silicon Valley headquarters, Huang promised employees he would be a better CEO the second time around.

Despite the rally, Nvidia still faces ongoing Justice Department and SEC investigations into alleged chip smuggling through blacklisted Singapore-based firm Megaspeed, prompting the board to appoint a new chief compliance officer.

Wall Street analysts were universally positive on the stunning turn of events, and think the rally may just be starting.

"I am still a buyer of Nvidia today," Antoine Chkaiban, New Street Research analyst, said on Sozzi Unleashed. "I think there's still plenty of upside with the stock. It's still very cheap. You know we have Nvidia generating 25 bucks of earnings in 2028. So the stock is still trading below ten times that [on a PE ratio]. And I think there's plenty of upside."
