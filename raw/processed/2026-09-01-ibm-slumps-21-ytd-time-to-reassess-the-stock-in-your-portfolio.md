# IBM Slumps 21% YTD: Time to Reassess the Stock in Your Portfolio?

IBM has fallen 21% year-to-date while its industry grew over 100%. Microsoft gained ~5% and Amazon rose ~12.6% over the same period.

## AI Disruption Risk

Anthropic's Claude Code poses a threat by automating COBOL-related tasks like "code analysis, documentation, refactoring and security assessment," potentially reducing enterprise reliance on IBM's legacy modernization services. This could erode IBM's consulting revenues and compress pricing.

## Competitive Pressures

AWS and Microsoft Azure are squeezing IBM's margins. Frequent acquisitions have inflated goodwill, intangibles, and debt levels.

## Estimate Revisions

- 2026 EPS estimates dipped 0.3% to $12.33
- 2027 estimates fell 1.6% to $13.22

These revisions reflect bearish sentiment among analysts.

## Potential Upside

IBM's hybrid cloud portfolio (Red Hat OpenShift, HashiCorp acquisition) and the watsonx AI platform offer growth pathways as enterprises adopt multi-cloud architectures.

## Conclusion

The stock carries a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold). New investors are advised to proceed cautiously given competitive headwinds and declining earnings estimates.
