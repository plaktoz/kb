# Singapore Stocks Fall on Thursday; STI Down 0.4%

Singapore stocks ended lower on Thursday (Aug 20). The benchmark Straits Times Index (STI) lost 0.4 per cent or 22.33 points to finish at 5,671.91.

CapitaLand Investment led the gainers on Singapore's blue-chip index, rising 1.5 per cent to S$2.67. The worst performer among STI constituents was Sats, which fell 13.6 per cent to S$4.12. The trio of local banks all ended lower: DBS lost 0.2 per cent to S$75.84, OCBC fell 0.5 per cent to S$30.78, and UOB was down 0.8 per cent at S$40.22.

Across the broader market, gainers trailed losers 271 to 284, after 1.4 billion securities worth S$2 billion changed hands. CapAlliance was the most actively traded stock by volume, while DBS was most actively traded by value.

Key regional indices were mixed to higher: Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index gained 0.8 per cent, the FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI advanced 0.3 per cent, Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 1.4 per cent and South Korea's Kospi surged 5.9 per cent. OCBC Group Research analysts described the past 12 hours of trading as "choppy," attributing it to the release of minutes from the Fed's July meeting, tariff talks, and a US Treasury announcement on liquidity support.
