# Anthropic Is Offering More Than $50,000 in Claude Perks for Entrepreneurs. Here's How to Get Them

Founders building with AI can take advantage of an expanded program from Anthropic called Claude Startups.

Using AI to launch your company? Anthropic just made it easier to begin with Claude.

On Tuesday, Anthropic expanded a program called Claude Startups, which is designed to help founders jumpstart their businesses. Launched in May, it previously offered eligible startups Claude credits and access to a founder community. The expansion adds a free year of Claude Team, a plan for small-and-medium-sized businesses, and up to $45,000 in discounts and credits for other AI tools, bringing the total package to more than $50,000. The program already includes thousands of startups and is now accepting new members at no cost.

For founders leveraging AI to help them build, token costs, access to agentic AI tools, and the lack of technical expertise can be barriers to scaling, Beth Robertson, Anthropic's head of Startups, tells Inc. By offering more credits to cover model usage, discounts on tools, and technical support, the program aims to help founders get their businesses off the ground with fewer upfront costs.

"There has never been a better time to start a company," Robertson says. "An idea that used to need a team and a funding round to prove can now be built by a founder with a laptop and conviction."

Claude Startups now offers up to $7,500 in Claude products and credits. That includes a free year of Claude Team with five Premium seats offering higher usage limits than standard seats, plus $1,000 in API credits to integrate Claude into your business operations and test new ideas.

The program also introduced the Claude Startup Stack: a set of AI tools founders can use across different parts of their businesses, with exclusive discounts and credits worth up to $45,000. The current stack includes up to 18 tools, including Attention, ElevenLabs, Granola, and Lovable, all of which can help founders track revenue, build customer support agents, take notes during meetings, and create software.

The expansion comes as AI plays a bigger role in helping entrepreneurs build their businesses. A 2026 survey of roughly 2,000 startup builders by software company [Supabase](https://supabase.com/state-of-startups) found that 63 percent used Claude Code and 10 percent used ChatGPT or Codex to code their products. Startups including HumanLayer, Ambral, and Vulcan Technologies have used Claude Code to build their products, according to [Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-companies-with-claude-code).
