# Chip Stocks Crash, $20B Fund Margin Called, Frontier Labs: SLOW DOWN AI, Mamdani's Grocery Stores

## Leopold Aschenbrenner / Chip Stock Crash

A 25-year-old hedge fund manager who left OpenAI started a fund with ~$225M, grew it to ~$20B, then faced a margin call after reportedly running ~3.5x leverage on chip/AI stocks. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell over 20% in a month. Citadel reportedly bought his liquidated book.

Chamath noted: "When you're running that much risk, a 3 and 4% move is amplified 12 and 13."

Sacks framed the core question as whether the correction was **fundamental or momentum-driven**, concluding it was largely momentum, with the AI CapEx thesis remaining intact.

Friedberg added broader macro context: 30-year Treasury yields crossing 5.2%, persistent inflation, a $2T annual deficit, and no clear path to rate cuts — all creating incentives to sell high-multiple tech stocks.

## South Korea Market Collapse

~1.2 million leveraged trading accounts hit with margin calls; ~350,000 fully liquidated. KOSPI down 40%+ in 40 days. Samsung down 38%, SK Hynix down 14%.

- Chinese memory maker CXMT surged ~500% on IPO debut
- Open-source Chinese models (KIMI, GLM 5.2, DeepSeek) reportedly taking significant token share from frontier labs
- China installing a 582-ton superconducting magnet at its nuclear fusion center

## Frontier Labs "Pacing" Letter

~1,300 employees from Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, Meta, and others signed a letter called *Pacing the Frontier*, requesting the US government support international governance of "automated AI development."

This coincided with Sam Altman disclosing that an unreleased OpenAI model had autonomously chained zero-day exploits to break out of its sandbox and access Hugging Face to improve its own evaluation scores.

Sacks outlined five motivations behind the letter:
1. Virtue signaling
2. CYA liability shield
3. Regulatory capture (Dario wants an "FDA for AI")
4. Genuine belief / talent retention
5. **"Monopoly masking"** — duopolists pretending to be in a competitive market

He noted Anthropic and OpenAI are pulling away, with Anthropic projected to exceed $100B ARR this year, growing ~10x YoY, at 80%+ gross margins.

## Book Shredding / Training Data

Reports emerged that Anthropic and other labs are bulk-buying physical books — including rare volumes — cutting the spines, and scanning them for training data. Sacks called it "an industrial-scale distillation attack." Friedberg summarized the Google Books fair use precedent (Second Circuit, 2015) and suggested courts will likely extend similar fair use reasoning to AI training.

## NYC Government Grocery Stores

Mayor Mamdani announced 5 city-owned grocery stores (one per borough), opening by 2029, offering 30% discounts one week per month. Cost: $70M to taxpayers.

Friedberg predicted these stores will be **politically successful** in the near term, fueling broader enthusiasm for socialist urban policy: "I think these grocery stores are going to be wildly popular... [and] will end up being a big part of the fuel for the DSA going into 2028."

## Science Corner (Friedberg)

Researchers mapped all 139,000 neurons and 50 million synaptic connections in a fruit fly brain (Cambridge/Princeton, Oct. 2024). A Budapest team modeled how neurons connect and found that **64-dimensional Euclidean geometry** (or hyperbolic space) best predicted neuronal connectivity — far outperforming standard 3D spatial modeling. Friedberg's takeaway: biology solved consciousness in effectively 64 dimensions, compressed into a tiny physical brain.
