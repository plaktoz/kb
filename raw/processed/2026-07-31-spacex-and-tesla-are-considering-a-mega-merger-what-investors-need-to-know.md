# SpaceX and Tesla Are Considering a Mega Merger -- Here's What Investors Need to Know

---

By Ryan Vanzo | Published July 31, 2026

Merger speculation between SpaceX and Tesla has intensified following new Wall Street Journal reporting. According to the WSJ, Musk structured Tesla's China operations with a deliberate separation — a "laser" — between its U.S. and China businesses, partly to facilitate a potential SpaceX merger by reducing regulatory complexity.

Key factors driving merger interest:
- Both companies are heavily invested in AI and share stakes in xAI
- They've partnered on chip-building venture Terafab
- Tesla appears over 80 times in SpaceX's IPO prospectus
- A merger could give Musk greater voting control, as he holds ~20% of Tesla's voting power vs. ~80% at SpaceX

Tesla may spin off or sell its China unit — which represents ~20% of vehicle sales and ~50% of production — to reduce regulatory risk and adjust relative valuations ahead of any merger.

Musk commented cautiously: "Obviously, we can't talk about...combining companies...on earnings calls. It has got to be done with the appropriate process."

Growing belief in the market is that this process has begun, potentially creating one of the world's largest AI companies.
