# Why Intel, Arm, and Other AI Chip Stocks Soared Today

Meta's new AI agent "Muse" topped U.S. iPhone download charts for three consecutive days, sparking investor enthusiasm around CPU demand growth. Monday's top gainers included ARM (+17%), Intel (+12%), and AMD (+10%).

The excitement centers on agentic AI's computing needs. Unlike model training — which is GPU-heavy — AI agents rely more on inference, which draws heavily on CPUs for tasks like "planning and orchestration." A surge in agentic AI adoption could therefore disproportionately benefit CPU makers.

Market size projections are striking: ARM sees the server CPU market growing 35% annually to reach $120B by 2030, while AMD forecasts 50% annual growth reaching $220B by decade's end.

Muse differentiates itself through Meta's massive user base of 3.6 billion daily active users and its data infrastructure, making it more accessible than rivals. Analysts suggest it could attract hundreds of millions of users within months.

Intel, AMD, and Arm are identified as primary beneficiaries given their roles as leading CPU designers.
