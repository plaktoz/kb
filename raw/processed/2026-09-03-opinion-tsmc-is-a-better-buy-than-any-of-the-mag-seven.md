# Opinion: TSMC Is a Better Buy Than Any of the "Magnificent Seven" Stocks Right Now

The author argues TSMC (TSM) outshines every Magnificent Seven stock as an investment. TSMC is up ~36% year-to-date and 78% over the past 12 months, beating all seven peers.

Key reasons cited:

- **Universal customer base:** Every Magnificent Seven company — Apple, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Tesla — relies on TSMC for chip manufacturing or foundry services.
- **Financial strength:** Q2 2026 revenue hit $40.2B (+33.7% YoY), with gross margin of 67.7% and net profit margin of 55.6%.
- **Technology leadership:** TSMC began producing 2-nanometer chips in Q2 2026, offering higher density and energy efficiency.
- **Massive U.S. expansion:** An additional $100B investment in Arizona brings total Arizona commitments to $265B.
- **AI tailwinds:** CEO C.C. Wei noted that agentic AI is driving CPU demand in data centers, benefiting TSMC regardless of architecture — "whether it's x86, Arm-based, or RISC-V."

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has described TSMC as producing something that "you can't overstate the magic" of, underscoring its indispensable role in global chip production.
