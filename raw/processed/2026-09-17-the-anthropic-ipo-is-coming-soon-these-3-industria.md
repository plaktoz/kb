# The Anthropic IPO Is Coming Soon. These 3 Industrial Stocks Will Profit Whether You Can Get Shares or Not.

With Anthropic's potential IPO drawing attention to AI infrastructure, three industrial companies are positioned to benefit from data center power demand:

**GE Vernova (GEV)** supplies gas turbines and grid equipment. Data center orders surpassed $5 billion year-to-date in Q2 2026—more than double the full 2025 total—and management expects data centers to represent 25% of orders in 2026, up from 10% in 2024. Active partnerships include a turbine supply deal with Crusoe and a Chevron collaboration targeting 4 gigawatts by 2027.

**Quanta Services (PWR)** constructs transmission lines and in-facility electrical systems for data centers. Its acquisition of Cupertino Electric strengthened data center design capabilities. The company's backlog reached $53.4 billion in Q2 2026, driven by power infrastructure demand.

**Eaton (ETN)** manufactures power distribution and management systems for data centers. The company posted record Q2 results with sales up 21% year-over-year to $8.5 billion. Eaton has collaborated with Nvidia on electrical systems tailored to AI workloads.

The article notes none of these stocks is currently cheap, but suggests pullbacks could offer attractive entry points.
