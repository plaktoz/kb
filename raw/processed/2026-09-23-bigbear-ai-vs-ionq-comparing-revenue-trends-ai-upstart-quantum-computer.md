# BigBear.ai vs. IONQ: Comparing Revenue Trends Between an Artificial Intelligence Upstart and a Quantum Computer Company

BigBear.ai (BBAI) provides AI/ML decision-support tools primarily for U.S. government clients, recently gaining Dutch regulatory approval for airport security software and launching a generative AI platform for classified defense environments.

IonQ (IONQ) builds and sells access to quantum computers via direct and cloud channels. It finalized its SkyWater Technology acquisition, launched a sixth-generation quantum platform, and announced a partnership with Nvidia to build "large-scale quantum supercomputers capable of solving the world's hardest problems."

## Revenue Comparison (Quarterly)

| Quarter | BBAI | IONQ |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 2024 | $41.5M | $12.4M |
| Q4 2024 | $43.8M | $11.7M |
| Q1 2025 | $34.8M | $7.6M |
| Q2 2025 | $32.5M | $20.7M |
| Q3 2025 | $33.1M | $39.9M |
| Q4 2025 | $27.3M | $61.9M |
| Q1 2026 | $34.4M | $64.7M |
| Q2 2026 | $36.7M | $80.0M |

IONQ reversed from a lower baseline to more than double BigBear.ai's revenue by Q2 2026. BigBear.ai suffered declines tied to federal budget cuts but showed modest recovery, partly through its Ask Sage acquisition, delivering roughly 13% year-over-year growth in Q2 2026.
