# AI-Designed Drug Lowers Biological Age Markers in Early Trial

A phase 2a clinical trial published in *Nature Biotechnology* (September 7, 2026) examined rentosertib — an AI-designed TNIK inhibitor — for its effects on biological aging markers in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF).

## Key Findings

- Blood samples from 42 IPF patients were collected over 12 weeks and analyzed for 2,800+ proteins
- Results were applied to **six proteomic aging clocks** (ipfP3GPT, OrganAge, PAC, PAOPAC, ProtAge)
- Peak effect occurred at **week 4**, showing roughly a 3–4 year reversal in predicted biological age, and up to six years on one specific clock

## Expert Reactions

**Dr. Dung Trinh** (MemorialCare) cautioned that biomarker shifts may simply reflect disease improvement rather than true age reversal, noting the study was small and short-term.

**Dr. Zeeshan Khan** (Hackensack Meridian) emphasized the real goal: extending *healthspan* — years of healthy, functional living — not just lifespan.

**Dr. Peter Gliebus** (Baptist Health) noted AI helped identify TNIK as a target and design the molecule, compressing early development to roughly 18 months, but stressed AI won't replace clinical rigor.
