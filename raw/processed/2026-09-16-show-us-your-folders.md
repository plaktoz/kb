# Show Us Your Folders

Every launched "Show Us Your Folders" — a recurring series where team members walk through their AI workspace organization. The pilot featured Kieran Klaassen (GM of Cora), who built a two-part system:

- **Tuin** ("garden" in Dutch) — personal workspace storing goals, notes, tasks, and memory
- **Erf** ("yard") — a coordinator that routes tasks to the appropriate agent folder

Key principles from Kieran's setup:
- One folder per job, with all needed context inside it
- Separate storage (Tuin) from dispatch (Erf)
- Memory organized by time scale: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly
- Build systems around how you *already* work, not someone else's template

## Workflow Tip

Use Compound Engineering's `/ce-doc-review` to audit your workspace. The suggested prompt asks the agent to map what each folder owns and identify conflicts before touching anything — "Do not move, rename, or delete anything until I approve."
