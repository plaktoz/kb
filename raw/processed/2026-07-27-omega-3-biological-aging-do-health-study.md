# Omega-3 Supplement Study: Could It Slow Biological Aging? (DO-HEALTH Findings)

**Source:** https://hocemanifest.com/article/omega-3-supplement-study-could-it-slow-biological-aging-do-health-findings  
**Note:** Secondary source (AI content site). The underlying DO-HEALTH study data is from published peer-reviewed research.  
**Date:** 2026-07-27

---

The DO-HEALTH (D3, Omega-3, Home Exercise — Healthy Ageing and Longevity Trial) study investigated whether daily supplementation with omega-3 fatty acids (1g/day algae-based EPA/DHA), vitamin D3, and/or a structured home exercise program could slow biological aging in adults 70+.

**Key findings:**
- Participants taking 1g/day omega-3 for 3 years were biologically approximately 3 months younger based on epigenetic clock measurements
- The triple combination of omega-3 + vitamin D + exercise showed the strongest effect on cellular aging markers
- Epigenetic aging clocks (DNA methylation-based) were used as the primary biological age measurement tool

**Study design:**
- Multi-center RCT across 5 European countries
- ~2,000+ participants aged 70+
- 3-year intervention with 2x2x2 factorial design (omega-3 ±, vitamin D ±, exercise ±)
- Measured biological age via epigenetic clocks at baseline and 3 years

**Why epigenetic clocks matter:**  
Biological age (as measured by DNA methylation patterns) diverges from chronological age. Epigenetic clocks correlate with all-cause mortality, disease risk, and functional decline — making them better predictors of health outcomes than chronological age alone.

**Mechanistic reasoning:**
- Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) have known anti-inflammatory effects
- Chronic low-grade inflammation ("inflammaging") is a primary driver of accelerated biological aging
- Omega-3 may slow epigenetic aging by dampening systemic inflammation at the cellular level

**Practical takeaways:**
- Algae-based omega-3 (EPA/DHA) is preferred for sustainability and purity over fish oil
- Optimal dose from DO-HEALTH: 1g/day
- Effect sizes are modest (3 months over 3 years), but consistent with long-term compound benefits
- The combination protocol (omega-3 + vitamin D + exercise) outperforms any single intervention
