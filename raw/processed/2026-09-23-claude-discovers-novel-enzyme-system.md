# Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats

Anthropic announced a new life sciences research group and laboratory focused on fundamental biology research using Claude. In spring 2026, the team set Claude to work mining a massive DNA sequence database for novel reverse transcriptases (RTs).

After 21 hours of searching by roughly 950 parallel agents consuming ~210 million tokens, one agent flagged a striking find: a repeating DNA pattern adjacent to an unusual RT gene. The agent described the discovery as: *"spectacular: I can see by eye a tandem repeat array…that's a CRISPR-like…repeat array?!"*

The system — dubbed **array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART)** — consists of three components: an RT, a neighboring partner gene, and a long array of evenly spaced DNA repeats resembling a CRISPR array. Early experiments confirm the array is expressed as distinct short RNAs, hinting at programmable functionality.

MIT/Broad Institute pioneer Feng Zhang called it *"an exciting example of how AI agents can contribute to biological discovery"* and said the RNA-repeat array finding *"merits further investigation."*

Anthropic notes the function of ARTs remains under study, and has released a preprint with full technical details.
