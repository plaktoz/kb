# Retail Investors' 2 Favorite AI Stocks for the Long Term: Alphabet, IBM

The article examines which AI stocks retail investors on Reddit's r/stockstobuytoday community are buying for a five-year horizon, identifying IBM (NYSE: IBM) and Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOG). The piece focuses primarily on IBM.

## Why Retail Investors Like IBM

Reddit users favor IBM because large quantum startups face fundraising and regulatory hurdles, while IBM is already deeply embedded in enterprise accounts with an established reputation. They also cite attractive valuation and dividend yield.

## Valuation Highlights

- Forward non-GAAP P/E: 19.12 (roughly 16% below sector median)
- Forward GAAP P/E: 23.13 (about 21% below sector median)
- Forward EV/EBITDA: 13.85 (modestly below sector median)

## AI Growth Catalysts

- **Software** (~45% of revenue, ~80% recurring): The Data segment grew 18% in Q2, driven by watsonx.data and Confluent — tools that help enterprises locate and govern data across systems, which is foundational for AI agents.
- **Hybrid Cloud:** Red Hat grew ~11% in the quarter. Regulated industries like banking and government require multi-cloud/on-premise flexibility.
- **Quantum Computing:** IBM plans to invest over $10 billion across R&D, capex, and M&A over five years, including a letter of intent with the Department of Commerce to build "Anderon," described as the world's first pure-play quantum foundry.

## Bear Case

AI itself poses a threat to IBM's legacy mainframe business. Tools from Google, Anthropic, Oracle, and AWS now assist with COBOL modernization — previously a strong moat for IBM. IBM's stock dropped 13% on news that Claude could automate COBOL modernization, and Bridgestone's migration of mainframe workloads to AWS is cited as a concrete example of this risk.
