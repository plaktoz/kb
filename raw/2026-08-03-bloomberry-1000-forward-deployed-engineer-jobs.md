---

## Overview

Analysis of 1,000 forward deployed engineer (FDE) job postings reveals three distinct role types hiding under one title, with demand growing 1,165% year-over-year.

---

## Three Types of FDE Roles

**Type 1 – The Builder FDE (60%):** Engineers who embed with customers to build and deploy production systems. Heavy coding (70–90%), significant travel (30–50%), salary range $140–250K plus equity.

**Type 2 – The Sales Engineer+ (30%):** Rebranded solutions/sales engineers supporting pre-sales cycles with demos and POCs. Less coding (30–40%), commission-based compensation.

**Type 3 – Internal Tools Builder (10%):** GTM/RevOps engineers building internal automation — "customers" are internal stakeholders, not external clients.

---

## Top 10 Responsibilities

1. Working directly with customers (55%)
2. Building/deploying AI/ML systems (37%)
3. Integrating systems/APIs (32%)
4. Technical architecture
5. Troubleshooting production issues
6. Performance optimization
7. Documentation
8. Cross-functional collaboration
9. Customer onboarding
10. Training/enablement

Notably absent: quota, pipeline generation, or revenue targets.

---

## Demand Growth

FDE job postings exploded **1,165% year-over-year** (Jan–Oct 2025 vs. same period 2024), with October 2025 representing the highest monthly total ever recorded.

---

## Compensation

- **Median base salary:** $173,816
- **70%** of jobs mention equity
- **8%** mention OTE
- **0%** are quota-carrying

Top-paying employers include AI/ML platforms, data infrastructure companies, and well-funded startups, with equity packages potentially worth an additional $100K+.

## FDE vs. Sales Engineer vs. Solutions Engineer

**Key differentiators:**

| Factor | FDE | Sales Engineer | Solutions Engineer |
|--------|-----|----------------|-------------------|
| Primary goal | Production success | Deal closure | Technical validation |
| Writes production code | Yes | Rarely | No |
| Quota | Never | Common | Sometimes |
| Engagement length | Weeks–months post-sale | Sales cycle only | Through POC/contract |
| Compensation | Base + equity | Base + commission | Varies |

Quick test: if a role has a quota → Sales Engineer. If you're writing production code and present months after go-live → FDE.

---

## Key Takeaways

FDE demand is surging because deploying AI agents into enterprise production is genuinely hard — demos aren't enough. The role pays like engineering because it *is* engineering, with customer-facing skills as the differentiator.

**To break in:**
- Learn Python and a front-end language
- Build AI/ML (especially agents and LLM) experience
- Develop customer communication skills
- Be prepared to travel
- Target growth-stage startups (11–200 employees)
