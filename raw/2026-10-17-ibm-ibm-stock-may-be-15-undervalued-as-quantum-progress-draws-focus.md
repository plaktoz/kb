## Does International Business Machines Look Undervalued on Cash Flow?

The Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model here takes IBM's future cash generation and discounts it back to today. On that view, International Business Machines is being valued on free cash flow that was about $13.1b over the last twelve months, with the model assuming that this gradually grows rather than shrinking away. The projections build in a moderate step up in annual cash available to shareholders over the coming decade, not a sharp spike or collapse.

The DCF outcome sits meaningfully above the current share price of $232.14, which means the market is not paying for all of those projected dollars of cash yet.
