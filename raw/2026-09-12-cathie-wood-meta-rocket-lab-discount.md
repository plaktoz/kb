# 2 Stocks Cathie Wood Is Snapping Up at a Discount Right Now

**Author:** John Ballard
**Date:** September 11, 2026
**Source:** https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/11/2-stocks-cathie-wood-snapping-up-discount/

Despite mixed ARK fund performance in 2026, Cathie Wood continues buying into AI and space economy leaders trading below recent highs.

## Meta Platforms (META)

Meta shares sit ~18% below their all-time high. Two ARK ETFs (ARKW and ARKK) purchased a combined $27.9M in Meta stock on Sept. 9, 2026. ARK sees Meta's 3.6 billion daily app users as a durable competitive moat. AI-driven ad improvements helped Q2 revenue rise 28% year-over-year. Meta projects $165–169B in 2026 capex, pressuring near-term free cash flow, though Wood appears to bet those investments will eventually pay off. The stock trades at roughly 21x forward earnings.

## Rocket Lab (RKLB)

Rocket Lab shares have fallen ~60% from an all-time high of $151. Three ARK ETFs bought roughly $45M in RKLB shares between Aug. 31–Sept. 8, 2026. Q2 revenue rose 62% year-over-year, with a record $2.36B backlog. CEO Peter Beck described launch demand as "extreme." A pending Iridium acquisition would add 66 satellites and over 2.5 million subscribers. Morgan Stanley estimates the space industry will reach $1 trillion by 2040.
