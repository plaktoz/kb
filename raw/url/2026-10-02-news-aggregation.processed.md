  URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260930225448.htm

- **Scientists Studied 1,300 Cancer Patients, Then Found a Pattern in the Gut**: Mayo Clinic researchers, publishing in Cell, linked specific gut bacteria to five major cancer groups, to early-onset colorectal and breast cancer, to survival, and to diarrhea caused by the chemotherapy drug 5-FU.
  URL: https://www.newsweek.com/scientists-studied-1300-cancer-patients-then-found-pattern-gut-12511218
