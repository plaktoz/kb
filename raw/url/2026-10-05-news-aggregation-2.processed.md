  URL: https://bioengineer.org/escape-room-learning-helps-nursing-students-with-adhd-but-hurts-those-with-language-difficulties/

## 🩺 Health

- **Health records from 68 adults link plant-based grocery support to lower blood pressure and weight**: Mass General Brigham researchers followed patients who received weekly plant-based grocery packages from a food pantry for about 2.7 years and found the benefit grew with the number of packages: lower diastolic blood pressure, weight and BMI, though A1c did not change significantly (published in Preventing Chronic Disease).
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-health-adults-link-based-grocery.html

- **Climate change affects Indigenous children's health worldwide, review of 53 studies finds**: A global review links climate change to malnutrition, infectious disease and pregnancy-related harms in Indigenous children, and finds the most consistent damage is to psychosocial and cultural well-being, with stress, anxiety and grief tied to weakening bonds with land and tradition (published in Globalization and Health).
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-climate-affects-indigenous-children-health.html

- **Brain scans show shared changes during depression treatment and distinct antidepressant effects in some patients**: An fMRI study of 386 adults with major depression found that one brain-network change appears within 1–2 weeks whether patients take a placebo or an SSRI, while drug-specific changes in areas such as the amygdala show up only in some medicated patients (published in Nature Mental Health).
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-09-brain-scans-depression-treatment-distinct.html
