## 🩺 Health

- **Children of centenarians may stay healthier and live longer too, study finds (Harvard Health)**: A JAMA Network Open analysis of three long-running cohorts found that adult children of centenarians had a 42% lower risk of death, lived about three years longer, and had roughly a third less heart disease and high blood pressure than peers whose parents died at 85 or younger, even after adjusting for lifestyle.
  URL: https://www.health.harvard.edu/healthy-aging-and-longevity/children-of-centenarians-may-stay-healthier-and-live-longer-too-study-finds

- **3 simple diet changes that can drastically boost your mental health (BBC Science Focus)**: Nutritionist and psychologist Kimberley Wilson links diet to brain health through micronutrients, fibre and the gut microbiome, and to avoiding fad diets; she cites a UK Biobank study of 181,990 people in which low-fibre eaters had less tissue volume in 11 brain regions.
  URL: https://www.sciencefocus.com/science/food-brain-health-2
