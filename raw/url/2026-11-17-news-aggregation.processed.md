## ⚡ Productivity

- **AI saves 11 hours a week, but botsitting takes 6.4 back**: Glean's Work AI Index 2026 (6,000 digital workers) finds AI users gain about 11 hours a week but lose 6.4 hours to "botsitting" (supplying context, checking output, fixing errors). Only 13% say their organization performs significantly better. A direct challenge to naive AI-productivity claims.
  URL: https://techinformed.com/ai-saves-11-hours-a-week-but-botsitting-takes-6-4-back
- **Why productivity gains don't always reach the bottom line**: AI productivity gains are easy to feel and hard to measure. McKinsey's 2026 survey shows 80% report personal gains but only 37% see a positive enterprise EBIT impact. A 7,137-worker field experiment found about 2 fewer hours on email per week with no change in task mix.
  URL: https://www.consultancy.uk/news/amp/46189/why-productivity-gains-dont-always-reach-the-bottom-line
- **The 32-Hour Workweek (With Full-Time Pay): Why OCCU Made the Move**: Oregon Community Credit Union is moving most of its ~700 staff to a four-day week at full pay by the end of 2027. It redesigns work by cutting unnecessary steps rather than compressing five days into four, a practical case study in time management at organizational scale.
  URL: https://thecudaily.com/the-32-hour-workweek-with-full-time-pay-why-occu-made-the-move-what-it-has-learned-and-advice-for-other-cus
