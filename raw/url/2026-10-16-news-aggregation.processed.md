## 🩺 Health

- **Total ultra-processed food intake not linked to colorectal cancer in large US cohort study**: An NCI analysis followed 461,682 adults in the NIH-AARP cohort for about 19 years (published in PLOS Medicine) and found that total UPF intake did not predict colorectal cancer, though ultra-processed meat raised risk and UPF-sourced whole grains, fiber and dairy lowered it, so UPFs "should not be treated as a monolith."
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-total-ultra-food-intake-linked.html

- **Prescribing antidepressants based on patients' genetic makeup does not improve the effectiveness of treatment**: The double-blind PRESIDE trial (552 adults, 19 Australian general practices) found that choosing antidepressants by CYP2C19/CYP2D6 genotype did not improve 12-week remission or response compared with standard guideline-based prescribing.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-antidepressants-based-patients-genetic-makeup.html

- **Social media detox research leaves youth mental health questions unresolved, review finds**: A review of 45 studies (2015-2025) on cutting back social media found weak methods, little long-term data and almost no participants under 16, which leaves youth social media bans without firm evidence; senior author John Torous argues for personalized interventions instead.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-social-media-detox-youth-mental.html
