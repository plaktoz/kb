## 🩺 Health

- **Triple-action drug retatrutide delivers major weight loss for people with diabetes, Phase III trial finds**: The TRIUMPH-2 trial, published in The Lancet, gave 1,152 adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes weekly retatrutide (a GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist) for 80 weeks; they lost up to 18.8% of body weight versus 5.1% on placebo, with better HbA1c and cardiovascular markers.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-triple-action-drug-retatrutide-major.html

- **Gut microbes from people with schizophrenia alter mouse behavior**: In a University of Coimbra study in Translational Psychiatry, mice given stool from people with schizophrenia showed behavioral changes, gut inflammation, a weaker gut barrier and microglia activation in the brain, adding to evidence on the gut-brain axis in mental illness.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-gut-microbes-people-schizophrenia-mouse.html

- **Extending Medicaid to 12 months after childbirth increases follow-up care for high-risk mothers**: Columbia Mailman researchers used New York and Texas Medicaid claims to show that 12-month postpartum coverage increased follow-up visits and screenings for mothers with diabetes or hypertensive disorders in pregnancy, which matters as federal Medicaid cuts approach.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-medicaid-months-childbirth-high-mothers.html
