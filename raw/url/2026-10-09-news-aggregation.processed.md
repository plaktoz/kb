## 🧠 Learning

- **Microdelays disrupt online learning (Nature Human Behaviour)**: A new study published 8 Oct 2026 tracked more than 80,000 learners (US K-12 math students and aspiring coders in Latin America). Loading lags of just 1–2 seconds led to lower engagement, more distraction, fewer completed lessons and lower scores, which makes page speed a real barrier to learning online.
  URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02598-y

- **What AI Changes in Teaching and Learning (Harvard Graduate School of Education)**: Ed. Magazine's October 2026 issue covers the book *Irreplaceable*, which separates AI as a "learning assistant" (an on-demand tutor) from AI as a "doing assistant". It also explains how teachers can use AI to research, plan lessons and give feedback without weakening students' basic skills.
  URL: https://www.gse.harvard.edu/ideas/ed-magazine/26/10/chatgpteach

- **The impact of artificial intelligence on students' metacognition: a systematic review (Frontiers in Psychology)**: This 2026 review looks at how AI affects metacognition and self-regulated learning. It finds that standard chatbot interfaces can hurt learning outcomes, while hint-based AI tutors and metacognitive prompts help students become more aware of their own thinking.
  URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1947198/full

## 🩺 Health

- **Twin study suggests screen time's link to mental health may be overstated**: A Karolinska Institutet study in Nature Human Behaviour followed more than 21,000 Swedish twins from age 9 into adulthood. It finds that shared family factors, such as genes and upbringing, explain much of the link between heavy screen use and depression or anxiety, although interactive screen use during adolescence still carried some risk.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-twin-screen-link-mental-health.html

- **New HF Stats 2026 Report Finds Heart Failure Mortality Rising as Disease Increasingly Affects Younger Americans**: The Heart Failure Society of America's annual report (Oct 5) says US heart failure deaths have hit a new peak, with some of the most worrying increases among younger adults and nearly 1 in 4 Americans expected to develop the disease.
  URL: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-hf-stats-2026-report-finds-heart-failure-mortality-rising-as-disease-increasingly-affects-younger-americans-302898512.html

- **Colorectal cancer is rising in young people and often found too late**: Researchers report that most colorectal cancer patients aged 21 or younger were already at Stage III or IV when diagnosed, and they urge that warning signs like rectal bleeding, weight loss and abdominal pain not be ignored.
  URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261003234019.htm
