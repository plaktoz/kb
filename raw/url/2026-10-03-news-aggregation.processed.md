## 🩺 Health

- **Nearly 6 in 10 people with high blood pressure don't know they have it**: Oxford Population Health looked at readings from about 1.4 million people in England's Our Future Health program and found that 59% of those with hypertension had never been diagnosed or treated, and half of those on medication were still not controlled. The authors say this calls for population-wide screening.
  URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260930225445.htm

- **Scientists may have found a "unifying theory" of chronic fatigue**: Researchers at UEA and Oxford BioDynamics analysed 3D genome folding and found that long Covid, ME/CFS, PTSD, rheumatoid arthritis and MS share disrupted networks in immunity, inflammation and mitochondrial energy, with hub genes such as LAG3. The work could eventually lead to objective blood tests.
  URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260929053543.htm

- **How big is Pennsylvania's measles outbreak?**: STAT's Morning Rounds reports that Pennsylvania's measles outbreak (943 confirmed cases, a fifth death) is likely much bigger than reported because of low vaccination rates in Amish communities. It also covers NIH funding fewer projects amid grant delays, and staffing shortages in disability care.
  URL: https://www.statnews.com/2026/10/02/health-news-how-big-is-pennsylvania-measles-outbreak/
