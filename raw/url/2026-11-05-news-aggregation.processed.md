## ❤️ Health

- **Study establishes weekly 'dose-of-nature' benchmarks for mental health benefits**: A University of Hong Kong analysis of 45 years of research sets the first weekly green-space exposure benchmarks for mental health. It finds the benefits rise quickly at low exposure and then taper off, which is practical for wellness routines.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-11-weekly-dose-nature-benchmarks-mental.html

- **Kids who eat more ultra-processed foods show a troubling brain response**: A JAMA Network Open study from Keck School of Medicine of USC found that children who ate more ultra-processed foods had stronger reward-system activity when shown appetizing foods. That activity was linked to body-fat gain over the following year, which is relevant to nutrition and child obesity.
  URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261101010549.htm

- **Longer estrogen exposure linked to better brain vessel health in older women**: A Gothenburg MRI study of 352 70-year-old women links longer lifetime estrogen exposure to healthier brain vessels. This bears on why women are more likely than men to develop Alzheimer's disease.
  URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-11-longer-estrogen-exposure-linked-brain.html
