## 🧠 Learning

- **Same researcher, opposite results**: The Hechinger Report's Proof Points column contrasts two new AI-tutor studies from the same University of Maryland researcher. An AI math tutor engaged 6,000 Arizona middle schoolers, while a university AI assistant hurt college students' engagement and grades. It shows that how an AI learning tool is designed and used matters more than whether it is used.
  URL: https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-ai-tutor-results
- **Want Students to Think With AI? Raise the Stakes.**: EdSurge opinion (Nov 2) on the NBER "One Click Away" study of Khanmigo in 18 Tennessee middle schools. Students rarely used the optional AI tutor, so the piece argues for making the AI conversation itself the assessment. It gives concrete strategies for getting learners to use AI to think rather than to get answers.
  URL: https://www.edsurge.com/news/want-students-to-think-with-ai-raise-the-stakes
- **The Research on AI in Education Is a Mess**: EdWeek (Nov 2) reports on a new audit finding that 14 widely cited meta-analyses claiming AI improves learning have serious flaws, including incoherent definitions of learning and "impossible" statistics. It is a useful caution for judging evidence on learning tools and skill development.
  URL: https://www.edweek.org/technology/the-research-on-ai-in-education-is-a-mess/2026/11
