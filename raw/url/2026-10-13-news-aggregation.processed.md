## ⚡ Productivity

- **After 100 Workations, I Stopped Blaming Willpower for Bad Deep Work (Asian Efficiency)**: Thanh Pham argues that poor focus usually comes from a badly designed environment, not weak discipline, and gives three fixes: add friction before you sit down, put your hardest work in your peak-energy hours, and treat rest and sleep as part of deep work.
  URL: https://www.asianefficiency.com/productivity/stopped-blaming-willpower-deep-work

- **What If You Treated Rest and Recovery As an Investment in Your Law Firm? (Attorney at Work)**: Uses survey data on more than 4,400 lawyers to show that presenteeism makes up nearly 70% of the cost of poor well-being, and argues that skipped recovery builds up as "operational debt" that small, regular doses of creative rest can reduce.
  URL: https://www.attorneyatwork.com/lawyer-burnout-cost-rest-recovery-investment

- **Spoiler Alert: The Future of Work Is Already Here (SAP News Center)**: SAP's research finds AI's value at work is still measured mainly in efficiency and time saved, while 88% of employees say AI has changed their jobs, often without guidance from managers. That raises the question of whether freed-up time goes to more of the same work or to redesigned roles.
  URL: https://news.sap.com/2026/10/future-of-work-is-here
