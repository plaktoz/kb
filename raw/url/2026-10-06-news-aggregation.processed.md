## ⚡ Productivity

- **AI Benefits at Work Unevenly Distributed (Gallup)**: New Gallup poll (Oct 5) finds 63% of U.S. employees who use AI say it makes them faster and 56% more creative, but the productivity gains go mostly to workers who already hold quality jobs (college grads, managers, higher earners).
  URL: https://news.gallup.com/poll/714602/benefits-work-unevenly-distributed.aspx

- **AI makes workers faster -- and reviewers busier (TechTarget)**: David Linthicum argues that time saved by individual AI users often shifts downstream as review, rework and exception handling, and urges CIOs to measure productivity at each handoff (acceptance rates, rework, queue time) rather than only per user.
  URL: https://www.techtarget.com/it-strategy/news/366651622/AI-makes-workers-faster-and-reviewers-busier

- **Improving NHS productivity is within our gift (The Health Foundation)**: Interview with NHS Productivity Commission co-chairs Anita Charlesworth and Tera Allas on the 10 recommendations in their final "Breaking the cycle" report for raising productivity in a complex, people-driven system.
  URL: https://www.health.org.uk/features-and-opinion/features/improving-nhs-productivity-is-within-our-gift

## 🧠 Learning

- **Six weeks, remarkable progress: How AI and teachers helped students learn in Nigeria**: Covers a World Bank randomized evaluation in Edo State, where six weeks of teacher-guided AI tutoring (Microsoft Copilot/GPT-4) produced English learning gains the researchers estimate at about 1.5–2 years of typical progress.
  URL: https://yalibnan.com/2026/10/05/six-weeks-remarkable-progress-how-ai-and-teachers-helped-students-learn-in-nigeria

- **Is UD all in on AI?**: The University of Dayton has made AI literacy a required part of first-year study through modules on AI fundamentals and human dignity, and separates "legitimate" use that amplifies a student's own work from "corrosive" use that replaces it, as research on AI tutors versus plain chatbots remains mixed.
  URL: https://flyernews.com/1226693/news/is-ud-all-in-on-ai

- **Pluralsight's 2026 AI Skills Gap Data: What It Proves and What It Doesn't**: A vendor-neutral review of Pluralsight's 2026 Tech Skills Report (released Sept 30): 96% of organizations have invested in AI tools but only 52% say most or all staff are AI literate. It also gives a practical method for measuring AI literacy and upskilling ROI before and after training.
  URL: https://lmspedia.org/pluralsight-ai-literacy-gap-96pct
