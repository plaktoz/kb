# Why Reading Matters

source_url: https://calnewport.com/why-reading-matters/

---

Newport responds to Rose Horowitch's Atlantic piece on reading's decline, citing alarming data: fewer than half of adults read any book in 2022, daily pleasure reading dropped from 28% (2004) to 16% (2023), and 13-year-olds who "rarely or never" read for fun rose from 8% to 29% since 1984.

Newport argues reading isn't merely a communication technology — drawing on Walter Ong's Orality and Literacy, he contends it fundamentally shaped human cognition, ethics, logic, and the concept of selfhood. The decline therefore represents genuine regression, not neutral cultural evolution.

His proposed remedy mirrors how society addressed physical health decline: treat distracting digital content like junk food and deliberately schedule daily time for reading, writing, and reflection — treating cognitive fitness as seriously as physical fitness.

Horowitch's conclusion, which Newport endorses, frames it as a choice about cultural inheritance: "An astonishing wealth of information and wisdom has been bequeathed to us."
