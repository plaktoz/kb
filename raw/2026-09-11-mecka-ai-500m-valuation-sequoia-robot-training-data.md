# Mecka AI Nears $500M Valuation in Sequoia-Led Deal Amid Rush for Robot Training Data

*Published September 11, 2026 | By Marina Temkin*

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/mecka-ai-nears-500m-valuation-in-sequoia-led-deal-amid-rush-for-robot-training-data/

---

## Summary

Mecka AI, a startup specializing in human motion data collection for training humanoid and general-purpose robots, is reportedly closing a Sequoia Capital-led funding round at roughly a $500M valuation, per two informed sources. Exact round size remains undisclosed and terms aren't finalized.

## Key Facts

- **Previous round:** $60M Series A led by Framework Ventures (announced ~3 months prior), with Menlo Ventures, SV Angel, and Kindred Ventures participating
- **Founded:** 2024, by four co-founders — Josh Gao, Mogen Cheng, Jason Chong, and Duy Nguyen
- **Background:** Gao and Cheng previously built a restaurant fintech; Chong joined Coinbase after it acquired his crypto exchange
- **Business model:** Pays individuals to record everyday tasks (e.g., making coffee, fixing cars) using body sensors and smartphones
- **Name origin:** Derived from "mecha," fictional human-controlled giant robots
- **Revenue projection:** ~$100M annual run rate by end of 2026, per Gao's earlier statements

## Context

The founders identified a shortage of physical-world data as the primary bottleneck for general-purpose robotics. Their approach — sometimes called "egocentric" data capture — mirrors what Scale AI and others did for large language models.

Competitors in the robot training data space include XDOF (reportedly nearing a $1.2B valuation) and Scale AI.
