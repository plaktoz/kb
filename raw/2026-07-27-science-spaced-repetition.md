# The Science of Spaced Repetition

**Source:** https://www.numberanalytics.com/blog/science-spaced-repetition  
**Author:** NumberAnalytics  
**Date:** 2026-07-27

---

Spaced repetition is a learning technique that schedules review sessions at increasing intervals to maximize long-term retention. It exploits two foundational cognitive phenomena: the spacing effect and retrieval practice.

**The spacing effect:**  
Distributing practice over time produces stronger memory traces than massed practice (cramming). Each time material is reviewed after a gap, the brain must reconstruct the memory — this reconstruction strengthens the encoding. Cramming creates strong short-term activation but weak long-term consolidation.

**Retrieval practice:**  
The act of retrieving information from memory — not just re-reading it — strengthens the neural pathways associated with that memory. The "testing effect" shows that actively recalling information is more effective for retention than passive review.

**The forgetting curve:**  
Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve shows that memories decay exponentially after initial encoding. Without review, ~50% of new information is forgotten within an hour and ~70% within 24 hours. Spaced repetition counteracts this by scheduling reviews at the optimal moment — just before the memory would be forgotten.

**Algorithm-driven scheduling:**  
Modern spaced repetition systems (SRS) like Anki use algorithms (e.g., SM-2) to calculate the optimal review interval for each card based on recall difficulty. Cards answered correctly get pushed further out; difficult cards are reviewed sooner.

**Key benefits:**
- Builds durable long-term memory with significantly less total study time than cramming
- Personalizes review frequency based on individual recall performance
- Applicable to any factual domain: languages, medicine, history, programming concepts

**Practical applications:**
- Medical education: used to memorize pharmacology, anatomy, and clinical guidelines
- Language learning: vocabulary and grammar pattern acquisition
- Professional certification prep: systematic coverage of large knowledge domains
- General knowledge work: maintaining familiarity with reference concepts over time

**Mental model:**  
Spaced repetition treats memory as infrastructure — scheduled maintenance prevents decay, and the cost of maintenance decreases as memories consolidate.
