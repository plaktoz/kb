# Why IBM's Q2 earnings could be more important than Google's and Tesla's

source_url: https://finance.yahoo.com/video/why-ibms-q2-earnings-could-be-more-important-than-googles-and-teslas-141538120.html

---

IBM was set to report Q2 earnings after market close. Simplify Asset Management's Paisley Nardini (managing director, head of multi-asset solutions) offered her perspective.

One analyst argued IBM's report mattered most that evening, noting that a major company with many management layers being "totally disrupted by AI" raises broader concerns — this vulnerability isn't unique to IBM, which worried investors seeking value.

Nardini emphasized that individual stock-picking carries heavy idiosyncratic risk amid macro uncertainty, particularly around interest rates. She noted volatility in small caps regardless of rate direction, suggesting markets are "trading a lot on technicals" and on speculation. As fundamentals reassert themselves, some companies are simply unable to keep pace with AI-driven demands — and IBM illustrated that challenge.
