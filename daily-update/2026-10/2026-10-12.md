## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**The Activation Gap meets the Persistence Gap.** [Cognizant](../../wiki/productivity/cognizant-ai-activation-gap-employee-adoption.md) says the job is to let eager employees use *more* AI, while [Berkeley's RCTs](../../wiki/learning/ten-minutes-ai-use-erodes-persistence-study.md) show that just 10 minutes of AI help makes people give up sooner once it's taken away. Taken together, the question for organizations becomes *how* to adopt AI rather than how fast. [Cambridge's teachers](../../wiki/learning/cambridge-study-ai-english-teaching-teachers-drivers-seat.md) reach the same answer: AI works best for people who already have independent skills. A good adoption policy would close the activation gap with **tutor-style, objective-first** tools rather than answer machines that quietly use up the **productive struggle** that builds the expertise Cognizant's "Precisionists" rely on.
