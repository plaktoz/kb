## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**McKinsey and Gartner are stress-testing the same AI bet from opposite cost directions.** McKinsey's [AI value curve](../../wiki/technology/ai-value-curve-economics-of-applying-ai-to-existing-work.md) shows the value pool is fragile if AI costs *rise* 30%, while Gartner's [neocloud forecast](../../wiki/finance/gartner-ai-returns-insufficient-neocloud-consolidation-forecast.md) describes a glut where tokens are already given away and capacity outruns demand.
