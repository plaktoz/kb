## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
**AI is moving from "spend at any cost" to "show me the returns."** Alphabet's record one-day drop, Microsoft's $25B bond deal and a hot GDP print driven by AI equipment imports all point to markets scrutinizing how AI buildouts are financed and monetized. Meanwhile, studies on AI tutoring, workplace AI use and omega-3 supplements share a quieter lesson: activity and intermediate metrics are not the same as real outcomes.

Today's market reaction fits the pattern too: investors are now asking whether AI spending turns into [monetization](../../wiki/technology/google-ai-mode-lower-revenue-per-query-q3-2026-stock-drop.md) rather than rewarding the spending itself.
