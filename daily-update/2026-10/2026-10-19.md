## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
**AI's costs are coming due, in capital markets and in human skill.** Tech stocks sold off on AI-financing worries (CoreWeave, Nscale, rising Treasury yields) just as earnings season tests whether the spending pays off. Meanwhile, learning and strategy notes converge on one idea: what you hand to AI, you stop owning, so protect the judgment and data that AI can't copy.

### 🎯 Strategy
- **[AI is draining the moat around your business](../../wiki/strategy/ai-draining-moat-around-your-business.md)**: Expertise, software features and customer relationships are commoditizing. The proposed replacements are decision velocity, proprietary context, customer experience and human trust.
- **[The AI moat isn't the model](../../wiki/strategy/ai-moat-isnt-the-model-proprietary-data.md)**: With foundation models licensable by anyone, proprietary data is the likelier edge, judged by exclusivity, depth and learning effects. ## 🧠 Emerging Connections
