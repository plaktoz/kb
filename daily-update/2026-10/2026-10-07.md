## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**The PNAS finding and the "selection crisis" describe the same failure.** In [AI in US schools](../../wiki/learning/ai-us-schools-learning-evidence-student-privacy.md), students who leaned on **unguarded GPT-4 scored 17% lower** once the tool was removed, and a hint-based tutor that made them do the thinking removed the harm. In [The Attention Realignment](../../wiki/productivity/attention-realignment-protect-focus-not-time.md), Pereira warns that AI's danger "isn't only AI thinking for us, but deciding what we think about." Read together, the learning and productivity notes point to the same design principle: **AI helps when it preserves your effortful attention and harms when it replaces it**. That suggests a test for any new tool in the vault, whether for studying or for work: *does this keep me doing the cognitive work, or does it do it for me?*
