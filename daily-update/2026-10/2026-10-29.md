## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Today's productivity note argues that AI value stalls because the organization's design, meaning how decisions and coordination work, is the bottleneck. That lines up with [Deming's NBER study of meetings as organizational capital](../../wiki/productivity/meetings-organizational-capital-deming-nber-hr.md), which treats coordination routines as investments in the organization itself. Together they suggest that AI rollouts may be measured wrongly when they count only individual time saved and ignore the coordination systems they touch.
