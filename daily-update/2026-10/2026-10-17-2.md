## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Today's learning notes and technology notes converge on a single insight about human attention as the irreducible bottleneck. The [Anthropic employment report](../../wiki/technology/how-employment-is-changing-at-anthropic-ai-agents-junior-hiring.md) documents that interactive Claude sessions spend ~80% of their time idle waiting for human input — and reading all agent output would consume half a non-technical employee's week. As agents log 6× human hours, the asymmetry is stark — the pipeline floods with outputs, but the human review bandwidth stays fixed at ~3 hours of usable focus. The lesson from both notes points to the same lever: **protect focused blocks ferociously, because they are now the scarcest resource in the entire production system, not just the individual workday.**
