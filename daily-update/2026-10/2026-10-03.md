### ⚡ Productivity
- **[An empty calendar is a constraints problem, not a discipline problem](../../wiki/productivity/empty-calendar-fake-work-constraints.md)**: Without time boundaries, people drift into **fake work**: tasks that feel busy but serve no goal. Pham's fixes are tagging every task with the goal it serves, named focus blocks, a Someday/Maybe list for low-priority items, and the 30/30 Rule. The line to remember: without prioritization, "AI will help you do the wrong things faster."
- **[Meeting count hurts more than meeting length](../../wiki/productivity/itoki-matsuo-ai-productivity-model-meeting-frequency.md)**: Itoki and the Matsuo Institute applied **VAR-LiNGAM** causal discovery to 70 behavioral signals from 854 workers. The number of meetings, more than their total length, worsened next-day mental condition in every job type, and days with 60+ minutes of uninterrupted work improved it for 88% of workers. It's hard evidence for consolidating meetings, not just shortening them, though the findings reflect only Itoki's workplace.

### ❤️ Health
- **[A unifying theory of fatigue: five illnesses, one set of networks](../../wiki/health/unifying-theory-chronic-fatigue-shared-biological-networks.md)**: Researchers used 3D genome modeling to show that ME/CFS, long Covid, PTSD, rheumatoid arthritis and multiple sclerosis share little gene overlap but feed into the same immune, mitochondrial and stress-response networks. It's a textbook case of **equifinality** and points toward objective blood tests, though hub genes such as LAG3 are still only computational candidates.
- **[59% of English adults with hypertension don't know they have it](../../wiki/health/undiagnosed-hypertension-england-our-future-health.md)**: Among 1.4M adults in the Our Future Health cohort, 37% had hypertension, and half of those on medication were still above 140/90. Over 15% of 18–39-year-olds are affected, yet they aren't eligible for NHS Health Checks. It's a sharp illustration of the **Rule of Halves**: losses compound at every step from diagnosis to treatment to control, and feeling fine says little.
- **[When productivity is hiding depression](../../wiki/health/productivity-hides-depression-overfunctioning.md)**: **Overfunctioning** means staying busy to avoid what's happening inside, so finishing a task brings no relief because another one immediately replaces it. The article's test: healthy structured activity (behavioral activation, backed by a 105-trial meta-analysis) brings relief, while overfunctioning leaves the strain in place. Its 10-minute weekly check on mood, sleep, energy and self-talk is a practical tool to keep.

## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Busyness is a proxy, and today's notes catch it in three different places.** The [DHH note](../../wiki/technology/dhh-37signals-death-of-coding-by-hand.md) and the [empty-calendar note](../../wiki/productivity/empty-calendar-fake-work-constraints.md) both cite **Goodhart's Law**. Once shipping volume or hours logged become the target, engineers work 12–13 hour days "just to press enter" and knowledge workers fill empty time with fake work. The [overfunctioning note](../../wiki/health/productivity-hides-depression-overfunctioning.md) shows the personal version of the same mistake: activity that looks like peak output can be a way to avoid distress. All three apply the same test: does the activity serve a goal and leave you better off? How much of it there is doesn't matter.
