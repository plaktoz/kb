## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Lever's "owner effect" in the productivity notes mirrors ITIF's argument in the strategy notes. A firm that names one owner for how people and AI divide work tends to report more value, and ITIF's Robert Atkinson makes the same structural case at national scale: a goal that spans every agency is owned by none, so it needs one lead institution. Read together, they suggest accountability design matters as much as the technology itself.
