## 🧠 Emerging Connections
The [[gun-violence-exposure-accelerates-biological-aging]] study's core mechanism — chronic, low-grade stress exposure quietly accumulating physiological "wear" (allostatic load) without any single acute event — is structurally the same story the [[vitality-workforce-health-productivity-index-2026]] tells about the workplace: 85% of its $2.33T productivity loss comes from *presenteeism*, not absence, meaning the damage from everyday work stress and AI-related anxiety is invisible until measured directly, just like biological aging from community violence exposure. Both notes argue that the most expensive harms are the ones that never show up as a discrete incident.
