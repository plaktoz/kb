### ⚡ Productivity
- **[Productivity, efficiency and effectiveness are three different questions](../../wiki/productivity/productivity-vs-efficiency-key-differences-examples.md)**: Count only **accepted output** so the metric doesn't reward rework, pair every efficiency target with the outcome it can distort, and **protect the constraint**, not the busiest team. - **[Manage your energy, not your time](../../wiki/productivity/manage-energy-not-time-allie-dunin.md)**: Allie Dunin's core insight is that time is the same every day but energy isn't. It's a practical fix for the **planning fallacy** in personal planning.

Supporting evidence for the **knowledge-rich curriculum** hypothesis, made more credible by the funder reporting a null result for science.

## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**The average is lying to you, in both the classroom and the ops dashboard.** The [escape-room study](../../wiki/learning/escape-room-gamification-nursing-adhd-language-difficulties.md) found what looked like a small, uniform shortfall that actually hid **opposite effects** in two subgroups: a large gain for ADHD students and a large loss for second-language learners. The [productivity vs efficiency guide](../../wiki/productivity/productivity-vs-efficiency-key-differences-examples.md) warns about the same failure in operations: "a stable average can hide one overloaded queue." Both point to one portable rule, the **flaw of averages**: segment by learner type, queue or team *before* judging whether an intervention works, because a flat aggregate can mean some groups are helped and others harmed.
