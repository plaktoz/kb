### 🧠 Learning
- **[1–2 second load lags measurably hurt learning](../../wiki/learning/microdelays-disrupt-online-learning-nature-2026.md)**: Two field studies of 80,000+ learners in *Nature Human Behaviour* found that **microdelays** cut engagement, completion, and achievement. - **[AI helps metacognition only when it shows the thinking](../../wiki/learning/ai-impact-student-metacognition-systematic-review.md)**: A 22-study review finds that guided-interaction AI scaffolds planning and monitoring. ## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Quality of use beats quantity, for screens and for AI alike.** The [twin study](../../wiki/health/twin-study-screen-time-mental-health-family-factors.md) finds hours alone are a weak signal for youth mental health once family factors are controlled, and points to *what* teens do on screens. The [metacognition review](../../wiki/learning/ai-impact-student-metacognition-systematic-review.md) reaches the same conclusion for AI in learning: guided interaction helps, direct answers erode self-monitoring. [Bialik's learning vs. doing assistant split](../../wiki/learning/irreplaceable-maya-bialik-ai-teaching-framework.md) turns that into a classroom rule. So "limit screen time" and "ban ChatGPT" make the same mistake. Both measure exposure when the evidence says **the interaction pattern** is what matters, which echoes yesterday's [UMD tutor RCT](../../wiki/learning/umd-virtual-study-assistant-rct-low-uptake.md), where 73.8% of queries simply asked for answers.
