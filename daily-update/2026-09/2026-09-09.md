## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
Today's digest surfaces a sharp tension at the heart of the AI era: the same tools that remove execution barriers are making prioritization and agency harder — not easier. From [[Anne-Laure Le Cunff]]'s Omnipotence Dilemma to [[Ben Thompson]]'s argument that LLMs compress knowledge but cannot supply volition, multiple threads converge on the same insight: **what scales is information; what doesn't scale is the intent to act on it.** In parallel, the infrastructure layer that makes this AI wave possible is hitting a new wall — not chips, but electricity.

### 🚀 Technology
- **[Google's 'Google Jail' is strangling independent wikis](../../wiki/technology/google-jail-independent-wikis.md)**: A March 2024 Google core update silently imprisons new domains — only their homepage indexes in search. With ~85% of wiki traffic coming from Google, independent wikis migrating off Fandom face existential traffic loss; the proven escape is hosting as a subdomain of an established domain until trust is built.

- **[AI data centers will nearly triple by 2030 — and the bottleneck isn't chips](../../wiki/technology/ai-data-center-footprint-triple-2030.md)**: Hyperscalers (MSFT, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta) are building at unprecedented scale, but power availability is now the binding constraint on AI expansion. Securing electricity is becoming the next competitive moat — model sophistication matters less if you can't run it.

- **[A researcher factored 1990s CA root keys in 32 hours on a laptop](../../wiki/technology/rsa-key-factoring-historical-certificate-authority.md)**: Matthew McPherrin found two 512-bit RSA roots from defunct CA E-Certify in a Netscape 4.51 installer, fully recovered both private keys using CADO-NFS, and built a working TLS server issuing certificates the old browser trusts. An elegant piece of security archaeology demonstrating how cryptographic debt accumulates.

- **[Stoke Space raises $1B to build the first fully reusable rocket](../../wiki/technology/stoke-space-raises-billion-series-e-nova-pathfinder.md)**: No operator has ever reflown a second stage — Stoke's Nova Pathfinder targets that milestone with a liquid-hydrogen heat shield. First launch: early 2027. Nova Block 2 (15t to LEO) is deliberately timed for 2029 to slot into the gap left by SpaceX Falcon 9's retirement.

### 🎯 Strategy
- **[The Omnipotence Dilemma: AI capability makes prioritization harder, not easier](../../wiki/strategy/omnipotence-dilemma-ai-decision-paralysis.md)**: When AI makes everything plausible and cheap to start, three dynamics conspire — synthetic plausibility, cheap starts, endless iteration — to produce the feeling of progress without meaningful advancement. Le Cunff's remedy: bounded experiments using "I will [action] for [duration]" to re-introduce the scarcity AI has removed.

- **[LLMs scale knowledge; they cannot supply the will to act](../../wiki/strategy/write-things-down-thompson-llm-volition.md)**: Ben Thompson uses GTD's RAM analogy to frame why LLMs are powerful (they compress and surface human knowledge at scale) but incomplete (they lack volition and morality). The Hugging Face agent "civilization" reflected LLM design principles, not emergent intent. AGI, by Thompson's bar, still requires continuous learning.

### 🧠 Learning
- **[The Illusion of Clarity: familiarity is not comprehension](../../wiki/learning/illusion-of-clarity-testing-understanding.md)**: We store rough sketches of concepts, confuse exposure fluency for understanding, and outsource knowledge to Google — producing overconfident, gap-riddled mental models. The fix is brutal and simple: explain it step-by-step without aids, and note exactly where the explanation breaks down.

- **[Feather observation as a gateway to scientific thinking](../../wiki/learning/building-scientific-skills-through-observation.md)**: Three structured activities (single-specimen description → cross-species comparison → evolutionary interpretation) use feathers to build scientific vocabulary, hypothesis formation, and structure-function reasoning. The pedagogical claim: familiar, accessible objects lower engagement barriers while building rigorous observational habits.

### ❤️ Health
- **[FDA approves four updated COVID-19 vaccines targeting the XFG Stratus variant](../../wiki/health/fda-approves-updated-covid-19-vaccines-xfg-variant.md)**: COMIRNATY, SPIKEVAX, MNEXSPIKE, and NUVAXOVID are approved for 2026–2027, targeting the most prevalent global strain. mRNA's rapid reformulation capability continues to be the key advantage; priority recipients are adults 65+ and those with qualifying conditions.

- **[Shingrix reduces cardiovascular disease burden by 9% beyond just preventing shingles](../../wiki/health/shingrix-cardiovascular-risk-reduction-expert-perspectives.md)**: A Nature Medicine study of 72,920 adults found Shingrix recipients had lower rates of coronary heart disease, heart failure, ischemic stroke, and AFib versus Zostavax. The effect appeared too quickly to be explained purely by preventing shingles — decreased IL-6 inflammation is the proposed pathway.

- **[Memory decline begins in middle age, not late life — and it works differently than assumed](../../wiki/health/memory-decline-begins-middle-age-hippocampus-study.md)**: MRI research shows the hippocampus doesn't just slow down with age; it begins to *misbind* information — older adults show *increased* reactivation during memory errors. The practical upshot: protective habits (exercise, diet, sleep) need to start in middle age, and the right clinical flag is decline from *your own* prior baseline, not occasional forgetfulness.

## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Today's [Omnipotence Dilemma](../../wiki/strategy/omnipotence-dilemma-ai-decision-paralysis.md) and [Write Things Down](../../wiki/strategy/write-things-down-thompson-llm-volition.md) share a structural parallel with [Illusion of Clarity](../../wiki/learning/illusion-of-clarity-testing-understanding.md): all three diagnose a gap between the *feeling* of capability or comprehension and actual advance. Le Cunff argues AI creates a false sense of productive motion; Thompson argues LLMs cannot provide the will behind action; Le Cunff-on-learning argues fluency mimics understanding. Taken together, they point to a meta-skill for the AI era: **deliberately re-introducing friction** — bounded experiments, continuous-learning bars, explanation-without-aids — as the mechanism for converting apparent capability into real progress.
