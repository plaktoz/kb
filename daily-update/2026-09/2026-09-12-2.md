### ❤️ Health

- **[Very hot drinks triple esophageal cancer risk — the threshold is 65°C and 3 minutes of cooling prevents it](../../wiki/health/hot-drinks-esophageal-cancer-risk.md)**: A near-million-person UK study found "very hot" beverage drinkers face 3× the esophageal squamous cell carcinoma risk versus "warm" drinkers, with 6+ daily cups adding another 71% — and the WHO already classifies beverages above 65°C as "probably carcinogenic." Critically, this effect is type-specific: adenocarcinoma showed no temperature link, isolating the mechanism to mucosal thermal injury rather than a systemic metabolic effect.

- **[A pancreatic cancer drug just showed 42% tumor shrinkage in treatment-resistant lung cancer](../../wiki/health/daraxonrasib-treatment-resistant-nsclc-ras-trial.md)**: Daraxonrasib, FDA-approved for metastatic pancreatic cancer, hit 42% tumor shrinkage and an 89% disease control rate in the best NSCLC subgroup (no prior docetaxel, n=38) — results good enough to trigger a Phase 3 randomized trial. The drug's multiselective RAS(ON) inhibition covers multiple RAS mutation variants, potentially reaching the ~30% of NSCLC patients with currently untreatable mutations who have run out of targeted options.

- **[First migraine prevention guideline update in 14 years — built around CGRP, not the 2012 drug list](../../wiki/health/updated-guidelines-migraine-prevention.md)**: The AAN and American Headache Society replaced their 2012 recommendations to incorporate CGRP-pathway therapies unavailable at the time, establishing 4+ migraine days/month as the threshold for preventive therapy discussions and explicitly addressing medication overuse headache as a distinct stratification factor. The practical shift: clinicians are now guided toward individualized treatment based on CGRP pathway targeting, not one-size-fits-all escalation from older drug classes.
