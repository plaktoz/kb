### ❤️ Health

- **[FDA approved Rasonque — a drug that nearly doubled pancreatic cancer survival](../../wiki/health/fda-approves-rasonque-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer.md)**: Daraxonrasib showed 13.2 months median survival versus 6.7 months on chemotherapy alone in Phase 3 — a near-doubling in one of oncology's historically hardest cancers. The mechanism is a "molecular glue" targeting RAS gene mutations present in >90% of pancreatic adenocarcinoma cases. The catch: ~$500,000 per year, and questions remain about first-line use and resistance over time.

- **[New ASA guidelines: stroke rehab must begin within 48 hours and treat the whole person](../../wiki/health/stroke-rehab-guidelines-2026-whole-person-recovery.md)**: The first major update since 2016, explicitly integrating physical, cognitive, communication, and mental health recovery into a single coordinated plan. The neurobiological rationale is timing: neuroplasticity genes remain elevated for 1–3 months post-stroke, so early therapy exploits that window. Post-stroke depression (1 in 3 survivors) is now a mandated screening target; caregiver mental health is formally addressed.

- **[HRT started ages 46–56 links to 16% lower Alzheimer's risk — if you carry APOE4, the effect is stronger](../../wiki/health/menopause-hrt-timing-dementia-risk.md)**: A 13-year UK Biobank study of 183,000 women finds ~10% lower dementia risk and 16% lower Alzheimer's risk for HRT users overall — with the protective window centered on perimenopause (46–56) and amplified by surgical menopause and APOE4 carrier status. Observational only; HRT for dementia prevention is not yet a clinical recommendation, but the effect-modifier pattern points precisely to where RCTs should focus.
