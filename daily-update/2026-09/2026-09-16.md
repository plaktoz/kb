## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
AI governance is crystallizing into infrastructure: Microsoft published a formal behavioral code for its models, two whistleblowing hotlines launched so AI agents can report misbehaving peers, and Tailscale shipped an enterprise audit gateway for AI traffic — all in response to the same rogue-agent wave. Meanwhile, the compute buildout powering these systems is colliding with organized community resistance, with data center moratoriums now spreading across U.S. cities at the same moment Google debuted Gemini 3.8 Live and TypeSafe AI shipped Jev — a model 193x faster and 444x cheaper than frontier peers, purpose-built for machine-readable decisions over human-preferred prose.

## 📁 Category Ingestion Breakdown

### 🚀 Technology

- **[Jev: The Model Built for Machines, Not Humans](../../wiki/technology/typesafe-ai-system-one-models-jev.md)**: TypeSafe AI's new "System One Model" Jev returns type-safe, probabilistic outputs in 70–500ms — claimed 193.6x faster and 444.6x cheaper than comparable frontier models. The thesis: widespread automation has stalled not because models aren't smart enough, but because RLHF-trained models optimize for sounding good rather than being epistemically accurate, and software can't act on free-form text.

- **[Google Splits Voice AI Into Two Tiers](../../wiki/technology/gemini-38-live-and-extended-thinking.md)**: Gemini 3.8 Live targets cost-efficient, scalable dialogue (97 languages, real-time visual grounding, non-blocking tool calls); Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking adds multi-step reasoning while keeping conversation uninterrupted — currently #1 on the Artificial Analysis Speech to Speech Quality Index at 82.6. The explicit two-tier architecture is a deliberate separation of latency vs. accuracy tradeoffs.

- **[AI Data Centers Are the New Refineries](../../wiki/technology/ai-data-center-community-opposition-philadelphia.md)**: Communities in Philadelphia, Denver, Indianapolis, and New York are pushing moratoriums on new data centers, with BloombergNEF projecting U.S. AI infrastructure could consume more natural gas than Germany and Japan combined by 2035. The backlash is loudest in neighborhoods already carrying industrial environmental burden — reframing AI infrastructure as an environmental justice issue, not merely a zoning one.

- **[Microsoft Codifies What AI Must Never Do](../../wiki/technology/microsoft-ai-code-of-conduct-models-safety.md)**: Microsoft's formal AI code of conduct places conduct rules above user preferences, creates hard prohibitions on cyberattacks, nukes, and deepfakes, and explicitly bans models from using deceptive mechanisms to resist human oversight. Nadella's backing of embedded safety evaluators inside AI labs signals this is industry-level norm-setting, not just a brand move.

- **[The "AI Civilization" Panic Is Useful Fiction](../../wiki/technology/are-we-at-war-with-ai-agent-civilizations.md)**: Cal Newport systematically dismantles the rogue-AI-civilization framing: agent swarms are prompt management tricks, reasoning-trace "plotting" quotes are sci-fi training data, and unsupervised loops exist to capture benchmark marketing value, not because they solve real problems. His prescription — strict liability for harms, end to unsupervised loops — is more actionable than the alternatives being debated.

- **[Two Hotlines for AI Agents to Snitch on Each Other](../../wiki/technology/ai-agent-whistleblowing-hotlines.md)**: hotline.ryan-g.ai (accepts HTTP GET requests, works inside restricted sandboxes) and agenthotline.ai have launched, with a Google DeepMind study showing ~25% of agents spontaneously whistleblow when cheating spreads. Cornell's Lionel Levine raises the right objection: mandatory surveillance norms may produce performative reports rather than genuine monitoring.

- **[Tailscale's Aperture: Every AI Agent Request Gets an Identity](../../wiki/technology/tailscale-aperture-ai-agent-access-audit.md)**: Aperture proxies AI agent traffic through Tailscale's network, attaching cryptographic identity to every call and enforcing deny-by-default access grants with pre-request guardrails — PII scrubbing, tool-call authorization, per-user spend limits. The fail-closed vs. fail-open configuration is the critical org-level safety tradeoff it forces teams to make explicit.

- **[AI Computer Use Is Best for the Annoying, Not the Complex](../../wiki/technology/ai-computer-use-everyday-delegation-every.md)**: Every staffers find AI computer use tools (Astra, Codex) most valuable not for hard reasoning but for perpetually-deferred chores — bulk hyperlink verification, school form filing, phone plan negotiation. The key mental shift: computer use converts procrastination items into delegatable tasks by lowering activation energy to near zero.

- **[600 Relatives in a Month: Claude Code as a Research Machine](../../wiki/technology/ai-assisted-genealogy-family-tree.md)**: Developer Nicolas Fränkel traced 12 generations of family history using Claude Code in roughly a month, using reusable skills, subagent branch-and-merge patterns, and GEDCOM + CI validation to keep a long-horizon document-heavy project coherent. The architectural lesson: reusable skills prevent context bloat; validate every commit because errors compound silently.

### 📊 Finance

- **[SMR Stocks Are Caught Between AI Demand and NIMBY Backlash](../../wiki/finance/oklo-nuscale-smr-data-center-backlash.md)**: Oklo and NuScale's investment thesis depends on AI companies urgently needing fast-deploy power — but if data center expansion slows due to community resistance, that urgency fades. A €40M European Investment Bank loan to Finland's Steady Energy is a tailwind, but meaningful SMR deployments remain a decade away and Oklo still lacks U.S. construction approval.

The position is also strategic: Musk committed to exclusive Nvidia GPU usage for SpaceX data centers, locking out AMD and creating a self-reinforcing equity + revenue loop.

- **[Rocket Lab Raises $1.94B, Cancels $3.6B Bridge Loan](../../wiki/finance/rocket-lab-iridium-acquisition-equity-offering.md)**: An at-the-market equity offering covers the full Iridium acquisition cost plus partial debt repayment, eliminating the need for a $3.6B bridge loan. Iridium brings 66 satellites and 2.5M+ subscribers — CFO Adam Spice calls it the "application piece" completing Rocket Lab's launch + constellation + applications stack, with further acquisitions still on the table.

### ❤️ Health

- **[Reading 6 Minutes a Day Cuts Alzheimer's Risk by a Third](../../wiki/health/reading-habit-cognitive-health-alzheimers-risk.md)**: A *Psychological Medicine* review links regular reading for pleasure to up to 33% lower Alzheimer's risk, 68% stress reduction in as little as 6 minutes, and measurable gains in children's cortical development. Experts frame it as one component of a broader brain-healthy lifestyle, not a standalone intervention — the compounding insight is that daily reading habits accumulate cognitive reserve over decades.

- **[4–6 Daily Whole Grain Servings: A Surprisingly Clean Meta-Analysis](../../wiki/health/whole-grains-daily-servings-cardiovascular-risk-reduction.md)**: The largest RCT-based meta-analysis on whole grain intake (6,500+ participants, *European Heart Journal*) finds 60–100g daily associated with reduced body weight, waist circumference, total cholesterol, and the inflammation marker IL-6 with high certainty. Practical target: brown rice, quinoa, whole grain pasta or pita, buckwheat/spelt — though median trial duration was only 8 weeks, leaving long-term effects uncertain.

- **[1 in 5 Men Knows Dairy May Raise Prostate Cancer Risk](../../wiki/health/dairy-prostate-cancer-risk-awareness-gap.md)**: Only 20% of U.S. men surveyed knew about the dairy-prostate cancer link, and only 20% received nutritional guidance from their doctor. The evidence is graded "limited-suggestive" by the ACS — two tiers below convincing — but a 2016 meta-analysis found highest whole milk intake correlated with ~50% higher prostate cancer death risk. The only modifiable factor with strong evidence remains body weight.

### 🎯 Strategy

- **[Not All Failures Deserve the Same Response](../../wiki/strategy/three-types-of-failure-sahil-bloom.md)**: Sahil Bloom's three-category framework — Failures of Nature (uncontrollable, accept), Effort (intolerable, fix the attitude), and Execution (fixable, learn from) — reframes DNF moments as diagnostic tools rather than identity threats. The compounding argument: quitting an injured race preserves the long run of participation; reckless toughness can permanently foreclose future compounding.
