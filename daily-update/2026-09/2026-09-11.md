Existing production ML assets are preserved, not replaced.

- **[Python dataclasses beat plain dicts at every trust level except "quick local prototype"](../../wiki/technology/dataclasses-for-structured-application-data.md)**: Mistyped keys in dicts fail silently; dataclasses raise `AttributeError` at construction. The guiding rule: dict → dataclass → Pydantic maps cleanly to local/trusted/untrusted data. Key pitfall: always use `field(default_factory=list)`, not `field(default=[])`, for mutable defaults — the latter shares a single list across all instances.
