## 🧠 Emerging Connections

The [[SuperAgers]] genetics study and [[Staff Work Part 3]] land on the same epistemic move from completely different domains: both argue that the *absence* of a negative factor doesn't prove the *presence* of a positive one. SuperAgers' sharp memories aren't explained by low Alzheimer's genetic risk, just as a manager's silent rewrite that fixes a bad paper doesn't prove the underlying officer actually learned anything — in both cases, the interesting question is what's *actively* producing the good outcome, not just what risk was absent.
