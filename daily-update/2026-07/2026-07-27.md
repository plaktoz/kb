- **[Workers report 11 hours saved weekly. Only 13% see improved company performance](../../wiki/productivity/ai-isnt-breaking-work-its-already-broken.md)**: Newport's core claim: botsitting, tool-switching overhead, and workplace theater are absorbing the AI gains — and none of these are new problems. AI made them visible; it didn't create them.

- **[Reading decline is a cognitive regression, not a cultural shift](../../wiki/productivity/why-reading-matters-cognitive-fitness.md)**: Half of adults read no books in 2022; pleasure reading among teens is down 50% since 1984. Newport draws on Walter Ong to argue reading restructured human cognition itself — its decline is more serious than the data suggests, and treating it like physical fitness is the remedy.

- **[AI isn't taking jobs — it's reshaping them](../../wiki/productivity/ai-job-displacement-measured-reality-2026.md)**: Altman and Amodei are walking back their strongest displacement predictions. On the ground, Newport found AI functioning as a "resourceful intern" — building internal tools, augmenting roles — not replacing workers wholesale. His "freestyle work" model describes the adaptation pattern.
