## 🧠 Emerging Connections

**Bug blindness + culture = the same root cause.** Dan Luu's [bug blindness](../../wiki/technology/bug-blindness-dan-luu.md) and Gregor Ojstersek's [culture-first productivity thesis](../../wiki/productivity/good-culture-biggest-productivity-hack-not-ai.md) are two lenses on the same organizational failure: when teams *adapt* to dysfunction rather than *fixing* it, the dysfunction becomes invisible. Luu shows this happens at the individual level (habitual workarounds mask bugs); Ojstersek shows it happens at the org level (bad culture normalizes broken communication). The link to [[Conway's Law]] is exact — organizations ship the shape of their own blind spots, and AI doesn't fix blindness, it accelerates whatever the organization is already producing. Both notes point to the same prescription: the cure requires *pointing at the problem*, which is a cultural act, not a technical one.
