## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Today's Learning and Strategy notes are describing the same underlying mechanism from different domains: [spaced repetition](../../wiki/learning/how-to-learn-faster-and-better-10-strategies.md) works because reviewing material right before you'd forget it produces a stronger memory trace than reviewing too early or too late — and Munger's [latticework](../../wiki/strategy/charlie-munger-lattice-of-mental-models.md) works on an analogous principle for decisions: pulling in a fresh model right as your default heuristic would otherwise take over produces a better judgment than either ignoring the decision or over-analyzing it. Both are, in effect, timing disciplines for when to deliberately re-engage rather than coast on the first answer available.
