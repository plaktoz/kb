## 🧠 Emerging Connections
Two notes from opposite sections tell the same story about how AI either builds or erodes skill. **[[AlphaWrite AI Student Writing]]** (Learning) shows AI used as a forced-engagement scaffold — it won't rewrite a sentence for a student, only diagnose and coach — and posts 3.6x growth as a result. **[[Workplace AI Damages Productivity Trust]]** (Productivity) shows the opposite pattern in the workplace: AI used as a shortcut to *generate* the work itself produces "workslop" that erodes trust and costs $186/employee/month to clean up. The dividing line in both domains isn't whether AI is used, but whether it's designed to preserve the cognitive work that actually builds skill — a distinction the vault's existing [[topics/mental-models]] note on decision frameworks could usefully formalize as a general principle for evaluating any new AI tool.
