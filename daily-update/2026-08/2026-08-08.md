## 🧠 Emerging Connections
The **[AI margin-problem note](../../wiki/technology/ai-margin-problem-uk-venture-funding.md)** and the **[workslop/deep-work note](../../wiki/productivity/ai-turned-best-work-into-bare-minimum.md)** are two halves of the same story: at the company level, AI's compute costs don't shrink with scale the way software's did — they grow as a share of spend — and at the individual level, AI's time savings don't shrink workloads the way efficiency gains are supposed to either.
