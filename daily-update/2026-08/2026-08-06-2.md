### ⚡ Productivity
- **[AI adoption is 80% — and focus efficiency just hit a three-year low](../../wiki/productivity/2026-state-of-workplace-ai-adoption-focus-erosion.md)**: ActivTrak's analysis of 443M work hours found AI added to every measured work category (email up 104%, chat up 145%) rather than replacing it, average focus sessions shrank to 13 minutes 7 seconds, and 1 in 4 workers are now disengaged. The "productivity sweet spot" is 7–10% of work time in AI tools — only 3% of employees are there.

Updated with practical tactics: batch shallow work into two fixed windows and post visible "heads-down until X" status to protect blocks without appearing unresponsive.

### 🧠 Learning
- **[L&D strategy vs. training plan: the distinction most organizations miss](../../wiki/learning/ld-strategy-framework-2026.md)**: A training plan lists activities; an L&D strategy explains which capability gaps it closes and how success is measured. The three failure modes recur everywhere: assumed (not validated) skill gaps, completion rates as a false proxy for effectiveness, and strategy disconnected from real-time operational decisions. Match intervention type to gap type: coaching for skill gaps, certification for compliance, mentoring for leadership.
- **[21 teaching strategies: the unglamorous ones have the largest effect sizes](../../wiki/learning/21-teaching-strategies-backed-by-research.md)**: Rosenshine's Principles and John Hattie's Visible Learning meta-analyses point to the same conclusion — direct instruction, retrieval practice, specific process-focused feedback, and scaffolded steps beat novelty-driven methods. Practical: forcing active recall (low-stakes quizzing) outperforms passive re-reading because retrieval itself strengthens the memory trace.

### ❤️ Health
- **[Vanderbilt creates the first selective TAOK-1 inhibitor — opening a new Alzheimer's research path](../../wiki/health/vanderbilt-taok1-alzheimers-tool-compounds.md)**: Two tool compounds produced: VU6083859 selectively inhibits TAOK-1 (a protein linked to Alzheimer's with no prior selective tools), while VU6080195 unexpectedly *activates* all three TAOK family members — a serendipitous dual output that opens both inhibition and activation as research directions. No curative Alzheimer's treatment exists for the 7M+ Americans affected.
- **[Stanford discovers "ruptoblasts" — immune cells that kill by exploding](../../wiki/health/stanford-ruptoblasts-exploding-immune-cells.md)**: Planarian flatworm immune cells (ruptoblasts) self-destruct via "ruptosis" in seconds–minutes, releasing a contained cytotoxic blast that killed E. coli, human kidney cells, and mouse blood cells in lab tests. Vertebrates likely lost the mechanism because repairing blast collateral requires the stem-cell reserves flatworms have but mammals lack — yet understanding it could inform targeted anti-tumor strategies.

### 🗂️ Other
- **[Bioengineered chewing gum cut HPV 93% in patient samples](../../wiki/others/bioengineered-chewing-gum-hpv-cancer-microbes.md)**: University of Pennsylvania researchers loaded a lablab-bean extract (FRIL, an antiviral protein) and an antimicrobial peptide (protegrin) into a chewing gum; a single dose nearly eliminated Porphyromonas gingivalis and Fusobacterium nucleatum — both linked to worse head and neck cancer outcomes — while preserving beneficial oral bacteria. Still preclinical (ex vivo samples only), but the selectively targeted mechanism is the compelling part.
