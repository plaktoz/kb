## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Two Forbes pieces attack the same fuzzy idea of the AI "moat" from opposite directions.** [Don't count the AI moat twice](../../wiki/strategy/ai-moat-network-effect-vs-data-advantage-double-counting.md) says data advantages are often transient while network effects harden with scale. [When to fine-tune a small model](../../wiki/technology/when-to-build-your-own-ai-model-post-training-slm-decision-framework.md) argues that fine-tuning itself is no moat, and that the defensible assets are the data, feedback loops and evals around the model. Together they suggest that durable AI defensibility lies in the system around the model, not in the model.
