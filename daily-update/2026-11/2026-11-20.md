### 🚀 Technology

It is U.S.- and English-only, needs a paid Google AI plan, and Google is a late entrant versus OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.
- **[Apple is hiring to build its own computer-use agent](../../wiki/technology/apple-developing-ai-agent-computer-use-mac-techcrunch-nov2026.md)**: A job listing points to an early-stage in-house agent, possibly launching by the end of next year. - **[The enterprise AI ROI gap is a data and visibility problem](../../wiki/technology/enterprise-ai-roi-gap-data-governance-visibility-forbes-nov2026.md)**: Only 39% of tech leaders expect AI to improve financial performance, and successful firms spend up to 4x more on data, governance and change management. - **[IBM, NVIDIA and Fermilab co-design modular quantum systems](../../wiki/technology/ibm-fermilab-nvidia-modular-quantum-co-design-slurm-qrmi.md)**: Open-source QRMI lets Slurm schedule quantum processors like CPUs and GPUs, so hybrid jobs need no separate queue. A 79-fold accuracy gain on an iron-sulfur cluster simulation shows the quantum-classical approach working.
- **[WEF: industrial AI succeeds when work is redesigned](../../wiki/technology/ai-workforce-transformation-industrial-leaders-frontline-upskilling-wef-nov2026.md)**: Only 5% of frontline staff say the AI vision is clear, against 35% of executives. The prescription is C-suite ownership of workforce redesign, frontline-first upskilling and talent pipelines with governments.

### 🧠 Learning
- **[Do AI tutors help or hurt?
