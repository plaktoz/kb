## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Optional AI gets ignored, and unmanaged AI gets absorbed.** The Khanmigo study in [AI tutors: optional fails](../../wiki/learning/ai-tutors-optional-fail-conversation-as-assessment-kentz.md) found students rarely used a tutor they were free to skip. The workplace data in [Hubstaff's State of the Workweek](../../wiki/productivity/hubstaff-state-of-workweek-2026-ai-workload-burnout.md) shows the mirror image: time AI saves is quietly absorbed by higher expectations unless leaders deliberately allocate the "AI dividend".
