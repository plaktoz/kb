### ⚡ Productivity
- **[Time saved by AI is not value created](../../wiki/productivity/ai-time-saved-net-capacity-value-evidence-mgo-invoice-example.md)**: In MGO's invoice pilot, 100 gross hours became 60 net hours once review and correction were counted. The lesson is to separate control evidence from value evidence before scaling a use case.
- **[48% of workers fear being judged for not using AI enough](../../wiki/productivity/lse-protiviti-fairplaytalks-48-percent-fear-judged-not-using-ai-training-psychological-safety.md)**: Users save about 19% of their week and reinvest 72% of it into more work. Training quality and psychological safety separate the confident from the anxious.
- **[How to enter a flow state, seven steps](../../wiki/productivity/how-to-enter-flow-state-research-seven-steps.md)**: Flow can't be switched on, but challenge-skill balance, clear goals and fast feedback set it up. Protect a 60 to 90 minute block; interrupted workers finished faster but felt more stress.

### ❤️ Health
- **[Students are turning to AI for mental health support](../../wiki/health/students-ai-mental-health-support-university-of-bath-survey.md)**: A University of Bath survey of 2,020 students found one in four used generative AI this way, rising to over a third among those with very low wellbeing. The authors flag accuracy, privacy and crisis-response risks, and a 9.1% response rate at one university limits how far it generalizes.
- **[Personality traits and mental illness share genetics](../../wiki/health/personality-big-five-mental-illness-genetic-overlap-hitop-nyt-nov24-2026.md)**: A Nature study of about 1 million people found genetic overlap between all Big Five traits and mental disorders. Conscientiousness cuts ADHD risk but raises OCD and anorexia risk, and the links are probabilistic.
- **[The MIND diet and cognitive resilience](../../wiki/health/mind-diet-cognitive-resilience-brain-pathology-frontiers-nutrition.md)**: In 66 older adults at elevated dementia risk, high MIND adherence blunted the link between brain pathology and worse cognition, which the general Healthy Eating Index did not. Treat it as an association from a small sample.

## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Review is the hidden cost of AI speed.** [Claude Ship](../../wiki/technology/claude-ship-ai-agents-review-fix-approve-code-changes.md) exists because agent-written code floods review and approval queues. [MGO's invoice pilot](../../wiki/productivity/ai-time-saved-net-capacity-value-evidence-mgo-invoice-example.md) shows the same cost in a different field: 30 review hours and 10 correction hours were deducted from 100 gross hours saved. The [LSE/Protiviti survey](../../wiki/productivity/lse-protiviti-fairplaytalks-48-percent-fear-judged-not-using-ai-training-psychological-safety.md) adds the human side, since saved time gets reinvested into more output. Automating the approval step shrinks one cost, but only the [net-capacity](../../wiki/productivity/ai-time-saved-net-capacity-value-evidence-mgo-invoice-example.md) measure shows whether it was ever the real constraint.
