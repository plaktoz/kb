## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**AI adoption is a time-and-friction problem, not an access problem.** Stanford's work in [cognitive offloading](../../wiki/learning/stanford-cognitive-offloading-ai-learning-tradeoffs.md) finds that tools with deliberate friction are safer for learning than general-purpose chatbots. The hackathon lessons in [AI adoption needs protected learning time](../../wiki/productivity/ai-adoption-needs-protected-learning-time-hackathon-lessons.md) reach a parallel conclusion for teams: people need protected time to work through problems rather than just receive answers. Both point to **designed effort** as the ingredient that turns AI access into capability.
