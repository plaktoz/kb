## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**Stanford's "understand vs. produce" finding and Stack Overflow's "only 20% use AI on production systems" are the same rule in two settings.** Students gain from AI when it supports their own thinking, and fail to gain when it hands them answers they cannot judge. Developers likewise delegate where they can still verify output (generating familiar code, debugging) and hold back where errors hit live systems. Both point to **verifiability as the real gate on AI value**, which also explains why METR's note that Anthropic redacted its most informative evidence matters: the claim is only as useful as the public's ability to check it.
