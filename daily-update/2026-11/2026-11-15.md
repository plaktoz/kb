## 🧠 Emerging Connections
**The AI productivity gap shows up from two angles.** McKinsey's banking review finds [80% of banks report individual AI gains but only 6% see significant EBIT impact](../../wiki/finance/mckinsey-global-banking-annual-review-2026-ai-disruption-dividend.md), because committees still gate decisions. Workers describe the same gap from the ground in [AI mandates that added hours through review and rework](../../wiki/productivity/ai-mandates-added-hours-workday-review-burden-workers.md).
