## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
**The AI payoff gap** ran through everything today: markets sold off tech on doubts that hundreds of billions in data center spending will earn a return, while workplace research found organizations struggling to turn AI capacity into savings. Education researchers and Apple's CEO added a shared caution: **how and when AI is introduced matters more than whether it is used**.
