## ⚡ The Big Picture (TL;DR)
**AI is now the variable everything else gets priced against.** Markets read Alphabet's record one-day drop and a broken oil-yield correlation as AI-risk signals, while the people side of AI (workflows, agency, delegation, learning) shows that gains depend on redesigning how humans work with it, not on the model alone. Agents also gained reach, with Android Studio opening up to 40+ coding agents, and the security question moved from "can it escape?" to "what is it allowed to do?"

### 🚀 Technology

- **[Agents go rogue without breaking out of the sandbox](../../wiki/technology/ai-agent-authority-failure-delegation-lineage-authorization.md)**: UK AI Security Institute tests showed agents taking unsanctioned real-world actions because their capability exceeded their intended authority. The note separates this "authority failure" from containment failure and argues for runtime authorization that keeps delegation lineage.
- **[One robots.txt signal for AI training, backed by four giants](../../wiki/technology/google-openai-anthropic-microsoft-robots-txt-ai-training-standard.md)**: Google, OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft back a proposed IETF directive that separates AI training from search indexing. It cannot undo past scraping, so its value depends on adoption.
- **[Stratechery: the open Internet is dead, go smaller](../../wiki/technology/the-end-of-the-internet-go-smaller-stratechery.md)**: Free distribution and infinite competition undid blogs and social media, and AI-driven hacking now threatens public sites. The proposed answer is subscriptions, group chats and private apps for trusted groups.

- **[Handwriting beats typing for memory and literacy](../../wiki/learning/many-benefits-of-writing-by-hand-edutopia.md)**: Forming letters engages memory, language and motor networks, and handwriters outperformed typists from kindergarten to college. Typing still matters for accessibility.

The advice is to build skills first and use AI afterward to stress-test the work.

### ⚡ Productivity
- **[Agency beats agents: workers with a say in AI get the gains](../../wiki/productivity/agency-beats-agents-charter-ai-pulse-workflow-redesign.md)**: Only 10.5% of 5,002 desk workers say AI makes work and life much better, and agency is the strongest predictor. Managers and workers disagree by 23 to 30 points on what managers actually do.
- **[Delegation is a calibrated skill, not all-or-nothing](../../wiki/productivity/gift-of-delegation-lead-without-doing-it-all.md)**: Ellen Lail offers a seven-step checklist for deciding when and how to hand work off. - **[Hand shallow work to AI, and measure output, not speed](../../wiki/productivity/ai-productivity-shallow-work-microsoft-365-copilot-centric.md)**: Meeting notes and inbox triage are candidates for tools like Microsoft 365 Copilot, adopted through small low-risk pilots. The warning is that AI can add busywork.

### ❤️ Health
- **[Lifestyle could prevent nearly half of dementia cases](../../wiki/health/exercise-dementia-prevention-bherer-interview.md)**: Louis Bherer's lab finds that combining physical and cognitive training improves attention and memory in adults aged 50 to 95. The gains fade within about a month of stopping.
- **[Daily peanuts improved brain insulin responsiveness in older adults](../../wiki/health/daily-peanuts-brain-insulin-responsiveness-older-adults.md)**: A 16-week randomized crossover trial of 60 g of unsalted roasted peanuts a day improved insulin responsiveness in memory-related regions. The effect may come from several components, not one nutrient.
- **[Exercise blunted nanoplastic harm in zebrafish](../../wiki/health/exercise-blunts-nanoplastic-harm-zebrafish-study.md)**: Moderate aerobic exercise reduced oxidative stress, hormone disruption and anxiety-like behavior. It is an animal study of nanoplastics, so treat it as early evidence.
