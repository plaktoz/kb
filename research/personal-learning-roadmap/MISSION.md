# Mission: Personal Learning Roadmap

## Why
Most people who want to improve themselves own a list of skills to acquire but never make sustained progress because they lack a system for how to learn, not just what to learn. Completing this course replaces scattered motivation with a repeatable, evidence-based approach to skill development — so you can deliberately grow across all eight life domains (technical, communication, problem-solving, leadership, personal development, creative, financial, and social) without burning out or losing direction.

## Success looks like
- You can map any new skill goal to a concrete daily practice using the deliberate practice framework
- You can apply the Eisenhower Matrix to protect learning time from reactive urgency in your own schedule
- You can name the technical roadmap stages for Python, data science, and web development, and know which stage you are currently in
- You can demonstrate active listening techniques in a real or simulated conversation
- You have a personal financial literacy baseline: you know your budget, emergency fund status, and one investing concept
- You have designed a habit system (cue, routine, reward, identity statement) for at least one skill you are actively learning

## Constraints
- Learning from synthesized research — no access to proprietary systems or live courses
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time

## Out of scope
- Creative and artistic skills (graphic design, photography, music) — not covered in depth by the source report
- Advanced investing strategies, tax optimization, or entrepreneurship — only foundational financial literacy is in scope
- Leadership and management theory beyond interpersonal communication and emotional intelligence
