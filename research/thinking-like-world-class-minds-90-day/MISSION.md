# Mission: Thinking Like World-Class Minds

## Why
The gap between knowing information and thinking clearly is a skill gap — not a knowledge gap. By studying how 11 world-class thinkers (Musk, Feynman, Munger, Dalio, Torvalds, Jeff Dean, Knuth, Ng, Karpathy, Sutskever, Pearl) actually operate, you will build a replicable cognitive operating system: one that strips assumptions, filters noise, reasons from structure, communicates with clarity, and consistently chooses work that matters. The goal is not to admire these thinkers — it is to run the same mental processes they run, in your own work and life, starting in the next 90 days.

## Success looks like
- You can apply first-principles thinking to any problem in under 5 minutes, by listing and questioning assumptions before reaching for a solution
- You have a written Circle of Competence and use it to decide when to speak, defer, or learn before weighing in
- You use the Feynman Technique weekly — explaining a concept in plain language — and use gaps in that explanation to guide your next study session
- You maintain a list of 10 important open questions, and every new thing you learn is filtered through "does this connect to one of these?"
- You run a daily 45-minute loop: morning model review, midday decision check, evening journal — and can point to decisions you made differently because of it

## Constraints
- Learning from published articles and research — no access to the thinkers directly
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time, ~15–25 minutes per lesson
- Exercises are designed for solo practice across work, learning, and everyday life

## Out of scope
- Biographies or personal histories of the thinkers
- Technical domain knowledge (physics, ML, finance) — this course is about the *process* of thinking, not the content domains
- Team or organizational change — these practices are for individual cognitive development
