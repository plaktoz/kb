# Mission: Jev AI (TypeSafe AI)

## Why
Jev is a new category of AI model purpose-built for automated software pipelines — it returns typed, probabilistic decisions rather than text, making it 100–400× faster and cheaper than LLMs for classification, routing, and scoring tasks. Mastering Jev means you can build production automation workflows that act autonomously on high-confidence decisions and escalate uncertain ones, without human review bottlenecks.

## Success looks like
- You can explain what a System One model is and why it behaves differently from an LLM
- You can call the Jev API (or Python/JavaScript SDK) and handle Choice, Score, and Noul question types
- You can implement confidence-gated routing to decide when to act automatically vs. escalate to a human
- You can build a working support ticket router or similar mini-project in under 3 hours
- You can assess whether a given task is a good fit for Jev vs. a generative LLM

## Constraints
- Learning from official documentation, blog posts, and SDK READMEs — no proprietary system access
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time
- Weekend project scope: 2–5 hours total hands-on time

## Out of scope
- Training or fine-tuning Jev models
- Deploying to production infrastructure (cloud, containers, CI/CD)
- Comparing every LLM alternative in depth (only high-level contrast with GPT-4o/Claude)
- TypeSafe AI internals beyond what is publicly documented
