# Mission: Levers in Business and Personal Life

## Why
Most people work harder when results stall — but the real problem is they're pushing in the wrong place. When you understand where to apply force in a system, you stop trading time for money and start building compounding assets that grow without you. This course turns leverage from a vague concept into a practical operating system for your consulting work, investments, and personal decisions.

## Success looks like
- Classify any business or personal situation using Naval's labor/capital/code/media framework in under 5 minutes
- Identify the single bottleneck (Theory of Constraints) in a client's or your own system within one working session
- Apply Donella Meadows' hierarchy to diagnose whether a problem is a parameter, information-flow, or paradigm issue — and intervene at the right level
- Recognize when an opportunity is "first-order negative, second-order positive" before committing
- Design and run a weekly leverage review that tracks your compounding activities vs. pure-labor activities
- Build an asymmetric personal portfolio: know where downside is capped and upside is open

## Constraints
- Learning from research and public frameworks — no proprietary system access
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time

## Out of scope
- Detailed financial modeling or valuation methods
- Specific stock picks or investment recommendations
- Organizational change management at enterprise scale
- Coding or software-building tutorials
