# Mission: PayloadCMS

## Why
Developers who pick the wrong CMS end up either locked into SaaS pricing as they scale, or fighting an inflexible data model that doesn't match their app. Learning PayloadCMS lets you ship a content-managed Next.js app where the CMS is part of your codebase — you own the data, control the schema in TypeScript, and customize every inch of the admin panel without leaving your editor.

## Success looks like
- You can scaffold a new Payload project, define collections and globals, and reach the admin panel in under 10 minutes
- You can model any content structure using the right field types (text, array, blocks, relationship, upload)
- You can extend the admin panel with custom React components and SCSS overrides to match a brand
- You can explain the trade-offs between Payload, Contentful, Sanity, and Strapi and pick the right tool for a given project
- You can apply lifecycle hooks to run server-side business logic without touching the frontend

## Constraints
- Learning from documentation and the official Payload blog — no access to a live Payload instance during lessons
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time

## Out of scope
- Payload's enterprise/cloud offering and pricing tiers
- Payload's AI auto-embedding and A/B testing features
- Specific database internals (MongoDB vs PostgreSQL schema differences)
- Deploying Payload to production infrastructure
