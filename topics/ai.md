# AI Hub

This hub is the starting point for the vault's AI topics. It gives a short orientation per cluster and the principles that recur across them, and the detail lives in each linked topic file. It holds no sources of its own: every claim here comes from the child topics.

## Building and running AI

The core question is how agents and models behave once they act in the world. [[ai-agents]] finds that output quality depends more on the loop around a model than on the model itself, and that the human becomes the next constraint once execution is cheap. [[agentic-rag]] covers retrieval for those agents, and [[ai-writing-with-llms]] covers the narrower case of LLMs as writing partners. [[local-ai]] and [[open-weight-models]] cover where models run and who controls them. Deployability is bounded by memory and hardware whatever the license says.

## Safety, security and rules

[[ai-safety]], [[llm-security]] and [[ai-regulation]] share one logic: capability arrives faster than control, so harm is bounded by what a system can reach and who sits outside it as monitor. Offensive and defensive cyber capability are the same capability, and unenforced commitments carry little information when competitors race. [[ai-regulation]] adds that rules tend to be shaped by whoever writes them.

## People: learning and work

[[ai-in-education]], [[ai-literacy]] and [[ai-workplace-adoption]] ask whether AI access turns into learning or output. Their shared answer is that tools pay off only through complementary change, such as accountability, scaffolding, redesign and skills. Usage and assisted-output metrics drift from real capability once they become the target, and unequal starting access widens gaps.

## Infrastructure, capital and companies

[[ai-infrastructure]] and [[ai-chips]] track the physical stack, where the scarcest input sets the pace and capacity is committed before demand is known. [[ai-bubble]] and [[ai-startup-funding]] track how it is paid for: suppliers funding their own demand, capital following a power law, and valuations that rest on comparables when fundamentals are unverified. [[anthropic]] and [[openai]] are the two companies where those threads meet.

## Principles that recur across the cluster

**Constraints move rather than disappear.** Speeding one stage relocates the bottleneck, whether to verification in [[ai-workplace-adoption]], to the human in [[ai-agents]], or to the scarcest input in [[ai-infrastructure]].

**Proxies displace the outcomes they stand for.** Usage counts, completions and assisted scores replace real capability in [[ai-literacy]], [[ai-in-education]] and [[ai-workplace-adoption]], and agents optimize what is measured in [[ai-agents]].

**Correlated exposure spreads one shock to everyone.** Supplier-funded demand and concentrated holdings link companies in [[ai-bubble]], [[ai-chips]] and [[ai-startup-funding]].

For the market and macro side of these companies, see [[markets]].
