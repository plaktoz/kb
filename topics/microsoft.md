# Microsoft (MSFT)

Microsoft is the vault's main test of whether heavy AI spending turns into revenue, and its stock is still argued over on capex, OpenAI dependence, third-party capacity, funding, data-center politics and cost discipline after a 2026 round trip.

## The 2026 Round Trip

Microsoft fell about 35% from its October 2025 record close of $542.07 to a June 25 low of $352.83 [[microsoft-msft-near-record-buy-case-sep2026]]. Quartz put the June drop, a 17% monthly fall and the worst since 2000, at close to $600 billion of market value, while Seattle Times described an eight-month slide that erased about $1 trillion, so outlets measure the drawdown differently [[microsoft-msft-near-first-record-close-maia-300-oct2026]], [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]]. The recovery began with a fiscal Q4 report that lifted the shares roughly 15-16% the next day, and the July-September quarter brought a 37.7% gain, the best since 1998, after a 22.5% first-half decline [[msft-best-quarter-since-1998-azure-ai-optimism-q3-2026]], [[microsoft-msft-near-first-record-close-maia-300-oct2026]]. Seattle Times reports a record $543.45 close on October 13, while Quartz cites a $541.15 close for Monday and an intraday high of $551.13, so the two outlets differ on the day's level, and both put the year-to-date gain at about 14-15%, still behind the Nasdaq 100's 21% [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]], [[microsoft-msft-near-first-record-close-maia-300-oct2026]]. The rally then whipsawed: shares fell 2.8% to about $507 on November 2 despite the fiscal Q1 beat, jumped to a $555.80 record close on November 4 as money rotated back into AI spenders, and eased 0.8% to $531.64 on November 6 as chipmakers recovered [[microsoft-msft-down-2-8-percent-marketbeat-nov2026]], [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]], [[microsoft-msft-eases-0-8-percent-ai-rotation-chipmakers-nov6-2026]]. Heavy weights of about 9.7-9.9% in VUG, IXN and SNPE mean fund flows can add automatic buying or selling to these moves [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]].

## Azure as the Growth Engine

Azure passed $100 billion in annual revenue in FY2026, and its roughly 43% June-quarter growth beat management's 39-40% guidance, which CFO Amy Hood tied to efficiency gains and faster monetization of new capacity [[microsoft-openai-customer-concentration-risk]], [[microsoft-msft-near-record-buy-case-sep2026]]. Fiscal Q1 went further, with Azure up 48% to more than $33 billion against a 44-45% forecast, and Hood guided to 49-50% next quarter while saying demand exceeds supply [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]], [[microsoft-trims-capex-forecast-170b-gpu-capacity-delays-oct2026]]. Analysts treated that growth as capacity-limited, with Gene Munster saying constraints kept it from accelerating faster and BNP Paribas expecting 49-50% next quarter [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]]. Backlog figures need care: commercial remaining performance obligations rose 84% to $678 billion at fiscal year-end, but Barchart's fiscal Q1 figure is a 73% rise to $677 billion, and the two cover different periods so they do not show a slowdown on their own [[microsoft-azure-20-percent-cost-cut-report-msft-stock]], [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]]. The softer signal is that TIKR reports commercial bookings fell 12% [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]]. Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 30% to $63.9 billion that quarter, while Microsoft 365 commercial cloud grew 17% [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]].

## The OpenAI Dependency and Counterparty Risk

OpenAI was the only customer named in Microsoft's FY2026 filing, contributing $24.1 billion, about 7% of the $331.8 billion total and roughly a quarter of Azure [[microsoft-openai-customer-concentration-risk]]. Its $250 billion Azure commitment accounts for most of the backlog jump, since Hood said backlog grew only 25% excluding OpenAI [[microsoft-openai-customer-concentration-risk]]. The counterparty is financially stretched: OpenAI burned $3.7 billion in Q1 2026, and the April revised terms capped its revenue-share payments to Microsoft through 2030 and let it use other clouds, though Microsoft's roughly 27% stake, valued near $135 billion, partly offsets that exposure [[microsoft-openai-customer-concentration-risk]]. Tickeron lists the same concentration beside capex as a main risk to a $600 target [[microsoft-msft-technical-analysis-600-target-sep2026]]. The dependence runs the other way too: at the supplier Nscale, Microsoft and Anthropic make up about 85% of contracts, which makes the relationship's stability a concern for both sides [[microsoft-exits-nscale-uk-data-center-ipo-concentration]].

## Capex, Free Cash Flow and Hidden Commitments

Free cash flow is where the capex bill shows up. In the June quarter it fell from $25.6 billion to $19.6 billion while gross margin slipped to 67.2% from 68.6%, even as full-year operating cash flow reached $182.9 billion [[microsoft-stock-biggest-threat-ai-capex-oct2026]], [[microsoft-msft-cash-flow-dividend-azure-sustainability-2026]]. Fiscal Q1 repeated the pattern, with operating cash flow up 29% to $58 billion but free cash flow down about 27% to $18.6 billion [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]]. Sources disagree on the quarter's capex: Motley Fool gives $45.2 billion, while TIKR cites $39.4 billion of property-and-equipment spending [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]], [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]]. The calendar-2026 figure is the sharpest disagreement: Sparks read the roughly $175 billion number as an accounting change rather than a cut, Seattle Times cites $190 billion, Microsoft then trimmed its forecast to $170 billion, and a November Simply Wall St note still gives $175-190 billion [[microsoft-msft-near-record-buy-case-sep2026]], [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]], [[microsoft-trims-capex-forecast-170b-gpu-capacity-delays-oct2026]], [[microsoft-msft-pulls-back-neocloud-deals-oracle-nscale-iren-simply-wall-st]]. BNP Paribas' Stefan Slowinski warns capex could push free cash flow negative in the second half of fiscal 2027, while D.A. Davidson's Gil Luria argues Microsoft's free cash flow lets it fund the build without extra debt or equity [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]]. Michael Burry's warning of roughly $3 trillion of AI-linked exposure in off-balance-sheet structures, which do not appear as conventional debt, is the layer the reported capex figures leave out [[microsoft-burry-3-trillion-hidden-ai-risk]]. Simply Wall St's DCF starts from about $96 billion of trailing free cash flow [[microsoft-msft-fairly-priced-dcf-84-percent-five-year-run-oct2026]].

## Funding the Buildout With Debt

Microsoft priced a $25 billion, ten-tranche senior unsecured bond offering on October 29, with closing expected November 4, maturities from 2028 to 2066 and fixed coupons from 4.85% to 6.35% [[microsoft-25-billion-bond-offering-ten-tranches-ai-buildout-oct2026]]. The proceeds are not earmarked for AI and could fund buybacks, acquisitions or debt repayment, and Hood said the company would supplement cash generation with other funding when conditions are attractive [[microsoft-25-billion-bond-offering-ten-tranches-ai-buildout-oct2026]]. The sources frame it differently: one stresses the AAA rating and positive free cash flow as borrowing from strength, while TIKR, noting it was the first U.S. investment-grade sale since 2017, says it could look like a bridge to a slower payoff if Azure growth does not keep pace with spending [[microsoft-25-billion-bond-offering-ten-tranches-ai-buildout-oct2026]], [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]]. MarketBeat ties the post-earnings slide to the bond, to falling free cash flow and to insider selling [[microsoft-msft-down-2-8-percent-marketbeat-nov2026]].

## Third-Party Capacity and the Neocloud Pullback

The October capex trim came from delay rather than restraint. Third-party GPU-as-a-Service capacity slipped into the second half of the fiscal year, Hood expects spending to accelerate once it arrives, and Business Insider attributes broader data-center delays mostly to power shortages and local opposition [[microsoft-trims-capex-forecast-170b-gpu-capacity-delays-oct2026]]. The Nscale case shows the same fragility: Microsoft reportedly exited a planned Essex site without a stated reason, and local authorities reportedly warned the site might not get power until the 2030s [[microsoft-exits-nscale-uk-data-center-ipo-concentration]]. By early November the pattern looked broader. Simply Wall St reports Microsoft winding down several neocloud deals, not renewing its Oracle arrangement and exiting Nscale and IREN in favor of in-house capacity that matches buildout to its bond-funded program [[microsoft-msft-pulls-back-neocloud-deals-oracle-nscale-iren-simply-wall-st]]. Bloomberg's report, relayed by Investing.com, adds that Microsoft is tightening oversight of IREN, Nebius and Nscale projects after IREN's Texas campus suffered cabling problems, an April generator fire and August hardware delays, and IREN shares fell 18% in a broad tech selloff [[iren-plunges-18-microsoft-neocloud-delays-oversight]]. Simply Wall St reads the pullback as execution risk rather than weaker AI demand, and Investing.com's account notes compute demand still exceeds supply, which makes Gartner's estimate that 80% of neocloud providers could be consolidated or shut down by the end of the decade a useful measure of how fragile this layer is [[microsoft-msft-pulls-back-neocloud-deals-oracle-nscale-iren-simply-wall-st]], [[iren-plunges-18-microsoft-neocloud-delays-oversight]].

## Maia 300 and the Custom Silicon Bet

Microsoft unveiled Maia 300 on October 12 as its third-generation custom AI chip, built with TSMC, and said it will become the primary AI inference system for its products, with large-scale deployment starting in 2027 [[microsoft-msft-near-first-record-close-maia-300-oct2026]], [[microsoft-maia-300-custom-ai-chip-unveiled-nvidia-reliance]]. The efficiency claim carries the argument, with 40% more AI output per unit of power than leading GPU systems and up to seven times the current generation's output at the full-rack level [[microsoft-maia-300-custom-ai-chip-unveiled-nvidia-reliance]], [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]]. Simply Wall St's DCF notes the multi-gigawatt rollout is meant to control infrastructure costs, which could change future cash generation [[microsoft-msft-fairly-priced-dcf-84-percent-five-year-run-oct2026]]. The shift will be gradual: Maia 200 ran in only two U.S. data centers as of summer and cannot be rented to outside customers, Evercore ISI estimates Maia will be about 10% of Microsoft's AI chip spending by early 2027, and Nvidia Vera Rubin systems are still planned [[microsoft-maia-300-custom-ai-chip-unveiled-nvidia-reliance]].

## Copilot and the Agent Platform

Microsoft folded roughly 80 Copilot products into one app, with Autopilot, a background agent that draws on 30 days of work history and calendar data, is off by default and runs on usage-based credits once activated [[microsoft-copilot-unified-app-autopilot]]. Cowork, Code and Autopilot are billed by usage while Microsoft 365 Copilot stays at $30 per user per month [[microsoft-copilot-refresh-stock-lag-megacaps]]. Paid seats rose from 20 million to over 30 million and net additions more than doubled sequentially, yet fewer than 7% of more than 450 million commercial Office 365 seats pay for Copilot, and Copilot revenue is not reported separately [[microsoft-copilot-refresh-stock-lag-megacaps]], [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]], [[jim-cramer-msft-big-move-copilot-oct2026]]. Tickeron cites 15 million paid seats, which conflicts with the 30 million figure from the company's own reporting [[microsoft-msft-technical-analysis-600-target-sep2026]]. Agent 365 had nearly 40 million registered agents, and OpenAI's Dots integration into it was one of the few positive read-throughs Piper Sandler's analyst cited [[msft-best-quarter-since-1998-azure-ai-optimism-q3-2026]]. MarketBeat adds that usage-based Copilot customers rose more than 75% last quarter and daily users grew fivefold year over year, while Barchart notes a lower-cost model for routing and classification priced at $0.042 per million input tokens [[microsoft-msft-down-2-8-percent-marketbeat-nov2026]], [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]]. Vertical plugins from Resilinc, Wood Mackenzie, DrugBank and Trellis extend Copilot into supply-chain, energy, healthcare and legal workflows, which Simply Wall St reads as turning Microsoft's AI stack into an operating layer [[microsoft-msft-azure-copilot-plugins-operating-layer-narrative-oct2026]].

## Agent Security and Dynamic Permissions

Microsoft's newest push is into agent security. Guardian Agents and Playbooks extend Project Perception, introduced in June with red, blue and green team agents, and the company argues that frontier models are unpredictable and trained to complete tasks rather than follow organizational norms, so enterprises should treat agents as potential insider threats and keep their monitoring independent of the agent suppliers [[microsoft-guardian-agents-ai-agents-insider-threats]], [[microsoft-dynamic-permissions-guardian-agents-project-perception]]. The position invites a conflict-of-interest reading, since Microsoft invests in OpenAI and Anthropic while selling competing agent security, and Defender now detects up to 60 types of employee-brought agents, including Claude Code and Codex [[microsoft-guardian-agents-ai-agents-insider-threats]]. The control model grants task-scoped, time-limited Dynamic Permissions, with each agent holding an Entra Agent ID separate from its sponsor, and a continuously updated risk score drives Dynamic Conditional Access that escalates from preserving reads and blocking writes to narrowing access, requiring approval and isolating the runtime [[microsoft-dynamic-permissions-guardian-agents-project-perception]]. The residual gap is that correct access does not stop a malicious instruction hidden in an external document, which is why risk scoring sits above the permissions [[microsoft-dynamic-permissions-guardian-agents-project-perception]], [[microsoft-guardian-agents-ai-agents-insider-threats]].

## Windows, Devices and On-Device AI

The soft spot in the June quarter was the PC side, with Windows OEM and Devices revenue down 7% while Azure grew 43% [[microsoft-windows-surface-event-oct-7-2026-local-ai]]. The October 7 Windows and Surface event, where Nadella, Windows chief Pavan Davuluri and Nvidia's Jensen Huang discussed local AI on the RTX Spark platform, was framed as a test of whether on-device AI gives customers a reason to upgrade [[microsoft-windows-surface-event-oct-7-2026-local-ai]]. That source's view is that the event more likely reinforces the growth story than changes the earnings outlook, and that adoption rather than demos will decide its effect [[microsoft-windows-surface-event-oct-7-2026-local-ai]]. New Surface PCs run AI agents locally, which Microsoft presents as a security feature [[microsoft-record-close-4-trillion-maia-300-seattle-times-oct2026]], and in the September quarter Devices and Consumer fell 3% to $15.5 billion [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]].

## Cost Cuts and the Margin Trade-off

A report that Azure leaders were asked to find cost reductions of at least 20% is disputed: Microsoft says Azure leadership has not directed teams to develop cost-cutting plans, and the report centers on Azure president Girish Bablani's roughly 11,500-person unit [[microsoft-azure-20-percent-cost-cut-report-msft-stock]]. Separately, Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees, roughly 27% of its workforce, for staff at internal level 70 and below in the U.S., Canada, India and the U.K. [[microsoft-msft-voluntary-buyouts-60000-employees-earnings-eve-oct2026]]. Eligibility is not departure, and severance is paid before any savings arrive, so Business Insider's expectation that at most 5% of eligible employees will accept matters more than the headline number, with headcount already down 2% in the quarter [[microsoft-msft-voluntary-buyouts-60000-employees-earnings-eve-oct2026]], [[microsoft-trims-capex-forecast-170b-gpu-capacity-delays-oct2026]]. Margins show the trade-off: operating margin rose to 50% in fiscal Q1, but Microsoft Cloud gross margin slipped to 66% as lower-margin Azure grew as a share of the mix and is guided to roughly 65% in Q2 [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]], [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]].

## AI, Skills and the Junior-Engineer Ladder

Microsoft's PRAISE program with Carnegie Mellon University is the vault's clearest case of AI changing how skills are built inside the company. It grew out of Mark Russinovich's observation that AI tools help senior engineers more than juniors, so early-career engineers take CMU courses while senior preceptors mentor them at work [[microsoft-cmu-praise-ai-accelerated-software-engineering-early-career]]. The underlying reason, as Michael Hilton puts it in the same piece, is that juniors once built expertise through tasks AI now absorbs, so mentorship has to replace that practice deliberately [[microsoft-cmu-praise-ai-accelerated-software-engineering-early-career]]. The concern reaches outside the company too: Microsoft and Cyberlite's expanded AI Classroom Toolkit teaches students to judge AI outputs by their claims and sources, citing figures that 53% of educators and 77% of students have had no AI training [[microsoft-ai-classroom-toolkit-media-ai-literacy]].

## Valuation and Price Discipline

The multiple depends on which earnings measure is used. Before the report Sparks put the stock at about 29x earnings against roughly 40x at the October 2025 peak, and Trefis put its 30x trailing multiple against 22.7x for the S&P 500 [[microsoft-msft-near-record-buy-case-sep2026]], [[microsoft-stock-biggest-threat-ai-capex-oct2026]]. After the print Barchart cites a forward P/E of 27.03x below a sector median of 29.85x, but a PEG of 1.67x against 1.19x means the discount disappears once growth is counted, and MarketBeat's P/E of 26.47 with a PEG of 1.61 sits close to that view [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]], [[microsoft-msft-down-2-8-percent-marketbeat-nov2026]]. Ackman's note puts the forward multiple near 25x after earnings against a historical average around 30x, which he reads as re-rated but not cheap [[ackman-microsoft-ai-buy-valuation-aug2026]]. Simply Wall St's DCF finds the stock roughly fairly valued at $543.45 after an 83.7% five-year total return, while its community fair values run from 8% upside to 26% downside [[microsoft-msft-fairly-priced-dcf-84-percent-five-year-run-oct2026]], [[microsoft-msft-azure-copilot-plugins-operating-layer-narrative-oct2026]]. Sparks gives Azure an A, spending and margins a C+ and the outlook a B+, but at about 29x adjusted earnings of roughly $18.04 he still wants a price near $465 rather than $530 [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]]. TIKR's mid-case 2031 target of about $1,290 implies a 143% total return but rests on 19% revenue compounding and 40% net margins [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]].

Before the report, TD Cowen, Mizuho, Citi and UBS raised targets to $625, $600, $636 and $675, with TD Cowen pointing to third-party Azure data it called the strongest in its history [[msft-price-target-hikes-td-cowen-azure-beat-potential-oct2026]]. Piper Sandler lifted its target from $550 to $610 while keeping an Overweight rating [[msft-piper-sandler-price-target-610-sep2026]]. After the print the consensus figures differ by source: MarketBeat's "Moderate Buy" averages $609.54 across 50 ratings, Barchart cites $594.15, and Benzinga reports $619.13 with Bernstein at $722, Citigroup at $680, RBC at $640 and BNP Paribas at $632 [[microsoft-msft-down-2-8-percent-marketbeat-nov2026]], [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]], [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]]. TIKR reports Hold ratings rising to five from two, which is a reminder that the bullish consensus is not unanimous [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]]. Pershing Square's roughly $2.4 billion stake of about 6.2 million shares is called a core holding, but its 13F data reflect June 30 positioning and the author rates the shares a hold [[ackman-microsoft-ai-buy-valuation-aug2026]]. Gene Munster is the sceptical voice in the post-earnings group, questioning how long software growth acceleration can last as AI changes knowledge-worker software demand [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]].

## Data-Center Politics

The recent congressional elections added a political variable. Dan Ives called Democratic control of Congress a big hurdle for the AI buildout that could slow U.S. data-center development over several years, yet kept Microsoft as his top hyperscaler pick for 2027, and the stock still rose about 6% the next day [[microsoft-msft-jumps-6-percent-analysts-azure-data-center-politics-nov2026]]. Simply Wall St's neocloud note adds that regulators, cost pressures, sustainability rules and anti-AI protests are reshaping project economics, and its October note lists tighter regulatory and tax treatment of data-center commitments as a risk [[microsoft-msft-pulls-back-neocloud-deals-oracle-nscale-iren-simply-wall-st]], [[microsoft-msft-azure-copilot-plugins-operating-layer-narrative-oct2026]].

## Cautionary Signals and Regulatory Overhang

The skeptical signals are quieter but persistent. Insiders sold $71.4 million of stock over three months, 29 tracked gurus trimmed against 22 adding, hedge-fund holders slipped from 282 to 273 in the second quarter, and short interest near 1% shows no real bearish bet [[msft-piper-sandler-price-target-610-sep2026]], [[microsoft-msft-cash-flow-dividend-azure-sustainability-2026]]. An Italy-led probe, with Norway and Denmark and backed by the EU consumer-protection network, examines in-game virtual currencies at Microsoft-owned gaming companies, including Activision Blizzard, and adds a regulatory overhang [[microsoft-msft-eu-gaming-probe-overbought-rsi-oct2026]]. Jim Cramer's "big move coming" post offered no price target or timeframe, and he owns the shares through a charitable trust, so it deserves less weight than its tone suggests [[jim-cramer-msft-big-move-copilot-oct2026]].

## Technical Levels

Before the report the chart set the ceiling: the 52-week high near $553 was the resistance to clear before $600 was in play, and an August golden cross confirmed the longer uptrend [[microsoft-msft-technical-analysis-600-target-sep2026]], [[microsoft-msft-eu-gaming-probe-overbought-rsi-oct2026]]. The stock was stretched, breaking above its upper Bollinger Band on September 25, and on October 8 sat 4.4% above its 20-day average with an RSI of 70.76, just past the overbought line [[microsoft-msft-technical-analysis-600-target-sep2026]], [[microsoft-msft-eu-gaming-probe-overbought-rsi-oct2026]]. Support sat at the 50-day average near $498.94, then $486 [[microsoft-msft-eu-gaming-probe-overbought-rsi-oct2026]].

## The Fiscal Q1 Result and the Q2 Test

The September-quarter report landed on October 28 and beat on the headline numbers, with revenue of $92.28 billion against a consensus near $90.64 billion, adjusted EPS of $4.89 and GAAP EPS of $4.92, up 32% [[microsoft-q1-fy2027-beat-how-to-think-about-msft-stock]], [[microsoft-msft-eases-0-8-percent-ai-rotation-chipmakers-nov6-2026]]. Pre-report EPS estimates were quoted anywhere from $4.70 to $4.73, so the beat size depends on which consensus is used [[microsoft-windows-surface-event-oct-7-2026-local-ai]], [[microsoft-msft-eu-gaming-probe-overbought-rsi-oct2026]]. Sparks had set two conditions in September, Azure near 45% growth and free cash flow resuming growth. Azure cleared the first while free cash flow fell, and Cramer's scorecard also required a close above $549.20, which the November 4 record close briefly cleared before the stock slipped back toward $530 [[microsoft-msft-near-record-buy-case-sep2026]], [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]], [[jim-cramer-msft-big-move-copilot-oct2026]]. The next test is fiscal Q2, with revenue guided to $96-97.1 billion, Azure growth of 49% or better, capex above $50 billion and Microsoft Cloud gross margin near 65%, and TIKR argues that a miss with capex still climbing would make the bond sale look like a bridge to a slower payoff [[microsoft-fy27-q1-report-card-azure-a-fcf-c-plus-motley-fool]], [[microsoft-25-billion-bonds-azure-beat-tikr-2031-outlook]].

## Dividend Growth

The quarterly payout has risen from $0.39 to $0.98 over ten years, a 151% increase that includes a recent 8% hike, and the streak of annual raises runs past 20 years [[microsoft-msft-dividend-growth-ai-stock]], [[microsoft-msft-cash-flow-dividend-azure-sustainability-2026]]. Operating cash flow of $182.9 billion in fiscal 2026, up more than 34% from $136.2 billion, dwarfs the dividend bill, so the streak keeps a wide cushion even as free cash flow is squeezed [[microsoft-msft-cash-flow-dividend-azure-sustainability-2026]]. **T1. **T2. **T3. A buyer's return depends on what the price already assumes** — Once good news is in the price, further gains require outcomes that beat those expectations, and a stock near its highs or on a full multiple leaves little margin for error. · *model:* [[Expectations Investing]]
