## Summary
[[IBM]] advanced its AI strategy on three fronts within days: a multi-year [[AMD]] partnership hosting Instinct MI300X GPU clusters for [[Zyphra]], a £320M ($430M) UK Ministry of Defence contract to modernize logistics with the DEEAMS AI platform, and the open-source release of [[Granite 4.0]] hybrid Mamba-Transformer models that cut memory needs by over 70%. IBM also became the first company to earn ISO 42001 AI security certification, with EY and Lockheed Martin already trialing the new models, ahead of its next quarterly report.

## Core Concepts
- [[IBM]]: Advancing its AI strategy simultaneously via cloud partnerships, government contracts, and open model releases.
- [[AMD]]: Multi-year collaboration hosting a major AMD Instinct MI300X GPU cluster on IBM's cloud for [[Zyphra]], one of the largest AMD-based AI training platforms available.
- [[Granite 4.0]]: IBM's open-source (Apache 2.0) hybrid Mamba-Transformer LLM family, cutting memory requirements over 70%.
- [[UK Ministry of Defence]]: Awarded IBM UK a £320M ($430M) contract for DEEAMS, an AI logistics platform serving 65,000+ users.
- [[ISO 42001]]: AI security/management certification IBM was the first organization to receive.

## Key Takeaways
- **AMD Cloud Deal**: Multi-year partnership hosts an AMD Instinct MI300X GPU cluster for Zyphra — one of the largest AMD-based AI training platforms available.
- **UK Defense Contract**: £320M ($430M) DEEAMS logistics platform for the UK Ministry of Defence, serving 65,000+ users.
- **Granite 4.0 Architecture**: Hybrid Mamba+Transformer design cuts memory requirements over 70%, enabling cheaper hardware deployment.
- **Open Licensing**: Released under Apache 2.0; EY and Lockheed Martin already trialing the models.
- **First-Mover Certification**: IBM is the first organization certified ISO 42001 for AI security.
- **Guidance Held**: IBM maintains full-year guidance of 5%+ constant-currency revenue growth and $13.5B free cash flow.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core narrative of IBM's cluster of AI announcements?
**A**: IBM used a compressed window to advance its AI strategy simultaneously across infrastructure (AMD cloud partnership), government/defense (UK MoD contract), and open-source models (Granite 4.0), positioning itself as a broad AI contender ahead of earnings.

**Q2**: What technical innovation does Granite 4.0 introduce, and why does it matter?
**A**: Granite 4.0 uses a hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture that cuts memory requirements by over 70%, letting businesses run sophisticated AI on cheaper hardware — a cost-efficiency edge versus pure-transformer competitors.

**Q3**: How do the AMD partnership and UK defense contract fit into IBM's broader AI positioning?
**A**: They show IBM monetizing AI on two fronts at once — infrastructure-as-a-service for AI labs like Zyphra via AMD GPUs, and applied AI for regulated government/defense clients via DEEAMS — reinforcing IBM's hybrid cloud and sovereignty strategy.
