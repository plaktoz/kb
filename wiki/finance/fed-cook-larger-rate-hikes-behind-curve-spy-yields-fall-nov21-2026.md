## Summary
Fed Governor Lisa Cook warned on November 20, 2026 that "additional and larger rate increases" may be needed if the Fed falls behind on inflation. Yet SPY fell about 4% while Treasury yields dropped, suggesting bond traders fear a slowdown more than Cook's hawkishness. The December 8-9 meeting will show which view wins.

## Core Concepts
- [[Lisa Cook]]: Fed governor who backed the September hike and dissented in October for another increase.
- [[Federal Reserve]] rate policy: single quarter-point hike in September, range 3.75% to 4.00%.
- "Behind the curve": letting inflation outrun rates, forcing faster hikes later.
- [[SPY ETF]] concentration: NVIDIA, Apple and Microsoft make up about 19% of the fund.
- [[Treasury Yields]]: 2-year fell to 4.63%, 10-year to 4.92%, despite a hawkish speech.
- [[AI Data Center Buildout]]: more than $2 trillion in announced projects, which Cook says policy is failing to slow.

## Key Takeaways
- **Cook's Warning**: Falling behind could require "additional and larger" hikes later to catch up.
- **Inflation Backdrop**: Inflation above 2% target for 67 months; October PCE estimated at 3.8%, core 3.2%.
- **Market Reaction**: S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910.39; Nasdaq dropped 5.28%.
- **Bond Signal**: Yields fell during the speech, implying slowdown fears over hike fears.
- **December Odds**: Futures priced about 92% odds of a quarter-point hike.
- **Weak Spots in Argument**: Cook was outvoted in October and relies on equity-supported spending.
- **Tests to Watch**: A hike above 25bp or more dissenters favors Cook; a single 25bp hike favors bonds.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Delayed tightening costs more** — If policy stays too loose while inflation persists, the eventual correction must be larger and faster than early action would have been. · *model:* [[Second-Order Effects]]
→ explains: Cook's Warning, Inflation Backdrop

**T2. Markets reveal beliefs through prices** — Yield moves during a policy speech show what investors believe about growth, independent of what the speaker argues. · *model:* [[Signal vs. Noise]]
→ explains: Bond Signal, Tests to Watch

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Lisa Cook's central warning?
**A**: That the Fed's single September hike is unlikely to change inflation's path, so "additional and larger rate increases" may be needed later to catch up if it falls behind.

**Q2**: What market evidence contradicts the hawkish reading of her speech?
**A**: The 2-year yield fell to 4.63% from 4.76% and the 10-year to 4.92% from 5.04% during the speech, suggesting investors fear a slowdown more than Cook's hikes.

**Q3**: How does the article suggest judging whose view is winning?
**A**: Watch the December 9 decision: a hike above a quarter point or more dissent supports Cook, while a single quarter-point hike with the 2-year under 4.76% supports the bond market's slowdown view.
