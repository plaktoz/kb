## Summary
The S&P 500 fell 2% on Thursday Nov 19, 2026 for a sixth straight loss, its longest streak in two and a half years, as AI-linked stocks kept sliding. A strong debut for Anthropic's IPO lifted the market early but the gains faded. The Dow dropped 1,101 points and more than 90% of S&P 500 stocks fell.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: priced at $83 (above the $70-$78 range), surged above $115, closed at $97.17, up 17.1%.
- **[[AI Bubble]] worries**: doubts that AI profits justify massive [[Data Center]] investment, plus high interest rates, midterm-driven regulation fears, and [[Jensen Huang]]'s resignation as Nvidia CEO.
- **[[Nvidia]], [[Micron Technology]], [[CoreWeave]]**: all reversed early gains into losses ("white-knuckle period", per analyst Dan Ives).
- **[[Palo Alto Networks]]**: up 6.4% on earnings beat and a $4.5 billion all-stock deal for Applied Compute.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: the 10-year yield eased to 5.04% from 5.07% as investors sought safety.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Moves**: S&P 500 -2% to 7,175.83; Dow -1,101 to 49,097.06; Nasdaq -2.3%.
- **Drawdown**: S&P 500 is 8.2% below its all-time high set last month.
- **Breadth**: More than 90% of S&P 500 stocks fell.
- **Anthropic Debut**: Priced at $83, closed at $97.17, up 17.1%.
- **Reversal Pattern**: Micron, CoreWeave and Nvidia flipped from morning gains to losses.
- **Retail Misses**: Williams-Sonoma fell 15.2%; Walmart fell 1% despite beating estimates.
- **Bond Market**: 10-year yield fell to 5.04% amid weaker jobless claims and mid-Atlantic manufacturing data.
- **Abroad**: Nikkei +0.7%, Kospi +1.4%, DAX -1.1%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the central story of Wall Street's session on Nov 19, 2026?
**A**: AI-related stocks kept falling on worries the AI boom may be over, driving a sixth straight S&P 500 loss and a 1,101-point Dow drop, even though Anthropic's IPO was well received.

**Q2**: How did Anthropic's IPO trade, and what did AI stocks do alongside it?
**A**: It priced at $83, above the $70-$78 range, topped $115 intraday and closed at $97.17 (+17.1%). Micron, CoreWeave and Nvidia all reversed morning gains into losses.

**Q3**: What succession of worries has weighed on AI stocks, according to the article?
**A**: First bubble fears and doubts about AI profits, then high interest rates, then regulation concerns after Democratic midterm wins, and most recently Jensen Huang's sudden resignation as Nvidia CEO.
