## Summary

[[CoreWeave]]'s credit default swap spreads crossed 1,000 basis points — the threshold for distressed debt — and bond yields on its 2031 notes topped 15%, as a broader investor revolt against AI infrastructure debt intensified. With over $51 billion in debt and obligations and negative free cash flow, CoreWeave is the center of a stress event that is spreading to Oracle and other AI-linked names, raising contagion concerns.

## Core Concepts

- **[[CoreWeave]]** — Neocloud company; took on $51B+ in debt and obligations to build AI infrastructure; backed by Nvidia
- **[[Credit Default Swaps]] (CDS)** — Insurance against default; spread above 1,000 bps signals distress; CoreWeave's 5-year CDS topped 1,140 bps
- **[[Neocloud]]** — Companies (CoreWeave, Nebius, Iren) that rent GPU compute built on heavy debt financing
- **Backlog vs. cash flow gap** — CoreWeave has $100B+ contracted backlog (2/3 with investment-grade counterparts: Microsoft, Meta, IBM, Caterpillar) but heavily negative free cash flow today
- **Self-fulfilling prophecy risk** — Jim Reid (Deutsche Bank): credit distress concerns can trigger broader issues; Oracle's CDS spiked ~10% in sympathy
- **CEO Mike Intrator** — Dismissed market moves; said backlog "more than supports all of our debt obligations"
- **Evercore ISI** — Described market as saying "no mas to AI debt without operating cash flow support"

## Key Takeaways

- **CDS threshold breached**: Spread above 1,000 bps = widely recognized credit distress signal.
- **Bond yield**: CoreWeave's 2031 bonds hit 15.78% yield-to-maturity during the episode.
- **Backlog defense**: $100B+ backlog, ~2/3 investment-grade, cited as cushion against default concerns.
- **Cash flow is the issue**: Negative free cash flow must eventually turn; the market is questioning timing.
- **Contagion risk**: Oracle CDS +10% in a week; AMD, Micron sold off in sympathy.
- **Self-fulfilling dynamics**: A downgrade from investment-grade could force IG-only investors to sell, worsening the spiral.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Credit stress can become self-fulfilling before fundamentals deteriorate.** Rising CDS spreads and bond yields increase refinancing costs, which worsens the cash flow problem the market is pricing in. · *model:* [[Reflexivity]]
→ explains: CDS threshold breached, Contagion risk

**T2. Free cash flow, not backlog, determines solvency.** Contracted revenue is only valuable if cash arrives before debt obligations come due; the gap between recognition and receipt is the core risk. · *model:* [[Duration Risk]]

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What level of CDS spread indicates credit distress, and where did CoreWeave's stand?
**A**: A spread above 1,000 basis points is widely viewed as a distress signal; CoreWeave's five-year CDS topped 1,140 bps during the episode.

**Q2**: Why does CoreWeave's large backlog not fully resolve investor concerns?
**A**: The backlog is contracted future revenue, but CoreWeave is currently deeply free-cash-flow negative; the market is questioning whether cash will arrive before debt obligations require repayment.

**Q3**: What is the self-fulfilling prophecy risk described in the article?
**A**: A potential investment-grade downgrade would force IG-only investors to sell CoreWeave (or related) bonds, driving yields higher, increasing borrowing costs, and making the original default concern more likely to materialize.
