## Summary

[[Microsoft]] shares eased 0.80% to $531.64 on November 6 as money rotated back into chipmakers hit hard the day before, reversing the earlier-week trade that sent Microsoft to a record close. Fiscal Q1 results were strong, and the stock trades about 11% below a GuruFocus GF Value. The key test is whether [[Azure]] growth holds in the high 40s if data center construction slows.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] (MSFT)**: $531.64 at 10:10 a.m. ET on November 6, after a 6.6% jump to a $555.80 record close on November 4.
- **[[AI Rotation]]**: Investors bought AI spenders and sold AI suppliers earlier in the week, then reversed.
- **[[Azure]]**: Growth accelerated to 48% in fiscal Q1.
- **[[GuruFocus GF Value]]**: GF Score 99/100 and a GF Value of $599.97, with shares roughly 28 times earnings.
- **[[Data Center Construction]]**: A slowdown is the risk to Azure's growth rate.

## Key Takeaways

- **Price Move**: Down 0.80% to $531.64 as chipmakers recovered.
- **Earlier Rally**: Up 6.6% on November 4 to a $555.80 record close.
- **Quarter**: Revenue up 19% to $92.3 billion; diluted EPS $4.92, up 32%.
- **Valuation**: About 11% below GF Value of $599.97, near 28 times earnings.
- **Key Test**: Whether Azure growth stays in the high 40s if construction slows.
