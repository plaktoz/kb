## Summary

Both [[Microsoft]] (MSFT) and [[Meta Platforms]] (META) are down in 2026 — Meta roughly 10% and Microsoft over 20% — despite both being major [[AI hyperscaler]]s. Microsoft's decline is considered less justified given its AI annual revenue run rate of $37 billion (up 123% YoY) and 40% cloud revenue growth, while Meta faces skepticism over its AI spending with no clear monetization path. The author favors Microsoft as the stronger buy due to its proven execution in AI and cloud.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] (MSFT)**: Down 20%+ in 2026 despite strong AI and cloud execution; AI revenue run rate at $37B, up 123% YoY.
- **[[Meta Platforms]] (META)**: Down ~10%; market skeptical of massive AI spending, drawing comparisons to the failed [[Metaverse]] bet.
- **[[AI Hyperscalers]]**: Large-scale companies (Microsoft, Meta) investing heavily in AI infrastructure and data centers.
- **[[Llama]]**: Meta's open-source AI model, cited as lacking broad adoption.
- **[[Azure]] / Cloud Revenue**: Microsoft's cloud segment growing 40% YoY, underpinning the AI monetization thesis.
- **[[AR Glasses]]**: Meta's long-term hardware bet paired with a superintelligence model as a future monetization path.
- **[[Forward P/E]]**: Both stocks trading below the S&P 500's ~21.5x forward earnings multiple, suggesting relative undervaluation.

## Key Takeaways

- **MSFT decline**: Microsoft down 20%+ despite AI run rate of $37B, up 123% YoY.
- **META skepticism**: Market compares Meta's AI spending to its failed metaverse pivot.
- **Llama gap**: Meta's AI model lacks clear monetization or broad enterprise adoption.
- **Meta catalyst**: Rumored cloud computing launch could clarify Meta's data center ROI.
- **Valuation**: Both trade below S&P 500's ~21.5x forward earnings multiple.
- **Author preference**: Microsoft favored as the "no-brainer" buy for its execution track record.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mr. Market]]**: Both stocks are priced below fundamentals — Microsoft's strong execution is being penalized by macro sentiment rather than business reality, illustrating how market prices can diverge sharply from intrinsic value in the short term.
- **[[Asymmetric Risk]]**: Microsoft offers a lower-risk AI bet with proven revenue conversion, while Meta's upside is larger but dependent on unproven monetization paths — classic asymmetric payoff comparison between execution certainty and optionality.
