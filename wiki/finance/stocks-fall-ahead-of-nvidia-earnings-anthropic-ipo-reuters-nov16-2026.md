## Summary

Global stocks fell on Nov 16, led by a 5.3% drop in US chipmakers, with [[Nvidia]] earnings and [[Anthropic]]'s IPO this week seen as tests of whether the AI-fuelled bull market can continue. Markets still price a Fed rate hike in December, while oil fell on signs of recovering flows through the [[Strait of Hormuz]].

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]] earnings**: Reported after the bell Tuesday; stock down 4.1% Monday and nearly 24% this month after CEO [[Jensen Huang]]'s departure. Analysts expect 90% earnings growth on $109 billion revenue.
- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: Expected to raise up to $80 billion at a near $1.5 trillion valuation, pricing Wednesday and trading Thursday on Nasdaq as ANT.O.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] outlook**: Futures imply a quarter-point hike in December and at least three hikes by end of 2027; Chair Kevin Warsh's comments did not dent expectations.
- **Oil and geopolitics**: US crude fell 3.59% to $85.38 and Brent to $92.20 on recovering Hormuz flows, despite tanker incidents.
- **Rates and commodities**: 10-year yield 5.129%; gold hit its lowest since October 2025 intraday; silver fell 3.68%.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index moves**: S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59; Nasdaq 2.49%; Dow 1.41%.
- **Global**: MSCI broadest index fell 1.8%; Korea fell 3.6% and Taiwan 6% overnight.
- **Barometer**: Analysts see Nvidia earnings and the Anthropic IPO as signals for the correction.
- **Succession worry**: Investors must value Nvidia's FY2028-29 possibly without its architect.
- **Dollar**: The dollar index hit its highest since April 2025, at 103.60.
- **Rate path**: Jefferies says a ceasefire and oil below $80 would remove the case for 2027 hikes.
- **Upcoming**: Fed October meeting minutes are due Wednesday.
