## Summary

US stocks mostly slipped on Aug. 24 after Treasury Secretary [[Scott Bessent]] announced "Operation Economic Outcast," a sweeping new sanctions campaign aimed at severing Iran's economic lifelines and any country that trades with it — raising fresh questions about US-China friction given China's role as Iran's primary oil buyer.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Operation Economic Outcast]]**: Bessent's sanctions package targeting Iran and any nation doing business with it, with countries tied to the regime threatened with removal "from the US dollar system."
- **US-Canada trade breakdown**: Talks collapsed over the weekend after the US imposed new tariffs on Canadian goods; PM [[Mark Carney]] suspended negotiations and vowed retaliatory tariffs.
- **Tariff escalation**: President Trump vowed to raise tariffs on Canadian autos, auto parts, and steel to 50% starting January 1, 2027.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index moves**: S&P 500 -0.3%, Nasdaq Composite -0.6%, Dow +0.2% (the lone index in the green) after the major indexes posted weekly declines.
- **Sanctions scope**: Any country dealing economically with Iran risks removal from the US dollar system — a threat explicitly extended beyond Iran itself.
- **Auto sector hit**: GM and Ford shares fell after Trump's 50% tariff threat on Canadian autos, auto parts, and steel.
- **Next catalyst flagged**: Nvidia's Wednesday earnings report identified as a pivotal moment for the AI trade amid this macro backdrop.
