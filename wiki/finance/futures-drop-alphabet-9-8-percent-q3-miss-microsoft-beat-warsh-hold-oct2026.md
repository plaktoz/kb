## Summary

U.S. stock index futures fell Wednesday evening as [[Alphabet]] dropped 9.8% after-hours on a wide Q3 profit miss driven by heavy AI spending, while [[Microsoft]] rose 2.7% on an earnings beat and stronger Azure growth. Earlier, the [[Federal Reserve]] held rates at 4.0% with two dissents favoring a hike, and Chair [[Kevin Warsh]]'s hawkish-sounding remarks pushed the 10-year yield back above 5%. Attention now turns to Q3 GDP and September PCE data.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Stock Futures]]**: [[Nasdaq-100]] futures -1.2% to 29,619.50, [[S&P 500]] futures -0.5% to 7,743.25, [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] futures -0.06%.
- **[[Alphabet]] Q3 earnings**: Revenue edged past expectations on [[Google Cloud]] returns, but profit missed widely on outsized AI and data center spending.
- **[[Microsoft]] fiscal Q1 earnings**: Top- and bottom-line beat; [[Azure]] grew more than expected, signalling progress monetizing AI.
- **[[Hyperscalers]]**: Alphabet, Microsoft and [[Amazon]] are the largest AI infrastructure spenders; [[Amazon]] reports Thursday.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]] returns**: Growing Wall Street doubt that years of AI spending will earn adequate returns has battered tech stocks since late September.
- **[[Gemini 4 Carbon]]**: New [[Google]] AI model largely overlooked by investors.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] decision**: Rates held at 4.0%; two of 12 [[Federal Open Market Committee]] members wanted a 25 bps hike; Warsh gave no forward guidance.
- **[[Treasury Yields]] and [[CME FedWatch]]**: 10-year yield back above 5%; December hike odds rose to 83.5% from 72.8%.
- **[[Core PCE]]**: The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, expected to remain well above the 2% target.

## Key Takeaways

- **Futures Slide**: Nasdaq 100 futures fell 1.2%, S&P 500 futures 0.5%, Dow futures 0.06%.
- **Alphabet Miss**: Shares lost 9.8% after-hours as profit missed by a wide margin.
- **Spending Drag**: Outsized AI and data center spending hurt Alphabet's profitability.
- **Microsoft Beat**: Shares rose 2.7%; Azure growth exceeded expectations.
- **Fed Hold**: Rates unchanged at 4.0%; two members dissented in favor of a hike.
- **Hawkish Read**: Warsh cited resilient spending and stable employment, lifting yields.
- **Hike Odds**: CME FedWatch put a December hike at 83.5%, up from 72.8%.
- **Next Catalysts**: Amazon earnings, Q3 GDP and September PCE data.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did U.S. stock futures fall on Wednesday evening?
**A**: Futures were pressured chiefly by Alphabet's 9.8% after-hours drop following a Q3 profit miss, which overshadowed Microsoft's gains. Hawkish-sounding Fed comments added to the pressure.

**Q2**: Why did Alphabet and Microsoft diverge after earnings?
**A**: Alphabet's profitability was hurt by outsized AI and data center spending despite a slight revenue beat. Microsoft beat on both lines, and faster-than-expected Azure growth suggested it is monetizing AI well.

**Q3**: What do the Fed's decision and Warsh's comments imply for rate expectations?
**A**: The Fed held at 4.0% but Warsh's remarks on a resilient economy were read as hawkish, pushing the 10-year yield above 5% and December hike odds to 83.5%. Upcoming GDP and PCE data will likely shape the Fed's next move.
