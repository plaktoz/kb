## Summary

US stocks staged a sharp comeback on July 30, 2026, with the [[Nasdaq Composite]] jumping 2.4% and the [[S&P 500]] up 1.2% after Wednesday's [[Federal Reserve]]-fueled selloff, as [[Microsoft]]'s blowout Azure earnings dominated sentiment. Meanwhile, the 30-year [[Treasury]] yield hit a multidecade high near 5.24% as the bond market revolted against the Fed's rate-hold decision, and [[Meta Platforms]] shares fell ~9% on an earnings miss tied to surging AI infrastructure costs.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] Azure milestone**: Azure cloud revenue topped $100 billion for the first time; MSFT stock surged 15% intraday, adding ~$439 billion in market cap — best single day since 2008
- **[[Meta Platforms]] earnings miss**: Shares fell ~9%, extending a historic losing streak; miss attributed to AI investment cost overruns
- **[[30-Year Treasury Yield]]**: Hit a multidecade high near 5.24% as bond investors revolted against the Fed's rate-hold decision
- **[[Leopold Aschenbrenner]] / Situational Awareness LP**: The $24 billion hedge fund run by the 25-year-old former [[OpenAI]] researcher sold all public equity holdings after large losses on chip-stock bets ([[SK Hynix]], [[Nebius]]); the fund had been up 439% YTD through June
- **AI wage suppression (Apollo Global)**: [[Apollo Global]] economists found workers in high-AI-exposure occupations saw wages grow 6.7 percentage points slower since 2023; ~5.8 million workers (3.7% of US workforce) in high-exposure roles; employment itself not significantly affected
- **[[PCE Inflation]]**: Core PCE +3.3% YoY in June, down from 3.4% in May — in line with expectations
- **[[GDP Growth]]**: Q2 2026 grew 1.5% annualized, below ~2% consensus
- **[[ESG Funds]]**: US sustainable funds posted first net inflow since 2022 in Q2, driven by AI data-center electric infrastructure demand and energy-security concerns
- **Jersey Mike's IPO**: Shares fell 8.7% after the $1 billion IPO, continuing weak retail-IPO trend

## Key Takeaways

- Nasdaq +2.4%, S&P 500 +1.2%, Dow +0.8% on July 30 rebound.
- Microsoft added ~$439B in market cap; Azure first to top $100B revenue.
- Meta fell ~9% on AI cost overruns and earnings miss.
- 30-year Treasury yield hit ~5.24% — multidecade high — on bond rout.
- Situational Awareness LP (Aschenbrenner) sold all public equity after chip-stock losses.
- AI-exposed workers earn 6.7 ppts less wage growth since 2023; jobs not cut.
- Core PCE 3.3% YoY in June; Q2 GDP +1.5%, below 2% forecast.
- ESG funds saw first net inflow since 2022, powered by AI data-center buildout.
- Jersey Mike's IPO fell 8.7% on debut; IPO market remains soft.
- Initial jobless claims 197K (week ended July 25), below 200K expected.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Second-Order Effects]]**: AI investment appears to simultaneously suppress wages in exposed occupations while boosting ESG/infrastructure fund flows and energy-grid demand — the macro impact of a technology wave operates through multiple indirect channels, not just direct displacement.
- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Aschenbrenner's fund collapse illustrates how concentrated AI-thesis bets can be self-defeating: chip stocks fell partly because market participants reassessed the very capex buildout the fund was positioned to profit from, triggering the losses.
