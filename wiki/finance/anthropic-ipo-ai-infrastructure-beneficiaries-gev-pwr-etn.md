## Summary

With [[Anthropic]]'s potential IPO spotlighting AI infrastructure investment, three industrial companies — [[GE Vernova]], [[Quanta Services]], and [[Eaton]] — are positioned to profit from surging data center power demand regardless of whether retail investors can access IPO shares. Each company has reported record order backlogs or revenue driven directly by AI-related electricity and grid infrastructure buildout. The article frames these as indirect beneficiaries for investors seeking AI exposure through less hyped entry points.

## Core Concepts

- **[[GE Vernova]] (GEV)**: Supplies gas turbines and grid equipment; data center orders surpassed $5 billion YTD through Q2 2026 — more than double full-year 2025 — with data centers expected to reach 25% of orders in 2026, up from 10% in 2024.
- **[[Quanta Services]] (PWR)**: Constructs transmission lines and in-facility electrical systems for data centers; backlog hit $53.4 billion in Q2 2026 driven by power infrastructure demand; strengthened capabilities via acquisition of Cupertino Electric.
- **[[Eaton]] (ETN)**: Manufactures power distribution and management systems for data centers; posted record Q2 2026 results with sales up 21% YoY to $8.5 billion; collaborated with [[Nvidia]] on AI-workload-tailored electrical systems.
- **[[Data Center Power Demand]]**: AI training and inference workloads require massive, reliable electricity; industrial companies building the upstream grid and power management layer are direct beneficiaries.
- **[[AI Infrastructure Investment Cycle]]**: The Anthropic IPO narrative is being used as a lens to identify picks-and-shovels plays in the industrial sector, analogous to selling tools during a gold rush.

## Key Takeaways

- GE Vernova data center orders topped $5B YTD Q2 2026, more than double all of 2025.
- Data centers projected to be 25% of GEV orders in 2026, up from 10% in 2024.
- Quanta Services backlog reached $53.4B in Q2 2026; Cupertino Electric acquisition deepens data center work.
- Eaton Q2 2026 sales up 21% YoY to $8.5B; partnered with Nvidia on AI-specific power systems.
- None of the three stocks are currently cheap; author suggests waiting for pullbacks.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: Rather than betting on which AI companies win the IPO lottery, owning the infrastructure layer (turbines, transmission, power distribution) captures value regardless of which AI model dominates — the same logic as selling tools to gold miners rather than mining yourself.
