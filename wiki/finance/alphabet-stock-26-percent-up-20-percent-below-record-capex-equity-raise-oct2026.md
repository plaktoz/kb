## Summary
[[Alphabet]] stock is up 26.5% over the past year to $320.21 but sits 20.5% below its $402.62 record close from May 13, a slide that followed an $80 billion equity raise and a raised 2026 capex guide. Analysts still model revenue rising from $500.14 billion in 2026 to $626.82 billion in 2027. Q3 results on October 28 hinge on what Alphabet says about 2027 capex relative to [[Google Cloud]] growth.

## Core Concepts
- [[Alphabet]]: GOOGL closed at $320.21 on October 23, 3.8% under its one-year average close of $332.77.
- [[AI Infrastructure Capex]]: 2026 guide raised to $195-205 billion from $180-190 billion; $44.9 billion of Q2 capex pushed free cash flow negative.
- [[Equity Raise]]: $80 billion plan announced June 1, including $10 billion from [[Berkshire Hathaway]] and a $40 billion at-the-market program.
- [[Google Cloud]]: Q2 revenue up 82% to $24.8 billion; Q1 backlog nearly doubled to over $460 billion.
- [[Anthropic]]: reportedly committed about $111 billion to Google Cloud, roughly 22% of disclosed backlog (Scotiabank estimates).
- [[Thomas Kurian]]: claimed AI server payback under 2 years, and half that on Alphabet's own silicon.
- [[Meta Platforms]]: raised the low end of its capex forecast and announced a non-voting share class on October 21, deepening the selloff.

## Key Takeaways
- **One-Year Gain**: Up 26.5% since late October 2025, mostly earned in spring.
- **Record Drawdown**: Shares are 20.5% below the May 13 record close of $402.62.
- **Funding Pressure**: June equity raise and July capex hike coincided with the decline despite faster revenue growth.
- **Cloud Acceleration**: Cloud growth rose from 63% in Q1 to 82% in Q2.
- **Equity-Gain Padding**: $99.0 billion in equity-security gains lifted Q2 EPS 294% to $9.11.
- **Anthropic Exposure**: A reported IPO valuation cut to about $1.5 trillion touches both gains and backlog.
- **Street Expectations**: Consensus revenue $626.82 billion in 2027, a 25.3% jump.
- **Q3 Bar**: Implied second-half revenue averages $135.22 billion a quarter, 12.9% above Q2.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why has Alphabet stock fallen about 20% from its May record despite accelerating revenue?
**A**: The decline coincided with the June $80 billion equity raise and the July capex guide increase, which pushed free cash flow negative even as revenue growth accelerated.

**Q2**: What evidence does management offer that AI spending will pay off?
**A**: Thomas Kurian said payback on AI servers is under 2 years in aggregate and half that on Alphabet's own silicon, and Cloud backlog stands above $460 billion with mostly five-year contracts.

**Q3**: What should investors watch in the October 28 Q3 report?
**A**: Whatever Alphabet says about 2027 capex against Cloud growth. If Cloud keeps accelerating and backlog grows beyond Anthropic's commitments, the price looks like an overcorrection; another open-ended capex raise with no payback figure would widen the gap.
