## Summary

[[Goldman Sachs]] reported Q3 2026 net earnings up 29% year over year to $5.29 billion ($17.01/share), well above the $13.50 consensus, as trading and dealmaking held up better than expected despite cooling from a record Q2. On the analyst call, CEO [[David Solomon]] rejected a [[Wall Street Journal]] report that the board had discussed him stepping down in 2027 or 2028, saying "there is no succession transition or timeline underway." The quarter was offset by charitable contributions, higher credit-loss provisions, and [[Apple Card]] markdowns.

## Core Concepts

- **Earnings beat**: Net revenue rose 23% to $18.7 billion versus $16.4 billion expected.
- **Trading strength**: Equities and FICC trading revenue totaled $9.9 billion, led by a 96% jump in equities financing, though below Q2's record $12 billion.
- **[[Investment Banking]] fees**: Up 21% to $3.22 billion, with record [[M&A Advisory]] revenue of $1.65 billion and stronger equity underwriting.
- **CEO succession**: Solomon denied a transition plan that WSJ said could elevate longtime deputy [[John Waldron]].
- **Headwinds**: $368 million credit-loss provisions (wholesale loan impairments) and a 72% drop in [[Platform Solutions]] revenue from Apple Card markdowns as the portfolio moves to [[JPMorgan Chase]].
- **Context**: Bank earnings season opens with investors watching whether higher borrowing costs temper Wall Street growth; JPMorgan reported weaker-than-expected results the same day. See [[Bank Earnings Season]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Profit Surge**: Net earnings up 29% to $5.29B, $17.01/share vs $13.50 expected.
- **Revenue Beat**: Net revenue up 23% to $18.7B vs $16.4B estimate.
- **Trading Haul**: $9.9B trading revenue beat $8.8B forecast; below Q2's $12B record.
- **Equities Financing**: Revenue jumped 96%, driving the trading beat.
- **FICC Softness**: Fixed income, currencies, commodities revenue fell slightly year over year.
- **Dealmaking**: IB fees up 21% to $3.22B; record $1.65B advisory revenue.
- **One-offs**: Charitable contributions cut earnings by about $1 per share.
- **Credit Provisions**: $368M, higher than expected, mostly wholesale loan impairments.
- **Apple Card Drag**: Platform Solutions revenue fell 72% on portfolio markdowns.
- **Succession Denial**: Solomon says no transition or timeline underway; stock up under 1%.
