## Summary
IBM shares fell 1.22% to $244.12 on Nov. 16 despite announcing a quantum-classical computing collaboration with NVIDIA and Fermilab. The article attributes the slide more to technical weakness, as the stock dropped below its downward-sloping 200-day average near $246.38.

## Core Concepts
- [[IBM]]: stock down despite quantum news, now below its 200-day and 20-day averages.
- [[NVIDIA]]: partner on a shared lab platform pairing DGX B200 servers with IBM quantum computers.
- [[Fermilab]]: third partner, targeting particle and nuclear physics questions classical machines cannot answer alone.
- [[Hybrid Quantum-Classical Computing]]: integrating classical and quantum systems inside modular fault-tolerant architectures.
- [[IBM Nighthawk]]: chip supporting up to 7,500 two-qubit operations across 120 qubits.
- [[Slurm]]: job manager; open-source work with [[Pasqal]] lets supercomputers schedule quantum processors as ordinary jobs.
- [[200-Day Moving Average]]: multi-month trend line near $246.38 that the stock broke below.
- [[MACD]]: momentum gauge that dipped below its signal line.
