## Summary

[[Nvidia]] is in talks to invest up to $10 billion as an anchor investor in [[Anthropic]]'s planned IPO, mirroring a prior $10 billion commitment made in November 2025 that was tied to Anthropic purchasing Nvidia-powered compute capacity. At a ~$2 trillion valuation, $10 billion would represent roughly 0.5% of the company — a tiny ownership slice despite being one-tenth of the anticipated $100 billion raise. The author argues this is structurally circular: Nvidia would be buying equity in a customer whose IPO proceeds could flow straight back to Nvidia as compute revenue.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Circular Financing]]**: Nvidia's IPO investment creates a loop — Anthropic raises capital, spends it on Nvidia GPUs, Nvidia books revenue — the original stake effectively seeds its own return via compute purchases.
- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: Targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation; seeking to raise ~$100 billion; Nvidia's $10B would anchor roughly 10% of the raise but yield only ~0.5% ownership.
- **[[Nvidia]] Private Equity Portfolio**: Grew from $3.8 billion a year prior to $47.9 billion as of late July 2026, reflecting a systematic strategy of taking equity stakes in AI customers.
- **[[Infrastructure Diversification]]**: Anthropic has secured up to 5 gigawatts each from [[Amazon]] and [[Google]]'s TPUs, and is developing its own chips — reducing single-vendor compute dependency.
- **[[Dual Risk Correlation]]**: If AI capital spending slows, Nvidia faces both a drop in GPU orders *and* simultaneous markdowns on its investment portfolio — a correlated drawdown across two revenue streams.

## Key Takeaways

- Nvidia's prior $10B Anthropic commitment (Nov 2025) was explicitly tied to compute purchases — the IPO stake extends that circular pattern into public equity.
- $10B at a $2T valuation = ~0.5% ownership; the deal is financially small but strategically symbolic.
- Nvidia PE portfolio: $3.8B → $47.9B in roughly one year — a dramatic acceleration.
- Anthropic's infrastructure is diversifying (Amazon, Google TPUs, own chips) — limiting how much of IPO proceeds actually return to Nvidia.
- Nvidia trades at ~$225, ~14x fiscal 2028 earnings; author views shares as fairly priced with the expanding PE portfolio as the key watch item.
- Dual risk: AI spending slowdown hits GPU orders and investment markdowns at the same time.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Nvidia investing in Anthropic's IPO reinforces the narrative of AI inevitability — anchoring the raise signals sector confidence, which can sustain the very AI spending that drives Nvidia's core business, creating a self-reinforcing loop.
- **[[Correlated Risk]]**: Concentrating both operating revenue and investment portfolio in the same underlying demand driver (AI compute spending) violates basic diversification logic — one macro shock produces a double loss rather than a hedge.
