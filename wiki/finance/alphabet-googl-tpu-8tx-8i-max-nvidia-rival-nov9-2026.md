## Summary

[[Alphabet]] stock climbed in early Monday trading after [[Google]] introduced two new custom AI chips, the TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, in a direct challenge to [[Nvidia]]. Google increasingly rents its tensor processing units to outside customers through [[Google Cloud]], which more than doubled revenue in Q3. Analysts hold a Strong Buy consensus with an average price target implying 35.6% upside.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Tensor Processing Unit]]**: Google's custom AI chips, long kept in-house for Search, YouTube, and Gemini and now rented to outside businesses through Google Cloud.
- **TPU 8tx**: Built for government agencies and regulated industries such as banking and healthcare; pairs with [[AMD]] processors to lock data on hardware and runs up to twice as fast as the prior version.
- **TPU 8i Max**: Combines two chips in one package for more memory and compute, suited to huge-context AI tools, math problems, and drug research.
- **[[Google Cloud]]**: Q3 revenue up 103% to $30.71B, with operating income of $11.12B.
- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]**: $51.4B of Q3 capital spending and full-year 2026 guidance of $195-$205B.
- **[[Amin Vahdat]]**: Google's chief technologist for AI infrastructure, who said the chips are reconfigured setups responding to cloud customer requests.

## Key Takeaways

- **New Chips**: Google launched TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max to rival Nvidia.
- **Q3 Revenue**: Alphabet revenue rose 25% to $127.6B on October 28.
- **Cloud Growth**: Google Cloud revenue jumped 103% to $30.71B; TPU demand was a key driver.
- **Capex**: $51.4B spent in Q3; $195-$205B expected for 2026.
- **Secure Compute**: The 8tx runs up to twice as fast without losing speed to added security.
- **Analyst View**: 29 Buy and four Hold ratings; average target $398.30 implies 35.6% upside.
