## Summary
Fox has completed its $22 billion acquisition of Roku, far earlier than the first-half-2027 timeline projected in June. Fox gains access to Roku's 100+ million households to sharpen ad targeting and reduce reliance on traditional TV distribution. The combined company ranks as the third-largest U.S. television business by viewership.

## Core Concepts
- [[Fox]]: Acquirer seeking a stronger position in streaming and [[Connected TV]].
- [[Roku]]: Streaming platform founded in 2002 by [[Anthony Wood]], now under Fox ownership.
- [[Mergers and Acquisitions]]: A $22B deal, one of the biggest media transactions in recent years.
- [[Antitrust Review]]: The Justice Department issued a second request for information two months before close.
- [[Targeted Advertising]]: Roku's household reach is expected to improve Fox's ad targeting.

## Key Takeaways
- **Deal Size**: Fox closed its $22 billion Roku acquisition.
- **Early Close**: Completed well ahead of the projected first half of 2027.
- **Regulatory Path**: DOJ second request came two months before closing; approval reports followed.
- **Audience Reach**: Roku brings more than 100 million households to Fox.
- **Strategic Rationale**: Better ad targeting and less reliance on traditional TV distribution.
- **Market Position**: Combined company is the third-largest U.S. TV business by viewership.
