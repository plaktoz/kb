## Summary
After a sharply lower week following the midterms, the [[S&P 500]] sits about 6% below its yearly peak at its lowest since July. This week's drivers are remaining earnings (neoclouds, Cisco, Applied Materials), Tuesday's consumer inflation report, and President Trump's decision on Iran.

## Core Concepts
- [[Q3 Earnings Season]]: 84% of S&P 500 companies have reported, with blended earnings growth of 31.4% per FactSet.
- [[Neoclouds]]: CoreWeave and Nebius report amid AI-industry jitters.
- [[Consumer Price Index]]: Tuesday's report is expected to show monthly softening but annual inflation above 2%.
- [[Federal Reserve]] December hike: CME FedWatch shows a 78.7% probability.
- [[Iran War]]: Trump's choice between renewed strikes and negotiations could swing oil prices and stocks.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Position**: S&P 500 about 6% below peak; Dow down 6.3%.
- **Earnings Progress**: 84% reported; blended growth 31.4%.
- **Earnings This Week**: CoreWeave, Nebius, Cisco, Applied Materials, Nu Holdings, Cerebras, KKR, Occidental.
- **Jobs Data**: October payrolls rose 165k; unemployment fell to 4.1%.
- **Rate Odds**: 78.7% probability of a December Fed hike.
- **Iran Scenarios**: Renewed strikes would lift oil and pressure stocks; talks give Iran leverage.
