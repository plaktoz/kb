## Summary
[[Ciena]] (NYSE: CIEN), a Howard County, Maryland-based networking company, committed an initial $200 million to a new investment arm, [[Ciena Ventures]], targeting AI networking, data center infrastructure, and adjacent technologies. The move comes amid a broader surge of corporate venture capital chasing AI startups, alongside growing political friction in Maryland over the data center buildout the fund is designed to serve.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Ciena Ventures]]** — Ciena's new corporate venture arm, led by VP of corporate development [[Loai Louis]], targeting AI-driven networking, connectivity, computing, and materials startups.
- **[[Corporate Venture Capital]]** — the broader trend of operating companies (Nvidia, Ciena) directly funding AI startups rather than only acquiring them.
- **[[Data Center Boom]]** — the rapid buildout driving Ciena's investment thesis, with Maryland now home to 58 data centers versus roughly 674 in Virginia and 537 in Texas.
- **[[Wes Moore]]** — Maryland's governor, who eased data center regulation via the 2024 Critical Infrastructure Streamlining Act but has since grown critical of data centers' environmental strain and pushed to repeal their tax exemption.

## Key Takeaways
- **$200 million committed initially** to Ciena Ventures, targeting AI networking and data center-adjacent startups.
- **Ciena's fiscal Q3 revenue hit $1.67 billion**, up 37% year over year, even as its stock has fallen from a May high.
- **41% of Carta-platform VC funding last year** went to AI startups, per the company's own data.
- **Nvidia invested $5 billion** in an AI startup and acquired Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, illustrating the scale of chipmaker-led AI investment.
- **Goldman Sachs projects** global AI investment will top $1 trillion this year.
- **Maryland has 58 data centers** in development, far behind Virginia (~674) and Texas (~537).
- **Governor Wes Moore is now pushing to repeal** a 2020 data center tax exemption, saying "they need to pay their own way, or they need to go away."
