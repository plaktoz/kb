## Summary

[[Meta]] reports Q2 2026 earnings on Wednesday, July 29, with the market focused almost entirely on whether it raises 2026 AI capex guidance following [[Alphabet]]'s hike to $195–205B. Analysts expect revenue up 26.7% to $60.22B, but Meta stock has already fallen 13% since July 15 on AI-spending anxiety despite otherwise strong advertising fundamentals.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Capex Guidance]]** — Wall Street's reaction to Alphabet's capex increase and subsequent selloff has set the template investors expect Meta, [[Microsoft]], and [[Amazon]] to follow this earnings week
- **[[Cloud Capacity Resale]]** — Zuckerberg has floated selling Meta's excess compute capacity to other AI companies, a potential monetization pivot investors want confirmed
- **[[Daily Active People]]** — Meta's cross-app user metric declined quarter-over-quarter for the first time in Q1 2026, attributed to outages from the Iran and Ukraine conflicts

## Key Takeaways

- **Consensus**: EPS roughly flat at $7.19, revenue +26.7% to $60.22B
- **Capex overhang**: prior guide of $125B–$145B expected to rise further, with 2027 preview also in focus
- **Stock context**: down 10% year-to-date; lost 13% since July 15 despite a $14B BlackRock joint venture for a Texas data center
- **Analyst split**: Evercore ISI reiterates outperform; Wedbush initiated at neutral, citing early-stage monetization outside the core ad stack
