## Summary
At 11:31 a.m. ET on November 13, 2026, the Nasdaq fell 1.45% as uncertainty around Nvidia and a 10-year Treasury yield of 5.10% drove a tech sell-off. AMD plunged after the resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, while defensive sectors outperformed. Investors await Nvidia earnings next week for reassurance.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nasdaq Composite]]: led losses, down 1.45% to 24,998.
- [[S&P 500]]: down 0.82% to 7,530; the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] slipped 0.12% to 52,234.
- [[Nvidia]]: leadership transition after [[Jensen Huang]] resigned raised questions about the dominant chipmaker.
- [[10-Year Treasury Yield]]: climbed to 5.10% after another round of inflation data.
- [[Advanced Micro Devices]]: down 9.89% as semiconductor selling intensified.
- [[Sector Rotation]]: investors moved from high-growth tech toward defensive consumer stocks.
- [[Federal Reserve]]: policy clarity is needed for a sustained recovery.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Moves**: Nasdaq -1.45%, S&P 500 -0.82%, Dow -0.12% midday.
- **Yields**: 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.10%, pressuring equity valuations.
- **Chip Contagion**: AMD fell 9.89%; Cisco fell 5.70%.
- **Winners**: Modine rose 28.41% on Samsung acquisition reports; Nu Holdings rose 10.95%.
