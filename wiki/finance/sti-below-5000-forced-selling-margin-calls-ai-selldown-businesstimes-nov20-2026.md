## Summary

Singapore's Straits Times Index fell 2.1% to 4,975.17 on Nov 20, its lowest close in five months, as Asian markets suffered their worst sell-off in seven months following the global AI unwind and Anthropic's underwhelming Nasdaq debut. Sources report forced selling and margin calls at private banks and brokerages, and analysts split between calling it a correction or a reset and warning that selling abroad may not be finished. The 5,000 level is seen as a key technical support.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]]**: Singapore benchmark, down more than 10% since early October and in eight of the last nine sessions.
- **[[AI Trade]]**: Confidence collapse in AI-linked stocks, hitting chip and infrastructure supply chains in Taiwan and South Korea.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Nasdaq debut closed up 17.1%, viewed as underwhelming; did not resolve concerns on financing, yields, and valuations.
- **[[Margin Calls]]**: Increased margin calls and forced selling at financial institutions, confirmed by Moomoo Singapore.
- **[[Kenneth Goh]]**, **[[Charu Chanana]]**, **[[Ritesh Ganeriwal]]**, **[[Dan Chang]]**: Analysts offering views from "wait and see" to "reset, not a retreat".
- **[[Technical Support Level]]**: The 5,000 mark on the STI viewed as crucial.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI Drop**: Fell 2.1% to 4,975.17, lowest close in five months.
- **Regional Rout**: Taiex -6.2%, Kospi -5.2%, Nikkei -5.1%, Hang Seng -3.7%.
- **Imported Selloff**: STI has little direct AI exposure, yet fell about 5% this week versus Taiex's 17%.
- **Forced Selling**: Margin calls reported at private banks and brokerages; end not clearly in sight.
- **US Spillover**: S&P 500 fell 2% and Nasdaq 2.3% after Anthropic's debut.
- **Unresolved Worries**: Financing, elevated bond yields, valuations, and whether AI spending yields sustainable profits.
- **Bull Framing**: Syfe sees a "reset, not a retreat"; investors getting choosier on price.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did the STI fall below 5,000 despite little direct AI exposure?
**A**: The sell-off began in the US with an AI unwind and spread through Asia; Wall Street set the tone, and forced selling and margin calls extended it well beyond the AI-linked names.

**Q2**: What data points show the scale of the regional sell-off?
**A**: The STI fell 2.1% to 4,975.17, while Taiex fell 6.2%, Kospi 5.2%, Nikkei 5.1%, and Hang Seng 3.7%. The STI fell in eight of the last nine sessions.

**Q3**: How should an investor decide whether the STI has bottomed?
**A**: Goh would wait for Taiwan and Korea to steady before concluding. Chang says holding the 5,000 level would show support is intact, though he cautions against calling a bottom.
