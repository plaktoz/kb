## Summary
Heading into the first week of August 2026, investors are balancing fresh U.S. labor data, another wave of Q2 earnings, and lingering uncertainty over [[Federal Reserve]] policy after new chair [[Kevin Warsh]] left the door open to a future rate hike. The S&P 500 sits about 1.6% below its June record as markets await clarity from Friday's jobs report and remaining earnings.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Jobs Report]] Sensitivity** — July payrolls expected near 83,000 with unemployment steady at 4.3%; a stronger print could reinforce tightening expectations
- **[[Q2 Earnings Season]]** — roughly a quarter of S&P 500 companies report this week, including Caterpillar, Palantir, and Merck, plus SpaceX's first earnings since its IPO
- **[[Sector EPS Dispersion]]** — energy sector profits projected to surge 121% on Iran-driven oil prices; information technology (Nvidia, Apple, Micron) projected near 62% growth; healthcare projected to decline ~9%
- **[[AI Capex Justification Risk]]** — Capital Economics warns AI-related equities may be nearing a point where lofty earnings and capex assumptions become hard to justify

## Key Takeaways
- **S&P 500** remains about 1.6% below its June record high.
- **Aggregate Q2 EPS growth** estimated at 22.5% (FactSet) to 25% (Bloomberg) year-over-year — the strongest since 2021 if achieved.
- **Energy sector profits** projected to jump 121% due to the Iran conflict's effect on oil prices.
- **Tech sector EPS growth** projected near 62%, led by Nvidia, Apple, and Micron.
- **Healthcare sector EPS** projected to decline roughly 9%.
- **Analyst warning**: a downward revision to AI capex/earnings assumptions could trigger a broader equity correction.
