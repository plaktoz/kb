## Summary

[[Alphabet]] shares rose more than 1.7% on Monday as tech stocks rebounded from Friday's sell-off, with the [[Philadelphia Semiconductor Index]] ending an eight-session losing streak. Google is reportedly preparing [[Gemini 4 Carbon]] amid intensifying competition with [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]]. Analyst targets diverge (KeyBanc cut to $410, JPMorgan raised to $425) ahead of Q3 earnings on October 28.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] rebound** — Rose after losing close to 2.5% in the prior session, alongside a broad tech recovery
- **[[Philadelphia Semiconductor Index]]** — Climbed more than 2.5%, ending its longest losing streak since 2008
- **[[Gemini 4 Carbon]]** — Reported upgrade to the recently released Gemini 4 Argon; strengths in visual tasks such as video analysis and website testing (per [[The Information]])
- **[[Gemini Notebook]]** — New Short Video Overviews (~60 seconds) and interactive Learning Overviews with quizzes and flashcards
- **[[Google Cloud]] and [[Tensor Processing Units]]** — Cited by [[JPMorgan]] analyst [[Doug Anmuth]] for rapid cloud expansion and strong enterprise TPU demand
- **Analyst split** — [[KeyBanc]] ([[Justin Patterson]]) warns the positive outlook may already be reflected in valuation; [[UBS]] stays Neutral

## Key Takeaways

- **Monday move**: Alphabet up more than 1.7% after a roughly 2.5% loss prior session.
- **Chip rebound**: Semiconductor index rose 2.5%+, ending an eight-session losing streak.
- **Macro relief**: Easing oil prices and stable Treasury yields supported equities.
- **AI model race**: Google, OpenAI and Anthropic reportedly all preparing new models.
- **JPMorgan target**: Raised to $425 from $420 on October 15.
- **KeyBanc target**: Cut to $410 from $435, keeping Overweight; valuation may be demanding.
- **UBS target**: Raised to $380 from $379, Neutral rating.
- **Next catalyst**: Q3 earnings on October 28 test whether AI investment is paying off.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Alphabet shares rise more than 1.7% on Monday?
**A**: Investors returned to technology stocks after Friday's sell-off, with a broad semiconductor rebound, easing oil prices and stable Treasury yields. Analysts also stayed broadly positive ahead of earnings.

**Q2**: How do the analyst price target changes differ?
**A**: JPMorgan raised its target to $425 citing Google Cloud growth and TPU demand, while KeyBanc cut to $410 from $435 warning that positives may already be priced in. UBS nudged its target to $380 with a Neutral rating.

**Q3**: What should investors watch next for Alphabet?
**A**: The October 28 third-quarter earnings, which will show whether AI investments are translating into stronger business performance, particularly in Google Cloud.
