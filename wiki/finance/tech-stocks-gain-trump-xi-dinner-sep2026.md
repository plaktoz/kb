## Summary

US equities rose on September 25, 2026, boosted by a high-profile dinner between President Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping, and prominent tech executives including [[Jensen Huang]] (Nvidia), [[Lisa Su]] (AMD), and [[Elon Musk]] (Tesla). The rally coincided with easing oil prices as hopes grew for a deal to reopen the [[Strait of Hormuz]], even as the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.17%. The Technology sector (XLK) led gains, with Microsoft surging roughly 3%, while Meta pulled back about 2% despite a strong prior four-day run.

## Core Concepts

- **Trump-Xi Tech Titan Dinner**: A geopolitically significant dinner involving [[Donald Trump]], [[Xi Jinping]], and top US tech CEOs — [[Jensen Huang]] (Nvidia), [[Lisa Su]] (AMD), [[Elon Musk]] (Tesla) — provided a catalyst for broad tech-sector optimism.
- **[[Strait of Hormuz]] Deal Hopes**: Oil prices eased as markets priced in prospects of reopening the critical oil chokepoint; Brent crude fell toward $98/barrel.
- **10-Year [[Treasury Yield]]**: Reached 5.17%, reflecting persistent rate pressure even as equities gained.
- **[[Technology Select Sector ETF]] (XLK)**: Sector gained with [[Microsoft]] as a standout, up approximately 3%.
- **[[Meta]] AI Pullback**: Meta declined ~2% after a 16% four-day rally driven by enthusiasm around its new [[Muse AI Agent]], suggesting near-term profit-taking.
- **US Dollar Index**: Softened, providing additional tailwind for equities.
- **Other Mentioned Stocks**: Costco, Greenland Energy, and Moderna drew reader attention.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Sentiment as a Short-Term Driver]]**: The dinner had no immediate policy outcome, yet the signal of US-China tech dialogue alone was enough to move markets — illustrating how investor sentiment responds to perceived thaw in geopolitical risk faster than fundamentals can justify.
- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: Meta's 2% pullback after a 16% four-day surge is a textbook example of short-term mean reversion; outsized momentum runs tend to pause or reverse when the initial catalyst (Muse AI hype) is fully priced in.
