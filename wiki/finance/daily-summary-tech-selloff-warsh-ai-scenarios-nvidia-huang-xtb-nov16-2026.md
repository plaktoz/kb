## Summary

XTB's Nov 16 market recap describes a continuing Wall Street tech selloff triggered by a governance crisis at Nvidia after CEO Jensen Huang's resignation. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh gave no rate guidance but outlined three contingency scenarios for AI's economic impact. Gold, silver, European gas, copper and bitcoin also fell, while the yen and Swiss franc drew safe-haven demand.

## Core Concepts

- [[Kevin Warsh]]: Fed Chair who declined rate guidance at the San Francisco Fed's Productivity and Jobs Task Force conference.
- [[Federal Reserve]]: analysts sketching AI scenarios that are explicitly not forecasts.
- [[Nvidia]]: shares fell 3.82% to $156.56 amid the departure of [[Jensen Huang]], ahead of quarterly earnings.
- [[Anthropic IPO]]: impending market debut adding to tech nervousness.
- [[Export Controls]]: tightened US Commerce Department rules closing loopholes on Chinese data centers.
- [[Safe-Haven Currencies]]: yen (USDJPY 159.53) and Swiss franc (USDCHF 0.8393) in demand.
- [[Commodities]]: gold $3,862, silver $47.12, copper $13,201, European gas down over 6% intraday.

## Key Takeaways

- **Warsh on AI**: No rate guidance; Fed analysts sketch three AI scenarios, not forecasts.
- **Optimistic Scenario**: Productivity boom with gains shared between employers and workers.
- **Jobless Growth**: Activity picks up at the cost of massive job losses.
- **Disappointment Scenario**: Capex-driven returns disappoint, causing equity selloff, slowdown and higher unemployment.
- **Nvidia Crisis**: Board is not considering Huang's return; stock fell 3.82%.
- **Macro Backdrop**: Weak China and Europe data, Japan GDP, Hormuz tensions, Fitch warning.
- **Commodities and Crypto**: Gold -0.54%, silver -3.20%, bitcoin -0.41% to $64,835.
- **Deals**: Eli Lilly buys Calytrix Bio (up to $1.5B); J&J buys Ollin Biosciences ($2.2B upfront).

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the central market story on Nov 16, 2026?
**A**: A tech stock selloff driven by a governance crisis at Nvidia after CEO Jensen Huang's resignation, compounded by tighter US export rules, Nvidia's upcoming earnings and Anthropic's impending listing.

**Q2**: What three AI scenarios did the Fed outline?
**A**: An optimistic productivity boom with shared gains, jobless growth with massive job losses, and a disappointment scenario where returns on huge capex disappoint and trigger an equity selloff and slowdown. The Fed stressed these are contingency scenarios, not forecasts.

**Q3**: What are the implications for the Fed's near-term policy?
**A**: Warsh deliberately gave no direct guidance on interest rates. External expert task forces set up in June are due to submit recommendations to the FOMC around year-end.
