## Summary

[[Amazon]] is launching **Project Mercury**, an aggressive plan to build 1,000+ same-day fulfillment centers over five years — a tenfold increase from its current 85 locations — targeting coverage within 10 miles of 80% of Prime customers by 2031. The $7 billion investment through 2027 is designed to close the geographic gap with [[Walmart]], which currently has stores near ~90% of the U.S. population. With [[AWS]] posting 37% YoY growth and Amazon trading near an all-time valuation low, the author sees an undervalued entry point.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Project Mercury]]** — Amazon's codename for its same-day fulfillment buildout: 1,000+ facilities by 2031, up from 85 today
- **[[Amazon Prime]] Same-Day Coverage** — target of reaching 80% of Prime customers within 10 miles by 2031
- **[[Walmart]] Geographic Advantage** — stores currently within reach of ~90% of U.S. population, the benchmark Amazon is chasing
- **[[AWS]] Growth** — 37% YoY revenue growth in Q2 2026, fastest pace in 18 quarters, holding 28% cloud market share
- **[[Amazon Valuation]]** — trading at "less than 20 times earnings," near an all-time low despite strong fundamentals

## Key Takeaways

- **Project Mercury**: 1,000+ same-day centers planned, up from 85 — a 10x expansion.
- **Coverage goal**: 80% of Prime customers within 10 miles by 2031.
- **Investment**: ~$7 billion committed through 2027.
- **Walmart gap**: Walmart covers ~90% of U.S. population; Amazon is closing in.
- **AWS**: 37% YoY growth in Q2 2026, fastest in 18 quarters, 28% market share.
- **E-commerce**: North American sales reached $426 billion in 2025, up 10%.
- **Valuation**: Under 20x earnings — near historical low amid AI investor uncertainty.
- Faster delivery drives higher purchase frequency and margin expansion.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Last-Mile Advantage]]**: Physical proximity to customers is a structural moat — whoever can place inventory nearest to buyers wins on speed and cost, which is why Amazon's 10-mile coverage target directly mimics Walmart's existing geographic network advantage.
- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: Faster same-day delivery → more purchases per Prime customer → higher retention → more fulfillment utilization → lower per-unit cost, compounding the investment's return.
