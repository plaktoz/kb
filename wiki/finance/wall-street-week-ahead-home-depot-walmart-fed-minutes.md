## Summary

This week's Home Depot, Target, Lowe's and Walmart earnings, plus the July Fed meeting minutes, will together give investors their clearest read yet on how consumers and the housing market are handling inflation still above 3%.

## Core Concepts

- [[Retail Earnings as Consumer Proxy]] — Target and Walmart results are used as a real-time gauge of household budgeting and spending behavior.
- [[Housing Improvement Spend]] — Home Depot and Lowe's results signal whether consumers are increasing or cutting back on home-improvement spending.
- [[FOMC Minutes]] — July 28-29 meeting minutes, released this week, will detail the reasoning behind the Fed's third-straight decision to hold rates, including the three dissents favoring a hike.

## Key Takeaways

- **Earnings calendar**: Home Depot (Tue), Target and Lowe's (Wed), Walmart (Thu).
- **Inflation backdrop**: Rate remains solidly above 3%; the US-Iran conflict has pushed oil and gasoline prices higher.
- **Fed context**: July meeting held rates steady for a fifth straight time; three officials dissented in favor of a hike — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh called the discussion a "good family fight."
- **Market expectation**: Wall Street expects at least one rate hike before the end of 2026.
