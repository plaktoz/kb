## Summary

Despite mixed ARK fund performance in 2026, [[Cathie Wood]] is aggressively accumulating [[Meta Platforms]] and [[Rocket Lab]] at significant discounts from their all-time highs. ARK deployed roughly $73M combined into these two positions in early September 2026, betting on AI-driven advertising growth at Meta and surging commercial launch demand at Rocket Lab. Both holdings reflect Wood's conviction in long-duration, high-growth themes — AI infrastructure and the emerging space economy.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Cathie Wood]]** / **[[ARK Invest]]**: Active high-conviction growth investing across disruptive themes; purchased both stocks at steep discounts via ARKW, ARKK, and other ETFs.
- **[[Meta Platforms]]**: 3.6 billion daily app users provide a durable moat; AI-enhanced ad targeting drove 28% YoY Q2 2026 revenue growth; heavy 2026 capex ($165–169B) pressures near-term free cash flow but signals long-term platform bets.
- **[[Rocket Lab]]**: Commercial launch leader with a $2.36B record backlog and 62% YoY Q2 2026 revenue growth; pending [[Iridium]] acquisition would add 66 satellites and 2.5M+ subscribers.
- **[[Space Economy]]**: Morgan Stanley projects the global space industry will reach $1 trillion by 2040, underpinning ARK's long-term RKLB thesis.
- **[[Discount Investing]]**: ARK's buy-the-dip strategy — Meta ~18% below ATH, Rocket Lab ~60% below ATH — reflects tolerance for volatility in exchange for asymmetric upside.
