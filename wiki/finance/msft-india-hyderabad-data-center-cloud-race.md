## Summary

[[Microsoft]] opened its fourth Azure cloud region in India — its largest data center deployment in the country — as part of a $20.5 billion India cloud/AI investment, intensifying a hyperscaler infrastructure race with [[Google Cloud]] and [[Amazon Web Services]]. MSFT shares gained 1.7% on the news, even as retail sentiment on Stocktwits cooled slightly.

## Core Concepts

- [[Hyperscaler Infrastructure Race]] — Microsoft, Google, and AWS are each committing billions to Indian data center capacity within the same window (Microsoft $20.5B, Google $15B, AWS $8.3B+$4.4B)
- [[India South Central Region]] — the new Hyderabad cloud region, described by Microsoft as its largest hyperscale cloud presence in India, spanning three availability zones
- [[Cloud Market Share]] — per Synergy Research Group: AWS ~28-31%, Azure ~21%, Google Cloud ~14% of global cloud infrastructure revenue
- [[Data Sovereignty]] — cited by Microsoft India's president as a key reason enterprises need in-country infrastructure to move from AI experimentation to production

## Key Takeaways

- **Investment**: $20.5B total India commitment ($3B announced Jan 2025, $17.5B added later that year).
- **Scale**: three availability zones roughly the size of two Eden Gardens cricket stadiums combined; early customers include Adani Group, Bajaj Finserv, HDFC Bank, and PB Pay.
- **Growth context**: in the most recent quarter, Azure grew 43% YoY ($157B annualized run rate) versus AWS's 37% YoY ($169B annualized) and Google Cloud's 82% YoY ($99B annualized, accelerating from 63%).
- **Competitive backdrop**: Google confirmed a $15B Andhra Pradesh data center in October 2025; AWS pledged $8.3B in India in January 2026 on top of an earlier $4.4B commitment.
- **Stock reaction**: MSFT gained 1.7% intraday; Stocktwits retail sentiment eased from "extremely bullish" to "bullish."

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What specifically did Microsoft announce in India, and why does it matter competitively?
**A**: A new Hyderabad cloud region (its fourth in India, largest data center deployment yet) as part of a $20.5B investment — directly competing with Google's $15B and AWS's $12.7B combined India commitments in the same infrastructure race.

**Q2**: How does Azure's growth rate compare to AWS and Google Cloud in the most recent quarter?
**A**: Azure grew 43% YoY versus AWS's 37% and Google Cloud's 82% (Google's growth accelerated sharply from 63% the prior quarter), even though AWS remains the largest by absolute revenue.

**Q3**: Why does Microsoft frame in-country data centers as important for enterprise AI adoption?
**A**: Because enterprises moving from AI experimentation to production-scale deployment need infrastructure, security, and data sovereignty that a local cloud region provides.
