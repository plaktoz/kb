## Summary

On July 28, 2026, major U.S. indexes closed mostly higher, with the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] gaining nearly 550 points (+1%) and the [[S&P 500]] up 0.2%, while the [[Nasdaq Composite]] slipped 0.2% as chip stocks dragged on tech. Strong earnings from Sherwin-Williams, Coca-Cola, and Boeing powered the Dow, even as semiconductor stocks fell sharply amid concerns about AI spending sustainability. The Fed kicked off a two-year meeting with markets pricing in rising odds of an interest-rate hike, and oil prices continued to fall on a pause in U.S.-Iran hostilities.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Earnings Season]] (Q2 2026)**: 169 S&P 500 companies reporting this week, including [[Magnificent Seven]] members [[Microsoft]], [[Meta]], [[Apple]], and [[Amazon]].
- **[[Semiconductor Stocks]] decline**: [[Roundhill Memory ETF]] (DRAM) and [[iShares Semiconductor ETF]] (SOXX) fell ~9% and ~5%; [[SK Hynix]], [[Sandisk]], [[Western Digital]], [[Seagate Technology]], [[Micron Technology]], and [[Intel]] all dropped sharply.
- **[[Apple]] record high**: Hit $5 trillion market cap; surpassed [[Nvidia]] to reclaim largest market cap after Nvidia's prior-day 5% drop.
- **[[AI Spending]] concerns**: [[Alphabet]] and [[Tesla]] saw stock declines despite strong results, as investors worried about AI capex sustainability — now flagged by [[Fitch]] as a systemic credit risk.
- **[[Hyperscaler]] capex**: Capital spending by hyperscalers ([[Amazon]], [[Google]], [[Meta]], [[Microsoft]], [[Oracle]]) projected at ~$700 billion in 2026, up $300 billion from 2025 — contributing ~1 percentage point to GDP growth.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] meeting**: Two-day FOMC meeting began; 31% probability of a rate hike priced in, up from 26% a week prior; 75% chance of at least a quarter-point hike by September.
- **[[West Texas Intermediate]] crude**: Fell 4.3% to ~$79/barrel on U.S.-Iran hostilities pause.
- **[[GDP]] outlook**: Bureau of Economic Analysis expected to report Q2 GDP grew at annualized 1.8%, down from 2.1% in Q1.

## Key Takeaways

- **Dow +550 pts**: Sherwin-Williams (+8%), Coca-Cola (+5%), Boeing (+4.5%) drove gains.
- **Nasdaq down**: Chip stocks fell 5–14%; semiconductor sector under pressure.
- **Apple $5T**: Hit record market cap, overtook Nvidia as largest by market cap.
- **Earnings beat rate**: ~90% of early S&P 500 reporters beat estimates; tech lagged by 2.6 pts post-beat.
- **AI credit risk**: Fitch warns AI bubble + Middle East tensions = major credit risk.
- **Hyperscaler capex**: $700B in 2026 AI infrastructure spending adds ~1 pt to GDP.
- **Rate hike odds rising**: September hike probability at 75%; 10-year Treasury above 4.60%.
- **Oil continuing decline**: Brent crude at $83.80, WTI at $79 on Iran pause.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Rising AI capex drives stock gains, but the same capex spending raises fears of unsustainable bubble dynamics — investor expectations are simultaneously inflating and scrutinizing the same trend, creating self-referential volatility.
- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: Chip stocks' sharp single-day declines after prior AI-driven run-ups illustrate how stretched valuations revert toward fundamentals when spending sustainability is questioned.
