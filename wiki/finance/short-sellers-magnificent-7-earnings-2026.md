## Summary

Short interest in [[Meta Platforms]] (META) and [[Amazon]] (AMZN) has reached its highest levels since 2022 as traders bet that rising AI capex guidance will disappoint investors despite both companies carrying over 90% beat odds. [[Alphabet]]'s post-earnings sell-off after raising its 2026 capex guide to $195–200B set the template that now haunts Meta, Amazon, and [[Microsoft]] heading into their own reports. A beat on earnings is largely priced in, making forward capex commentary and cloud growth the true swing variables.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Short Selling]]**: Bearish traders are loading up on [[Magnificent 7]] names anticipating a repeat of Alphabet's capex-driven sell-off.
- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]**: Hyperscalers — Meta, Amazon, Microsoft — are collectively anchoring roughly a quarter of [[S&P 500]] capital expenditures; rising guidance is the bears' primary thesis.
- **[[Earnings Expectations]]**: Prediction markets price in 90–95% beat odds across all three names, meaning upside beats alone will not move stocks — guidance does.
- **[[Options Market Sentiment]]**: Meta's 0.58 put/call ratio and Amazon's 0.32 show calls still outnumbering puts, complicating the purely bearish setup.
- **[[Short Squeeze Risk]]**: Historical analogs — Meta's late-2022 peak short interest before its historic rally, Amazon's late-2023 short build ahead of a massive 2024 run — suggest heavy shorts have been wrong before.
- **[[Azure]]** / **[[AWS]]** growth trajectories are the key metrics investors watch inside Microsoft and Amazon reports respectively.

## Key Takeaways

- Short interest in META and AMZN at highest since 2022 despite 90%+ beat odds.
- [[Alphabet]] raised 2026 capex to $195–200B; stock sold off post-earnings as template.
- META beat odds: 90.2%; FY26 capex guide $125B–$145B; last quarter surprise +56.79%.
- AMZN beat odds: 95.5%; FY26 capex ~$200B; last quarter surprise +60.69%.
- MSFT beat odds: 91.5%; Q3 capex $30.88B (+84% YoY); last quarter surprise +4.9%.
- **AWS** holding 28% growth pace — fastest in 15 quarters — is key Amazon watch item.
- **Azure** constant-currency growth: traders put 53.5% odds on the 40–42% band.
- MSFT commercial RPO $627B; AI revenue run rate $37B, up 123% YoY.
- Meta Q1 2026 beat still produced an 8.55% earnings-day decline — a beat is table stakes.
- Historical shorts at these levels were squeezed in 2022 (META) and 2023 (AMZN).

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Rising short interest creates a self-referential dynamic — heavy shorts increase squeeze potential on any positive surprise, which itself deters further shorting at the margin, making the trade crowded even before earnings land.
- **[[Reference Class Forecasting]]**: Bears appear to anchor on Alphabet's one-off reaction rather than the broader base rate of Magnificent 7 beats, overfitting to a recent vivid example rather than the historical distribution.
