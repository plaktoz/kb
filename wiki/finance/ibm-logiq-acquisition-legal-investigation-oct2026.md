## Summary

Over three days in late September, [[IBM]] announced the acquisition of UK cybersecurity consultancy [[Logiq Consulting]], extended its [[IBM Digital Asset Haven]]-[[Swift]] tokenized-deposit integration with a local beta deployment option, and added Frank Baker to its board — all while law firm [[Hagens Berman]] opened a preliminary investigation into IBM's disclosures around [[IBM Z]] mainframe performance and its Q2 2026 earnings miss. The stock has lost 23–24% year-to-date, with the next hard data point due at Q3 2026 earnings on October 21.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Logiq Consulting]]**: UK cybersecurity advisory firm (clients mainly defense, critical infrastructure, public sector) acquired by [[IBM Consulting]] on September 24; purchase price undisclosed. Slots alongside [[SiXworks]] in IBM's sovereign-tech and cyber-defense push.
- **[[Hagens Berman]] Securities Investigation**: A preliminary legal review (no finding of wrongdoing) into whether IBM's disclosures about [[IBM Z]] mainframe performance and its Q2 2026 earnings miss violated US securities laws.
- **[[Frank Baker]]**: Co-founder and managing partner of [[Siris Capital Group]], joined IBM's board effective October 1 (now 14 seats), serving on the nominating and corporate governance committee.
- **Sovereign Tech Strategy**: IBM's pattern of pursuing regulation-driven, higher-margin markets (government, banking, defense) — cybersecurity, tokenized deposits, and air-gapped AI tooling — away from classic IT services.

## Key Takeaways

- **New acquisition**: IBM bought Logiq Consulting (UK cybersecurity, defense/public-sector clients) on Sep 24, price undisclosed.
- **Legal overhang**: Hagens Berman's preliminary probe targets IBM Z disclosures and the Q2 2026 earnings miss.
- **Governance change**: Frank Baker (Siris Capital) joined the board Oct 1, bringing it to 14 members.
- **Stock performance**: Shares down 23–24% YTD; closed at EUR 197.78 (-1.5%) before a 0.6% bounce to EUR 198.90.
- **Next catalyst**: Q3 2026 earnings land October 21, with focus on software segment progress and hardware outlook.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q2**: What is the Hagens Berman investigation specifically examining?
**A**: It is a preliminary review of possible US securities law violations tied to IBM's disclosures about IBM Z mainframe performance and the company's Q2 2026 earnings miss; no wrongdoing has been found yet.

**Q3**: What should an investor watch for on October 21?
**A**: IBM's Q3 2026 results, with particular attention to progress in the software segment and the outlook for the hardware (mainframe) architecture, as the next concrete data point against the stock's YTD decline.
