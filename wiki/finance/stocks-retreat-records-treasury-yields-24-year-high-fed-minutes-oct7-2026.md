## Summary

On October 7, 2026, US stocks pulled back from record highs after the 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.365%, its highest level since April 2002, reviving fears about inflation and the federal debt load. The S&P 500 ended its four-session winning streak. Minutes from the Fed's September meeting showed officials split on why they had approved the first rate hike since July 2023, and futures cut the odds of an October hike to 17.2%.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Treasury Yields]] surge**: The 10-year touched 5.365% and the 30-year 5.732%, both highs not seen since 2002. The move extends a weeks-long [[Global Bond Selloff]] driven by sovereign and corporate debt worries and energy-driven [[Inflation]].
- **Index moves**: [[S&P 500]] -0.22% to 7,801.75; [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] -0.66% to 51,180.17; [[Nasdaq Composite]] -0.22% to 27,538.69; [[Russell 2000]] -1.3%.
- **[[FOMC Minutes]]**: The September hike passed unanimously. Some officials framed it as blunting energy shocks, others as curbing demand-driven inflation.
- **[[CME FedWatch]]**: October hike odds fell to 17.2% from 37.6% a week earlier, so the market expects a pause.
- **Oil and the [[Iran Conflict]]**: Brent settled at $100.20 and WTI at $88.28. The [[International Energy Agency]] agreed to speed up stockpile releases, prioritizing diesel.
- **[[Equity Risk Premium]] pressure**: Higher yields make bonds more competitive with stocks and raise the discount rate applied to future earnings.
- **[[Market Breadth]] weakness**: Roughly 73% of S&P 500 stocks fell. The NYSE had 115 new highs against 497 new lows, and the Nasdaq 32 new highs against 250 new lows.
- **Housing**: The [[Mortgage Rates|30-year fixed mortgage rate]] reached 7.49% (MBA), its highest in nearly three years. Homebuilders fell 2.9%.
- **AI capex**: [[SpaceX]] fell 2.5% on reports it is seeking $40B to buy [[Nvidia]] chips. The [[Philadelphia Semiconductor Index]] fell about 2.3%.
- **Q3 [[Earnings Season]]**: LSEG expects S&P 500 earnings growth of 30.6% year over year, led by tech and energy.

## Key Takeaways

- **Yield milestone**: 10-year at 5.365%, the highest since April 2002.
- **Streaks snapped**: The S&P 500 and Dow each ended a four-day winning run.
- **Fed repricing**: October hike odds dropped from 37.6% to 17.2% in a week.
- **Auction demand**: Indirect bidders took 80.3% of the $39B 10-year sale, against a 72.4% average.
- **Breadth warning**: Decliners beat advancers 3.34-to-1 on the NYSE despite near-record indexes.
- **Rate-sensitive losers**: CrowdStrike -5%, Palo Alto -3%+, Meta -2%+, banks about -1%.
- **Sector split**: Industrials fell the most; healthcare led the gainers.
- **Strategist view**: Thomas Martin (Globalt) expects an October pause after the Q3 correction never arrived.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Discounted Cash Flow]]**: A higher risk-free rate lowers the present value of future earnings. That hits long-duration, AI-premium tech hardest when yields reach multi-decade highs.
