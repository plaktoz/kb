## Summary

[[Alphabet]], [[Amazon]], and [[Meta]] have committed a combined $520–550B in AI infrastructure capital expenditure for 2026, with [[Microsoft]] adding another ~$190B, marking what [[Jensen Huang]] called "the largest infrastructure expansion in human history." [[Nvidia]] is the primary hardware beneficiary, posting 85% YoY revenue growth to $81.6B and guiding for ~$91B next quarter at ~75% gross margins. Investor uncertainty around whether this spending translates to adequate returns has kept Nvidia stock ~12% below its 52-week high despite explosive fundamentals.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Infrastructure Buildout]]**: The coordinated, multi-hundred-billion-dollar investment by hyperscalers in GPU clusters, data centers, and networking to power the next wave of AI services.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Dominant GPU supplier capturing the lion's share of hyperscaler AI spend; data center revenue grew 92% YoY to $75.2B.
- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Nvidia CEO who framed the current AI buildout as historically unprecedented infrastructure investment.
- **[[Hyperscaler CapEx]]**: [[Alphabet]] ($195–$205B), [[Amazon]] (~$200B), [[Meta]] ($125–$145B), [[Microsoft]] (~$190B) — combined >$700B in 2026 AI-related infrastructure spend.
- **[[Customer Concentration Risk]]**: Nvidia's revenue is heavily dependent on a small number of large customers, creating exposure if any one reduces orders.
- **[[Custom Silicon]]**: [[Meta]] is diversifying hardware with custom chips alongside [[Broadcom]] and [[AMD]], reducing Nvidia dependency at the margin.
- **[[Semiconductor Cycles]]**: Historical pattern where chip demand surges are followed by corrections; a structural risk for Nvidia's current trajectory.

## Key Takeaways

- **Alphabet CapEx**: Guiding $195–$205B for 2026 AI infrastructure.
- **Amazon CapEx**: ~$200B committed to AI infrastructure in 2026.
- **Meta CapEx**: $125–$145B range for 2026 AI spend.
- **Microsoft CapEx**: ~$190B additional spend, not included in the $500B figure.
- **Nvidia Revenue**: $81.6B last quarter, up 85% YoY; guided ~$91B next quarter.
- **Data Center Mix**: 92% of Nvidia revenue now comes from data center segment.
- **Valuation**: Nvidia at ~32x trailing / ~21x forward earnings — modest for its growth rate.
- **Stock Dip**: ~12% below 52-week high despite record results, reflecting ROI uncertainty.
- **Competitor Inroads**: Meta deploying Broadcom and AMD silicon alongside Nvidia GPUs.
- **Author View**: Nvidia is the most direct play on AI buildout at current prices, with position sizing caveat.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: During a gold rush, the most reliable profits come from selling the tools — Nvidia supplying GPUs to all hyperscalers regardless of which AI application wins mirrors this principle exactly.
- **[[Goodhart's Law]]**: Hyperscalers' race to announce ever-larger CapEx figures risks optimizing for signaling AI seriousness to investors rather than disciplined capital allocation — the metric (spend size) may diverge from the goal (AI ROI).
