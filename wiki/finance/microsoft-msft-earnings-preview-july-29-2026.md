## Summary
Microsoft reports FY2026 Q4 earnings on July 29 with the stock down ~20% YTD, weighed by concerns about $190bn in AI capex and weak Copilot adoption (only 20M paid seats vs 450M+ Microsoft 365 subscribers). Morgan Stanley's bull case ($795 price target) hinges on Azure acceleration and Copilot monetization. The stock trades at ~23x trailing earnings, well below its 5-year average of 32.5x.

## Core Concepts

- [[Microsoft]] (MSFT) — reporting FY26 Q4 on July 29, 2026
- [[Azure]] — Microsoft's cloud business growing ~40% annually; AI infrastructure is the primary driver
- [[Microsoft Copilot]] — only 20M paid enterprise seats vs 450M+ Microsoft 365 subscribers; low penetration is the key bear case
- [[AI Capex Concern]] — $190bn guided capex for 2026; investors questioning ROI
- [[Morgan Stanley]] — $795 bull case price target; current price ~$381

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock at ~23x trailing earnings** — below 5-year average of 32.5x; historically cheap for MSFT
- **Azure growth ~40% annually** — AI spending paying off on cloud revenue
- **Copilot penetration gap**: 20M seats / 450M subscribers = only 4.4% — ceiling for upside is enormous
- **Two paths for stock**: soar on strong Azure + Copilot adoption; sell off on high capex guidance or weak subscriber growth
- **Long-term thesis intact**: Microsoft best positioned to survive AI correction due to diversified enterprise moat

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mr. Market]]**: At 23x earnings vs a 32.5x 5-year average, the market is pricing in permanent impairment — yet Azure's 40% growth is not consistent with impairment. The gap between current multiple and historical average is the opportunity signal.
