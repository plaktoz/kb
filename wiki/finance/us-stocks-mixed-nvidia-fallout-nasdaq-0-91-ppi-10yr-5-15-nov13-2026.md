## Summary

US stocks ended mixed on November 13 as fallout from Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation dragged AI chip and data-center shares lower while the Dow edged up. A 6.5% year-on-year PPI rise and hawkish Fed remarks pushed the 10-year Treasury yield to 5.15% and made a December rate hike near certain.

## Core Concepts

- [[Nvidia]] fell 2.01% to $162.85 after [[Jensen Huang]]'s resignation, its sixth straight weekly loss and longest losing streak since October 2012; CFO [[Colette Kress]] is interim CEO.
- [[AI Infrastructure Stocks]] including [[AMD]], [[Broadcom]], Dell, Cisco, [[CoreWeave]], Nebius, IREN and Applied Digital all fell together.
- [[Producer Price Index]] rose 6.5% year on year, the fastest pace since November 2022.
- [[Federal Reserve]] officials Mary Daly and Susan Collins argued for further tightening; [[CME FedWatch]] shows a December hike as a near certainty.
- [[US Treasury Yields]]: the 10-year climbed to 5.15%.
- [[Oil Prices]] were near flat, with Brent at $95.84 and WTI at $88.56.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index Moves**: Nasdaq -0.91% to 25,136.27; S&P 500 -0.40%; Dow +0.10%.
- **Chip Rout**: AMD fell 8.75%, Broadcom 4.8%, Philadelphia Semiconductor Index 3.4%.
- **AI Infrastructure**: Dell fell 7.18% and Cisco 6.24%; IREN and Applied Digital also dropped.
- **Succession Uncertainty**: Financial Times says Nvidia's board approached Arm CEO Rene Haas.
- **Inflation Data**: Monthly PPI rose 0.3% in October, slowing from September's 1.4%.
- **Rate Odds**: Chance of a 0.5-point December hike rose to about 10%.
- **Breadth**: Over 60% of S&P 500 components advanced despite tech weakness.
- **Sentiment**: Ameriprise's Saglimbene says confidence is damaged; volatility likely persists.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did US stocks end mixed on November 13?
**A**: Nvidia's CEO resignation fallout and rising rates hit AI chip and tech stocks, dragging the Nasdaq and S&P 500 lower. Financial and healthcare gains kept the Dow slightly higher.

**Q2**: What data pushed rate-hike expectations and yields higher?
**A**: PPI rose 6.5% from a year earlier, the fastest since November 2022, and Fed officials Daly and Collins called for more tightening. The 10-year yield rose to 5.15% and a December hike is near certain.

**Q3**: What does the day's trading imply about exposure to the AI trade?
**A**: AI chip and infrastructure stocks fell together, even AMD and Broadcom, which had risen a day earlier as Nvidia rivals. Strategist Saglimbene expects AI and tech stocks to stay volatile.
