## Summary

On November 10, 2026, US stocks fell after October headline and core CPI both topped forecasts, boosting bets on Fed rate hikes and lifting Treasury yields. Nvidia dropped 5.53% amid an independent probe into CEO Jensen Huang and scrutiny of AI financing costs. Anthropic set an IPO price range of $70 to $78, for a deal of up to about $90.4 billion.

## Core Concepts

- [[Consumer Price Index]]: October CPI rose 0.4% month over month against 0.3% expected; core rose 0.3% against 0.2%.
- [[Federal Reserve]]: [[Christopher Waller]], [[Lorie Logan]], and [[Michelle Bowman]] signalled further hikes.
- [[Nvidia]]: fell 5.53% to $168.78 as independent directors hired Joon H. Kim to run the Jensen Huang probe.
- [[Anthropic IPO]]: $70 to $78 per share, 825 million shares, ticker "ANT" on Nasdaq.
- [[AI Infrastructure Financing]]: Morgan Stanley sees leveraged finance for AI staying subdued through year-end.
- [[Treasury Yields]]: rose after the CPI report, pressuring tech valuations.
- [[Claude Sheets]]: Anthropic's new workplace tools, competing with Microsoft and Google.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index Closes**: Dow -0.30% to 52,231.89; Nasdaq -0.74% to 25,065.87; S&P 500 -0.38% to 7,520.93.
- **Hot CPI**: Headline CPI +0.4% and core +0.3% month over month, both above forecasts.
- **Hike Debate**: Waller backed a 25bp December hike; a 50bp hike gained discussion.
- **Logan View**: At least two more 25bp hikes needed; pace could be faster this winter.
- **Bowman Warning**: Disappointing AI returns could trigger a sharp stock valuation correction.
- **Nvidia Drop**: Fell 5.53% on the Huang probe and AI financing scrutiny.
- **Anthropic IPO**: Up to about $90.4B including $26B private placements; roughly $1.4T valuation.
- **AI Debt**: $5B October AI leveraged issuance; $646B year-to-date versus $216B in all of 2025.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did US stocks fall on November 10, 2026?
**A**: October headline and core CPI both came in above expectations, strengthening bets on faster Fed rate hikes and pushing Treasury yields up, which pressured tech stocks.

**Q2**: What did Morgan Stanley report about AI financing?
**A**: AI-related leveraged finance issuance was just $5 billion in October, all from a Volta Infrastructure term loan yielding over 11%. High borrowing costs and possible construction delays should keep it constrained through year-end, even as year-to-date AI and data center debt issuance reached $646 billion.

**Q3**: What does the Fed commentary imply for investors?
**A**: Officials see inflation as stubborn and suggest more hikes, possibly at a faster pace. Bowman added that disappointing AI returns could trigger a sharp valuation correction, so tech valuations and AI financing costs warrant close scrutiny.
