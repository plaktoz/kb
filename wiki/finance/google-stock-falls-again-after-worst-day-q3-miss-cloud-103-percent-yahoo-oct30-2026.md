## Summary

[[Alphabet]] fell another 1% on Friday after its worst day since its 2004 IPO, when it missed Q3 EPS ($2.12 vs. $3.01 expected) and advertising expectations despite 103% [[Google Cloud]] growth. Negative free cash flow, the Gemini 4 Carbon launch and competition from [[Meta Muse]] shape the debate over whether hyperscaler AI spending will pay off.

## Core Concepts

- [[Alphabet]] Q3 results: revenue $127.58B, advertising $82.01B vs. $83.5B expected, cloud $30.71B vs. $29.1B expected.
- [[Free Cash Flow]]: worsened from negative $5.85B in Q2 to negative $9.06B in Q3.
- [[Gemini 4 Carbon]]: new frontier model, claimed to beat [[GPT-6 Astra]] and [[Claude Opus 5.5]].
- [[Google Cloud]] and TPUs: [[Oracle]] will be the first to buy Google's AI processors for its own data centers.
- [[Meta Muse]]: 10 million users have set up the agent, mostly for shopping, a possible long-term search threat.
- [[AI Capex]] concerns: [[CoreWeave]] debt fears hit the market last week.
- [[Sundar Pichai]] and [[Dan Ives]]: Pichai calls agents an "expansion opportunity" for search; Ives is bullish on cloud and TPUs.

## Key Takeaways

- **Record Drop**: Nearly 13% fall Thursday, Google's worst day since 2004.
- **Earnings Miss**: EPS $2.12 versus $3.01 expected; ads missed by $1.5B.
- **Cloud Strength**: Cloud revenue $30.71B versus $29.1B expected, growing 103%.
- **Cash Burn**: Free cash flow fell to negative $9.06B from negative $5.85B.
- **Gemini Scale**: The Gemini app passed 1 billion users, the company's fastest-growing.
- **Search Risk**: Meta's Muse could threaten [[Google Search]] if shopping use grows.
- **Cannibalization Debate**: Pichai says agents expand search rather than cannibalize it.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Google stock fall despite strong cloud growth?
**A**: Q3 EPS and advertising sales missed expectations, and free cash flow worsened to negative $9.06B. Investors also doubt whether hyperscaler AI investments will pay off.

**Q2**: What key figures show where Google beat and missed?
**A**: Cloud revenue of $30.71B beat the $29.1B forecast, but advertising sales of $82.01B missed the $83.5B estimate. EPS was $2.12 versus $3.01 expected.

**Q3**: What long-term risk does the article flag for Google Search?
**A**: Meta's Muse agent, which 10 million people have set up mostly for shopping, could take ground in online sales. The risk is migrating users to agentic search while maintaining strong monetization.
