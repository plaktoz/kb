## Summary

[[Michael Burry]] has flagged roughly $3 trillion in obscured investment exposure tied to [[Microsoft]] and other AI companies, warning that massive AI data center projects are financed through "off balance sheet structures that do not appear as conventional debt." He argues that if AI demand comes in softer than anticipated, this could trigger major writedowns and strain both tech firms and their lenders.

## Core Concepts

- [[Michael Burry]] — investor known for prescient bearish calls; here warns of hidden AI-related financial exposure
- [[Microsoft]] — specific target of the warning via "AI infrastructure commitments, long term energy deals and capacity contracts"
- [[Off-Balance-Sheet Financing]] — obligations structured so they don't appear as conventional debt on a company's balance sheet
- [[AI Infrastructure Capex]] — the underlying spending driving these financing structures
- [[Azure]] — Microsoft's cloud unit whose upcoming FY27 capex and revenue disclosures are framed as the next key indicator

## Key Takeaways

- **Hidden exposure**: Burry estimates ~$3 trillion in AI-linked obligations obscured from conventional debt metrics.
- **Mechanism**: Off-balance-sheet structures cover AI infra commitments, long-term energy deals, and capacity contracts.
- **Downside scenario**: Softer-than-expected AI demand could cause major writedowns for tech firms and lenders.
- **Not new, but reinforcing**: Framed as reinforcement of Microsoft's existing "heavy AI and cloud investment" risk narrative.
- **Next signal to watch**: Microsoft's upcoming capex and Azure revenue disclosures under its revamped FY27 reporting format.
- **Separate consideration**: Article notes leadership decisions and executive compensation can independently shape long-term outcomes.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Off-Balance-Sheet Risk]]**: Structuring obligations so they don't register as conventional debt doesn't eliminate the liability — it just moves the point of discovery to a stress event, echoing prior financial-crisis-era warnings about hidden leverage.
