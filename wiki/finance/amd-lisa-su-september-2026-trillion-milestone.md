## Summary

[[AMD]] CEO [[Lisa Su]] emerged as one of tech's most prominent leaders in September 2026, attending the US-China state dinner seated next to [[Elon Musk]] and ringing the NYSE opening bell alongside the First Lady. AMD's market cap crossed $1 trillion for the first time, with shares up 193% year-to-date, fueled by record Q2 results and landmark deals with [[Microsoft]] Azure and [[Anthropic]].

## Core Concepts

- **AMD's $1 Trillion Market Cap**: AMD shares surged 9.9% to breach the $1 trillion threshold in late September 2026, far outpacing [[Nvidia]]'s 22% YTD gain with a 193% stock price rise.
- **Q2 2026 Record Results**: AMD posted record non-GAAP EPS of $1.66 and revenue of $11.5 billion (up 50% YoY), with [[Data Center]] revenue more than doubling to $6.7 billion.
- **[[Instinct MI450]] Series GPUs**: AMD's new flagship AI accelerators, part of the Helios rack-scale AI system, secured a major Azure deployment deal and the Anthropic partnership.
- **[[Helios]] Rack-Scale AI System**: AMD's integrated rack system designed for hyperscale AI deployments; [[Microsoft]] will deploy Helios across [[Azure]] AI services in H2 2026.
- **AMD-Anthropic Partnership**: AMD committed up to $5 billion in [[Anthropic]] with up to 2 gigawatts of MI450 GPUs to be deployed starting H1 2027 — a major vote of confidence in AMD's AI hardware stack.
- **[[EPYC Venice]] CPUs**: AMD's 6th Gen server CPUs, alongside MI450, anchor the company's compute portfolio for 2026–2027 data center build-outs.
- **[[Lisa Su]] Political Visibility**: Su was seated next to Elon Musk at the Trump-Xi state dinner and joined the First Lady at the NYSE — signaling AMD's arrival as a peer to Nvidia at the highest levels of US tech policy.
