## Summary

IBM shares rose 3.9% on August 27, 2026 to $238.79 on reaffirmed free-cash-flow guidance and the completed HRL Laboratories acquisition, with GuruFocus's GF Value model still placing the stock about 1.4% below fair value despite the rally.

## Core Concepts

- [[GF Value]] — GuruFocus's intrinsic value estimate derived from historical trading multiples, past growth and future performance projections; used here to argue IBM retains a slim margin of safety even after a sharp one-day rally.
- [[GF Score]] — a composite 0-100 rating combining financial strength, profitability, growth, valuation and momentum; IBM scores 81/100, with a maxed-out valuation rank (10/10) offset by only middling financial strength (5/10).
- [[Insider Buying Signal]] — insiders purchased $0.4M in IBM shares over the past 12 months with zero selling, a pattern GuruFocus frames as a confidence signal.

## Key Takeaways

- **Price move**: IBM rose 3.9% to $238.79 on Aug 27, within a 52-week range of $199.19–$332.46.
- **Valuation read**: GF Value of $242.26 puts the post-rally price 1.4% below fair value — still a marginal buy signal.
- **Historical discount**: current P/E of 21.2x sits well below the 5-year median of 25.2x.
- **Score breakdown**: GF Score 81/100 — Valuation 10/10, Profitability 8/10, Growth 6/10, Momentum 5/10, Financial Strength 5/10.
- **Guru sentiment**: 13 gurus hold IBM, with 6 adding and 5 trimming positions in recent quarters — a mixed but net-positive institutional signal.
- **Catalyst**: the rally followed IBM's completed HRL Laboratories acquisition (expanding quantum hardware capabilities) and management's reaffirmed guidance on free-cash-flow growth.
