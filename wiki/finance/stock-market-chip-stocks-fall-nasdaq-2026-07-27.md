## Summary

On July 27, 2026, [[Nvidia]] shares fell 5% — dropping its market cap below [[Apple]] — on concerns it may backstop a ~$250B [[OpenAI]] data-center project, dragging the [[Nasdaq]] down 0.2%. [[Oil prices]] tumbled below $89/barrel (Brent crude off 8.7%) after the Trump administration paused its Iran military escalation, benefiting travel stocks while pressuring the energy sector. Markets awaited a pivotal [[Federal Reserve]] rate decision and major tech earnings from [[Microsoft]] and [[Meta]] later in the week.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]]** — Led chip-stock selloff; market cap fell below [[Apple]] amid $250B OpenAI backstop concerns
- **[[OpenAI]] data-center project** — Potential ~$250B financial backstop by Nvidia raising investor concern
- **[[Brent Crude]]** — Front-month futures dropped 8.7%, falling below $89/barrel
- **[[Trump]] Iran policy** — Pause in war escalation triggered oil price decline and travel stock rally
- **[[CXMT]]** — Chinese memory-chip maker debuted as the most valuable company listed in mainland China
- **[[Federal Reserve]] rate decision** — Described as one of the least predictable in years; due Wednesday
- **[[Salesforce]]** — 6.1% rally lifted the Dow 0.5% even as broader tech sold off
- **[[Nasdaq]] / [[S&P 500]] / [[Dow]]** — Mixed session: Dow +0.5%, Nasdaq -0.2%, S&P 500 near flat

- **Nvidia drop**: Shares fell 5%, market cap slipped below Apple's on data-center backstop risk.
