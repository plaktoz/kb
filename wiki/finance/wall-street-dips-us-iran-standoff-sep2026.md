## Summary

US stocks fell Monday (Sept. 28) as the unresolved [[US-Iran Conflict]] kept oil prices and Treasury yields elevated, with the Nasdaq down 0.9%, the S&P 500 down 0.8%, and the Dow down 0.7%. Trump reportedly rejected a conditional Iranian peace offer and plans to resume military action after the November midterms, while investors braced for this week's PCE inflation data, September nonfarm payrolls, and a raft of corporate earnings.

## Core Concepts

- **[[US-Iran Conflict]]**: [[Donald Trump]] reportedly turned down a conditional peace offer in which Iran proposed reopening the [[Strait of Hormuz]] within seven days in exchange for renewed negotiations; plans reportedly exist to resume military action after the November midterms.
- **Diplomatic maneuvering**: Trump told Axios he expects further engagement with Iran this week; [[Qatar]] mediators were reportedly arranging separate talks with both sides; [[Saxo Bank]] said Trump believes Iran "had overplayed its hand."
- **[[Oil Prices]]**: WTI crude rose 0.9% to $93.28/barrel; Brent rose 1.7% to $106.09, reflecting the geopolitical risk premium.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: 10-year yield climbed to 5.24%; two-year yield rose to 4.93%.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] rate odds**: CME FedWatch data showed odds of a rate hike next month rising to 70%.
- **Macro data week**: Investors awaiting [[PCE Inflation]] data and the September [[Nonfarm Payrolls]] report; [[Jefferies]] projected payroll growth above 175,000, citing labor market improvement since mid-2025.
- **[[Boeing]]**: Shares fell 6.9% on a reported software issue in [[737 MAX]] jets tied to navigation and landing safety concerns.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Shares rose 1.7% after unveiling a $150 billion buyback expansion and a new AI governance software platform.
- **Earnings week ahead**: [[Micron]], [[Accenture]], and [[Nike]] among companies reporting.
- **Precious metals**: Spot gold rose 0.4% to $4,131.44/oz; silver fell 5.9% to $61.01/oz.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index moves**: Nasdaq -0.9% to 26,820.38; S&P 500 -0.8% to 7,683.69; Dow -0.7% to 51,481.51.
- **Sector split**: Communication services led declines; consumer staples outperformed.
- **Iran peace offer rejected**: Trump turned down Iran's conditional Strait of Hormuz reopening proposal.
- **Post-midterm timeline**: Military action against Iran reportedly planned to resume after November midterms.
- **Oil and yields both up**: WTI +0.9%, Brent +1.7%, 10-year yield to 5.24% on unresolved tensions.
- **Fed hike odds rise**: 70% probability priced in for next month per CME FedWatch.
- **Boeing safety scare**: 737 MAX navigation/landing software issue drove a 6.9% share drop.
- **Nvidia buyback news**: $150B buyback expansion plus new AI governance platform lifted shares 1.7%.
