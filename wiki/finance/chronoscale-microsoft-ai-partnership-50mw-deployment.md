# ChronoScale plans 50 MW AI deployment under two-year Microsoft partnership

## Summary

[[ChronoScale]] and [[Microsoft]] formed a two-year strategic partnership to support a planned 50 MW AI compute deployment, disclosed in ChronoScale's annual report alongside fiscal 2026 revenue of $71.6 million, down 15% year-over-year. The filing withheld key deal terms — location, investment size, pricing, and whether Microsoft committed to a take-or-pay purchase of the full capacity — while ChronoScale continues to post large losses and depends on financial backing from parent company [[Applied Digital]].

## Core Concepts

- [[ChronoScale]]: An [[Applied Digital]] subsidiary providing cloud services from third-party [[Colocation Centers]] in Colorado, Minnesota, and Utah, pivoting toward AI/ML customers.
- [[Microsoft]]: Counterparty in a two-year deal granting it penalties and credits if ChronoScale misses project milestones, without a confirmed take-or-pay commitment.
- [[NVIDIA GB300]]: The AI compute hardware platform planned for the 50 MW deployment, paired with advanced [[Liquid Cooling]] for high-density workloads.
- [[Applied Digital]] (NASDAQ: APLD): Parent company funding ChronoScale's operations via a demand promissory note.
- [[Working Capital Deficit]]: ChronoScale's financial position — $9.7 million cash against a $42.6 million deficit as of May 31.
- [[Ekso Bionics]]: Non-core business ChronoScale plans to divest during fiscal 2027 to focus on AI cloud services.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal scope**: 50 MW AI compute deployment paired with [[NVIDIA GB300]] systems and liquid cooling.
- **Timeline**: ChronoScale targets Q1 2027 completion, contingent on financing and construction milestones.
- **Disclosure gaps**: Location, investment size, pricing, and take-or-pay status were not disclosed.
- **Downside protection for Microsoft**: Microsoft can collect penalties/credits if ChronoScale misses conditions.
- **Financial strain**: Fiscal 2026 revenue fell 15% to $71.6 million after a December 2024 customer loss.
- **Improving but still large losses**: Operating loss narrowed to $37.5 million from $55.3 million; net loss improved to $49.3 million from $72.7 million.
- **Liquidity lifeline**: A $100 million demand promissory note from [[Applied Digital]] has $93 million undrawn after a $7 million July 1 draw.
- **Strategic refocus**: ChronoScale plans to divest [[Ekso Bionics]] and concentrate on AI/ML cloud services.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Asymmetric Information]]**: ChronoScale's filing discloses the partnership's existence and Microsoft's downside protections while withholding financial terms that would let investors assess the deal's real value — a classic case of one party (the company) controlling what gets revealed to another (investors).
