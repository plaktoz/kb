## Summary
CBS reports that a tech rout on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, took the [[S&P 500]] and Dow into correction territory and erased the [[Nasdaq Composite]]'s 2026 gains with a 5.3% drop. Anthropic's IPO debut fizzled and Nvidia kept sliding. An E*TRADE strategist notes that fundamentals remain solid and only five of 15 corrections since 1983 became bear markets.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Stock Market Correction]]**: A fall of more than 10% from the recent high; S&P 500 and Dow both qualify.
- **[[AI Stock Valuations]]**: AI names drove earlier gains and now drive the slide.
- **[[Semiconductor Export Controls]]**: Chip losses tied to data center slowdown worries and tighter federal chip export policy.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Down 9.3% to $88.14 versus an $83 IPO price.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Down about 13% on the ninth decline in 10 days, pressured by the [[Jensen Huang]] scandal.
- **[[Chris Larkin]]** and **[[Michael Antonelli]]**: Strategists offering a measured read on the sell-off.

## Key Takeaways
- **Nasdaq**: Fell 1,261 points (5.3%) to 22,618, its second-worst day of 2026.
- **Losing streak**: S&P 500's seventh straight loss, longest since February 2020.
- **Chip drivers**: AMD and Broadcom led losses on data center and export-policy worries.
- **Base rate**: Only five of 15 corrections since 1983 became 20% bear markets.
- **Fundamentals**: Underlying US economic and business fundamentals remain solid, per E*TRADE.
- **Defensive gains**: General Mills rose 4.4% and Lennar 2.5%.
