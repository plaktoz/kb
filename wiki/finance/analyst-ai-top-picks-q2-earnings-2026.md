## Summary

Wall Street analysts made five major moves on AI-exposed equities ahead of Q2/fiscal Q4 earnings, with Morgan Stanley flagging [[Microsoft]] as highly attractive, Wedbush adding [[Alphabet]] and [[Reddit]] to its Best Ideas List, and Mizuho naming [[Meta Platforms]] its top mega-cap pick. Citi argued the semiconductor pullback is a buying opportunity, while Jefferies downgraded [[Datadog]] on valuation after a 94% YTD rally. Across the board, analysts are watching AI capex discipline versus monetization as the central Q2 earnings narrative.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] (MSFT)** — Morgan Stanley top pick; $600 PT (57% upside); Azure growth 41% constant-currency; [[M365 Copilot]] seats at 20M; July price hike adds $2–6B incremental revenue FY2027–2029.
- **[[Alphabet]] (GOOGL)** — [[Google Cloud]] revenue +82% YoY to $24.8B; cloud operating margin expanded to 35.6%; backlog $514B; capex guidance raised to $195–205B.
- **[[Reddit]] (RDDT)** — Active advertisers +75% YoY; [[AI data licensing]] deals with [[Google]] (~$60M/yr) and [[OpenAI]] (~$70M/yr).
- **[[Meta Platforms]] (META)** — Mizuho Outperform, $835 PT; lease deal with [[Anthropic]] worth up to $10B over two years flagged as first capex ROI signal.
- **[[Semiconductor]] sector** — Citi buying opportunity thesis; data centers = 34% of total semiconductor demand, on track to exceed full semiconductor TAM by 2030; [[Intel]] 2026 capex >$20B; [[TSMC]] 2026 capex $60–64B + additional $100B Arizona investment.
- **[[Datadog]] (DDOG)** — Jefferies downgrade to Hold; ~18x EV/CY27 revenue, 4-turn premium to [[Snowflake]]; shares +94% YTD.
- **[[Palantir]]** — Jefferies turned negative citing escalating comps and competition.
- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]** — Cross-company theme: whether massive infrastructure spend will convert to measurable earnings uplift.

## Key Takeaways

- **Morgan Stanley**: MSFT "highly attractive"; $600 PT; Azure growth 41% CC confirmed by CIO surveys.
- **Copilot momentum**: M365 Copilot seats grew from 15M → 20M in one quarter.
- **Google Cloud acceleration**: Revenue growth jumped from 63% → 82% YoY in a single quarter.
- **Reddit AI licensing**: ~$130M/yr in aggregate from Google + OpenAI data deals.
- **Meta/Anthropic deal**: Up to $10B compute lease; Mizuho estimates $1–1.25/share EPS annually.
- **Semis as infrastructure**: Data center demand could exceed full semiconductor TAM by 2030.
- **Datadog valuation stretched**: 4-turn EV/revenue premium to Snowflake leaves no cushion.
- **Jefferies favors**: Amazon, Microsoft, Atlassian into earnings; negative on Palantir.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Price-to-Value Gap]]**: Morgan Stanley's 57% implied upside thesis on MSFT reflects the principle that temporary market pessimism creates asymmetric risk/reward when fundamentals (CIO survey data, Copilot seat growth) remain intact — price and value diverge during uncertainty, converging over time.
- **[[Capex as a Moat Signal]]**: The multi-hundred-billion capex commitments by Microsoft ($190B), Alphabet ($195–205B), and TSMC ($60–64B + $100B Arizona) exemplify the first-principles idea that scale infrastructure spending, once deployed, creates durable competitive barriers that are nearly impossible for smaller rivals to replicate.
