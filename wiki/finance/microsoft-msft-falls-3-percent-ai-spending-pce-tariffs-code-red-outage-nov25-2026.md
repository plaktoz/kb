## Summary

[[Microsoft]] ([[MSFT]]) fell more than 3% on Wednesday as AI spending worries, sticky [[PCE Inflation]] and proposed tariffs hit tech stocks, while a 24-hour [[Microsoft 365]] outage raised reliability questions. Microsoft reportedly launched a "Code Red" engineering overhaul, and analysts still carry a Buy consensus with a $644.46 average target.

## Core Concepts

- [[Microsoft]] ([[MSFT]]): Down more than 3%, trading below its 20-day and 50-day moving averages.
- [[AI Infrastructure Spending]]: Investor doubts about sustainability weighed on Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet and Meta.
- [[PCE Inflation]]: October PCE rose 3.8% year over year; core PCE rose 3.3% against a 3.2% forecast.
- [[Trump Tariffs]]: [[Daniel Newman]] of Futurum Group warned they could lift yields and inflation and push the Fed hawkish.
- [[Microsoft 365 Outage]]: 24-hour disruption of Outlook, Teams and Copilot tied to a Windows scalability defect from July.
- [[Code Red]]: Reported engineering overhaul covering traffic management, safeguards and faster recovery.
- [[Simple Moving Average]]: Technical signal used to judge short-term weakness.
- [[Vanguard Growth ETF]]: Holds Microsoft at 9.74%, alongside iShares Global Tech and Xtrackers funds near 9.8-9.9%.

## Key Takeaways

- **Market Move**: MSFT fell over 3% while S&P 500 futures dropped about 2.80%.
- **Peer Declines**: Amazon, Alphabet and Meta fell between 3% and 6%.
- **Inflation Data**: Core PCE of 3.3% beat the 3.2% forecast.
- **Outage Cause**: An undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July.
- **Fix Speed Goal**: Deploy critical security fixes worldwide in one hour, versus five to six days.
- **Technical Picture**: 6.1% below the 20-day SMA of $516.90; 0.9% below the 100-day.
- **Analyst View**: Buy consensus, $644.46 average target; Bernstein highest cited at $722.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Microsoft stock fall on Wednesday?
**A**: AI infrastructure spending doubts, sticky inflation, and proposed Trump tariffs pressured tech stocks, while a major cloud outage raised reliability questions.

**Q2**: What caused the Microsoft 365 outage and how is Microsoft responding?
**A**: An undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July caused a 24-hour disruption. Microsoft reportedly launched a "Code Red" overhaul with better traffic management, safeguards and faster recovery, aiming for one-hour global security fixes.

**Q3**: How does Microsoft's weight in major ETFs matter for its stock?
**A**: It makes up roughly 9.7-9.9% of VUG, IXN and SNPE, so ETF flows and rebalancing can influence trading activity in the stock.
