## Summary

[[Alphabet]] CEO [[Sundar Pichai]] described the company as "supply constrained" despite committing up to $205 billion in capital spending in 2026, underscoring that AI compute demand is outpacing even its massive buildout capacity. Paradoxically, Alphabet has agreed to sell multi-gigawatt blocks of that scarce capacity to [[Anthropic]] — including a 5-gigawatt next-gen TPU deal announced in April 2026 — concentrating a significant share of its future contracted cloud revenue on a single young AI company. Investors face a dual risk: extraordinary growth (Google Cloud up 82% YoY to $24.8B in Q2) funded by negative free cash flow, and a $514B backlog whose realization depends substantially on Anthropic's continued commercial success.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Google Cloud]]**: Q2 2026 revenue of $24.8B (+82% YoY); operating income tripled from $2.8B to $8.8B; margin expanded to ~36% from ~21%.
- **[[Compute Scarcity]]**: Alphabet's self-described "supply constraint" — despite $44.9B in Q2 CapEx — illustrates that AI infrastructure demand is structurally outpacing supply even for the largest hyperscalers.
- **[[Anthropic]]**: Secured two major TPU capacity deals with Alphabet: an October 2025 deal (1M TPUs, 1+ GW, "tens of billions") and an April 2026 expansion (~5 GW next-gen TPU via Google and Broadcom, starting 2027).
- **[[Concentrated Revenue Risk]]**: A Broadcom securities filing flags that Anthropic's use of the expanded capacity "is dependent on Anthropic's continued commercial success" — making Alphabet's future contracted revenue partially hostage to a single startup.
- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]**: Alphabet raised $49.6B from stock sales and issued $20.3B in senior notes to fund infrastructure at a scale where CapEx ($44.9B) exceeded Q2 operating cash flow ($39.1B).

## Key Takeaways

- **Google Cloud Q2**: $24.8B revenue (+82% YoY); operating income $8.8B; margin ~36%.
- **Cloud backlog**: $514B (up from $462B at end of Q1 2026).
- **Anthropic October deal**: 1M Google TPUs, 1+ GW capacity coming online 2026; "tens of billions."
- **Anthropic April 2026 deal**: ~5 GW next-gen TPU capacity via Google and Broadcom, starting 2027.
- **Google's Anthropic investment**: Up to $40 billion ($10B immediate, up to $30B more tied to milestones).
- **Anthropic revenue**: Annualized run rate surpassed $30B in April 2026 (up from ~$9B end of 2025).
- **2026 CapEx guidance**: $195–$205B; Q2 CapEx $44.9B exceeded operating cash flow.
- **Financing**: $49.6B raised from stock sales (June); $20.3B in senior notes issued.
- **GOOGL valuation**: Trading ~$339, ~23x forward earnings; total revenue +24% YoY.
- **Key risk**: A material slice of Alphabet's $514B backlog depends on Anthropic scaling into its commitments.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Competitive Moat via Resource Acquisition]]**: Selling scarce compute capacity to Anthropic locks in a dominant AI lab as a long-term customer, but also creates a moat-cum-liability — Alphabet's capacity buildout is partially co-financed by Anthropic's commitments, meaning the moat's value tracks Anthropic's survival.
- **[[Concentration Risk]]**: Diversification is a first-principles hedge against correlated failure; Alphabet's backlog is exposed to single-company concentration, violating that principle even as overall cloud revenue diversifies rapidly.
