## Summary
Anthropic set an IPO range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million shares, planning to price Nov. 18 and trade Nov. 19 on the Nasdaq as "ANT". At the high end it would raise up to $64.35 billion at a fully diluted value of up to $1.405 trillion, below earlier $2 trillion hopes. Institutional demand is reportedly stronger than SpaceX's at this stage, despite political and interest-rate headwinds.

## Core Concepts
- [[Anthropic]]: 5-year-old AI lab behind the Claude models, led by CEO [[Dario Amodei]]; sells mainly to businesses.
- [[Initial Public Offering]]: 825 million shares at $70-$78; ticker "ANT" on the [[Nasdaq]].
- [[Retail Allocation]]: targeted at about 20%, versus a typical 5% to 10%.
- [[Private Placement]]: $26 billion of Class N stock sold at the IPO price to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC, with no lock-up.
- [[SpaceX IPO]]: the record $85.7 billion offering five months earlier, used as the benchmark.
- [[AI Valuation Risk]]: investors doubt even a $1.5 trillion valuation amid rising rates and an AI-skeptical public market.

## Key Takeaways
- **Pricing Terms**: 825 million shares at $70-$78; pricing Nov. 18, trading Nov. 19.
- **Proceeds and Valuation**: Up to $64.35B raised; up to $1.405T fully diluted, versus $965B in May.
- **Valuation Reset**: Earlier $2T target; investors now call even $1.5T a "stretch".
- **Demand Signal**: Book already covered at the range on an allocable basis; stronger than SpaceX.
- **Retail Tilt**: About 20% retail allocation, well above the typical 5%-10%.
- **Strategic Placement**: $26B private placement by chip and cloud partners at the IPO price, unlocked.
- **Financials**: $33.24B nine-month revenue, up about 1,200%; $104B net loss, mostly fair-value changes.
- **Run Rate**: Roughly $81B run-rate revenue at end of October, up $10B month over month.
- **Headwinds**: Democratic midterm sweep, ballot measures that could slow AI buildout, rising rates.
- **Model Launch**: Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 released Monday, the first frontier models since September.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What are the key terms and timing of Anthropic's IPO?
**A**: Anthropic will offer 825 million shares at $70 to $78, pricing on Nov. 18 and trading on the Nasdaq as "ANT" on Nov. 19. The high end would raise up to $64.35 billion and value the company at up to $1.405 trillion fully diluted.

**Q2**: What evidence shows strong demand despite the valuation doubts?
**A**: The book is reportedly already covered at the range on an allocable basis, with institutional demand stronger than SpaceX's at this point. Seven partners, including Nvidia, Google and Microsoft, also agreed to buy $26 billion of stock in a private placement at the IPO price.

**Q3**: What risks could weigh on the offering?
**A**: A Democratic congressional sweep and state ballot measures that could slow AI buildout, plus a public market worried that rising rates make infrastructure debt unaffordable. Investors also warn the valuation may fall well short of the earlier $2 trillion target.
