## Summary

The Straits Times Index fell 0.4% to 5,671.91 on Aug. 20, 2026, snapping the index's recent run of gains covered in [[Singapore Sti Rally May Stall Aug2026]] and related notes, with local banks DBS, OCBC and UOB all lower and Sats the worst-performing constituent.

## Core Concepts

This is a short-term pullback within the broader [[Straits Times Index]] narrative — regional indices were mostly higher (Hang Seng +0.8%, KLCI +0.3%, Nikkei +1.4%, Kospi +5.9%), suggesting the STI's dip on Aug. 20 was more idiosyncratic (bank and Sats weakness) than a region-wide risk-off move, per OCBC Group Research's characterization of "choppy" trading tied to Fed minutes, tariff talk, and a US Treasury liquidity announcement.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI down 0.4%**: Closed at 5,671.91, a 22.33-point drop.
- **Bank weakness**: DBS, OCBC and UOB — the STI's three local banks — all closed lower.
- **Sats worst performer**: Fell 13.6% on the day.
- **Region diverged**: Hong Kong, Malaysia, Japan and South Korea indices all rose the same session.
