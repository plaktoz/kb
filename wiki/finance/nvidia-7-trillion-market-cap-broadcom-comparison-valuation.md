## Summary

Nvidia's market cap of roughly $5.45 trillion sits about $1.55 trillion short of $7 trillion — a gap nearly equivalent to all of [[Broadcom]]'s market value (~$1.69 trillion). Reaching $7 trillion would require the stock to climb from ~$225 to ~$290 per share, a ~28% gain, which would imply roughly 18x FY2028 earnings — in line with what Broadcom trades at today.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]] Market Cap Gap**: At ~$5.45 trillion, Nvidia needs to add ~$1.55 trillion to hit $7 trillion — comparable to adding nearly one entire [[Broadcom]] (~$1.69 trillion).
- **[[Stock Valuation]] Multiple Path**: Reaching $290/share at current forward earnings implies roughly 18x FY2028 earnings, up from the current ~14x forward earnings multiple.
- **Q2 FY2027 Earnings**: Revenue hit $96.2 billion (+106% YoY), net income $59.7 billion (+126% YoY); Q3 guidance is $108 billion.
- **FY2028 Revenue Projection**: CFO [[Colette Kress]] guided ~70% revenue growth for FY2028 — a "supply-constrained outlook" — implying ~$700 billion from an analyst consensus FY2027 base of ~$412 billion.
- **[[Gross Margin]] Outlook**: Expected to settle around 72–73% for FY2028.

## Key Takeaways

- **Cap gap in concrete terms**: Reaching $7T requires adding roughly a whole Broadcom (~$1.69T).
- **Share price target**: $290/share implies ~28% upside from ~$225; already climbed 37% from 52-week low.
- **Multiple expansion needed**: Price path requires re-rating from ~14x to ~18x FY2028 earnings.
- **Revenue trajectory**: FY2027 analyst consensus ~$412B; ~70% growth yields ~$700B for FY2028.
- **Key unknown**: Earnings forecast is largely in place; the swing variable is what valuation multiple the market assigns.
- **Supply-constrained**: CFO framed the 70% growth outlook as limited by supply, not demand.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mr. Market]]**: The article illustrates that near-term price targets are primarily a function of market sentiment (the multiple assigned) rather than earnings fundamentals — the earnings forecast is known, but the multiple is what moves the stock toward or away from $7 trillion.

**Q3**: What does CFO Colette Kress's "supply-constrained" framing imply for investors?
