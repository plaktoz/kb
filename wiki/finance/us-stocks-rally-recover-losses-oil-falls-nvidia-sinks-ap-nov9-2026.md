## Summary
The [[S&P 500]] jumped 2.3% on Monday, its best day since April, recovering most of the prior week's loss as Brent crude fell 5.8% to $92.57 on signs of restraint in the Iran war. AI-linked stocks such as [[Advanced Micro Devices]] and [[CoreWeave]] rebounded, while [[Nvidia]] fell 4.3% amid an investigation of CEO [[Jensen Huang]]. Data-center scrutiny after Democratic election wins remains a worry for the AI build-out.

## Core Concepts
- [[S&P 500]]: up 173.08 points to 7,549.94, now 3.4% below its all-time high set last month.
- [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] and [[Nasdaq Composite]]: +2.2% (52,389.75) and +2.7% (25,253.14).
- [[Brent Crude]]: down 5.8% to $92.57 from above $112 at the start of the month.
- [[Donald Trump]] and [[Marco Rubio]]: Trump called resumed strikes "highly unlikely" if talks progress; Rubio called Iranian demands "outrageous and ridiculous."
- [[Constellation Energy]]: up 10.5% on earnings beats and data-center power deals.
- [[AI Data Center Build-Out]]: faces voter backlash over power bills and possible government restrictions.
- [[Nvidia]] and [[Jensen Huang]]: board is investigating Huang's relationship with an executive allegedly gathering information for China.
- [[10-Year Treasury Yield]]: 4.87%, well below last month's two-decade peak above 5.30%.

## Key Takeaways
- **Best Day Since April**: S&P 500 gained 2.3%, trimming its gap to the record high to 3.4%.
- **Oil Relief**: Brent fell 5.8% to $92.57 on Iran negotiation optimism.
- **AI Rebound**: AMD rose 13.3% and CoreWeave 5.6%, recovering last week's losses.
- **Nvidia Divergence**: Shares fell 4.3% as the board probed CEO Jensen Huang.
- **Data-Center Risk**: Statehouse Democratic wins raise fears of restrictions on AI infrastructure.
- **Yields Steady**: 10-year Treasury inched up to 4.87% from 4.86%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What drove Monday's stock market rally?
**A**: Falling oil prices on signs of restraint in the Iran war, plus strong corporate profit reports, lifted stocks. The S&P 500 rose 2.3%, its best day since April.

**Q2**: How did AI-linked stocks behave, and what was the exception?
**A**: AMD rose 13.3% and CoreWeave 5.6%, recovering last week's losses, and Constellation Energy climbed 10.5%. Nvidia fell 4.3% as its board investigated CEO Jensen Huang.

**Q3**: What risk to the AI boom does the article highlight?
**A**: Voter blame for higher power bills could lead governments to restrict data centers, meaning fewer purchases of AI chips, electricity and related services. Borrowing costs and bubble worries add further pressure.
