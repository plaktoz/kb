## Summary

[[Anthropic]] raised over US$81 billion in the largest IPO in history on Nov 19 and closed 17% above its offer price, yet the debut failed to lift an AI-led tech sell-off. Gold had its worst weekly drop since March (about 7%) as rising real yields, a stronger dollar, and forced selling to cover equity losses weighed on it. Singapore's Straits Times Index fell to a six-month low below 5,000.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: Biggest IPO ever, topping [[SpaceX]]'s US$75 billion base deal; shares closed at US$97.17.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Fell 3.3% on Nov 19, a six-session losing streak, after reporting 103% revenue growth to US$115.8 billion.
- **AI sell-off drivers**: Expected further US rate hikes, chip export scrutiny, an AMD short-seller report, and Jensen Huang's departure as CEO.
- **[[Gold]] slump**: About 7% weekly fall to just above US$3,500, 37% below January's US$5,602 peak.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] outlook**: A December hike is largely priced in; key dates are the Dec 8-9 meeting and Dec 10 inflation data.
- **[[Straits Times Index]]**: Closed at 4,972.23; DBS, OCBC, and UOB all fell over the week.
- **HomesToLife SGX listing**: Secondary listing raised about S$8.6 million, the second SGX-Nasdaq dual listing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Record IPO**: Anthropic raised over US$81 billion; shares closed 17% above offer.
- **Sell-off Persists**: S&P 500 fell six sessions, down about 8% from October record.
- **Index Moves**: Nasdaq fell 2.25% and S&P 500 2.02% on Nov 19.
- **Gold Pressure**: Worst weekly decline since March; down 8.5% in 2026.
- **Yield Squeeze**: 10-year Treasury above 5%; real yields rose from 2.66% to 2.88%.
- **Singapore Weakness**: STI hit 4,980.81 low before closing at 4,972.23.
- **Gold Miners Hit**: CNMC Goldmine fell 13.61% on the week, roughly 60% below its March high.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Higher real yields raise the cost of holding non-yielding assets** — When inflation-adjusted yields rise, an asset that pays no interest becomes less attractive relative to alternatives. · *model:* [[Opportunity Cost]]
→ explains: Gold Pressure, Yield Squeeze

**T2. In drawdowns, investors sell what they can, not what they want** — Liquid winners get sold to cover losses elsewhere, so a falling asset does not necessarily mean its hedge thesis has failed. · *model:* [[Safe Haven Assets]]
→ explains: Gold Pressure, Sell-off Persists

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What happened to markets in the week of Anthropic's IPO?
**A**: Anthropic's record IPO closed up 17%, but tech stocks kept falling and the S&P 500 logged six straight losses. Gold also had its worst week since March.

**Q2**: Why did gold fall so sharply?
**A**: The 10-year yield rose above 5% and real yields climbed from 2.66% to 2.88%, while a stronger dollar added pressure. Investors were also selling gold to raise cash against equity losses.

**Q3**: What should investors watch next?
**A**: The Fed's Dec 8-9 meeting and US inflation data on Dec 10, with a December rate hike largely priced in. Gold needs real yields and the dollar to ease for a lasting recovery above US$3,850-3,900.
