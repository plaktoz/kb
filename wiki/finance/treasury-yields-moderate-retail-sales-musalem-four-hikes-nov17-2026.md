## Summary
Treasury yields gave back early gains on Nov. 17 as investors digested October retail sales and Fed commentary. A stronger-than-expected control-group reading and St. Louis Fed President Musalem's warning of up to four more hikes support the case for tighter policy, with traders pricing a 95% chance of a December quarter-point hike.

## Core Concepts
- [[10-Year Treasury Yield]]: slipped 1 basis point to 5.109%; the [[30-Year Treasury Yield]] was flat at 5.46% and the [[2-Year Treasury Yield]] fell 2 basis points to 4.806%.
- [[Retail Sales]]: October sales rose 0.2% to $748.2 billion (in line), up 6.88% year over year.
- [[Control Group Retail Sales]]: ex-gas and autos rose 0.6% versus a 0.4% consensus.
- [[Alberto Musalem]]: says the economy may need several more rate increases before spring 2027 to reach 2% inflation.
- [[Federal Reserve]] speakers and [[FOMC Minutes]]: [[Christopher Waller]] and [[John Williams]] due to speak; minutes due Wednesday.
- [[CME FedWatch]]: prices a 95% chance of a 25 basis point December hike.

## Key Takeaways
- **Yield Reversal**: 10-year yield fell 1 bp to 5.109% after earlier gains.
- **Retail Sales**: October headline +0.2% matched expectations; year over year +6.88%.
- **Hot Core Spending**: Ex-gas-and-autos sales +0.6% beat the 0.4% estimate.
- **Hawkish Fed**: Musalem sees possibly four more hikes before spring 2027.
- **December Odds**: Traders price a 95% chance of a quarter-point hike.
- **Next Catalyst**: FOMC minutes on the October hold arrive Wednesday.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**A**: Control-group sales rose 0.6% against a 0.4% consensus, which could reinforce the case for higher Fed interest rates.

**A**: The 10-year slipped to 5.109%, the 30-year held at 5.46% and the 2-year eased to 4.806%, while Musalem said several more hikes may be needed before spring 2027.

**Q3**: What should investors watch next?
**A**: Remarks from Waller and Williams and the Wednesday FOMC minutes, while the market prices a 95% chance of a December quarter-point hike.
