## Summary
Crunchbase's weekly roundup for Oct. 17-23, 2026 shows AI-related startups again dominating large U.S. funding rounds. Tenstorrent ($1.5B) and Flapping Airplanes ($1B) led the list, with biotech, space tech and defense rounds also appearing. The quirkiest was Warhound's $200M seed to breed military dogs.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Tenstorrent]]**: AI processor developer; $1.5B Series E at a $6.2B valuation.
- **[[Flapping Airplanes]]**: AI lab building cheaper-to-train models; $1B Series A led by [[Lux Capital]] and Index Ventures.
- **[[Warhound]]**: Co-founded by [[Palmer Luckey]], it will equip breeding dogs with [[Anduril]] technology.
- **[[AI in Drug Discovery]]**: Pathos AI ($237M) and Warp Sciences ($215M) apply AI to medicines.
- **[[Venture Capital]]**: Sequoia, Lightspeed, Spark and others led or joined the rounds.

## Key Takeaways
- **Top Round**: Tenstorrent raised $1.5B Series E at a $6.2B valuation.
- **Fast Follow-On**: Flapping Airplanes raised $1B within 10 months of a $180M seed.
- **Infrastructure AI**: ASI raised $325M Series C at a $3B valuation.
- **Space Tech**: Xona raised $250M Series D at a $2.4B valuation for next-gen GPS.
- **Biotech**: Pathos AI ($237M) and Star Therapeutics ($200M) both raised large rounds.
- **Marketplace**: Whop raised $200M Series D at a $5.1B valuation.
- **Manufacturing AI**: Arda Industries raised $160M Series A at a $1.5B valuation.
