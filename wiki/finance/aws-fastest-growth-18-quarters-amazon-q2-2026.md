## Summary

Amazon reported blockbuster Q2 2026 results with total revenue of $200.6 billion (+20% YoY) and operating income of $27.5 billion (+43% YoY), both exceeding consensus estimates. [[AWS]] achieved 37% revenue growth — its fastest pace in roughly five years — while its AI and chips businesses within AWS surpassed a combined $25 billion annual revenue run rate, each growing triple digits year-over-year. E-commerce, advertising, and grocery segments also hit multi-year highs, with shares rising ~15% on the results.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AWS]] (Amazon Web Services)** — cloud division posting 37% growth, fastest in ~18 quarters (~5 years), driven by AI infrastructure demand
- **[[Amazon AI & Chips]]** — both businesses surpassed $25B annual revenue run rate with triple-digit YoY growth inside AWS
- **[[Amazon Advertising]]** — $19.8 billion revenue (+26%), fastest growth since 2023
- **[[Prime Day Timing Shift]]** — moved from July to June, adding an estimated ~500 basis points to e-commerce growth figures
- **[[Amazon Now]]** — ultra-fast delivery service expanded to 80 new cities in the quarter
- **[[Andy Jassy]]** — Amazon CEO who highlighted grocery as a "needle mover" with monthly active perishable customers up 50%+ since January

## Key Takeaways

- Total revenue: $200.6B (+20%), beat estimate of $196.4B.
- Operating income: $27.5B (+43% YoY).
- AWS revenue growth: 37% — fastest in 5 years, mirrors Alphabet and Microsoft acceleration.
- AI + chips within AWS: $25B run rate, triple-digit YoY growth each.
- North America e-commerce: $116.2B (+16%), five-year high.
- International e-commerce: $42.2B (+15%), five-year high.
- Advertising: $19.8B (+26%), fastest since 2023.
- Same-day/overnight delivery exceeded 40% of Prime orders.
- Grocery expanded to 2,300 U.S. cities; perishable customers +50% since Jan.
- AMZN shares rose ~15% post-earnings.
