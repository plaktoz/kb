## Summary

Chinese officials reportedly instructed some government-linked organizations to remove a modified version of Windows 10 from state-linked systems ahead of its planned February 2027 retirement, part of Beijing's broader push toward indigenous technology. The financial impact on Microsoft (MSFT) is likely small given China's minor contribution to sales, but it signals continued erosion of U.S. tech access to Chinese government customers.

## Core Concepts

This is a small but telling data point in the ongoing [[US-China Tech Decoupling]], where [[Beijing]] accelerates replacement of Western software (here, Microsoft Windows) with domestic alternatives for data-security and self-reliance reasons, a trend also visible in China's semiconductor and cloud strategies.

## Key Takeaways

- **Accelerated phaseout**: A modified Windows 10 build is being removed from state-linked systems before its Feb. 2027 retirement date.
- **Small near-term revenue hit**: China is historically a minor contributor to Microsoft's overall sales.
- **Strategic signal**: Reflects Beijing's multi-year effort to reduce reliance on American hardware and software.
