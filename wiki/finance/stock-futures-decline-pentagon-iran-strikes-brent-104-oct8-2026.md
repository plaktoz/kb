## Summary
US stock futures fell on Thursday, October 8, 2026, after reports that the [[Pentagon]] is preparing potential military strikes on [[Iran]]'s nuclear and energy targets sent Brent crude to around $104 per barrel. September [[FOMC]] minutes showed most officials leaning toward another rate hike before year-end, though markets priced over an 80% chance of an October hold. Jobless claims fell to 197,000, while [[BlackRock Investment Institute]] kept a risk-on, overweight stance on US equities.

## Core Concepts
- **Geopolitical oil shock**: Pentagon strike preparations, a burning Marshall Islands-flagged tanker in the Gulf of Oman, and Tehran's refusal to reopen the [[Strait of Hormuz]] under economic pressure drove supply fears.
- **Fed hawkishness**: September [[FOMC Minutes]] show a lean toward another hike, citing energy costs, tariffs, and AI spending; [[CME FedWatch]] showed a 21.6% chance of an October hike.
- **Bond yields**: 10-year [[Treasury Yields]] at 5.32%, 2-year at 4.79%.
- **Labor resilience**: Initial [[Jobless Claims]] fell 2,000 to 197,000; four-week average down to 198,000; continuing claims up 17,000 to 1.716M.
- **Stocks in focus**: [[Applied Digital]] (APLD) +3.95% on a Q1 beat; [[Levi Strauss]] (LEVI) −1.08% on mixed Q3; [[Marvell Technology]] (MRVL) −1.60% despite ~4x TAM expansion and 2030 targets; [[Micron Technology]] (MU) −1.45% as 99% of voting union members at its Taoyuan, Taiwan plant authorized a strike; [[Caterpillar]] (CAT) −1.28% after FTC/USDA sought comments on ag-equipment competition.
- **BlackRock view**: Overweight US equities on AI-driven earnings; foreign investors poured nearly $1T into US equities and fund shares in 12 months to July, including a record $426B in Q2. Fed tightening expectations are "too much"; favors short- to medium-term Treasuries over long duration.

- **Futures lower**: SPY −0.36% to $774.40; QQQ −0.57% to $753.41 premarket.
- **Oil spike**: Brent ~$104; WTI up 4% to ~$91.81.
- **Hike lean**: Most Fed officials favor another hike before year-end.
- **October odds**: Over 80% chance the Fed holds in October.
- **Long yields high**: 10-year Treasury at 5.32%.
- **Jobless claims**: 197,000, below expectations, signaling labor resilience.
- **Micron risk**: Taiwan plant strike authorization threatens production.
- **Prior session**: Industrials, materials, real estate led Wednesday's decline.
- **Other assets**: Gold ~$4,125; DXY 102.27; Bitcoin −1.1% to $83,059.
- **Global markets**: Asian markets fell; Europe mostly lower.
- **BlackRock contrarian**: Says markets overprice Fed tightening; limited scope for dollar rally.
