## Summary

Databricks closed a $5 billion funding round at a $190 billion valuation — up 42% from $134 billion just six months earlier — after crossing $7 billion in revenue run rate (+80% YoY), joining a growing cohort of AI companies (including Anthropic and OpenAI) opting to stay private longer rather than pursue an IPO.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Private Market AI Funding]]**: Databricks' rapid re-up (from $134B to $190B valuation in six months) reflects how much capital is still chasing enterprise AI infrastructure and application companies outside public markets.
- **[[IPO Delay Trend]]**: Databricks, Anthropic, and OpenAI have all deferred public listings despite market readiness, preferring private funding rounds; SpaceX's volatile post-IPO performance may be reinforcing this caution.
- **[[Lakebase]]**: Databricks' newer database product, competing with Oracle and SAP, has already surpassed a $100 million revenue run rate.
- **[[Revenue Run Rate Growth]]**: Databricks crossed $7 billion in revenue run rate, growing more than 80% year-over-year in Q2 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Valuation jump**: $190 billion valuation, up from $134 billion six months prior (a 42% increase).
- **Funding size**: $5 billion raised in this round alone.
- **Revenue scale**: Exceeded $7 billion revenue run rate, +80% YoY growth.
- **Market position**: Already exceeded public rival Snowflake in market value; ranked No. 3 on CNBC's 2026 Disruptor 50 list.
- **Diversification**: Expanding into new verticals including Lakebase (database, >$100M run rate) and Lakewatch (cybersecurity, launched March 2026).
