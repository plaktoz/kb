## Summary
[[Nvidia]] shares fell over 7% in Tuesday afternoon trading after a 31.2% surge on news that founder [[Jensen Huang]] was reinstated as CEO, 12 days after he resigned amid a personal scandal and federal chip-export probes. The reversal followed lobbying by President Trump and Vice President JD Vance, but Justice Department and SEC investigations remain open. Analysts stayed bullish, arguing the stock is still cheap on 2028 earnings.

## Core Concepts
- **[[CEO Reinstatement]]**: Huang returned to Nvidia's Silicon Valley headquarters and promised employees he would be a better CEO the second time around.
- **[[Political Intervention]]**: [[Donald Trump]] and [[JD Vance]] lobbied directors directly and said Huang had been cleared of national security concerns.
- **[[Chip Smuggling Investigations]]**: Ongoing [[US Department of Justice]] and [[SEC]] probes into alleged smuggling through blacklisted Singapore-based firm [[Megaspeed]]; the board appointed a new chief compliance officer.
- **[[Analyst Sentiment]]**: [[Antoine Chkaiban]] of [[New Street Research]] remains a buyer, citing about $25 of 2028 earnings per share.
- **[[Price-to-Earnings Ratio]]**: Chkaiban argues the stock trades below 10 times 2028 earnings.

## Key Takeaways
- **Pullback**: Shares fell over 7% on Tuesday after a 31.2% surge.
- **Timeline**: Reinstatement came just 12 days after Huang resigned.
- **Political Pressure**: Trump and Vance lobbied directors and said Huang was cleared.
- **Open Probes**: DOJ and SEC investigations into Megaspeed smuggling continue.
- **Governance Response**: The board appointed a new chief compliance officer.
- **Analyst View**: Wall Street universally positive; New Street sees "plenty of upside".
- **Valuation Claim**: About $25 of 2028 earnings puts the stock below 10x.
