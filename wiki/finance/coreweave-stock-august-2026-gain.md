## Summary

[[CoreWeave]] (CRWV) gained 18% in August 2026, driven by a Piper Sandler overweight initiation, Indonesia expansion news, and a strong Q2 earnings beat with revenue up 112% to $2.58 billion. The company disclosed a $104 billion revenue backlog plus $25 billion in new commitments, prompting raised full-year guidance. Gains were partially capped by rising interest rates and a net loss of $626 million due to heavy debt service costs.

## Core Concepts

- **[[CoreWeave]]** — neocloud AI infrastructure company specializing in GPU-based compute; ticker CRWV
- **[[Neocloud Stocks]]** — AI-focused cloud providers sensitive to interest rate movements due to capital-intensive leverage
- **[[Revenue Backlog]]** — $104 billion in contracted future revenue, a key demand-confidence signal for AI infrastructure
- **[[Situational Awareness Hedge Fund]]** — blowup whose unwinding provided broader sector tailwinds in early August
- **[[Piper Sandler]]** — initiated coverage at overweight with $151 price target, catalyzing the August rally
- **[[Fed Chair Kevin Warsh]]** — signaled continued inflation-fighting priority, implying higher rates ahead

## Key Takeaways

- **August gain**: CoreWeave rose ~18% in August 2026.
- **Q2 revenue**: $2.58B, up 112% YoY, beating $2.56B estimate.
- **Backlog**: $104B contracted revenue + $25B new commitments.
- **Guidance raised**: Full-year guidance lifted to $12.4B–$13.2B.
- **Net loss**: $626M in Q2, driven by $640M interest expense.
- **Rate risk**: Rising rates disproportionately pressure leveraged neoclouds.
- **Indonesia expansion**: New geographic market announced alongside Piper Sandler initiation.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Leverage Risk]]**: CoreWeave's $640M quarterly interest expense means its profitability is highly sensitive to both AI demand levels and prevailing interest rates — a double-edged exposure typical of capital-heavy infrastructure bets.
