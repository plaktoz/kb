## Summary
After IBM's worst single-day stock decline ever (-25%), CEO Arvind Krishna defended IBM's hybrid/sovereignty AI strategy on the Q2 earnings call: revenue of $17.2B met lowered estimates, infrastructure fell 7%, and software grew only 5%. CFO Jim Kavanaugh attributed the miss to extraordinary AI hardware price increases forcing enterprise clients to prioritize infrastructure build-out over software purchases — a demand-timing issue, not structural erosion. IBM remains in the "penalty box" until improved quarters rebuild investor trust.

## Core Concepts

- [[Arvind Krishna]] — IBM CEO; defended hybrid/sovereignty AI strategy post-stock crash
- [[IBM Q2 2026 Results]] — Revenue $17.2B (+1%); Infrastructure -7%; Software +5%; EPS $2.93 (+5%)
- [[Hybrid Sovereignty Strategy]] — IBM's positioning: AI built on hybrid cloud with data sovereignty for regulated industries
- [[AI Infrastructure Price Shock]] — hardware and memory prices spiked 30 days before Q2, causing enterprise clients to shift capex toward infrastructure, delaying software purchases
- [[IBM Breakup Speculation]] — Wall Street chatter about splitting IBM; Kavanaugh confirmed IBM remains committed as an integrated company
- [[Quantum Computing (IBM)]] — cited as long-term integrated portfolio differentiator alongside hybrid cloud and AI

## Key Takeaways

- **Krishna's defense**: "Our AI strategy is the right one for IBM. It comes down to execution. That is where we fell short."
- **Q2 numbers**: Revenue $17.2B (+1%), EPS $2.93 (+5%), Infrastructure -7%, Software +5%
- **2026 guidance cut**: Full-year constant currency growth guidance lowered to 4%–5% (from "more than 5%")
- **Kavanaugh's explanation**: AI hardware/memory prices surged in 30 days; enterprises bought infrastructure instead of software — a timing shift, not demand erosion
- **IBM inventory +$600M YoY**: Buy-aheads for server storage to protect against further price increases
- **Cost discipline**: $5.5B in annual exit run rate savings, being raised further; generated 300 bps operating leverage
- **Breakup denied**: Kavanaugh: "IBM is strongest as an integrated company" — hybrid cloud, AI, quantum portfolio
- **Market verdict**: IBM in "penalty box" — needs several improved quarters before trust returns

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Innovator's Dilemma]]**: IBM's infrastructure business -7% is the classic incumbent displacement signal — AI clients are building new infrastructure layers that bypass IBM's legacy hardware, not just delaying purchases.
- **[[Timing vs Structure]]**: Kavanaugh distinguishes between a timing issue (deal delays due to hardware capex surge) and a structural one (IBM being irrelevant to AI). The distinction is critical: if timing, IBM recovers; if structural, the thesis is broken. See also [[ibm-q2-2026-earnings-ai-disruption-risk]].
