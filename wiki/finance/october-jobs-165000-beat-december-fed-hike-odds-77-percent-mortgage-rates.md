## Summary
The US added 165,000 nonfarm payroll jobs in October, nearly triple the 59,000 forecast, lifting CME FedWatch odds of a December Fed rate hike to roughly 77%. Economists expect mortgage rates to stay above 7%, though the MBA flags rising underemployment beneath the headline. Brokers are advised to prepare clients for sudden rate swings.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nonfarm Payrolls]]: 165,000 jobs versus 59,000 expected; August and September revised higher.
- [[Federal Reserve]] rate path: December 8-9 [[FOMC]] meeting hike odds near 77-80% per [[CME FedWatch]].
- [[Mortgage Rates]]: expected to remain above 7% as tighter policy is anticipated.
- [[U-6 Underemployment]]: rose to 7.9% from 7.6%, undercutting the headline unemployment drop to 4.1%.
- [[Mortgage Bankers Association]]: forecasts a December hike and another in Q1 2027.
- [[MOVE Index]]: bond volatility gauge behind Barry Habib's warning of sudden price swings.
- [[Residential Construction]]: shed over 15,000 jobs in October.

## Key Takeaways
- **Jobs Beat**: 165,000 payrolls versus 59,000 forecast; unemployment at 4.1%.
- **Wages**: Average hourly earnings up 0.4% monthly and 3.1% annually.
- **Revisions**: August revised up 53,000 to 186,000; September up 31,000 to 60,000.
- **Hike Odds**: CME FedWatch December hike probability jumped to roughly 77%.
- **Hidden Weakness**: U-6 rose to 7.9%; unemployment spells are lengthening.
- **Housing Drag**: Residential construction lost over 15,000 jobs on weak purchase demand.
- **Origination Outlook**: Fitch expects a smaller 2027 origination market to cap lender earnings.
- **Broker Advice**: Warn clients of sudden price swings and shifting spreads.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did the October jobs report raise the odds of a December Fed hike?
**A**: Payrolls rose 165,000 against a 59,000 forecast, unemployment fell to 4.1%, and prior months were revised higher. Combined with still-high inflation, the MBA expects the FOMC to hike in December, pushing CME FedWatch odds to about 77%.

**Q2**: What data point does the MBA's Mike Fratantoni cite to temper the headline?
**A**: The U-6 underemployment rate rose to 7.9% from 7.6%, as some people took part-time work they did not prefer, and unemployment spells are lengthening. Residential construction also shed more than 15,000 jobs.

**Q3**: How should mortgage brokers act on this outlook?
**A**: Brokers should tell clients to be on guard for sudden price swings tied to the MOVE index and changing spreads when advising on lock timing. Mortgage rates are expected to stay above 7% in the near term.
