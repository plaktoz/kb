## Summary
In the week of Nov. 16-20, 2026, the S&P 500 fell 8.6% and the Dow 8%, both closing Friday in correction, as the Nasdaq lost about 10%. Anthropic's IPO debut failed to turn the market, Nvidia slid on an SEC accounting probe report, and the selling spread across Asia, Europe, gold, and bitcoin. Fed officials meanwhile argued for more rate increases.

- [[Anthropic IPO]] priced at $83, opened at $111.50, then faded to $88.30 by day two.

## Key Takeaways
- **Weekly Losses**: S&P 500 -8.6% (worst since April 2025); Dow -8% (worst since March 2020).
- **Friday Rout**: S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39; Nasdaq fell 5.28%.
- **Anthropic Debut**: Closed day one at $97.17, day two at $88.30, as stabilization support ended.
- **Nvidia**: Fell about 14% Friday after Reuters reported SEC scrutiny of accounting.
- **Asia and Europe**: Taiex lost a record 17.5%; Nikkei -7.7%; STOXX 600 -5.8%.
- **Havens Failed**: Gold headed for about -9% weekly; bitcoin fell below $60,000.
- **Fed Hawkishness**: Officials discussed more hikes; Waller floated a half-point December increase.
- **Policy Risk**: Trump threatened "tremendous penalties" on US companies building data centers abroad.
- **Defensives Held**: Walmart, Costco, Johnson & Johnson, and Procter & Gamble each gained about 1%.
