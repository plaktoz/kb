## Summary

Nvidia posted a blowout fiscal Q2 earnings report, with revenue more than doubling and beating analyst forecasts by the widest margin in two years, sending its stock up ~7% premarket and lifting Nasdaq-100 futures by 1%. The company projected 70% revenue growth for fiscal year 2028, reinforcing the AI infrastructure demand thesis. The rally spread broadly across chip stocks and software names citing [[Agentic AI]] demand.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]] Q2 Earnings Beat**: Revenue more than doubled year over year, surpassing analyst consensus by the widest margin in two years — a decisive reaffirmation of AI chip demand.
- **[[Nasdaq-100]] Reaction**: Futures climbed 1% in premarket trading, with [[S&P 500]] futures up 0.4% and Dow futures marginally negative, reflecting a tech-led rally.
- **[[Semiconductor Sector]] Spillover**: [[Marvell]] (+5%), [[Micron]] (+4%), and [[Arm Holdings]] (+4%) all gained in sympathy, confirming the AI hardware buildout narrative.
- **[[Salesforce]] and [[Okta]] Beats**: Salesforce surged 12% premarket on strong Q2 results; Okta jumped 19%+ after beating estimates and citing growing customer interest in [[Agentic AI]].
- **[[Jackson Hole Symposium]]**: Investors monitored the Fed's annual gathering; [[UBS]]'s Mark Haefele cited "robust fundamentals" from the Q2 earnings season.
- **Global Market Reaction**: Asian markets were mixed (South Korea's [[Kospi]] +1.53%, China's [[CSI 300]] +0.86%, Japan's [[Nikkei]] -0.20%); European stocks broadly lower but tech gained 1.5%.

## Key Takeaways

- **Earnings Upside**: Nvidia Q2 revenue more than doubled; beat forecasts by widest margin in 2 years.
- **Forward Guidance**: Nvidia projected 70% revenue growth for fiscal year 2028.
- **Premarket Surge**: Nvidia stock rose ~7% premarket; Nasdaq-100 futures gained 1%.
- **Chip Sector Lift**: Marvell, Micron, and Arm each gained 4–5%+ in sympathy.
- **Software Winners**: Salesforce +12%, Okta +19%+ — both cited AI demand as growth driver.
- **Fed Watch**: Jackson Hole symposium running concurrently as macro backdrop.
