## Summary

The week of July 26, 2026 marks one of the most event-dense weeks of the quarter, with four [[Magnificent Seven]] companies reporting earnings, a [[Federal Reserve]] rate decision, and [[Brent Crude]] crossing $100/barrel. Big Tech capex continues to surge — [[Alphabet]] set a precedent with ~$200B in 2026 capex and its first-ever negative free cash flow — raising investor concerns about whether AI spending will yield returns. A geopolitical oil shock driven by Houthi attacks on Saudi vessels compounds already-strained market conditions.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Magnificent Seven]] Earnings Cycle**: [[Microsoft]] and [[Meta]] report Wednesday; [[Apple]] and [[Amazon]] on Thursday. All face scrutiny on AI capex justification following [[Alphabet]]'s selloff despite beating estimates.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] Rate Decision**: A rate hold is widely expected; markets fully price a September hike. [[Beth Hammack]] (Cleveland Fed) signals broad-based inflation concerns from businesses.
- **[[Brent Crude]] at $100**: Crossed $100/barrel following Houthi attacks on Saudi vessels threatening the [[Bab el-Mandeb Strait]]. [[Rystad Energy]] warns of sustained price risk without a ceasefire.
- **[[AI Capex]] Pressure**: Deutsche Bank forecasts [[Microsoft]] will raise capex guidance to $238B, with free cash flow near breakeven — down from over $70B in recent years.
- **[[Free Cash Flow]] Compression**: [[Alphabet]]'s negative free cash flow as a public company signals a structural shift in Big Tech's capital allocation priorities toward AI infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways

- **Market Performance**: S&P 500 -0.6%, Dow -0.4%, Nasdaq -2.1% the prior week.
- **Alphabet Precedent**: Beat estimates but sold off; ~$200B capex and first-ever negative FCF spooked investors.
- **Microsoft Capex Forecast**: Deutsche Bank projects $238B capex guidance; FCF near breakeven from $70B+ prior.
- **Fed Signal**: Beth Hammack hears "broad-based" inflation from businesses; September hike fully priced.
- **Oil Shock**: Brent at $100; Houthi attacks on Saudi vessels disrupt Bab el-Mandeb Strait shipping.
- **Rystad Warning**: Fewer shipping routes + strained refineries + depleted buffers = substantial rebound risk.

## First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Cost-Benefit Asymmetry]]**: Big Tech is front-loading massive capex on AI infrastructure whose returns are speculative and distant — the market is beginning to discount future cash flows more aggressively as FCF turns negative, exposing the asymmetric timing risk in capital-intensive bets.
- **[[Geopolitical Risk Premium]]**: Oil crossing $100 illustrates how a single chokepoint (the [[Bab el-Mandeb Strait]]) can cascade into broad macro pressure — supply buffers are thin, and the risk premium compounds when infrastructure and diplomacy both fail simultaneously.
