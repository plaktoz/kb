## Summary

US stocks closed higher on July 31, capping a volatile month as [[Magnificent Seven]] earnings reaffirmed aggressive AI infrastructure spending by Big Tech hyperscalers. The 10-year Treasury yield spiked to 4.73%, its highest since January 2025, reflecting ongoing uncertainty after the Fed held rates without forward guidance. Amazon surged 15% on strong earnings while Apple fell 7% on weak Services and China revenue.

## Core Concepts

- **[[S&P 500]]**, **[[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]**, **[[Nasdaq Composite]]** all rose on the final trading day of July, with the Nasdaq up ~1%.
- **[[10-Year Treasury Yield]]** hit 4.73% — a 17-month high — as investors sold Treasurys following the [[Federal Reserve]]'s rate-hold with no forward guidance.
- **[[Magnificent Seven]]** earnings: [[Amazon]] +15%, [[Apple]] -7%, [[Microsoft]] previously +15%; results broadly reassured investors on AI demand.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: The four hyperscalers — [[Amazon]], [[Microsoft]], [[Meta]], and [[Alphabet]] — collectively forecast $720–$745 billion in capital spending for 2026.
- **[[Strait of Hormuz]]** tensions re-escalated, pushing [[WTI Crude]] to ~$85/barrel and [[Brent Crude]] to ~$90/barrel.
- **[[University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment]]** showed a broad pickup despite higher energy prices weighing on budgets.

## Key Takeaways

- **Nasdaq up ~1%, S&P 500 +0.7%, Dow +0.5%** on July 31; Dow gained 0.7% for the month.
- **10-year yield at 4.73%**: highest level since January 2025.
- **Amazon earnings beat**: chip business expansion drove a 15% stock rally.
- **Apple missed**: Services and China revenue disappointed; stock fell 7%.
- **$720–$745B hyperscaler capex forecast** for 2026 signals sustained AI infrastructure build-out.
- **Oil prices rebounded**: Strait of Hormuz disruption lifted WTI to ~$85 and Brent to ~$90.
- **Consumer sentiment improved** despite higher energy costs (University of Michigan survey).

## First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Risk-On / Risk-Off]]**: Big Tech earnings served as a sentiment anchor — strong AI capex guidance shifted markets from risk-off (rate fear) back to risk-on despite the yield spike.
- **[[Earnings as Referendum]]**: Quarterly results for the Magnificent Seven function as a real-time vote on whether AI investment thesis is intact, making them outsized market-moving events relative to their index weight.
