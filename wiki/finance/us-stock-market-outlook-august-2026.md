## Summary

Wall Street enters August 2026 cautiously after a mixed July: the Dow and S&P 500 posted marginal monthly gains while the Nasdaq Composite and Nasdaq 100 fell sharply. Charles Schwab's Nathan Peterson issued a "Cautious" outlook for August 3–7, citing rising Treasury yields and unresolved U.S.-Iran tensions as risks, against a packed earnings calendar led by SpaceX's first public-company report.

## Core Concepts

The [[Stock Market Seasonality|seasonal pattern]] entering August/September is historically more bearish, according to [[Charles Schwab]] strategist Nathan Peterson. Rising [[Treasury Yields]] — the 10-year hitting its highest level since January 2025 — compress the equity risk premium and can divert capital away from stocks. Meanwhile, a historic week of [[Earnings Season|earnings]] (Palantir, AMD, SpaceX, Disney, Eli Lilly, and more) will test whether strong results from [[Microsoft]] and [[Amazon]] have genuinely calmed [[AI Trade|AI economics]] concerns.

## Key Takeaways

- **July recap**: Dow +0.34%, S&P 500 +0.09% for the month, while Nasdaq Composite fell -2.6% and Nasdaq 100 fell -5.15%.
- **Yield pressure**: 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.741%, the highest since January 2025, seen as economically restrictive for equities.
- **SpaceX's first earnings report** as a public company arrives Tuesday, August 4, alongside AMD, Caterpillar, and McDonald's.
- **Bullish counterpoint**: The S&P 500 Equal Weight Index continues climbing steadily, and strong Amazon/Microsoft earnings have helped ease AI spending concerns.
