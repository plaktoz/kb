## Summary

[[Meta]]'s [[Muse AI Agent]] topping U.S. iPhone download charts for three consecutive days ignited a broad CPU stock rally on September 21, 2026 — [[ARM Holdings]] (+17%), [[Intel]] (+12%), and [[AMD]] (+10%). The market thesis is that agentic AI workloads rely heavily on CPUs for inference-time planning and orchestration, unlike model training which is GPU-dominated, making a Muse-scale adoption wave a direct revenue catalyst for CPU designers.

## Core Concepts

- **Agentic AI CPU Demand**: [[AI Agents]] require CPUs — not just GPUs — for "planning and orchestration" tasks during inference, unlike compute-intensive model training. A surge in agent adoption disproportionately benefits CPU makers over GPU vendors.
- **[[Muse AI Agent]] Consumer Adoption**: Meta's Muse topped U.S. iPhone download charts for three straight days, signaling mass-market agentic AI uptake and fueling investor demand projections for CPU infrastructure.
- **Server CPU Market Projections**: [[ARM Holdings]] projects server CPU market growing 35% annually to $120B by 2030; [[AMD]] forecasts 50% annual growth reaching $220B by decade's end — two independent bull cases for the same secular trend.
- **CPU Designers as AI Beneficiaries**: [[Intel]], [[AMD]], and [[ARM Holdings]] are the primary identified beneficiaries as leading CPU designers — a notable reframe from the "AI = Nvidia/GPU" narrative that has dominated since 2023.
- **[[Meta]] User Base Moat**: Muse's 3.6 billion daily active users give it a distribution advantage over rival AI agents, supporting analysts' projections of hundreds of millions of users within months.

## Key Takeaways

- **ARM +17%, Intel +12%, AMD +10%** on September 21 — all driven by Muse download surge.
- **CPU vs GPU distinction**: training is GPU-heavy; inference/planning for agents is CPU-heavy.
- **ARM's server CPU market**: 35% CAGR to $120B by 2030 (company guidance).
- **AMD's server CPU outlook**: 50% CAGR to $220B by decade's end (company forecast).
- **Muse's moat**: Meta's 3.6B daily active users give unmatched distribution for agent adoption.
- **Analyst outlook**: Muse could attract hundreds of millions of users within months.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Second-Order Thinking]]**: The market initially framed AI capex as a GPU/Nvidia story; the Muse rally represents investors reasoning one step further — if agents scale, the inference layer (CPUs) becomes the binding constraint, not the training layer.
- **[[Narrative Shift]]**: The "AI = GPU" thesis that defined 2023–2025 is being partially displaced by a CPU inference narrative — a reminder that dominant investment narratives create blind spots in adjacent opportunities.
