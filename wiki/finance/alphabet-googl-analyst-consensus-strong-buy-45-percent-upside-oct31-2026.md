## Summary

Alphabet ($3.4 trillion market cap) has lagged the S&P 500 and is down 10.6% year to date after tumbling 12.6% on its Q3 2026 results, which missed on revenue and showed negative free cash flow amid heavy AI spending. Despite this, 53 analysts give it a "Strong Buy" consensus, with a mean price target of $406.04, a 45.1% premium to the current price. The Street-high target of $515 implies 84% upside.

## Core Concepts

- [[Alphabet]]: operates Google Services, [[Google Cloud]], and Other Bets including [[Waymo]] and Verily.
- [[GOOGL]]: down 10.6% YTD versus the [[S&P 500]]'s 10.7% rise.
- [[Q3 2026 Earnings]]: revenue $127.6B, adjusted EPS $2.67, advertising revenue $82.01B, all weaker than expected.
- [[AI Mode]]: increased use is slowing search advertising growth.
- [[AI Capital Expenditure]]: 2026 forecast of $195B-$205B, with plans to raise it further.
- [[Analyst Consensus]]: 45 Strong Buy, 3 Moderate Buy, 5 Hold among 53 analysts.
- [[Free Cash Flow]]: negative $9.07B versus expectations near break-even.
- [[Ross Sandler]]: Barclays analyst who cut the price target to $405 and kept Overweight.

## Key Takeaways

- **Post-earnings drop**: Shares fell 12.6% after the Oct. 28 Q3 report.
- **Profit hit**: Net income fell 25% to $26.17B on AI and data center spending.
- **Cash burn**: Free cash flow was negative $9.07B against near break-even expectations.
- **Cloud strength**: Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.71B; backlog reached $561B.
- **Capex fear**: Plans to exceed $195B-$205B capex raised margin-pressure worries.
- **Consensus**: "Strong Buy," slightly less bullish than three months ago (46 Strong Buys).
- **Targets**: Mean $406.04 (45.1% upside); Street-high $515 (84% upside).

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Spending that outruns near-term returns pressures valuation even when growth is strong** — Heavy investment cuts free cash flow and margins, so investors can punish a company despite booming segments if cash burn keeps rising.
→ explains: Capex fear, Cash burn, Profit hit

**Q1**: What is Wall Street's overall view of Alphabet stock in this article?
**A**: The consensus rating among 53 analysts is "Strong Buy," with a mean price target of $406.04, 45.1% above the current price. This is slightly less bullish than three months ago.

**Q2**: Why did Alphabet shares fall 12.6% after Q3 2026 results?
