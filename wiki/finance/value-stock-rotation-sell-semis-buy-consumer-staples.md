## Summary

Investors rotated sharply out of growth and momentum names into value stocks in July 2026, with the iShares Semiconductor ETF down more than 26% for the month and the momentum-factor ETF MTUM down over 15%, while the large-cap value ETF IWD gained 3.5% in July (+19% YTD) and small-cap value ETF IWN rose 22%. Investment chief Peter Boockvar calls the trend "sell semis, buy ketchup," favoring under-loved consumer staples like Kraft Heinz and Coca-Cola, while strategists at JPMorgan and Yardeni Research point to healthcare and small caps as further beneficiaries of the rotation.

## Core Concepts

- [[Value Stocks]] — large-cap (IWD, +3.5% July, +19% YTD) and small-cap (IWN, +22% July) value ETFs sharply outperforming growth/momentum
- [[Momentum Trade]] — MTUM down over 15% in July, on track for its worst month on record; iShares Semiconductor ETF down over 26% in July
- [[Kraft Heinz]] and [[Coca-Cola]] — Peter Boockvar's favored "ketchup rotation" consumer staples picks, both up ~8% in the first three days of the week alone
- [[Healthcare Sector Rotation]] — JPMorgan's Dubravko Lakos-Bujas favors biopharma and medtech/life-science tools as healthcare's S&P 500 weighting recovers from a low of 9% (down from a 16% peak)
- [[AI Fatigue]] — Ed Yardeni's term for investor exhaustion with parsing AI winners, driving rotation into businesses "everybody understands"
- Small-cap positioning — Jefferies found hedge funds overweight small caps, with 13% of net portfolios in Russell 2000 names

## Key Takeaways

- iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) down more than 26% in July; Nasdaq Composite down over 6%, nearing correction
- Momentum factor ETF (MTUM) down more than 15% in July, its worst month on record
- Large-cap value ETF (IWD) up 3.5% in July, +19% YTD; small-cap value ETF (IWN) up 22% in July
- Kraft Heinz and Coca-Cola both up ~8% in just the first three trading days of the week
- Healthcare's S&P 500 weighting has fallen to 9% from a 16% peak, which JPMorgan views as an inflection point given favorable valuations and accelerating 2027 earnings growth
- Yardeni recommends overweighting energy, healthcare, financials, and industrials amid "AI fatigue"
- Hedge funds are overweight small caps by a wide margin per Jefferies/MSCI data
