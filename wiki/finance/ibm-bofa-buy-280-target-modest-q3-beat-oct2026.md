## Summary
[[Bank of America]] expects [[IBM]] to post a modest third-quarter earnings beat on October 21 and leave its fiscal 2026 outlook unchanged, reiterating a "buy" rating and $280 price target. The bank cites IBM's shift toward higher-margin software, improving profitability and strong [[Free Cash Flow]], while calling 2026 a transitional year ahead of a stronger 2027. It trimmed its 2026 revenue and EPS estimates slightly but kept the target, which rests on an enterprise-value-to-free-cash-flow multiple.

## Core Concepts
- **Q3 forecast**: [[Bank of America]] models revenue of $16.8B and EPS of $2.82, with possible upside from favourable currency moves and profitability.
- **Investor focus areas**: Conversion of delayed deals, recovery in customer spending, and improving growth in [[Transaction Processing]] software.
- **Software mix**: Software growth is below ideal, but strength is expected in [[Red Hat]]'s [[OpenShift]] and [[Ansible]], plus data-business expansion after the [[Confluent]] acquisition.
- **Segment outlook**: Infrastructure revenue +3% at constant currency in Q3; [[IBM Consulting]] revenue +3.6% as second-half growth improves.
- **Valuation method**: Target assumes 19x estimated 2027 [[Enterprise Value]] to free cash flow.
- **Transitional year**: 2026 framed as a bridge to stronger 2027 operating performance as software growth and margins improve.

## Key Takeaways
- **Rating Reiterated**: BofA keeps "buy" and a $280 price target on IBM.
- **Q3 Estimates**: $16.8B revenue and $2.82 EPS; modest beat expected October 21.
- **Software Growth**: 4.5% reported software growth forecast for fiscal 2026; ~1% organic.
- **Segment Growth**: Infrastructure +3% (constant currency); consulting +3.6% in Q3.
- **Trimmed Estimates**: 2026 revenue cut to $70.3B from $70.5B; EPS to $12.40.
- **Valuation Basis**: 19x 2027 EV-to-free-cash-flow underpins the unchanged target.
- **2027 Inflection**: Stronger performance expected next year as software and margins improve.
