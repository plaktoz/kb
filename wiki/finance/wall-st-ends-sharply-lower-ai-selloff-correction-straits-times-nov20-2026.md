## Summary
On Nov 20, 2026, US stocks extended their rout and the [[S&P 500]] and Dow confirmed corrections after [[Anthropic IPO]] shares fell 9.1% in their second day, deepening worries about the AI trade. The S&P 500 fell 3.70% on a seventh straight loss, and [[Nvidia]] dropped 13.8% despite a strong earnings report. Markets also price a near-certain 25-basis-point [[Federal Reserve]] hike in December.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Stock Market Correction]]**: The S&P 500 and Dow are both more than 10% below record closes; the Nasdaq is negative for the year.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Closed up 17.1% over its US$83 offer on Nov 19, then fell 9.1%.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Down 13.8% after what one strategist called one of the greatest earnings quarters in history.
- **[[AI Trade]]**: Fears over AI risks and massive spending; hyperscalers and neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius) also fell.
- **[[Federal Reserve]]**: Governor [[Lisa Cook]] said the policy rate is not high enough to deal with inflation.
- **[[VIX]]**: The "fear gauge" hit its highest close since April 7, 2025.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Moves**: S&P 500 -3.70% to 6,910.42; Nasdaq -5.28%; Dow -1.93%.
- **Losing Streak**: S&P 500's seventh straight drop, its longest since February 2020.
- **Weekly Losses**: S&P and Dow worst week since April 2025; Nasdaq worst since March 2020.
- **Failed Catalyst**: Investors hoped the Anthropic debut would clear the air; it did not.
- **Rate Risk**: A December 25-bp hike is near-certain after hot October inflation.
- **Spillover**: Caterpillar fell 5%; crypto firms fell 6.3% to 9.6% as bitcoin slid below $60,000.
- **Breadth**: NYSE had 1,199 new lows versus 38 new highs.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Sentiment dominates short-run prices; fundamentals prevail over time** — In the immediate term, market mood can override strong earnings and economic strength, while fundamentals tend to reassert themselves later.
