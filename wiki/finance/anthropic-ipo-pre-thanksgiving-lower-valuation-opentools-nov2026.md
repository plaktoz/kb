## Summary
Anthropic is pressing ahead with a Nasdaq IPO before Thanksgiving, aiming to raise $80-100 billion at a $1.25-1.75 trillion valuation, down from the $2 trillion once floated. The tech sell-off, voter backlash against AI data centers, and OpenAI's undersubscribed raise make it a test of public appetite for AI at trillion-dollar valuations.

## Core Concepts
- [[Anthropic]]: the Claude developer, with about $81 billion in annualized revenue, up from roughly $65 billion at the end of July.
- [[Initial Public Offering]]: the first pure-play AI company to list, via a Nasdaq Global Select Market filing (amended S-1 dated November 2).
- [[OpenAI]]: closed only $11 billion of a $30 billion target at a $1.25 trillion valuation.
- [[AI Data Center Backlash]]: voters rejected expansion in 40 of 41 local ballot measures across 10 states.
- [[Claude Code]]: Anthropic's dominant coding tool, whose users may see pricing and product changes under public ownership.
- [[Compute Commitments]]: about $895.4 billion in purchase commitments and leases as of September 30.

## Key Takeaways
- **Valuation Reset**: Target of $1.25-1.75 trillion versus $2 trillion floated weeks ago.
- **Raise Size**: Anthropic aims to raise $80-100 billion.
- **Capital Intensity**: $895.4 billion in commitments, substantially all for compute and infrastructure.
- **Polling Opposition**: Gallup found 71% oppose a local AI data center; USC found 62%.
- **Risk Disclosure**: The S-1 says data-center opposition is "increasingly organized across jurisdictions."
- **Spotlight Debate**: Experts split on whether public status brings harsher scrutiny than OpenAI faces.
- **Market Repricing**: Nasdaq 100 fell 6.4% Thursday after the Democratic midterm sweep.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: How has Anthropic's IPO plan changed amid the tech sell-off?
**A**: It still plans to list before Thanksgiving but now targets a $1.25-1.75 trillion valuation, below the $2 trillion once floated. Some investors have discussed as low as $1.1 trillion.

**Q2**: What evidence shows public opposition to AI data centers?
**A**: Voters rejected expansion in 40 of 41 local ballot measures, Gallup found 71% opposed a local data center, and USC Annenberg found 62% opposed construction. Anthropic's S-1 itself flags organized opposition as a risk.

**Q3**: What does public ownership change for Claude users and Anthropic?
**A**: Quarterly earnings will reveal revenue, margins, and customer metrics, and revenue pressure could bring pricing or rate-limit changes. Safety incidents will also be amplified through SEC filings and shareholder scrutiny.
