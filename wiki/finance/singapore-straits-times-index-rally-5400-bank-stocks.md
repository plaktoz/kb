## Summary
Singapore's Straits Times Index rallied above 5,400 points on Thursday, July 8, driven by strong gains in local bank stocks ahead of the second-quarter earnings season. DBS Group briefly crossed SG$70 for the first time, while OCBC and UOB also hit new highs. Investors were positioning ahead of earnings and to secure first-half dividend payouts.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Straits Times Index]]** — Singapore Exchange benchmark, rose 0.7% to open at 5,406.020.
- **[[DBS Group]]** — briefly crossed SG$70 for the first time.
- **[[Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation]]** — hit a new record high alongside DBS.
- **[[United Overseas Bank]]** — hit a new record high alongside DBS and OCBC.
- **[[Singapore Exchange]]** — hosts the STI benchmark.
