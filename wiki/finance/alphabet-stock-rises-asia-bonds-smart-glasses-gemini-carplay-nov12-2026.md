## Summary

Alphabet stock rose about 3% on Thursday after it raised nearly $4 billion in its first Hong Kong dollar and Singapore dollar bond offerings. Google and Samsung also opened preorders for Gemini-powered smart glasses and began rolling Gemini out to Apple CarPlay. The moves show Alphabet financing AI investment while expanding Gemini across consumer devices.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]]**: Google parent whose stock rose about 3% on Thursday.
- **[[Asian Bond Offering]]**: About $3.78B raised via HKD and SGD bonds, following a euro bond sale earlier in the month.
- **[[AI Infrastructure Financing]]**: Major technology companies seeking debt funding for AI buildout.
- **[[Google Smart Glasses]]**: Samsung, Warby Parker, and Gentle Monster glasses running [[Gemini]], competing with [[Meta]] in AI wearables.
- **[[Gemini for Apple CarPlay]]**: Hands-free voice assistant for iOS drivers, rolling out globally.
- **[[Gemini 4 Carbon]]**: Model whose access Google expanded earlier in the week.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock Move**: Alphabet shares rose about 3% Thursday.
- **HKD Bonds**: 16 billion HKD ($2B) across two-, five-, and 10-year maturities.
- **SGD Bonds**: 2.28 billion SGD ($1.78B) with two- to 30-year maturities.
- **Strong Demand**: HKD orders exceeded 32.5 billion; SGD orders topped 4 billion.
- **Use of Proceeds**: General corporate purposes, potentially including debt repayment.
- **Glasses Pricing**: Nine styles from $395 (Warby Parker) and $449 (Gentle Monster); ship Dec. 10.
- **Glasses Specs**: 12-megapixel camera, translation, navigation, up to nine hours battery.
- **CarPlay Rollout**: Gemini global iOS rollout began Wednesday.
