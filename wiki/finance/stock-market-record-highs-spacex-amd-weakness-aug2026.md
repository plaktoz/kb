## Summary
Major indexes pushed to fresh records on August 5, 2026, extending a strong earnings-driven rally even as SpaceX and AMD both fell sharply despite beating estimates — a pattern of "burden of high expectations" repeating across this earnings season. Strong upside surprises from [[Eli Lilly]] (EPS beat by $2.37, revenue +47.7% YoY) and [[Walt Disney]] provided positive counterweight, while broader market breadth (71% of S&P 500 stocks above their 200-day average) suggested the rally has legs beyond just chip stocks.

## Core Concepts
- **Earnings expectations vs. "whisper numbers"**: Investors trading on unofficial expectations above official consensus, so even beats can trigger sell-offs when whisper numbers aren't met.
- **Market breadth**: The percentage of stocks trading above their 200-day moving average, used as a signal of whether a rally is broad-based or narrowly concentrated.
- **[[Forward P/E ratio]]**: The S&P 500's forward P/E fell below 20 even as prices hit records, because ~47% anticipated Q2 earnings growth outpaced price gains.
- **AI capex overhang**: Heavy AI capital expenditure forecasts can weigh on even strong earnings reports, as seen with [[SpaceX]] investors shifting focus from beat-to-consensus to future spending commitments.
- **Geopolitical risk / [[Strait of Hormuz]]**: Crude oil ticked up amid fresh Iranian-proxy attacks on Middle East shipping, though Trump signaled a short-term deal to reopen the strait may be near.

## Key Takeaways
- **AMD fell >8% and SpaceX ~10%** in early trading despite beating consensus, driven by unmet "whisper numbers" and, for SpaceX, concern over heavy AI capex forecasts.
- **[[Eli Lilly]] surged ~5%**: EPS beat by $2.37, revenue +47.7% YoY to $22.97B; Mounjaro sales +91%, Zepbound U.S. revenue +44%; raised full-year guidance above consensus.
- **[[Walt Disney]] added ~3%** after EPS topped consensus and the company reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance.
- **[[Shopify]] surged 27%** on an earnings beat and strong forward revenue guidance — standout mover of the session.
- **[[Arista Networks]] climbed 9%** on an earnings and revenue beat.
- **[[Nvidia]] rose 1.5%** after Elon Musk confirmed SpaceX will exclusively use Nvidia chips for AI services — a demand signal for Nvidia.
- **[[Uber]] dropped >3%** despite reporting in-line results.
- **Market breadth improved sharply**: 71% of S&P 500 stocks above their 200-day average, up from 55% in June, suggesting broader participation than the spring's chip-stock-led rally.
- **Valuations look reasonable despite record prices**: forward P/E of 19.6 sits below the 19.9 five-year average because earnings are growing faster than prices.
- **ADP jobs data missed** (44,000 vs. 75,000 expected), a soft-data signal ahead of Friday's nonfarm payrolls report.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core claim about the state of the August 2026 rally?
**A**: Stocks are hitting fresh record highs on strong earnings and improving market breadth, not just concentrated gains in a handful of chip stocks.

**Q2**: What mechanism explains AMD and SpaceX falling despite beating estimates?
**A**: Investors had priced in unofficial "whisper numbers" above the official consensus, so results that beat consensus but missed those higher informal expectations triggered sell-offs.

**Q3**: How should an investor interpret the S&P 500's falling forward P/E ratio during a record-high rally?
**A**: It signals that earnings growth (~47% YoY expected) is outpacing price appreciation, meaning current valuations may be more reasonable than the record index level alone suggests — though maintaining that earnings growth rate going forward could be difficult.
