## Summary

Three hyperscalers — [[Alphabet]], [[Amazon]], and [[Meta]] — are projected to spend over $500B on AI infrastructure in 2026, with [[Microsoft]] adding ~$190B. This historic spending wave is driven by AI model training and inference demand, and is the primary catalyst for [[Nvidia]]'s 85% year-over-year revenue growth. For investors, this spending is simultaneously a bullish signal for AI infrastructure plays and a risk factor for these companies' near-term margins.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]** — unprecedented corporate spending on GPUs, data centers, and AI infrastructure
- **[[Nvidia]] Beneficiary** — 85% YoY revenue growth directly attributable to hyperscaler AI infrastructure demand
- **[[Hyperscaler AI Budgets]]** — Alphabet $195-205B, Amazon ~$200B, Meta $125-145B, Microsoft ~$190B (2026 guidance)
- **[[Infrastructure vs. Returns]]** — tension between massive upfront AI spend and unclear near-term revenue payoff

## Key Takeaways

- **$500B+ combined**: Alphabet + Amazon + Meta alone surpass half a trillion in 2026 AI capex
- **Microsoft adds ~$190B**: total "hyperscaler 4" AI infrastructure spend approaches $700B
- **Nvidia +85% YoY**: direct beneficiary; GPU demand driven by AI training and inference buildout
- **Margin pressure**: AI capex weighing on free cash flow for each company in the near term
- **[[MSFT]] holding implication**: AI capex is an investment thesis driver but also a margin headwind
