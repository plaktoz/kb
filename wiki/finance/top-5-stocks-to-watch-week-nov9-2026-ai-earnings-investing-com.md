## Summary
Investing.com previews a week in which five AI-infrastructure companies report earnings while the Nasdaq sits in correction territory and October inflation data lands. The reports span power, computing, cloud, chip equipment and networking, and will serve as a gauge of the AI investment cycle ahead of Nvidia's November 17 report.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nasdaq Composite]]: in correction, down nearly 12% from its October 6 record.
- [[Constellation Energy]]: power producer benefiting from AI data-centre electricity demand.
- [[CoreWeave]] and [[Nebius Group]]: neocloud AI computing providers with volatile earnings reactions.
- [[Applied Materials]]: semiconductor equipment supplier signalling chipmaker spending.
- [[Cisco Systems]]: AI data-centre networking exposure.
- [[AI Infrastructure Buildout]]: the spending theme linking all five reports.
- [[Nvidia]]: its November 17 earnings are the next major catalyst.
- [[Consumer Price Index]] and [[Federal Reserve]]: CPI Tuesday, PPI Friday, shaping rate expectations.

## Key Takeaways
- **Market Backdrop**: S&P 500 fell 2.7% and Nasdaq 3.4% last week on data-centre spending worries.
- **Inflation Test**: Core CPI expected up 0.2% month over month; annual rate 2.7% versus 2.6%.
- **Constellation Energy**: Reports Monday before open; EPS $3.72 expected; shares down 30.2% year to date.
- **CoreWeave**: Reports Tuesday after close; EPS 9 cents expected; shares down 43% in a month.
- **Nebius**: Reports Thursday before open; shares down 41% in a month but up 48.9% year to date.
- **Applied Materials**: Thursday after close; EPS $4.06 expected; estimates up 10.5% in 90 days.
- **Cisco**: Thursday after close; EPS $1.32 expected; estimates up 15.4% in 90 days.
- **Next Catalyst**: Nvidia reports November 17, offering a broader read on AI demand.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the article's central framing of the week ahead?
**A**: Five earnings reports spanning electricity, computing, cloud, chip equipment and networking will give a snapshot of whether AI investment commitments remain intact amid a Nasdaq correction.

**Q2**: What data points show how sensitive these stocks are to earnings?
**A**: CoreWeave fell 17.3% after a Q1 miss and rose 22.2% after a Q2 beat; Nebius surged 32% after Q2 results and 23.5% after Q1.

**Q3**: How should investors interpret Applied Materials and Cisco's setups?
**A**: Both have rising earnings estimates (10.5% and 15.4% over 90 days) despite share-price declines, so expectations may be more supportive than price weakness suggests. Applied Materials' results will show whether weakness is a valuation reset or a capex slowdown.
