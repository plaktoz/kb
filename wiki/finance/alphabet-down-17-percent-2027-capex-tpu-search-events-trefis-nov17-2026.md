## Summary
Alphabet fell 16.7% between October 16 and November 16, 2026 (S&P 500: -3.1%), and its next six months hinge on three items in the February 2027 Q4 report: the 2027 capex plan, TPU system sales in Google Cloud, and Search growth. The article closes by asking whether an investor could sit through a repeat of the May-to-November drawdown, since all three events land in one report.

## Core Concepts
- [[Alphabet]] (GOOGL): down 16.7% in a month, and 30.7% from May 13 to November 2, 2026.
- [[Capital Expenditure]]: 2026 guide held at $195B-$205B; 2027 plan expected to "rise significantly" and drove negative free cash flow in fiscal Q3 2026.
- [[Google Cloud]] and [[TPU Systems]]: complete hardware, compute, storage and networking systems; Cloud revenue rose 103% to $30.7B.
- [[Google Search]] and AI Mode: growth expected to slow in fiscal Q4 2026; ads in AI Mode are being scaled internationally.
- [[Concentration Risk]]: every catalyst hits a single company.

## Key Takeaways
- **Drawdown**: Stock fell 16.7% in a month; 12.6% one-day drop on October 29.
- **Capex Plan**: 2026 capex guide is $195B-$205B; 2027 spend to rise significantly.
- **Free Cash Flow**: Capex and TPU inventory keep pressure on free cash flow.
- **TPU Sales**: Expected to bring a meaningful 2027 revenue rise via Google Cloud partnerships (Crux AI, Oracle).
- **Search Slowdown**: Q3 Search revenue grew 12% to $63.3B; Q4 growth expected to slow.
- **Single Report Risk**: Capex, Cloud and Search figures all arrive in February 2027.
- **Payoff Condition**: Cloud growth must keep pace with spending for the risk to pay.

**Q3**: How should an investor apply the article's closing test?
