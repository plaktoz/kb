## Summary
[[IBM]] reported Q3 2026 revenue of US$17.1B and basic EPS of US$2.33, with operating pretax margin up 70 basis points, yet the stock rose only 0.3% after a 17.7% three-month gain. The author reads the muted move as investors pausing rather than reacting to fresh news. Software strength and a mainframe rebound ease the bear case, though lumpiness concerns remain.

## Core Concepts
- [[IBM]]: Revenue +4.7%, net income +26.2% to US$2.2B, basic EPS +24.7%.
- [[Operating Pretax Margin]]: Widened 70 basis points year on year.
- [[Red Hat OpenShift]]: ARR of US$2.4B, growing above 30%.
- [[Confluent Acquisition]]: Helped lift data software 20%.
- [[Lightwell]]: Carries a roughly US$700M pipeline.
- [[IBM Z Mainframe]]: Z Infrastructure revenue fell 42% in Q2; Transaction Processing rebounded 10% in Q3.
- [[Discounted Cash Flow]]: Cash flow analysis suggests the stock is about 11% undervalued.
- [[Simply Wall St]]: Source of the narrative-based valuation analysis.

## Key Takeaways
- **Muted Reaction**: Stock up 0.3% on the day despite a strong quarter.
- **Margin Expansion**: Operating pretax margin widened 70 basis points.
- **Software Growth**: Software revenue +9%; data software +20%.
- **Infrastructure Rebound**: Infrastructure +5%; Distributed Infrastructure +32%.
- **Bull Thesis**: OpenShift, security remediation, quantum must offset mainframe pressure.
- **Bear Thesis**: Rebound after a 42% drop supports lumpiness, not smoothness.
- **Valuation**: DCF suggests about 11% undervalued.

## 🧠 Core Principles

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did IBM's stock barely move after its Q3 2026 earnings?
**A**: After a 17.7% three-month gain, the author says the 0.3% tick reflects investors catching their breath rather than reacting to fresh information.

**Q2**: What evidence from Q3 partially rebuts the mainframe bear case?
**A**: Infrastructure revenue grew 5%, Distributed Infrastructure 32%, and Transaction Processing 10%, suggesting slipped Z deals closed. A sharp rebound after a prior drop still doesn't prove smoother cycles.

**Q3**: What does the bull thesis require to hold?
**A**: IBM must convert Red Hat OpenShift, automated security remediation, and quantum investment into durable software and services growth that outweighs pressure on mainframe and CapEx-sensitive revenues.
