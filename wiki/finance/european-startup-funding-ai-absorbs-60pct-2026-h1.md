## Summary

European tech startups raised €44.1 billion in H1 2026, a 27% rebound from the prior year, but deal count fell to a six-year low of ~1,740 as capital concentrated into mega-rounds. [[Artificial Intelligence]] absorbed 60.3% of all European venture deal value for the first time in a half-year period, up from 37.9% for full-year 2025. Seed-stage deal counts collapsed 44% year-over-year, creating a structural pipeline shortfall expected to constrain fundable companies in 2027–2028.

## Core Concepts

- **[[European Venture Capital]] bifurcation**: total capital recovering while deal count shrinks to lowest since 2020
- **[[AI Funding]] concentration**: AI crossed absolute majority (60.3%) of European VC deal value in H1 2026
- **[[Seed Stage Funding]] collapse**: 44% YoY decline in Q1 2026; early-stage Series A volumes down 30%
- **[[Mega-Round]] dominance**: financings above €100M accounted for >50% of all H1 deal value; 7 of largest VC deals in European history closed in H1
- **[[Fund Size Concentration]]**: median European VC fund size rose to €60M from €50M, pushing minimum check sizes above seed-viable thresholds
- **[[Exit Market]] backlog**: IPO activity muted; >150 acquisitions in Q2 worth >$11.5B including [[Cognite]] (Schneider Electric) and [[VOI Technology]] (Uber)
- **Key companies**: [[Nscale]] (€2.9B), [[Isomorphic Labs]] ($2.1B Series B), [[Neura Robotics]] ($1.4B Series C), [[Wayve]] ($1.2B Series D), [[Recursive Superintelligence]] ($650M+)
- **UK dominance**: 6 of 10 largest H1 deals; €18.7B raised — over 3x Germany's €6.3B

## Key Takeaways

- European tech raised €44.1B in H1 2026, up 27% YoY but deal count hit a 6-year low.
- AI absorbed 60.3% of European VC deal value — first time crossing 50% in a half-year.
- Seed deal count fell 44% YoY in Q1 2026; Series A volumes dropped 30%.
- 7 of the largest VC deals in European history were recorded in H1 2026.
- Mega-rounds (>€100M) accounted for >50% of all H1 deal value.
- Median European VC fund size grew to €60M, eliminating sub-€1M seed capital supply.
- Founders now must show PMF, paying customers, and unit economics just to raise seed.
- Median Series A round size rose to ~$14M from $8–10M just a few years ago.
- UK raised €18.7B (6 of top 10 deals); Germany €6.3B; France €6.0B.
- Exit backlog growing: IPOs muted, late-stage private valuations under pressure.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Trickle-Down Capital Fallacy]]**: LP capital concentrating into large funds does not automatically flow down to seed — fund size inflation crowds out the capital layer that seeds early companies, hollowing the pipeline from the bottom up.
- **[[Barbell Distribution]]**: European VC now exhibits a classic barbell: abundant capital at the very large end and near-zero at the seed end, with the middle (Series A/B) shrinking — a distribution that looks healthy by total capital but is structurally fragile.
