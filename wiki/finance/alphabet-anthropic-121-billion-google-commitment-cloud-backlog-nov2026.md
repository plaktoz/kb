## Summary

[[Anthropic]]'s amended IPO filing raised its minimum spending commitment with [[Alphabet]] to about $121.3 billion between April 2026 and July 2033, from about $114.5 billion in the prior filing. That equals roughly 22% of [[Google Cloud]]'s $560.8 billion backlog, tying a large share of it to one customer. The author sees the next few years as solid revenue and the later years as a bet on Anthropic's growth, and still finds Alphabet reasonably priced at about 19 times expected 2027 earnings.

## Core Concepts

- [[Anthropic]]: amended its [[IPO Filing]] on Nov. 2, adding Q3 results and about $895.4 billion of purchase commitments and leases, almost all for compute and infrastructure.
- [[Alphabet]] and [[Google Cloud]]: Q3 cloud revenue rose 103% year over year to $30.7 billion, with operating income of $11.1 billion.
- [[Cloud Backlog]]: contracted revenue not yet recognized; $560.8 billion on Sept. 30 versus $513.9 billion three months earlier.
- [[Customer Concentration Risk]]: Anthropic's minimum averages about $16.5 billion a year, around 13% of the cloud unit's current sales.
- [[Take-or-Pay Commitments]]: Anthropic must pay the difference if it spends less than its minimum with Google.
- [[Vendor Financing]]: Google has agreed to provide up to $20 billion in convertible notes or stock, so part of what Anthropic pays Google might come from Google itself.
- [[Custom AI Chips]]: Anthropic estimates another $125 billion to $150 billion of spend on Google's chips, some possibly via third parties.

## Key Takeaways

- **Commitment Increase**: Anthropic's minimum Google spend rose to about $121.3B from $114.5B.
- **Backlog Share**: The minimum equals around 22% of Google Cloud's $560.8B backlog.
- **Backlog Growth Slowing**: About $47B added in Q3 versus almost $52B in Q2.
- **Near-Term Ability to Pay**: $69.8B due within 12 months; cash and investments cover it almost twice.
- **Anthropic Growth**: Q3 revenue up about 47% sequentially to $17.0B, over 12 times year-ago.
- **Profitability**: Operating loss of $567M in Q3, versus $3.1B a year earlier; nine-month operating cash flow was +$6.6B.
- **Later Years Risk**: About $447.5B due before end of 2030, roughly $105B a year.
- **Filing Warning**: Anthropic says it must grow revenue rapidly to satisfy these commitments.
- **Alphabet Valuation**: About 19 times expected 2027 earnings, 31% under its 52-week high.
- **Author's View**: Treat near-term commitment as solid revenue and later years as a bet on Anthropic.
