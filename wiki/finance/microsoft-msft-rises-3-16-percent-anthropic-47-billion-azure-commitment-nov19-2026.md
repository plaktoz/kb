## Summary
Microsoft shares rose 3.16% to $524.36 on Nov 19 after Anthropic's final prospectus confirmed a $1.0 billion Microsoft share purchase at the $83 IPO price and a non-cancelable Azure commitment of at least $47.2 billion through June 2033. The floor averages roughly $7.2-7.5 billion a year, real revenue but small next to Microsoft's cloud base and $3.89 trillion market value. The next test is the January quarterly report and whether Azure growth reflects the new floor.
