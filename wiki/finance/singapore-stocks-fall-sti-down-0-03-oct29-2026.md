## Summary

Singapore stocks ended marginally lower on Thursday, Oct 29, 2026, with the [[Straits Times Index]] (STI) down 0.03% at 5,396.03 amid mixed regional markets. [[Wilmar International]] was the worst blue-chip performer and [[Genting Singapore]] the best, while the three local banks ended mixed. [[UltraGreen.ai]] dropped 15.9% after warning its FY2026 revenue would miss guidance.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Lost 1.9 points (-0.03%) to finish at 5,396.03.
- **[[Genting Singapore]]**: Top STI gainer, up 1.7% (S$0.01) to S$0.60.
- **[[Wilmar International]]**: Worst STI performer, down 4.9% (S$0.16) to S$3.11, extending a months-long decline amid an Indonesian police investigation into plantation fires and weaker underlying Q3 earnings.
- **Singapore Banks**: [[OCBC]] +1.1% to S$29.34, [[DBS Group]] -0.3% to S$73.80, [[UOB]] -0.1% to S$41.50.
- **[[iEdge Singapore Next 50 Index]]**: [[Top Glove]] led (+3.3% to S$0.315); [[UltraGreen.ai]] was the biggest decliner (-15.9% to US$0.475).
- **Market Breadth & Activity**: Losers beat gainers 304 to 250; 1.1 billion securities worth S$1.8 billion changed hands. Salt Investments was most active by volume (67.3 million shares); DBS by value (S$262.5 million).
- **Regional Markets**: Mixed. [[Hang Seng Index]] -0.6%, [[Nikkei 225]] +1.1%, [[Kospi]] -1.2%, [[FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI]] -0.2%.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI Level**: Edged down 0.03% to 5,396.03.
- **Top Blue-Chip Gainer**: Genting Singapore rose 1.7% to S$0.60.
- **Laggard**: Wilmar fell 4.9% to S$3.11 on investigation and weak earnings.
- **Banks Mixed**: OCBC gained 1.1% while DBS and UOB slipped.
- **Small-Cap Drop**: UltraGreen.ai fell 15.9% on a revenue guidance miss.
- **Breadth**: Slightly negative at 304 losers versus 250 gainers.
- **Regional Divergence**: Nikkei rose 1.1% while Kospi fell 1.2%.
