## Summary

[[Microsoft]] (MSFT) fell ~35% from its October 2025 record close of $542.07 to a June 25 low of $352.83, then rebounded ~46% to trade near $516 — roughly 5% below the record. The sharp recovery was driven by a fiscal Q4 earnings beat in which [[Azure]] grew 43% YoY, accelerating above management's 39–40% guidance, and EPS grew 32%. The author views the stock as approximately fairly valued at current prices and would only consider buying if Azure reaches ~45% growth and free cash flow resumes rising.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] (MSFT) Recovery Arc**: A 35% drawdown followed by a 46% rebound brings the stock to within ~5% of its all-time high, compressing the margin of safety for new buyers.
- **[[Azure]] Growth Acceleration**: Q4 FY2026 Azure grew 43% YoY, beating management's 39–40% guidance. CFO [[Amy Hood]] attributed the beat to efficiency gains and faster capacity delivery that "was quickly monetized," and guided ~45% Azure growth for Q1 FY2027.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: Q4 capex rose 69% YoY to $41 billion. The full-year calendar 2026 figure was revised down to ~$175 billion due to an accounting change — not an actual reduction — and spending is expected to rise again in FY2027.
- **[[Multiple Compression and Expansion]]**: Shares now trade at ~29x earnings versus ~40x at the October 2025 peak, aided by 32% EPS growth. The valuation recovery reflects the earnings beat but leaves limited upside if growth slows.
- **[[Free Cash Flow]] Decline**: Q4 FCF fell ~23% YoY, and FCF also declined on a full-year basis. CFO Amy Hood's FY2027 FCF goal was simply to "remain free cash flow positive" — a notably cautious bar.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock near record**: MSFT at ~$516 is ~5% below the $542.07 October 2025 all-time high.
- **Azure beat guidance**: 43% YoY growth vs. 39–40% guided; next quarter guided at ~45%.
- **Valuation reset**: ~29x earnings now vs. ~40x at the October peak.
- **EPS up 32%**: Strong earnings growth absorbed into a lower multiple.
- **Q4 FCF fell ~23%**: Full-year FCF also declined; capex intensity remains high.
- **Capex revision was accounting-only**: ~$175B calendar 2026 capex reflects reclassification, not reduced spending.
- **Author's buy criteria**: Azure must hit ~45% growth AND FCF must resume rising.
- **Current stance**: Approximately fairly valued — much of the good news is already priced in.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mr. Market]]**: The 46% rebound from the June low illustrates how rapidly Mr. Market reprices a business on a single earnings catalyst — the Azure beat acted as a sentiment reset, pulling the stock from deeply oversold to near-record in weeks, compressing the rational entry point for new investors.
- **[[Margin of Safety]]**: At ~29x earnings with FCF declining and capex rising, the margin of safety for new buyers is thin; the author's two-condition buy thesis (Azure ~45% + FCF recovery) reflects this discipline — waiting for evidence before adding risk.
