## Summary

[[IBM]] shares rose 5.2% on July 28, 2026 to $227.42 on volume 59% above average, supported by reported insider buying over the past six months and analyst price targets sitting well above the current trading level. The move comes as IBM remains roughly 38% below its June 2 52-week high of $332.46, following a sharp decline to a 52-week low of $204.44 on July 16.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Insider Buying Signal]]** — insider purchases during a stock's downturn are read by the market as a confidence signal in a recovering name
- **[[Index-Level Contribution]]** — IBM's 4.3% move was cited as a meaningful driver of the S&P 500 index fund [[VOO]]'s 0.4% daily gain, alongside Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, and Apple

## Key Takeaways

- **Price action**: +5.2% to $227.42 on 13.1M shares traded (vs. 8.2M average)
- **52-week range**: $204.44–$332.46, meaning the stock remains well below its 2026 high despite the rally
- **Analyst targets** cited: BofA $330, Citigroup $375, Barclays $350, JP Morgan $291, Morgan Stanley $267 — a wide dispersion reflecting disagreement on IBM's AI-era trajectory
