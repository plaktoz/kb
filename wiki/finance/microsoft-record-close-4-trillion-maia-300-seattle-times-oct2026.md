## Summary

[[Microsoft]] closed at a record $543.45 on October 13, ending a four-month rally after an eight-month slide that erased about $1 trillion. The turn came with fiscal 2026 results showing the fastest cloud growth in four years, and the market cap returned above $4 trillion after the Maia 300 custom AI chip announcement. The company expects $190 billion of capex this calendar year and reported more than 30 million paid Copilot users.

## Core Concepts

- **Record close**: $543.45, beating the prior record of $542.07 set in October 2025; up almost 39% since the July 29 earnings report.
- **June drop**: A 17% fall, the worst month since 2000, driven by concerns over [[AI Capex]] returns and slightly slower cloud growth.
- **[[Maia 300]]**: Third-generation custom AI chip, to be widely deployed in data centers next year, reducing reliance on [[Nvidia]]; Guthrie says it delivers 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPUs.
- **Relative performance**: Up 15% for the year versus 21% for the Nasdaq-100; ahead of Amazon and Alphabet, behind Meta (17%).
- **[[Copilot]] pivot**: After complaints about buggy Windows features, Microsoft pulled back consumer AI and launched a Copilot "super app" in September; paid users rose from 20 million to over 30 million.
- **Local AI hardware**: New Surface PCs run AI agents locally, framed as a security feature.

## Key Takeaways

- **Rebound Trigger**: Fastest cloud revenue growth in four years reversed sentiment.
- **Capex Scale**: $190 billion on pace for this calendar year.
- **Own Silicon**: Maia 300 is Nadella's "biggest bet yet" on custom chips.
- **Copilot Traction**: Paid users rose from 20 million to over 30 million.
- **Still Lagging Nasdaq**: Microsoft's 15% trails the index's 21%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What drove Microsoft's record close?
**A**: A four-month rally that began with fiscal 2026 earnings showing the fastest cloud growth in four years, then accelerated after the Maia 300 chip announcement.

**Q2**: What concern drove the earlier slide?
**A**: Investors doubted returns on huge AI capex, which is on pace for $190 billion this calendar year, and cloud growth had slowed slightly, culminating in a 17% drop in June.

**Q3**: How does Maia 300 relate to Microsoft's strategy?
**A**: It lets Microsoft rely less on outside chipmakers like Nvidia and promises 40% more tokens per megawatt, improving efficiency of its AI infrastructure.
