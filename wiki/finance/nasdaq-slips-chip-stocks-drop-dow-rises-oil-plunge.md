## Summary

Major US indexes posted mixed results on July 27, 2026, as chip stocks weighed on the Nasdaq (-0.2%) while the Dow (+0.5%) and S&P 500 edged higher. Oil prices plunged sharply — WTI down 7.8% and Brent down 9.1% — after reports of a pause in US-Iran hostilities. Attention turned to the busiest earnings week of the season, with Magnificent Seven members Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, and Apple set to report alongside a Fed interest rate decision.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nasdaq Composite]]** underperformed as [[semiconductor]] stocks sold off broadly; the [[iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX)]] closed down ~2% and memory names like [[Sandisk]], [[Western Digital]], and [[Seagate]] fell 4–11%.
- **[[Nvidia]] vs. [[Apple]] market cap**: Nvidia dropped ~5.5% after reports of a $250B Ohio data center financing deal with [[OpenAI]], handing Apple the top market cap spot at ~$4.94 trillion.
- **Oil price collapse**: [[West Texas Intermediate (WTI)]] futures sank 7.8% to $82.30/barrel; [[Brent Crude]] fell 9.1% to $88 on geopolitical de-escalation. [[Chevron (CVX)]] and [[ExxonMobil (XOM)]] each declined ~1.5–2.5%.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] rate hike expectations**: Traders priced in a 38% chance of a rate hike at the July meeting (up from 16% a week prior) and 82% probability of at least a 25bp hike at the September meeting.
- **[[Quantum computing]] stocks**: [[D-Wave Quantum (QBTS)]] surged 18% after signing a deal with [[AT&T]] to cut network optimization processing time from ~1 hour to under 15 seconds. [[Rigetti (RGTI)]] and [[IonQ (IONQ)]] rose 9% and 7% respectively.
- **[[Household wealth]] and equities**: [[Goldman Sachs]] noted US households hold 32% of all assets in stocks — the highest in Fed records dating to the 1950s and above the 27% dot-com peak.
- **[[AI infrastructure spending]]**: Big Tech earnings focus centered on capital expenditure levels for AI data centers; [[Alphabet]]'s prior-week miss was attributed partly to raised capex guidance.

## Key Takeaways

- Nasdaq -0.2%, Dow +0.5%, S&P 500 roughly flat on July 27.
- WTI oil -7.8% to $82.30; Brent -9.1% to $88 on US-Iran pause.
- Nvidia lost largest market cap to Apple; NVDA fell ~5.5%.
- SOXX ETF -2%; memory stocks (SNDK, WDC, STX) down 4–11%.
- Fed rate hike odds climbed: 38% for July, 82% for September.
- 10-year Treasury yield pulled back ~4bps to ~4.64% after hitting 4.72%.
- D-Wave +18% on AT&T network deal; RGTI +9%, IONQ +7%.
- US households at record 32% stock allocation vs. 27% dot-com peak.
- Meta, MSFT, AMZN, AAPL earnings and Fed decision dominate the week.
- **AI capex scrutiny**: Alphabet selloff foreshadows pressure on Mag-7 results.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Risk-Off Rotation]]**: The divergence between chip stocks and defensive blue chips illustrates how a single sector catalyst (geopolitics + AI capex fears) can fragment an otherwise rising market — different risk profiles unwind at different speeds.
- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Rising rate-hike expectations push Treasury yields higher, which compress tech valuations and amplify the chip selloff, which in turn heightens investor anxiety — the feedback loop reinforces itself until a new anchor (earnings, Fed signal) breaks it.
