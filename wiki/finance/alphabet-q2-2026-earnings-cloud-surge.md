## Summary
Alphabet beat Q2 2026 expectations with $119.8B in revenue (+24% YoY) and Google Cloud growing 82% to $24.8B — its fastest growth rate in recent quarters. Nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 now uses Gemini Enterprise, validating AI as a real enterprise revenue driver rather than hype. However, CapEx of $44.9B pushed free cash flow negative (-$5.9B), making this the clearest single-quarter demonstration of the AI investment dilemma: extraordinary growth at extraordinary cost.

## Core Concepts

- [[Alphabet]] — Google's parent company (GOOG); Q2 2026 results confirm AI-driven growth acceleration
- [[Google Cloud]] — $24.8B revenue (+82% YoY); operating income grew from $2.8B to $8.8B
- [[Gemini Enterprise]] — Google's flagship enterprise AI product; deployed by 90% of Fortune 100
- [[AI Capex Cycle]] — massive spending on AI infrastructure (Alphabet: $44.9B in Q2) that precedes and enables revenue growth
- [[FCF Compression]] — free cash flow turned negative ($-5.9B) as CapEx outpaced operating cash flow
- [[AI Infrastructure Trade]] — Alphabet's numbers are the clearest data point that AI investment translates to accelerating cloud revenue

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue**: $119.8B (+24% YoY), beat consensus $116.93B
- **Google Cloud**: $24.8B (+82%), operating income $8.8B (vs $2.8B prior year)
- **Google Services**: $94.5B (+15%); Search $63.3B (+17%), YouTube $11.1B (+13%)
- **Operating income**: $40.8B (+30%), margin expanded to 34%
- **Net income**: $112.1B — includes $99B unrealized gains on equity securities
- **CapEx**: $44.9B; FCF: -$5.9B — AI investment phase creates short-term cash drag
- **Gemini reach**: 90% of Fortune 100 using Gemini Enterprise product
- **Funding**: Raised $49.6B by issuing stock in June to fund AI infrastructure expansion
- **Dividend**: $0.22/share quarterly, payable September 14, 2026
- **Cloud trajectory**: 48% (Q4 2025) → 63% (Q1 2026) → 82% (Q2 2026) — acceleration confirmed

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[J-Curve Investing]]**: Alphabet's negative FCF is the classic J-curve — heavy upfront investment (CapEx $44.9B) creating a cash trough before the revenue curve inflects upward. Cloud's 82% growth is the inflection already occurring.
- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: Gemini Enterprise at 90% Fortune 100 penetration creates a data and adoption moat — each enterprise customer trains the model further, makes switching costlier, and deepens Alphabet's AI infrastructure advantage.
