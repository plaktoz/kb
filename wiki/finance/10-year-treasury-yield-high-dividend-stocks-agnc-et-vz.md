## Summary

With 10-year Treasury yields near 4.8% — a high not seen since November 2023 — income investors face a high bar for dividend stocks to justify the added equity risk. Three stocks stand out as offering yields well above the Treasury benchmark: AGNC Investment (~13.5%), Energy Transfer (~6.3%), and Verizon (~5.6%).

## Core Concepts

- **[[10-Year Treasury Yield]]**: Benchmark rate at ~4.8%, setting the risk-free hurdle for income-seeking investors.
- **[[AGNC Investment]]**: Mortgage [[REIT]] holding leveraged agency [[Mortgage-Backed Securities]] (MBS); default risk is minimal due to government backing, but performance is sensitive to interest rate movements and MBS spread compression.
- **[[Energy Transfer]]**: Large-cap [[Midstream Energy]] pipeline operator; ~90% of adjusted EBITDA is fee-based, providing distribution stability; exposed to AI data center electricity demand and NGL exports as growth tailwinds.
- **[[Verizon Communications]]**: Mature telecom with a recovering subscriber trajectory; [[Frontier Acquisition]] adds fiber capacity; trades at ~9.5x forward P/E on 2027 estimates.
- **[[MBS Spreads]]**: Currently ~200 bps above Treasuries — historically elevated — creating potential for spread compression that would benefit AGNC's portfolio value.
- **[[Distribution Coverage Ratio]]**: Energy Transfer's distribution is covered at 2.2x, indicating strong payout safety.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treasury Hurdle**: 10-year yield at 4.8% raises the bar for dividend stock selection.
- **AGNC Yield**: ~13.5% yield; agency MBS default risk is minimal but rate sensitivity is high.
- **MBS Spread Opportunity**: Spreads near 200 bps; potential tailwind if Fed buybacks reduce supply.
- **Energy Transfer Growth**: AI data center demand and NGL exports drive distribution growth of 3–5% annually.
- **ET Coverage**: Distribution covered at 2.2x; ~90% fee-based EBITDA reduces cash flow volatility.
- **Verizon Subscriber Trend**: 184,000 postpaid phone net adds last quarter — best result in five years.
- **Verizon Valuation**: Forward P/E of ~9.5x on 2027 estimates signals value relative to yield.
- **Frontier Acquisition**: Adds substantial fiber network capacity to Verizon's infrastructure.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Risk-Adjusted Return]]**: A 13.5% dividend yield from AGNC only beats Treasuries after accounting for duration risk and spread volatility — high nominal yield does not equal superior risk-adjusted return without contextualizing the embedded risks.
- **[[Margin of Safety]]**: Energy Transfer's 2.2x distribution coverage ratio and fee-based contract structure provide a buffer against earnings shocks — the distribution would need to fall dramatically before being cut.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core argument for considering these three dividend stocks over 10-year Treasuries?
**A**: With 10-year yields at ~4.8%, these three stocks offer yields of 5.6%–13.5%, meaningfully above the risk-free benchmark, making them viable for income investors willing to accept equity-specific risks.

**Q2**: Why is AGNC's ~13.5% yield considered relatively safe from a credit standpoint, and what is its primary risk?
**A**: AGNC holds agency-backed MBS, so government backing eliminates default risk; its primary risk is interest rate sensitivity and MBS spread movements, not borrower default.

**Q3**: How could an income investor use Energy Transfer's distribution coverage ratio in their investment decision?
**A**: The 2.2x coverage ratio signals the distribution is well-protected — ET's adjusted EBITDA would need to fall by more than half before the payout faced pressure, providing confidence in yield sustainability alongside the planned 3–5% annual growth.
