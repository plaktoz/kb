## Summary

A [[Chartmill]] snapshot of [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] components in the Wednesday, October 14, 2026 session shows healthcare and consumer-staples names leading gains, with [[Merck]] up 3.84% and [[Amgen]] up 3.17%. Losers were led by [[Caterpillar]] (-3.32%), [[Nvidia]] (-3.09%) and [[Goldman Sachs]] (-2.11%), alongside declines in [[Amazon]], [[Microsoft]] and [[Salesforce]]. Several moves came a day after Q3 earnings releases from [[UnitedHealth Group]] and [[JPMorgan Chase]].
