## Summary

US tech stocks fell for a second day as investors punished weaker-than-expected third-quarter earnings against a backdrop of roughly $800bn of 2026 AI spending by five big tech firms. Intel dropped 28% and Alphabet fell another 1.4% after a 12% plunge, while the Nasdaq posted its worst monthly slide since March 2025. Analysts framed it as investors demanding payback on AI investment, while noting valuations remain above year-ago levels.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Bubble]] fears**: Worry that the cost of building AI data centres and software will exceed the money it earns, undermining sky-high valuations.
- **[[Alphabet]]**: Lost over $500bn in value after saying AI search summaries were affecting core search advertising revenue; expects up to $205bn of largely AI investment this year.
- **[[Intel]]**: Shed more than $130bn after forecasting lower Q4 revenue, negative free cash, and a PC slowdown through the first half of next year, while raising AI-related spending.
- **[[Amazon]]**: Spent more cash than it made, with spending up six-fold in three months and $220bn committed this year.
- **[[Reddit]] and [[Roblox]]**: Fell 29% and 18% on weak daily user numbers, with Reddit citing Google's AI-driven search hurting referral traffic.
- **[[AI Capex]]**: Meta, Alphabet, Oracle, Amazon, and Microsoft committed about $800bn to AI spending in 2026.
- **[[Nasdaq]]**: Closed 2.8% lower, yet remains up 7.8% over the last year.

## Key Takeaways

- **Earnings Punishment**: Investors are "ruthless", punishing any Q3 earnings disappointment amid heavy AI spending.
- **Intel Plunge**: Shares fell 28%, erasing over $130bn, on weak guidance and a PC slowdown.
- **Alphabet Search Risk**: AI summaries are hurting its core search ad revenue, costing $500bn+.
- **Spending Scale**: Five big tech firms committed roughly $800bn to AI in 2026.
- **Cash Burn**: Amazon's spending exceeded its cash generation, rising six-fold in three months.
- **Context**: Nasdaq is still up 7.8% over twelve months despite the October slide.
- **Peak Signal**: Continued AI stock weakness could suggest the AI trade is past its peak.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did US tech stocks fall sharply in this episode?
**A**: Investors grew concerned that hundreds of billions of dollars in AI spending is not being matched by earnings, and punished disappointing Q3 results from firms like Alphabet, Intel, Amazon, and Reddit.

**Q2**: What specific evidence of strain did Alphabet and Intel show?
**A**: Alphabet said AI search summaries were affecting search ad revenue (a $500bn value loss) while it plans up to $205bn of spending. Intel forecast lower Q4 revenue and no excess cash this year, losing over $130bn in value.

**Q3**: How should one read the selloff given the longer-term picture?
**A**: Nasdaq and Amazon remain up over the last year, so the move is a reset rather than a collapse. Analysts said a continued AI stock struggle in early November could signal the AI trade is past its peak and investors will seek a new theme.
