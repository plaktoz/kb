## Summary

U.S. stock futures pointed to a solid rebound on Nov. 2, 2026 after a bruising October close, as energy prices cooled on [[Donald Trump]]'s remarks about ending the [[Iran]] conflict. A [[Polymarket]] contract priced a 76% chance of a higher S&P 500 open, with [[Apple]] and [[Palantir]] earnings due after the close.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Polymarket]] contract**: "S&P 500 (SPX) Up or Down on November 2?" showed a 76% chance of a higher open. The prior Oct. 30 contract resolved "Down" on $9,246 in trading volume.
- **Index futures**: [[Nasdaq 100]] futures +0.92%, [[Russell 2000]] +0.62%, S&P 500 +0.47%, Dow +0.36%.
- **Oil and geopolitics**: Trump said the US will "put it away very soon" regarding Iran, though Iran reported two tankers hit naval mines in the [[Strait of Hormuz]]. Brent fell 2.99% to $110.04 and WTI fell 3.14% to $100.85.
- **Earnings docket**: Apple, watched for its AI strategy under new CEO [[John Ternus]], and Palantir report after the bell, with midterm elections the next day.
- **[[Yardeni Research]] view**: Investors sought safety in government bonds during last week's AI sell-off, easing yields. October's biggest losers were this year's AI winners (South Korea -16.4%, Taiwan -10.4%), yet the US market (-0.9%) still outperforms, with the US share of global market cap at 65.3%.
- **Friday's close**: SPY fell 1.77% to $755.72, QQQ fell 3.47% to $690.49, and DIA fell 1.07% to $517.13.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prediction Odds**: Polymarket gave a 76% chance of a higher S&P 500 open.
- **Futures Rebound**: Nasdaq 100 futures led at +0.92%.
- **Oil Retreat**: Brent fell to $110.04 and WTI to $100.85 on Iran de-escalation.
- **Tech Sell-Off Context**: QQQ dropped 3.47% on Friday; SPY fell 1.77%.
- **Earnings Focus**: Apple's first Ternus-led call and Palantir report after the close.
- **Global Divergence**: Korea and Taiwan fell sharply in October; US fell only 0.9%.
