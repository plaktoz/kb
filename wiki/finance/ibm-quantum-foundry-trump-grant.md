## Summary

[[IBM]] shares jumped 12.4% after the US [[Department of Commerce]] proposed a $1 billion grant supporting [[Anderon]], IBM's new quantum chip foundry venture in Albany, New York, part of a broader $2 billion government push across nine quantum computing firms. Analysts, including [[Wedbush]], see this as a durable catalyst that could make IBM the first purpose-built US quantum manufacturing foundry, even though despite the rally IBM shares remained down 14% year-to-date.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anderon]]** — IBM's quantum wafer foundry spinout, matching the government's $1B with its own $1B investment plus IP and workforce; 300mm quantum wafer fab open to external customers
- **[[Loon]] and [[Nighthawk]]** — IBM's existing quantum processor projects, fabricated at NY CREATES Albany since November 2025, underlying Anderon's buildout
- **[[Quantum Computing]]** — Emerging technology sector; US government projected $850B in economic value by 2040; [[Department of Commerce]] committed $2B package across quantum firms
- **[[Quantum Chip Foundry]]** — A merchant-scale semiconductor fab dedicated to quantum processors — analogous to TSMC but for quantum hardware; Anderon aims to be America's first
- **[[National Security and Tech Competition]]** — Trump administration framed the investment as critical to maintaining US technological superiority over [[China]]
- **[[Quantum Computing Grants]]** — $2B Commerce Department package also benefiting Rigetti Computing, D-Wave Quantum, and Infleqtion
- **[[Retail Sentiment Shift]]** — Stocktwits sentiment on IBM moved from "bearish" to "extremely bullish" within a day

## Key Takeaways

- **IBM stock jumped 12.4%** in its best single day in over a year, plus 4.6% more overnight.
- **Anderon aims to be America's first dedicated quantum manufacturing foundry**, estimated to unlock $850B in economic value by 2040.
- **IBM matched the $1B grant** with $1B investment plus IP, assets, and workforce.
- **Anderon is an open foundry**: 300mm quantum wafer fab welcoming external customers — the TSMC model for quantum hardware.
- **Wedbush maintained Buy with a $225 price target**, about 11% below the stock's post-rally price.
- **IBM remained down 14% YTD** despite the rally, reflecting a rough year overall for the stock.
- **Government-backed quantum bets are spreading**: Rigetti, D-Wave, and Infleqtion also received funding and rallied.

**Update (additional source, 2026-08-22, Stocktwits, reporting on the May 2026 announcement):** Frames the same Anderon $1B match within a larger **$10 billion, five-year total spending plan** covering R&D, capex, manufacturing expansion, and targeted acquisitions, aimed at a commercial fault-tolerant quantum system by 2029 capable of 100 million quantum gate operations. Confirms IBM already operates 90+ quantum installations globally. Adds analyst-rating detail at the time: of 21 analysts, 13 rated IBM "buy," 7 "hold," 1 "sell," with retail sentiment on Stocktwits "extremely bullish."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: Rather than betting on which quantum application wins, IBM positions Anderon as the infrastructure layer — supplying quantum chips to all players — capturing value regardless of which use case dominates, much like TSMC's foundry model in classical semiconductors.
- **[[Crowding In Effect]]**: Government capital commitment signals credibility to private investors, reducing perceived risk and unlocking additional private investment — IBM's dollar-for-dollar match illustrates this dynamic directly.
