## Summary
Third-quarter earnings season opens the week of October 5, 2026, with early reports from Delta, Pepsi and Levi Strauss before big banks kick it off in earnest the following week. Analysts are unusually upbeat. They raised S&P 500 Q3 EPS estimates 1.3% during the quarter, when estimates usually get cut, and now expect 29.1% earnings growth on 12.1% revenue growth. The other key event is Wednesday's release of the FOMC minutes, as investors weigh whether the Fed's tightening cycle continues after its mid-September hike.

## Core Concepts
- **Q3 [[Earnings Season]] kickoff**: [[Delta Air Lines]], [[PepsiCo]], [[Levi Strauss]], [[Applied Digital]], [[Lamb Weston]] and [[Aritzia]] report this week. [[JPMorgan Chase]] and [[Bank of America]] mark the unofficial start next week.
- **Upward [[Earnings Revisions]]**: [[FactSet]] data show analysts raised Q3 S&P 500 EPS estimates 1.3% during the quarter. That bucks the usual pattern of cuts as a quarter goes on.
- **[[Wolfe Research]] view**: Strong revisions raise the bar, but the [[AI Buildout]] remains strong, and earnings season should be a positive catalyst amid a choppy macro backdrop.
- **[[FOMC Minutes]]**: Minutes from the September [[Federal Open Market Committee]] meeting may show how policymakers see the tightening cycle. The [[Dot Plot]] showed 4 of 19 members expecting two more hikes in 2026.
- **[[John Williams]]**: The New York Fed President said another hike may not be needed in October after a cooler-than-expected inflation reading. See [[ny-fed-williams-rate-increase-can-wait-sep2026]] and [[october-rate-hike-odds-below-50-williams-barr-cook-sep2026]].
- **Rates backdrop**: 10-year Treasury yields hit multi-decade highs. See [[10-year-treasury-yield-highest-since-2002-bond-selloff-oct2026]].
- **Consumer data**: The [[University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment Index]] for October is due Friday, after another confidence gauge plunged in September to levels not seen since 2014.

## Key Takeaways
- **Q3 expectations**: S&P 500 revenue +12.1% y/y, earnings +29.1% y/y (FactSet).
- **Streak**: If met, it would be a third straight quarter of 25%+ earnings growth.
- **Revisions**: Analysts raised Q3 EPS estimates 1.3% instead of the usual cuts.
- **Last week**: Dow -1.3%, S&P 500 -0.3%, Nasdaq +0.5% with an intraday record.
- **September**: Dow -4.3%, S&P 500 -0.5%, Nasdaq +1.9%.
- **Wed Oct 7**: FOMC minutes at 2 p.m. ET; APLD and Levi Strauss report.
- **Thu Oct 8**: Pepsi reports at 6 a.m. ET after narrowly beating expectations last quarter.
- **Fri Oct 9**: Delta reports; [[Ed Bastian]] said demand held despite fuel-driven fare hikes.
- **APLD context**: Shares fell earlier this year after Nvidia sold its stake.
- **Fri Oct 9**: University of Michigan consumer sentiment at 10 a.m. ET.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why are analysts optimistic heading into Q3 2026 earnings season?
**A**: Analysts raised S&P 500 Q3 EPS estimates 1.3% over the quarter, when estimates usually fall. Wolfe Research expects the strong AI buildout to make earnings season a positive catalyst for stocks.

**Q2**: What growth rates are expected for S&P 500 companies in Q3, and what streak would they extend?
**A**: FactSet expects 12.1% revenue growth and 29.1% earnings growth year over year. Hitting those numbers would give the S&P 500 a third straight quarter of earnings growth above 25%.

**Q3**: What should investors watch in the FOMC minutes this week?
**A**: Look for how policymakers see the tightening cycle evolving after the mid-September hike. The dot plot shows four of 19 members expecting two more hikes in 2026, while NY Fed President Williams has suggested an October hike may not be needed.
