## Summary
Minutes of the October FOMC meeting and public remarks from Fed officials show markets bracing for further rate hikes into 2027, with rate-cut hopes all but gone. The Fed is alarmed that inflation is moving away from its 2% target, but officials are split on the size of the December hike. CME FedWatch prices roughly a 100% chance of a December hike, 75.4% for January, and roughly 100% for March 2027.

## Core Concepts
- [[Federal Reserve]]: [[FOMC]] held rates steady in October on a 10-2 vote, with [[Lisa Cook]] and [[Beth Hammack]] preferring a 25-basis-point hike.
- [[Kevin Warsh]]: Fed chairman who dropped forward guidance after taking over in May and calls internal debate "a good family fight".
- [[Christopher Waller]]: calls for a 50-basis-point hike in December, moving the range to 3.75%-4%.
- [[Forward Guidance]]: communication tool used to signal the future rate path and manage expectations.
- [[Inflation Expectations]]: minutes warn elevated inflation could become entrenched in household and business expectations.
- [[AI Build-Out]]: strains supply and raises prices of technology-related consumer goods.
- [[CME FedWatch]]: market-implied probabilities of hikes in December, January, and March.
- [[John Williams]], [[Michael Barr]], [[Mary Daly]]: favor further tightening but a more measured pace.

## Key Takeaways
- **Hike Path**: FedWatch shows about 100% December, 75.4% January 2027, about 100% March.
- **October Vote**: 10-2 hold, described by several participants as a "close call".
- **Jumbo Camp**: Waller wants 50 basis points to "shock people" unless guidance signals January.
- **Measured Camp**: Williams and Daly prefer deliberate steps, avoiding "dramatic action".
- **Inflation Drivers**: Tariffs, trade wars, the Iran War, AI demand, shelter, and food.
- **Bank Forecasts**: BofA sees 4.5%-4.75% after three hikes, possibly 5% next year.
- **Cuts Off Table**: No 2027 cuts barring sharp contraction or severe labor-market shock.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Entrenched expectations make inflation harder to reverse** — Prolonged above-target inflation can become embedded in household and business expectations, which is why officials argue for acting early and decisively. The article presents this as the minutes' central worry.
→ explains: Jumbo Camp, Inflation Drivers

**T2. Forward guidance steers behavior by managing expectations** — Signaling the future policy path influences economic behavior, so dropping guidance raises uncertainty and pressure for explicit commitment. Waller ties his 50-basis-point call to the absence of such guidance.
→ explains: Jumbo Camp, Hike Path

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the main conclusion of the article about 2027 rates?
**A**: Rate-cut hopes for 2027 have all but evaporated, because stubborn inflation is pushing the Fed toward further hikes beyond December.

**Q2**: What market and committee evidence supports this?
**A**: FedWatch prices about 100% odds of a December hike, 75.4% for January and about 100% for March, while the October minutes show nearly all policymakers expecting additional hikes.

**Q3**: How does the internal Fed split affect the December outlook?
**A**: Waller wants a 50-basis-point hike while Williams and Daly prefer measured steps, setting up a contentious Dec. 8-9 meeting over the size of the move.
