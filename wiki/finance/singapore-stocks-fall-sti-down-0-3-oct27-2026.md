## Summary

Singapore stocks ended lower on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, with the [[Straits Times Index]] (STI) down 0.3% to 5,397.03 as regional indices were mixed. A Julius Baer analyst attributed recent accelerated declines in Chinese equities to tighter capital and tax policy, weaker growth expectations, and a poor start to earnings season.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Lost 18.88 points (0.3%) to 5,397.03.
- **[[DFI Retail Group]]**: Top STI gainer, up 5% to US$3.55.
- **[[Thai Beverage]]**: Worst STI performer, down 3.4% to S$0.425.
- **Singapore Banks**: [[OCBC]] +0.04% to S$28.96, [[UOB]] +0.2% to S$41.37, [[DBS Group]] -0.4% to S$73.92.
- **[[iEdge Singapore Next 50 Index]]**: [[UOB Kay Hian]] top gainer (+5.7%); [[Aims Apac Reit]] biggest decliner (-3.6%).
- **Market Breadth and Activity**: Losers beat gainers 257 to 250; 933.4 million securities worth S$1.4 billion changed hands.
- **Regional Markets**: [[Nikkei 225]] +1.7%, [[Kospi]] +3.1%, [[FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI]] +0.5%, [[Hang Seng Index]] -0.7%, [[Shanghai Composite Index]] -1.3%.
- **China Equities Slump**: [[Richard Tang]] of [[Julius Baer]] cited three drivers of "accelerated declines."

## Key Takeaways

- **STI Level**: Down 0.3% to 5,397.03 on Tuesday.
- **Top Gainer**: DFI Retail Group rose 5% to US$3.55.
- **Laggard**: Thai Beverage fell 3.4% to S$0.425.
- **Banks Mixed**: DBS fell 0.4% while OCBC and UOB edged up.
- **Slightly Negative Breadth**: 257 losers versus 250 gainers.
- **Regional Split**: Kospi rose 3.1% while Shanghai fell 1.3%.
- **China Policy Drag**: Tighter outbound investment and offshore trust tax rules hurt sentiment.
- **Growth Pessimism**: Slower exports and property pre-sale phase-out weigh on 2027 outlook.
- **Weak Earnings Start**: Several large-cap China stocks missed in reporting season.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: How did the STI and Singapore market breadth finish on October 27, 2026?
**A**: The STI lost 0.3% (18.88 points) to 5,397.03, and losers outnumbered gainers 257 to 250 after 933.4 million securities changed hands.

**Q2**: What three factors did Julius Baer's Richard Tang give for China's recent equity declines?
**A**: Tighter capital and tax policy in H2 2026, more pessimistic growth expectations (slower exports, property pre-sale phase-out), and a weak start to the reporting season with large-cap earnings misses.

**Q3**: What does the regional picture imply for reading Singapore's move?
**A**: Regional indices diverged, with Japan, South Korea and Malaysia up while Hong Kong and Shanghai fell, so the STI's small decline reflected a mixed rather than uniform regional trend.
