## Summary

U.S. stocks slipped on August 11, 2026 as the [[US-Iran]] standoff intensified, casting doubt on reopening the [[Strait of Hormuz]] ahead of the July CPI report; [[Alphabet]] led declines, falling roughly 3.6%, amid renewed scrutiny of AI-infrastructure fundraising.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Strait of Hormuz]] standoff**: President Trump rebuked Iran's reparations demand and said he'd let economic pressure build; Iran's foreign minister called negotiations impossible under current conditions, despite Pakistan's defense minister suggesting the US and Iran were "close to some sort of arrangement."
- **[[Brent Crude]]**: Hovered near $89/barrel amid the standoff, a key input into inflation expectations ahead of CPI.
- **AI fundraising scrutiny**: New capital raises by [[Intel]] and [[Nvidia]] renewed investor questions about the sustainability of capital-intensive AI infrastructure spending.

## Key Takeaways

- Dow fell ~0.3% (53,791.85, -184.13 points), S&P 500 -0.3%, Nasdaq -0.6%.
- Big Tech broadly declined: Alphabet, Apple, and Amazon all fell.
- Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack said it may take more than one rate hike to rein in inflation, shifting rate-hike odds toward September.
- Earnings from [[CoreWeave]] and [[Super Micro Computer]], due after the close, were seen as bellwethers for AI hardware demand health.
