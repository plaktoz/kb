## Summary
US and Singapore stocks rebounded in the week to Nov 15 on strong AI earnings and Anthropic's oversubscribed IPO, despite Nvidia's slide after CEO Jensen Huang's exit and a 10-year Treasury yield above 5%. Beansprout's founder uses the Nvidia drop to argue for sizing positions so a sharp loss leaves room to reassess, and for keeping money with a near-term purpose separate from volatile investments.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nvidia]] and [[Jensen Huang]]: shares fell after Huang's exit; he has been a key backer of emerging AI companies, so succession, governance, and AI start-up funding are in question.
- [[Anthropic]] IPO: about six times oversubscribed, adding to AI enthusiasm alongside [[CoreWeave]], [[TSMC]], and [[Applied Materials]] updates.
- [[Treasury Yields]] and inflation: US core inflation rose 0.3% month-on-month in October versus 0.2% expected, pushing the 10-year yield above 5%.
- [[Position Sizing]] and the Opportunity Pot: keeping exposure manageable gives breathing room when a conviction idea takes a sharp hit.
- [[Diversification]]: ETFs such as the Nasdaq-100 and new SGX-listed iFAST Xtrackers AI ETFs spread exposure, with concentration risks to note.
- Four Pots of Wealth: giving each pot of money a clear purpose, compared across [[T-Bills]], fixed deposits, and SSBs for near-term cash.
- Singapore earnings: Singtel, Singapore Airlines, ST Engineering, SATS, CDL, Genting Singapore, ComfortDelGro, CSE Global, NTT DC REIT, Centurion, and UMS Integration reported results.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the founder's core advice after Nvidia's fall?
**A**: Before deciding to buy the dip, ask how much room an investment has to go against you. Sizing within the Opportunity Pot keeps exposure manageable so you can assess what changed.

**Q2**: What pushed the 10-year Treasury yield above 5%?
**A**: US core inflation rose 0.3% month-on-month in October, above the 0.2% expected. Higher rates could remain a headwind even as earnings look good.

**Q3**: Why keep holiday money separate from stock investments?
**A**: If a stock falls sharply just as you are ready to book the trip, you face selling at a loss or delaying the holiday. Separate pots let the family's plans stay provided for.
