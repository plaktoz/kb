## Summary

Snowflake (SNOW) reported fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of $1.55 billion, a 35% year-over-year increase, beating expectations and sending shares up as much as 20% in premarket trading. CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy attributed the acceleration to an "agentic enterprise flywheel" in which AI adoption drives customers to consume more of Snowflake's data platform. AI products accounted for roughly half of the quarter's growth acceleration, with adjusted EPS of $0.62.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Snowflake]] (SNOW)** — cloud data platform company whose revenue scales with customer data consumption
- **[[Agentic Enterprise]]** — Ramaswamy's framing: enterprises deploying AI agents at scale naturally increase data platform usage, creating a self-reinforcing growth loop
- **[[Cortex]]** — Snowflake's AI coding assistant product, cited as a rapid-adoption driver in Q2
- **[[CoWork]]** — Snowflake's enterprise chatbot product, also driving AI-related revenue
- **[[AI Monetization]]** — AI workloads amplify existing platform spend rather than requiring separate pricing tiers
- **[[Cloud Data Platforms]]** — the broader market context; Snowflake competes with [[Databricks]], [[Google BigQuery]], and [[AWS Redshift]]

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue**: Q2 2027 revenue hit $1.55B, up 35% YoY.
- **EPS**: Adjusted EPS of $0.62 for the quarter.
- **AI flywheel**: AI adoption drives higher data consumption, amplifying Snowflake's usage-based revenue.
- **AI contribution**: AI products drove ~50% of the quarter's incremental growth acceleration.
- **Stock reaction**: SNOW surged ~20% premarket; shares are up 40%+ year-to-date.
- **Key products**: [[Cortex]] (AI coding) and [[CoWork]] (enterprise chatbot) saw rapid uptake.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: More AI use → more data queries → more Snowflake revenue → investment in better AI tooling → more AI use; Ramaswamy explicitly named this dynamic as "accelerating."
- **[[Usage-Based Pricing]]**: Unlike seat-based SaaS, Snowflake's model means AI's appetite for data directly maps to revenue without renegotiating contracts — a structural advantage when AI workloads grow faster than human-user counts.
