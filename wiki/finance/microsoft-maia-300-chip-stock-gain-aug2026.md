## Summary
Microsoft stock rose about 1% on August 10, 2026, after reports it will unveil its new Maia 300 AI chip this fall and has secured TSMC manufacturing capacity for 300,000+ units in 2027, aimed at reducing reliance on Nvidia. An analyst price target raise and JPMorgan's more bullish S&P 500 target added further tailwinds.

## Core Concepts
[[Microsoft]]'s [[Maia 300]] chip is the company's second-generation custom AI silicon (following the original [[Maia]] chip from November 2023), aimed at reducing dependence on [[Nvidia]] processors. Microsoft is reportedly negotiating with [[Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC)]] for capacity to produce over 300,000 units for 2027 delivery, and hopes to persuade cloud customers including [[Anthropic]] to adopt it. [[Bernstein SocGen]] raised its price target citing measured [[Datacenter]] footprint expansion relative to cloud revenue growth, addressing concerns about [[AI Infrastructure Overbuilding]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Chip roadmap**: Maia 300 to be publicly unveiled this fall, with TSMC capacity secured for 300,000+ units in 2027.
- **Strategic aim**: Reduce reliance on Nvidia's costly processors for Microsoft's own AI infrastructure and cloud offerings.
- **Analyst reaction**: Bernstein SocGen raised its price target to $660 from $647, keeping an Outperform rating.
- **Sector tailwind**: JPMorgan raised its S&P 500 year-end target to 8,000, citing cloud capex converting to realized revenue — a thesis centered on hyperscalers like Microsoft.
- **Stock move**: MSFT rose ~1% to $504.54 in early trading, bucking a flat-to-down broader market.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core catalyst behind Microsoft's stock gain?
**A**: Reports that Microsoft will unveil its new Maia 300 AI chip this fall, alongside secured TSMC manufacturing capacity, reduced investor concern about the company's reliance on Nvidia.

**Q2**: What specific numbers characterize the chip deal?
**A**: Microsoft is in talks with TSMC to secure capacity for more than 300,000 Maia 300 units for delivery in 2027.

**Q3**: How does this connect to the broader AI infrastructure investment thesis?
**A**: JPMorgan's raised S&P 500 target and Bernstein's price target increase both reflect a view that AI capex is beginning to convert into realized cloud revenue, positioning hyperscalers like Microsoft favorably.
