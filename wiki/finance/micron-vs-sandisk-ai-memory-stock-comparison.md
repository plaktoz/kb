## Summary

Analysts favor [[Micron Technology]] (MU) over [[Sandisk]] (SNDK) for AI memory exposure, projecting ~53% upside for Micron versus ~24% for Sandisk. Micron's advantage stems from its diversified presence in both [[DRAM]] and [[NAND Flash Memory]] markets, while Sandisk is limited to NAND. With [[Deloitte]] projecting DRAM prices could quadruple by 2026 and supply staying tight through 2029–2030, Micron's broader portfolio positions it as the stronger long-term pick at a similar valuation.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Micron Technology]] (MU)**: Operates in both DRAM and NAND flash markets; holds 15% NAND market share and grew DRAM share from 22% to 24% in early 2025. Analysts project ~53% upside.
- **[[Sandisk]] (SNDK)**: Pure-play NAND flash memory maker; holds 11% NAND market share. Analysts project ~24% upside — solid but below Micron's.
- **[[DRAM]]**: Dynamic random-access memory critical for AI compute; prices projected to quadruple by 2026 with supply tight through 2029–2030 — Micron's primary differentiator versus Sandisk.
- **[[NAND Flash Memory]]**: Non-volatile storage for AI data centers; both companies compete here, but Micron holds larger share (15% vs. Sandisk's 11%).
- **[[AI Data Center Infrastructure]]**: The structural demand driver for both companies — memory intensive workloads sustaining elevated pricing across DRAM and NAND.
- **[[Forward P/E Ratio]]**: Both stocks trade at similar valuations, making Micron's diversification and growth premium the deciding factor.

## Key Takeaways

- **Analyst upside gap**: Micron ~53% projected gain vs. Sandisk ~24%.
- **Micron diversified**: Competes in both DRAM and NAND; Sandisk is NAND-only.
- **DRAM price catalyst**: Deloitte projects DRAM prices could quadruple by 2026.
- **Supply tight long-term**: DRAM supply constraints expected to persist through 2029–2030.
- **Micron NAND share**: 15% vs. Sandisk's 11% — larger even in Sandisk's home market.
- **Micron DRAM share growth**: Rose from 22% to 24% in early 2025.
- **Similar valuations**: Both at comparable P/E, making Micron's growth edge decisive.
- **EPS growth edge**: Long-term EPS growth estimates favor Micron significantly.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Diversification as Optionality]]**: Micron's exposure to both DRAM and NAND means it captures upside from whichever memory market outperforms — Sandisk's NAND-only focus is a concentration risk if DRAM prices quadruple as Deloitte projects.
- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: Both companies sell the foundational memory layer that all AI workloads depend on; the comparison reduces to which supplier has the broader shovel — Micron's portfolio depth makes it the higher-leverage pick within the same investment thesis.
