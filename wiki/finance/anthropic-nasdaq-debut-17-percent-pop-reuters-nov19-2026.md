## Summary

[[Anthropic]] debuted on the Nasdaq at a fully diluted valuation of about $2 trillion, opening 34% above its $83 IPO price before paring gains to close at $97.17, up 17%. The early exuberance faded as investors locked in quick gains and indexes retreated amid jitters over the [[AI Bubble]] debate, circular deals, and mounting debt. Analysts see the listing as a key reassurance for AI but only a beginning, with OpenAI, Nvidia, and data-center politics still unresolved.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: 980 million shares sold at $83, above the initial $70-$78 range; proceeds over $107 billion including a $26 billion private placement from AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC.
- **Fully diluted vs outstanding valuation**: The $2 trillion figure counts unvested options and RSUs; on outstanding shares the value peaked near $1.5 trillion, on par with [[Tesla]].
- **[[AI Bubble]] debate**: Soaring valuations and a web of circular deals among AI developers, chipmakers, cloud providers, and investors fuel durability questions.
- **[[Dario Amodei]]**: CEO who says Anthropic hopes to become investment grade and may use debt markets.
- **Compute commitments**: Nearly $900 billion in commitments with partners including [[Amazon]] and [[Google]].
- **Passive index demand**: [[Intropic]] estimates index-tracking funds could buy up to 30% of the offered shares by year-end.
- **Employee windfall**: Amodei and six co-founders hold about 10%, worth nearly $150 billion at the IPO price.
- **[[Goldman Sachs]] and [[Morgan Stanley]]**: Lead underwriters earning a $550 million fee.

## Key Takeaways

- **Debut Pop**: Opened at $111.50 (+34%), closed at $97.17 (+17%) versus the $83 IPO price.
- **Valuation Gap**: $2 trillion fully diluted versus up to $1.5 trillion on outstanding shares.
- **Growth Slowing**: Q3 revenue rose 47% quarter over quarter, but pace slowed sharply.
- **Debt Ambitions**: Amodei wants investment-grade status and may tap debt markets.
- **FOMO Demand**: Some buying may reflect fear of missing exposure to a leading AI company.
- **Retail Caution**: Strategist warns day-one hot tech IPO buyers underperform indexes over three years.
- **Open Risks**: OpenAI, Nvidia's situation, and data-center politics still shape AI sentiment.
- **Safety Tension**: Prospectus warns AI could behave unpredictably and outpace oversight mechanisms.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the central story of Anthropic's Nasdaq debut?
**A**: Anthropic reached roughly a $2 trillion valuation and closed 17% above its IPO price, but early gains faded as investors took profits amid renewed doubts about the AI boom.

**Q2**: Why does the $2 trillion valuation differ from the $1.5 trillion figure?
**A**: The $2 trillion is fully diluted and includes promised but unvested options and RSUs, while $1.5 trillion reflects Anthropic's actual outstanding shares.

**Q3**: What does the article suggest retail investors should do?
**A**: A strategist advised against treating Anthropic as a buy-at-any-price opportunity, noting hot tech IPO buyers on day one have historically underperformed broad indexes over three years.
