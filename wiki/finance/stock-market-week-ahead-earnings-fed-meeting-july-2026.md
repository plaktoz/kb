## Summary

The week of July 27, 2026 features the busiest stretch of summer earnings — [[Meta Platforms]], [[Microsoft]], [[Amazon]], and [[Apple]] all report — alongside a two-day [[Federal Reserve]] meeting where Chairman [[Kevin Warsh]] must weigh resurgent oil-driven inflation against a soft June CPI print. The June [[PCE Price Index]] and Q2 GDP, both due Thursday, are the week's most consequential data points, with the market pricing a one-third chance of a July hike and 95% odds of one by September.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Hyperscaler Capex]]** — Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon must all justify AI infrastructure spending against skeptical investors following Alphabet's capex raise
- **[[Azure Growth]]** — Microsoft's key metric; investors also watching Copilot paid-seat growth and stance on cheaper Chinese open-weight models
- **[[PCE Price Index]]** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge; core PCE expected up 0.2% month-over-month, 3.3% year-over-year
- **[[AI Capex Inflation Debate]]** — Fed Governor Lisa Cook flagged AI spending as an inflation risk; Warsh has argued AI will be deflationary long-term
- **[[Tim Cook]] Transition** — Apple's Thursday report is Cook's last earnings call as CEO before becoming executive chairman in September

## Key Takeaways

- **Four mega-cap reports this week**: Meta and Microsoft (Wed night), Amazon and Apple (Thurs night).
- **Fed decision Wednesday 2pm ET**: one-third market-implied odds of a hike, 95% odds by September.
- **PCE and GDP both due Thursday**: the week's most influential economic releases.
- **Boeing free cash flow is the key metric** to watch Tuesday, given its $1B–$3B full-year guidance.
- **Cook's final call as CEO**: Apple's Thursday report precedes his September transition to executive chairman.
