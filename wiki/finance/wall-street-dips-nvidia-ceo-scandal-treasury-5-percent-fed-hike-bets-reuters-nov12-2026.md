## Summary

Reuters reports global stocks were hit on Nov. 12 as reports that Nvidia's board met to decide CEO Jensen Huang's fate sent the stock down nearly 7% premarket, while the 10-year Treasury yield returned to 5% and the dollar hit a 19-month high. Traders now price a 90% chance of a 25-basis-point Fed hike next month after hot inflation data. Strategists see inflation repricing as the deeper driver, with Nvidia a risk-off catalyst.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]]**: Shares fell nearly 7% premarket on board reports about CEO [[Jensen Huang]]; earnings due next Tuesday.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: US 10-year yield back at 5%; German 10-year Bund yield at 3.3%.
- **[[US Dollar]]**: At a 19-month high, up over 1.5% since the strong inflation data.
- **[[Federal Reserve Rate Hike]]**: CME FedWatch shows a 90% chance of 25bp and 9.2% of 50bp.
- **[[Chinese Tech Stocks]]**: Alibaba fell 6.7% and Tencent 2.6% on possible curbs on offshore datacentre access.
- **[[Governance Discount]]**: Saxo's Charu Chanana says strong results may not remove it.

## Key Takeaways

- **Nvidia Plunge**: Premarket shares fell nearly 7% on reports of board deliberations.
- **Yields**: US 10-year yield touched 5% again after Wednesday's bond market closure.
- **Fed Repricing**: A week ago, one-in-three odds of a hold; now 90% odds of a hike.
- **Dollar**: Highest since April, after the 'Liberation Day' tariff aftermath.
- **Europe Divergence**: STOXX 600 hit its first record high since August.
- **Wall Street Futures**: About 0.5% weaker.
- **China Ripple**: Domestic Chinese GPU makers surged on expected share gains.
- **Next Test**: Nvidia earnings next Tuesday.
