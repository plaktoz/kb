## Summary

Netflix's [[Revenue Growth Deceleration]] has accelerated across three consecutive quarters — from a 17.6% peak in Q4 2025 to 13.4% in Q2 2026, with management guiding for roughly 12% in Q3 2026. The slowdown is attributed primarily to toughening year-over-year comparisons rather than weakening underlying demand, as evidenced by growing engagement and doubling advertising revenue. At roughly 19x forward earnings and 40% below its 52-week high, the stock is fairly priced at ~12% growth but not a clear bargain, prompting a hold recommendation for existing shareholders and a wait-and-see posture for new buyers.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Revenue Growth Deceleration]]**: Netflix top-line growth fell from a peak of 17.6% (Q4 2025) → to Q2 2026 at 13.4% → guided ~12% for Q3 2026 — a potential third straight quarter of deceleration.
- **[[Year-over-Year Comparison Drag]]**: Management attributes the slowdown to tough prior-year comps rather than demand deterioration — a distinction critical to evaluating the bull thesis.
- **[[Netflix Advertising Revenue]]**: Ad revenue is on pace to roughly double to ~$3 billion in 2026, representing an emerging growth engine partially offsetting subscription growth moderation.
- **[[Viewing Engagement]]**: Netflix logged over 97 billion viewing hours in H1 2026, with engagement growing 2% YoY — a signal that audiences remain active even as revenue growth cools.
- **[[Operating Leverage]]**: Operating income rose 11% YoY to $4.2 billion in Q2, demonstrating margin expansion even amid revenue deceleration.
- **[[Forward P/E Valuation]]**: The stock trades near $72, roughly 40% below its 52-week high, at ~19x forward earnings — described as "arguably fair if growth settles near 12%."

## Key Takeaways

- **Growth Trajectory**: Revenue growth peaked at 17.6% (Q4 2025), slowed to 13.4% (Q2 2026), guided ~12% for Q3.
- **Cause**: Tough year-over-year comps, not weakening demand, per management.
- **Engagement Holds**: 97 billion viewing hours in H1 2026; +2% YoY.
- **Advertising Surge**: Ad revenue on pace to ~$3 billion, roughly doubling in 2026.
- **Operating Income**: $4.2 billion in Q2 2026, up 11% YoY.
- **Full-Year Guidance Maintained**: 2026 revenue forecast held at $51.0–$51.4 billion.
- **Valuation**: ~$72/share, ~40% off 52-week high, ~19x forward earnings.
- **Verdict**: Hold for existing shareholders; new buyers should await a quarter confirming growth stabilization.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: The "tough comps" explanation is precisely mean reversion — Netflix's prior hypergrowth created a high base that mechanically compresses percentage growth even with continued absolute gains. Recognizing this prevents mistaking cyclical deceleration for structural decline.
- **[[Margin of Safety]]**: At 19x forward earnings pricing in ~12% growth, the stock offers no buffer against further deceleration — a single miss on the Q3 guidance would reprice it meaningfully lower, which explains the author's wait-and-see posture for new buyers.
