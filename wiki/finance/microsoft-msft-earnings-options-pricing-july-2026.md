## Summary

Ahead of [[Microsoft]]'s fiscal Q2 2026 earnings (consensus: $87.42B revenue, $4.21 EPS, +15.34% YoY), options market positioning shows a bullish skew — a 0.45x put-to-call ratio and upper-bound pricing near $413 — despite MSFT trading more than 15% below its June high. Technical setup and valuation (forward P/E under 20x) both favor upside, though insider selling over the trailing three months is a mild counter-signal.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Options Expected Move]]** — using the put/call ratio and contract pricing around an earnings date to infer the market's implied directional bias
- **[[Forward P/E Valuation]]** — MSFT's sub-20x forward multiple framed as attractive specifically because of its AI-driven earnings growth profile

## Key Takeaways

- **Consensus**: $87.42B revenue, $4.21 EPS (+15.34% YoY)
- **Options signal**: 0.45x put/call ratio on contracts expiring July 31 implies a bullish skew; upper strike at $413 implies >6% potential upside
- **Seasonality**: MSFT has a history of closing both July and August positive
- **Wall Street view**: "Strong Buy" consensus with an average price target near $545, implying ~40% upside over 12 months
- **Caution flag**: insiders have been net sellers over the trailing three months
