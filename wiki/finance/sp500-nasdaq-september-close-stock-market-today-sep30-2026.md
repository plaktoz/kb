## Summary

The [[S&P 500]] and [[Nasdaq Composite]] closed out a historically weak month with relative resilience — the S&P fell just 0.2% in September and the Nasdaq gained 2% — even as [[PCE Inflation]] stayed hot and Treasury yields climbed, with strategists pointing to favorable midterm-year seasonality as a Q4 tailwind. In single-stock news, Citi downgraded [[Moderna]] to Sell after its sevenfold rally, while BofA named Nvidia, Intel, Marvell, Micron, and Lam Research as its top Q4 chip picks.

## Core Concepts

- **[[PCE Inflation]]**: August PCE rose 0.2% month-over-month and 3.4% year-over-year; core PCE rose 0.2% monthly and 3.0% annually — better than expected, though BEA methodology changes affected the year-over-year comparison.
- **September jobs report**: Expected to show 84,000 jobs added (down from August's 162,000), with ADP data already showing private payrolls up 90,000 vs. a 68,000 forecast.
- **[[Rate hike odds]]**: CME Group FedWatch showed a 65% probability the Fed holds rates in October (up from 49% the day before), with a December hike still favored.
- **Midterm-year seasonality**: [[Mark Hackett]] (Nationwide) notes the six-month stretch around midterm elections has historically averaged 14% gains, double the all-year average of 7%.
- **[[Moderna]] downgrade**: Citi's [[Geoff Meacham]] cut MRNA to Sell, citing an "outsized run" (nearly sevenfold since mid-August) on its intismeran melanoma treatment with [[Merck]], arguing the re-rating reflects "unrealistic implied sales."
- **Chip stock picks**: [[BofA Securities]] analyst [[Vivek Arya]] favors [[Nvidia]], [[Intel]], [[Marvell Technology]], [[Micron Technology]], and [[Lam Research]] for Q4, citing historical seasonal outperformance of 300-500 basis points vs. the S&P 500.

## Key Takeaways

- **September close**: Dow -0.9% to 50,906 (-4.0% for the month); Nasdaq +0.2% to 28,861 (+2% for the month); S&P 500 -0.3% to 7,651 (-0.2% for the month).
- **Historical context**: S&P 500 has averaged a 1.1% September loss since 1928 — this year's -0.2% outperformed that norm despite a Fed hike and rising long-end rates.
- **Inflation data mixed**: PCE came in better than expected monthly, but core PCE annual rate stayed at 3.0%, still above the Fed's 2% target.
- **Moderna slumps**: MRNA fell 5.4% on the Citi downgrade; consensus among 23 analysts remains Hold (5 Buy/Strong Buy, 15 Hold, 3 Sell/Strong Sell).
- **Semiconductor momentum**: iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) finished September up 11.2%, with BofA expecting continued Q4/Q1 outperformance.
