## Summary
US stocks soared on Nov 23 after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO following pressure from President Trump, reversing part of the prior week's global rout. Nvidia closed 31% higher, the Nasdaq 100 rose 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.7%, while short-dated Treasury yields and the dollar rose on hawkish Fed bets. Last week's drop had been the Nasdaq's worst since March 2020.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Jensen Huang]] reinstatement**: Huang resigned after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China; the board first ruled out a return, then Trump pushed for it.
- **[[Nvidia]] as AI linchpin**: Source of funds for fledgling AI companies and primary chip supplier.
- **[[AI Spending Doubts]]**: Market already worried about lofty AI investment valuations.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: 10-year at 4.94%, 2-year at 4.69%, reflecting [[Federal Reserve]] expectations.
- **[[Safe Haven Assets]]**: Gold up 2.9% to $3,661; safe-haven bond buying reversed.
- **Asian market intervention**: Taiwan short-selling limits, China margin-financing easing, South Korea stabilization pledge.

## Key Takeaways
- **Nvidia Surge**: Shares rose as much as 36%, closing up 31%.
- **Index Moves**: Nasdaq 100 up 6.5%, S&P 500 up 4.7%, STOXX 600 up 1.8%.
- **Prior Week's Rout**: Nasdaq fell 10%, its worst since March 2020; VIX topped 40.
- **Yields and Dollar**: 2-year yield up 7.44 bps to 4.69%; dollar index at a 19-month high of 104.31.
- **Asia**: Taiwan up 2.1% and iShares MSCI Taiwan ETF up nearly 18%; KOSPI down 3.3%, having lost half its value.
- **Commodities**: Gold up 2.9%; Brent down 3.5% to $89.34 on higher Middle East supply.
- **Political Pushback**: Lawmakers questioned Trump's claim that Huang's return poses no national security risk.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What drove the Nov 23 rally?
**A**: Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after pressure from Trump, reversing the leadership crisis that had helped trigger the previous week's global equity rout.

**Q2**: What data show the scale of the move?
**A**: Nvidia closed 31% higher, the Nasdaq 100 gained 6.5%, and the S&P 500 gained 4.7%, after a 10% Nasdaq drop the prior week.

**Q3**: What does the episode imply for investors?
**A**: Concentration of AI sentiment in one figure and company amplifies volatility; analysts quoted said Huang's return should take heat out of the market, with one saying it is not too late to add back tech exposure.
