## Summary

US stock futures ticked higher and bond yields eased on August 13, 2026 as traders raised the odds the Fed holds rates steady next month, while Asian markets rallied further on AI enthusiasm ahead of the day's producer-price index release.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Fed Rate-Hold Odds]]**: CME Group data showed a 66% chance of the Fed holding rates steady next month, up from 45% a week earlier, with bets against any 2026 hike also rising.
- **[[Producer Price Index]] (PPI)**: The day's key data release, watched closely alongside the recent CPI report for signs of persistent or easing inflation.
- **[[Asian AI Rally]]**: South Korea's Kospi gained over 3% for a second straight day on Samsung and SK Hynix strength, while Lenovo shares surged 19% in Hong Kong on record revenue tied to AI infrastructure demand.
- **[[Summer Trading Lull]]**: Low trading volumes persisted as investors were away on vacation, with Brent crude drifting below $88/barrel amid quiet Iran-war developments.

## Key Takeaways

- **Fed rate-hold probability jumped** to 66% from 45% a week prior, per CME Group data.
- **Kospi gained 3%+ for a second consecutive day**; Lenovo shares jumped 19% in Hong Kong.
- **PPI release at 8:30 a.m. ET** was the day's key inflation data point, following the prior day's in-line CPI report.
- **Brent crude retreated slightly below $88/barrel** as Iran-war developments stayed quiet.
