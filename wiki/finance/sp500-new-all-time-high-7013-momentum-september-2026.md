## Summary
The S&P 500 has cleared a fresh all-time high above 7,013 on September 5, 2026, driven by strong bank earnings, recovered AI narrative, and pricing in a potential Middle East ceasefire that would reopen Strait of Hormuz oil flows, with the FOREX.com technical outlook remaining constructive despite the RSI crossing 70 (overbought) and key support at 7,000–7,013.

## Core Concepts
- **[[S&P 500 All-Time High 7,013]]**: The S&P 500 cleared the January 2026 peak of 7,013 for a fresh all-time high in early September 2026, extending the rally that began from the March 30 low (+22%). Now trading above 7,013 with upside targets at 7,100, 7,200, and 7,205 (127.2% Fibonacci extension).
- **[[Middle East Ceasefire Pricing]]**: Markets are pricing in the possibility of a permanent ceasefire and Strait of Hormuz reopening — risk appetite improvement despite Brent crude still near $95/barrel (absence of further escalation is sufficient catalyst).
- **[[RSI Overbought Signal]]**: Daily RSI crossed 70 but this reflects strong bullish trend momentum rather than an immediate sell signal; only a confirmed reversal pattern would validate the RSI warning.
- **[[S&P 500 Technical Levels]]**: Upside — 7,100, 7,200, 7,205 (127.2% Fib extension). Downside support — 7,000–7,013 (former resistance now support), 6,970, 6,890, 6,847.
- **[[Rate Cut Re-Pricing]]**: Fed rate cut expectations are returning to market pricing in September 2026 — additional tailwind alongside geopolitical improvement and strong earnings.
