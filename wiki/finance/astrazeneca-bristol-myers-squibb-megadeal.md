## Summary

AstraZeneca is reportedly in talks to merge with Bristol Myers Squibb in a deal that would value the combined pharmaceutical company at roughly $400 billion, according to the Financial Times. The deal, still unconfirmed, would be one of the largest mergers in history.

## Core Concepts

A merger between [[AstraZeneca]] and [[Bristol Myers Squibb]] would create one of the largest [[Mergers and Acquisitions|pharmaceutical mega-mergers]] ever, combining two major players in [[Oncology]] and broader drug development. The deal follows AstraZeneca's earlier successful defense against a takeover attempt by Pfizer roughly a decade ago, and comes as the company has been pursuing a U.S. direct listing to capture stronger American market valuations while remaining listed in London.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal size**: A merger would value the combined company at approximately $400 billion.
- **Status**: Still unconfirmed — talks have occurred over recent months, but the deal could materialize soon, be delayed, or fall apart entirely.
- **Historical context**: AstraZeneca previously fended off a takeover attempt by Pfizer about a dozen years earlier.
