## Summary

U.S. stocks fell broadly (Nasdaq -1.7%, S&P 500 -1.5%, Dow -2.2%) after Iranian missiles struck a U.S. base in Jordan and Trump vowed retaliation, sending crude oil up 6.6% to $84.46/barrel and pushing the 30-year Treasury yield to its highest level since 2007. [[Microsoft]] beat earnings with 43% Azure growth while [[Meta Platforms]] missed on earnings but beat on sales and raised capex guidance; Fortinet, Starbucks, and Glaukos were overnight winners while Robinhood and Woodward fell. The Fed held rates steady, and small caps slipped below key support as the broader market entered a fragile, hard-to-trade stretch.
