## Summary
Microsoft's Azure unit was reportedly asked to identify cost cuts of at least 20%, which Microsoft denies directing, amid heavy AI infrastructure spending. Cuts could improve operating leverage but raise questions about sacrificing investment to protect margins. Strong Q4 FY2026 results and elevated capex frame the profitability debate.

## Core Concepts
- [[Microsoft]] ([[MSFT]]) and its [[Azure]] cloud unit, led by President Girish Bablani (about 11,500 people).
- [[Operating Leverage]]: cost reductions offsetting the expense of expanding [[AI Infrastructure]] capacity.
- [[Maia 300]] custom AI chip aimed at cutting reliance on third-party accelerators.
- [[Capital Expenditures]]: roughly $50B in Q1 FY2027 and $175B for calendar 2026.
- Analyst views: UBS (Karl Keirstead) target $675, Jefferies (Brent Thill) target $650.

## Key Takeaways
- **Reported Cuts**: Azure leaders allegedly asked to find 20%+ cost reductions; Microsoft pushed back.
- **Q4 FY2026**: Revenue up 18% to $90B; Azure growth accelerated 43% YoY.
- **Backlog**: Commercial remaining performance obligations surged 84% to $678B.
- **Capex Pressure**: About $175B planned for calendar 2026 keeps margin concerns alive.
- **Valuation**: 27.46x forward earnings and 12.07x sales, a premium to peers.
- **Stock Performance**: Up 10% YTD, 8% over the past month, 4% over one year.
- **Analyst Consensus**: Strong Buy; average target $590.88, implying 11% upside.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the reported cost-cutting story at Azure, and how has Microsoft responded?
**A**: Azure President Girish Bablani reportedly asked senior leaders to identify cost reductions of at least 20%. Microsoft says Azure leadership has not directed teams to develop cost-cutting plans.

**Q2**: What evidence shows demand remains strong despite the cost pressure?
**A**: In Q4 FY2026, Azure and other cloud services revenue accelerated 43% YoY and commercial remaining performance obligations rose 84% to $678 billion. Microsoft also projected 45% Azure growth.

**Q3**: What should investors watch for given the reported cuts?
**A**: Evidence that Microsoft can improve cloud economics and margins while sustaining rapid AI-driven growth, rather than sacrificing investment to protect margins.
