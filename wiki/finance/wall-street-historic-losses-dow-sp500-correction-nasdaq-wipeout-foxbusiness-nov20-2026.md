## Summary

On Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, an AI-led sell-off sent the Dow and S&P 500 into correction territory and erased the Nasdaq's gains for the year. Nvidia fell about 14% and newly public Anthropic about 9% in its second session, despite strong Nvidia earnings and Anthropic's anticipated debut. Reports of tech outages at companies such as United Airlines and JPMorgan Chase added to investor unease.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Market Correction]]**: A fall of at least 10% from recent record highs; the Dow and [[S&P 500]] both entered it.
- **[[Nasdaq Composite]]**: Fell 5.28%, its second-largest single-day point drop on record, wiping out its 2026 gains.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Shares tumbled about 14% despite strong earnings.
- **[[Anthropic]]**: Fell about 9% in its second trading session as a public company.
- **[[AI Valuations]]**: Soaring valuations and spending drove the investor anxiety.
- **[[VIX]]**: The "fear gauge" closed at its highest since April 2025.
- **[[Gold]]**: Rose as investors sought safer assets.
- **[[Gerri Willis]]**: FOX Business anchor who reported on the session.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index Moves**: Dow fell nearly 950 points; S&P 500 dropped 3.7%; Nasdaq plunged 5.28%.
- **Weekly Damage**: S&P 500's worst week since April 2025; Nasdaq's worst since March 2020.
- **Losing Streak**: Seventh straight S&P 500 decline, longest since February 2020.
- **Good News Ignored**: Strong Nvidia earnings and Anthropic's debut failed to calm AI-valuation fears.
- **Tech Outages**: Meltdowns at United Airlines and JPMorgan Chase fed investor anxiety.
- **Safe Haven**: Gold rose while the VIX hit its highest close since April 2025.
