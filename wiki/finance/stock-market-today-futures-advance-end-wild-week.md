## Summary

US stock futures advanced on Friday to cap a volatile week, driven by Amazon's 11% premarket surge on strong cloud results and a prior-session 15.5% jump in Microsoft that added a record $450 billion to its market cap. The week included a mid-week sell-off after the Fed held rates steady despite ongoing inflation concerns, with Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's "noncommittal" stance drawing skepticism from bond markets and analysts. Apple dragged on sentiment with a 7.5% premarket decline tied to weaker-than-expected services revenue, while semiconductor and memory stocks posted outsized gains.

## Core Concepts

- [[Magnificent Seven]] earnings divergence: [[Amazon]] soared on cloud strength, [[Apple]] sank on services miss, [[Microsoft]] set a record single-day market-cap gain
- [[Federal Reserve]] policy under Chair [[Kevin Warsh]]: held rates steady, reaffirmed 2% inflation target but declined to signal hikes — bond markets questioned credibility
- [[S&P 500]], [[Nasdaq Composite]], and [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] rebounded sharply Thursday (+1.7%, +2.8%, +1.2%) after Wednesday's sell-off
- [[Microsoft]] added $450 billion in market cap in a single session, surpassing [[Nvidia]]'s prior record of $440 billion from April
- [[Semiconductor stocks]] ([[Roundhill Memory ETF]] DRAM, [[iShares Semiconductor ETF]] SOXX) surged 5% and 4% premarket after 16% and 8.5% gains the prior day; memory stocks ([[Micron Technology]], [[Western Digital]], [[Sandisk]], [[Seagate Technology]]) jumped 11–26% Thursday
- [[Novo Nordisk]] sank 11% premarket after heart drug ziltivekimab failed Phase 3 trial primary endpoint
- Oil prices modestly higher: [[WTI crude]] ~$85/barrel, [[Brent crude]] ~$90.30; [[10-year Treasury yield]] near 4.68%

## Key Takeaways

- **Amazon earnings**: Cloud strength drove 11% premarket rally for [[Amazon]]
- **Apple miss**: Services revenue disappointment sent [[Apple]] down 7.5% premarket
- **Microsoft record**: $450B single-day market-cap gain — largest ever for any US company
- **Fed credibility gap**: [[Bank of America]] called Warsh press conference "all hat, no cattle"
- **Memory stocks**: [[Micron Technology]] and peers surged 11–26% Thursday on chip optimism
- **Novo Nordisk**: Phase 3 heart drug trial failure; no 2026 profit impact but Q3 impairment charge
- **Week's arc**: Sell-off Wednesday on Fed hold → sharp Thursday rebound → positive Friday futures
- **Treasury yield**: 10-year above 4.68%, slightly elevated amid inflation uncertainty

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Credibility as Capital]]**: Warsh's refusal to signal rate hikes depleted the Fed's inflation-fighting credibility with bond markets — trust in an institution is only as durable as its willingness to act, not just to speak.
- **[[Earnings Asymmetry]]**: Within the same peer group ([[Magnificent Seven]]), outcomes diverged sharply based on segment-level execution, illustrating that macro tailwinds do not guarantee uniform results at the stock level.
