## Summary

[[Microsoft]] (MSFT) stock edged slightly lower on Thursday, Oct. 8, 2026, as tech stocks declined, while Italy's antitrust authority opened an EU-backed probe into in-game virtual currencies at Microsoft-owned gaming companies, including [[Activision Blizzard]]. Technically, MSFT remains in a long-term uptrend, but its [[Relative Strength Index]] of 70.76 signals overbought conditions ahead of Oct. 28 earnings. Analysts hold a Buy consensus, and several firms raised their price targets in early October.

## Core Concepts

- **EU Gaming Probe**: Italy leads the investigation, with Norway and Denmark, into whether complex virtual-currency conversions hide the real cost of purchases and violate EU consumer protection rules, especially rules protecting minors.
- **[[Consumer Protection Cooperation Network]]**: The EU body coordinating the broader crackdown. Last month it acted against nine gaming companies, including [[Riot Games]], [[Ubisoft]], and [[Supercell]].
- **Technical Setup**: The stock trades 4.4% above its 20-day [[Simple Moving Average]] ($508.55) and 22.4% above its 200-day SMA ($433.80). A [[Golden Cross]] formed in August.
- **[[Relative Strength Index]]**: A reading of 70.76 is above the overbought threshold of 70, which suggests a pullback or consolidation.
- **Benzinga Edge Rankings**: Growth 96.91, Momentum 89.49, Quality 86.67, but Value is only 19.3, which indicates a relatively expensive valuation.
- **ETF Exposure**: MSFT is a large holding in [[Vanguard Growth ETF]] (9.59%), the Technology Select Sector SPDR Fund (9.83%), and IUSG (9.75%), so flows into and out of these funds can move the shares.

## Key Takeaways

- **Price**: Down 0.09% at $529.26; the Nasdaq fell 0.48% and the S&P 500 fell 0.20%.
- **Regulatory risk**: An EU-backed probe targets Activision Blizzard's in-game currency practices.
- **Resistance**: $553.50, near the 52-week high of $553.72.
- **Support**: The 50-day SMA at $498.94, then $486.
- **Earnings Oct. 28**: Consensus EPS is $4.73 vs. $4.13 a year ago; revenue is $90.64B vs. $77.67B.
- **Price targets**: Average $578.32; Evercore $635, Scotiabank $615, Wells Fargo $725.
- **Valuation flag**: A low Value score may increase downside risk in market declines.
