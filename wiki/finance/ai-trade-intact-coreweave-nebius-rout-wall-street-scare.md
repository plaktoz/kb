## Summary

Tech (XLK) fell this week on financing concerns, pulling the S&P 500 and Nasdaq negative, with [[CoreWeave]] and [[Nebius]] each down more than 25%. Wall Street voices including Patrick Moorhead, Dan Ives and UBS framed the rout as "just another scare" or a "recalibration of expectations" rather than a break in the AI investment story. Strong demand commentary from [[ASML]] and [[TSMC]] supported that view.

## Core Concepts

- **[[CoreWeave]] and [[Nebius]]** — Neocloud providers that each closed the week down more than 25%.
- **[[Neocloud]] free cash flow** — Baird's Ted Mortonson put CoreWeave, Nebius and [[Oracle]] "probably at the bottom" of tech on free cash flow.
- **[[Vendor Financing]] scrutiny** — Mortonson cited "a higher cost of capital" and scrutiny of "circular finance."
- **[[Nvidia]] backing** — Jensen Huang voiced optimism on CoreWeave; Nvidia holds roughly a 10% stake.
- **Backlog as cushion** — [[Dan Ives]] noted CoreWeave's roughly $104 billion revenue backlog exiting Q2.
- **[[Patrick Moorhead]]** — Lists DeepSeek, the tariff crash and the "AI death scare" as earlier scares.
- **[[SaaSpocalypse]]** — Ives calls this AI infrastructure's version of the earlier AI-disruption software sell-off.
- **UBS view** — Ulrike Hoffmann-Burchardi calls it a "recalibration of expectations."

## Key Takeaways

- **Weekly rout**: CoreWeave and Nebius each fell over 25%; S&P 500 and Nasdaq ended negative.
- **Financing worries**: Higher cost of capital and circular finance drew scrutiny.
- **Nvidia support**: Huang's optimism matters given Nvidia's roughly 10% CoreWeave stake.
- **Backlog defense**: CoreWeave's roughly $104 billion backlog reflects commitments already in place.
- **Scare pattern**: Moorhead lists DeepSeek, tariff crash, "AI death scare" as precedents.
- **Sell-bias**: After big AI gains, "people are looking for a reason to sell."
- **Demand signals**: ASML and TSMC commentary pointed to robust AI demand.
- **UBS focus**: Watch where AI spending converts into sustainable earnings growth.
