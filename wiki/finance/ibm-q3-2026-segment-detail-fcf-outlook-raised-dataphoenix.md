## Summary
[[IBM]] reported Q3 2026 revenue of $17.103B, up 4.7% (5.6% at constant currency), and raised its full-year free-cash-flow growth outlook to more than $1B from about $1B. Software grew 8.2% while Infrastructure grew 4.2%, with Distributed Infrastructure up 31.3% and IBM Z down 19.6%. GAAP gross and pre-tax margins slipped slightly, and the article flags CEO [[Arvind Krishna]]'s "Super Intelligence" deployment claim as unquantified.

## Core Concepts
- [[IBM Q3 2026 Earnings]]: Revenue $17.103B versus $16.331B a year earlier; GAAP income from continuing operations $2.199B, $2.31 per diluted share.
- [[IBM Software]]: $7.804B, +8.2%; Hybrid Cloud +10.6%, Data +19.1%, Transaction Processing +9.3%, Automation -3.3%.
- [[IBM Infrastructure]]: $3.707B, +4.2%; Distributed +31.3%, Hybrid +8.3%, [[IBM Z]] -19.6%, Support -3.1%.
- [[Free Cash Flow]]: A non-GAAP measure; nine-month FCF $7.787B, up $606M, after subtracting financing receivables and net capex from operating cash flow.
- [[GAAP Margins]]: Gross margin 56.8% (-0.5 points); pre-tax margin 14.6% (-0.3 points).
- [[Company Guidance]]: Full-year FCF growth above $1B; constant-currency revenue growth 4% to 5% maintained; currency neutral.
- [[Super Intelligence]] (IBM term): Krishna's claim that clients deploy it across operating models.

## Key Takeaways
- **Revenue**: $17.103B, +4.7% reported, +5.6% constant currency.
- **Software**: $7.804B, up 8.2%; Data +19.1%, Automation -3.3%.
- **Infrastructure Split**: Distributed +31.3% while IBM Z fell 19.6%.
- **Margin Slip**: GAAP gross margin 56.8%, down 0.5 points year on year.
- **Cash Flow**: Nine-month FCF $7.787B; operating cash flow up $2.639B.
- **Raised Outlook**: FY FCF growth now above $1B versus about $1B before.
- **Unverified Claim**: Super Intelligence deployments were not identified or quantified in filings.
- **Revenue Guide Held**: Constant-currency growth of 4% to 5% maintained.
