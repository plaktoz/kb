## Summary

US stocks closed higher Friday, August 7, after a surprise nonfarm-payrolls decline of 23,000 reduced expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hike, with the S&P 500 setting a record close of 7,758 and [[Palantir]] and [[SpaceX]] posting outsized gains.

## Core Concepts

- **Weak jobs report**: Nonfarm payrolls unexpectedly fell 23,000, while unemployment and labor-force participation both declined, easing inflation-driven rate-hike fears.
- **[[Palantir]]**: Jumped 10.3%, extending gains after strong Q2 results and a raised 2026 outlook.
- **[[SpaceX]]**: Surged 15.8% on top of a prior-day rally, after Argus Research upgraded it to "Buy" citing rapid returns on AI infrastructure investment.
- **Laggards**: Sandisk (-3.7%) and Western Digital (-3.8%) fell, while Berkshire Hathaway slipped ahead of its earnings report.

## Key Takeaways

- S&P 500 rose 0.6% to a record 7,758; Nasdaq gained 1.3%; Dow added 152 points.
- Lower Treasury yields following the jobs report supported credit-sensitive stocks broadly.
- Palantir and SpaceX were the standout single-stock gainers, both tied to AI infrastructure narratives.
- Berkshire Hathaway shares slipped ahead of its weekend earnings release, a pattern often seen pre-earnings.
