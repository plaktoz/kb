## Summary

[[DocuSign]] (DOCU) peaked at $310 in late 2021 before falling 78% to ~$68 as pandemic demand faded, creating a potential buying opportunity. The company launched its [[Intelligent Agreement Management]] (IAM) platform to capture a $2 trillion market opportunity in contract workflow inefficiencies. With Q2 FY2027 revenue beating guidance and IAM adoption accelerating, the stock trades at a price-to-sales ratio of ~4.1 versus its historical average of 11.8.

## Core Concepts

- **[[DocuSign]] (DOCU)**: E-signature and contract lifecycle management company; IPO'd at $29 in 2018, peaked at $310 in 2021.
- **[[Intelligent Agreement Management]] (IAM)**: New platform addressing contract workflow inefficiencies; built around three products:
  - **Agreement Manager** — repository of 300M+ contracts with AI-powered search
  - **[[Iris AI]] assistant** — answers questions about any contract in an organization
  - **Agent Studio** — lets businesses build custom AI agents for drafting and negotiating agreements
- **Contract Management Market**: Deloitte research estimates businesses waste ~55 billion hours annually on poor contract management, representing $2 trillion in lost economic value.
- **[[Price-to-Sales Ratio]]**: DocuSign trades at ~4.1x vs. historical average of 11.8x since 2018 IPO — a significant valuation discount.

## Key Takeaways

- **78% drawdown**: Stock fell from $310 peak (2021) to ~$68, far below historical valuation norms.
- **IAM adoption**: Grown from 2.3% to projected ~18.5% of ARR by end of FY2027.
- **Small penetration**: Only ~40,000 of 1.9 million customers have adopted IAM so far.
- **Revenue beat**: Q2 FY2027 revenue of $875.7M beat guidance range of $865–$869M.
- **Revenue growth**: 9% year-over-year; GAAP net income up 23% to $77.7M.
- **Cost discipline**: Operating expenses grew just 2% while revenue grew 9%.
- **ARR target**: Management targets $3.55B in annual recurring revenue by end of FY2027.
- **P/S discount**: ~4.1x vs. 11.8x historical average suggests significant rerating potential.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: DocuSign's P/S of 4.1x vs. historical 11.8x suggests valuations tend to revert toward fundamental norms when a business re-establishes growth — the IAM platform could be the catalyst that closes the gap.
- **[[Diffusion of Innovation]]**: IAM adoption at only 2.1% of the customer base with strong early-adopter growth (~18.5% ARR by FY2027) mirrors the early-majority inflection point where growth accelerates sharply.
