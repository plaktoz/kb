## Summary

US stocks fell in shaky trading on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, but the market still posted its first winning week since early October. Technology stocks were the biggest drag, with Nvidia falling again a day after CEO Jensen Huang's resignation rocked the AI industry. Treasury yields rose while oil was little changed.

## Core Concepts

- [[S&P 500]]: fell 0.4% to 7,561.67, but gained 2.5% for the week.
- [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]: rose 0.1% to 52,347.55.
- [[Nasdaq Composite]]: fell 0.9% to 25,136.27.
- [[Russell 2000]]: fell 0.3% to 2,790.49.
- [[Nvidia]]: shares fell again after the resignation of CEO [[Jensen Huang]].
- [[Treasury Yields]]: rose in the bond market.

## Key Takeaways

- **Friday Close**: S&P 500 down 0.4%, Nasdaq down 0.9%, Dow up 0.1%.
- **Weekly Gain**: S&P 500 up 2.5%, Dow up 2.1%, Nasdaq up 2.3%, Russell 2000 up 1.3%.
- **Tech Drag**: Technology stocks were the biggest weights on the market.
- **Nvidia Turmoil**: Nvidia fell again after Jensen Huang's resignation.
- **Year to Date**: S&P 500 up 10.5%, Dow 8.9%, Nasdaq 8.2%, Russell 2000 12.4%.
