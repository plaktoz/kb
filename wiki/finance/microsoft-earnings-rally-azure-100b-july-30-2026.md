## Summary

Markets rebounded sharply on July 30 as [[Microsoft]] stock surged more than 16% — its best day since 2008 — after [[Azure]] cloud revenue topped $100 billion for the first time, while [[Meta Platforms]] fell about 9% on an earnings miss and rising AI costs. The Nasdaq jumped 2.4% even as the 30-year Treasury yield hit a multidecade high and fresh US strikes on Iran kept investors on guard.

## Core Concepts

- [[Microsoft]] — Azure revenue crossed $100B, driving a 16%+ single-day stock surge and ~$439B in added market cap
- [[Meta Platforms]] — shares fell ~9% after an earnings miss deepened AI-spending-return concerns
- [[Situational Awareness LP]] — Leopold Aschenbrenner's $24B hedge fund, reportedly sold all public equity holdings after heavy chip-stock losses
- [[AI Wage Compression]] — Apollo Global research finding AI-exposed occupations saw wages grow 6.7 points slower since 2023, with little effect on employment
- [[ESG Fund Revival]] — US sustainable funds saw net inflows for the first time since 2022, driven by AI data-center and energy-security interest

## Key Takeaways

- **Microsoft's historic day**: stock +16%, best single-day performance since 2008, on Azure crossing $100B in revenue
- **Meta divergence**: −9% on earnings miss despite record $60.8B revenue, as AI infrastructure costs surged 55%
- **Hedge fund unwind**: ex-OpenAI researcher's $24B fund reportedly liquidated its public book after the chip-stock rout
- **Labor market signal**: only 1% employment effect from AI exposure so far, but a measurable 6.7-point wage-growth gap
- **Macro backdrop**: 30-year Treasury yield near a multidecade high; GDP growth slowed to 1.5% in Q2, below the ~2% expected

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Regression to the Mean]]**: A single earnings beat driving a 16% single-day move after a prior sell-off illustrates how far sentiment (not fundamentals) had drifted from a reasonable valuation — the "surprise" is partly the market correcting an overly pessimistic prior, not purely new information.
