## Summary

AI stocks including Nvidia, Oracle, and CoreWeave fell on October 8, 2026, after reports that OpenAI told investors it reached about $50 billion in annualized revenue at the end of September. The widely reported $68 billion figure had included gross revenue from OpenAI's partners. The gap matters because OpenAI must justify an $852 billion valuation ahead of an expected 2027 IPO, while rival Anthropic reports a $65 billion run rate and is reportedly seeking a $2 trillion valuation.

## Core Concepts

- **[[OpenAI]] revenue disclosure**: About $50B annualized at the end of September, first reported by the [[Financial Times]] and confirmed by CNBC.
- **Gross vs. net revenue**: The $68B figure included partners' gross revenue, framed as a closer comparison with [[Anthropic]].
- **Growth metrics**: 77% total run-rate growth in Q3; 107% run-rate growth in enterprise.
- **[[Annualized Revenue Run Rate]]**: The metric both labs use to support their valuations.
- **Stock reaction**: [[Nvidia]] -3%, [[Oracle]] ~-6%, [[CoreWeave]] ~-8%, [[AMD]] -4%, [[Broadcom]] -4%, [[Intel]] -5%, [[Super Micro Computer]] ~-5%.
- **[[OpenAI IPO]]**: Valued at $852B; prospectus confidentially filed in June; a 2027 debut is eyed.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Reportedly seeking $2T; $65B run rate at end of July. [[New Constructs]] values it at only $150B; 2025 revenue was $4.6B with a $42B net loss (per Reuters).
- **[[AI Safety]] overhang**: OpenAI pulled GPT-6.1 Astra over safety standards; [[Sam Altman]] called now an "ill-advised moment to go public."
- **Funding**: OpenAI is in early talks on a round of about $30B, after a $122B round in March; CFO [[Sarah Friar]] says it is "very well capitalized."

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue reset**: OpenAI's actual run rate was about $50B, not the reported $68B.
- **Accounting gap**: The $68B figure counted partners' gross revenue.
- **Growth still strong**: 77% total and 107% enterprise run-rate growth in Q3.
- **Sector contagion**: CoreWeave fell nearly 8% and Oracle nearly 6%.
- **Valuation pressure**: $852B OpenAI valuation needs revenue to back it before the IPO.
- **Rival gap**: Anthropic's $65B run rate now tops OpenAI's net figure.
- **Skeptic view**: New Constructs values Anthropic at $150B, not $2T.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Anchoring]]**: The market had anchored on $68B, so a still-large $50B figure read as a disappointment and hit stocks across the AI supply chain.
