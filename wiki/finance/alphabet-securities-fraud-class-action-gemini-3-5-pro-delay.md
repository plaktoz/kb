## Summary

A securities fraud class action (Stewart v. Alphabet Inc., et al.) has been filed against [[Alphabet]] and certain senior executives, alleging they misrepresented the development progress of the [[Gemini 3.5 Pro]] AI model. The suit follows a 4.4% stock drop on July 16, 2026, after [[Bloomberg]] reported Google was months behind schedule on the flagship model despite a May announcement of a June launch. The press release, issued by plaintiff firm [[Bleichmar Fonti & Auld LLP]], solicits investors ahead of a December 1, 2026 lead plaintiff deadline.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Securities Class Action]]**: Claims brought under Sections 10(b) and 20(a) of the [[Securities Exchange Act of 1934]] on behalf of Alphabet securities holders.
- **Alleged misrepresentation**: On May 19, 2026, Alphabet announced Gemini 3.5 Pro would launch in June 2026; the complaint alleges defendants misrepresented its development progress.
- **Corrective disclosure**: Bloomberg's July 16 report said Google was "months behind schedule" after a late-June training-data update produced disappointing results.
- **Venue**: Pending in the [[U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California]], No. 26-cv-11290.
- **Plaintiff-firm solicitation**: BFA works on contingency and cites prior recoveries from [[Tesla]]'s board ($900M+) and [[Teva Pharmaceutical]] ($420M).
- Related: [[Google DeepMind]], [[AI Model Release Timelines]], [[Litigation Risk]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Lead Plaintiff Deadline**: Investors must apply by December 1, 2026.
- **Stock Drop**: Class A shares fell $16.46 (4.4%), $370.92 to $354.46.
- **Trigger Date**: Drop occurred July 16, 2026, during market hours on Bloomberg report.
- **Allegation**: Gemini 3.5 Pro progress misrepresented after promising a June 2026 launch.
- **Root Cause Reported**: Updated Gemini training data in late June gave disappointing results.
- **Legal Basis**: Claims under Exchange Act Sections 10(b) and 20(a).
- **Source Caveat**: Attorney advertising from a plaintiff firm, not a court finding.
