# CoreWeave vs AWS: Cloud Market Share & Investment Thesis

## Summary
[[CoreWeave]] is growing at 112% YoY versus [[AWS]]'s 37%, but AWS is 20× larger by revenue, making it statistically unlikely CoreWeave will overtake it in under seven years even at current growth differentials. CoreWeave carries significant execution risk — it is unprofitable, reliant on debt and equity issuance — while AWS operates at a 39% operating margin. The article concludes CoreWeave is a viable long-shot investment with asymmetric upside, while Amazon remains a more reliable compounder.

## Key Takeaways
- **AWS scale**: AWS Q2 revenue was $42.2B, 20× CoreWeave's $2.1B.
- **CoreWeave growth rate**: 112% YoY vs. AWS 37% YoY in Q2.
- **Backlog gap**: CoreWeave ~$100B backlog vs. Amazon's $496B, but proportionally large.
- **Profitability gap**: AWS runs 39% operating margin; CoreWeave is currently unprofitable.
- **Time to parity**: At sustained rates, CoreWeave needs ~7 years to match AWS revenue.
- **Bull case**: 104% growth forecast for 2027; 39% margin at scale = ~$10.2B operating income.
- **Valuation upside**: At 30× hypothetical operating income, CoreWeave could be a ~$300B company vs. $48B today (6×).
- **Key risk**: CoreWeave must fund growth via debt/shares until it turns profitable — execution-dependent.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Scale Creates Compounding Entrenchment** — A dominant incumbent's absolute revenue lead grows even when a challenger outgrows it in percentage terms, making displacement increasingly difficult over time. · *model:* [[Compounding]]
→ explains: AWS's head start, 7-year overtake scenario

**T2. Economic Moat Through Operating Margin** — Once a cloud provider achieves mature operating margins (AWS at 39%), its profitability funds further investment, widening the moat against unprofitable challengers. · *model:* [[Economic Moat]]
→ explains: CoreWeave's profitability deficit vs. AWS's self-funding advantage

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core thesis on whether CoreWeave can unseat AWS as the top cloud provider?
**A1**: Unlikely in the foreseeable future — at current growth rates (112% vs. 37%), it would take CoreWeave roughly seven years to match AWS's quarterly revenue, and neither growth rate will likely be sustained.

**Q2**: What financial metrics illustrate CoreWeave's risk-reward trade-off versus AWS?
**A2**: CoreWeave grows faster (112% vs. 37% YoY) but is unprofitable while AWS runs a 39% operating margin; CoreWeave's $100B backlog signals demand, but reliance on debt/equity to fund growth is a key risk.

**Q3**: How would an investor size the CoreWeave bull case?
**A3**: If CoreWeave achieves AWS-level margins (39%) on projected 2027 revenue (~$10.2B operating income) and trades at 30× that, it becomes a ~$300B company — roughly 6× from its current $48B market cap.
