## Summary

Meta Platforms is set to report Q2 2026 earnings on July 29, with market focus squarely on capital expenditures and CEO Mark Zuckerberg's AI spending strategy. Analysts expect revenue to jump 26.7% to $60.22 billion, but the central debate is whether AI capex intensity will outpace Meta's ability to diversify monetization beyond advertising. Meta stock has lagged in 2026, down 10% year to date, as investor anxiety over AI spending weighs on the stock.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Meta Platforms]] Q2 2026 Earnings**: Revenue expected at $60.22B (+26.7% YoY); EPS growth less than 1% at $7.19 per share.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: Meta's 2026 capex guide is $125B–$145B; market expects a raise following [[Google]] (Alphabet) hiking its 2026 capex to $195B–$205B.
- **[[Cloud Hyperscalers]]**: Meta, [[Microsoft]], and [[Amazon]] face rising expectations to increase AI spending forecasts in line with [[Alphabet]].
- **[[Meta AI]] and Monetization Pipeline**: Meta AI assistant, Muse Spark, Business AI, subscription tiers, potential Meta cloud offering, and custom silicon are all early-stage initiatives.
- **[[Meta Family of Apps]]**: Daily active people metric declined QoQ in Q1 2026 (3.58B → 3.56B) due to outages from conflicts in Iran and Ukraine.
- **[[BlackRock]] Joint Venture**: Meta announced a $14B JV with BlackRock to build a Texas data center.

## Key Takeaways

- **Capex Watch**: Evercore ISI expects Meta to raise 2026 capex guidance above $125B–$145B.
- **Revenue Beat Expected**: Analysts project 26.7% revenue growth to $60.22B for Q2.
- **Advertising Resilience**: Wedbush sees another strong ad business quarter despite AI spend concerns.
- **Monetization Gap**: Gap between capex intensity and diversified revenue is the key stock debate.
- **Cloud Capacity Sales**: Market watching for confirmation Meta will sell excess cloud capacity externally.
- **DAP Decline**: Daily active people fell QoQ in Q1 for the first time; recovery a watch item.
- **Stock Underperformance**: META down 10% YTD; lost 13% since July 15 on AI spending fears.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Opportunity Cost]]**: Every dollar Meta allocates to AI capex is a dollar not returned to shareholders or invested in nearer-term monetization — the market is essentially pricing in the risk that returns on this spend remain speculative.
- **[[Innovator's Dilemma]]**: Meta must invest aggressively in AI infrastructure to avoid being left behind, even though this spending pressures near-term earnings and creates uncertainty — classic tension between defending the present business and building the future one.
