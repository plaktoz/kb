## Summary

Jim Cramer previewed the week of October 12, 2026, when major bank earnings (Goldman Sachs, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup, Bank of America, Morgan Stanley) and semiconductor reports (ASML, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing) will test both corporate performance and the strength of the AI trade. He laid out specific stock views across banks, J&J, and chip names, while warning that rising bond yields remain the market's biggest overhang.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Jim Cramer]]**: CNBC's "Mad Money" host, who frames Q3 2026 earnings season as the moment markets stop guessing and start pricing results.
- **Bank earnings**: [[Goldman Sachs]] and [[Wells Fargo]] (Cramer bullish, Charitable Trust holdings), [[JPMorgan Chase]] (cautious, priced for near-perfect execution), [[Citigroup]] (watching for a rebound), [[Bank of America]] and [[Morgan Stanley]] (Wednesday reporters, Cramer likes MS's wealth management growth).
- **[[Johnson & Johnson]]**: Club holding reporting Tuesday; Cramer frames post-earnings sell-offs as buying opportunities given its drug pipeline and cardio/oncology franchises.
- **Semiconductor reports**: [[ASML]] guidance seen as a signal to buy [[Lam Research]] or [[Applied Materials]]; [[Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing]] (TSMC) seen as the week's biggest potential catalyst for chip stocks.
- **[[Charles Schwab]]**: Reports and holds an analyst meeting Thursday, offering a read on growing individual-investor influence.
- **Inflation data**: [[Consumer Price Index]] (Wednesday) and [[Producer Price Index]]/retail sales (Thursday) as key macro checkpoints during earnings week.
- **Bond market risk**: Cramer flags a "plus 6% long bond" driven by Treasury and private-sector (data center) demand for money as the market's chief near-term risk.

## Key Takeaways

- **Tuesday**: Goldman Sachs, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan, Citigroup, and J&J report.
- **Cramer's bank calls**: Bullish Goldman and Wells Fargo, cautious JPMorgan, watching Citigroup for a rebound.
- **J&J strategy**: Buy weakness around earnings calls given its drug pipeline strength.
- **Wednesday**: CPI release, ASML earnings, plus Bank of America, Morgan Stanley, and BlackRock.
- **ASML signal**: Strong guidance should prompt buying Lam Research or Applied Materials.
- **Thursday**: TSMC earnings seen as the biggest potential catalyst for chip stocks; PPI, retail sales, and Charles Schwab's analyst day also land.
- **Top risk**: Rising long-bond yields (toward 6%+) from Treasury and data-center-driven demand for money.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Cramer's core framing for the week of October 12, 2026?
**A**: Earnings season removes the guesswork of trading on scattered data points, giving investors a clearer read on corporate performance and the AI trade through bank and chip earnings.

**Q2**: What specific signal is Cramer watching from ASML, and what would he do with it?
**A**: If ASML raises guidance and reports solid demand, Cramer says that's a signal to buy semiconductor capital equipment stocks, specifically Lam Research or Applied Materials.

**Q3**: What does Cramer identify as the market's biggest risk despite the busy earnings calendar?
**A**: Rising bond yields, pushed toward 6%+ on the long bond by Treasury borrowing and private data-center investment demand for money, which he says is "coloring everything" in the market.
