## Summary

Despite a 20%+ decline in 2026, Microsoft's AI annual revenue run rate hit $37B (+123% YoY) with cloud revenue growing 40%, making its selloff look like a market mispricing rather than fundamental deterioration. In contrast, Meta's ~10% decline reflects genuine monetization uncertainty around its AI spending, with no clear revenue path for Llama. The author describes Microsoft as having "little execution risk" and calls it "about as no-brainer a buy in the stock market as it gets."
