## Summary
Global sovereign bond yields sat near multi-week lows on November 23 as a pandemic-scale tech and AI selloff drove investors into government debt. The U.S. 10-year yield fell to 4.96%, below 5% for the first time in a while, after the Nasdaq's 10% weekly plunge, its worst since March 2020. The article ties the rout to doubts that borrowed AI data-center spending will pay off, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt exit, and Anthropic's faltering public debut.

## Core Concepts
- [[Safe Haven Assets]]: government bonds as "portfolio ballast" during an equity rout.
- [[Bond Yields]]: move inversely to prices, so falling yields signal surging demand.
- [[US 10-Year Treasury Yield]]: 4.96%, near two-week lows, after its biggest single-session rally in over a month.
- [[German Bund Yield]]: 10-year at 3.3167%; 2-year at 3.096%.
- [[Australian Government Bonds]]: 10-year at 4.914%, lowest since August.
- [[AI Trade Unwind]]: doubts that mostly borrowed [[Data Center]] capex will earn profits fast enough to repay lenders.
- [[Jensen Huang]] and [[Nvidia]]: his exit questioned the chipmaker's strategy of financing some of its own customers.
- [[Anthropic IPO]]: its faltering debut confirmed the abandonment of tech euphoria.

## Key Takeaways
- **Yield Drop**: U.S. 10-year at 4.96%, under the 5% threshold, near two-week lows.
- **Bund Rally**: German 10-year had its largest rally since May 2026 on Friday.
- **Nasdaq Plunge**: Last week's 10% drop was its largest weekly decline since March 2020.
- **Asia Contagion**: Australian 10-year hit its lowest since August; regulators reportedly prepare stock-market support.
- **AI Financing Doubt**: Traders question whether borrowed data-center spending can repay lenders.
- **Catalysts**: Huang's exit and Anthropic's weak debut fed the unwinding.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Capital flees risk assets into safe debt during rout** — When risky assets sell off sharply, investors seek refuge in government bonds, which raises their prices and lowers yields. · *model:* [[Safe Haven Assets]]
→ explains: Yield Drop, Bund Rally, Asia Contagion
