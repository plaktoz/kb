## Summary

[[Microsoft]] shares rose 37.7% from July to September 2026, the stock's best quarter since 1998. The rally reversed a 22.5% first-half drop, its worst half-year since 2022. The turnaround came from [[Azure]] growth speeding up to 43%, a 45% growth forecast, and OpenAI's new Dots product integrating with [[Microsoft Agent 365]], which renewed confidence that Microsoft's AI spending is turning into revenue. Analysts are still overwhelmingly bullish, and the stock trades at about 26 times forward earnings, mid-pack among the [[Magnificent Seven]].

## Core Concepts

- **Quarterly rally**: The stock rose 37.7% in Q3 2026, the best quarter since 1998 and the top performance in the Magnificent Seven.
- **Mag 7 Q3 scorecard**: [[Meta Platforms]] +28.7%, [[Apple]] +15.1%, [[Nvidia]] +14.1%, [[Amazon]] +4.5%, [[Alphabet]] −3.7%, [[Tesla]] −15.6%.
- **Fiscal Q4 results (July)**: Azure grew 43% YoY. Revenue rose 18% to $90B, adjusted EPS was $4.74, and the stock jumped about 15% the next day.
- **Guidance**: Microsoft forecast 45% constant-currency Azure growth for fiscal Q1 2027.
- **AI monetization metrics**: [[Microsoft 365 Copilot]] has passed 30M paid seats, and [[Microsoft Agent 365]] has nearly 40M registered agents.
- **[[OpenAI]] Dots**: Launched at OpenAI's Developer Day. Piper Sandler's [[Billy Fitzsimmons]] called its Agent 365 integration one of the few positive read-throughs for Microsoft.
- **[[Flight to Quality]]**: StoneX's [[Yi Fu Lee]] says investors are favoring tech firms with clearer paths to [[AI Monetization]].
- **Valuation**: About 26x forward earnings. That is above Nvidia (19.1x), Meta (22.6x) and Alphabet (24.5x), but below Amazon (27.3x) and Apple (35.9x).
- **Analyst consensus**: 53 of 55 analysts rate it Buy or higher. The average target of $578.90 implies 13% upside. [[Piper Sandler]] raised its target to $610.

## Key Takeaways

- **Best quarter since 1998**: MSFT gained 37.7% from July to September.
- **Sharp reversal**: Follows a 22.5% first-half decline, the worst since 2022.
- **Azure acceleration**: 43% growth last quarter; 45% guided for fiscal Q1 2027.
- **Copilot traction**: 30M+ paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats reported.
- **Agent platform**: Nearly 40M agents registered on Agent 365.
- **Dots tailwind**: OpenAI's Dots integrates with Agent 365; Piper target raised to $610.
- **Mag 7 leader**: Beat Meta's 28.7% Q3 gain; Tesla fell 15.6%.
- **Mid-pack valuation**: About 26x forward P/E vs. Nvidia 19.1x and Apple 35.9x.
- **Retail interest**: Stocktwits message volume up 55%; sentiment neutral.

**Q2**: What metrics did Microsoft cite as evidence that its AI investments are paying off?
**A**: Microsoft 365 Copilot passed 30 million paid seats, and Agent 365 had nearly 40 million registered agents across tens of thousands of companies. Fiscal Q4 revenue also rose 18% to $90 billion.

**Q3**: How does Microsoft's valuation compare with its Magnificent Seven peers after the rally?
**A**: At about 26 times forward earnings, it sits mid-pack: pricier than Nvidia, Meta and Alphabet, but cheaper than Amazon and Apple. The average analyst target still implies about 13% upside.
