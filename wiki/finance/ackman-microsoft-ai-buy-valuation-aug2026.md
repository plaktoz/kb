# Ackman Buys Microsoft: Is It Still the Best AI Buy?

## Summary

[[Bill Ackman]]'s [[Pershing Square]] increased its [[Microsoft]] stake by nearly 10% in Q2 2026, making MSFT roughly 12% of the fund's portfolio, though the filing reflects positions as of June 30 before a sharp rally. Microsoft's late-July earnings showed 18% revenue and operating income growth with [[Azure]] up 43%, boosted by the market's positive reaction to flat data center spending guidance. The author views MSFT at ~25x forward earnings as fairly valued — below its historical ~30x but no longer a bargain — suggesting other AI stocks may offer better entry points.

## Core Concepts

- [[Bill Ackman]] and [[Pershing Square]] as a signal for retail investors via [[13F Filings]].
- [[Microsoft]] and its [[Azure]] cloud platform as an [[Artificial Intelligence]] infrastructure bet.
- [[Forward P/E Ratio]] as a relative valuation tool for comparing growth stocks to historical norms.
- [[Data Center Capital Expenditure]] guidance as a market sentiment lever.

## Key Takeaways

- **2026 Underperformance**: MSFT largely flat while [[S&P 500]] climbed over 10%.
- **Dip Buyers Won**: Investors buying the late-June dip saw roughly 30% gains.
- **Pershing Square Position**: Ackman raised MSFT stake ~10%; it's now ~12% of the fund.
- **Lag Warning**: 13F data reflects June 30 positioning — pre-rally.
- **Earnings Beat**: 18% revenue and operating income growth; Azure up 43%.
- **Capex Restraint**: MSFT held data center spending guidance flat; peers raised theirs.
- **Valuation Shift**: Pre-earnings ~20x forward P/E; post-earnings ~25x forward P/E.
- **Historical Context**: Historical average is ~30x; current pricing is below that but not cheap.
- **Author's Take**: MSFT is a solid hold; other AI names may offer better upside at today's prices.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Relative Valuation]]**: Comparing MSFT's 25x forward P/E to its own ~30x historical average reveals it has re-rated upward after earnings but still sits below typical premium — a framework for assessing "fair" vs. "cheap" vs. "expensive" without needing a peer comparison.
- **[[Signal vs. Noise]]**: Ackman's 13F buy is already a 45-day-old data point by publication; the stock had already rallied ~30% by then, meaning the "signal" of a billionaire's conviction has been partly arbitraged away before most readers can act on it.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the article's core claim about Microsoft as an AI investment today?
**A**: Microsoft is fairly valued at ~25x forward earnings — a solid hold but no longer the clear bargain it was pre-earnings, and other AI stocks may offer better entry points right now.

**Q2**: What specific earnings data drove Microsoft's stock re-rating from ~20x to ~25x forward P/E?
**A**: Microsoft reported 18% revenue and operating income growth with Azure cloud up 43%, and did not raise its data center spending guidance — a move the market interpreted as disciplined capital allocation.

**Q3**: Why should investors be cautious about using Ackman's 13F filing as a buy signal?
**A**: The filing reflects positions as of June 30, before the stock's sharp ~30% rise; by the time the filing is public, the price advantage Ackman captured may already be gone.
