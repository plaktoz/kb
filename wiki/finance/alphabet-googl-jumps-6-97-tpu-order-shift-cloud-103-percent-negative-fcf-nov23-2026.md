## Summary
Alphabet shares rose 6.97% to $283.78 on November 23 after a report that Google trimmed 2027 orders for its Broadcom-linked TPU 8i in favor of more MediaTek-designed TPU 8t units. Google Cloud revenue grew 103% to $30.7 billion in Q3 2026 at a 36% operating margin, but capex of $51.4 billion pushed quarterly free cash flow to about negative $9.1 billion. At roughly 2.5% above GuruFocus's fair-value estimate, the rally has used up the model's discount.

## Core Concepts
- [[Alphabet]] (GOOGL): rallied 6.97%, outpacing chip partner [[Broadcom]] by more than 4 percentage points.
- [[Ming-Chi Kuo]] report: via TF International and TradingKey, Google trimmed 2027 TPU 8i orders and plans to ship more TPU 8t.
- [[Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)]]: Broadcom-linked TPU 8i versus [[MediaTek]]-designed TPU 8t; TPU system sales contributed to Cloud revenue.
- [[Google Cloud]]: Q3 revenue of $30.7 billion (+103%), operating income of $11.1 billion versus $3.6 billion, 36% margin.
- [[AI Capex]] and [[Free Cash Flow]]: $51.4 billion of property and equipment purchases exceeded $42.3 billion of operating cash flow.
- [[GuruFocus GF Value]]: GF Score 98/100, GF Value $276.83, "Fairly Valued" label.

## Key Takeaways
- **Supplier Shift**: Trimmed Broadcom-linked TPU 8i orders suggest more [[Bargaining Power]] for Google; details not final.
- **Cloud Growth**: Google Cloud revenue rose 103% to $30.7 billion in Q3 2026.
- **Margin Expansion**: Cloud operating income jumped to $11.1 billion from $3.6 billion.
- **Capex Burden**: Capex hit $51.4 billion, more than double the prior year.
- **Negative FCF**: Quarterly free cash flow was about negative $9.1 billion.
- **Valuation**: Price sits 2.5% above GF Value of $276.83, so the discount is gone.
- **Next Test**: Cloud growth must stay above capex growth in Q4.
