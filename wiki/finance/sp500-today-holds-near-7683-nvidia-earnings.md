## Summary

The S&P 500 held near 7,683 on August 26, 2026, trading above both its 50- and 200-period moving averages after a choppy 48 hours, with the RSI at 58.35 reflecting a market that had recovered from overbought and oversold extremes. Attention was fixed on [[Nvidia]]'s after-close earnings report and the upcoming Core PCE and GDP data ahead of the Jackson Hole symposium.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Moving Average Support]]**: The index traded above its 50-period (7,673.98) and 200-period (7,678.17) moving averages, a technically constructive position.
- **[[RSI Recovery]]**: The 14-period RSI swung from near 80 (overbought) during Tuesday's rally to near 30 (oversold) in the pullback, settling at a neutral 58.35.
- **[[Nvidia Earnings Catalyst]]**: Nvidia's fiscal Q2 report, due after the US close, carried consensus estimates of ~$92B revenue (above its own $91B guidance) and ~$2.09 EPS.
- **[[Jackson Hole Symposium]]**: Fed Chair [[Kevin Warsh]]'s first keynote as Chair, scheduled for August 28, adds macro risk alongside the GDP and Core PCE releases on August 26.
