## Summary

US stocks fell sharply on July 29, 2026, with the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] dropping over 1,100 points (2.2%) as the [[Federal Reserve]] held interest rates steady while three FOMC members dissented in favor of a hike. A renewed US-Iran military exchange pushed oil above $90/barrel, and a sell-off in chip stocks deepened after SK Hynix's Q2 profits disappointed Wall Street. Markets braced for make-or-break [[Magnificent Seven]] earnings from Microsoft and Meta after the bell.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Federal Reserve]] Hawkish Hold**: The Fed voted to keep rates unchanged, but three [[FOMC]] members dissented and voted for a hike — a signal that inflation remains a live concern above the 2% target.
- **[[Chip Stock]] Sell-off**: [[SK Hynix]] beat Q2 profit records but missed Wall Street estimates, reigniting fears that the [[AI boom]] may be decelerating and triggering broad semiconductor selling.
- **[[Magnificent Seven]] Earnings Risk**: [[Microsoft]] (MSFT) and [[Meta]] (META) reported after the bell as a key catalyst; [[Alphabet]]'s prior capex guidance had already spooked markets on AI sustainability.
- **[[Middle East]] Geopolitics**: Iran launched an attack on US forces, resuming hostilities after a brief pause, causing [[Brent Crude]] to jump over 7% past $90/barrel.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: Long-dated yields rose following the Fed decision, compressing equity valuations and contributing to broad market weakness.

## Key Takeaways

- **Dow fell 1,100+ pts (2.2%)**: Largest single-day drop driven by Fed and geopolitics.
- **S&P 500 -1.5%**, closing at 7,316.15; Nasdaq -1.7%.
- **3 FOMC dissents**: Voted for rate hike — unusually hawkish signal within a hold decision.
- **SK Hynix miss**: Q2 profits surged but fell short of estimates, triggering chip sector sell-off.
- **Brent crude +7%**: Surpassed $90/barrel on renewed US-Iran fighting.
- **Upcoming catalysts**: MSFT, META, SBUX, CMG, QCOM, ARM all reporting after close.
- **Inflation above 2% target**: Remains primary constraint on Fed easing pivot.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Market fear about AI capex sustainability (seeded by Alphabet) amplified the SK Hynix miss into a broad chip rout — sentiment itself became a cause of the price action, not just a reaction to fundamentals.
- **[[Risk-Off Cascade]]**: Rising yields, geopolitical shock, and earnings uncertainty arrived simultaneously, compressing the multiple across equities in a self-reinforcing flight to safety.
