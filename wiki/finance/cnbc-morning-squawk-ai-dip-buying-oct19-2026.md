## Summary

CNBC's Oct. 19 Morning Squawk reports futures popping after an AI-driven "nightmare week" in which the [[Nasdaq Composite]] fell 4.5%. Investors split between dip-buyers such as Dan Ives and cautious voices such as BTIG, while the [[VIX]] hit its highest level since June. The newsletter also covers neocloud rebounds, Russia sanctions relief, French unrest, and legally contentious AI wearables.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Trade]]**: Cracks emerged last week; futures indicate a partial rebound at the open.
- **[[Dan Ives]]**: Calls the "white-knuckle drawdown" a golden buying opportunity.
- **[[BTIG]]**: Advises using any semiconductor rebound to reduce exposure ("where there is smoke, there is fire").
- **[[VIX]]**: Cboe Volatility Index at its highest since June, signalling fear of a larger correction.
- **[[Neocloud]]**: [[CoreWeave]] and [[Nebius]] each lost about 25% last week and were set to open about 3% higher.
- **[[CXMT]]**: China's memory chipmaker fell despite a swing to profit, as its supply spooked memory chip markets.
- **[[Earnings Season]]**: 85% of reporting S&P 500 companies have beaten expectations.
- **[[French Bond Yields]]**: Rising amid unrest in Paris, which economists say could boost U.S. Treasurys.

## Key Takeaways
