## Summary

US stocks rebounded on August 21, 2026 (Dow +0.98%, Nasdaq +0.43%, S&P 500 +0.43%) after the August Composite PMI hit a four-year high, easing economic-slowdown fears despite rising Treasury yields, with financial and gold stocks leading and Tesla surging on Cybercab robotaxi news.

## Core Concepts

- **[[PMI as Slowdown Signal]]**: A four-year-high Composite PMI directly countered market anxiety about a cooling economy, allowing stocks to rally even as Treasury yields climbed — a reminder that strong activity data can outweigh rate-related headwinds in the short term.
- **[[Off-Balance-Sheet AI Debt Financing]]**: Broadcom's reported $70–80 billion debt-financing talks to support AI companies (including Anthropic) in procuring chips illustrates how chip/infrastructure spending is increasingly financed through structured debt rather than direct equity or cash.
- **[[Fiscal Dominance Hedge]]**: Ray Dalio's recommendation to cut bond holdings and allocate 10–15% of a portfolio to gold, framed as protection against a possible US debt crisis within three years — connects directly to the same day's gold rally driven by Treasury buyback news.

## Key Takeaways

- Tesla (TSLA) +5.14% after reports it will open public Cybercab (steering-wheel-less, pedal-less) test rides by month-end and connect it to Austin's Robotaxi network.
- Apple is cutting 200+ jobs across Siri and Vision Pro teams to redirect resources toward AI and new devices, including largely shutting down a Vision Pro gaming team.
- Nvidia is in advanced talks to invest hundreds of millions in Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a data-center power developer with 10+ gigawatts of pipeline capacity.
- Trump publicly backed AI data center construction as a jobs/tax-revenue driver, while acknowledging public opposition means the industry "needs a little PR help."
- Bridgewater's Ray Dalio warned of a potential US debt crisis within three years, recommending reduced bond exposure and 10–15% gold allocation.
