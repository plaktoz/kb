## Summary

[[Microsoft]] shares fell 2.8% to about $507 despite a fiscal first-quarter beat, with revenue up 18.8% to $92.28 billion and adjusted EPS of $4.89. MarketBeat weighs strong [[Azure]] and Copilot growth and a "Moderate Buy" analyst consensus against falling free cash flow, a planned $25 billion bond offering and insider selling.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]] (MSFT)**: Market cap of $3.77 trillion, PE of 26.47, PEG of 1.61 and beta of 1.05.
- **[[Azure]]**: Revenue grew 48%, ahead of management's 44%-45% forecast.
- **[[Microsoft Copilot]]**: Usage-based customers up more than 75% last quarter; daily users up fivefold year over year.
- **[[Free Cash Flow]]**: Fell to $18.6 billion as capital expenditures climbed.
- **[[Bond Offering]]**: Roughly $25 billion, intended partly to fund AI infrastructure capacity.
- **[[Analyst Consensus]]**: "Moderate Buy" with an average price target of $609.54 across 50 ratings.
- **[[Insider Selling]]**: Named as a continuing risk alongside heavy AI spending.

## Key Takeaways

- **Price Reaction**: Shares fell 2.8% to about $507 despite the earnings beat.
- **Earnings Beat**: Revenue of $92.28B (+18.8%) and adjusted EPS of $4.89 topped estimates.
- **Azure Acceleration**: 48% growth beat the 44%-45% management forecast.
- **Committed Demand**: Reports cite roughly $677 billion in signed contracts or committed demand.
- **Cash Flow Squeeze**: Free cash flow dropped to $18.6B as capex rose.
- **Debt Funding**: A $25B bond offering would help fund additional AI capacity.
- **Ratings Mix**: One Strong Buy, forty-five Buy and four Hold ratings.
- **Moving Averages**: Stock trades below its $512.91 fifty-day average, above its $438.43 200-day.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Microsoft shares fall despite a better-than-expected quarter?
**A**: Rising AI infrastructure spending cut free cash flow to $18.6 billion, and the planned $25 billion bond offering and insider selling added concern. Investors are watching whether debt and capex earn adequate returns.

**Q2**: What evidence supports optimism about Microsoft's AI growth?
**A**: Azure revenue grew 48% against a 44%-45% forecast, and usage-based Copilot customers rose more than 75% with daily users up fivefold. Reports also point to roughly $677 billion in signed contracts or committed demand.

**Q3**: How should an investor read the analyst data?
**A**: The consensus is "Moderate Buy" with a $609.54 average target, well above the roughly $507 price. The mix of one Strong Buy, forty-five Buy and four Hold ratings shows broad but not unanimous support.
