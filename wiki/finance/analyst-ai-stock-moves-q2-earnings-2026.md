## Summary

Ahead of Q2 2026 earnings, [[Morgan Stanley]] named [[Microsoft]] "highly attractive" with a $600 price target on Azure and [[Copilot]] momentum, [[Wedbush]] added [[Alphabet]] and [[Reddit]] to its Best Ideas list on cloud share gains and ad momentum, and [[Mizuho]] named [[Meta Platforms]] its top pick partly on a reported compute-leasing deal with [[Anthropic]]. [[Citi]] called the semiconductor pullback a buying opportunity favoring equipment makers over chipmakers, while [[Jefferies]] downgraded [[Datadog]] to Hold after a 94% year-to-date rally left little valuation cushion.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Azure Growth]]** — Morgan Stanley models 41% constant-currency growth; Microsoft's July 1 M365 price hike could add $2B–$6B in revenue through fiscal 2029
- **[[Google Cloud]]** — grew 82% year-over-year to $24.8B with expanding margins, though Alphabet's raised capex guidance pushed free cash flow negative
- **[[Meta-Anthropic Compute Deal]]** — reported $10B, two-year compute-leasing agreement seen as "first sign of capex return on investment"
- **[[Semiconductor Capital Equipment]]** — Citi's preferred sub-sector over chipmakers, citing Intel and TSMC capex raises
- **[[Datadog]]** — downgraded on valuation (18x EV/CY27 revenue) despite maintained AI-beneficiary thesis

## Key Takeaways

- **Microsoft bull case**: $600 Morgan Stanley price target implies ~57% upside on Azure and Copilot momentum.
- **Google Cloud backlog hit $514B**: with margin expansion to 35.6% from 20.7% a year earlier.
- **Meta-Anthropic deal could add $1-1.25/share**: in tax-affected EPS annually, per Mizuho.
- **TSMC raised 2026 capex guidance to $60-64B**: plus an added $100B Arizona investment commitment.
- **Datadog trades at a 4-turn premium to Snowflake**: leaving little room for execution slippage, Jefferies says.
