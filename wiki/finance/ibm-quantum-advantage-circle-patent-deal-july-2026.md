## Summary

[[IBM]] shares fell 2.1% on July 30 to $221.77 despite positive quantum-computing news — multi-partner quantum-advantage results with [[Algorithmiq]], [[Qedma]], RIKEN, and the University of Chicago — as investors instead focused on Q2 revenue that missed consensus ($17.16B vs $17.46B expected) and new securities-law investigations tied to its earlier guidance warning. [[Circle]] separately acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio (~1,000 patents) for undisclosed terms.

## Core Concepts

- [[IBM]] — down 2.1% on the day to $221.77; market cap $208.93B, consensus "Moderate Buy" with $265.40 average price target
- [[Quantum Advantage]] — IBM and academic/industry partners reported computations beyond practical classical-computing capability, though commercialization remains unproven
- [[Circle]] — acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio (~1,000 issued patents, 680+ families) for digital-asset infrastructure
- [[IBM 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Report]] — found AI-enabled attacks are rising and more costly than average breaches
- [[Securities Fraud Investigation]] — Pomerantz and Bleichmar Fonti & Auld opened investigations tied to IBM's earlier guidance and an alleged IBM Z slowdown

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock down 2.1%** to $221.77 despite positive quantum news, showing growth concerns are dominating sentiment
- **Q2 revenue miss**: $17.16B vs $17.46B consensus, only 1.1% YoY growth
- **Mixed analyst actions**: Piper Sandler and DZ Bank upgraded; Oppenheimer, Wolfe Research, and Weiss Ratings downgraded within the same six-week window
- **Quantum-advantage demonstrations** strengthen IBM's long-term positioning but carry no near-term revenue impact
- **Dividend intact**: $1.69/share quarterly ($6.76 annualized, 3.0% yield), paid September 10

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Narrative vs Numbers]]**: A genuinely positive technical milestone (quantum advantage) was overwhelmed by a revenue miss and litigation headlines — a reminder that markets weight near-term financial numbers over long-horizon technology narratives when the two point in different directions.
