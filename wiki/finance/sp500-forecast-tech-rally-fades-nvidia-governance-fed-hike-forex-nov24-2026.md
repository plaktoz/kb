## Summary

Monday's relief rally on Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO faded, with the S&P 500 down about 1% after failing at former support of 7,224 to 7,298. Nvidia fell around 5% on governance doubts and Anthropic slipped below its $83 IPO price. Fed rate-hike signals and chip export tensions keep the burden of proof on bulls, though year-end seasonality offers some hope.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. A bottom requires confirmation** — A rebound from a low is not a confirmed bottom until price reclaims the level where it previously broke down, so the burden of proof stays on the bulls until then.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why does the article say the burden of proof is on the bulls?
**A**: The S&P 500 failed at its old support zone of 7,224 to 7,298, and the Nvidia-led relief rally lost momentum. Until the index reclaims that zone, a bottom is unconfirmed.

**Q2**: What levels would confirm a market bottom?
**A**: A closing break above the 200-day moving average and, ideally, a move above 7,421, the high of last Thursday's thrust candle. Short-term support sits near 7,150.

**Q3**: What macro factors could shape the weeks ahead?
**A**: The Fed has strongly signalled a December rate hike, and a firm October core PCE could reinforce it. China's stance on chip export curbs weighs on AI trade, while year-end seasonality offers a possible Santa rally.
