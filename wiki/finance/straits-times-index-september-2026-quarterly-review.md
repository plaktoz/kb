## Summary
The Straits Times Index (STI) September 2026 quarterly review resulted in no constituent or reserve list changes, effective September 21, 2026 — signaling stability among Singapore's current market leaders and providing continuity for investors and product issuers benchmarking against STI-linked instruments.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Straits Times Index (STI)]]**: Singapore's main equity benchmark, jointly calculated by FTSE Russell, SPH Media Trust, and SGX Group. The benchmark underpins STI ETFs (including ES3), warrants, futures, and derivatives.
- **[[STI Quarterly Review Process]]**: Governed by FTSE ST index rules to ensure the STI accurately reflects Singapore's investable universe and can be efficiently replicated for index-linked products. Reviews occur quarterly.
- **[[STI Reserve List (Sep 2026)]]**: First Resources, Keppel REIT, Olam Group, Sheng Siong Group, and Suntec REIT — all unchanged from the prior quarter.
- **[[ES3 STI ETF]]**: Singapore ETF tracking the Straits Times Index; unchanged constituents mean no rebalancing-driven trading events in September.

## Key Takeaways
- **No changes**: zero constituent additions or deletions in the September 2026 quarterly review.
- **Effective date**: September 21, 2026.
- **Reserve list**: 5 names unchanged — First Resources, Keppel REIT, Olam Group, Sheng Siong Group, Suntec REIT.
- **Implication for ES3**: no forced rebalancing trades; index composition stable.
- **FTSE Russell scope**: covers 98% of the investable market globally; STI indices widely used by asset owners, managers, and ETF providers.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the outcome of the STI September 2026 quarterly review?
**A**: No changes — zero constituent additions or deletions. All outcomes take effect September 21, 2026, with the reserve list also unchanged (First Resources, Keppel REIT, Olam Group, Sheng Siong Group, Suntec REIT).

**Q2**: Why does a "no changes" STI quarterly review matter for ES3 holders?
**A**: The STI ETF (ES3) tracks the Straits Times Index; no constituent changes means no forced rebalancing trades, providing continuity in portfolio composition and no tracking-error events from index reconstitution.

**Q3**: Who governs the STI quarterly review process?
**A**: FTSE Russell (part of London Stock Exchange Group), jointly with SPH Media Trust and SGX Group — governed by FTSE ST index rules to ensure accurate representation of Singapore's investable universe.
