## Summary
Ahead of its Aug. 26 fiscal Q2 2027 earnings, Wall Street broadly expects Nvidia to beat its own guidance again, but analysts say the bigger swing factor for NVDA stock is the company's $500 billion AI financing plan and its up-to-$105 billion guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio data center, not the headline earnings beat itself.

## Core Concepts

- [[Nvidia Q2 2027 Earnings]] — consensus expects revenue near $92.01B (vs. Nvidia's own $91B ±2% guidance) and EPS of $2.01, both above the company's guidance midpoint
- [[Circular AI Financing]] — Nvidia's $105B guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio data center and its broader $500B financing plan with six asset managers, echoing a pattern where AI infrastructure vendors invest in their own customers
- [[Beat and Raise]] — the pattern Wall Street analysts (Stifel, TD Cowen, UBS) expect Nvidia to repeat: beating the current quarter and raising forward guidance
- [[Off-Balance-Sheet Risk]] — Bank of America's Vivek Arya flags the OpenAI guarantee as a real risk not captured on Nvidia's balance sheet, even while finding the stock's valuation "compelling"

## Key Takeaways

- **Consensus estimates**: ~$92.01B revenue (nearly double YoY), EPS $2.01, GAAP gross margin guided at 74.4%–75.4%.
- **Analyst framing**: Goldman's James Schneider flags four Q2-call watch items — the $500B financing plan, the Rubin platform ramp, gross-margin sustainability amid AI-chip competition, and CPU demand from agentic AI.
- **Valuation**: NVDA trades at a 25.59x forward P/E and a 0.44x PEG ratio, which the author frames as attractive despite financing-related risk.
- **Peer context**: Microsoft was the only major hyperscaler that did *not* raise 2026 AI capex guidance in its own earnings call, a mild caution signal against otherwise bullish capex commentary from Amazon, Alphabet, and others building competitive custom chips.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Circular Financing (Vendor Financing)]]**: When a supplier funds its own customer's ability to buy from it (Nvidia guaranteeing OpenAI's data-center debt), reported revenue and demand signals become harder to separate from financially engineered demand — a red flag pattern worth tracking across the AI capex cycle.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core claim of this article?

**Q2**: What specific financial structure is raising analyst concern?
**A**: Nvidia's up-to-$105 billion guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio data center sits off-balance-sheet and exemplifies "circular financing" — Nvidia effectively backstopping demand from one of its largest customers.

**Q3**: How should an investor weigh the earnings beat against the financing risk?
**A**: Per the article, strong fundamentals (valuation, margins, Rubin ramp) support the bull case, but investors should temper return expectations and watch management's commentary on financing-deal risk rather than treating a headline beat as the whole story.
