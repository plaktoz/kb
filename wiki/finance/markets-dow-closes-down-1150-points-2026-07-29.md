## Summary

Major U.S. stock indexes sold off sharply on July 29, 2026, with the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] dropping more than 1,150 points — its worst single day in 15 months — after the [[Federal Reserve]] voted 9-3 to hold interest rates steady while signaling continued resolve to push inflation to its 2% target. Oil prices surged 7–8% after Iran launched a surprise missile attack on U.S. forces in the Middle East and President Trump threatened retaliation. Retail investors recorded their largest net sell of individual stocks since the COVID crash, concentrated in memory and semiconductor names.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Federal Reserve]] Rate Decision**: Chair [[Kevin Warsh]] held rates steady; hawkish language pushed September hike probability to 54% (down from 79% pre-meeting) per [[CME FedWatch]]
- **[[Iran Attack on U.S. Forces]]**: Iran launched ballistic missiles at U.S. Middle East bases; Trump threatened severe retaliation — geopolitical shock accelerated risk-off selling
- **[[Oil Price Spike]]**: [[WTI Crude]] jumped 7.4% to $85.10/barrel; [[Brent Crude]] surged 8.2% to $91 on geopolitical premium
- **[[Magnificent Seven]] Under Pressure**: All Magnificent Seven stocks closed lower except [[Alphabet]]; [[Microsoft]] and [[Meta Platforms]] declined pre-earnings
- **[[Memory Chip Sell-Off]]**: [[Roundhill Memory ETF]] (DRAM) -6%; [[iShares Semiconductor ETF]] (SOXX) -5%; [[SK Hynix]] -2.5%
- **[[Retail Investor Sentiment]]**: [[Vanda Research]] flagged largest net retail selling of individual stocks since COVID — concentrated in [[Micron Technology]], [[Sandisk]], [[Seagate]], [[Western Digital]]
- **[[10-Year Treasury Yield]]**: Rose to ~4.67%, up 6 bps from prior close, pressuring equity valuations
- **[[Q2 2026 Earnings Season]]**: Mix of results — [[GE HealthCare]] +12%, [[Teradyne]] +8%, [[Ford]] +2%; [[Vertiv]] -17%, [[VF Corp]] -17%, [[Lennox International]] -20%

## Key Takeaways

- **Worst Day in 15 Months**: Dow -2.2%, S&P 500 -1.5%, Nasdaq -1.7%; Nasdaq's 6th straight losing session
- **Fed Holds, Hawkish Tone**: September rate hike repriced to 54% chance; Zentner (Morgan Stanley) calls it a "live meeting"
- **Oil Shock**: Iran attack sent WTI +7.4% and Brent +8.2% in a single session
- **Retail Panic in Memory Stocks**: DRAM, SNDK, MU, STX, WDC drove largest retail sell since COVID crash
- **Memory Still Up YTD**: Sandisk +300%+ YTD despite sell-off; Seagate, WDC, Micron ~tripled in 2026
- **Safe Havens Bid**: Gold +0.8% to $4,070/oz; USD index -0.5%; Bitcoin ~$63,800 (unchanged)
- **Earnings Divergence**: GE HealthCare and Teradyne surged; Vertiv and VF Corp collapsed on guidance misses
- **P&G Revenue Miss**: Sales of $21.20B slightly missed $21.22B estimate; CEO cited "challenging geopolitical environment"
- **Apple Next**: AAPL set to report Thursday; options pricing ~4% swing; market cap briefly crossed $5T

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Narrative Contagion]]**: Three bearish narratives — Fed hawkishness, Iran geopolitical shock, AI bubble fears — converged simultaneously, amplifying sell-off beyond any single catalyst and triggering retail panic selling not seen since COVID.
- **[[Second-Order Effects]]**: The Fed hold was expected, but the hawkish forward guidance shifted the rate-hike probability distribution, which repriced bonds, then equities, then oil-sensitive names in sequence — each leg reinforcing the next.
