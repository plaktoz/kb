## Summary
PBS NewsHour's Friday news wrap reports that Wall Street tumbled to end the week as investors weighed the health of the AI sector after Nvidia's leadership upheaval and Anthropic's record IPO. The Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains and the S&P 500 entered correction territory. The wrap also covers non-market headlines such as court blocks on Trump's arch and the Army detonation, flooding, Poland and Ukraine, and a UK-EU deal.
