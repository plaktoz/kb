## Summary
U.S. stocks slipped Friday Aug 14, 2026 as weak retail sales and consumer sentiment data outweighed a mostly-strong earnings season, though the S&P 500 and Nasdaq still closed out a third straight week of gains. [[Reddit Joins S&P 500 (Aug 18, 2026)]] was the standout individual mover, while chip stocks continued a sharp rally toward a new bull market.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Retail Sales as Rate-Cut Signal]]**: July retail sales fell 0.6% (vs. +0.1% expected); Morgan Stanley's Ellen Zentner noted markets may read weak consumer data positively short-term because it strengthens the case against further rate hikes — a "bad news is good news" dynamic.
- **[[Post-Earnings Sell-the-News]]**: Applied Materials (AMAT) fell 5% despite beating EPS/revenue estimates and guiding Q4 above forecasts — investors had already priced in strength after shares more than doubled year-to-date.
- **[[Semiconductor Sector Rotation]]**: The PHLX Semiconductor Sector rallied to the cusp of a new bull market (up ~20% off recent lows) on moves in SanDisk (+14%), Micron (+4%), and Arm (+3%); Bank of America named AMD its top pick for AI server chip growth alongside Nvidia.

## Key Takeaways
- The 10-year Treasury yield rose above 4.69%, up ~5bps, even as equities weakened — not a simple flight-to-bonds pattern.
- Elon Musk disclosed a 48.4% stake in SpaceX (6.42B shares), valuing his holding above $900B.
- Bitcoin fell to ~$62,500 after the SEC canceled a planned crypto-rulemaking meeting and the Senate recessed without advancing the Clarity Act — regulatory uncertainty weighing on crypto.
- Workday (WDAY) gave back gains from an 18% acquisition-talk rally involving Silver Lake.
- Crude oil (WTI) rose 1.3% to $82.25/barrel on Iran sanctions rhetoric from Treasury Secretary Bessent.

## 🃏 Review Questions
**Q1**: Why might weak retail sales data be interpreted positively by markets in the short term?
**A**: Because unexpectedly soft consumer spending strengthens the case for the Federal Reserve to avoid further rate hikes, so despite signaling economic weakness, it can be read as reducing near-term monetary tightening risk.

**Q2**: Why did Applied Materials stock fall despite beating earnings estimates and guiding higher?
**A**: The stock had already more than doubled year-to-date heading into the print, so a merely-strong (rather than blowout) result triggered profit-taking — a classic "sell the news" reaction after high expectations were priced in.

**Q3**: What two regulatory events contributed to Bitcoin's decline to around $62,500?
**A**: The SEC canceled a planned meeting on crypto-startup fundraising rules, and the U.S. Senate recessed without advancing the Clarity Act, both dashing near-term hopes for clearer crypto regulation.
