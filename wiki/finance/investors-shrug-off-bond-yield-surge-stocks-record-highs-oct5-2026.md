## Summary

On Monday, October 5, 2026, investors brushed off a fresh spike in the [[10-Year Treasury Yield]] and bought tech, which lifted the [[Nasdaq Composite]] to a new all-time high. The 10-year hit 5.35%, its highest since 2002. Stocks still rose on AI optimism and lower oil prices, a sign of the "animal spirits" carrying the market late in 2026. Strategists warn that market leadership is narrowing. They also point to contagion risk from Europe's bond sell-off, where France is close to a debt crisis.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Bond Market Sell-off]]**: The 10-year Treasury yield rose as much as 7 bps to 5.35% before paring slightly. That is its highest level since 2002 and threatens equity valuations.
- **[[Animal Spirits]]**: Risk appetite has kept stocks rising through the final months of 2026, even with yields at multi-decade peaks.
- **Oil relief**: [[Brent Crude]] fell 1% to $100 a barrel after [[G7]] nations released oil stocks and Middle East producers exported more, easing supply-tightness fears.
- **[[AI Trade]] leadership**: [[SpaceX]] rose 6% after [[Morgan Stanley]] called it "cheap". [[Microsoft]] gained over 2% on a [[Melius Research]] buy rating, and [[Hyperscalers]] and chip giants also rose.
- **Earnings growth vs. discount rates**: [[Luke Lango]] argues that capital is flowing to the parts of the market whose earnings can grow fast enough to outrun higher discount rates, and right now that is "overwhelmingly the AI complex."
- **[[Market Concentration]]**: [[Raymond James]] says performance has been "heavily weighted" toward tech and energy, so the [[Equal-Weight S&P 500]] and other equal-weighted indexes are lagging the benchmarks.
- **French debt stress**: The spread between France's and Germany's 10-year yields ([[French Government Bonds]] vs. [[Bund]]) recently passed 150 bps, the widest in about 15 years, as unrest grows.
- **[[Financial Contagion]]**: Economists warn that Europe's bond sell-off could spread and push yields higher in other markets.
- **Catalysts ahead**: The [[FOMC Minutes]] from the September meeting are next. The [[CME FedWatch Tool]] shows a 76% chance the Fed holds rates in October.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Discounted Cash Flow]]**: Higher discount rates shrink the present value of future earnings. Only companies whose earnings grow faster than rates rise can keep their valuations, which explains why money is crowding into AI names.
