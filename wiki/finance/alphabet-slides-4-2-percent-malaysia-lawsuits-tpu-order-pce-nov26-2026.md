## Summary

[[Alphabet]] fell 4.2% in morning trading after Malaysia's communications regulator filed two lawsuits against [[Google]] seeking about $146 million over harmful YouTube content. An unconfirmed report of a roughly 20% cut to 2027 TPU-8i orders, a securities class action and a cancelled publisher deal added pressure, while hot PCE data and tariff threats hit the broader market. The stock touched a new 52-week low of $262.73.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] (GOOGL)**: Down 4.2% intraday, its lowest level in over a year.
- **[[Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission]]**: Filed two lawsuits over online gambling, scams, and fraud on [[YouTube]].
- **[[TPU]] Orders**: Unconfirmed supply-chain report of a roughly 20% cut to 2027 TPU-8i orders.
- **[[Securities Class Action]]**: Alleges Alphabet misled investors on the [[Gemini 3.5 Pro]] launch timing; lead-plaintiff deadline December 1.
- **[[PCE Inflation]] and Tariffs**: Hotter-than-expected data and new Trump administration tariff threats raised concerns about the [[Federal Reserve]] ahead of December.
- **[[AI Infrastructure Credit Risk]]**: Credit default swap spreads on major AI infrastructure names hit record levels.

## Key Takeaways

- **Lawsuits**: Malaysia seeks combined damages of roughly $146 million from Google.
- **Earnings Backdrop**: Q3 search revenue growth slowed to 12%; EPS missed on a large equity investment loss.
- **Chip Report**: Unconfirmed 20% cut to 2027 TPU-8i orders unsettled AI capital allocation views.
- **Broad Selloff**: Nasdaq fell over 3%, S&P 500 over 2%, Dow over 1%.
- **Sector Weakness**: Software and IT Services underperformed the market.
- **New Low**: Intraday 52-week low of $262.73.
