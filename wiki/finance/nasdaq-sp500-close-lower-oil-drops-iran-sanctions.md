## Summary

Crude oil fell over 2% and Treasury yields eased modestly on Aug. 24 as markets digested the Treasury Department's new Iran sanctions, with the S&P 500 Energy sector the day's worst performer and attention already shifting to Nvidia's Wednesday earnings.

## Core Concepts

- **Oil price move**: WTI crude fell 2.2% to $85.15/barrel; Brent fell 2.3% to about $92.20 amid the new sanctions news.
- **Treasury yields**: The 10-year yield eased roughly 3 basis points to ~4.71%; the 30-year yield eased 4 basis points to 5.23% after hitting a near two-decade high of 5.34% the prior week.
- **[[XPeng]]**: CEO He Xiaopeng touted expectations for rapid humanoid-robot scaling in China and overseas following mass production, supporting continued physical-AI R&D investment even as US-listed XPeng shares are down 45% year-to-date.

## Key Takeaways

- **Energy sector underperformance**: The S&P 500 Energy sector was the worst performer among the 11 tracked industries as crude prices pulled back.
- **Yields still elevated**: Despite the day's modest easing, both 10-year and 30-year Treasury yields remain near multi-week/multi-year highs.
- **XPeng's robotics pivot**: Despite a 45% YTD share decline, XPeng's CEO frames humanoid robotics as a coming growth driver funded by physical-AI R&D investment.
