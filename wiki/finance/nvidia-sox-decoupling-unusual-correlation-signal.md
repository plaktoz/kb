## Summary
Nvidia's three-month correlation with the PHLX Semiconductor Index (SOX) has dropped to just 0.03 — the lowest among all index components — even though Nvidia is the index's largest member by market cap (~$5.25 trillion). This decoupling means Nvidia is increasingly trading on its own AI-infrastructure fundamentals rather than moving in lockstep with the broader chip sector, creating distinct risks and opportunities for investors.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]] (NVDA)** is decoupling from the **[[PHLX Semiconductor Index]] (SOX)**, with a three-month correlation of just 0.03 versus peers like [[Intel]] (>0.90), [[Taiwan Semiconductor]], and [[Micron]] (~0.90).
- **[[AI Infrastructure Demand]]** — hyperscaler capex and GPU orders — is now the primary driver of NVDA price action, replacing traditional chip-cycle dynamics.
- **[[Hedging]]** complexity increases when a single holding dominates an index but moves independently: owning SOXX does not effectively offset Nvidia-specific risk, nor does shorting a semiconductor ETF serve as a clean hedge.
- **[[Stock Correlation]]** as a risk management signal — low correlation with sector peers can indicate both resilience and idiosyncratic tail risk.
- **[[Forward P/E vs. Forward Price/Sales]]** diverge sharply for Nvidia: a 23.4x forward P/E looks near-sector, but a 12.8x forward Price/Sales is ~269% above the sector median, highlighting the premium placed on AI revenue growth.

## Key Takeaways

- **SOX Correlation**: NVDA's 3-month SOX correlation is just 0.03; Intel is above 0.90.
- **Revenue Scale**: Q2 FY2027 revenue hit $96.2B (up 106% YoY), vs. $6.7B in Q2 FY2023.
- **Guidance**: Management guided $108B for the current quarter; ~70% growth projected for FY2028.
- **Market Cap**: ~$5.25 trillion as of Aug. 28, 2026; a single-day 4.6% drop erased ~$250B.
- **Resilience Case**: If PC/memory/smartphone weakness drags SOX, Nvidia may stay insulated as long as AI capex holds.
- **Risk Case**: Nvidia can fall during broad chip rallies if AI-return concerns or custom-chip competition intensify.
- **Hedging Gap**: SOXX ETF hedges are structurally inadequate for concentrated NVDA exposure.
- **Valuation**: Forward P/E of 23.4x (~3% above sector median) but Price/Sales of 12.8x (~269% above median).
- **Technical levels**: Support $208–$210 (50/100-day MA); resistance $228; record high ~$236.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Idiosyncratic Risk]]**: When a single holding's fundamentals diverge from the sector's mean-reversion forces, standard sector-ETF hedges fail — NVDA's near-zero SOX correlation is a live example of idiosyncratic risk overwhelming systematic exposure.
- **[[Price/Sales as a Growth Tax]]**: A forward P/S 269% above the sector median implies the market is pricing in sustained revenue-growth premiums far beyond current-year earnings, making the stock highly sensitive to any deceleration signal.
