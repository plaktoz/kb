## Summary

[[IBM]] reported Q3 2026 revenue of US$17.1b and net income of US$2.19b, reaffirmed 4% to 5% constant currency revenue guidance, and kept its US$1.69 quarterly dividend. Simply Wall St argues the update supports the existing software-led thesis rather than changing it, with the near-term swing factor being how fast IBM converts its US$5.5b generative AI book and z17 cycle into sustained demand. The main risk remains clients delaying discretionary IT and AI projects.

## Core Concepts

- [[IBM]]: Q3 2026 revenue US$17.1b, net income US$2.19b, regular US$1.69 quarterly dividend.
- **Full-year guidance**: Constant currency revenue growth of 4% to 5% reaffirmed, with currency expected to be neutral.
- [[Generative AI]] book of business: Reported at US$5.5b, the key conversion test for software and consulting demand.
- [[IBM z17]]: Mainframe cycle expected to feed software and consulting demand, though infrastructure cycles are lumpy.
- **Open weight models**: IBM's focus on cost-efficient open weight AI models ties AI adoption to operational outcomes for enterprises.
- **Partnerships**: [[Procore]] and [[HashiCorp]] partnerships illustrate the enterprise AI push.
- [[Red Hat]]: Part of the recurring software push aimed at supporting margins and the free cash flow target.
- [[Simply Wall St]]: Source of the narrative-based investment framing and analyst forecasts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Quarter Results**: Revenue US$17.1b and net income US$2.19b in Q3 2026.
- **Guidance Held**: 4% to 5% constant currency revenue growth reaffirmed, currency neutral.
- **Gen AI Book**: US$5.5b of generative AI business is the near-term swing factor.
- **Main Risk**: Clients delaying discretionary IT and AI projects would hit Consulting and software.
- **Analyst Consensus**: US$79.1b revenue and US$11.7b earnings by 2029, 4.2% yearly growth.
- **Earnings Baseline**: Implies roughly US$0.5b earnings increase from US$11.2b today.
- **Bullish View**: Quantum and AI security ambitions imply US$81.5b revenue, US$14.4b earnings by 2029.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What does Simply Wall St conclude about IBM's latest quarter and its investment case?
**A**: The Q3 results and reaffirmed guidance support the existing software-led thesis rather than changing it. Higher-margin software, hybrid cloud and AI must keep offsetting slower transactional software and lumpy infrastructure.

**Q2**: What is the near-term swing factor, and what is the main risk?
**A**: The swing factor is how quickly IBM converts its US$5.5b generative AI book and z17 cycle into sustained software and consulting demand. The main risk is clients delaying discretionary IT and AI projects, hurting Consulting utilisation and software consumption.

**Q3**: How do the base and bullish analyst views differ on 2029 forecasts?
**A**: The base narrative projects US$79.1b revenue and US$11.7b earnings by 2029, while the bullish view leaning on quantum and AI security pencilled in US$81.5b and US$14.4b. New AI partnerships and guidance could push forecasts either way.
