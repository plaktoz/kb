## Summary

[[Microsoft]] stock has lost nearly a fifth of its value in 2026 on fears over AI-infrastructure capex and weak [[Copilot]] adoption, but its July 29 fiscal Q4 earnings could reverse sentiment if [[Azure]] growth and Copilot seat adoption impress. [[Morgan Stanley]]'s bull case carries a $795 price target, more than double the current price, while the stock's trailing P/E of ~23 sits well below its five-year average of ~32.5.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Copilot Seat Adoption]]** — ~20 million paid enterprise seats versus 450M+ total Microsoft 365 subscribers, a key skepticism point
- **[[Azure]]** — growing ~40% annually in recent quarters; the central metric for validating Microsoft's AI capex spend
- **[[AI Capex Guidance]]** — Microsoft has guided for ~$190B in calendar-2026 capital expenditures
- **[[Software Armageddon]]** — investor fear that AI will erode the moat of traditional office-software incumbents like Microsoft

## Key Takeaways

- **Microsoft stock down nearly 20% in 2026** on AI-capex and software-disruption fears.
- **Morgan Stanley bull case: $795 price target**, implying more than a double from current levels.
- **Trailing P/E of ~23** versus a five-year average of ~32.5, an undemanding multiple if the thesis holds.
- **Azure growing ~40% annually**, the clearest signal that AI investment is paying off.
- **Earnings risk cuts both ways**: a strong print could send the stock sharply higher, but weak Copilot or capex commentary could trigger a sell-off.
