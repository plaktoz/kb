## Summary

IBM and Red Hat widened access to the Lightwell Network security offering through Microsoft's cloud marketplace, but IBM's shares fell 9.18% over 7 days and 10.75% over 30 days. At $224.44, a popular Simply Wall St narrative puts fair value at $256.08, implying IBM is about 12% undervalued. The article frames the question as whether the rerating is done or upside remains once valuation is set against cash flows and peers.
