## Summary

US stock futures edged higher heading into a packed earnings week featuring Berkshire Hathaway, Eli Lilly, Caterpillar, McDonald's, Disney, Palantir, AMD, and SpaceX, alongside Friday's closely watched jobs report. Oil prices declined after Trump said Iran peace talks would resume following his cancellation of a planned strike, even as markets digest a volatile month marked by a rotation out of semiconductors and AI-related stocks into software.

## Core Concepts

The week's dense [[Earnings Season|earnings]] calendar is being read by investors as a fresh catalyst for the [[AI Trade]], with results from [[Palantir]], [[AMD]], and [[SpaceX]] providing signals on both enterprise AI adoption and infrastructure spending. The prior month's [[Sector Rotation|rotation]] out of semiconductors into software reflects investor uncertainty about where AI-driven value will actually accrue. Geopolitical developments — Trump's cancellation of a planned Iran strike and resumed peace talks — are directly moving [[Oil Prices]], illustrating how [[Geopolitical Risk]] continues to intersect with equity market sentiment.

- **Futures moved higher** Monday ahead of a heavy week of earnings reports.
- **Key earnings this week**: Berkshire Hathaway, Eli Lilly, Caterpillar, McDonald's, Disney, Palantir, AMD, and SpaceX.
- **Labor market focus**: Friday's monthly US jobs report is a key data point investors are watching.
