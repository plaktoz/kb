## Summary
Singapore stocks closed lower on Monday, Nov 23, following Wall Street's Friday decline and extending the prior week's fall. The Straits Times Index lost 0.4% to 4,953.81, and regional indices were mostly lower, with Korea's Kospi down 3.3%.

## Core Concepts
- [[Straits Times Index]] (STI): benchmark blue-chip index, down 21.36 points on the day.
- [[Hongkong Land]]: top STI gainer after raising full-year profit guidance.
- [[Genting Singapore]]: worst STI performer.
- Local banks ([[DBS]], [[OCBC]], [[UOB]]) continuing a losing streak.
- [[iEdge Singapore Next 50 Index]]: CSE Global top gainer, Hong Leong Asia biggest decliner.
- [[Stephen Innes]]'s read-through from Wall Street's correction-territory move.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Move**: STI fell 0.4% (21.36 points) to 4,953.81.
- **Top Gainer**: Hongkong Land rose 2.4% to US$8.59 on guidance upgrade.
- **Guidance Driver**: Hongkong Land's Q3 underlying profit rose 38%.
- **Worst Performer**: Genting Singapore fell 2.8% to S$0.525.
- **Banks**: DBS -0.5%, OCBC -0.5%, UOB -0.8%.
- **Breadth**: Losers beat gainers 339 to 247; 1.2 billion securities, S$2.1 billion traded.
- **Regional**: Hang Seng -0.1%, Kospi -3.3%, KLCI -0.5%.
- **Wall Street Spillover**: S&P 500 entered correction; Nasdaq 100 briefly crossed 20% drawdown.
