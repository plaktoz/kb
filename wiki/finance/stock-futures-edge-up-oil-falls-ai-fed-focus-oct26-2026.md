## Summary

US stock futures edged up early Monday, October 26, 2026, as [[Brent Crude]] fell 1.7% to $114.77, while worries about AI-linked stocks and the coming [[Federal Reserve]] decision stayed in focus. Friday's selloff pushed the [[S&P 500]] and [[Nasdaq Composite]] to a second straight weekly loss, and [[CoreWeave]] and other neocloud stocks slid premarket after [[Elon Musk]] warned of widespread failures in the sector. Fed funds futures point to 90% odds of a hold on Wednesday, though [[Bank of America]] expects a 0.25-point hike.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Stock Futures]]**: Dow futures +209 points (0.4%), S&P 500 futures +0.1%, [[Nasdaq-100]] futures roughly flat.
- **[[Oil Prices]]**: Brent at $114.77 and [[West Texas Intermediate]] at $100.97 after Brent hit its highest since April on Friday on reports of possible US and Israeli action against [[Iran]] after the midterms.
- **[[Neocloud]] stocks**: [[CoreWeave]] fell premarket; Musk warned upward of 200 firms face "guppy-level death" amid investor fears of debt overextension.
- **[[Hyperscalers]]**: [[Alphabet]], [[Amazon]] and [[Microsoft]] report this week and will update investors on [[AI Capital Expenditure]].
- **[[Federal Reserve]] decision**: Wednesday announcement under Chair [[Kevin Warsh]]; rates currently in the 3.75% to 4% band.
- **[[Bank of America]] view**: Economist [[Claudio Irigoyen]] frames the decision as a test of Warsh's resolve to bring "sea change" to forward guidance.
- **[[Brazil]] election**: President [[Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]] beat [[Flávio Bolsonaro]]; the [[iShares MSCI Brazil ETF]] is poised for its steepest drop on record.
