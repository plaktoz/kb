## Summary

Tech stocks traded mixed heading into a pivotal Big Tech earnings week, with results expected from [[Microsoft]], [[Meta Platforms]], [[Apple]], and [[Amazon]]. Investor focus is heavily on capital expenditure guidance after [[Alphabet]]'s capex outlook rattled markets, raising free cash flow concerns amid the AI spending boom. [[Nvidia]] added to the uncertainty, reportedly in talks with [[OpenAI]] to provide $250 billion in financing for an Ohio data center, reviving questions about circular financing in the AI industry.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Big Tech Earnings]]**: Q3 2026 reporting cycle with [[Microsoft]], [[Meta Platforms]], [[Apple]], [[Amazon]], [[SK Hynix]], and [[Qualcomm]] all reporting.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: Alphabet's capex guidance spooked investors concerned about free cash flow sustainability amid the AI buildout.
- **[[Circular Financing]]**: Nvidia's reported $250 billion deal to finance an OpenAI data center raises structural questions about who ultimately bears AI infrastructure risk.
- **[[AI Trade]]**: Market narrative around AI-driven tech stock valuations at an inflection point as earnings season tests whether capex translates to revenue.
