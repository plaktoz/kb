## Summary
On Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, an AI-led sell-off dropped the [[S&P 500]] 3.7% to 6,910.39, its worst week in over 18 months, and pushed the [[Nasdaq Composite]] down 5.3% into negative territory for the year. The index is now in a correction, more than 10% below its Oct. 6 high, after a seven-day losing streak. Chip stocks and [[Anthropic]], down 9.1% a day after its IPO, led the declines while most non-tech stocks rose.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Stock Market Correction]]**: S&P 500 down more than 10% from its Oct. 6 record; last correction was March 2025.
- **[[AI Stock Sell-Off]]**: Chip and AI-buildout names, including [[Nvidia]], Micron, Broadcom, AMD, Corning and Bloom Energy, bore the brunt.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Fell 9.1% to $88.30, above the $83 IPO price but about 23% below its $115.10 peak.
- **[[Jensen Huang]] scandal**: Nvidia has dropped more than 25% since the scandal erupted earlier in November.
- **[[AI Data Center Spending]]**: Bubble fears worsened as midterm winners advocated slowing data center construction.
- **[[Michael Antonelli]]**: Baird strategist who says the AI spending notion is being repriced, not the whole bull market.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index moves**: S&P 500 -3.7%, Nasdaq -5.3%, Dow -1.9%; Nasdaq now negative for 2026.
- **Streak**: Seven straight losing days, the longest since the 2020 pandemic onset.
- **Chip carnage**: Micron -13.9%, AMD -13.3%, Broadcom -12.1%, Nvidia -13.8%; semiconductor index -10.7%.
- **Global contagion**: Japan's Nikkei and South Korea's Kospi each fell over 5%.
- **Anthropic debut**: Biggest IPO ever, briefly above $2 trillion, then a steeper drop than Facebook's 2012 debut.
- **Rotation**: General Mills rose 3.7% and Lennar 1% as non-tech gained.
- **Macro backdrop**: 10-year yield fell to 4.93%; Brent rose 0.6% to $92.57; consumer sentiment improved.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What happened to US stocks on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026?
**A**: An AI-led sell-off cut the S&P 500 by 3.7%, putting it in correction and marking its worst week in over 18 months. The Nasdaq fell 5.3% and turned negative for the year.

**Q2**: Which stocks led the rout, and how large were the moves?
**A**: Chip and AI-buildout names led: Micron fell 13.9%, Nvidia 13.8%, AMD 13.3% and Broadcom 12.1%, and the semiconductor index sank 10.7%. Anthropic dropped 9.1% a day after its IPO.

**Q3**: What does the strategist quoted say the sell-off does and does not mean?
**A**: Baird's Michael Antonelli says the market is pricing the end of the idea that AI can spend unlimited amounts and support the market. He does not think the bull market that began in October 2022 is over.
