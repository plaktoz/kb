## Summary

[[Microsoft]] (MSFT) stock surged 15.51% on July 30 after its fiscal Q4 2026 earnings report beat Wall Street estimates on both revenue and earnings, with [[Azure]] growth accelerating to 43% and [[Microsoft Copilot]] paid seat additions more than doubling sequentially. The results eased persistent investor anxiety about whether aggressive AI capital expenditures would generate sufficient returns, suggesting the payoff is beginning to materialise. Analysts remain bullish with a consensus "Strong Buy" and an average price target of $543.08 — over 20% upside from post-earnings levels.

## Core Concepts

- [[Microsoft]] (MSFT) — fiscal 2026 revenue reached $331B (+18%), Q4 revenue $90B (+18% YoY)
- [[Azure]] — cloud revenue grew 43% in Q4, accelerating from 40% the prior quarter; crossed $100B for the first time
- [[Microsoft Copilot]] — paid seat additions more than doubled sequentially, total paid seats surpassed 30 million
- [[AI Capital Expenditure]] — capex to increase again in fiscal 2027 to expand AI infrastructure; near-term margin pressure expected to continue
- [[Commercial RPO]] — remaining performance obligation rose 84% YoY to $678B; all sequential growth from non-Frontier-Model customers

## Key Takeaways

- **MSFT +15.51%** on July 30; fiscal Q4 revenue $90B, full-year revenue $331B
- **Azure growth accelerated** to 43% in Q4, up from 40% prior quarter; guided ~45% in Q1 FY2027
- **Copilot momentum**: paid seats surpassed 30M, more than doubling sequentially
- **Commercial RPO $678B (+84% YoY)**: broad-based enterprise demand beyond frontier AI labs
- **Cloud revenue $59.3B in Q4** (+27%); full-year Microsoft Cloud exceeded $214B
- **Capex headwind persists**: FY2027 capex planned to rise again, likely continued gross margin pressure
- **Analyst consensus**: "Strong Buy"; avg. price target $543.08, implying 20%+ upside from $451.10
- **AI ROI narrative shift**: AI investments transitioning from "margin headwind" to "durable growth catalyst"

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Delayed Gratification / J-Curve Investing]]**: Heavy upfront AI capex depressed margins and sentiment for quarters before revenue inflected — Azure's 43% acceleration is the J-curve payoff, confirming that capacity built during investor skepticism is now the moat.
- **[[Market as Expectations Machine]]**: The 15.51% one-day surge reflects not just strong results but a reset of excessively pessimistic expectations; the signal that matters was Azure acceleration plus capex restraint relative to feared levels — the gap between expectation and reality drove the magnitude of the move.
