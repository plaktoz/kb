## Summary
IREN shares fell 18% on Thursday during a broad tech selloff after Bloomberg reported Microsoft is tightening oversight of data center projects with neocloud partners IREN, Nebius, and Nscale. Delays at IREN's Texas campus and Microsoft's exit from several Nscale projects point to execution risk, even as compute demand exceeds supply.

## Core Concepts
- [[IREN]]: neocloud whose Texas campus has cabling issues, an April generator fire, and August hardware delivery delays.
- [[Microsoft]]: reconsidering additional third-party infrastructure agreements and prioritizing its own buildout.
- [[Neocloud]] providers: [[Nebius Group]] and [[Nscale]] also face tighter Microsoft oversight.
- [[Bloomberg]]: source of the report on Microsoft's concerns.
- [[Gartner]]: estimates 80% of neocloud providers could be consolidated or shut down by decade's end.
- [[Data Center Power Constraints]]: grid power difficulties delayed a roughly $3 billion Nscale UK deal.

## Key Takeaways
- **Stock Reaction**: IREN fell 18% amid a broad technology selloff.
- **Oversight**: Microsoft is tightening oversight of IREN, Nebius, and Nscale projects.
- **IREN Delays**: Revised timeline could slip completion by several weeks or months.
- **Nscale Exit**: Microsoft walked away from several projects, including a ~$3B UK deal.
- **Strategy Shift**: Microsoft increasingly favors its own data center expansion.
- **Sector Outlook**: Gartner sees 80% of neoclouds consolidated or shut by 2030.
