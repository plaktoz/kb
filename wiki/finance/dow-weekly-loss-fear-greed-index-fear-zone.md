## Summary

U.S. stocks posted a mixed session on July 25, 2026, with the Dow gaining ~236 points on the day while all three major indices logged a third consecutive week of declines. The CNN Business [[Fear & Greed Index]] remained in "Fear" territory at 39.4, signaling persistent investor caution. Economic data offered a mild positive surprise as the composite [[PMI]] rose to a multi-month high of 53.6.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]** — ended the week down 0.4%, its third straight weekly loss, despite a Friday bounce to 51,947.
- **[[S&P 500]]** — down 0.6% for the week; closed Friday at 7,411.98 with sector rotation into consumer staples and real estate.
- **[[Nasdaq Composite]]** — worst weekly performer at -2.1%, dragged by weakness in [[Chip Stocks]] and information technology.
- **[[Fear & Greed Index]]** — CNN Business composite of seven equal-weighted sentiment indicators; reading of 39.4 places the market in "Fear" zone (scale 0–100).
- **[[PMI]] (S&P Global Composite)** — rose to 53.6 in July from 51.9, highest since November; manufacturing PMI edged down slightly to 53.8.
- **[[Earnings Season]]** — [[Verizon]] beat Q2 estimates; [[American Express]] delivered mixed results and trimmed FY26 sales guidance.

## Key Takeaways

- Dow rose ~236 pts on Friday but posted a third straight weekly loss (-0.4%).
- Nasdaq led weekly declines at -2.1%, pressured by chip-stock weakness.
- Fear & Greed Index at 39.4 — sustained "Fear" zone reading signals caution.
- Composite PMI hit 53.6, highest since November — a bright macro data point.
- Consumer staples, real estate, and materials outperformed on Friday.
- Verizon beat Q2 estimates; American Express narrowed FY26 guidance below consensus.
- Upcoming earnings to watch: Nucor Corp, Celestica Inc, F5 Inc.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: Three consecutive weeks of index declines alongside a Fear reading below 40 historically precede recoveries — markets tend to overshoot pessimism before reverting toward fair value.
- **[[Sentiment as a Contrarian Indicator]]**: A sustained Fear zone reading (39.4) can signal that negative expectations are already priced in, making further downside incrementally harder to sustain without a genuine fundamental catalyst.
