## Summary

[[Gotrade]] News reports that September 2026 US CPI rose 0.6% on the month and 3.7% year over year, above the 3.6% expected, driven by a 9.1% monthly jump in gasoline, with [[Core CPI]] at 2.6% vs. a 2.5% forecast. Stock futures fell and [[Treasury Yields]] rose ahead of the Fed's October 27-28 meeting, while a hot [[Producer Price Index|PPI]] print the next day pushed rate-hike odds higher. Technology shares, including [[Nvidia]], bore much of the pressure as the [[Nasdaq Composite]] fell at the open.

## Core Concepts

- **CPI detail** ([[Bureau of Labor Statistics]]): energy index +5.2% in September, gasoline +9.1% m/m and 34.2% y/y; shelter +0.4%; food unchanged; core +0.3% m/m and 2.6% y/y; monthly pace has picked up since July.
- **Market reaction** (via [[TipRanks]]): [[Nasdaq 100]] futures -0.60%, [[S&P 500]] futures -0.28%, Dow futures -0.15%; 10-year yield +9 bp to 5.306%, 30-year +5 bp to 5.612%; trackers include [[SPY]] and [[QQQ]].
- **Bank earnings**: [[Bank of America]] fell about 4% premarket after a regulatory fine of more than $1.2 billion and a Q3 miss.
- **PPI and Fed odds** (via [[Investing.com]]): headline PPI rose 1.3% in September vs. a 0.3% estimate, the largest gain in about 4-1/2 years; [[CME FedWatch]] quarter-point hike odds rose to nearly 34% from about 29%.
- **Commentary**: [[Heather Long]] (Navy Federal) said every category of business inflation was hot even ex-energy, implying a longer inflation fight.
- **Calendar**: [[FOMC]] meets October 27-28 and December 8-9 (with Summary of Economic Projections); October CPI due November 10, 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Headline Beat**: CPI 3.7% y/y vs. 3.6% expected; 0.6% m/m as forecast.
- **Gasoline Driver**: Gasoline jumped 9.1% m/m, now 34.2% above a year ago.
- **Core Beat**: Core CPI 2.6% y/y vs. 2.5% forecast.
- **Futures Slip**: Nasdaq 100 futures -0.60%, S&P 500 futures -0.28%.
- **Yields Jump**: 10-year Treasury yield up 9 bp to 5.306%.
- **PPI Shock**: Producer prices up 1.3% vs. 0.3% estimate.
- **Hike Odds**: October quarter-point hike odds rose to nearly 34%.
- **Tech Pressure**: Nasdaq Composite fell 1.5% at open; Dow rose 0.6%.
- **BofA Hit**: Bank of America fell ~4% on $1.2B+ fine and Q3 miss.
