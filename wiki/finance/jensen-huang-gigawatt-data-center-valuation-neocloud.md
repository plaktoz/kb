## Summary

[[Jensen Huang]] stated at the [[G20 Summit]] that a 1-gigawatt AI data center facility costs $50B–$60B to construct, offering a replacement-value lens for sizing neocloud assets beyond traditional ARR multiples. Applied to [[Nebius Group]]'s projected 5 gigawatts of contracted power by end of 2026, this framework implies up to $300B in total facility value. The article also highlights how rising per-megawatt contract values and customer prepayments have meaningfully strengthened neocloud balance sheets.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Jensen Huang]] Gigawatt Valuation Framework**: A per-gigawatt replacement-cost model ($50B–$60B/GW) for AI data centers, introduced at the G20 Summit as an alternative to ARR-based valuation.
- **[[Nebius Group]] (NBIS)**: A neocloud provider with Q2 revenue of $582M (+454% YoY), targeting $7B–$9B ARR by year-end 2026 and 5 GW of contracted power.
- **[[Annual Recurring Revenue]] (ARR)**: The conventional SaaS-style metric used to value neocloud revenue; the article argues it understates data center asset value vs. replacement cost.
- **[[Per-Megawatt Contract Value]]**: A pricing metric for AI compute leases; rose from $12M/MW at the start of 2026 to over $20M in Q2, with Q3 short-term deals "above $40M per megawatt."
- **[[Customer Prepayments]]**: Advance payments by customers that self-finance 50–60% of Nebius' capital expenditures, contributing to an $8B cash position.
- **[[Iren]] (IREN)**: Another neocloud operator whose CEO Dan Roberts noted the company's data center portfolio is "unencumbered," enabling potential asset-backed borrowing if needed.
- **[[GPU-Backed Financing]]**: The primary financing route used by neoclouds; data center assets serve as an additional fallback collateral source.

## Key Takeaways

- **Jensen's rule of thumb**: 1 GW AI data center = $50B–$60B construction cost.
- **Nebius implied asset value**: 5 GW contracted × $60B = up to $300B in facility replacement value.
- **Per-MW pricing surge**: $12M → $20M → $40M+ per megawatt across three quarters of 2026.
- **Prepayment flywheel**: Customer prepayments now cover 50–60% of Nebius' CapEx.
- **Cash cushion**: Prepayment-driven cash position of $8B strengthens Nebius' balance sheet.
- **Unencumbered assets**: Iren's data center portfolio is debt-free, providing a financing safety net.
- **Valuation lens shift**: Replacement-cost framing may reveal ARR multiples significantly understate neocloud asset value.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Replacement Cost Valuation]]**: Huang's per-gigawatt benchmark anchors neocloud assets to their physical construction cost — a first-principles floor that ARR-based multiples can miss when markets price only current cash flows, not the irreplicable scarcity of built capacity.
- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: Rising per-megawatt contract values generate larger prepayments, which self-finance more CapEx, which accelerates capacity expansion, which attracts larger contracts — a compounding loop that explains Nebius' accelerating cash position.
