## Summary

Nvidia's Q2 fiscal 2026 13F filing revealed a ~$99 billion investment portfolio in which SpaceX debuted as the second-largest holding at ~$21 billion. The SpaceX position traces back to Nvidia's initial $10 billion investment in xAI, which merged with SpaceX in February 2026, converting private shares to public ones when SpaceX IPO'd in June. The relationship is also strategic: [[Elon Musk]] committed to using [[Nvidia]] GPUs exclusively as the primary hardware for SpaceX's data centers.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nvidia]] 13F Filing** — quarterly SEC disclosure revealing Nvidia's investment portfolio (~$99 billion total)
- **[[SpaceX]]** — rose to Nvidia's second-largest holding (~$21B) after xAI merger and IPO conversion
- **[[Intel]]** — remains Nvidia's largest holding at ~$30 billion
- **[[CoreWeave]]** — third-largest holding at ~$4.7 billion
- **xAI–SpaceX Merger** — Nvidia's initial $10B xAI investment became SpaceX equity via the February 2026 merger
- **GPU Exclusivity Commitment** — Elon Musk agreed Nvidia GPUs would be the exclusive primary hardware for SpaceX data centers, locking out [[AMD]]
- **AI Inference & CPUs** — Nvidia is expanding into CPUs for AI inference, deepening the SpaceX partnership as SpaceX scales its data center strategy

## Key Takeaways

- **Portfolio size**: Nvidia's disclosed investment portfolio totals ~$99 billion as of fiscal Q2.
- **SpaceX rank**: SpaceX is now the #2 holding (~$21B), ahead of CoreWeave (~$4.7B).
- **Intel leads**: Intel remains the largest holding at ~$30 billion.
- **Origin of SpaceX stake**: Nvidia's $10B xAI investment converted to SpaceX shares via merger + IPO.
- **GPU exclusivity**: Musk committed to Nvidia as exclusive primary GPU supplier for SpaceX data centers.
- **AMD excluded**: The exclusivity deal specifically locks AMD out of SpaceX data center competition.
- **Strategic deepening**: Analysts expect the partnership to expand as SpaceX grows data centers and Nvidia moves into AI inference CPUs.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: Nvidia's equity stake in SpaceX combined with an exclusive GPU supply agreement creates a self-reinforcing loop — financial upside aligns directly with hardware demand growth, locking in both capital return and revenue.
- **[[Network Effects]]**: As SpaceX scales its data center footprint exclusively on Nvidia hardware, switching costs rise and the relationship becomes increasingly sticky for both parties.
