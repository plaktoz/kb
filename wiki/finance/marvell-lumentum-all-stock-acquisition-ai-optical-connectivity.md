## Summary
Marvell Technology will acquire optical components maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal valuing Lumentum at an implied enterprise value of $115 billion, combining semiconductors and optical connectivity for AI data centers. Both stocks sold off sharply on Monday (Marvell down 13%, Lumentum nearly 19%) despite huge gains over the past year. Marvell's CEO said the deal doubles down on supporting Nvidia and dismissed concerns about circular dealmaking.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Marvell Technology]]**: Acquirer; CEO [[Matt Murphy]] says the deal creates "a new category of company" joining semiconductors and optical.
- **[[Lumentum]]**: Optical components maker; CEO [[Michael Hurlston]] called the opportunity "enormous."
- **[[Optical Interconnect]]**: Companies increasingly rely on optical connections to link chips that run and train large language models.
- **[[Celestial AI]]**: Optical interconnect startup Marvell bought earlier this year in a multibillion-dollar deal.
- **[[NVIDIA]]**: Recently invested multibillion dollars in both companies; Murphy calls it the "ecosystem leader."
- **[[Vendor Financing]]**: Criticism that Nvidia effectively funds itself by taking stakes in customers and suppliers.
- **Fixed exchange ratio**: Lumentum holders get 4.5 Marvell shares per share, so deal value floats with Marvell's price.

## Key Takeaways
- **Deal Value**: Implied enterprise value of $115 billion for Lumentum.
- **Structure**: All-stock, fixed ratio of 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share.
- **Timeline**: Expected to close in the first half of 2028.
- **Market Reaction**: Marvell fell 13% and Lumentum nearly 19% on Monday.
- **Prior Run-Up**: Marvell more than doubled in a year; Lumentum quadrupled.
- **Strategic Claim**: Combined electrical and optical solutions will be "highly sticky" and sold out.
- **Nvidia Link**: Murphy rejected circular-dealmaking concerns as a "double down" on Nvidia support.
- **Sustainability Concern**: The deal lands amid doubts over long-term AI infrastructure spending.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. In a fixed-ratio stock deal, the target's value moves with the acquirer's share price** — With no cash component, the price paid changes whenever the acquirer's stock changes.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core of the Marvell-Lumentum deal?
**A**: Marvell will acquire Lumentum in an all-stock transaction with an implied enterprise value of $115 billion, joining semiconductors and optical connectivity for AI data centers.

**Q2**: Why does the deal's value depend on Marvell's share price?
**A**: It is a fixed-ratio deal with no cash: Lumentum shareholders receive 4.5 Marvell shares per share, so value moves with Marvell's stock.

**Q3**: How did Marvell's CEO respond to concerns about Nvidia's circular dealmaking?
**A**: Matt Murphy called the deal a "double down" on supporting Nvidia, the ecosystem leader, and said bringing together two key suppliers would benefit that ecosystem.
