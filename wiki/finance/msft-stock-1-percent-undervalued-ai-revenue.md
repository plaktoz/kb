## Summary
Microsoft screens as roughly fairly valued on a discounted-cash-flow basis (~0.8% above intrinsic value) but undervalued on a P/E basis relative to its fundamentals-adjusted fair multiple, with the key uncertainty being whether AI/cloud spending converts into durable free cash flow.

## Core Concepts
- [[Discounted Cash Flow]] (DCF) valuation vs. [[Earnings Multiple]] valuation as competing lenses on the same stock.
- The tension between rising operating profit and falling free cash flow as an [[AI Capex]] signal.
- [[Holdings]]: MSFT.

## Key Takeaways
- **Mixed Valuation Signal**: Scores 3/6 on Simply Wall St's valuation checks.
- **DCF View**: ~$96B trailing free cash flow; intrinsic value ~$499/share, about 0.8% above market price (fairly valued).
- **P/E View**: Trades at 27.5x vs. software industry average 31.4x; a fundamentals-adjusted fair P/E of 48.8x implies undervaluation.
- **Bull vs Bear Split**: Bull case sees 12% undervaluation on AI integration (Azure AI, Copilot, GitHub); bear case sees 25% overvaluation as free cash flow fell even as operating profit rose $26.7B.
- **Key Open Question**: Whether AI/cloud spend converts into cash flow durable enough to justify the price.

**Q3**: What would resolve the bull/bear disagreement?
**A**: Whether AI-driven revenue (Azure AI, Copilot, Dynamics 365, GitHub, Fabric) converts into cash flow durable enough to justify current and further valuation expansion.
