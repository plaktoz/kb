## Summary

Dell Technologies reported record fiscal Q2 2027 results, with revenue of $47 billion (up 58% YoY) and $60.9 billion in AI server orders, driving a 10% single-day stock surge. The company exited the quarter with a $95 billion AI server backlog and raised its full-year FY2027 revenue outlook by $25 billion to $192 billion. Enterprise adoption of agentic AI workloads running locally on Dell infrastructure is cited as the primary demand driver.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dell Technologies]] (DELL)**: Hardware OEM emerging as a major [[AI Infrastructure]] beneficiary through on-premise server demand.
- **[[Agentic AI]] Workloads**: Enterprise deployment of autonomous AI agents running locally on Dell servers is driving hardware procurement cycles.
- **[[AI Server]] Backlog**: A $95 billion order backlog signals multi-quarter visibility and sustained enterprise capex commitment to AI infrastructure.
- **[[Enterprise AI]] On-Premise**: Enterprises choosing local AI deployments over cloud, benefiting hyperscaler-adjacent hardware vendors like Dell and [[Hewlett Packard Enterprise]] (HPE).

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue**: $47B in Q2 FY2027, +58% YoY — a company record.
- **EPS**: Adjusted EPS of $7.04, a 203% year-over-year increase.
- **AI Orders**: $60.9B in AI server orders in a single quarter — record high.
- **Backlog**: $95B order backlog exiting the quarter.
- **Guidance Raise**: Full-year FY2027 revenue outlook raised to $192B (+$25B), ~70% YoY growth.
- **Stock Performance**: DELL up ~10% on earnings day; up 230%+ year-to-date.
- **Peer Lift**: [[Hewlett Packard Enterprise]] (HPE) also rallied, up ~110% YTD.
- **Broad Strength**: Growth not limited to AI — traditional servers, networking, and storage also improved.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: During infrastructure buildouts, hardware suppliers often capture more durable value than application-layer bets — Dell's explosive AI server growth reflects the same dynamic seen with network equipment vendors during prior tech cycles.
- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Rising AI server backlog signals strong forward demand, which boosts investor confidence, which re-rates the stock, which lowers Dell's cost of capital — reinforcing its ability to invest and compete for the next procurement cycle.
