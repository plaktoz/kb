## Summary

President Trump announced Saturday, August 1 that he is calling off major new strikes against Iran, easing geopolitical tension ahead of a heavy earnings week featuring SpaceX's first public-company report, AMD, Sandisk, and Eli Lilly. The prior week's market ended with solid index gains despite an AI-stock-driven hedge fund liquidation, a Treasury yield spike, and continued caution around the durability of the rebound.

## Core Concepts

- **[[SpaceX First Earnings Report]]**: SpaceX (ticker SPCX) reports its first earnings since its June IPO this week, with its IPO lockup expiring two days later — both events are catalysts investors are watching closely.
- **[[Follow-Through Day]]**: An IBD technical concept — a confirmed rally attempt requires a "follow-through day" where an index posts a strong gain on higher volume after a rebound; the Nasdaq was on day two of an attempted rally at the time of writing.
- **[[Treasury Yield Spike]]**: The 10-year yield hit a fresh 18-month high of 4.74%; the 30-year hit a 19-year high of 5.27%, signaling reduced odds of a near-term Fed rate cut and lingering inflation concerns.
- **[[AI Hardware Earnings Read-Through]]**: Sandisk, Western Digital, and AMD earnings are framed as key signals for underlying AI hardware demand, distinct from the "AI trade" narrative driven by hyperscaler capex.

## Key Takeaways

- **Trump's Saturday announcement to hold off on new Iran strikes** eased geopolitical risk premium heading into the trading week.
- **Prior week's rebound**: Dow +1%, S&P 500 +1.05% (back above its 50-day line), Nasdaq +1.6% (off a three-month low), Russell 2000 roughly flat.
- **Microsoft and Amazon drove an AI-led rebound** after a highly leveraged hedge fund's forced liquidation had intensified AI-stock losses earlier in the week.
- **Rising long-term yields remain the biggest headwind** to further gains, even as equity indexes advanced.
- **SpaceX stock sits at record lows**, well below its $135 IPO price, heading into its first earnings report and lockup expiration — a period of unusually high stock-specific volatility risk.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What geopolitical development eased market tension heading into this trading week?
**A**: President Trump announced on Saturday, August 1 that he was calling off major new strikes against Iran after requests from Mideast countries, reducing the risk premium priced into markets.

**Q2**: What two SpaceX-specific events converge this week, and why do they matter?
**A**: SpaceX's first earnings report since its June IPO and its IPO lockup expiration occur within the same week — the earnings report is the first real data point on financial performance, while the lockup expiration allows insiders to sell, both driving elevated stock-specific volatility.

**Q3**: What technical signal was the market watching for to confirm the rebound was durable?
**A**: A "follow-through day" — a strong-volume up day for the Nasdaq confirming the rally attempt — since the index was only on day two of an attempted rebound and the S&P 500 hadn't decisively cleared prior resistance.
