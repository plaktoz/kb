## Summary

July's Core PCE inflation held at 3.3% year over year in line with expectations while Q2 GDP stayed at a low +1.5% on its first revision, and durable goods orders more than doubled forecasts at +1.1%. Attention shifted to Nvidia's after-close Q2 earnings, where expectations of ~99% earnings growth are clouded by scrutiny over "circular financing" arrangements with AI customers like OpenAI.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Core PCE]]**: The Fed's preferred inflation gauge rose 0.2% for the month and 3.3% year over year in July, matching expectations.
- **[[Q2 GDP Revision]]**: GDP held at +1.5% on its first revision, while the price index grew to +6.4% year over year, the highest in four years.
- **[[Durable Goods Orders]]**: Orders rose +1.1%, more than double the +0.5% forecast, the second-strongest month of the year after April.
- **[[Circular Financing]]**: Concern that [[Nvidia]]'s $30 billion investment in [[OpenAI]] (with no chip-deployment milestones) let OpenAI fund a rival chip, [[Jalapeno]], from [[Broadcom]] — raising questions about the durability of Nvidia's AI-trade narrative.

## Key Takeaways

- Core PCE matched expectations at +3.3% year over year in July.
- Q2 GDP remained at a soft +1.5% on first revision.
- Durable goods orders beat expectations by more than double, at +1.1%.
- Nvidia stock is up +14% YTD and +850% over five years heading into earnings.
- Analysts expect Nvidia's Q2 earnings growth near +99%, revenue growth near +96.5%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core claim of the article?
**A**: July's economic data (PCE, GDP, durable goods) came in largely in line with or better than expectations, but market focus was squarely on Nvidia's after-close Q2 earnings and mounting questions about its financing arrangements with AI customers.

**Q2**: What specific mechanism underlies the "circular financing" concern around Nvidia?
**A**: Nvidia gave OpenAI $30 billion in February with no milestones for chip deployment, and OpenAI then developed its own competing "Jalapeno" chip with Broadcom, a direct Nvidia competitor — raising doubts about whether Nvidia's AI revenue is fully organic.

**Q3**: How should an investor use the durable goods and PCE data alongside Nvidia's report?
**A**: Strong durable goods orders and in-line Core PCE suggest resilient demand and contained inflation, but Nvidia's earnings and any commentary on circular financing will likely matter more for near-term market direction given AI's outsized weight in the index.
