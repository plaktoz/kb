## Summary
Alphabet closed at $265.29 on November 21, 2026, its lowest close since October 2025 and 34% below its May record, as investors question its heavy borrowing. It has raised about $10.9 billion in bonds this month to fund data center spending that has pushed free cash flow negative. The article says the company is not in financial trouble, but the bet pays off only if AI computing demand keeps climbing.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why is Alphabet's borrowing weighing on the stock?
**A**: It is financing data center spending with bonds and new shares, and investors are starting to question whether AI computing demand will keep climbing enough to justify it.

**Q2**: What evidence shows capex has outrun the business's cash generation?
**A**: Alphabet spent $132 billion on property and equipment in nine months, and free cash flow turned negative $9.1 billion in Q3 after being positive $24.6 billion in Q4 2025.

**Q3**: What should an investor watch to judge the bet?
**A**: Free cash flow and Google Cloud growth. If cloud revenue keeps doubling, the borrowing looks smart; if it slows while spending rises, the lower price may not be the bottom.
