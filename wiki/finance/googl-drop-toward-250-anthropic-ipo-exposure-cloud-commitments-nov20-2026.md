## Summary
GOOGL traded around $280-281 on Nov. 20 after closing at $272.66 the day before, in a downtrend from a May high near $409 as AI valuation worries hit tech. Anthropic's completed IPO clarified Alphabet's roughly $147 billion stake and a $121.3 billion cloud commitment, but also tied Alphabet to AI-company volatility and financing risk. The author sees continued risk of a drop toward $260-250, with a rebound toward $320-340 if buyers return.

## Core Concepts
- [[Alphabet]] (GOOGL): Fell 3.46% on Nov. 19 while the Nasdaq Composite lost about 2.3% and the S&P 500 about 2%.
- [[Anthropic IPO]]: About $93.5 billion raised, of which the company received about $91.6 billion; ANT opened at $111.50, closed day one near $97.17, then fell about 9% early on Nov. 20.
- [[Alphabet's Anthropic Stake]]: About 1.775 billion shares, 13.4% of capital, worth about $147 billion at the $83 IPO price.
- [[Google Cloud]]: Anthropic committed at least $121.3 billion between April 2026 and July 2033, about $16.7 billion a year, with minimum payments regardless of usage.
- [[AI Infrastructure Financing]]: Anthropic's total compute, equipment, and lease commitments are about $900.9 billion, about $451.6 billion of it by end of 2030.
- [[Project Suncatcher]]: Google and Blue Origin plan New Glenn launches of TPU-equipped satellites, first launch scheduled for Q1 2029.
- [[Technical Support Levels]]: Support at $272-275, then $280; breakdown targets $260-250.
