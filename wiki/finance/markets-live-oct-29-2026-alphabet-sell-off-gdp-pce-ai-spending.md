## Summary

Wall Street closed lower on Oct. 29, 2026 as a roughly 12% drop in [[Alphabet]] after weak Q3 revenue reignited worries about returns on AI infrastructure spending. Stronger-than-expected 3.4% GDP growth and accelerating inflation (core PCE up 0.5% in September) complicated the interest-rate outlook. The live blog also tracks single-stock movers, UK/European markets, oil, and gold.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] Q3 miss**: Shares fell about 12% on weaker search and YouTube advertising, despite [[Google Cloud]] growth of 103%; XTB's Kathleen Brooks cited two consecutive quarters of negative cash flow.
- **[[Major US Indexes]]**: Dow -0.3% to 52,343, S&P 500 -0.3% to 7,720, Nasdaq -1.4% to 26,185; about 390 S&P 500 constituents advanced.
- **[[US GDP]]**: Q3 growth of 3.4% annualized beat the 3.2% forecast, supported by the AI spending boom.
- **[[PCE Inflation]]**: Headline PCE rose 0.5% in September (3.6% year over year); core PCE 3.1% year over year.
- **[[Federal Reserve]]**: Held rates at 3.75% to 4% on a 10-2 vote; a December hike is expected by some.
- **[[Microsoft]]**: Reversed early gains despite a beat as investors weighed AI demand against higher infrastructure spending.
- **[[DoorDash]] / [[Instacart]]**: Roughly $17 billion cash-and-stock acquisition agreement.
- **[[Shell]] and UK markets**: Q3 adjusted earnings of $10.8 billion and a $5 billion buyback lifted the FTSE 100.
- **[[Oil and Gold Prices]]**: Brent about $110.51 and spot gold about $4,029.50 an ounce.

## Key Takeaways

- **Alphabet Slump**: Shares closed around 12% lower on a Q3 revenue and earnings miss.
- **Cash Flow Worry**: Two consecutive quarters of negative cash flow alarmed investors.
- **Selective AI Trade**: Microsoft rose early while Alphabet and Amazon fell; AI is not moving as one group.
- **Strong Growth**: Q3 GDP grew 3.4% annualized, beating the 3.2% forecast.
- **Hot Inflation**: Headline PCE hit 3.6% year over year; core PCE rose 0.5% in September.
- **Rate-Hike Risk**: Fifth Third's economist said demand outpacing capacity strengthens the case for hikes.
- **Rotation**: About 390 S&P 500 stocks rose despite the index decline.
- **Other Movers**: Fortinet -23%, Marvell -12%, Carvana +16%, Eli Lilly +6%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Wall Street close lower on Oct. 29, 2026?
**A**: A sharp Alphabet sell-off, about 12%, reignited concerns about returns on heavy AI investment, while strong growth and accelerating inflation added uncertainty about rates.

**Q2**: What specifically disappointed investors about Alphabet?
**A**: Q3 revenue and earnings missed expectations, with weaker search and YouTube advertising overshadowing strong Google Cloud growth. Analysts also pointed to two consecutive quarters of negative cash flow from AI investment.

**Q3**: How could the economic data affect interest rates?
**A**: GDP grew 3.4% and PCE inflation accelerated, which could complicate the rate outlook. Fifth Third's chief economist said demand outpacing capacity strengthens the case for further rate increases.
