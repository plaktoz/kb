## Summary
Gramercy's weekly says growth risk now competes with inflation risk as the Fed tightens: a firm core PCE print all but locks in a December hike while Treasury yields and metals fell. EM hard currency gained on lower yields, but the strong dollar capped local markets. Highlights cover Venezuela's recovery plan and Moody's Hungary outlook upgrade.

## Core Concepts
- [[Federal Reserve]] tightening: A 25bp December hike to 4.25% is 95.5% priced after October core PCE rose 3.3% y/y.
- [[Core PCE]]: The print that mattered; a tenth above consensus.
- [[Emerging Market Debt]]: EMBI Global Diversified returned 0.44% for the week, with the spread at 228bps.
- [[Venezuela Renace]]: Recovery plan with macroeconomic projections to 2036 and debt restructuring as a first-phase priority.
- [[Hungary]] sovereign rating: [[Moody's]] moved the outlook to Stable and affirmed Baa2.
- [[Section 301 Tariffs]]: USTR proposed duties on 14 trading partners, largely restoring rates lost with the February IEEPA ruling.
- [[OPEC+]] and [[Brent Crude]]: Brent held $90-93 as surplus expectations outweighed geopolitical risk.
- [[Strong Dollar]]: Near 19-month highs, keeping EM local markets under pressure.

## Key Takeaways
- **Fed Path**: December 25bp hike to 4.25% is 95.5% priced; 10-year yield fell about 9bps to 4.8%.
- **US Growth**: Q3 GDP revised up to 4.3% annualized; jobless claims fell to 189k.
- **Oil**: Brent steadied near $93 after a roughly 21% slide in November.
- **Metals**: Copper fell 4.4% and gold 2.6% in the week.
- **Tariffs**: Section 301 duties add about 5 percentage points on targeted partners and are unlikely before January.
- **Venezuela**: Total public debt $237.5bn; bonds rose 2-3 points and the country returned 7.7%.
- **Venezuela Plan**: Oil output projected to reach 2.9mn b/d by 2036; primary deficit to balance by 2031.
- **Hungary**: Bonds were largely unchanged; S&P review on November 27 and Fitch on December 4.
- **Local Markets**: GBI-EM returned -0.10%, with Colombia -4.15% and Mexico -1.48% driven by currencies.
- **Corporates**: CEMBI returned 0.05%, lagging sovereigns; high yield has yet to recover.
- **Week Ahead**: Taiwan elections, OPEC+ meeting November 29, US payrolls, and the December 9 FOMC.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the central macro message of this EM Weekly?
**A**: Growth risk is now competing with inflation risk as the Fed tightens. A firm core PCE print all but locked in a December hike, yet yields and metals fell on slowdown fears.

**Q2**: What did Venezuela's Renace plan reveal and how did markets react?
**A**: It sized total public debt at $237.5bn, named restructuring a first-phase priority, and gave no negotiation timetable or debt-service terms. Sovereign and PDVSA bonds rose 2-3 points.

**Q3**: What should investors watch next, per the article?
**A**: The OPEC+ meeting, US November payrolls ahead of the December 9 FOMC, Hungary's S&P and Fitch reviews, and the IMF's response to Venezuela's assumptions.
