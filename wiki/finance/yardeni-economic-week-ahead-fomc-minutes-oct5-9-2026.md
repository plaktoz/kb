## Summary
[[Ed Yardeni]] and [[Toby Hearst]] preview a light data week (October 5-9, 2026) whose main event is Wednesday's [[FOMC Minutes]] from the September meeting. Most of last week's indicators were strong, but September's weak payrolls report cut the odds of an October rate hike to 22%, while markets still expect a hike by December. Yardeni Research argues the [[Federal Reserve]] should keep hiking at least until it reverses all three of last year's 25bps "insurance" cuts. September's hike reversed one, so two remain.

## Core Concepts
- **Mixed labor signals**: [[Initial Jobless Claims]] fell to near a 57-year low, [[ADP National Employment Report|ADP]] private payrolls rose 90,000, and the [[ISM Manufacturing PMI]] expanded for a ninth month. [[Nonfarm Payrolls]] rose just 29,000, with July and August revised down 60,000 combined. See [[september-jobs-report-29000-payrolls-unemployment-4-2-percent]].
- **[[Federal Funds Rate]] odds**: There is a 22% chance of a 25bps hike at the October 27-28 FOMC meeting and 67% by December. Two hikes by December are priced at just 3%.
- **Reversing "insurance" cuts**: Yardeni's view is that the Fed should undo last year's three 25bps precautionary cuts. September's hike undid one.
- **[[FOMC Minutes]] focus**: Investors want to see how much support the Committee has for tightening beyond what the September [[Summary of Economic Projections]] [[Dot Plot]] implies.
- **[[Kevin Warsh]]**: The Fed Chair said the September hike "removed a dose of accommodation," which implies the FFR is still below the [[Neutral Rate of Interest|neutral rate]]. See [[warsh-timelier-return-fomc-dot-plot-ai-sep2026]].
- **Futures vs. the Fed**: [[Fed Funds Futures]] are more hawkish than officials' median, implying 3.3 hikes over 12 months and 1.7 over six months.
- **Services inflation**: [[Supercore PCE]] inflation has been rising since October 2025, so the services prices-paid components of the [[ISM Services PMI]] deserve close attention.
- **Global calendar**: [[Eurozone]] retail sales, a speech by [[Bank of England]] Governor [[Andrew Bailey]] on energy-driven hikes, and [[Canada]]'s September employment report.

## Key Takeaways
- **Payroll miss**: September payrolls rose only 29,000. Unemployment edged up to 4.2%.
- **Hike odds**: 22% for October, 67% for December, and 3% for two hikes.
- **Dot plot**: The median sees the FFR at 4.1% at end-2026 and end-2027, i.e. one more hike, then a hold.
- **Neutral rate**: The median longer-run estimate rose to 3.25%, a post-pandemic high.
- **2-year yield**: At 4.78%, it is well above the 4.00% top of the FFR target range.
- **Services strength**: The S&P Global flash NM-PMI jumped to 58.7 from 56.5.
- **Claims**: Initial claims were 197,000, the lowest since July 18. The four-week average is 200,000.
- **Continuing claims**: 1,701,000, the lowest since March 2023.
- **Weekly ADP**: The four-week average reached 20,000, up from a late-July bottom of 8,250.
- **BoE**: Markets put 88% odds on a November hike, with ~66bps more priced for 2027.
- **Canada**: August lost 41,700 jobs, and unemployment held at 6.4%.
- **Eurozone**: July retail sales ex autos fell 0.6% m/m, after June's record high.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Yardeni Research's core policy view for the Fed heading into the October 5-9 week?
**A**: The Fed should hike at least enough to reverse all three of last year's 25bps "insurance" cuts. September's hike reversed one, which leaves two more.

**Q2**: How do market expectations compare with Fed officials' projections?
**A**: The September dot plot median implies just one more 25bps hike in 2026, followed by a hold through 2027. FFR futures are more hawkish, implying 3.3 hikes over the next 12 months, and the 2-year Treasury yield of 4.78% sits well above the 4.00% top of the target range.

**Q3**: Why should investors watch the FOMC minutes and the services PMI this week?
**A**: The minutes should show how much support exists for tightening beyond the dot plot, which could move hike odds after the weak payrolls report. Services prices-paid data matter because supercore PCE inflation has been rising since October 2025.
