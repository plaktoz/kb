## Summary
Ahead of Singapore's National Day holiday, the Straits Times Index gained nearly 120 points (about 2%) over back-to-back sessions to close at 5,698.43 on Friday, just under the 5,700 mark — but analysts flagged the rally may stall given a weak Asian markets outlook amid Middle East tensions and rising oil prices.

## Core Concepts
The [[Straits Times Index (STI)]], tracked by the [[SPDR STI ETF (ES3)]], closed Friday at 5,698.43, up 59.44 points (1.05%), driven by gains in [[Financial Shares]] and [[Property Stocks]] while industrials were mixed. The broader [[Asian Markets]] outlook was weak due to the ongoing [[Middle East Conflict]] and a resulting spike in [[Crude Oil]] prices, with U.S. and European markets both soft heading into the session.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index level**: STI closed at 5,698.43, gaining 59.44 points (1.05%) on Friday, trading between 5,644.56 and 5,700.07.
- **Multi-session rally**: The index gathered almost 120 points (~2%) over back-to-back sessions ahead of the National Day holiday.
- **Sector drivers**: Financial shares and property stocks led gains; industrials were mixed.
- **Risk flag**: Analysts warned the rally may stall as Middle East tensions and oil price spikes weigh on the broader Asian markets outlook.
