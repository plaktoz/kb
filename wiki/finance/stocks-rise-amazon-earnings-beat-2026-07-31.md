## Summary

US equity indexes finished higher on July 31, 2026, snapping their weekly losing streaks, driven by Amazon's 15.3% surge after a strong Q2 earnings beat anchored by [[AWS]] cloud growth. Gains were tempered by rising Treasury yields hitting 2007 highs, as hawkish Fed dissenter comments from [[Beth Hammack]] and [[Neel Kashkari]] spooked the bond market. Apple fell 7.4% on weak forward guidance and rising memory costs, while Roblox suffered its worst single-day drop ever at -26.9%.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Amazon]] Q2 Earnings Beat**: Revenue outperformed on AWS cloud strength; AI capex budget raised to $220B; custom silicon and AI commercialization gaining traction.
- **[[Apple]] Post-Earnings Decline**: Q3 beat overshadowed by soft Q4 guidance and surging memory costs; Tim Cook noted paying more per memory unit each quarter.
- **[[Roblox]] Bookings Miss**: Q2 bookings below expectations; no full-year forecast provided; largest single-day decline in company history.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: 10-year hit 4.712% and 30-year hit 5.253% — both highest since 2007 — on hawkish Fed dissenter commentary.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] Dissent**: [[Beth Hammack]] and [[Neel Kashkari]] both pushed for a rate hike at the July meeting, pressuring the bond market even as the Fed held steady.
- **[[Bond Vigilantes]]**: Market participants are pricing in higher inflation expectations, protesting the Fed's patient posture under Chair [[Kevin Warsh]].
- **[[AI Capex Monetization]]**: Amazon's results cited as "real evidence that AI capex is being monetized," a key inflection point for the AI investment thesis.

## Key Takeaways

- **Dow +0.5%**, S&P 500 +0.7%, Nasdaq +1.0% on July 31.
- **Amazon +15.3%**: AWS cloud drove Q2 beat; capex raised to $220B for memory chips.
- **Apple -7.4%**: Weak Q4 revenue guidance; Tim Cook's final earnings call as CEO.
- **Roblox -26.9%**: Worst single-day drop ever; Q2 bookings miss and no full-year forecast.
- **10-year yield at 4.712%**: Highest since 2007, dampening equity upside.
- **Fed hawks dissenting**: Hammack and Kashkari both favored a July rate hike.
- **AI monetization signal**: Amazon's results framed as proof AI capex returns are materializing.

## First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: Rising Treasury yields signal bond market doubts about the Fed's credibility, which in turn pressure equity valuations — the market's reaction to the Fed shapes future Fed behavior, a self-reinforcing loop Soros identified.
- **[[Signal vs. Noise]]**: Amazon's AI capex monetization beat is a high-signal data point amid a noisy macro environment, separating genuine AI revenue traction from hype-driven investment theses.
