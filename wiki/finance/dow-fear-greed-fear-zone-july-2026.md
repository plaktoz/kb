## Summary
US markets ended the week of July 21–25 mixed. The Dow gained 236 points on Friday but still closed the week down 0.4% — its third consecutive weekly decline. S&P 500 fell 0.6% and Nasdaq dropped 2.1% for the week, with the CNN Fear & Greed Index remaining in "Fear" territory at 39.4. Chip stocks led the tech decline amid ongoing AI capex uncertainty.

## Core Concepts

- [[CNN Fear & Greed Index]] — reading at 39.4 (Fear zone); scale 0–100; based on 7 equal-weighted market indicators
- [[S&P 500]] — weekly close at 7,411.98, down 0.6%; third consecutive weekly loss for the Dow
- [[Chip Stock Selloff]] — information technology stocks closed lower on Friday while other sectors gained
- [[S&P Global PMI]] — composite PMI climbed to 53.6 in July (highest since November); manufacturing PMI at 53.8

## Key Takeaways

- **Three consecutive weekly losses** for the Dow heading into the high-stakes earnings week
- **Fear zone at 39.4** — below 40 signals elevated pessimism; contrarian signal for patient buyers
- **Sector rotation**: consumer staples, real estate, and materials outperformed on Friday while tech declined
- **PMI data positive**: 53.6 composite PMI above 50 signals expansion; manufacturing at 53.8 also solid
- **Dow at 51,947** — Nasdaq at 24,975 — both well off 2026 highs

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Contrarian Thinking]]**: Fear & Greed at 39.4 with strong PMI data (53.6) signals a disconnect between sentiment and underlying economic activity — historically, this divergence represents a buying opportunity rather than a signal to sell.
