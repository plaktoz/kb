## Summary

US stock futures were mixed and oil rose on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, as Wall Street prepared for a week of big-bank earnings and September [[Consumer Price Index]] and [[Producer Price Index]] data. Escalating [[Houthis]] attacks on Saudi Arabia's main airport and tanker strikes threatened further energy disruption, while [[Donald Trump]]'s deal to import Russian diesel drew bipartisan criticism. Markets nonetheless remain near record highs on concentrated earnings growth, with analysts expecting about 30% EPS growth this quarter.

## Core Concepts

- **Week ahead**: [[JPMorgan Chase]], [[Goldman Sachs]], and [[Citigroup]] report Tuesday; CPI Wednesday, PPI Thursday. Friday's close was higher on a broad rebound as AI/tech jitters eased.
- **[[Kyle Rodda]] ([[Capital.com]])**: Geopolitical risk and global monetary-policy uncertainty weigh on markets, but highly concentrated earnings growth keeps Wall Street near records; bank earnings will test corporate fundamentals before the [[Magnificent Seven]] report.
- **[[Houthis]] escalation**: Strikes on Riyadh's international airport killed at least 13 (including a US national) and injured hundreds; Trump is considering joining the Saudi-led campaign in [[Yemen]]. The Houthis also threaten shipping through the [[Bab el-Mandeb Strait]].
- **Russian diesel deal**: Trump announced an agreement with [[Vladimir Putin]] to import millions of tons of Russian diesel to lower fuel prices before the midterms; six bipartisan senators urged a reversal, citing conflict with [[Russia Sanctions]]. Trump also claimed a Russia–Ukraine "energy ceasefire," but Ukraine reported continued attacks.
- **[[Iran War]] / [[Strait of Hormuz]]**: Iran is reviewing a US response to its Hormuz reopening proposal via intermediaries ([[Qatar]], [[Pakistan]]); the US wants the nuclear program addressed first, Iran wants Hormuz security first. Pre-war, Hormuz carried ~one-fifth of global oil and LNG trade.
- **[[SpaceX]]**: Shares rose 1.06% to $164.30 premarket after announcing an ~$8 billion acquisition of [[Grain Management]]'s 800 MHz low-band spectrum; the [[FCC]] approved 15,000 direct-to-device satellites for [[Starlink]] Mobile. Telecom stocks in the US, Europe, Australia, and NZ fell on satellite competition fears.
- **Commodities**: [[WTI Crude]] +1.13% to $92.89; [[Brent Crude]] +1.17% to $106; gold −0.50% to $4,195.10; silver −0.28% to $60.88. Two tankers were attacked near Oman's Sohar and in Hormuz.
- **Bond market**: Closed for Columbus Day; stock market open normal hours.
