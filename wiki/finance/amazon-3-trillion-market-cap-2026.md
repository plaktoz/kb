## Summary
[[Amazon]] crossed a $3 trillion market capitalization for the first time after a stronger-than-expected Q2 earnings report showed surging [[AWS]] cloud revenue driven by AI demand, joining [[Apple]], [[Microsoft]], [[Alphabet]], and [[Nvidia]] in the $3T club.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Amazon]] Market Cap Milestone** — reached $3 trillion roughly two years after crossing $2 trillion in June 2024
- **[[AWS]]** — posted $42.2 billion in Q2 revenue, beating estimates by more than $1.6 billion
- **[[AI Capex]]** — Amazon raised 2026 capital expenditure guidance to $220 billion (from $200 billion), citing rising memory prices tied to the AI buildout
- **[[Magnificent Seven]] Club** — Nvidia remains the most valuable company, approaching a $5 trillion market cap

## Key Takeaways
- **New all-time high**: Amazon's best single-day stock performance since May 5, up roughly 4%.
- **Earnings beat**: adjusted EPS of $1.97 vs. $1.82 estimated; revenue $200.61B vs. $196.47B estimated.
- **AWS growth**: $42.2B in Q2 revenue, topping expectations.
- **Capex raised to $220B for 2026**, up from a prior $200B guide, driven by AI-related memory cost increases.
- **Five companies** have now ever reached a $3T market cap: Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Nvidia, and Amazon.

## 🃏 Review Questions
**Q1**: What triggered Amazon's market cap crossing $3 trillion?
**A**: A stronger-than-expected Q2 earnings report showing a surge in AWS cloud revenue fueled by AI demand.
**Q2**: How did Amazon's actual results compare to analyst estimates?
**A**: Adjusted EPS came in at $1.97 versus $1.82 estimated, and revenue was $200.61 billion versus $196.47 billion estimated.
**Q3**: Why did Amazon raise its capex guidance for 2026?
**A**: CEO Andy Jassy attributed the increase from $200B to $220B largely to rising memory prices tied to the AI infrastructure buildout.
