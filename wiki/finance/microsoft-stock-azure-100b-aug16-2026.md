## Summary
[[Microsoft]] (MSFT) shares jumped 18% in a week after [[Azure]] cloud revenue topped $100 billion for fiscal 2026 (+41% YoY), proving the company can convert heavy AI capex ($175B in fiscal 2026) into real revenue while still generating positive free cash flow ($19.6B) — a contrast to peers like Alphabet and Meta.

## Core Concepts
- **[[AI Capex-to-Revenue Conversion]]**: The article's central thesis is that Microsoft "laid out the playbook" for turning AI infrastructure spending into monetizable revenue, distinguishing it from peers whose capex has crushed free cash flow.
- **[[Free Cash Flow Discipline]]**: Despite $175B in capex, Microsoft posted $19.6B positive FCF in fiscal Q4 2026, versus Alphabet's negative $5.9B and Meta's 91% FCF decline to $784M — a key differentiator investors are rewarding.
- **[[Valuation Relative to Peers]]**: Microsoft's trailing P/E of 28 sits below the technology sector average of 35, framed as still-reasonable valuation despite the stock's recent 29% one-month run.

## Key Takeaways
- **Azure milestone**: $100B+ annual revenue for the first time, up 41% YoY.
- **Stock move**: MSFT +18% in a week, +29% over the past month.
- **Capex**: $175B fiscal 2026 capex (adjusted from $190B due to an accounting change); industry-wide AI capex estimated at $750B this year.
- **FCF comparison**: Microsoft +$19.6B vs. Alphabet -$5.9B and Meta's 91% FCF decline to $784M in their most recent quarters.
- **Valuation**: P/E of 28 vs. sector average of 35.
