## Summary

TeraWulf shares jumped 19.7% after the former Bitcoin miner disclosed a 20-year, roughly $19 billion data-center lease with Anthropic, reinforcing its pivot from crypto mining to long-duration AI infrastructure contracts even as the company posted a nearly $940 million quarterly net loss.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Bitcoin Miner AI Pivot]]**: TeraWulf is one of several crypto miners (alongside peers like Bitdeer, CleanSpark, and IREN) repositioning toward AI/HPC data-center leasing as demand for compute outpaces crypto-mining economics.
- **[[Long-Duration AI Lease Contracts]]**: The 20-year, ~$19 billion Anthropic lease follows earlier deals with Fluidstack and Google, building a base of contracted, multi-decade AI infrastructure revenue.
- **[[Counterparty Concentration Risk]]**: Reliance on a small number of hyperscale/AI tenants for long-duration leases increases exposure to execution risk if projects slip or a major tenant pulls back.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock rose 19.7%** on the lease news despite a Q2 2026 net loss of about $939.9 million on revenue of $44.77 million.
- **20-year, ~$19 billion Anthropic lease** materially reshapes TeraWulf's business mix toward contracted AI infrastructure revenue.
- **Analyst estimates for 2029 revenue vary widely** — from a pessimistic ~$1.8 billion to a bullish narrative projecting $2.1 billion revenue and $254.8 million in earnings, implying sharply divergent views on execution risk.
- Heavy capex and tenant concentration remain the key risks flagged by analysts if AI demand growth softens.
