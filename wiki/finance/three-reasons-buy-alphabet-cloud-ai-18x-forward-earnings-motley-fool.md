## Summary

The Motley Fool argues the post-earnings dip in [[Alphabet]] is a buying opportunity despite a Q3 revenue miss, a 26% EPS drop and negative free cash flow. Its case rests on accelerating Google Cloud growth, AI tailwinds, and a forward P/E of 18x versus 17x for communication services. The stock is down 11% year to date while the S&P 500 is up 10%.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] (GOOG, GOOGL)**: Q3 revenue rose 25% to $127.6B, below estimates; EPS fell 26% to $2.12; free cash flow was negative $9.1B.
- **[[Google Cloud]]**: Q3 sales of $30.7B, up 103% y/y, with a $561B backlog up 262%.
- **[[Cloud Backlog]]**: 55% of it, about $309B, is expected to be recognized over 24 months.
- **[[Gemini]]**: The app reached one billion users.
- **[[Custom AI Chips]]**: Alphabet has started selling them, citing strong demand.
- **[[Forward P/E]]**: 18x versus a 17x average for communication services stocks.
- **[[AI Capex]]**: Heavy spending pressuring profit and free cash flow.

## Key Takeaways

- **Q3 Miss**: Revenue of $127.6B trailed estimates; EPS fell 26% and free cash flow turned negative.
- **Cloud Acceleration**: Cloud revenue of $30.7B grew 103% y/y.
- **Backlog**: $561B cloud backlog, up 262%, implies roughly $38.6B per quarter over eight quarters.
- **Gemini Scale**: The Gemini app has one billion users.
- **Chip Sales**: Custom AI chips are a new revenue line.
- **Valuation**: 18x forward earnings is a slim premium to the 17x sector average.
- **Author's View**: The post-earnings dip is unjustified; five-year returns could beat average.
