## Summary
Earnings season accelerates the week of Oct 18, 2026 after a bruising week for tech stocks, with SpaceX (Tuesday), Tesla and Meta (Wednesday) as the headline reports. Investors will focus on [[Elon Musk]]'s AI ambitions and the economics of the [[AI Capex Boom]]. Netflix, IBM, GE Aerospace and major airlines round out the calendar.

## Core Concepts
- [[Tesla]] (TSLA): Q3 deliveries are already known, so attention shifts to margins, cash flow and funding of robotaxi, Optimus and AI.
- [[SpaceX]] (SPCX): focus beyond rockets on [[Starlink]] growth and emerging AI operations.
- [[Meta Platforms]] (META): kicks off [[Hyperscaler Earnings]], with scrutiny on data center and AI infrastructure returns.
- [[Western Digital]] (WDC): tests the AI memory trade.
- [[Netflix]], [[IBM]], [[GE Aerospace]], and airlines (UAL, AAL, LUV): read on consumer demand, corporate spending and travel.
