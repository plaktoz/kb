## Summary

Piper Sandler maintained an Overweight rating on [[Microsoft]] on September 29, 2026, while raising its price target from $550 to $610 (+10.91%), citing confidence in cloud and AI-driven earnings growth. GuruFocus data shows MSFT trading at $508.96, a 13.6% discount to its $588.77 GF Value™, alongside a perfect GF Score™ of 100/100. Institutional sentiment is mixed: 53 gurus hold shares (22 adding, 29 trimming), while insiders sold $71.4 million worth of stock over the past three months.
