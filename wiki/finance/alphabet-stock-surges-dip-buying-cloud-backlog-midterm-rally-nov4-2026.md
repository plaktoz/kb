## Summary

Alphabet stock rose nearly 4.9% in morning trading to $293.71 as investors bought the dip after last week's historic sell-off following mixed Q3 earnings. The article credits dip-buying, [[Google Cloud]] strength, AI infrastructure deals and a broad market rally on U.S. midterm election day. The stock remains well below its 52-week high of $408.61, and concerns over negative free cash flow and elevated capex persist.

## Core Concepts

- [[Alphabet]]: shares hit their lowest level in over a year after the Q3 report, then rebounded as valuation looked deeply discounted versus its own history and mega-cap peers.
- [[Dip Buying]]: buyers stepped in after the sell-off, alongside institutional repositioning.
- [[Google Cloud]]: Q3 revenue more than doubled year over year to about $31 billion.
- [[Cloud Backlog]]: the enterprise backlog reached $561 billion.
- [[AI Infrastructure]]: a $15 billion Malaysia AI data center commitment through 2027-2028, plus credit support for a roughly $19 billion [[Anthropic]] data campus.
- [[Analyst Consensus Rating]]: "Strong Buy" with an average 12-month price target well above current levels.
- [[Midterm Elections]]: the S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq rose on election day as the AI trade rebounded.

## Key Takeaways

- **Price Move**: Shares rose nearly 4.9% to $293.71 in morning trading.
- **Rebound Context**: Follows last week's historic sell-off to a more-than-one-year low.
- **Cloud Fundamentals**: Cloud revenue roughly $31B in Q3, with a $561B backlog.
- **AI Dealmaking**: $15B Malaysia data center pledge and credit support for ~$19B Anthropic campus.
- **Market Tailwind**: S&P 500 up over 1.7%, Dow over 2.0%, Nasdaq over 2.3%.
- **Analyst Stance**: Consensus "Strong Buy" despite negative free cash flow and heavy capex guidance.
- **Remaining Gap**: Stock still well below its $408.61 52-week high.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Alphabet stock surge on this day, according to the article?
**A**: A combination of dip-buying and institutional repositioning after last week's sell-off, helped by a broad market rally and Alphabet's Cloud and AI infrastructure strengths. The valuation had fallen to deeply discounted levels versus its own history and mega-cap peers.

**Q2**: What data points do analysts cite to argue the sell-off was overdone?
**A**: Google Cloud revenue more than doubled year over year to about $31 billion in Q3, and its enterprise backlog reached $561 billion. Alphabet also announced a $15 billion Malaysia AI data center commitment and credit support for a roughly $19 billion Anthropic data campus.

**Q3**: What risks remain despite the rebound?
**A**: Analysts still flag negative free cash flow and elevated capital expenditure guidance, and the stock is well below its 52-week high of $408.61. Investors continue to monitor the trajectory of the company's AI spending cycle.
