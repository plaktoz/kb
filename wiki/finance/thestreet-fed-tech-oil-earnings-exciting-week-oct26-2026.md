## Summary
TheStreet previews a week of heavy earnings and a Fed decision on Oct. 28 that could produce market gyrations. Tech earnings should be solid, oil earnings could be astonishing, and the Fed is expected to hold rates before a hike in December. Investors entered the week on edge after a flat prior week.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Federal Reserve]]** and **[[Kevin Warsh]]**: the Oct. 28 decision is expected to hold rates at 3.75% to 4%, with a hike expected in December.
- **[[Big Tech Earnings]]**: Alphabet and Microsoft (Oct. 28), Amazon and Intel (Oct. 29), plus Visa, Coca-Cola, Boeing, and Eli Lilly.
- **[[Oil Company Earnings]]**: Exxon Mobil and Chevron report Oct. 30, boosted by crude up nearly 80% this year.
- **[[AI Data Center Investment]]**: a Gartner report cited by CNBC warns that data center developers may consolidate if the business does not support the spending.
- **[[Mortgage Rates]]** and **[[10-Year Treasury Yield]]**: the 30-year mortgage rate is about 7.3%, down from a 7.61% peak on Oct. 5.
- **[[Iran War]]**: the U.S.-Israeli war against Iran is cited as the driver of oil's gains.

## Key Takeaways
- **Fed Outlook**: Most watchers expect a hold on Oct. 28 and a hike in December.
- **Earnings Calendar**: Alphabet and Microsoft report Oct. 28, Amazon and Intel Oct. 29.
- **Oil Earnings**: Exxon EPS is expected at $4.03, up 114%, on revenue of $106 billion.
- **Chevron**: EPS is expected to rise from $1.85 to $4.86 on revenue of $61.7 billion.
- **Tech Risk**: Billions in data center spending may outrun the business it generates.
- **Last Week**: The S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell 0.5% to 0.8%; the Dow rose 0.4%.
- **Rates**: The 10-year yield was 5% on Oct. 23, above 5% in 21 of 22 sessions.
- **Data Releases**: New-home sales and consumer confidence on Oct. 27, consumer inflation on Oct. 29.
