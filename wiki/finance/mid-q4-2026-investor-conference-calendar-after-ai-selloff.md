## Summary

The Nasdaq 100 fell 7% in October as investors rotated from AI stocks into defensive and real-economy sectors. With Q3 results in, CEO commentary at the November-December investor conferences is the next catalyst, before sell-side 2027 outlooks arrive. Capex trends and consumer health are the key themes to watch.

## Core Concepts

- [[Nasdaq 100]]: shed 7% in October during the AI-trade selloff.
- [[Sector Rotation]]: Health Care hit record highs, and Consumer Staples had their best month since 2000.
- [[Credit Default Swaps]]: spreads were widely quoted in the financial press, even before post-earnings crashes in [[Alphabet]] (GOOGL) and [[Intel]] (INTC).
- [[Hyperscaler Capex]]: continued debt financing by hyperscalers could push rates higher.
- [[Investor Conferences]]: CEOs set the tone before sell-side 2027 outlooks.
- [[Consumer Spending]]: wages lag inflation, raising the risk of a holiday pullback.
- Upcoming tech catalysts: [[Microsoft]] Ignite, [[NVIDIA]] earnings, and the [[Anthropic]] IPO, potentially before Thanksgiving.

## Key Takeaways

- **October Selloff**: The Nasdaq 100 lost 7% as investors rotated to defensives.
- **Defensive Leadership**: Health Care hit record highs; Staples had their best month since 2000.
- **Commentary Window**: CEOs have only weeks to set the tone before 2027 outlooks.
- **Bond Market Clue**: Borrowing costs eased in October, but hyperscaler debt financing could lift rates again.
- **Capex Watch**: Capex clues from conferences could drive markets through the end of 2026.
- **Consumer Risk**: Negative real income trends raise the chance of a protracted spending pullback.
- **Conference Calendar**: Sector lists run from Nov 4 to Dec 10, spanning tech, health care, financials, and energy.
