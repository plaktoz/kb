## Summary

The week of July 28, 2026 is the busiest of summer earnings season, headlined by [[Amazon]], [[Meta]], [[Microsoft]], and [[Apple]] — each under scrutiny for AI capex justification. The [[Federal Reserve]]'s two-day meeting adds macro tension as resurgent oil prices push markets to price a one-third chance of a rate hike this meeting and near-certainty of one by September. Thursday's PCE price index and preliminary Q2 GDP reading are the key economic data points.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Big Tech Earnings]] Gauntlet**: [[Meta]] (AI capex and cloud rental plans), [[Microsoft]] (Azure growth, [[Copilot]] seats, [[OpenAI]] partnership), [[Amazon]] (AWS vs Azure/Google Cloud), [[Apple]] (Services, memory cost impact; final earnings call for CEO [[Tim Cook]] before September chairman transition).
- **[[Federal Reserve]] Rate Decision**: Chairman [[Kevin Warsh]] press conference Wednesday; oil-driven inflation pressure; debate over whether [[AI Capex]] boom is generationally inflationary vs [[Warsh]]'s view that AI will be deflationary long-term.
- **[[PCE Price Index]]**: Fed's preferred inflation gauge — economists forecast -0.07% m/m, +3.7% y/y; core PCE +0.2% m/m, +3.3% y/y. [[Bank of America]] argues underlying inflation remains above the 2% target.
- **Secondary Earnings**: [[Boeing]] (free cash flow, delivery targets), [[Starbucks]] (same-store sales, [[Brian Niccol]] turnaround), [[Procter & Gamble]] (consumer softness, input costs), [[Corning]] (fiber optic shift, data center exposure), [[Eaton]] (liquid cooling, [[Boyd Thermal]] acquisition).

## Key Takeaways

- **Meta bar lowered**: Stock 11% below pre-April earnings level; market expects AI capex and cloud rental details.
- **Microsoft key metric**: Azure growth + Copilot seat growth + [[Satya Nadella]] view on cheap Chinese open-weight models.
- **Apple CEO transition**: Tim Cook's last earnings call before becoming executive chairman in September.
- **Fed hike odds**: One-third chance of a hike this meeting; 95% chance by September per market pricing.
- **PCE forecast**: Core PCE +3.3% y/y expected — well above the Fed's 2% target; Bank of America sees no relief.
- **AI inflation debate**: [[Fed Governor Lisa Cook]] flagged AI capex as a potential price driver; Warsh sees AI as deflationary.
- **Q2 GDP**: Preliminary reading Thursday — key for growth vs. inflation tradeoff framing.
- **Boeing watch**: Free cash flow guidance ($1B–$3B) and 2026 plane delivery target (500) are the key metrics.

## First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Dual Mandate Tension]]**: The Fed faces simultaneous pressure from resurgent oil-driven inflation and an uncertain growth picture — the AI capex boom complicates the standard rate framework because it could be inflationary (demand-pull on energy/hardware) or deflationary (productivity gains) depending on your time horizon, forcing the Fed into a judgment call with imperfect signals.
- **[[Hurdle Rate Compression]]**: As interest rates rise, the discounted value of future AI returns shrinks — Big Tech's massive capex commitments made at near-zero rates must now be justified against a higher cost of capital, which is why markets are scrutinizing FCF rather than just revenue growth.
