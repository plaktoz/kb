## Summary

Singapore stocks closed higher on Tuesday, Oct 6, 2026, tracking overnight gains on Wall Street, with the [[Straits Times Index]] (STI) up 0.7% to 5,701.54. [[DFI Retail Group]] led blue-chip gainers while [[ST Engineering]] was the worst performer, and all three local banks ended higher. A Tiger Brokers strategist attributed the move to US earnings optimism outrunning bond pressure, led by the [[Magnificent Seven]] lifting the [[Nasdaq]] to a record close.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Gained 37.21 points (+0.7%) to finish at 5,701.54.
- **[[DFI Retail Group]]**: Top STI gainer, up 4% (US$0.12) to US$3.15.
- **[[ST Engineering]]**: Worst STI performer, down 2% (S$0.23) to S$11.
- **Singapore Banks**: [[DBS Group]] +0.9% to S$78.56, [[OCBC]] +1% to S$32.20, [[UOB]] +1.5% to S$43.72.
- **[[iEdge Singapore Next 50 Index]]**: [[Top Glove]] led (+14.6% to S$0.275); [[Centurion Corp]] was the biggest decliner (−3.6% to S$1.34).
- **Market Breadth & Activity**: Gainers beat losers 267 to 232; 958 million securities worth S$1.9 billion traded. [[Golden Agri-Resources]] was most active by volume (72.8 million shares); DBS by value (S$303.5 million).
- **Regional Markets**: Mixed — [[Hang Seng Index]] +1%, [[Nikkei 225]] +1.1%, [[FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI]] +0.1%, [[Kospi]] −0.9%.
- **US Market Driver**: [[James Ooi]] (Tiger Brokers) cited earnings optimism outrunning bond pressure, with the [[Magnificent Seven]] pushing the [[Nasdaq]] to a record close.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI Level**: Up 0.7% to 5,701.54, tracking Wall Street gains.
- **Top Blue-Chip Gainer**: DFI Retail Group rose 4% to US$3.15.
- **Laggard**: ST Engineering fell 2% to S$11.
- **Banks Up**: UOB led local lenders with a 1.5% gain.
