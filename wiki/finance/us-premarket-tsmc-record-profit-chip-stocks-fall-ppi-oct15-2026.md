## Summary

On October 15, 2026, US index futures diverged pre-market — Nasdaq 100 futures down 0.99% while Dow futures rose 0.27% — as a hotter-than-expected 3.7% September [[Consumer Price Index|CPI]] revived Fed rate-hike fears and AI infrastructure financing risks weighed on tech. [[TSMC]] fell about 1.9% despite a sixth straight record quarterly profit, as cautious 2027 commentary and margin pressure from overseas expansion outweighed the results. Credit default swap costs for [[Oracle]] and [[CoreWeave]] hit record highs, while gold rose to about $4,160 and WTI held near $95.2 on [[Strait of Hormuz]] disruptions.

## Core Concepts

- **Futures divergence**: Capital rotated into blue-chip and defensive sectors, lifting Dow futures while [[S&P 500]] and [[Nasdaq 100]] futures fell.
- **Inflation surprise**: September CPI rose 3.7% YoY vs. 3.6% expected, raising concerns about further [[Federal Reserve]] hikes.
- **TSMC Q3**: Revenue $46.72B (+41.2% YoY); net profit NT$763.06B (+68.7%); Q4 guidance $52.3B–$54.3B; caution on 2027 macro and overseas capacity margins.
- **Chip selloff**: [[Micron Technology]] -4.5%, [[AMD]] -3%, [[Nvidia]] nearly -2% on fears high financing costs slow [[AI Data Center]] construction.
- **AI credit risk**: Oracle five-year [[Credit Default Swap]] spread at 288bp (from 260bp a week earlier); CoreWeave above 1,000bp with 2032 bonds yielding ~16%, per [[S&P Global Market Intelligence]].
- **Anthropic revenue**: [[Anthropic]] Q3 revenue reportedly rose to $16.8B from $11.6B (+45% QoQ), adjusted profitable, per pre-IPO meeting attendees.
- **Beige Book**: The Fed's [[Beige Book]] shows "low hiring and low layoffs"; three districts report strong price growth, eight moderate; diesel, tariffs and insurance cited.
- **Hormuz shipping**: Two cargo ships, GFS Pride and Cape Dao, sank after being struck by projectiles near the strait.
- **Other movers**: [[Rocket Lab]] fell over 10% after delaying its Neutron maiden launch to 2027; [[Bitcoin]] fell ~0.7% to ~$81,200; [[Gold]] rose ~1.3% as a [[Safe Haven Assets|safe haven]].
- **Upcoming events**: September [[Producer Price Index|PPI]], retail sales, jobless claims; speeches by [[Beth Hammack]] and Fed Chair [[Kevin Warsh]].

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Stocks trade on the forward outlook, not past results** — Record results already reflected in expectations cannot lift a stock when guidance signals tougher conditions ahead. · *model:* [[Expectations Investing]]
→ explains: TSMC Record Ignored, Cautious Guidance

**T2. Debt-funded growth is fragile when financing costs rise** — Companies expanding on borrowed money see credit risk and funding costs climb as rates rise, which can slow their expansion and hit their equity and bonds together. · *model:* [[Leverage]]
→ explains: Debt-Funded AI Risk, CoreWeave Spread, Chip Rout, Hot CPI

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did chip stocks fall on October 15, 2026 despite TSMC's record profit?
**A**: A hotter-than-expected September CPI revived rate-hike risks and fueled concerns that high financing costs could slow AI data center construction, while TSMC's cautious 2027 outlook and margin warnings overshadowed its results.

**Q2**: What credit-market data showed rising AI infrastructure risk?
**A**: Oracle's five-year CDS spread rose to 288bp from 260bp a week earlier, and CoreWeave's broke above 1,000bp with its 2032 bonds yielding around 16%.

**Q3**: What does this session suggest for evaluating AI infrastructure stocks?
**A**: Investors should watch financing costs and credit spreads alongside earnings, since debt-funded AI expansion becomes riskier when inflation keeps rate-hike expectations alive.
