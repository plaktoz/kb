## Summary

Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley have captured nearly 80% of the top-five-bank equity trading revenues in 2026, generating a combined $52 billion driven by hedge fund demand for leveraged bets on AI hardware and Asian semiconductor supply chains. Their massive balance sheets allow them to absorb large single-trade risks that force smaller rivals to cede share, transforming equity desks from cyclical businesses into durable, high-margin lending engines. Trump-era regulatory easing of leverage rules further unlocked balance sheet capacity, though the IMF and some analysts have cautioned about rising systemic risks.

## Core Concepts

- [[Goldman Sachs]], [[JPMorgan]], [[Morgan Stanley]] — the "Big Three" prime brokers capturing ~80% of top-5 bank equity trading revenues (~$52B combined through Q3 2026)
- [[Prime Brokerage]] — the business of providing hedge funds margin loans, securities lending, and trading services; now a key growth engine for the Big Three
- [[AI Trading Boom]] — institutional demand for leveraged exposure to AI hardware, hyperscalers, and semiconductor supply chains driving record equities financing
- [[Picks and Shovels Investing]] — institutional flows into South Korean and Taiwanese memory chipmakers and semiconductor manufacturers ("picks and shovels" of AI supply chain)
- [[Leverage]] — hedge fund leverage described by JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon as "the highest it's ever been"; Goldman equities financing jumped 96% YoY to $3.37B in Q3
- [[Economies of Scale]] — prime brokerage as "the ultimate scale business"; large balance sheets enable absorbing single-trade risks of $100M+ that smaller banks cannot
- [[Regulatory Arbitrage]] — eased supplementary leverage ratio rules under Trump administration removed balance sheet caps, unlocking growth for the Big Three
- [[Concentration Risk]] — Big Three capturing ~80% of revenue, widening the divide between giants and mid-tier banks (BofA, Citigroup)

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue Record**: Top-5 banks generated ~$67B equities revenue through Q3 2026, already $7B above full-year 2025.
- **Big Three Dominance**: Goldman, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley earned $52B combined (~80% share).
- **Hedge Fund Leverage**: Insatiable demand for AI-stock leverage is the primary growth driver.
- **Goldman Surge**: Equities financing activities jumped 96% YoY to a record $3.37B in Q3.
- **Scale Moat**: Big Three can absorb potential $100M single-trade losses; smaller banks cannot.
- **Asia Goldmine**: South Korea and Taiwan semiconductor plays were the fastest-growing frontier.
- **Regulatory Tailwind**: Revised leverage rules (finalized 2025) gave Big Three greater flexibility.
- **IMF Warning**: Growing leverage, crowded positions, and correlated hedge fund selling pose stability risks.
- **Hedge Fund AUM**: Global hedge fund assets reached an estimated $13 trillion (IMF).
- **Structural Shift**: Equity desks have transformed from cyclical to durable, high-margin lending engines.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Scale creates winner-take-most dynamics in capital-intensive intermediary businesses** — when underwriting large leveraged trades requires absorbing potential nine-figure losses, only institutions with the largest balance sheets can compete, causing revenues to concentrate among the few firms that can meet that size threshold. · *model:* [[Economies of Scale]]
→ explains: **Scale Moat**, **Big Three Dominance**

**T2. Regulatory easing acts as a leverage multiplier in prime brokerage** — relaxing supplementary leverage ratio rules effectively expands the capacity of big banks to finance speculative positions, amplifying both upside revenues and systemic risk exposure during crowded trades. · *model:* [[Leverage]]
→ explains: **Regulatory Tailwind**, **IMF Warning**

**T3. Picks-and-shovels positioning in emerging tech cycles concentrates gains in infrastructure providers** — hedge funds allocating to AI hardware, memory chipmakers, and semiconductor manufacturers rather than pure AI software companies follow the pattern of investing in the enabling layer of a technology cycle. · *model:* [[Picks and Shovels Investing]]
→ explains: **Asia Goldmine**, **Hedge Fund Leverage**
