## Summary

[[IBM]]'s Apptio unit launched the public preview of **[[Apptio AI Value & ROI]]**, a tool that connects AI spending — including token costs — to measurable business outcomes across revenue, cost, speed, productivity, and risk. The launch responds to Gartner data showing 84% of finance leaders cannot measure AI ROI, positioning IBM to capture enterprise budget scrutiny around AI spend.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Apptio AI Value & ROI]]**: Connects each AI initiative to customer-selected "proof metrics" (cycle time, cost avoided, conversion rate, incident volume) with baseline/target/actual/realized tracking over time.
- **[[IBM Cloudability]]**: Provides visibility and attribution of token spend, feeding into AI Value & ROI's outcome tracking.
- **[[Technology Business Management]] (TBM)**: The broader discipline Apptio pioneered — translating technology spend into financial and business outcomes — now extended specifically to AI/token economics.
- **AI ROI measurement gap**: Gartner estimates 84% of finance leaders can't measure AI ROI, and only ~40% of those who tried succeeded — the core problem this product targets.

## Key Takeaways

- **Real client outcome cited**: One client reportedly saw a 50% cost reduction using Apptio, freeing budget for new AI initiatives.
- **Public preview now, GA in Q3 2026**: Available to existing IBM Apptio Costing Standard and AI TCO & Usage customers first.
- **Positions IBM as an AI "accountability" layer**: Rather than competing on model capability, IBM is selling governance/measurement tooling that sits above AI spend from any vendor.
- **Timed to a real market problem**: Comes as enterprises broadly signal frustration over unclear returns on large AI infrastructure and token spend commitments.
