## Summary

IBM shares fell 25% after preliminary Q2 results showed customers shifting spend toward hardware (servers, storage, memory) and delaying software deals as AI infrastructure spending dominates budgets, per CEO Arvind Krishna. Palantir, by contrast, is a pure-play beneficiary of enterprise AI data/decision software, posting 85% revenue growth and 150% net dollar retention, though it trades at a much richer valuation than IBM's 17x forward earnings.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM]]** — holding; fell 25% as hardware spend crowded out software deals
- **[[Palantir Technologies]]** — pure-play enterprise AI beneficiary, posted 85% revenue growth
- **[[Arvind Krishna]]** — IBM CEO who attributed the shortfall to hardware-driven budget shifts
- **[[AI Infrastructure Spending]]** — customer spend rotating toward servers/storage/memory over software

## Key Takeaways

- **IBM fell 25%** as hardware spend crowded out software deals.
- **IBM trades at ~17x forward earnings** versus Palantir's ~39x sales.
- **Palantir posted 85% revenue growth**, its 11th accelerating quarter.
- **Palantir's total remaining deal value** climbed 98% to ~$12B.
