## Summary
[[Cloudflare]] entered its August 6 Q2 2026 earnings report with consensus expecting $665.4M revenue (30% YoY growth) and 27 cents non-GAAP EPS, driven by AI-agent traffic, the [[Workers]] developer platform, and [[Zero Trust]] security adoption — though rising infrastructure costs from that same AI traffic growth are pressuring margins.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Cloudflare]]**: Global network and security platform positioning itself around the "agentic Internet" — AI agents and autonomous workflows driving new categories of network traffic.
- **[[Zero Trust]] architecture**: Enterprises consolidating networking and security vendors onto unified platforms (Cloudflare One, SASE, Browser Isolation, Gateway, Access) rather than point solutions.
- **AI Gateway / Agent Cloud**: Cloudflare's AI-specific product lines capturing demand from developers building AI-native applications.

## Key Takeaways
- **30% YoY revenue growth expected**, with EPS growth of 28.6%, continuing a streak of four straight quarterly beats (average surprise 11.62%).
- **AI traffic is a double-edged driver**: it boosts demand for Cloudflare's platform but also raises infrastructure costs, pressuring gross margins.
- **Zero Trust consolidation** is a structural tailwind as enterprises simplify their security vendor stack.
- **Earnings model was neutral**: Cloudflare's Earnings ESP sat at 0.00%, so the standard beat-predictor didn't signal a strong likelihood of outperformance this quarter.
