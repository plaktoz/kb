## Summary

Momentum has emerged as one of the strongest-performing equity factors in 2026, with the [[Invesco S&P 500 Momentum ETF]] (SPMO) attracting over $3.59 billion in net inflows over three months. SPMO tracks the [[S&P 500 Momentum Index]], weighting holdings by risk-adjusted price momentum rather than market capitalization, enabling concentrated exposure to market leaders. Over the past 12 months, SPMO returned 26.89%, outpacing both [[MTUM]] (26.18%) and the broader S&P 500 Total Return Index (17.43%).

## Core Concepts

- **[[Momentum Investing]]**: A factor-based strategy that identifies stocks already outperforming peers and bets on continuation of relative strength — not prediction but pattern-following.
- **[[SPMO]] (Invesco S&P 500 Momentum ETF)**: Tracks the S&P 500 Momentum Index; weights by momentum rather than market cap; rebalances semiannually; expense ratio 0.13%.
- **[[S&P 500 Momentum Index]]**: Selects and weights S&P 500 stocks based on risk-adjusted price momentum, distinct from broader large/midcap momentum indices.
- **[[MTUM]] (iShares MSCI USA Momentum Factor ETF)**: A competing momentum ETF with a broader large- and midcap universe using risk-adjusted momentum; returned 26.18% over the same 12-month window.
- **[[Factor Investing]]**: Systematic approach to equity exposure based on quantitative characteristics (here: momentum) rather than fundamental analysis.
- **Semiannual Rebalancing**: SPMO refreshes its portfolio twice yearly as market leadership evolves, maintaining systematic momentum exposure without emotional decision-making.
- **Market Leadership Broadening**: AI still drives markets but leadership has extended into industrials, financials, and cyclicals — rewarding stocks with sustained relative performance.

## Key Takeaways

- **Momentum outperformance**: SPMO returned 26.89% vs. S&P 500's 17.43% over 12 months.
- **Massive inflows**: $1.02B in 1 month; $3.59B in 3 months into SPMO.
- **Momentum-weighted, not cap-weighted**: SPMO concentrates in current market leaders by momentum score.
- **Semiannual rebalancing**: Systematic refresh reduces emotional bias, captures evolving leadership.
- **SPMO vs. MTUM**: Narrower universe (S&P 500 only) vs. broader large/midcap gives different exposures.
- **Low cost**: 0.13% expense ratio makes SPMO competitive among factor ETFs.
- **Leadership broadening**: Industrials, financials, cyclicals joining tech as momentum leaders.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Trend Following]]**: Momentum investing is trend-following applied to individual equities — the same principle that prices already incorporate known information, so persistently outperforming stocks signal durable structural advantages worth riding until the trend breaks.
- **[[Systematic vs. Discretionary Decision-Making]]**: Semiannual rebalancing enforces rule-based discipline, removing the cognitive biases (recency bias, loss aversion) that cause discretionary investors to overstay losers and undersell winners.
