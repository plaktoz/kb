## Summary

JPMorgan raised its bull-case target for Singapore's Straits Times Index to 7,000 over the next 12 months — a 22% upside — citing robust economic growth, a narrowing valuation gap with developed markets, and continued haven demand amid geopolitical and AI-driven volatility.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Singapore's benchmark equity index, up more than 23% in 2026, outperforming rival financial hub Hong Kong.
- **[[Goldilocks Economic Backdrop]]**: JPMorgan's framing for Singapore's growth environment — strong enough to support earnings growth without overheating, aided by fiscal room from an Equity Market Development Programme.
- **[[Valuation Reprising Thesis]]**: JPMorgan's core argument — that even though STI valuations have broken out of historical averages, the index should keep reprising closer to developed-market peers given high yield and currency stability.
- **[[Haven Demand]]**: Investors have rotated into Singapore equities partly as a haven from geopolitical tensions (Middle East conflict) and AI-driven market volatility elsewhere.
- **[[SGX ETF Expansion]]**: The Singapore Exchange plans to widen its ETF offerings and partnerships to strengthen its role as Southeast Asia's key multi-asset exchange ($21B AUM as of Aug 2026).

## Key Takeaways

- **Bull case target of 7,000** implies 22% upside from the STI's August 11 close; JPMorgan had raised its base case to 6,000 back in January, and the index is already up 16% since then.
- **Singapore upgraded its 2026 GDP growth forecast** on August 11, from 2-4% to 4.5-5.5%, citing AI-driven trade and manufacturing tailwinds offsetting Middle East conflict drag.
- **Top JPMorgan stock picks**: DBS Group Holdings, Singapore Exchange, Keppel, and UOL Group.
- **STI has gained more than 23% in 2026**, outperforming Hong Kong's benchmark over the same period.
- **Q2 earnings roundup (week of Aug 11)**: City Developments Limited (CDL) nearly tripled first-half net profit to $300M on strong residential demand; Sea Ltd raised its 2026 Shopee EBITDA forecast to ~US$1B on rising ad revenue; Sembcorp raised its interim dividend despite a 25% profit decline, citing confidence from its Alinta Energy acquisition. Singtel's headline profit fell 71.6% only due to a prior-year one-off gain — underlying profit was actually up 21%. Hotel Properties Limited (HPL) swung to a $39.1M net loss as Middle East conflict disruption weighed on travel.
- **Graphics card shortage**: PC Partner Group (Singapore-listed) warned a worsening GPU shortage — driven by AI infrastructure demand — is likely to raise PC prices in H2 2026.
