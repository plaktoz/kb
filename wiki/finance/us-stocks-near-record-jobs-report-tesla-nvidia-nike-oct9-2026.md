## Summary

U.S. stocks rose Friday, pulling within 1% of the [[S&P 500]]'s August record, after a softer-than-expected jobs report eased fears the economy was running hot enough to reignite inflation, cutting the market-implied odds of an October [[Federal Reserve]] rate hike to under 23% from 64% a week earlier. [[Tesla]] rallied 5.2% on stronger-than-expected Q3 deliveries and [[Nvidia]] led S&P 500 gains, while [[Nike]] fell 4% on a weak revenue outlook; volatile oil prices and swinging [[10-year Treasury Yield]]s pared the market's gains intraday.

## Core Concepts

- **September jobs report**: Employers added 29,000 payrolls, below forecasts and a slowdown from August's revised 133,000 pace, easing concerns about an overheating economy.
- **[[Federal Reserve]] rate-hike odds**: Fell to under 23% for the October meeting, down from 64% a week earlier, per [[CME Group]] data, after the Fed's recent rate hike (its first in three years).
- **[[10-year Treasury Yield]]**: Dropped briefly below 5.17% from a Thursday peak near 5.35%, then rebounded to 5.28% as oil prices pared losses.
- **[[Tesla]]**: Rallied 5.2% after delivering 486,532 vehicles in the latest quarter, beating analyst expectations.
- **[[Nike]]**: Fell 4% after revenue came in weaker than feared despite a profit beat, and its fiscal-year profit forecast missed expectations.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Rose 1.6%, the single strongest force lifting the S&P 500, as lower yields boosted AI-industry valuations.
- **Oil prices**: Brent crude swung between $98 and $103, settling at $102.25, amid uncertainty over how the Iran conflict will reshape the global oil industry.
- **Global markets**: European indexes rebounded from a prior day's bond-yield-driven selloff; Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 2.6% while South Korea's Kospi rose 0.5%.

## Key Takeaways

- **S&P 500**: Rose 0.7%, within 1% of its August record.
- **Dow and Nasdaq**: Dow up 233 points (0.5%); Nasdaq up 1.2%.
- **Jobs miss**: +29,000 payrolls vs. expectations, down from August's 133,000 pace.
- **Rate-hike odds**: Dropped to under 23% from 64% a week earlier.
- **Treasury yields**: Swung between 5.17% and 5.35% over two days.
- **Tesla**: +5.2% on a Q3 delivery beat (486,532 vehicles).
- **Nike**: -4% on a weak revenue outlook despite a profit beat.
- **Vanguard's Adam Schickling**: Said the report "strengthens the case for the Federal Reserve to remain patient."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Second-Order Thinking]]**: A weaker-than-expected jobs report is "bad" news on its face, but markets read through to its implication for Fed policy — fewer odds of a near-term hike — and rallied instead of selling off.
