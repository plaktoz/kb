## Summary

[[IBM]] has been one of Zacks.com's most-searched stocks after falling 6.6% over the past month, while the [[S&P 500]] gained 2.4% and its industry gained 5%. Consensus estimates are steady to slightly rising, with current-quarter EPS of $2.87 (+8.3% YoY) and fiscal-year EPS of $12.33 (+6.4%). Zacks rates IBM a Rank #3 (Hold) with a Value Score of C, suggesting it will perform in line with the market in the near term.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Fair value is the present value of future earnings** — A stock's fair value is set by its expected stream of future earnings, so upward estimate revisions raise fair value and pull the price toward it. · *model:* [[Discounted Cash Flow]]
→ explains: Quarter EPS Estimate, Fiscal-Year Estimates, Rating

**T2. Earnings growth depends on revenue growth** — A company can't keep increasing earnings over an extended period without increasing revenue, so revenue growth limits how long earnings growth can last.
→ explains: Revenue Outlook, Revenue Dependency
