## Summary

In Q4 FY2026, [[Microsoft]] recorded a $3.2 billion gain from its [[Anthropic]] investment — nearly matching its entire full-year gain from [[OpenAI]] in a single quarter — while the OpenAI stake was simultaneously marked down $600 million. The contrast highlights how Microsoft's AI investment portfolio is increasingly bifurcated, with a newer, cloud-tethered bet on Anthropic outperforming its longer-standing OpenAI stake in terms of near-term mark-to-market returns.

## Core Concepts

- [[Microsoft]] — reported Q4 FY2026 revenue of $90B, net income $35.8B, diluted EPS $17.95 for the full year
- [[Anthropic]] — Microsoft's $5B investment (November 2025) generated a $3.2B Q4 gain; deal included a $30B Azure services commitment from Anthropic
- [[OpenAI]] — Microsoft holds ~27% stake; Q4 saw a ~$600M markdown (reducing EPS by ~$0.07); full fiscal year still generated a $5B gain ($0.67 EPS)
- [[Azure]] — Anthropic's $30B cloud services commitment ties the investment return directly to Azure demand growth
- [[AI Investment Portfolio Risk]] — holding stakes in competing frontier AI labs creates both upside and correlated downside exposure

## Key Takeaways

- **Anthropic Q4 gain**: $3.2B, boosting diluted EPS by $0.33
- **OpenAI Q4 markdown**: ~$600M, reducing EPS by ~$0.07 — mixed quarter despite $5B full-year gain
- **Single-quarter catch-up**: Anthropic nearly matched OpenAI's entire FY2026 contribution in one quarter
- **Anthropic deal structure**: $5B invested November 2025; Anthropic committed to purchasing $30B in [[Azure]] services
- **Microsoft stake in OpenAI**: approximately 27%
- **FY2026 overall**: $331.8B revenue, $133.7B net income, $17.95 diluted EPS

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Optionality vs Commitment]]**: Microsoft's Anthropic deal bundles an equity gain with a $30B Azure services lock-in, converting an investment into a customer commitment — this is optionality deliberately collapsed into a strategic dependency, concentrating upside and reducing counterparty risk simultaneously.
- **[[Portfolio Diversification Paradox]]**: Holding competing AI labs (OpenAI and Anthropic) looks like diversification, but because both are valued on the same AI-hype cycle, a broad AI sentiment shift would compress both simultaneously — the "diversification" is more operational (cloud revenue) than financial.
