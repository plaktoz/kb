## Summary

Singapore's [[Straits Times Index]] (STI) edged higher at Tuesday's open, rising 2.91 points (0.05%) to 5,731.93, with positive market breadth of 84 gainers against 78 decliners. The three local banks — [[DBS Group]], [[OCBC]], and [[UOB]] — were among the most active counters, with DBS up 0.22% to S$78.28. Regional derivatives, including FTSE China A50, Nikkei 225, SGX FTSE Taiwan, and MSCI Singapore futures, were also tracked as early context for the session.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Singapore's benchmark of the 30 largest SGX-listed companies by market capitalisation; opened at 5,731.93, up 0.05%.
- **Singapore Banks**: [[DBS Group]], [[OCBC]], and [[UOB]] were among the active counters driving early trade.
- **REIT segment**: [[CapitaLand Ascendas REIT]], [[CapitaLand Integrated Commercial Trust]], and [[Keppel DC REIT]] traded in a mixed range.
- **Regional futures**: FTSE China A50, Nikkei 225, SGX FTSE Taiwan, and MSCI Singapore index futures provided a cross-market read on sentiment heading into the session.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI opens at 5,731.93**: Up 2.91 points or 0.05% as of 9.06am Tuesday.
- **Positive breadth**: 84 gainers vs. 78 decliners; volume of 48.73 million shares worth S$102.48 million.
- **Banks active**: DBS rose 0.22% to S$78.28; UOB and OCBC also among active counters.
- **Other counters**: Yangzijiang Shipbuilding at S$5.18, City Developments at S$7.52, Hongkong Land at US$8.30, Geo Energy Resources at S$0.615.
- **REITs mixed**: CapitaLand Ascendas REIT fell 0.44% to S$2.28; CapitaLand Integrated Commercial Trust at S$2.23; Keppel DC REIT at S$2.11.
