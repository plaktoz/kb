## Summary
[[Alphabet]] (GOOGL) closed at $324.22 on October 22 after breaking down from a triangle pattern, ahead of Q3 earnings on October 28. The author argues capex and free cash flow matter more than EPS because Q2 showed 82% [[Google Cloud]] growth alongside negative free cash flow and an $80 billion equity raise. Technically the stock is bearish below $335.72, and a daily close under $315.29 would open $297.45.
