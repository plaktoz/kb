## Summary

[[Microsoft]]'s fiscal Q1 2027 report earned an A for [[Azure]] growth (48% vs. a 44-45% forecast), a C+ for spending and margins, and a B+ for the outlook. Free cash flow fell about 27% as capex rose, so the author is closer to buying but still wants a price near $465 rather than $530.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Azure]] growth vs. guidance**: Revenue growth of 48% beat the company's own forecast under the narrowed Azure definition.
- **[[Capital Expenditures]] and [[Free Cash Flow]]**: Capex of $45.2B squeezed free cash flow to $18.6B even as operating cash flow rose.
- **[[Gross Margin]] mix effect**: Microsoft Cloud gross margin fell to 66% as lower-margin Azure grew as a share of cloud sales.
- **[[Price-to-Earnings Ratio]] valuation**: About 29x trailing adjusted EPS of roughly $18.04 at a $530 share price.
- **[[Amy Hood]] guidance**: Demand exceeds supply; Q2 Azure growth guided to 49-50%.

## Key Takeaways

- **Azure Beat**: 48% growth versus 44-45% forecast, despite late third-party data center capacity.
- **Acceleration**: Azure growth went 40%, then 42%, then 48% over three periods.
- **Capex Surge**: $45.2B quarterly capex, up about 30% year over year.
- **Cash Flow Hit**: Free cash flow fell about 27% to $18.6B while operating cash flow rose 29%.
- **Margin Mix**: Operating margin rose to 50%, but Microsoft Cloud gross margin slipped to 66%.
- **Outlook**: Q2 revenue guided to $96-97.1B; calendar 2026 capex around $170B.
- **Valuation**: Stock at about 29x adjusted earnings; the author would buy nearer $465.
