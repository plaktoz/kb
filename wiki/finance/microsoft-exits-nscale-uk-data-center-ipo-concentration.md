## Summary
Microsoft has reportedly withdrawn from a planned UK data center deal with [[Nscale]] at Loughton, Essex, just as Nscale prepares a potential $3 billion IPO. Power supply delays and Nscale's heavy reliance on Microsoft and Anthropic (about 85% of contracts) heighten concerns. The news lands amid a rough stretch for AI infrastructure names such as [[CoreWeave]].

## Core Concepts
- [[Microsoft]] (MSFT): expected main customer of the Essex site; stock fell 1.93% to $529.05 Friday.
- [[Nscale]]: British AI infrastructure startup planning a ~$3B IPO as soon as October 2026.
- [[Anthropic]]: with Microsoft, accounts for about 85% of Nscale's contracts.
- [[Data Center Power Constraints]]: local authorities reportedly warned the Essex site may not get power until the 2030s.
- [[CoreWeave]] (CRWV): shares down 25% last week on debt worries; [[Firmus]] abandoned its IPO.

## Key Takeaways
- **Deal Exit**: Microsoft reportedly pulled out, per the Telegraph; reason undisclosed.
- **Delays**: Norway flagship is six months behind, with 6.4 of 30 MW operating.
- **Customer Concentration**: Microsoft and Anthropic make up about 85% of Nscale contracts.
- **Sector Stress**: CoreWeave fell nearly 12% Friday on data center debt concerns.
- **Nscale Response**: Says Microsoft relationship remains strong; exploring other power and UK sites.
- **Analyst View**: MSFT Strong Buy consensus, $602.72 average target (about 14% upside).
