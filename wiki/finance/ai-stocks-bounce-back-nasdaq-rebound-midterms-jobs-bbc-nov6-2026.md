## Summary
AI and tech shares recovered slightly after a rout that wiped trillions off market values, with the Nasdaq up 1.1% after a 5.5% fall. The sell-off was tied partly to fears that a Democrat midterm win would slow AI development, plus doubts over AI spending payoffs, while strong jobs data offered a counterweight. Asian and European markets fared worse than the US.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nasdaq Composite]] rebound of 1.1% after a 5.5% fall, alongside a 0.3% gain in the [[S&P 500]] and a 0.4% dip in the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]].
- [[AI Regulation]] risk: Democrats campaigned on new rules for AI and data centres and won both houses of Congress.
- [[AI Infrastructure Spending]] concerns: whether enormous investment will pay off economically.
- [[Semiconductor Stocks]]: [[Nvidia]], [[SK Hynix]] and the [[Kospi]] were hit hardest; Kospi fell 6%.
- [[Bank of England]] rate rise and a signal of another, weighing on UK traders.
- [[US Jobs Report]]: 165,000 jobs added in October, more than double expectations; unemployment at 4.1%.
- [[Donald Trump]] reiterating support for "super intelligence".

## Key Takeaways
- **Partial Rebound**: Nasdaq closed up 1.1%, recovering only part of its 5.5% drop.
- **Global Spillover**: Kospi fell 6%, SK Hynix over 10%, FTSE 100 down 1.01%.
- **Political Trigger**: Investors feared a Democrat-controlled Congress would slow [[AI Regulation]]-sensitive development.
- **Spending Doubts**: A CEO called the move a "necessary market correction" on AI infrastructure outlays.
- **Market Purgatory**: Raw tech must now prove widespread economic value, per Neural Concept's CEO.
- **Jobs Cushion**: 165,000 new jobs and 4.1% unemployment suggest the real economy is resilient.
- **Next-Week Test**: AJ Bell said next week shows whether the tech rebound holds.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the central story of markets on this Friday?
**A**: AI and tech stocks bounced back modestly after a sell-off that wiped trillions off values, with the Nasdaq up 1.1% after a 5.5% fall. The rout was driven partly by midterm-election fears over AI regulation and doubts about AI spending returns.

**Q2**: What data points showed the uneven global impact and the economic backdrop?
**A**: Kospi closed down 6% and SK Hynix fell over 10%, while European indexes ended lower. Meanwhile US employers added 165,000 jobs in October, more than double expectations, with unemployment easing to 4.1%.

**Q3**: How should investors interpret the strong jobs figures against the tech sell-off?
**A**: Premier Miton's Neil Birrell argued a strong economy may mean the risks markets worry about are not such a threat to the real economy. Others said the following week would reveal whether the tech rebound is sustained.
