## Summary

[[Morgan Stanley]] and [[Bank of America]] reported diverging Q3 2026 results on October 14, closing out earnings from the six largest US banks. Morgan Stanley's net income rose to $4.97 billion on a $5.8 billion equity-trading beat and record $232 billion in wealth-management net new assets, while Bank of America's net income fell 17% after a $1.2 billion non-tax-deductible charge for alleged money-laundering and sanctions-compliance failures. Both stocks fell, with BofA slumping 7.2% early in New York trading.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Morgan Stanley]] trading beat**: Equity-trading revenue of $5.8B versus $4.9B expected; total trading $7.81B versus $7.31B estimate.
- **[[Wealth Management]] engine**: Division revenue rose to $9.1B from $8.2B, with record net new assets of $232B.
- **IPO outlook**: CFO [[Sharon Yeshaya]] expects Morgan Stanley to benefit if the [[IPO Market]] stays active over the next 12 months.
- **[[Bank of America]] regulatory charge**: $1.2B provision for [[Anti-Money Laundering]] and sanctions violations cut EPS by 17 cents.
- **BofA trading mix**: Fixed-income trading fell 17% to $2.55B; equity trading rose 10% to $2.51B.
- **BofA [[Investment Banking]]**: Revenue fell 11% to $1.82B (above expectations); [[M&A Advisory]] fees down 19%; equity issuance +9.1%, debt issuance -14%.
- **Peer context**: [[Goldman Sachs]], [[Citigroup]] and [[Wells Fargo]] beat estimates on Tuesday, while [[JPMorgan Chase]] missed profit expectations for the first time in nearly three years. See [[Bank Earnings Season]].
- **Macro backdrop**: Conflict in [[Iran]], elevated energy prices and a [[Global Bond Selloff]] weighed on markets and dealmaking.

## Key Takeaways

- **MS Profit**: Net income rose to $4.97B from $4.66B.
- **MS Equities**: $5.8B equity-trading revenue beat $4.9B estimate.
- **MS Wealth**: $9.1B revenue; record $232B net new assets.
- **MS Shares**: Fell 2.2% early despite the beat, after 5.1% gain through Tuesday.
- **BofA Charge**: $1.2B regulatory penalty cut EPS by 17 cents.
- **BofA Profit**: Net income down 17%; revenue near $30B, below consensus.
- **BofA FICC**: Fixed-income trading down 17% to $2.55B.
- **BofA Loans**: Loan balances up 6.3% to $1.23T, matching estimates.
- **BofA Shares**: Slumped 7.2% at 9:32 a.m. New York time.
- **JPMorgan Miss**: First profit miss versus estimates in nearly three years.
