## Summary

FinTech Global reports $541m raised across 16 fintech deals in the week to 25 October 2026, a sharp drop from $2.8bn the prior week, which was inflated by Ramp's $1.85bn round. Whop ($200m Series D at $5.1bn) and Salmon ($140m) made up more than half the total. Infrastructure and enterprise software led by subsector, AI-agent security and agentic finance featured heavily, and the US hosted 10 of 16 deals.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Fintech Funding]]**: $541m across 16 deals; Q3 2026 totalled $27.5bn across 897 deals, up 35% on Q3 2025, with average deal size of $30.6m versus $21.2m.
- **[[Whop]] and [[Salmon]]**: The only two rounds above $100m; Whop closed a Series D co-led by Sequoia and Shopify Ventures, and Salmon is a Philippine bank and lender using an AI-driven credit engine.
- **[[AI Agent Security]]**: [[Opsin]], [[Ultra Security]] and [[Asymptote Labs]] all raised to govern and monitor autonomous agents.
- **[[Agentic Finance]]**: [[Mural]], [[ScribeUp]], [[Plinto]] and [[Cognitive Credit]] apply agents to payments, bills, controlling and credit research.
- **[[Embedded Finance]] and [[Embedded Insurance]]**: [[CapitalOS]], [[Vrtx]], [[Slingshot Insurance]] and [[Cachet]] provide financial or insurance products inside other platforms.
- **[[Cash Flow Underwriting]]**: [[Prism Data]] raised $20m and launched REFRACT, a transaction-categorisation engine trained on 30 billion transactions.
- **[[RegTech]]**: Mural, Pennant and CoComply were the RegTech deals; Pennant applies AI to proxy voting.
- **[[WealthTech]]**: No deals this week despite Q3 funding tripling quarter on quarter to $2.8bn.

## Key Takeaways

- **Weekly Drop**: $541m across 16 deals versus $2.8bn across 17 the prior week.
- **Concentration**: Two deals, Whop and Salmon, accounted for more than half the total.
- **Whop Valuation**: $5.1bn Series D, months after a $1.6bn valuation in February.
- **Top Subsector**: Infrastructure and enterprise software took six deals; RegTech and CyberTech three each.
- **Geography**: The US had 10 of 16 deals; the UK was the only other country with multiple.
- **Global Spread**: Q3 top-ten deals spanned seven countries, including Saudi Arabia, Indonesia and Italy.
- **Agent Security Theme**: Three raises target runtime oversight of autonomous AI agents and MCP connectors.
- **Embedded Insurance Outlook**: Embedded policies are about 3% of P&C premiums, projected near 20% in ten years.
- **Q3 Funding**: $27.5bn across 897 deals, up 35% year on year.
