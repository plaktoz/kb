## Summary

Microsoft shares rose after the company announced a long-term AI partnership with Saudi-backed [[HUMAIN]] to bring Arabic-language AI tools to Foundry and 365 Copilot, adding to broader software-sector strength following Salesforce's strong earnings.

## Core Concepts

- [[HUMAIN]] — an AI company backed by Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, whose Arabic-language ALLAM models are being connected to Microsoft's Foundry platform and 365 Copilot.
- [[Moonshot Revenue-Sharing Talks]] — the Chinese AI startup behind Kimi K3 is reportedly negotiating with Microsoft, Amazon and Google for cloud placement in exchange for roughly 30% of resulting revenue, an unusually direct financial arrangement between a Chinese AI lab and major American cloud operators.
- [[Software Sector Sympathy Rally]] — Salesforce's earnings beat (four-year high in new order growth, Agentforce usage up sixfold) lifted broader software names, including Microsoft, independent of company-specific news.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal terms**: Microsoft and HUMAIN will pair HUMAIN's Arabic-language ALLAM models with Microsoft's Foundry platform and 365 Copilot, with more collaboration planned across models, hardware and infrastructure.
- **Enterprise angle**: Brad Smith described the pairing as combining Microsoft's AI systems with HUMAIN's Arabic-language expertise built to satisfy strict security and compliance demands from large enterprises and governments.
- **Moonshot negotiation**: Kimi K3 is independently benchmarked as competitive with leading American models (top performer on an Arena.ai web-interface benchmark); Moonshot is pushing for close to 30% revenue share from US cloud providers.
- **Price action**: MSFT shares were up 1.05% at $501.49 at time of publication, part of a broader software-sector rally following Salesforce's quarterly beat.
