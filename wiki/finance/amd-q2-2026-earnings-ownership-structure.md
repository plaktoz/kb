## Summary
AMD reports Q2 2026 earnings on August 4, with shares up more than 120% year-to-date on major customer wins and data center growth. Wall Street expects EPS of $1.62 (up 235% YoY) and revenue of $11.3 billion (up 47% YoY). Ownership is dominated by public and individual investors, with [[Vanguard]] the largest single shareholder, while analysts hold a Strong Buy consensus.
