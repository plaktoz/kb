## Summary

Dow futures surged ~550 points on July 27, 2026, as oil prices fell sharply following a weekend pause in U.S.-Iran hostilities, with Brent crude dropping over 9%. Markets were also positioning ahead of a critical week featuring megacap earnings from Amazon, Apple, Meta, and Microsoft, alongside a Federal Reserve rate decision. Investors remain caught between fears of runaway AI capex spending and the risk of those same companies cutting back, either outcome potentially destabilizing equities.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]** futures: +550 points (+1.1%); [[S&P 500]] futures +1%; [[Nasdaq-100]] futures +1.7%
- **[[US-Iran Conflict]]**: Weekend pause in attacks cooled geopolitical risk premium in oil markets
- **[[Oil Prices]]**: Brent crude -9.2% to ~$87.89/bbl; [[WTI Crude]] -7% to $82.46/bbl
- **[[Megacap Earnings]]**: [[Amazon]], [[Apple]], [[Meta Platforms]], [[Microsoft]] — results expected to either validate or challenge AI spending narratives
- **[[Federal Reserve]]** rate decision: consensus for September hike, but a July quarter-point hike is priced in as a meaningful possibility
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: The "biggest risk" framing — too much spend scares markets; too little spend also scares markets
- **[[Singapore MAS Policy]]**: Singapore tightened monetary policy preemptively against oil price risk; increased SGD NEER appreciation rate
- **[[China Industrial Profits]]**: Rose 15.1% YoY in June, slowing from May's 21.1% as AI-driven chip/equipment gains moderated
- **[[CXMT IPO]]**: China memory chipmaker debuted on Shanghai STAR Market, surging ~470%, raising $8.6B — Asia's largest IPO of 2026

## Key Takeaways

- **Futures Surge**: Dow +550 pts, S&P +1%, Nasdaq-100 +1.7% on geopolitical relief
- **Oil Plunge**: Brent -9.2%, WTI -7% as US-Iran pause removes risk premium
- **AI Spending Dilemma**: Cutting or growing AI capex both risk negative market reactions
- **Fed Watch**: July hike possibility live; September hike remains consensus
- **Back-to-Back Losses**: S&P -0.6%, Nasdaq -2.1% in the prior week
- **CXMT IPO**: $8.6B raised, +470% on debut — Asia's biggest IPO of the year
- **China Profits**: June industrial profit growth decelerates to 15.1% from 21.1%
- **Singapore Tightens**: MAS moves preemptively against oil-driven inflation risk

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: The AI capex paradox illustrates reflexivity — markets have priced in aggressive AI spending, so both maintaining and reducing it now triggers volatility, because investor expectations have become part of the underlying reality.
- **[[Risk Premium Repricing]]**: The sharp oil drop on a mere pause (not resolution) in conflict shows how quickly markets reprice geopolitical risk premiums when tension signals shift, even temporarily.
