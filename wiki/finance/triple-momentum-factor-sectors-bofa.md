## Summary

BofA's "triple momentum" screen — combining rising earnings expectations, 12-month price gains, and positive news flow — has outperformed negative-momentum stocks by 60 percentage points cumulatively over five years, with semiconductors, tech hardware and banks currently showing the strongest readings.

## Core Concepts

- [[Triple Momentum]] — BofA strategist Nigel Tupper's screen combining three momentum signals: earnings revisions, price trend, and news sentiment.
- [[Factor Persistence]] — the finding that positive-momentum stocks have beaten negative-momentum stocks by 11 points YTD and 60+ points over 5 years, despite short-term reversals (e.g. underperforming by 3.5 points in July).
- [[Sector Momentum Divergence]] — semiconductors/tech hardware and banks currently lead; media/entertainment, consumer staples and software lag.

## Key Takeaways

- **5-year record**: Triple-positive-momentum stocks beat triple-negative by 60.2 percentage points cumulatively.
- **Recent performance**: Outperformed the MSCI ACWI by 7.3 points over the past 3 months, despite a rough July.
- **Strongest sectors**: Semiconductors and tech hardware lead; global banks follow, boosted by Canadian lenders.
- **Weakest sectors**: Media/entertainment, consumer staples and software show the weakest momentum readings.
- **This holding's relevance**: Directly describes the factor (price + earnings + sentiment momentum) that momentum ETFs like SPMO and IWMO are built to capture.
