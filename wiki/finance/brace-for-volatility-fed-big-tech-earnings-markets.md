## Summary

Markets face a high-stakes week with the [[Federal Reserve]] policy meeting and earnings from major [[Magnificent Seven]] companies, coming after Alphabet and Tesla disappointed investors with elevated capex guidance. The probability of a surprise 25bp rate hike rose to 36% amid oil price spikes above $100/barrel and geopolitical instability in the Red Sea. Investor focus has shifted to whether [[AI Hyperscalers]] can justify their surging infrastructure spending with tangible returns.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Federal Reserve]] Rate Decision**: July meeting outcome uncertain; hike probability jumped from 13% to 36% in one week driven by inflation concerns.
- **[[AI Hyperscalers]] Capex Scrutiny**: [[Alphabet]] and [[Tesla]] sold off after reporting elevated capex outlooks; investors demanding clearer ROI on AI infrastructure.
- **[[Magnificent Seven]] Earnings**: [[Microsoft]], [[Meta]], [[Amazon]], and [[Apple]] all reporting within the week; results seen as a referendum on the AI trade.
- **Oil Prices and Inflation**: [[Brent Crude]] topped $100/barrel; Red Sea tanker attacks and Hormuz tensions stoking inflation fears.
- **[[CME FedWatch]] Tool**: Real-time market-implied Fed rate probabilities; July hike probability at ~36%.
- **[[David Rosenberg]]** (Rosenberg Research): Advising caution until a clear uptrend re-establishes.
- **[[Neil Dutta]]** (Renaissance Macro Research): Argues a hike now gives the Fed future flexibility.
- **[[Kevin Warsh]]** (Fed Chair): Reiterated zero tolerance for high inflation before Congress.

- [[Brent Crude]] broke $100/barrel for first time since May on war escalation.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Burden of Proof Shifts]]**: Once a narrative (AI capex = growth) is priced in, any data point that fails to confirm it causes outsized repricing — Alphabet beat estimates yet fell 7% purely on forward guidance, illustrating how the market now demands proof of ROI, not just spending commitment.
- **[[Risk-Off Contagion]]**: When the two largest market pillars (Fed policy and mega-cap tech earnings) are simultaneously uncertain, even positive macro news (Iran ceasefire) provides only partial relief — uncertainty compounds rather than diversifies.
