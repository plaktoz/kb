## Summary

US stock indices closed Friday September 26, 2026 higher, with the S&P 500 up 0.5% to 7,743.41 and the Dow up 0.9% to 51,828.62, as oil prices fell on signs of US-Iran diplomatic progress and semiconductor stocks extended gains. Iran's Foreign Minister announced a week-long proposal to resolve hostilities and unblock the [[Strait of Hormuz]], pushing WTI crude down ~2.3% to $92.41/barrel. Semiconductor ETFs SMH and SOXX each rose ~1.4%, tracking gains in [[Nvidia]], [[Broadcom]], and [[Micron]].

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Correlation vs. Causation in Markets]]**: Oil price drops and equity gains co-occurred on a geopolitical headline, but the 10-year yield simultaneously reached a 19-year high — illustrating that markets can compartmentalize macro signals, pricing geopolitical relief separately from rate risk.
- **[[Sentiment as a Short-Term Driver]]**: Iran's submission of a diplomatic proposal — with no confirmed resolution — was enough to move crude ~2.3%; markets price in probability shifts, not just outcomes.
