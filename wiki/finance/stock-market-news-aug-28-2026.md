## Summary

U.S. stocks ended sharply higher on August 28, 2026, as a tech-led rally powered all three major indexes into positive territory after [[NVIDIA]] reported blowout Q2 earnings and issued a robust 70% revenue-growth forecast for fiscal 2028. [[Salesforce]] compounded the enthusiasm with a massive earnings beat, while investors positioned ahead of Fed Chair [[Kevin Warsh]]'s Jackson Hole speech on Friday.

## Core Concepts

- **[[NVIDIA Earnings Beat]]**: NVIDIA reported Q2 EPS of $2.22 (vs. $2.09 estimate) and revenue of $96.22 billion, surpassing consensus by 4.82%, sending its stock up 8.7%.
- **[[AI Boom Validation]]**: NVIDIA's 70% revenue-growth forecast for fiscal 2028 reassured markets that the [[AI]] investment cycle is durable, not a bubble.
- **[[Semiconductor Sector Rally]]**: NVIDIA's blowout results lifted chipmakers broadly — [[Broadcom]] +4.5%, [[Intel]] +4.4%.
- **[[Salesforce Earnings]]**: Salesforce reported Q2 EPS of $5.90 (vs. $3.27 estimate) and revenues of $11.35 billion, sending shares up 22.6%.
- **[[Jackson Hole Symposium]]**: Markets awaited [[Kevin Warsh]]'s speech on Friday for clarity on the Fed's future monetary path.
- **[[Jobless Claims]]**: Initial claims fell 4,000 to 203,000 for the week ending Aug. 22, signaling continued labor market resilience.

## Key Takeaways

- DJIA gained 0.2% (+105.56 pts) to 53,569.44; S&P 500 jumped 0.7% (+55.29 pts) to 7,730.99; Nasdaq rose 1.6% (+411.16 pts) to 26,541.35.
- XLK (tech sector ETF) gained 3.2%; 10 of 11 S&P 500 sectors ended negative.
- VIX fell 4.60% to 14.51, reflecting reduced near-term fear.
- NVIDIA's results framed as proof the AI buildout is not a bubble.
- Salesforce's +22.6% surge was driven by a dramatic EPS beat ($5.90 vs. $3.27 estimate).
- Initial jobless claims at 203,000 — below the 4-week moving average of 205,500.
- Total shares traded (14.9B) came in below the 20-session average of 16.3B.
