## Summary
October PCE data showed sticky inflation (headline 3.8%, core 3.3%, the highest since November 2023), alongside Q3 GDP revised up to 4.3%. Wall Street opened sharply lower, with the Nasdaq down nearly 900 points and Nvidia and Anthropic plunging. Markets price a roughly 97.8% chance of a 25-basis-point Fed hike in December and another in January.

## Core Concepts
- [[PCE Price Index]]: the Fed's primary inflation gauge, above the 2% target since 2021.
- [[Federal Reserve]] and [[FOMC]]: December 8-9 meeting expected to raise the range to 4.00%-4.25%.
- [[Nvidia]] and [[Anthropic]]: Nvidia near $142 (down 34% in a month); Anthropic near $75, down over 8%.
- [[Tariffs]]: proposed on 14 trading partners; China faces an additional 20% on top of the 25% duty from 2018.
- [[US Dollar Index]]: back above 104, pressuring precious metals.
- [[GDP Growth]]: Q3 annualized 4.3%, fastest in three years.

## Key Takeaways
- **Headline PCE**: 3.8% year over year in October, up from 3.6%.
- **Core PCE**: 3.3% year over year, above the 3.2% forecast.
- **Open**: Dow down 800+ points, S&P 500 down ~2.5%, Nasdaq down 3.76%.
- **Fed Odds**: About 97.8% probability of a December 25bp hike.
- **Rate Path**: Fed funds at 3.75%-4.00% after September hike; December would lift to 4.00%-4.25%.
- **Tariffs**: New duties proposed on 14 partners; China's could rise to 40%.
- **Labour Market**: Strengthening signs and strong confidence support further hikes.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did the Nasdaq sink about 1,000 points on November 25?
**A**: Sticky October PCE inflation fuelled rate-hike fears, hitting AI stocks like Nvidia and Anthropic, while new tariff proposals added pressure.

**Q2**: What did the October PCE data show?
**A**: Headline PCE rose 3.8% year over year (from 3.6%) and core rose 3.3% (from 3.1%), above the 3.2% forecast and the highest core reading since November 2023.

**Q3**: What does the market expect from the Fed as a result?
**A**: Roughly a 97.8% chance of a 25-basis-point hike in December, with another expected in January, which would keep AI stocks and the broader market under pressure.
