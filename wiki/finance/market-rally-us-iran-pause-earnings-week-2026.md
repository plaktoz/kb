## Summary
US stock futures rallied sharply on Monday July 27 as the US and Iran announced a pause in hostilities, driving oil prices down ~9%. Markets face a demanding week with Magnificent Seven earnings (MSFT, AMZN, META, AAPL) and a potential Fed rate hike decision — the key test of whether AI capex spending is translating into growth.

## Core Concepts

- [[US-Iran Conflict 2026]] — nearly two weeks of escalating hostilities paused over the weekend, easing geopolitical risk premium
- [[Magnificent Seven Earnings]] — MSFT, AMZN, META, AAPL reporting this week; Alphabet already disappointed
- [[Federal Reserve 2026]] — markets pricing in possible rate hike as soon as Wednesday; consensus expects September hike
- [[AI Capex Overspend Debate]] — key investor concern: are hyperscalers spending too much on AI infrastructure without sufficient revenue return?
- [[Singapore MAS]] — tightened monetary policy for second consecutive time as oil prices rekindle inflation risk
- [[CXMT IPO]] — China memory chipmaker debuted +470% on Shanghai STAR Market, raising $8.6bn; Asia's largest IPO of 2026

## Key Takeaways

- **S&P 500 futures +1%**, Nasdaq-100 futures +1.7% at open; oil (Brent) -9.2% to $87.89/barrel
- **Three consecutive weeks of declines** for S&P 500 heading into this week
- **Treasury yields fell**: 10-year at 4.63%, signaling rate pause expectations
- **Seesaw risk**: if hyperscalers cut capex, semiconductors sell off; if they don't cut, investors worry about sustainability
- **Ukraine-Iran escalation**: Ukraine struck Iranian vessels in Caspian Sea, complicating the ceasefire picture
- **Asia-Pacific markets all closed green** on the US-Iran pause

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reflexivity]]**: The AI capex debate has a self-fulfilling quality — if investors expect capex cuts, companies may preemptively reduce them to appease markets, which then reduces AI infrastructure buildout, validating the original fear.
