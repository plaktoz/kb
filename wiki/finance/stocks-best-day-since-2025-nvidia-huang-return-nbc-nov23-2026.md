## Summary
US stocks posted their best day since April 2025 on Nov. 23, 2026, with the S&P 500 up 4.7% and the Nasdaq 100 up almost 7%, after [[Jensen Huang]] was reinstated at [[Nvidia]]. Nvidia rose 31.5%, the largest one-day value gain in market history, but analysts warn volatility may persist and the Nasdaq 100 remains in correction.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Reinstated as Nvidia chief after a brief removal following an admitted affair; a woman involved was reportedly a suspected Chinese asset.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Added about $950 billion in value in one day, yet remains 30% below its Oct. 6 record.
- **[[Stock Market Correction]]**: The S&P 500 exited correction territory; the Nasdaq 100 is still down almost 15%.
- **[[AI Trade]]**: Semiconductors (+14%), memory stocks (+18%), Dell (+21%), Oracle, CoreWeave and Nebius rallied.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: Its market debut triggered the prior week's selling; shares fell 1.4% Monday.
- **[[Donald Trump]]**: Took credit for pushing Huang's return.
- **[[SEC Investigation]]**: Reported probe of Nvidia over the Huang revelations.
- **[[Semiconductor Export Controls]]**: Feared tighter controls hurt chip demand and prices.
- **[[Ed Yardeni]]**, **[[Jim Reid]]**, **[[Jonathan Krinsky]]**: Analysts offering cautious reads on the rebound.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index moves**: S&P 500 +4.7%, Nasdaq 100 nearly +7%, Dow about +1,450 points.
- **Nvidia record**: +31.5% added about $950 billion; tech sector gained about $2.7 trillion.
- **Key-person overhang**: Stifel said Huang's return removes a key-person overhang on AI infrastructure.
- **Prior rout**: Nasdaq 100 had its worst week since 2020; S&P fell seven straight days.
- **Still scarred**: Nvidia down 30% from its record; Nasdaq 100 still in correction.
- **Volatility lingers**: Reid says similar shocks typically take 6-8 weeks to subside.
- **Caution**: Krinsky says extreme one-day gains often occur in downtrending markets.
- **Forecast cut**: Yardeni lowered his 2026 S&P 500 target to 7,500, citing no impending recession.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Dependence on one leader concentrates risk in the company and its ecosystem** — When a firm's strategy and market confidence hinge on a single person, that person's removal or return becomes a sector-wide price driver. · *model:* [[Key Person Risk]]
→ explains: Key-person overhang, Nvidia record

**T2. Volatility shocks decay slowly, so one rebound day doesn't end the turbulence** — Large moves tend to be followed by more large moves until a durable bottom forms; extreme up-days often occur within downtrends. · *model:* [[Volatility Clustering]]
→ explains: Volatility lingers, Caution

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did US stocks surge on Nov. 23, 2026?
**A**: Wall Street cheered Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, which sent Nvidia up 31.5% and lifted the whole tech sector. It also followed a sharp AI-led selloff the prior week.

**Q2**: How large was Nvidia's move, and what does it show?
**A**: Nvidia added about $950 billion in value, the largest one-day gain in market history, and tech gained about $2.7 trillion. Even so, Nvidia is still down 30% from its Oct. 6 high.

**Q3**: Should investors treat the rally as the end of the correction?
**A**: Not necessarily. Reid notes volatility shocks typically take 6-8 weeks to subside, and Krinsky says extreme one-day gains are more often seen in downtrending markets.
