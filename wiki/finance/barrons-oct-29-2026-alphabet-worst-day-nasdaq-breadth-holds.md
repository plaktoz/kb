## Summary

On Oct. 29, 2026, megacap tech losses dragged all three major indexes lower, led by [[Alphabet]]'s worst trading day in its 22 years as a public company. Beneath the index-level weakness, most S&P 500 stocks and sectors finished higher, with small- and mid-caps gaining. A strategist argued that strong AI demand no longer guarantees strong stock performance as the market re-underwrites capex risk.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] sell-off**: Worst trading day since its public listing, hitting the communications sector hardest.
- **[[Nasdaq Composite]]**: Fell 1.4%, while the [[S&P 500]] and [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] each fell 0.3% (132 points for the Dow).
- **[[Market Breadth]]**: Most S&P 500 stocks and most sectors ended higher despite the index decline.
- **[[Small-Cap Stocks]]**: The [[Russell 2000]] and S&P 400 ended the day higher.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: Investors are re-underwriting the headline risk of heavy AI capex.
- **[[Will McGough]]**: Prime Capital Financial strategist who framed the shift in market attitude toward AI demand.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index Losses**: Nasdaq fell 1.4%; S&P 500 and Dow each fell 0.3%.
