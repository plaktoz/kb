## Summary

Fiverr's stock collapsed more than 20% after lackluster Q2 2026 earnings, with revenue and EPS both missing estimates and declining year-over-year. CEO Micha Kaufman attributed the drop to accelerating AI adoption compressing demand for low-value transactional freelance work.

## Core Concepts

[[Fiverr]]'s earnings miss is a concrete data point in the broader debate over [[AI Disruption|AI's impact]] on the [[Gig Economy]]. As [[Large Language Models]] improve, customers are increasingly automating the kind of low-value, transactional work that once flowed to freelance marketplaces, while high-value creative and specialized work — only 15% of Fiverr's completed project value — continues to grow as a share of the business.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock drop**: FVRR shares fell over 20% after Q2 2026 earnings.
- **Revenue miss**: $97.78M vs. $100.38M estimate (-2.59%), down 10% year-over-year.
- **EPS miss**: $0.50 vs. $0.52 estimate, down from $0.69 a year earlier.
- **Root cause per CEO**: "Ongoing compression in high volume, low value transactional work driven by accelerating AI adoption."
- **Bright spot**: High-value work is only 15% of gross order value today but is expected to keep growing as transactional work keeps compressing.
