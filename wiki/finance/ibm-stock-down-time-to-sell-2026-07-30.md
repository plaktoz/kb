## Summary

IBM's share price fell 2.1% on July 30, 2026, closing at $221.77, weighed down by a Q2 revenue miss ($17.16B vs $17.46B expected) and concerns about weak generative-AI visibility and a slowdown in the IBM Z product line. Analyst sentiment is mixed — a "Moderate Buy" consensus with a $265.40 average price target — while multiple analyst downgrades and securities-law investigations add headline risk. Positive catalysts include quantum-advantage results and rising demand for AI-enabled cybersecurity, though neither offsets near-term growth concerns.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM]] (NYSE: IBM)** — legacy enterprise technology company with cloud, AI consulting, and quantum computing divisions
- **[[IBM Quantum]]** — IBM's quantum computing platform; reported multiple quantum-advantage results with partners [[Algorithmiq]], [[Qedma]], [[RIKEN]], and [[University of Chicago]]
- **[[Generative AI]]** consulting demand — flagged as less visible in the latest earnings discussion, a key investor concern
- **[[IBM Z]]** — IBM's mainframe product line; alleged slowdown under securities-law investigation
- **[[Cost of a Data Breach Report]]** — IBM's 2026 research showing AI-enabled attacks are costlier than average, a tailwind for cybersecurity consulting
- **[[Circle]]** — acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio (~1,000 issued patents) at undisclosed terms
- **[[Analyst Ratings]]** — Piper Sandler (overweight), DZ Bank (buy/$295 PT), Oppenheimer (downgrade to market perform), Wolfe Research (peer perform)
