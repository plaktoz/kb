## Summary
Midday on Oct. 20, 2026, all three major indexes were higher, led by a 523-point gain in the [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]], on an oil price pivot and upbeat earnings. The [[Russell 2000]] was heading for its best session since Aug. 4. Stock-specific moves included 3M and Eli Lilly higher and Nuscale lower after a downgrade.

## Core Concepts
- [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]], [[S&P 500]] and [[Nasdaq Composite]]: the Nasdaq and S&P pared modest morning losses; the Dow led.
- [[Russell 2000]]: small caps eyeing their best day since Aug. 4.
- [[Crude Oil Prices]]: front-month crude neared $110/barrel in the morning, then fell 2.5%.
- [[3M]] (MMM): up 6% to $171.91 on a Q3 beat-and-raise; options volume triple average, led by the November 175 call.
- [[Eli Lilly]] (LLY): up 5.4% to $1,304.31 after a record high of $1,311.75; Goldman Sachs raised its target to $1,413.
- [[Nuscale Power]] (SMR): down 0.2% to $5.69 after a Barclays downgrade to underweight with target cut to $5 from $11.

## Key Takeaways
- **Index Move**: Dow up 523 points midday, leading all three major indexes higher.
- **Small Caps**: Russell 2000 on pace for its best day since Aug. 4.
- **Oil Pivot**: Crude fell 2.5% after nearing $110 earlier in the session.
- **3M**: Shares up 6% on earnings beat-and-raise; up 6.3% year to date.
- **Eli Lilly**: Record high, up 20% in 2026, supported at its 100-day average.
- **Nuscale**: Down 60% year to date; Barclays cites slower milestones and execution risk.
