## Summary

[[Visa]] is aggressively expanding into stablecoins through three major initiatives: a Wallet-as-a-Service platform for banks, an on-chain lending rail powered by smart contracts, and a rapidly scaling card program network. Stablecoin settlement volume has grown more than 15x year-over-year and now exceeds a $20 billion annualized run rate, signaling Visa's intent to bridge traditional finance with blockchain infrastructure.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Visa Stablecoin Platform]] (VSP)**: Launched July 2026, VSP offers a "Wallet-as-a-Service" toolkit for banks and fintechs, initially supporting [[Open USD]] with [[USDC]] integration planned.
- **[[On-Chain Lending Rail]]**: Announced September 8, 2026 — pairs [[VisaNet]] settlement data with [[Smart Contracts]] to enable blockchain-based short-term credit.
- **[[Credit Coop]]**: Visa's partner that has facilitated over $2.5 billion in settlement financing since 2023 with zero defaults.
- **[[Stablecoins]]**: Digital assets pegged to fiat currencies (e.g. USD) used as a bridge between crypto and traditional payment rails.
- **Crypto-to-Fiat Bridge**: Stablecoin holders can spend via standard Visa cards while merchants automatically receive local fiat — eliminating friction at the consumer/merchant interface.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: 160+ stablecoin-linked card programs live globally; volume up ~200% year-over-year.
- **Settlement Volume**: Annualized stablecoin settlement run rate surpassed $20 billion.
- **Industry Volume**: Organic stablecoin payment volume exceeds $350 billion monthly industry-wide.
- **On-Chain Credit**: Smart contract + VisaNet pairing enables blockchain-based short-term credit.
- **Zero Defaults**: Credit Coop's $2.5B in settlement financing since 2023 has had no defaults.
- **Consumer UX**: Seamless spend — crypto in, local fiat out — removes the biggest consumer friction point.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Network Effects]]**: Visa's 160+ stablecoin card programs and massive merchant acceptance create a self-reinforcing loop — more users attract more programs, which attract more merchants, compounding adoption.
- **[[Platform Strategy]]**: By offering Wallet-as-a-Service to banks and fintechs rather than competing with them directly, Visa extends its infrastructure layer into crypto rails while locking in institutional partners.
