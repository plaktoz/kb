## Summary

Southeast Asia's native AI startups have raised $4.1 billion in 2026, more than double 2025's full-year total, though the surge is heavily concentrated in a single $2.8 billion round and in Singapore, which accounts for nearly all of the region's disclosed funding.

## Core Concepts

- [[Kling AI]] — raised a $2.8B Series D, accounting for ~68% of Southeast Asia's 2026 native AI funding.
- [[Capital Concentration]] — funding more than doubling while the number of rounds falls sharply signals larger bets on fewer companies, not broader acceleration.
- [[AI Infrastructure Investment]] — AI Infrastructure and Data Center Infrastructure segments together account for more than 65% of the ecosystem's total equity funding.

## Key Takeaways

- **Headline number**: $4.1B raised across 23 disclosed rounds in 2026, vs. $2B across 41 rounds in 2025.
- **Concentration risk**: Excluding Kling AI's mega-round, the region raised only ~$1.3B — a more modest picture.
- **Singapore dominance**: The city-state accounts for essentially all of Southeast Asia's $9.3B in cumulative native AI funding.
- **Segment leaders**: AI Infrastructure ($4.3B/56 rounds) and Data Center Infrastructure ($2.2B/4 rounds, all Princeton Digital Group) dominate.
