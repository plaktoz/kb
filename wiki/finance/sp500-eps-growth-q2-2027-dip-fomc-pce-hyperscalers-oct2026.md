## Summary
The article tracks S&P 500 EPS growth expectations, noting Q2 2027 is projected at just +3.6%, the weakest since Q2 2023, while Q3 and Q4 2027 look healthy. It previews a busy October 28 with September PCE, the FOMC decision and Alphabet and Microsoft earnings. WTI crude above $103 remains a concern, though below the April all-time high.

## Core Concepts
- **[[S&P 500]] EPS growth estimates**: Expected y-o-y growth by quarter through early 2028, sourced from LSEG.
- **[[Anthropic]] mark-ups**: Non-operating gains at [[Alphabet]] and [[Amazon]] distort Q1 and Q2 2027 year-over-year comparisons.
- **[[FOMC]]**: October 28 hold is near-certain; a 25 bp hike to a 5% midpoint is expected December 9.
- **[[PCE Inflation]]**: September PCE expected at 0.4% and Core PCE at 0.3%.
- **[[Hyperscalers]]**: Alphabet and Microsoft report October 28; Amazon October 29.
- **[[Crude Oil]]**: WTI above $103 versus the $113 record in early April 2026.

## Key Takeaways
- **Q2 '27 Dip**: Expected EPS growth is +3.6%, lowest since Q2 2023's -2.8%.
- **Q1 '27 Recovery**: Expected growth fell to 12.1% after mark-ups, then rebounded to 17.1% by July 31.
- **Historical Average**: Quarterly EPS growth averaged 7.3% (Q4 2011 to Q4 2019).
- **Recent Average**: 19.8% from Q1 2022 to Q2 2026, excluding COVID quarters.
- **FOMC Outlook**: Hike unlikely October 28 near the midterms; December 25 bp hike expected.
- **Data Calendar**: Q3 GDP expected at +3%, with PCE the likelier bond-market mover.
- **Oil Watch**: WTI above $103 stays contained unless it makes a new all-time high.
