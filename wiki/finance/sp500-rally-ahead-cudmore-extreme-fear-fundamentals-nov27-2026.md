## Summary
Bloomberg analyst [[Mark Cudmore]] argues the [[S&P 500]] is set to rally because earnings, oil prices, bond yields, and GDP data all look positive. Meanwhile the [[Fear and Greed Index]] sits in extreme fear after about $20 trillion was wiped from global stocks since September. He expects investors returning from Thanksgiving to chase a higher market.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Mark Cudmore]]**: Bloomberg analyst who laid out the bullish catalysts.
- **[[S&P 500]] earnings season**: [[FactSet]] data shows 32.9% earnings growth, a third straight quarter above 25%.
- **[[Oil Prices]] and [[Treasury Yields]]**: Brent and WTI down over 22% from October highs; 10-year yield at 4.89%, 5-year at 4.70%.
- **[[GDP Growth]]**: third-quarter growth revised to 4.3% from 3.4%; [[Goldman Sachs]] expects 2.4% growth in 2027.
- **[[Fear and Greed Index]]**: in extreme fear, with momentum, breadth, options, and safe-haven demand all in the red.
- **[[Fund Flows]]**: substantial inflows this month into [[Invesco QQQ]] and [[Vanguard VOO]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Earnings**: S&P 500 earnings growth of 32.9%, slightly above expectations.
- **Energy and Rates**: Oil down over 22% from October peak; yields turning lower.
- **Macro**: Q3 GDP grew 4.3% versus a prior estimate of 3.4%.
- **Sentiment Gap**: Fear and Greed Index in extreme fear despite positive fundamentals.
- **Drawdown**: About $20 trillion wiped from global stocks since September.
- **Flows**: QQQ and VOO have seen substantial inflows this month.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Sentiment extremes that diverge from fundamentals can precede reversals** — When fear is extreme while earnings and macro data are strong, panic selling can overshoot, and bull markets have often begun from such conditions. · *model:* [[Contrarian Investing]]
→ explains: Sentiment Gap, Drawdown, Flows

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Mark Cudmore's central argument about the S&P 500?
**A**: Almost everything fundamentally looks positive, so the market should rally even though sentiment is extremely fearful.

**Q2**: What data supports the bullish fundamental case?
**A**: S&P 500 earnings growth of 32.9%, oil down over 22% from October highs, falling bond yields, and Q3 GDP growth of 4.3% versus 3.4% previously estimated.

**Q3**: How does the extreme fear reading factor into the outlook?
**A**: Extreme fear helps explain the recent drop through panic selling, but bull markets have often started in such conditions, and Cudmore expects investors returning from Thanksgiving to chase the move higher.
