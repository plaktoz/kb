## Summary
Fed officials are stressing the need for more tightening, shifting market attention from whether rates will rise to how quickly and how often. Hotter-than-expected October CPI and PPI readings have raised the odds of hikes beyond December, with talk of a possible half-point move. Forecasters differ: Bank of America expects three straight quarter-point hikes, while Santander expects two.

## Core Concepts
- [[Federal Reserve]]: officials increasingly emphasize further tightening to return inflation to the 2% target.
- [[John Williams]]: New York Fed president; says the economy and labor market are solid but more work remains on inflation.
- [[Lorie Logan]]: Dallas Fed president; says at least two more quarter-point hikes are needed and the Fed could move faster this winter.
- [[Christopher Waller]]: Fed governor; raised the possibility of a 0.5 percentage-point hike in December.
- [[Susan Collins]]: Boston Fed president; says direction is clear but pace and end point are uncertain.
- [[Consumer Price Index]]: October CPI rose 0.4% (forecast 0.3%); core rose 0.3% (forecast 0.2%).
- [[Producer Price Index]]: October PPI rose 0.3% after September's 1.4%; annual gain of 6.5% is the highest since November 2022.
- [[Bank of America]] and [[Santander]]: diverging forecasts for the pace of tightening.
- [[Federal Open Market Committee]]: December meeting is the next decision point, with January and March also in play.

## Key Takeaways
- **Hawkish Chorus**: Williams, Goolsbee, Logan, Waller and Collins all signaled further tightening or concern.
- **December Hike**: Widely viewed as all but certain; base case is 0.25 points.
- **Larger Move**: Waller raised a 0.5-point December hike if price pressures fail to ease.
- **Hot CPI**: October CPI up 0.4% versus 0.3% expected; core up 0.3% versus 0.2%.
- **PPI Pressure**: Annual PPI hit 6.5%, highest since November 2022.
- **BofA Forecast**: Quarter-point hikes in December, January and March, lifting the range to 4.50%-4.75%.
- **Santander Forecast**: Only two hikes, in December and March.
- **Current Range**: Benchmark target is 3.75%-4.00% today.
- **Caution Inside Fed**: Collins urges care on size and speed of future moves.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the central shift in market expectations described in the article?
**A**: Attention has moved from whether the Fed will raise rates to how quickly and how often, with hikes now discussed for December, January and March. Fed officials have repeatedly stressed the need for additional tightening.

**Q2**: What data supported the hawkish case?
**A**: October CPI rose 0.4% versus a 0.3% forecast, with core CPI up 0.3% versus 0.2%. October PPI rose 0.3% month over month, and its 6.5% annual gain was the highest since November 2022.

**Q3**: How do forecasts for the policy path differ, and what would the endpoint be?
**A**: Bank of America expects three quarter-point hikes (December, January, March), taking the range from 3.75%-4.00% to 4.50%-4.75%. Santander expects only two, in December and March.
