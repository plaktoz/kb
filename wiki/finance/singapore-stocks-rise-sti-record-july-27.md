## Summary

The Straits Times Index rose 0.6% to 5,620.24 on Monday, led by Seatrium's 8.9% jump after the company guided to a "material" year-on-year profit improvement, while regional markets broadly advanced as Hong Kong, Japan, South Korea and Malaysia indices also gained. All three Singapore local banks (DBS, OCBC, UOB) ended higher on the day.
