## Summary
Chandler Asset Management's weekly note says October inflation data showed price pressures are not fully eased, with CPI above expectations, while housing and small business indicators softened. Hawkish comments from Fed officials pushed Treasury yields up, though the S&P 500 gained on strong earnings. Chandler expects two more quarter-point hikes, in December and early 2027.

## Core Concepts
- [[Consumer Price Index]]: headline up 0.4% (consensus 0.3%, September 0.6%); core up 0.3%, above expectations.
- [[Producer Price Index]]: headline up 0.3% (forecast 0.4%); year-over-year 6.5%, up from 6.3%.
- [[Federal Reserve]] officials [[Christopher Waller]] and [[Lorie Logan]]: signaling further [[Rate Hikes]].
- [[Treasury Yields]]: 2-year at 4.84%, 10-year at 5.13%, 2s/10s spread at 29 basis points.
- [[Housing Market]]: existing home sales down 5.5% and 30-year mortgage rate at 7.11%.
- [[Chandler Asset Management]]: favors credit quality, liquidity and duration management.

## Key Takeaways
- **Inflation**: October CPI rose 0.4% and core CPI 0.3%, both above consensus.
- **Fed Tone**: Waller floated a 50bp December move; Logan wants two or more quarter-point hikes.
- **Yields**: The 2-year rose 33bp and the 10-year 27bp on the week.
- **Housing**: Existing home sales fell 5.5% to 3.75 million annualized.
- **Small Business**: NFIB Optimism slipped to 97.8 from 97.9.
- **Equities**: S&P 500 gained 2.2% with Q3 earnings growth estimated at 31.4%.
- **Outlook**: Chandler expects hikes in December and early 2027.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the note's main message about inflation and the Fed?
**A**: Inflation remains a concern, with October CPI above expectations and Fed officials turning hawkish, so Chandler expects two more quarter-point hikes, in December and early 2027.

**Q2**: What data showed economic softness?
**A**: Existing home sales fell 5.5% to 3.75 million annualized, the NFIB Small Business Optimism Index slipped to 97.8, and mortgage rates stayed elevated at 7.11%.

**Q3**: How does Chandler position its investment approach given this outlook?
**A**: It emphasizes credit quality and liquidity, manages duration to each client's objectives, and seeks attractive risk-adjusted returns while staying flexible.
