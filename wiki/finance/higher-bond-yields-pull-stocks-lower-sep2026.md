## Summary

U.S. stocks fell on September 29, 2026, as the 10-year Treasury yield briefly topped 5.27% — its highest in roughly two decades — pressured by inflation worries, U.S. debt load, and a resilient economy. The S&P 500 dropped 0.8% to 7,683.69, the Dow lost 347 points, and the Nasdaq fell 0.9%, with oil-price swings tied to the Strait of Hormuz standoff and a 3.5% slide in gold adding to the pressure.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: The 10-year yield briefly topped 5.27% before settling at 5.23%, a level last seen before the 2007 financial crisis; the 30-year hit 5.55%, a 2004-era level.
- **[[Strait of Hormuz]]**: Oil-tanker flows remain disrupted amid the [[Iran]] conflict; [[Donald Trump]] rejected an Iranian offer to reopen the strait and resume nuclear talks.
- **[[Gold Price Decline]]**: Gold sank 3.5% as rising yields made non-yielding assets like gold less attractive relative to bonds.
- **[[Nvidia]]**: Rose 1.7% after its board approved up to $150 billion in additional stock buybacks, bringing the total remaining buyback authorization to $235 billion.
- **[[MongoDB]]**: Fell 18.5% after CEO Chirantan "CJ" Desai announced his immediate departure to take a senior role at [[Meta Platforms]].
- **[[AI Safety]]**: AI stocks broadly weakened after industry leaders said development needs to slow to let safety measures catch up.

## Key Takeaways

- **S&P 500 fell 0.8%** to 7,683.69, giving back most of last week's gains near record highs.
- **10-year Treasury yield hit 5.27%**, its highest level in about two decades, before easing to 5.23%.
- **Brent crude settled at $97.83**, still far above the ~$72 level before the US-Israel-Iran conflict began.
- **Airlines fell** on fuel-cost worries: American Airlines -2.5%, United Airlines -2.2%.
- **Nvidia rose 1.7%** on a new $150 billion buyback approval and a new AI-agent security platform.
- **MongoDB dropped 18.5%** on its CEO's abrupt departure to Meta.
