## Summary

Major indexes were mixed as chip stocks dragged the Nasdaq while oil prices plunged nearly 9% on a US-Iran de-escalation, with Apple briefly reclaiming the market-cap crown from Nvidia. A third of the S&P 500 reports earnings this week, and traders now price a 38% chance of a Fed rate hike, up sharply from 16% a week earlier.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nasdaq Composite]]** — pulled back 0.2% as chip stocks weighed on the index
- **[[Apple]]** — closed at a record high, intraday market cap topping [[Nvidia]]'s
- **[[Federal Reserve]]** — rate-hike odds for this week's meeting jumped to 38%
- **[[Oil Prices]]** — WTI and Brent crude plunged 7.8% and 9.1% on Iran de-escalation
- **[[US-Iran Conflict]]** — pause in hostilities drove the oil-price reversal

## Key Takeaways

- **Oil (WTI/Brent) plunged ~8-9%** on US-Iran de-escalation.
- **Apple's market cap topped Nvidia's** as chip stocks sold off.
- **Fed rate-hike odds jumped to 38%** from 16% a week earlier.
- **10-year Treasury yield eased to ~4.64%** after nearing a 2025 high.
