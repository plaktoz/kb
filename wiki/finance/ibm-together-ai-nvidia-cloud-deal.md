## Summary

[[IBM]] signed a multi-year, $240 million agreement with [[Together AI]] to deploy a large cluster of [[NVIDIA]] HGX B300 systems on IBM Cloud, expanding IBM's enterprise AI-inference infrastructure and deepening its broader NVIDIA partnership across GPU-accelerated analytics, hybrid cloud, and consulting. The deal positions IBM to compete with Microsoft and Amazon for enterprise AI infrastructure spend, though IBM shares have lagged the industry over the past year and the stock carries a Hold rating.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Together AI]]** — an open-source AI inference/cloud provider that will run enterprise-grade workloads on IBM Cloud infrastructure under this deal.
- **[[NVIDIA HGX B300]]** — the GPU system cluster IBM will deploy, paired with NVIDIA's Spectrum-X Ethernet networking for high-performance AI inference.
- **IBM-NVIDIA partnership expansion** — spans GPU-accelerated data analytics, unstructured data processing, hybrid cloud infrastructure, and consulting services.
- **Competitive landscape** — Microsoft (Copilot, Maia 300 custom chip) and Amazon (Bedrock, SageMaker) are named as IBM's primary AI-infrastructure competitors.

## Key Takeaways

- **$240 million, multi-year agreement** between IBM and Together AI for enterprise AI inference capacity.
- **Deal expands IBM Cloud's open-source AI workload offerings**, targeting cost-effective, large-scale inference.
- **IBM trades at a forward price-to-sales ratio of 3.07**, below the industry average of 5.32.
- **IBM shares lost 0.5% over the past year** versus 190.8% industry growth — a significant underperformance gap.
- **2026 and 2027 EPS estimates have both declined** slightly (0.2% and 1.4% respectively) over the past 60 days.
- **IBM currently carries a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold)**.
