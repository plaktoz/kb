## Summary

IBM announced a strategic partnership with OpenAI to embed frontier models like GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage, launching a dedicated OpenAI Practice and joining OpenAI's Elite partner tier to help enterprises modernize workflows and manage AI-related cybersecurity risk.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM Consulting Advantage]]**: IBM's AI delivery platform for consulting services, which will embed OpenAI's frontier models and products (GPT-5.6, Codex, ChatGPT Work) to analyze workflows and automate finance, procurement, customer operations, and HR processes.
- **[[OpenAI Elite Partner Tier]]**: IBM will join this tier, reflecting a deep strategic commitment; IBM is also launching a dedicated OpenAI Practice with thousands of consultants certified through the OpenAI Partner Network.
- **[[IBM Autonomous Security]]**: A multi-agent-powered service that will combine with OpenAI frontier AI and the OpenAI Daybreak Cyber Partner Program to help clients manage both cyber risk and AI model risk.
- **[[Three Partnership Focus Areas]]**: (1) transforming legacy operations into AI-ready workflows, (2) application modernization and product development, and (3) cybersecurity and AI risk management.

## Key Takeaways

- **Joint go-to-market initiatives** target financial services, government, telecom, and retail, plus cross-industry finance, procurement, customer operations, and HR functions.
- **Forward-deployed engineering units**, trained via the OpenAI Partner Network, will work directly with clients in complex, highly regulated environments.
- **This follows IBM's earlier participation** in the OpenAI Daybreak Cyber Partner Program, extending that cybersecurity collaboration into a broader enterprise AI deployment partnership.
- IBM SVP Andy Baldwin: "The challenge is not access to AI technologies — it's integrating AI securely and at scale into complex enterprise environments and workflows."
