## Summary

US stocks closed higher on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, as tech stocks rebounded from an earlier sell-off and falling bond yields eased investor concerns, with all three major indexes gaining for a third straight session. The rally was led by a chip-stock rebound ahead of Nvidia's earnings, even as consumer confidence dropped and US-Canada trade tensions escalated.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Chip Stock Rally]]**: Nvidia, Micron, and AMD all rose ahead of [[Nvidia]]'s quarterly earnings, pulling the Nasdaq up 0.7%.
- **[[Bond Yield Relief]]**: The 10-year Treasury yield fell over 7 basis points to 4.625% after a report that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent could tap nearly $1 trillion in General Account funds for bond buybacks.
- **[[US-Canada Trade War]]**: Canada imposed retaliatory tariffs on US imports, adding to investor caution despite the day's gains.
- **[[Jackson Hole Symposium]]**: Markets awaited a speech from Fed Chair [[Kevin Warsh]] at the Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium.

## Key Takeaways

- Dow gained 0.3% to 53,577.40; S&P 500 rose 0.3% to 7,677.24; Nasdaq jumped 0.7% to 26,151.30.
- VIX fell 2.52% to 15.45, signaling reduced near-term fear.
- Chip rally ahead of Nvidia earnings drove tech and communication services gains.
- Consumer confidence fell to a seven-month low of 89.4 in August.
- Home prices rose 0.4% month over month and 1.5% year over year (Case-Shiller).
