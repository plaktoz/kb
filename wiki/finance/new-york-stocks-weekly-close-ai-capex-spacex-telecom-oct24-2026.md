## Summary
US stocks ended the week lower as selling in technology stocks over AI investment concerns spread to other sectors, while Middle East tensions pushed Brent crude above $117. Mixed AI earnings and a SpaceX-driven telecom selloff added to the pressure. Investors now look to Big Tech earnings and the Fed's October 27-28 meeting.

## Core Concepts
- [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]]: down 1.23% (652.82 points) to 52,215.16; the [[S&P 500]] fell 0.52% to 7,623.68; the [[Nasdaq Composite]] fell 0.21% to 25,955.34.
- [[VIX]]: the "fear index" rose 2.21% to 19.45.
- [[AI Capital Expenditure]]: SpaceX and Super Micro results lifted optimism, while Tesla and Meta results reignited spending concerns.
- [[SpaceX]]: reported talks with cellular infrastructure companies on a nationwide US wireless network.
- [[Brent Crude]]: above $117 on Middle East tensions and energy supply concerns, heightening inflation worries.
- [[Federal Reserve]]: meeting October 27-28; markets price an unchanged rate.
- [[University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment]]: revised up to 48 for October, still a five-month low.

## Key Takeaways
- **Sector Contagion**: Tech selling over AI investment spread to other sectors.
- **Telecom Hit**: AT&T fell 7.96%, T-Mobile 7.71%, Verizon 1.18%; SpaceX rose 1.89%.
- **Mixed AI Earnings**: SpaceX and Super Micro beat; Tesla and Meta renewed capex worries.
- **Amex Cuts**: American Express fell 3.62% on a roughly 5% workforce reduction.
- **Yields**: The 10-year Treasury hovered at 4.99% after dipping below 5% Thursday.
- **Europe Lower**: Stoxx Europe 600 down 0.31% to 635.77; Italy's and Spain's moves diverged.
- **Next Catalysts**: Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon earnings and the Fed meeting.

**A**: Selling in tech stocks over AI investment concerns spread to other sectors, while Middle East tensions pushed oil prices higher.

**A**: Earnings from Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon, plus the Fed's October 27-28 meeting where an unchanged rate is expected. Oil above $117 has heightened inflation concerns heading into it.
