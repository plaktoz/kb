## Summary

[[Rocket Lab]] completed a $1.94 billion at-the-market equity offering (29.3 million shares) to fund its pending acquisition of [[Iridium Communications]], covering the full purchase price plus partial debt repayment. The company simultaneously amended its $1.77 billion credit facility and canceled a $3.6 billion bridge loan originally secured for the deal. The acquisition is expected to close mid-2027, pending an Iridium shareholder vote on September 24 and regulatory approval.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Rocket Lab]] (RKLB)**: Commercial launch company pursuing an end-to-end space business; currently unprofitable while investing in the [[Neutron]] reusable rocket program.
- **[[Iridium Communications]] (IRDM)**: Satellite communications operator providing the "application piece" for Rocket Lab's vertical integration ambitions; brings 66 satellites and 2.5M+ subscribers.
- **[[At-the-Market Equity Offering]]**: A form of share issuance that lets companies sell stock into the open market at prevailing prices, used here to raise $1.94B without a traditional underwritten deal.
- **[[Bridge Loan Cancellation]]**: Rocket Lab cancelled a $3.6B bridge loan after the equity offering removed the need for short-term debt financing, reducing financing risk.
- **[[End-to-End Space Strategy]]**: CFO Adam Spice frames Iridium as completing Rocket Lab's stack — launch + constellation + applications — with further acquisitions possible in constellation and applications segments.

## Key Takeaways

- Equity offering: $1.94B raised via 29.3M new shares at-the-market.
- Existing cash + offering proceeds cover the full Iridium acquisition cost plus partial debt repayment.
- Amended credit facility: $1.77B; canceled bridge loan: $3.6B.
- Iridium shareholder vote scheduled for September 24, 2026.
- Both Rocket Lab and Iridium boards unanimously approved the combination.
- Deal expected to close mid-2027 pending regulatory approval.
- CFO Spice: Iridium is "incredibly transformational" for end-to-end space ambitions.
- Future acquisitions remain on the table, particularly in constellation and applications.
- **Neutron rocket**: EBITDA positive expected after successful test launch; positive cash flow "within 18 months to two years thereafter."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: Rocket Lab is deliberately stacking launch, satellite operations, and subscriber applications — Iridium fills the top of the stack, mirroring how platform businesses capture margin at each layer.
- **[[Liability Management]]**: Canceling the $3.6B bridge loan and amending the credit facility reduces near-term refinancing risk — classic liability management to match capital structure to a longer deal timeline.
