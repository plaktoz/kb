## Summary

Singapore stocks closed higher on Monday, July 27, 2026, with the [[Straits Times Index]] (STI) gaining 0.6% to finish at 5,620.24. [[Seatrium]] was the top blue-chip gainer, surging 8.9% after signalling a material improvement in net profit, while all three local banks ended in positive territory. Broader regional indices also advanced, with [[Hang Seng Index]], [[Nikkei 225]], [[Kospi]], and [[FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI]] all posting gains.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Straits Times Index]] (STI)**: Singapore's benchmark equity index, up 31.90 points (+0.6%) to 5,620.24.
- **[[Seatrium]]**: Top STI gainer (+8.9%) on expectations of material year-on-year profit improvement driven by divestment gains and margin recovery.
- **Singapore Banks**: [[DBS Group]], [[OCBC]], and [[UOB]] each gained between 0.4%–0.7%; DBS was the most actively traded stock by value (S$241.9 million).
- **[[iEdge Singapore Next 50 Index]]**: [[PC Partner]] led gains (+7.2%); [[Raffles Medical Group]] was the biggest decliner (−4.2%).
- **Regional Market Sentiment**: Positive across Asia — [[Hang Seng Index]] +1%, [[Nikkei 225]] +0.5%, [[Kospi]] +1%, [[FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI]] +0.7%.
- **Market Breadth**: Gainers outnumbered losers 324 to 223; 1.4 billion securities worth S$1.9 billion traded.

## Key Takeaways

- **STI closes at 5,620.24**: Up 0.6% or 31.90 points on Monday.
- **Seatrium surges 8.9%**: Profit guidance upgrade cited divestment gains and margin improvement.
- **DBS most traded by value**: S$241.9 million in shares changed hands.
- **All three local banks up**: DBS +0.6%, OCBC +0.7%, UOB +0.4%.
- **Thai Beverage worst STI performer**: Fell 1.1% to S$0.45.
- **Regional tailwinds**: Broad Asia-Pacific rally supported Singapore's advance.
- **Market breadth positive**: 324 gainers vs 223 losers across broader market.
