## Summary
Despite hot inflation and oil above $100, bonds rallied as investors sold AI stocks and bought Treasuries, taking the 10-year yield from 5.33% to 5.08%. Credit spreads widened with the weakest and most indebted borrowers hit hardest, so the market is differentiating by quality. This week's earnings will test whether the flight to quality lasts.

## Core Concepts
- [[Flight to Quality]]: investors favored defensive sectors and stronger balance sheets over leveraged AI names.
- [[10-Year Treasury Yield]]: spiked to 5.33% after CPI, then finished the week at 5.08% from 5.24%.
- [[Credit Spreads]]: investment-grade widened from 81 to 84 basis points; the lowest-rated high yield widened fastest.
- [[AI Buildout Financing]]: concerns hit CoreWeave (down over 15% in a day) and Oracle (down nearly 9%).
- [[Consumer Price Index]]: September CPI 3.7% y/y and core 2.6%, both above forecasts.
- [[Federal Reserve]] blackout period ahead of the Oct. 28 decision.

## Key Takeaways
- **Hot Data**: CPI and core CPI beat forecasts; PPI posted its biggest monthly gain since March 2022.
- **Bond Rally**: Yields fell despite inflation as investors sold AI stocks for Treasuries.
- **Spread Decompression**: Investors demand more yield from weaker borrowers; spreads remain historically tight.
- **Leverage Punished**: Indebted AI names fell hardest; chips had their worst week since April 2025.
- **Defensives Win**: Consumer staples rallied as the market rewarded stronger balance sheets.
- **Week Ahead**: 20-year reopening, 5-year TIPS auction and big-name earnings test the trend.
