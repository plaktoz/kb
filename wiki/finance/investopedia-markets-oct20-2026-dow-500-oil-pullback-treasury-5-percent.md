## Summary

The [[10-Year Treasury Yield]] sat near 5.12%, and a WSJ poll found most economists expect it to stay above 5% into mid-2027. Individual stories included Salesforce buying Braze, 3M and Hasbro beating, and GM and Steel Dynamics falling despite results.
