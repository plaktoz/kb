## Summary
[[Royal Bank of Canada]] reaffirmed its "outperform" rating on [[IBM]] on October 13, 2026, with a $270 price target, implying about 17% upside. Wall Street sentiment stays mixed: the consensus is "Hold" with an average target of $252.96, after a run of target cuts and downgrades. IBM's latest quarter matched EPS estimates, but revenue missed while growing just 1.1% year over year.

## Core Concepts
- **Analyst divergence**: RBC's bullish call contrasts with recent cuts from [[HSBC]] ($175→$174), [[Evercore]] ($310→$250, still outperform), [[Wolfe Research]] (downgrade to peer perform), [[Oppenheimer]] (downgrade to market perform), [[Robert W. Baird]] (neutral, $230) and [[Truist Securities]] (initiated at hold). The tally is 18 Buy, 13 Hold and 2 Sell ratings.
- **Q2 2026 results**: EPS of $2.93 matched consensus (vs. $2.80 a year earlier); revenue of $17.16B missed the $17.46B estimate (see [[ibm-q2-2026-earnings-ai-disruption-risk]]).
- **Valuation and balance sheet**: P/E 20.48, PEG 2.16, beta 0.73, debt-to-equity 1.63, current ratio 0.79. The stock trades below both its 50-day ($232.30) and 200-day ($243.84) [[Moving Average|moving averages]].
- **Ownership flows**: [[BlackRock]] opened a new stake worth ~$21.6B in Q2, and [[Wellington Management]] raised its holdings 2,362%. Institutions own 58.96%; SVP Robert Thomas sold 25,000 shares (~$5.76M) in August.
- **Narrative catalysts**: IBM advanced to Stage C of [[DARPA]]'s [[Quantum Benchmarking Initiative]], and a ~$350M Pentagon [[Quantum Computing]] initiative may benefit it. The ~2.98% [[Dividend Yield]] supports its appeal as a retirement stock.

## Key Takeaways
- **RBC call**: Outperform reaffirmed, $270 target, ~17% upside.
- **Consensus**: "Hold" rating, average target $252.96.
- **Stock move**: IBM rose $3.74 to $230.76 on lighter-than-average volume.
- **Trading range**: 12-month range of $199.19–$332.46; market cap $217.41B.
- **Earnings**: Q2 EPS $2.93 met estimates; FY EPS forecast 12.33.
- **Revenue miss**: $17.16B vs. $17.46B expected, up only 1.1% year over year.
- **Profitability**: Return on equity 35.65%, net margin 15.52%.
- **Leverage concern**: Debt-to-equity is higher than peers like debt-free Arista Networks.
- **Quantum tailwind**: DARPA QBI Stage C advance strengthens the long-term technology story.
- **Insider sale**: SVP cut his position by 34% at ~$230 in August.
