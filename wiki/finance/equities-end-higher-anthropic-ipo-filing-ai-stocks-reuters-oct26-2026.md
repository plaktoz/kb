## Summary

US stocks closed higher on Monday, Oct 26, 2026, as [[Anthropic]]'s IPO filing lifted sentiment in AI-related stocks after consecutive weekly declines for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq. The session opens a heavy week of Fed policy, growth and inflation data, and earnings from hyperscalers [[Alphabet]], [[Microsoft]] and [[Amazon]]. Strategists caution that sentiment could whipsaw if the week's releases diverge.

## Core Concepts

- [[Anthropic]]: Filing disclosed steep losses and red-hot revenue growth, pushing ahead despite a slowed autumn IPO calendar.
- [[AI Trade]]: Under pressure this month on worries that debt-funded spending is harder to justify as borrowing costs climb.
- [[Neocloud]] firms: [[CoreWeave]] and peers erased earlier declines after the filing.
- [[Meta]]: Fell 1.9% after saying it plans to raise up to $95 billion in equity for AI data centers.
- [[Federal Reserve]]: Policy statement due Wednesday; CME FedWatch priced a 12.7% chance of a hike.
- [[Hyperscalers]]: Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon earnings will show whether AI investment is translating into growth.
- **Photonics and chips**: Lumentum rose about 8% and Coherent about 6%.

## Key Takeaways

- **Index Moves**: Dow +0.28% to 52,361.58; S&P 500 +0.70% to 7,676.77; Nasdaq +0.83%.
- **Chip Rally**: Philadelphia chips index up 2.8% on the day but down 12.2% on the month.
- **Tech Leadership**: Technology index climbed 1.5%, leading all 11 S&P 500 sectors.
- **Meta Raise**: Up to $95 billion equity raise to fund AI data centers and infrastructure.
- **Tesla**: Jumped 5.2% to a three-month high after a Barclays upgrade to overweight.
- **Earnings Load**: More than a third of S&P 500 companies report this week.
- **Event Risks**: Fed statement Wednesday, growth and inflation data Thursday, November 3 midterms.
- **Breadth**: Advancers beat decliners 1.36-to-1 on the NYSE; volume 16.7 billion shares.
