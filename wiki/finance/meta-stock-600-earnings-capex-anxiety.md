## Summary

[[Meta]] stock slipped below $600 ahead of its Q2 2026 earnings report as investors weighed strong fundamentals against mounting AI capital expenditure concerns. Revenue grew 33% year-over-year in Q1, and the stock trades at roughly 22× earnings — reasonable for a high-growth tech company. But capex guidance of $125-145B for 2026 is creating anxiety about whether AI spending will translate into returns before pressuring margins.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Meta Platforms]]** — social media + AI infrastructure company; Q1 2026 revenue +33% YoY
- **[[Capex Anxiety]]** — market concern that Meta's AI infrastructure spending exceeds identifiable near-term revenue opportunity
- **[[AI Monetization Timeline]]** — gap between current AI spending and expected revenue impact from AI-powered products
- **[[Earnings Pre-Announcement Dynamics]]** — stock movement in the days before earnings driven by sentiment and analyst expectations

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock at $595 pre-earnings**: down from higher levels as capex fears weigh on sentiment
- **22× earnings**: reasonable valuation for a company with 33% revenue growth — not obviously overpriced
- **$125-145B AI capex**: 2026 guidance; investors questioning spend discipline
- **Q1 revenue +33% YoY**: strong fundamentals provide floor; question is how long capex pressures margins
- **Capex vs. returns**: Meta's core challenge — demonstrate AI spending produces revenue before margin erosion accelerates
