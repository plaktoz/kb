## Summary

In July 2026, investors rotated sharply out of tech and momentum stocks — with the Nasdaq down 6% and the iShares Semiconductor ETF down over 26% — into value and consumer staples names. The [[Value Investing]] thesis is gaining traction as "AI fatigue" sets in, with investors pivoting toward companies whose earnings are easily understood and tied to the real economy. Strategists highlight consumer staples, healthcare, energy, financials, and industrials as the key beneficiaries.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Value Investing]]**: Stocks with stable earnings and low valuations (e.g., [[Kraft Heinz]], [[Coca-Cola]]) outperforming amid AI skepticism.
- **[[Market Rotation]]**: Capital flows shifting from [[Growth Stocks]] and momentum names to value, small caps, and defensive sectors.
- **[[AI Fatigue]]**: Investor exhaustion around determining who will profit from AI infrastructure spend, leading to de-risking of hyperscaler-exposed names.
- **[[Semiconductor Sector]]**: Represented by the iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX), down 26%+ in July — central to the "sell semis" narrative.
- **[[Consumer Staples]]**: Kraft Heinz, Coca-Cola cited as favorites; framed as beneficiaries of fundamental bottoming and investor flow shift.
- **[[Small Cap Value]]**: iShares Russell 2000 Value ETF (IWN) up 22% YTD; hedge funds reportedly overweight small caps at 13% of net portfolio.
- **[[Healthcare Sector]]**: Sector weighting in S&P 500 at 9%, down from 16% peak; JPMorgan strategist [[Dubravko Lakos-Bujas]] sees durable inflection with 2027 earnings acceleration.
- **[[Peter Boockvar]]**: Investment chief at One Point BFG Wealth Partners; coined "sell semis, buy ketchup" framing.
- **[[Ed Yardeni]]**: President of Yardeni Research; recommends overweighting energy, healthcare, financials, and industrials.

## Key Takeaways

- **Semiconductor crash**: iShares Semiconductor ETF down 26%+ in July 2026.
- **Momentum collapse**: MTUM ETF on track for its worst month on record (-15%).
- **Value outperformance**: iShares Russell 1000 Value ETF (IWD) up 3.5% in July, +19% YTD.
- **Small cap surge**: iShares Russell 2000 Value ETF (IWN) up 22% YTD.
- **Kraft Heinz up 14% YTD**: Twice the S&P 500 gain; rated only "hold" by analysts.
- **Coca-Cola and Kraft Heinz**: Each up ~8% in just three days this week.
- **Healthcare undervalued**: Sector weighting at 9% vs. 16% historical peak; 2027 earnings reacceleration expected.
- **Hedge funds overweight small caps**: 13% of net portfolio in Russell 2000 names per Jefferies.
- **AI fatigue framing**: Investors prefer businesses they understand over AI infrastructure bets.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: Healthcare's weighting compressing from 16% to 9% of the S&P 500 creates a statistical setup for reversion — Lakos-Bujas's "durable inflection point" thesis rests on this dynamic.
- **[[Narrative Economics]]**: The "sell semis, buy ketchup" phrase encapsulates how a pithy narrative crystallizes and accelerates a rotation — once the story is simple enough to repeat, flows follow the story, not just the fundamentals.
