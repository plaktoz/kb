## Summary

The week of July 26, 2026 is the most consequential of Q3: Microsoft, Meta, Apple, and Amazon all report earnings while the Fed holds a rate decision and Brent crude crosses $100/barrel. Alphabet's recent earnings beat with negative free cash flow — and subsequent stock selloff — sets a cautionary template: spending surges are outpacing returns, and investors are no longer giving Big Tech a free pass on capex.

- **Oil at $100**: First time crossing triple-digits this cycle; shipping disruption compounds refinery strain.
