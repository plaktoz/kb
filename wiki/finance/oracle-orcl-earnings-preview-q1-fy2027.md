## Summary

[[Oracle]] (ORCL) is set to report Q1 FY2027 earnings on Thursday, with options markets pricing in an 11% swing in either direction — a range of roughly $145 to $179 from Wednesday's close. The stock has fallen nearly 20% in 2026 and shed more than half its value since its all-time high, weighed by AI spending concerns and backlog concentration risk. Analyst consensus remains bullish, with nine of eleven analysts rating it a buy and an average price target of $239 (~50% upside).

## Core Concepts

- **[[Options Market Implied Move]]**: Traders are pricing an 11% swing by week's end around the earnings release, indicating elevated uncertainty about near-term direction.
- **[[ORCL Stock Drawdown]]**: Oracle shares are down nearly 20% in 2026 and more than 50% from their September 2025 record high, driven by concerns over AI capex commitments and customer concentration in its contracted backlog.
- **[[Earnings Consensus Estimates]]**: Wall Street models call for Q1 FY2027 revenue of $19.06B (+28% YoY) and adjusted EPS of $1.74 (up from $1.47 a year prior).
- **[[Oracle Remaining Performance Obligations]]**: Backlog is expected to hit ~$640B, a roughly 40% annual gain per Visible Alpha data — the key metric investors watch to gauge sustained AI infrastructure demand.
- **[[Morgan Stanley]] neutral rating**: Analysts warn that rebuilding investor confidence may take "several quarters of solid results," signaling the earnings bar extends beyond one report.
- **[[Citi]] opportunity thesis**: Citi analysts frame the drawdown as "one of the most extreme dislocations and drawdowns in the stock's history," arguing a contrarian entry case.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models
