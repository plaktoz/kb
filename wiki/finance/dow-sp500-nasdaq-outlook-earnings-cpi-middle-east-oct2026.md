## Summary
The [[Dow Jones]], [[S&P 500]] and [[Nasdaq 100]] have held steady despite rising US bond yields and an escalating Middle East crisis, but their resilience will be tested this week. Three catalysts dominate: the Q3 earnings season led by big banks, Wednesday's September [[Consumer Price Index]] report, and further escalation involving the Houthis and Iran. A worsening conflict would lift oil, push inflation higher and pressure the [[Federal Reserve]] toward rate hikes.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Earnings Season]]**: Kicks off Tuesday with [[Goldman Sachs]], [[Morgan Stanley]] and [[JPMorgan Chase]], plus [[Johnson & Johnson]], [[UnitedHealth Group]] and [[Kinder Morgan]].
- **Earnings expectations**: [[FactSet]] data puts the analyst estimate for earnings growth at 29.5%; actual growth historically beats estimates by about 7 points, implying possible 40%+ growth.
- **Valuation**: The S&P 500 forward [[Price-to-Earnings Ratio]] is 19.1, below its five- and ten-year averages, framed as stocks trading at a discount.
- **Inflation data**: Headline [[CPI]] expected to rise from 3.4% (August) to 3.6% (September) on higher gasoline and diesel prices; [[Core Inflation]] seen at 2.5% vs 2.4%.
- **[[Monetary Policy]] path**: Economists expect the Fed to hold at this meeting and hike in December.
- **[[Treasury Yields]]**: Ten-year and five-year yields have pulled back in recent days.
- **[[Middle East Crisis]]**: [[Houthis]] attacked Saudi Arabia's main airport, [[Donald Trump]] hinted at US intervention, and [[Iran]] continues striking ships in the [[Strait of Hormuz]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Earnings Catalyst**: Bank-led earnings season starts Tuesday; main driver this week.
- **Growth Estimate**: Analysts expect 29.5% earnings growth per FactSet.
- **Beat Pattern**: Actual growth historically ~7 points above estimates; 40%+ possible.
- **Discount Valuation**: S&P 500 forward P/E of 19.1 sits below 5- and 10-year averages.
- **CPI Forecast**: Headline CPI seen at 3.6%, core at 2.5%.
- **Fed Outlook**: Hold expected now, rate hike expected in December.
- **Yield Pullback**: Five- and ten-year yields eased in recent days.
- **Geopolitical Risk**: Houthi airport attack and Iranian Hormuz strikes threaten escalation.
- **Oil-Inflation Chain**: Escalation lifts crude, raising inflation and Fed hike pressure.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Energy shocks transmit through inflation into policy rates** — A supply shock that lifts crude oil prices feeds into consumer inflation, which in turn pushes the central bank toward tighter policy, and that tightening weighs on equities.
