## Summary
CoreWeave and IREN are in a bear market, down 66% and 70% from their highs, and Benzinga warns the sell-off may continue as Microsoft reassesses its neocloud deals. Hyperscaler demand limits, rising competition, and Gartner's impairment warning add pressure ahead of CoreWeave's earnings next week.

## Core Concepts
- [[CoreWeave]] (CRWV): closed the week at $46.40, down 66% from its year-to-date high.
- [[IREN]]: down 70% from its highs.
- [[Microsoft]]: top neocloud client with over $80 billion in deals, reportedly considering a halt to new ones.
- [[Neocloud]]: third-party AI compute providers, including Nebius, Nscale, Lambda, and Firmus.
- [[Hyperscalers]]: Microsoft and Meta, which may lease out extra space; Microsoft is also turning off leased Oracle space.
- [[Gartner]]: warns neoclouds are most exposed to impairments, with $3.5 trillion potential.
- [[Bitcoin Miners]]: Riot Platforms, MARA, and Core Scientific are pivoting into the neocloud industry.

## Key Takeaways
- **Bear Market**: CoreWeave and IREN have fallen up to 70% from highs this year.
- **Microsoft Pullback**: Microsoft is tightening oversight and considering halting new neocloud deals.
- **Customer Costs**: OpenAI is exploring cost cuts that may alter neocloud contracts.
- **Renewal Risk**: Hyperscaler lease renewals are uncertain; Microsoft called Oracle's Nvidia GPUs outdated.
- **Rising Competition**: Bitcoin miners, Lambda, Nscale, and SpaceX (raised $40 billion) are entering.
- **Earnings Watch**: Analysts expect CoreWeave Q3 revenue up 159% to $3.54 billion.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Dependence on a few large customers concentrates risk** — When a supplier's revenue rests on a handful of buyers, one buyer's pullback can hit the whole business. · *model:* [[Concentration Risk]]
