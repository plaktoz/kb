## Summary

Major U.S. banks — [[JPMorgan Chase]], [[Bank of America]], [[Wells Fargo]], [[Citigroup]], [[Goldman Sachs]], and [[Morgan Stanley]] — are set to report Q2 earnings with analysts expecting year-over-year profit growth across the board. Tailwinds include AI-driven deal flow, resilient consumer spending, loan growth, and a $500 million windfall from [[SpaceX]]'s IPO. Despite strong fundamentals, analysts caution that the quarter may not materially recalibrate expectations higher, given investor skepticism about sustained peak performance.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Big Bank Earnings Season]]**: Q2 results for all six major U.S. banks due within two days; universal profit growth expected.
- **[[Consumer Spending Resilience]]**: [[Bank of America]] card spending rose 6.3% YoY in June — the strongest growth in four years — driven by discretionary purchases.
- **[[Capital Markets Supercycle]]**: Analysts reference a potential multiyear AI-driven capital markets boom with Wall Street operations described as "firing on all cylinders."
- **[[SpaceX IPO]]**: Generated a $500 million windfall for participating banks, acting as a one-time earnings tailwind.
- **[[Credit Quality]]**: No stress signals in credit markets; the overall picture described as "benign."
- **[[Jamie Dimon]]**: Acknowledged JPMorgan is "overearning" while tempering long-term optimism.

## Key Takeaways

- **Profit Growth**: All six major banks expected to show YoY profit growth in Q2.
- **Consumer Spending**: BofA card spend up 6.3% YoY in June — strongest in four years.
- **SpaceX Windfall**: $500M IPO boost benefited participating banks this quarter.
- **AI Tailwind**: AI deals and capital markets activity driving Wall Street strength.
- **Benign Credit**: No credit stress signals; fundamental backdrop described as good.
- **Tempered Optimism**: Dimon warns bank is "overearning"; analysts flag investor skepticism.
- **Analyst View**: HSBC's Martinez says backdrop is good but won't recalibrate expectations higher.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Mean Reversion]]**: Dimon's "overearning" admission signals awareness that above-trend profit margins are temporary — a classic setup for reversion to historical averages once macro tailwinds fade.
- **[[Availability Bias]]**: The vividness of a $500M SpaceX windfall and record card spending may cause investors to overweight one-time gains relative to sustainable earnings power.
