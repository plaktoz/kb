Chip stocks weighed on the Nasdaq as Nvidia fell 5% on OpenAI-financing concerns, even as oil prices tumbled after Trump paused Mideast escalation. Salesforce rallied 6.1%, lifting the Dow, while markets await Microsoft and Meta earnings and the Fed's rate decision, both due Wednesday.
