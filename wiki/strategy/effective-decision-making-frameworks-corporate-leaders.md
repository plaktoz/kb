## Summary

A decision-making framework is a structured approach that guides individuals and teams through evaluating options and selecting a course of action, offering consistent methodology that enhances objectivity and accountability compared to ad-hoc decision processes — reducing inconsistent outcomes, wasted resources, and missed opportunities.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Decision-Making Framework]]**: A structured, repeatable process for evaluating options, contrasted with ad-hoc decision-making that lacks consistent methodology.
- **Governance and accountability**: Frameworks are framed as tools that improve not just decision quality but organizational governance and stakeholder confidence.

## Key Takeaways

- **Core value proposition**: Structured frameworks reduce inconsistent outcomes, wasted resources, and missed opportunities compared to ad-hoc decisions.
- **Objectivity and accountability**: Consistent methodology is framed as the mechanism by which frameworks improve both the quality and defensibility of decisions.
- **Future direction**: The piece frames the future of decision-making as combining structured frameworks with advanced analytics and AI to further improve speed and quality.
