## Summary

[[Ravical]] raised a $17M seed round led by [[MMC Ventures]] to help accounting and tax firms grow without proportional hiring. Its platform turns how a firm's best people work into a shared way of working that AI carries out across every client, and more than 100 European firms already use it.

## Core Concepts

- [[Ravical]]: AI platform for accounting and tax firms, founded by [[Joris Van Der Gucht]], Ken Bastiaensen and Benjamin Vandermarliere.
- [[Tacit Knowledge]]: a firm's expertise sits with individuals and reaches only the clients they serve.
- [[Professional Services Scaling]]: firms traditionally grow by hiring more people and selling their knowledge.
- [[Talent Shortage]]: fewer graduates enter accounting while demand rises.
- [[Point Solutions]]: MMC says most accounting AI startups build single-task tools.
- [[Silverfin]]: cloud platform co-founded earlier by Van Der Gucht.

## Key Takeaways

- **Funding**: $17M seed led by MMC Ventures, with Lakestar, Pitchdrive and Shapers.
- **Traction**: More than 100 European accounting firms already build on Ravical.
- **Expansion**: Targets UK, Belgium, Netherlands, Germany, rest of Europe, then the US.
- **Model Pressure**: Demand rises while graduates and experienced staff become scarcer.
- **Client Behavior**: 70% of UK SMEs act on AI advice without consulting their accountant.
- **Firm vs. Individual**: Personal AI assistants improve individuals, not the firm.
- **Investor Thesis**: Winners will make best people count across every client.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Individual expertise only scales when made shared** — Knowledge held by individuals reaches only the clients they serve; capturing it as a shared system lets the whole organization benefit from it. · *model:* [[Tacit Knowledge]]
→ explains: Firm vs. Individual, Investor Thesis

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Ravical's core claim about how accounting firms should use AI?
**A**: Every firm will use AI, so the question is who controls it. Ravical turns how a firm's best people work into a shared way of working that AI carries out across every client.

**Q2**: What evidence shows pressure on the traditional accounting model?
**A**: Demand keeps rising while fewer graduates enter the profession, and 70% of UK SMEs act on AI advice without consulting their accountant. Expert knowledge is also only a search away.

**Q3**: How does MMC Ventures expect the winners in this space to emerge?
**A**: Not by replacing people, but by making their best people count across every client. Most competing startups build point solutions for single tasks.
