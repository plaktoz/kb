## Summary

Vanessa Larco, co-founder of [[Premise VC]] and former [[NEA]] partner, outlines her framework for evaluating early-stage founders and AI startups. Premise assesses candidates against seven proprietary founder attributes, requiring world-class performance in at least two — prioritizing the person's potential over the pitch. Larco views AI wrapper startups through a defensibility lens and considers the broader VC retreat from consumer tech short-sighted.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Premise VC]]**: Pre-seed and seed fund co-founded by [[Vanessa Larco]] and [[Mercedes Bent]], focused on technical founders building durable, high-growth software.
- **[[Founder Evaluation Framework]]**: Seven proprietary attributes distilled from the best founders at previous firms; founders must be world-class in at least two.
- **[[Urgently Dissatisfied Founder Trait]]**: A counterintuitive founder quality — appears disagreeable, but pushes teams to accomplish what seemed impossible.
- **[[AI Wrapper Startups]]**: Companies built on top of existing AI models; Larco draws a parallel to early [[AWS]] skepticism, arguing defensibility comes from deep understanding of underlying mechanics.
- **[[Consumer vs Enterprise AI]]**: Larco challenges the VC consensus that enterprise AI is superior, citing consumer software's historic track record of producing the most important companies.
- **[[Contrarian Investing]]**: Using category-wide VC flight as a signal to look for emerging tailwinds.
- **[[SVB Collapse Fallout]]**: Post-SVB, founders learned which investors treated them as priorities, accelerating the shift toward emerging specialized funds.

## Key Takeaways

- **Premise Check Size**: Invests $500K–$3M at pre-seed/seed; networks concentrated in SF, New York, and Atlanta.
- **Founder Over Idea**: The pitched idea rarely survives 5–10 years; founder's ability to find [[PMF]] matters most.
- **Seven Attributes**: World-class in 2+ of seven proprietary traits is the minimum bar.
- **Urgently Dissatisfied**: Disagreeable-seeming founders who prioritize goals over approval drive outsized outcomes.
- **AI Wrappers**: Not inherently weak — AWS analogy; risk is founders who can't adapt when model costs or quality shift.
- **AI Agents**: Users want research help, not full autonomous execution — trust and decision ownership still matter.
- **Consumer AI**: VC retreat from consumer is "short-sighted"; contrarian opportunity signal worth monitoring.
- **Emerging Funds Rising**: Post-SVB, specialized funds increasingly beat established VCs for competitive lead slots.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Contrarian Investing]]**: When capital floods out of a category en masse, mispricing emerges — Larco uses VC consensus flight from consumer AI as a signal to investigate, not follow.
- **[[Infrastructure Analogy]]**: The dismissal of AI wrappers mirrors early AWS skepticism; the moat isn't the infrastructure layer but the builder's depth of understanding — a first-principles defense of wrapper-based companies.
