## Summary

[[Marty Cagan]] introduces **Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)** — engineers (and increasingly PMs/designers) who embed directly with customers to discover real problems and deliver genuine outcomes rather than building to specification. The concept originates from [[Palantir]]'s model, which bridges traditional product companies and custom consulting by treating client work as a platform-building exercise. Cagan argues the FDE approach accelerates [[Product Discovery]] and is increasingly relevant for AI agent startups.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Forward Deployed Engineers]] (FDEs)**: Technical people embedded with target customers to understand context, observe real workflows, and prototype solutions that solve genuine problems.
- **[[Palantir]] Platform Model**: Rather than acting as a consulting firm, Palantir applies the [[Product Model]] — FDEs build prototypes using internal platform services, and a central platform team generalizes learnings into reusable capabilities.
- **[[Product Discovery]]**: The FDE approach replaces assumption-driven development with direct, immersive customer knowledge — the core of effective product discovery.
- **Platform-as-Generalization Loop**: FDE learnings feed back into the platform, creating a compounding knowledge cycle rather than one-off custom builds.
- **[[AI Agent Product Discovery]]**: Modern AI startups are adopting the FDE model precisely because building effective AI agents requires deep understanding of the customer's workflow environment.
- **[[Marty Cagan]]**: Author and SVPG founder, from his *Product Creator* series.

## Key Takeaways

- **FDE Origin**: [[Palantir]] pioneered embedding engineers with clients to apply the product model.
- **Not just consulting**: FDEs bet on solving the problem, not on executing client specs.
- **Platform feedback loop**: FDE prototypes generalize into reusable platform capabilities.
- **Scalable discovery**: Even visiting a few customers reveals cross-customer patterns.
- **AI applicability**: Many AI agent startups already use FDE-style customer embedding.
- **Career leverage**: FDE experience correlates with outsized product leadership careers.
- **Low barrier to start**: "Have your best engineers visit the offices of your customers."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[First-Principles Thinking]]**: FDEs reject the inherited assumption that engineers should stay abstracted from customers — by going to the source, they compress the feedback loop between reality and product decisions.
- **[[Feedback Loops]]**: The Palantir platform model is a structural application of tight feedback loops: FDE learnings continuously flow back into a generalizing platform, compounding knowledge over time.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core purpose of a Forward Deployed Engineer?
**A**: An FDE embeds with target customers to deeply understand their environment and real problems, aiming to discover solutions that deliver genuine outcomes rather than building to customer specification.

**Q2**: How does the Palantir FDE model differ from traditional consulting?
**A**: Rather than building what clients specify, Palantir bets on its own ability to solve the problem — FDEs build prototypes using platform services, and a central team generalizes those learnings into reusable capabilities.

**Q3**: Why is the FDE model especially relevant today, and how can most companies start?
**A**: The model is particularly relevant for AI agent product discovery, where immersive customer knowledge is essential; Cagan's minimal recommendation is simply having your best engineers visit customer offices to observe real workflows firsthand.
