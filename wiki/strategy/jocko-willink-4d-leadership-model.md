## Summary

Jocko Willink's "Leadership Strategy and Tactics" distills hard-won lessons from Navy SEAL command into a four-part model: Demote, Detach, Diffuse, and Determine. The model treats ego, clarity, accountability, and balance as the four levers a leader must consciously manage to build cohesive, high-performing teams. Its central insight is that effective leadership is paradoxically selfless — the leader's role is to serve the mission and the team, not to be seen as indispensable.

## Core Concepts

- [[4D Leadership Model]] — the four-part framework: Demote your ego, Detach to decide, Diffuse with extreme ownership, Determine the optimal balance
- [[Decentralized Command]] — leaders articulate intent and empower subordinates to devise and own plans; builds trust and commitment
- [[Extreme Ownership]] — leaders accept fault for negative outcomes first, which lowers tension and motivates the team to fix problems
- [[Detachment (Leadership)]] — mentally and physically stepping back during chaos to gain a clear view before making a call
- [[Leadership Dichotomies]] — opposing forces (ownership vs. delegation, standards vs. ease, love vs. challenge) that leaders must balance rather than oscillate between
- [[Jocko Willink]] — retired Navy SEAL Commander, author, and leadership consultant

## Key Takeaways

- **Demote your ego**: empower the team to devise plans; your trust earns their commitment.
- **Decentralized command**: state intent clearly, then let subordinates own the execution.
- **Detach to decide**: exhale, step outside the chaos, expand your field of vision, then call it.
- **Extreme ownership**: say "I let this happen — how do we fix it?" to eliminate blame loops.
- **Three effects of extreme ownership**: lowers tension, triggers guilt-driven effort, builds respect.
- **Dichotomy balance**: great leaders surf the optimal middle — not flipping between extremes.
- **Three critical dichotomies**: ownership vs. micromanaging; high standards vs. pushover; care vs. hard challenge.
- **Core ethos**: "It's all on you, but it's not about you."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Ego Depletion and Decentralized Systems]]**: Willink's "demote your ego" principle is a direct application of systems thinking — a centralized, ego-driven leader is a single point of failure; distributing decision authority makes the system resilient.
- **[[Radical Ownership]]**: The extreme ownership tactic is a practical implementation of locus-of-control thinking: by internalizing causality for every outcome, the leader converts blame energy into problem-solving energy.
- **[[The Dichotomy of Leadership]]**: Willink's "optimal balance" maps directly to the principle that virtues exist on a spectrum — courage sits between cowardice and recklessness — and a leader's job is to find the context-dependent optimum, not a fixed rule.
