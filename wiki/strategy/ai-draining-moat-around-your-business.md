## Summary

AI is eroding traditional [[Economic Moat]]s built on specialized knowledge, functional software and direct customer relationships. Leaders should instead build moats from decision velocity, proprietary operational context, superior customer experience, and human judgment and trust.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Economic Moat]] erosion**: Legacy advantages such as expertise, proprietary code and large teams are disappearing quickly.
- **[[Commoditization]] of knowledge work**: Standard industry knowledge built into public AI tools means clients will not pay premiums for basic advice.
- **Software feature cloning**: AI coding makes it easy to copy features and interfaces; great software is now the bare minimum.
- **[[Disintermediation]] by AI assistants**: Assistants choose vendors, so brands risk becoming invisible backend utilities.
- **Decision velocity**: Acting on information faster, not having better information, is the differentiator.
- **Proprietary operational context**: Supplier networks, regulatory constraints and customer nuances that AI models do not know.
- **Human judgment and trust**: Accountability, delicate interpersonal situations and relationships AI cannot take over.

## Key Takeaways

- **Knowledge threat**: Businesses selling basic answers see margins shrink rapidly.
- **Software threat**: Feature-only products that do not evolve are especially vulnerable.
- **Customer threat**: AI assistants stand between you and customers, weakening loyalty and cross-sell.
- **Speed moat**: Shorten approval cycles and empower faster, trusted decisions.
- **Context moat**: Turn complexity into a defensible asset outsiders cannot mirror.
- **Experience moat**: Buyers judge responsiveness, integration and ease of doing business.
- **Trust moat**: Human trust closes deals and retains clients long-term.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Commoditized inputs move differentiation elsewhere** — When the mechanical output of work becomes widely available, buyers choose on delivery quality, reliability and ease of doing business. · *model:* [[Commoditization]]
→ explains: Knowledge threat, Software threat, Experience moat

**T2. Whoever controls the interface to the customer controls the relationship** — When AI assistants make the vendor choice, the provider loses direct brand contact and loyalty. · *model:* [[Disintermediation]]
→ explains: Customer threat, Trust moat
