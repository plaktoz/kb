## Summary
As foundation models commoditize conversation and product discovery, competitive advantage in AI commerce shifts to specialized, outcome-grounded data that helps agents make purchase decisions. True Fit's fit and sizing data (purchase, keep and return outcomes) is offered as an example of such "decision intelligence". Such intelligence can be plugged into other agents via protocols like MCP.

## Core Concepts
- [[Agentic Commerce]]: AI agents that discover, compare and complete purchases; McKinsey sees up to $1 trillion of US B2C retail revenue by 2030.
- [[Decision Intelligence]]: domain-specific knowledge that turns conversation into confident decisions.
- [[Proprietary Data Moat]]: outcome data that is hard to recreate with AI, built over years and strengthened by [[Network Effects]].
- [[True Fit]]: fit and sizing data covering $600 billion in transactions, 100 million shoppers and 91,000 brands.
- [[Model Context Protocol]]: lets a primary agent call outside specialized intelligence at the moment of decision.
- [[Virtual Try-On]]: addresses visualization, not the fit decision; Glance and True Fit partnered on both.
- [[Scot Wingo]] and [[Jessica Murphy]]: sources on data moats and trust.

## Key Takeaways
- **Fit Dominates**: Over 70% of shopper questions to retail agents involve fit or sizing.
- **Decision Over Discovery**: Sales are lost on lack of confidence, not lack of options.
- **Outcome Data**: What shoppers bought, kept and returned beats clicks and views.
- **Commoditized Chat**: Summaries and natural-language search become expected, not differentiating.
- **Decision-Grade Data**: Real outcomes, continuously updated, reliable enough to influence a transaction.
- **Pluggable Intelligence**: Specialists can supply answers to others' agents via [[Model Context Protocol]].

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Proprietary outcome data is the durable moat once models commoditize** — When access to general capability is democratized, advantage moves to data that others cannot recreate, such as real-world outcomes accumulated over years. · *model:* [[Economic Moat]]
→ explains: Commoditized Chat, Outcome Data, Decision-Grade Data

**T2. Confidence, not interest, converts a purchase** — Creating desire is not the same as creating confidence; value lies in resolving the uncertainty that blocks the decision. · *model:* [[Choice Architecture]]
→ explains: Fit Dominates, Decision Over Discovery

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Where does the author say competitive advantage in AI commerce will come from?
**A**: From specialized, proprietary intelligence grounded in real outcomes, since foundation models make conversation and discovery widely available.

**Q2**: What data point and mechanism support the focus on fit?
**A**: True Fit found over 70% of shopper questions to retail agents involve fit or sizing. Outcome data on what shoppers bought, kept and returned lets an agent answer these decision questions.

**Q3**: How can a business with proprietary expertise apply this?
**A**: Turn what it uniquely knows into intelligence that other AI systems can access, for example through MCP, rather than building another general-purpose AI destination.
