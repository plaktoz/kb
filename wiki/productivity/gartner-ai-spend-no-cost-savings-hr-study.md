## Summary

[[Gartner]] research finds that although 77% of board members expect AI to deliver productivity and cost savings, 97% of organizations cannot cut expenses through productivity measures alone. Companies are creating capacity but struggling to redirect it to valuable work, while 38% are over budget and 85% plan to spend more. Gartner's HR analysts urge leaders to align on where AI matters and to imagine new ways of working.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Gartner]]**: Source of the HR-practice research on AI cost savings and productivity.
- **[[AI ROI Gap]]**: Boards expect returns, yet few organizations exceed cost-savings goals.
- **[[Capacity Redirection]]**: Time saved by AI must be moved into new capabilities, outcomes, and growth.
- **[[Leadership Alignment on AI]]**: 78% of leaders disagree on how AI helps in their workplace.
- **[[Workforce Upskilling]]**: Needed so employees produce better-quality AI-enabled work.
- **[[CV Viverito]]** and **[[Arj Bagga]]**: Gartner HR analysts quoted in the study.
- **[[AI Agents]]**: Only 9% of CEOs call them transformational; 43% report moderate, targeted impacts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Expectation Gap**: 77% of boards expect AI savings; only 3% of organizations exceed savings goals.
- **Budget Overruns**: 38% of companies are over budget on AI; 85% plan to spend more.
- **Redirection Shortfall**: Just 21% of AI-saved time goes to more innovative work.
- **Capacity Not Scarce**: Organizations create capacity; making it usable is the struggle.
- **Alignment Gap**: 78% of leaders lack a shared view of AI's workplace value.
- **Future-Focused Talk**: About 95% of earnings-call AI productivity talk concerns future gains.
- **Priority Persists**: 84% of CEOs still rank AI a top three-year investment priority.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Freed capacity is not realized value until it is redirected** — Time saved by a tool produces savings or growth only when it is deliberately reallocated to valuable work; otherwise it is absorbed. The article argues organizations create capacity readily but struggle to make it usable.
→ explains: Redirection Shortfall, Capacity Not Scarce, Expectation Gap

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Gartner's central finding about AI and cost savings?
**A**: Despite board expectations of productivity and savings, 97% of organizations cannot cut expenses through productivity measures alone. Gartner's analyst says organizations will not generate cost savings from AI at this point.

**Q2**: What data point shows where the productivity gains are being lost?
**A**: In a survey of more than 1,800 employees, only 21% of time saved by AI was redirected into more innovative work. Organizations create capacity but struggle to make it usable.

**Q3**: What should HR and leaders do in response?
**A**: Build a shared view of where AI matters most, upskill employees, and discover how work will get done in future rather than anchoring on today's processes. The aim is to use AI for things not previously possible, such as new capabilities and sources of growth.
