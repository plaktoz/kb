## Summary

A 5-minute pre-block planning conversation with [[Claude]] — brain dump, prioritization, and breakdown into timed steps — turns a vague intention like "work on the business" into a concrete, executable checklist for [[Deep Work]] sessions. The author argues Claude outperforms ChatGPT for this specific use case because it pushes back on overambitious plans and forces scope realism, rather than enthusiastically planning the impossible.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Deep Work]]** — extended, uninterrupted focus blocks (the author uses 3-4 hour morning blocks) requiring a clear plan to be effective
- **[[Brain Dump Prompting]]** — listing everything competing for attention so an AI can identify the highest-leverage focus
- **[[Weekly Deep Work Map]]** — a Sunday-review exercise mapping weekly priorities to specific deep-work blocks, flagging what won't fit
- **[[Scope Realism]]** — Claude's tendency to push back on overcommitted plans, contrasted with ChatGPT's tendency to accept unrealistic scope

## Key Takeaways

- **Five-minute planning session** structure: brain dump → prioritization → timed breakdown.
- **One primary focus per block**: splitting attention across two ambitious deliverables means neither gets done well.
- **80/20 buffer rule**: plan for 80% of block time as work, reserving 20% buffer since tasks run long.
- **Match tasks to energy**: creative work in high-energy morning blocks, analytical work in lower-energy afternoon blocks.
- **Plan before the block, not during it**: planning inside the block itself already represents lost focus time.
