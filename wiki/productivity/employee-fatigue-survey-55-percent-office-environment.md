## Summary
A survey reported by Business Forum finds 55% of employees feel tired or overworked daily or several times a week, driven mainly by workload and financial pressure. Genesis Property argues that constant fatigue makes the office environment more important, and says it is adapting its Bucharest developments accordingly.

## Core Concepts
- [[Employee Fatigue]] and [[Burnout]] driven by [[Workload]] and economic uncertainty.
- [[Professional Development]] barriers: lack of time or energy outranks training cost.
- [[Workplace Design]]: offices expected to support focus, collaboration, creativity and recharging.
- [[Genesis Property]] and its YUNITY Park development in Bucharest.
- [[Teodor Stoica]], HR Manager at Genesis Property.

## Key Takeaways
- **Prevalence**: 55% feel tired or overworked daily or several times weekly.
- **Energy Drains**: Heavy workload 20.4%, economic uncertainty 15.9%, personal finances 15.4%.
- **Financial Stress**: 23% say financial pressure raises stress and fatigue.
- **Development Barrier**: Over 33% cite lack of time or energy as the main barrier.
- **Office Role**: About 20% want focus, 19.24% collaboration, 17.54% creativity.
- **Work-Life Balance**: Financial stability 15.6%, family time 14.2%, extra days off 12.5%.
- **Developer Response**: YUNITY Park adds green spaces and relaxation areas; investment exceeds €50 million.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the headline finding of the survey?
**A**: 55% of employees feel tired or overworked daily or several times a week. Heavy workload, economic uncertainty and personal financial pressure are the main drains.

**Q2**: What is the biggest barrier to professional development reported?
**A**: More than 33% of employees name lack of time or energy, ahead of training cost or insufficient employer support.

**Q3**: What implication does Genesis Property draw for office design?
**A**: With constant pressure and fatigue, the office must provide conditions for focus and productivity plus collaboration, connection and chances to recharge. It has added green spaces, relaxation areas and community facilities at YUNITY Park.
