## Summary

[[G-P]] (Globalization Partners) released five 2027 future-of-work predictions based on a survey of 4,000 employees and 3,100 executives worldwide. Respondents expect the 9-to-5 and single-employer career to fade, more workers to oversee AI agents, and teams to hand off work across time zones. This is a vendor press release, so its optimistic framing serves its global-employment platform business.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Micro-Week]]**: Workloads set hours rather than a fixed 40-hour week; G-P targets a 30-hour week for salaried staff within five years.
- **[[AI Agents]] as Coworkers**: Workers become "directors" who assign work to agents, review output, and decide when a human must step in.
- **[[Global Hiring]]**: Leaders plan to spend more on remote-work and global-hiring tools than on office space.
- **[[Follow-the-Sun]] Handoffs**: Teams pass work across time zones so one signs off as another picks up.
- **[[Part-Time and Fractional Roles]]**: Younger workers push employers to rethink the full-time job.
- **[[Employer of Record]]**: G-P's core product category, coding labor laws into corporate workflows.

## Key Takeaways

- **9-to-5 Decline**: 33% expect it to end as the standard by 2030; 62% by 2035.
- **Multiple Incomes**: 43% of workers expect multiple jobs or income streams within three years.
- **AI Coworkers**: 38% predict AI coworkers will be as common as human colleagues.
- **Agent Reviews**: 67% of executives already conduct formal performance reviews for AI agents.
- **Global Over Offices**: 67% of leaders plan more remote/global-hiring spend versus 24% expanding offices.
- **Follow-the-Sun**: 41% of enterprises expect formal handoffs by 2030; 54% of employees would stay longer.
- **Generational Gap**: 59% of millennials and Gen Z expect part-time roles, versus 41% of Gen X.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is G-P's central claim about work in 2027 and beyond?
**A**: The convergence of AI and global teams will give workers more control over when, where, and how they work, ending the 9-to-5 and single-employer career as standards. G-P frames this as an optimistic era of human flourishing.

**Q2**: What survey data supports the prediction about AI agents on teams?
**A**: 68% of workers expect AI agent headcount on their teams to surge within three years, and 38% expect AI coworkers to be as common as humans. 67% of executives already run formal performance reviews for AI agents.

**Q3**: How would follow-the-sun work affect employees and employers?
**A**: 41% of global enterprises expect formal handoffs across time zones by 2030, and 54% of employees say they would be more likely to stay at such a company. Cited reasons are work-life balance, daily satisfaction, and productivity.
