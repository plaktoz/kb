## Summary

A Talker Research survey commissioned by Fiverr, covering 5,750 freelancers and full-time employees across five countries, finds workers expect AI assistants, work-from-anywhere and four-day weeks to define the future of work. Full-time employees are increasingly adopting freelancer-style habits, and many want more control over how they work. Respondents also see AI upskilling as important.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Talker Research]] and [[Fiverr]] survey**: 2,850 freelancers and 2,900 full-time employees in the US, UK, Canada, Germany and Australia, surveyed 21–29 September 2026.
- **[[AI Assistants]] and [[AI Fluency]]**: Expected to become common and a job requirement.
- **[[Four-Day Workweek]]**: Wanted by over a third of respondents at no more than 28 hours.
- **[[Flexible Work]]**: Preferences for earlier start and finish times, fewer meetings and Friday-start weekends.
- **[[Freelancer-First Practices]]**: Flexible hours, setting own priorities and tracking own output spreading to full-time employees.
- **[[Skills-Based Hiring]]**: Skills valued over degrees or titles.
- **Definition of a "real job"**: Still tied to steady pay, but increasingly to growth and specialised skills.

## Key Takeaways

- **Future Expectations**: AI assistants (32%), work-from-anywhere (30%) and four-day weeks (29%) seen as the future.
- **AI Fluency**: 26% expect it to become a job requirement; 22% of employees already see it.
- **Schedule Preferences**: 51% want the ideal workday to start before 9 a.m.; 42% want to finish before 5 p.m.
- **Shorter Weeks**: 37% want a workweek of four days or fewer, not exceeding 28 hours.
- **Meeting Cap**: The average respondent wants no more than three meetings a day.
- **Freelancer Habits**: Flexible hours (26%), own priorities (23%) and output tracking (22%) among employees.
- **Employer AI Push**: 37% observe their company encouraging generative AI tools.
- **Pay-Cut Appetite**: 43% of employees would take a pay cut, averaging a quarter of salary, to be their own boss.
- **AI Education**: 74% say it is important to invest time learning how AI can improve workflow.
- **Early Adopters**: 33% of freelancers wanted to adopt AI immediately versus 27% of full-time employees.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What does the survey say respondents see as the future of work?
**A**: AI assistants (32%), working from anywhere (30%) and four-day weeks (29%) lead the list. Skills being valued over degrees or titles (27%) and AI fluency as a job requirement (26%) follow.

**Q2**: How are full-time employees' habits changing?
**A**: They are adopting freelancer-first practices such as flexible hours (26%), setting their own daily priorities (23%) and tracking their own output (22%). Many see their companies encouraging generative AI tools (37%).

**Q3**: What implication does Fiverr draw about AI upskilling?
**A**: Fiverr's CHRO says those who have upskilled with AI improve their work quality and free up time for pursuits like freelancing or passion projects. Most respondents agree that investing time in AI education matters (74%).
