## Summary

[[Katie Parrott]], Every staff writer, built a sophisticated AI writing system called [[Compound Writing]] after turning a post-layoff ChatGPT subscription into a professional workflow. Her approach centers on feeding AI rich foundational context — audience personas, brand positioning, competitive data — rather than relying on clever prompts alone. She warns that AI productivity gains risk concentrating among those with time and resources to experiment, underscoring the importance of equitable access.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Compound Writing]]**: An open-source AI writing plugin ported from [[Kieran Klaassen]]'s compound engineering system, incorporating editorial frameworks inspired by [[Kurt Vonnegut]] and [[Alfred Hitchcock]].
- **[[Foundational Context Feeding]]**: The practice of pre-loading AI with rich context (personas, brand positioning, competitive data) so prompts can be simple and outputs are high quality.
- **[[AI-Assisted Career Pivot]]**: Using AI tools like [[ChatGPT]] for self-directed questioning and career coaching during a job transition, then scaling the same approach to professional writing.
- **[[Equitable AI Access]]**: The concern that AI productivity gains will disproportionately benefit those with resources and time to experiment, rather than distributing broadly.

## Key Takeaways

- **Origin Story**: Parrott discovered AI writing workflows after losing her crypto job in 2023.
- **Core Method**: Feed AI rich foundational context, not just clever prompts.
- **Workflow Efficiency**: Context-first approach enabled handling massive freelance workloads.
- **Creative Renewal**: AI offloads mechanical tasks, freeing mental energy for deeper creative work.
- **Open Source**: Compound Writing plugin is open source, adapted from an engineering system.
- **Equity Warning**: AI gains may concentrate among the resource-rich, not benefit all writers.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Separation of Concerns]]**: Parrott's system separates the "context layer" (who am I writing for, what's the brand) from the "prompt layer" — this division is what makes outputs consistently high quality rather than prompt-dependent.
- **[[Compounding Returns]]**: The "compound" in Compound Writing is literal — each piece of foundational context added to the system yields disproportionate improvements in all future outputs, mirroring how compound interest works over time.
