## Summary

Deep work blocks fail more often from a missing plan than from a lack of time. A 5-minute pre-block conversation with [[Claude]] converts a vague time slot into a specific, timed checklist via three sequential prompts: brain dump, prioritization, and breakdown. An advanced weekly version maps all priorities to blocks during a Sunday review, flagging anything that won't fit.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Deep Work]]**: Cognitively demanding, distraction-free focus sessions that require deliberate pre-planning to succeed.
- **[[Claude]]**: AI assistant used here as a planning interlocutor; preferred over [[ChatGPT]] for its willingness to push back on unrealistic scope and its persistent context via Projects.
- **[[Time Blocking]]**: Assigning specific work tasks to discrete calendar blocks; the workflow maps each priority to a block during a weekly review.
- **[[Energy Management]]**: Matching task type to energy level — creative work in high-energy morning blocks, analytical work in lower-energy afternoon slots.
- **[[Sunday Review]]**: Weekly planning ritual where a deep-work map is built, assigning priorities to available blocks for the coming week.
- **[[Thanh Pham]]**: Author and founder of Asian Efficiency, advocate for systems-based productivity.

## Key Takeaways

- **5-minute rule**: Planning before the block saves 30–60 minutes of wandering.
- **3-prompt sequence**: Brain dump → prioritization → timed breakdown with "done" criteria.
- **Realistic scoping**: Claude flags overambitious plans ("that's 8 hours in 3").
- **One primary focus**: Never load two ambitious deliverables into a single block.
- **80/20 buffer**: Plan ~80% of block time; leave 20% as overflow buffer.
- **Energy matching**: Creative tasks in high-energy windows; analytical in low-energy.
- **Weekly map**: Sunday review assigns each priority to a named block; surfaces what won't fit.
- **Common mistakes**: Planning during the block, overloading, and failing to protect blocks from Slack/interruptions.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Planning Fallacy]]**: People systematically underestimate task duration — the 80% buffer rule and Claude's scope pushback directly counteract this bias by forcing realistic time estimation before the block begins.
- **[[Forcing Function]]**: The 5-minute pre-block session acts as a mandatory constraint that converts vague intentions into specific commitments, making it harder to drift into low-leverage work.
