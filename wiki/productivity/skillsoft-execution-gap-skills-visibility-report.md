## Summary
Skillsoft's Workforce Readiness Report: Execution Gap Edition finds organizations recognize skill gaps yet keep assigning work that exceeds employees' skills. Poor skills visibility leads to missed quality, delayed AI adoption, burnout, and lost revenue. Skillsoft frames this as an execution problem, not merely a learning problem, and promotes its Skills Supply Chain framework (a vendor press release).

## Core Concepts
- [[Skillsoft]] Workforce Readiness Report: Execution Gap Edition.
- [[Skills Gap]]: the distance between required and available capabilities.
- [[Skills Management]]: identifying, measuring, developing, and deploying workforce capabilities against business demand.
- [[Skills Supply Chain]]: framework linking business outcomes to human and AI work, skills, and readiness.
- [[AI Adoption]] risk: adopting AI without skills to use it creates as much risk as moving too slowly.
- [[Employee Burnout]] from compensating for missing skills.
- Manager oversight burden: 38% spend more time verifying AI-generated work.
- [[Ciara Harrington]], Chief People Officer at Skillsoft.

## Key Takeaways
- **Awareness-Action Gap**: 77% of managers see skill gaps; 54% say work is assigned beyond skills.
- **IC View**: 60% of individual contributors see gaps; 37% report mismatched assignments.
- **Late Discovery**: 25% find gaps while struggling, 25% at performance review.
- **Confidence Gap**: 62% of managers vs 35% of contributors are very confident about identifying needed skills.
- **Slow Support**: 30% of contributors waited 3 to 12 months for help; 1 in 10 got none.
- **Manager Impact**: Quality assurance issues (31%), burnout (26%), delayed AI adoption (23%).
- **Contributor Impact**: Workload and burnout (31%), lower engagement (24%), customer dissatisfaction (21%).

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Late detection raises the cost of capability gaps** — When gaps surface only after work is affected, organizations react after quality, productivity, or capacity has already suffered; earlier signals allow earlier intervention. · *model:* [[Feedback Loops]]
→ explains: Late Discovery, Slow Support, Manager Impact

**T2. Differing vantage points produce different views of capability** — Those who assign work and those who do it hold different information, so leaders and workers diverge in what they believe about readiness. · *model:* [[Information Asymmetry]]
→ explains: Confidence Gap, Awareness-Action Gap, IC View

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the report's core finding?
**A**: Organizations widely recognize skill gaps yet keep assigning work requiring skills employees lack, turning a learning problem into an execution problem.

**Q2**: What data shows leaders and employees see capability differently?
**A**: 62% of managers are very confident their organization can identify needed skills versus 35% of individual contributors, a 27-point gap. Also, 54% of managers but 37% of contributors say work is frequently assigned beyond skills.

**Q3**: What does Skillsoft recommend organizations do?
**A**: Build an evidence-based view of the skills work requires and the readiness of people and AI to deliver it, closing gaps before execution suffers, via a Skills Management approach such as the Skills Supply Chain.
