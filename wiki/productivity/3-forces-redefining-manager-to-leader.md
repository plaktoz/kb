## Summary
The classic shifts from functional manager to enterprise leader still apply, but generative AI, geopolitical instability, and a compressed leadership pipeline now demand new capabilities: governing human-AI decision systems, practicing dynamic strategy, and cutting through noise to set priorities.

## Core Concepts
- The manager-to-[[Enterprise Leadership]] transition, updated for an AI- and geopolitics-driven environment.
- [[Human-AI Decision Systems]] governance as a new leadership competency.
- [[Dynamic Strategy]] replacing static, annual strategic planning.

## Key Takeaways
- **Three New Forces**: Generative AI, geopolitical instability, and a compressed leadership pipeline are reshaping the leadership transition.
- **New Capability**: Leaders must govern human-AI decision systems they only partially understand.
- **Strategy Shift**: Static annual strategy is giving way to dynamic, continuously-adjusted strategy.
- **Development Gap**: Organizations must give high-potentials more exposure to AI governance and enterprise-level trade-offs earlier.
