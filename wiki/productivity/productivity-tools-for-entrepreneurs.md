## Summary
For solopreneurs handling most of their business alone, seven tools each solve one distinct friction point rather than one platform solving everything: [[Notion]] for a unified workspace, [[Motion]] for AI-driven calendar scheduling, [[Todoist]] for fast task capture, [[Toggl Track]] for time auditing, [[Calendly]] for scheduling, [[Freedom]] for distraction blocking, and [[Obsidian]] for long-term knowledge building.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Notion]]** — unified workspace for tasks, notes, and lightweight CRM; risk of over-building before real use
- **[[Motion]]** — AI auto-schedules tasks onto your calendar based on deadlines and available time ($29/month)
- **[[Todoist]]** — natural-language task capture ("Email vendor quote Friday at 10am" auto-parses date/time)
- **[[Toggl Track]]** — time tracking that reveals how admin work and context-switching consume more hours than expected
- **[[Obsidian]]** — markdown-based, locally stored linked notes for long-term idea development, distinct from Notion's operational focus

## Key Takeaways
- **Pick one friction point** — task tracking, scheduling, time awareness, or distraction — rather than adopting all seven tools at once.
- **Motion costs $29/month** and needs a couple weeks of use before its AI scheduling adapts to your patterns.
- **Calendly** eliminates scheduling back-and-forth via shareable booking links (free tier available, paid from $10/month).
- **Freedom** (~$3.33/month annually) blocks distracting sites/apps across all devices with a "Locked Mode" that prevents early exit.
- **Obsidian** stores everything as local plain-text markdown files — no cloud lock-in.

## 🃏 Review Questions
**Q1**: What is the article's core recommendation for choosing productivity tools as a solo founder?
**A**: Identify the single biggest friction point in your workflow and start there, rather than adopting many tools simultaneously.
**Q2**: How does Motion differ from a standard task manager like Todoist?
**A**: Motion uses AI to automatically schedule tasks onto your calendar based on deadlines and available time, reshuffling as your schedule changes, while Todoist is a fast manual task list.
**Q3**: What distinguishes Obsidian from Notion in the author's framing?
**A**: Notion is described as the operational hub for managing tasks and projects, while Obsidian is positioned as a long-term thinking tool for developing and linking ideas, stored locally as plain-text files.
