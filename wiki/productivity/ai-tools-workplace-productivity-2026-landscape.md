## Summary

Workplace AI in 2026 has shifted from standalone chatbots to AI embedded directly inside productivity suites and persistent, always-on work agents, with workers using AI daily reporting 64% higher productivity and 81% greater job satisfaction per a Workforce Labs study. The frontier is agents like OpenAI's [[ChatGPT Work]] and Anthropic's [[Claude Cowork]] that execute multi-step tasks across email, calendars, and files, continuing to run even after a laptop is closed.

## Core Concepts

- [[ChatGPT Work]] — OpenAI's 2026 agent (powered by GPT-5.6) that executes multi-step tasks across email, calendars, code repositories, and messaging apps from a persistent cloud VM
- [[Claude Cowork]] — Anthropic's agent completing multi-step tasks across files, calendar, email, and the web; over 90% of usage is everyday knowledge work rather than software development
- [[Microsoft Copilot]] and [[Google Gemini]] — AI embedded directly into Word/Excel/Outlook/Teams and Gmail/Docs/Sheets/Drive respectively
- [[ZoomMate]] — Zoom's 2026 AI assistant connecting to Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft 365, Jira, and Zendesk to trigger workflows from meeting discussions, priced from $20/user/month
- [[Delegation vs Automation]] — the framing that modern AI agents aren't just automating tasks, but taking on "the work around the work" that rarely appears in a job description

## Key Takeaways

- **Workers using AI daily are 64% more productive** and report 81% greater job satisfaction (Workforce Labs study)
- **Three structural shifts define 2026**: AI embedded in core suites, standalone assistants for flexible reasoning, and automation platforms (Zapier) becoming essential
- **Choose tools by biggest time-waster first**: email → Grammarly/ChatGPT, meetings → Otter.ai, task management → ClickUp/Motion
- **Persistent agents can run scheduled tasks with no device online** — e.g., a client-prep briefing built and a follow-up email drafted overnight while a laptop is closed
- **Data privacy is the main risk flagged**: entering sensitive company data into personal AI accounts without organizational approval

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Amplification Not Replacement]]**: the tools that stick long-term are framed as amplifying existing judgment (drafting, summarizing, triaging) rather than replacing decision-making — consistent with the broader finding that AI adoption reduces wage growth in exposed occupations by degrees, not by eliminating jobs outright (see [[Pro-Worker AI]]).
