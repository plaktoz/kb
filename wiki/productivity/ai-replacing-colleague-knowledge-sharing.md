## Summary

Research highlighted by ZDNet finds that 75% of workers now ask AI tools instead of colleagues for workplace questions, gaining speed but losing the informal mentorship, context, and relationship-building that come from human interaction. Organizations face a balancing act: AI adoption is too productive to restrict, but left unmanaged it risks eroding institutional knowledge transfer, team cohesion, and onboarding for new hires. Some companies are responding by deliberately engineering structured mentorship and team interaction to replace what informal AI-displaced conversations used to provide.

## Core Concepts

- [[Tacit Knowledge]] — informal, contextual knowledge (e.g. who really owns a decision) lost when colleague conversations are replaced by AI
- [[Institutional Knowledge]] — organizational memory of past decisions and rationale, at risk as AI becomes the default info source
- [[Informal Mentorship]] — organic learning through casual questions, threatened by declining coworker interaction
- [[Workplace AI Adoption]] — the broader trend of AI displacing human-to-human information exchange at work

## Key Takeaways

- 75% of workers now ask AI instead of colleagues for workplace questions
- AI gives fast, consistent answers but strips out context, relationships, and war stories
- Junior employees risk missing informal mentorship and internal network-building
- Organizations should route factual queries to AI, judgment/context queries to humans
- Some companies are engineering deliberate team interaction to counter AI-driven isolation
