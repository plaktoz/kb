## Summary

Gallup data shows hybrid work does not hurt productivity when managed well — work location matters less than management quality — with 52% of remote-capable US employees now hybrid, averaging 2.3 office days per week. Full schedule autonomy actually raises burnout risk 76%, while teams with a formal hybrid collaboration plan are 66% more engaged and 29% less burned out.

## Key Takeaways

- **52% of remote-capable US workers are hybrid**; average 2.3 office days.
- **Full schedule autonomy raises burnout risk** by 76%.
- **Formal hybrid plans**: 66% more engaged, 29% less burnout.
- **Only 11% of teams** have jointly set their own hybrid policy.
- **RTO mandates haven't shifted** hybrid patterns since 2023.
