## Summary
Reschedule deep work away from predictable distraction zones, such as a recurring lawn mower, and eliminate known external distractions before tackling internal ones. Following Brooks's framework, a 60-day roadmap builds focus from the base up: eliminate distractions, build habits, then optimize biology, targeting one uninterrupted 90-minute block a day.

## Core Concepts
- [[Traffic Jam Rule]]: avoid scheduling deep work at times or places prone to known interruptions.
- [[Brooks's Rule]]: a focused person can grind an hour without hacks, say no to interruptions, and prioritize.
- [[Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs]] applied to focus: distraction removal, then habits, then biological optimization.
- [[Deep Work]]: one uninterrupted 90-minute daily block is the benchmark.
- [[Time Blocking]] and [[Pomodoro Technique]]: habit-layer tactics for weeks 3-4.
- [[Dopamine Reset]]: cut notifications and screen time in weeks 1-2.

## Key Takeaways
- **Known Distractions First**: List your top 3 and remove them before internal ones.
- **Reroute Focus**: Move deep work to a different room, library, or hour.
- **Three Capabilities**: Grind one hour, refuse interruptions, and prioritize.
- **Yes Before No**: Name one committed goal; saying no gets easier.
- **60-Day Roadmap**: Eliminate, build habits, upgrade brain, then reflect and maintain.
- **Weeks 1-2**: Audit notifications, cut screen time 50%, fix email and phone check times.
- **Weeks 3-4**: Fill calendar gaps with named intentions, such as "Write newsletter."
- **Weeks 5-6**: Exercise 3-4 times weekly, protect sleep, drink 16 oz water on waking.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the article's core advice about deep work scheduling?
**A**: Move deep work away from predictable distraction zones (the "traffic jam rule") and eliminate known external distractions before addressing internal ones.

**Q2**: How is the 60-day roadmap structured and why in that order?
**A**: It follows a pyramid from the base up: weeks 1-2 eliminate distractions, 3-4 build habits, 5-6 upgrade biology (exercise, sleep, hydration), 7-8 reflect and maintain. People fail when they skip the foundational removal of distractions.

**Q3**: What can you do tomorrow to apply this?
**A**: Fill empty calendar gaps with named intentions, move any gap in a distraction zone to another room or hour, and protect a 90-minute block for your one committed goal.
