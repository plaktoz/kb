## Summary
SAP SuccessFactors' Future of Work Research Lab finds that organizations are optimizing AI for efficiency (the "AI Upgrade" future) when they should be redesigning work for value. Most AI-driven job changes happen without organizational guidance, so leaders need a north star for what work should become. The research endorses six work-design shifts and a three-pillar HR mandate (Automate, Redesign, Reinvent) to shape the future of work deliberately.

## Core Concepts
- **[[SAP SuccessFactors]] Future of Work Research Lab** — monitored 500+ resources and surveyed 2,718 employees, 500 C-suite leaders, 60 HR executives, and AI-native founders
- **"AI Upgrade" Future** — AI value measured through efficiency, productivity, and time savings; currently best-evidenced of 10 predictions from October 2025
- **[[Productivity Trap]]** — reinvesting AI-freed capacity into more of the same work instead of redesigning it
- **[[Work Redesign]]** — restructuring tasks, workflows, roles, and teams across people and AI
- **Six Work-Design Shifts** — specialist→[[Generalist]], static jobs→dynamic roles, pyramid→diamond org, output→impact, centralized→[[Decentralized Decision-Making]], people management→[[Self-Leadership]]
- **[[Organizational Psychology]] Work Enrichment** — job characteristics linked to motivation, effort, performance, and retention
- **HR Mandate Pillars** — Automate, Redesign, Reinvent
- Related: [[Future of Work]], [[Human-AI Collaboration]], [[HR Transformation]]

## Key Takeaways
- **Efficiency Bias**: Evidence strongest for "AI Upgrade" future focused on time savings.
- **Measurability Pull**: Productivity dominates because output and cost savings are easy to measure.
- **Unguided Change**: 88% say AI changed their jobs; half without meaningful guidance.
- **Missing North Star**: 55–70% of employees received no clear guidance on work shifts.
- **Automation Masquerade**: 72% of C-suite say "transformation" is mostly faster automation of jobs.
- **Six Shifts**: Generalist, dynamic roles, diamond org, impact, decentralization, self-leadership.
- **Validated Enrichment**: Decentralization gave 77% more perceived autonomy; dynamic roles 49% more challenge.
- **HR Automation**: Executives expect 49% of HR work automated in five years vs. 35% overall.
- **Redesign Value**: Executives rank work redesign as top value pillar, owned by HR.
- **Reinvent Talent**: 75% say AI-era success requires reinventing how people are measured and rewarded.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Organizations optimize what is easiest to measure** — When value is hard to quantify, effort defaults to easily counted metrics like time saved and cost cut, even if the larger gains lie elsewhere. · *model:* [[Streetlight Effect]]
→ explains: Efficiency Bias, Measurability Pull, Automation Masquerade

**T2. Freed capacity defaults to more of the same without a guiding vision** — Capacity released by a new technology is absorbed into existing work patterns unless a deliberate, shared target for what work should become directs its reuse.
→ explains: Unguided Change, Missing North Star, Automation Masquerade, Redesign Value

**T3. Work design shapes motivation and performance** — Job characteristics such as autonomy and challenge drive employee motivation, effort, performance, and retention, so restructuring roles changes outcomes. · *model:* [[Intrinsic Motivation]]
→ explains: Six Shifts, Validated Enrichment

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the central concern of SAP's latest future-of-work research?
**A**: Organizations may be optimizing the future of work for efficiency when they should be designing it for value, with evidence strongest for the efficiency-focused "AI Upgrade" future.

**Q2**: What data shows employees are navigating AI-driven change without direction?
**A**: 88% of employees said AI changed their jobs, but half of those changes lacked meaningful guidance, and 55–70% received no clear guidance on expected work shifts.

**Q3**: How should HR respond if work shifts this substantially?
**A**: Adopt a three-pillar mandate: automate administrative work to free capacity, lead enterprise work redesign, and reinvent recruitment, development, performance, and rewards.
