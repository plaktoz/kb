## Summary

A curated library of 20 ChatGPT prompts for time management organized into four categories — priority/planning, time blocking, focus techniques, and time audits — designed to be copy-pasted with placeholders filled in. The collection operationalizes established productivity frameworks (Eisenhower Matrix, Pomodoro, deep work blocking) into reusable AI prompts rather than presenting them as abstract theory.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Eisenhower Matrix]]** — sorting tasks into urgent/important quadrants to shift effort toward important-not-urgent strategic work
- **[[Deep Work Protection]]** — treating 2–3 hour focused blocks as sacred, scheduled like meetings, with notifications off
- **[[Time Leakage]]** — small recurring drains (10-minute scroll sessions, "quick" 30-minute meetings) that compound into hours of lost time weekly
- **[[Pomodoro Technique]]** — 25-minute focused intervals with 5-minute breaks, useful for lowering the activation barrier to starting work

## Key Takeaways

- **Weekly planning ritual**: 30–45 minutes invested reportedly returns 5–10 hours — described as the single highest-ROI productivity practice
- **Time audits reveal a gap**: people who report working 50 hours often show only 25–35 actual working hours when tracked
- **Meeting reduction**: asking "could this be async?" or "could this be 15 minutes?" each cut meeting time roughly in half
- **Task-switching cost**: multitasking is framed as a myth — task-switching carries a real ~40% productivity cost
- **Boundary-setting**: "I can't take that on right now" is presented as a complete sentence, no further justification required
