## Summary

AI has collapsed production timelines, but management systems were designed for human-speed work — creating a new bottleneck at the review, judgment, and direction layer. Managers now face a flood of AI-generated output they weren't trained to evaluate quickly or delegate effectively. The challenge is no longer producing work but assessing it at pace.

## Core Concepts

- [[AI Productivity Gap]] — the mismatch between AI-accelerated production speed and human management bandwidth
- [[Output Velocity]] — AI compresses time-to-deliverable from days to minutes, overwhelming traditional review cadences
- [[Management Cadences]] — weekly one-on-ones, monthly reviews, quarterly roadmaps designed for pre-AI pace
- [[Judgment Bottleneck]] — the new constraint is managerial review speed, not worker production speed
- [[Liz Fosslien]] / [[Mollie West Duffy]] — HBR authors on emotions at work and organizational behavior

## Key Takeaways

- **Bottleneck shift**: Production is no longer the constraint — managerial review and judgment is.
- **"Every 30 minutes, someone creates something I have to look at"** — quoted manager signal.
- **Cadence mismatch**: Monthly reviews can't keep pace with daily AI output cycles.
- **Quality signal erosion**: Speed of output doesn't correlate with quality; managers need new evaluation heuristics.
- **Psychological toll**: Managers feel perpetually behind — a systemic, not personal, problem.
