## Summary
A new Resume Now survey ("Fleeting Flow Report") finds only 31% of U.S. workers reach deep focus every day, with meetings, "busy work," and constant interruptions cited as the primary barriers — echoing Microsoft's description of an "infinite workday" where employees are interrupted roughly every two minutes.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Flow State]] Frequency** — 31% of workers hit flow daily, 46% several times a week, 23% rarely or never
- **[[Busy Work]]** — 77% say low-value repetitive tasks consume a meaningful share of their week; 40% estimate at least a quarter of their hours
- **[[Meeting Overload]]** — 64% say only half or fewer of their meetings are productive; 38% spend 5+ hours/week in meetings
- **[[Infinite Workday]]** — Microsoft's term for the blurring of work/personal time; 58% of surveyed workers feel pressure to stay available after hours

## Key Takeaways
- **31% reach flow daily**; 8% rarely or never do.
- **Burnout/fatigue (41%)** is the top-cited barrier to deep focus, ahead of personal distractions and coworker interruptions (35% each).
- **79% report Zoom fatigue** to some degree.
- **44% work extra hours** simply because they cannot finish their workload in normal time.
- **Recommendation**: reduce unnecessary meetings and protect uninterrupted blocks rather than adding more productivity software — the real gap is attention, not tools.
