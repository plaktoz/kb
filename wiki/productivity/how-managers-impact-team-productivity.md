## Summary

Gallup research finds managers explain 70% of the variance in team engagement, making manager quality the single greatest driver of productivity, yet global manager engagement has fallen nine points since 2022 to just 22%. Organizations that select managers by innate talent rather than tenure see 48% higher profitability and 22% higher productivity, and manager support is also the strongest predictor of frequent employee AI use.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Gallup]]** — source of the 2026 State of the Global Workplace research
- **[[Employee Engagement]]** — 70% of team-level variance explained by manager quality
- **[[Manager Talent]]** — only ~1 in 10 people have the innate talent to be a great manager
- **[[Cascade Effect]]** — engagement flows downward from executives through managers to employees
- **[[AI Adoption]]** — manager support is one of the two strongest drivers of frequent AI use

## Key Takeaways

- **Managers drive 70%** of the variance in team engagement.
- **Global manager engagement fell to 22%**, down nine points since 2022.
- **Talent-based manager selection** yields 48% higher profitability.
- **Manager AI support** drives 79% frequent AI use vs. 46% without it.
- **82% of hiring decisions** miss high-talent manager candidates.
