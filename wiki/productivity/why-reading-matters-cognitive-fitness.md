## Summary

Newport synthesizes alarming reading decline data — half of adults read no books in 2022, pleasure reading among teens halved since 1984 — with Walter Ong's argument that reading fundamentally shaped human cognition, ethics, logic, and selfhood. He frames the decline as genuine cognitive regression, not neutral cultural evolution, and proposes treating it like the physical health crisis: schedule daily reading/writing/reflection as cognitive fitness, treating distracting digital content as junk food.

## Core Concepts

- [[Walter Ong]]'s Orality and Literacy: reading as a cognitive technology that restructured the human mind
- [[Reading Decline]] data: 28%→16% daily pleasure reading (2004–2023); teens 8%→29% "rarely/never" reading
- [[Cognitive Fitness]] as an analogue to physical fitness — requiring deliberate scheduling and habit
- [[Digital Junk Food]] metaphor: distracting content should be treated like unhealthy food, not neutral choice

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale of decline**: Fewer than half of adults read any book in 2022
- **Teen trajectory**: 29% of 13-year-olds rarely/never read for fun — up from 8% in 1984
- **Ong's thesis**: Reading isn't just communication; it shapes cognition, ethics, and selfhood
- **Cultural inheritance framing**: "An astonishing wealth of information and wisdom has been bequeathed to us"
- **Prescription**: Daily scheduled reading + writing + reflection, treated as non-negotiable as exercise
