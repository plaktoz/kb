## Summary

HSBC initiated SpaceX (NASDAQ: SPCX) coverage with a Hold and $115 price target, applying a "2x premium" for Elon Musk's track record yet still only justifying $115 — below the $135 IPO price and 50% off the post-IPO peak. Wall Street targets range from $62 to $800 (consensus ~$237), reflecting extraordinary valuation uncertainty for a company trading at 75x trailing revenue that has yet to report a single quarter as a public company. SpaceX's first earnings report is due August 4, 2026.
