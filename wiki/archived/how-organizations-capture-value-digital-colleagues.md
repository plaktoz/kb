## Summary

MIT CISR research defines "digital colleagues" as AI systems that collaborate with people as part of workflows rather than as on-demand assistants, requiring redesigned roles and governance. A survey of 132 enterprises found current value comes mostly from efficiency gains, but 75% expect a 25% average revenue-per-employee increase within three years — though fewer than 20% of firms currently have the capabilities to capture it.

## Core Concepts

- **[[MIT Center for Information Systems Research]]** — authored the "Leveraging Digital Colleagues for Enterprise Value" briefing
- **[[Digital Colleagues]]** — AI-enabled systems that act as discussion partners and perform autonomous tasks
- **[[Agentic AI]]** — underlying capability shift from individual-productivity tools to embedded workflow actors
- **[[AI Governance]]** — humans must remain accountable for consequential digital-colleague decisions

## Key Takeaways

- **Digital colleagues are AI systems embedded in workflows**, not just assistants.
- **75% of enterprises expect ~25% revenue-per-employee gains** within 3 years.
- **Fewer than 20% of enterprises** have the capabilities to capture that value.
- **Humans must remain accountable** for digital colleagues' consequential decisions.
