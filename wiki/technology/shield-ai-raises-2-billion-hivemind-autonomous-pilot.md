## Summary
[[Shield AI]] raised $2 billion at a $12.7 billion valuation to scale [[Hivemind]], its autonomous AI pilot system, and to acquire simulation company [[Aechelon Technology]]. The raise reflects a broader surge in defense-tech venture funding, with Shield AI's valuation growing nearly fivefold in two and a half years on the strength of combat-proven autonomy in GPS-denied environments.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Shield AI]]** — San Diego defense tech company founded 2015 by Brandon Tseng, Ryan Tseng, and Andrew Reiter
- **[[Hivemind]]** — AI pilot system operating aircraft autonomously via onboard sensors and reasoning, without external navigation
- **[[Aechelon Technology]]** — simulation platform acquired by Shield AI, supports the Pentagon's Joint Simulation Environment
- **[[X-BAT]]** — VTOL stealth fighter drone piloted entirely by Hivemind, unveiled October 2025
- **[[V-BAT]]** — reconnaissance drone that has logged 130+ sorties in Ukraine using Hivemind
- **[[DDIL]]** — military designation for disconnected, degraded, intermittent, or low-bandwidth operating conditions
- **[[Anduril]]** — Shield AI's most direct competitor in the defense-tech funding boom
- **[[Advent International]]** — led the $1.5 billion Series G round

## Key Takeaways
- **$2 billion raised**: $1.5B Series G plus $500M preferred equity, at $12.7B valuation.
- **Valuation grew** from $2.7B (Oct 2023) to $12.7B (Mar 2026), nearly fivefold.
- **Aechelon acquisition** supplies synthetic training data for a "Hivemind Foundation Model for Defence."
- **V-BAT drone** logged 130+ Ukraine sorties, located a Russian SA-11 Buk-M1 air defense system.
- **X-BAT** strike drone targets first VTOL flight this year, operational deployment by 2028.
- **Defense VC funding** hit $49.1 billion in 2025, nearly double 2024's $27.2 billion.
- **Competitor Anduril** raised $2.5B at $30.5B valuation, reportedly pursuing $60B valuation round.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models
- **[[Asymmetric Bets]]**: Advent's $1.5B lead round bets that combat-proven Ukraine deployment signals an imminent shift from R&D to production-scale revenue, justifying the valuation jump.
