## Summary

Monday.com's 600-person (~20%) workforce reduction is part of a 2026 wave where 20+ major tech companies explicitly cited AI when announcing layoffs. U.S. tech firms eliminated nearly 140,000 jobs this year, with Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft alone accounting for ~50,000. A notable counter-signal: companies citing AI in layoff announcements have underperformed the Nasdaq by ~10% in the following 30 days.

## Core Concepts

- [[AI Displacement]] — structured workforce reductions explicitly attributed to AI automation
- [[Agentic AI]] — AI agents replacing support, compliance, and operational roles (e.g., Salesforce, IBM)
- [[Middle Management Elimination]] — Cloudflare's CEO described cuts targeting "measurers" — roles that track rather than create
- [[Monday.com]] — 2026 layoffs: 600 employees, ~20% workforce
- [[Amazon]] — 16,000 corporate cuts; [[Salesforce]] — 5,000 support roles; [[Oracle]] — 21,000 over 12 months
- [[Meta]] — 8,000 laid off, 7,000 redeployed into AI roles
- [[Nasdaq Underperformance]] — AI-citing layoff announcements preceded 30-day underperformance

## Key Takeaways

- **140,000 U.S. tech jobs cut in 2026**; AI explicitly cited across 20+ companies.
- **Not just efficiency**: Many cuts target middle management, compliance, and measurement roles.
- **Markets skeptical**: Stocks citing AI layoffs underperformed Nasdaq -10% in 30 days post-announcement.
- **Meta's reallocation model**: 8,000 cut but 7,000 redeployed — signals internal AI bet over headcount reduction.
- **Coinbase signal**: Engineers "ship in days what used to take a team weeks" — productivity compression is real.
- **Cloudflare pattern**: Record revenue + 1,100 cuts = AI enabling growth without headcount growth.
