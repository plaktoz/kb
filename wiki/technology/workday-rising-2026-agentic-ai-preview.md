## Summary
DailyPay previews [[Workday Rising]] 2026 (Las Vegas, October 12–15), framing it as the most AI-focused edition yet, with over 400 sessions and keynotes from Workday's own leadership on [[Agentic AI]] in HR and finance. The piece highlights a hands-on track for testing AI agents directly, plus DailyPay's own sessions on frontline-worker research and a new native Workday integration.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Workday Rising]]** — Workday's flagship annual customer conference, this year centered on agentic AI in enterprise HR and finance software.
- **[[Agentic AI]]** — AI agents capable of taking autonomous action in workflows, the keynote theme presented by [[Aneel Bhusri]], [[Gerrit Kazmaier]], and [[Joel Hellermark]].
- **[[Rising Central]]** — a hands-on conference track letting attendees operate AI agents directly rather than watch demos.
- **[[On-Demand Pay]]** — the DailyPay + Workday native integration being demoed, giving frontline workers access to earned wages before payday.
- **[[Frontline Workers]]** — the employee segment centered in DailyPay's own research and conference sessions on workplace expectations and Gen Z technology trust.

## Key Takeaways
- **400+ sessions** are on this year's agenda, described as Workday's most AI-focused Rising yet.
- **Reported results from early AI agent adopters**: 20% productivity gains and 95% frontline hiring automation.
- **New research finding**: half of frontline workers feel their workplace has stagnated or worsened over the last five years.
- **DailyPay is demoing** the first native Workday integration for On-Demand Pay, built by Workday's own engineers.
- **Braindates** offer peer-to-peer, vendor-pitch-free problem-solving sessions between attendees facing similar challenges.
