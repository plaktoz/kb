## Summary

[[AfterQuery]], an AI training-data startup, has reportedly closed a funding round valuing it at $3.2 billion — a 10x jump from its $300M valuation just five months prior — making it [[Y Combinator]]'s fastest-ever launch-to-unicorn. The company, founded by two early-20s founders from YC's Winter 2025 cohort, focuses on teaching AI models and agents to reason and work like domain experts by encoding patterns from doctors, lawyers, and other professionals. By April 2026, AfterQuery had reached a $100M annualized revenue run rate with customers including [[Nvidia]] and Korean AI lab Motif Technologies.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AfterQuery]]**: AI training-data startup focused on encoding expert reasoning patterns for model and agent training.
- **[[Y Combinator]]**: Startup accelerator whose Winter 2025 cohort produced AfterQuery; [[Gustaf Alströmer]] called this its fastest launch-to-unicorn.
- **[[AI Training Data]] / Expert Knowledge Encoding**: AfterQuery's niche — employing specialists (doctors, lawyers) to teach models "the patterns, decisions, and reasoning of the world's best practitioners."
- **[[Scale AI]] / [[Mercor]]**: Comparable companies in the AI data-labeling and specialist-employment space.
- **[[Unicorn Valuation]]**: $1B+ private company threshold; AfterQuery reached $3.2B in roughly 18 months post-YC.

## Key Takeaways

- **10x valuation jump**: $300M → $3.2B in five months, post $30M Series A in April.
- **Fastest YC unicorn**: Confirmed by YC partner Gustaf Alströmer.
- **Founders' ages**: 22 and 23 at time of unicorn milestone.
- **Revenue**: $100M annualized run rate disclosed by April 2026.
- **Customers**: Nvidia, Legora, Motif Technologies (Korean AI lab).
- **Niche**: Specialist-staffed model training, distinct focus on expert reasoning patterns.
- **Comparables**: Similar model to Mercor and Scale AI.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Power Law Distribution]]**: YC's portfolio returns follow a power law — one outlier like AfterQuery can move the entire cohort's aggregate value, which is why the accelerator treats velocity-to-unicorn as a signal worth tracking.
- **[[Scarcity Moat]]**: Expert practitioner time (doctors, lawyers) is inherently scarce and hard to commoditize, giving AfterQuery a defensible supply-side advantage over labeling shops that rely on general-purpose workers.
