## Summary

[[Snorkel AI]] raised a $350M Series E, tripling its valuation to $3.5B in 17 months, driven by surging demand for high-quality AI training data. The company pivoted from data-labeling software to a "data-as-a-service" model that combines synthetic data generation with human subject-matter experts. It now reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375M — an 18x increase over the prior 12 months.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Snorkel AI]]**: Stanford-originated startup commercialized in 2019; pivoted to selling completed [[AI Training Data]] datasets rather than labor or software tools.
- **[[Data-as-a-Service]] (DaaS)**: Hybrid model using [[Synthetic Data Generation]] plus domain-expert humans to deliver production-ready datasets and [[Reinforcement Learning]] environments.
- **[[AI Training Data]] market boom**: Parallel growth at peers — [[Mercor]] ($2B gross annualized revenue), [[Handshake]] ($1B), [[Micro1]] ($500M) — confirms sector-wide demand surge.
- **[[Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback]] (RLHF) environments**: Snorkel now sells these directly, shifting from labor marketplace to end-to-end dataset provider.
- **Investors**: [[Insight Partners]], [[S32]], [[Addition]], [[Lightspeed]], [[Greylock]], [[GV]], [[Wells Fargo]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Valuation**: $3.5B after Series E — nearly 3× the prior $1.3B mark in 17 months.
- **Revenue growth**: $375M annualized run rate, an 18× increase year-over-year.
- **Pivot**: Moved from data-labeling SaaS to full dataset delivery (DaaS model).
- **Hybrid delivery**: Combines synthetic generation with human subject-matter experts.
- **Peer benchmarks**: Gross revenues at Mercor, Handshake, Micro1 show sector momentum; 60–70% of gross flows to domain specialists.
- **RL environments**: Snorkel sells reinforcement learning training environments, not just labeled datasets.
- **Origins**: Founded by CEO Alex Ratner out of [[Stanford University]] AI research.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Picks and Shovels Investing]]**: As AI model builders race to scale, demand flows upstream to the training-data layer — Snorkel's 18× revenue growth reflects the classic dynamic where infrastructure suppliers capture value in a gold rush.
- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: By owning both synthetic generation and human expert networks, Snorkel controls the full data-production stack, reducing client switching costs and defending margin.
