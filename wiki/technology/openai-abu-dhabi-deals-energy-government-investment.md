## Summary

[[OpenAI]] signed deals across energy, investment and government in the UAE after [[Sam Altman]] met Abu Dhabi's crown prince, as the lab works to secure regional investors for a $20 billion round. [[MGX]] has already backed both OpenAI and [[Anthropic]], showing how frontier labs lean on Middle East capital despite ongoing regional conflict.

## Core Concepts

- **[[OpenAI]]**: Seeking $20 billion in a new round co-led by UAE investors, per CNBC.
- **[[Sam Altman]]**: Met Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Abu Dhabi's crown prince and Executive Council chairman.
- **[[MGX]]**: Abu Dhabi investment firm; co-led Anthropic's $30 billion raise and OpenAI's $122 billion raise.
- **[[ADNOC]] and [[G42]]**: Signed an agreement to deploy OpenAI's frontier technology.
- **[[Mubadala]] and [[Inception42]]**: Agreed with OpenAI to apply advanced AI to investment analysis.
- **[[Machine-Readable Policy]]**: Abu Dhabi Government initiative to translate regulations into machine-readable form.
- **[[Middle East AI Hub]]**: Region attracting big tech data centers, though conflict has tested its appeal.

## Key Takeaways

- **Sector Spread**: Deals span energy, investment analysis, government and education.
- **Funding Link**: Agreements arrive as OpenAI seeks UAE investors for a $20B round.
- **MGX Double Bet**: MGX co-led Anthropic's $30B (February) and OpenAI's $122B (March) raises.
- **Capital Hunt**: OpenAI and Anthropic seek ever-larger capital pools from Middle East investors.
- **Government Adoption**: Five-year Inception42 agreement targets frontier AI in government, enterprise and education.
- **Regional Risk**: Ongoing conflict has tested the Middle East's appeal as an AI hub.
