## Summary

Meta's general-purpose [[Muse]] AI agent exceeded internal projections so dramatically that Chief AI Officer [[Alexandr Wang]] said usage had "blown way past our projections," sending META stock up roughly 6% — one of the S&P 500's top performers on an otherwise weak market day. Muse autonomously handles everyday tasks such as sending emails, booking reservations, and arranging flights, with a free tier for standard use and paid subscriptions for more complex tasks.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Muse AI Agent]]**: Meta's general-purpose agentic product designed to handle autonomous everyday tasks — emails, reservations, travel booking — distinct from [[Muse Code]] (coding agent) and [[Muse Glimmer]] (open-weight model).
- **[[Meta Superintelligence Labs]]**: The AI division led by [[Alexandr Wang]] that produced the Muse family of AI products, now including a consumer-facing AI agent.
- **[[AI Agent Monetization]]**: Muse's freemium structure — free for standard tasks, paid subscription for complex ones — represents a potential new recurring revenue stream for [[Meta Platforms]].
- **[[Tech Stock Market Dynamics]]**: Heavy AI capex had pressured META shares in recent months; the Muse agent's reception reversed that trend, returning the stock to roughly its year-opening levels.

## Key Takeaways

- **META stock rose ~6%**, ranking among the S&P 500's top performers on the day.
- **Usage exceeded internal projections** — Alexandr Wang described demand as blowing "way past our projections."
- **Freemium revenue model**: free tier for standard tasks; paid subscriptions unlock more complex agentic workflows.
- **Capex concerns had pressured the stock** in recent months despite AI boosting Meta's advertising business.
- **Stock returned to year-opening levels** after the Wednesday rally.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Expectation Anchoring]]**: The stock's 6% surge was amplified by the contrast with prior investor pessimism over capex — markets respond not to absolute performance but to the gap between expectations and reality.
