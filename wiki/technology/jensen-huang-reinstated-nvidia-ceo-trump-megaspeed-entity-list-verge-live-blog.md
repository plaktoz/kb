## Summary
The Verge's running timeline traces [[Jensen Huang]]'s resignation as [[Nvidia]] CEO over alleged affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset, and his reinstatement on November 23rd, less than two weeks later. The White House openly claimed credit for the reversal while Huang told staff that was "completely untrue". Meanwhile Commerce blacklisted [[Megaspeed]], Nvidia halted chip shipments to four Southeast Asian countries, and regulators and lawmakers opened probes.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Resigned November 12th after admitting he was "unfaithful"; reinstated by a unanimous board vote, then told staff he'd be "a better CEO the second time around".
- **[[Colette Kress]]**: Nvidia CFO who served as interim CEO during the gap; the permanent-CEO search was still ongoing at the time of writing.
- **[[Alice Huang]] and [[Megaspeed]]**: Alice Huang, then a Megaspeed executive, reportedly stayed in contact with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security from spring 2025 until fall.
- **[[Entity List]]**: Commerce added Megaspeed and its Malaysian and Thai affiliates over risk of diverting advanced computing chips to unauthorized end users.
- **[[Trump Administration]] involvement**: Trump said "I convinced them" and the White House said he was "totally involved"; the board cited input "from senior levels of the United States government".
- **[[Defense Production Act]]**: The Post reported Pentagon officials discussed using the 1950 law to compel reinstatement, though it proved unnecessary.
- **[[AI Chip Export Controls]]**: Nvidia stopped selling AI chips to China and halted shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia after tighter US rules.
- **[[Nvidia Stock Decline]]**: Shares fell to $125.53, a seventh straight weekly drop and a 46 percent slide, with an SEC accounting scrutiny report.

## Key Takeaways
- **Rapid Reversal**: Board announced return on November 23rd, under two weeks after resignation.
- **Government Pressure**: Board cited senior US government input; the extent of White House involvement remains contested.
- **Huang's Denial**: Told staff claims Trump got him rehired are "completely untrue".
- **Megaspeed Blacklisted**: Commerce Entity List cites chip-diversion risk; probe running since 2024.
- **Shipments Halted**: Nvidia stopped shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia.
- **Investigations Multiply**: California AG subpoena, reported SEC scrutiny and House oversight requests.
- **Stock Slide**: Shares down 46 percent over seven straight weeks of declines.
- **Deepfake Meme Wave**: Hundreds of AI deepfakes of Huang circulated, one with nearly 6 million views.
