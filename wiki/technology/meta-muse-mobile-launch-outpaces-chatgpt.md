## Summary

Meta's [[Muse]] AI app outperformed [[ChatGPT]]'s early mobile numbers in an iOS-equivalent comparison, recording 1.8 million downloads versus ChatGPT's 1.3 million in the first 12 days. Daily active users also favored Muse at 642,000 versus ChatGPT's 231,000 at the equivalent launch stage. Meta's cross-promotion leverage across [[Facebook]] and [[Instagram]] is the primary driver, mirroring the strategy used to scale [[Threads]] past 500 million monthly users.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Muse Mobile App]]**: Meta's consumer-facing AI app, launched on iOS and Android in the U.S. and Canada, reaching 2.8 million total global installs within 12 days of launch.
- **[[App Launch Benchmarking]]**: [[Apptopia]] isolated iOS-only U.S./Canada data to compare Muse's launch against ChatGPT's iOS-global debut — an apples-to-apples methodology for differing rollout strategies.
- **[[Meta Cross-Promotion Strategy]]**: Over 95% of Muse's users are [[Facebook]] users and 63% are [[Instagram]] users, reflecting Meta's ability to redirect its existing social audience to new products.
- **[[Threads Growth Playbook]]**: Meta previously used cross-app promotion to grow Threads to 500+ million monthly users; Muse's launch metrics suggest the same flywheel is operating again.

## Key Takeaways

- **1.8M downloads** in Muse's first 12 days vs. ChatGPT's 1.3M (iOS-equivalent comparison).
- **642K daily active U.S. users** for Muse vs. ChatGPT's 231K at equivalent launch stage.
- **2.8 million total global installs** within 12 days across iOS and Android.
- **Muse climbed from No. 2 to No. 1** on the U.S. App Store, surpassing ChatGPT.
- **95%+ of Muse users are Facebook users** — cross-promotion is the core growth lever.
- Muse launched iOS + Android (U.S./Canada only); ChatGPT launched iOS-only but globally.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Distribution as Moat]]**: Meta's social graph gives Muse a structural advantage that cannot be replicated by a standalone AI startup — scale of existing relationships converts directly into install velocity, making raw model quality a secondary factor in consumer adoption.
