## Summary

President [[Donald Trump]] announced $2.4 billion in computing power commitments from tech giants including [[Nvidia]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Anthropic]] to expand the [[Genesis Mission]], the federal effort to use AI for scientific research, to 14 agencies across energy, health, and space. Nvidia's $1 billion pledge is the largest single contribution. Trump paired the announcement with an escalation of his [[Super Intelligence Rebrand]], declaring anyone who still says "Artificial Intelligence" to be "THE ENEMY."

## Core Concepts

- **[[Genesis Mission]]**: Federal initiative launched in November 2025 through the [[Department of Energy]] to apply AI to government scientific work; now extended to 14 agencies.
- **Compute Pledges**: [[Nvidia]] $1B, [[AMD]] $500M, [[OpenAI]] $200M, [[Anthropic]] and [[Google]] $150M each, AMP and [[Emerald AI]] $100M each, and [[AWS]], Armada, [[Crusoe]], and [[Micron]] $50M each.
- **"Science: A New Golden Age" Summit**: The White House event where the pledges were announced.
- **[[Super Intelligence Rebrand]]**: Trump's push to rename AI as "Super Intelligence" to counter [[AI Doomerism]] and cast the technology more favorably.
- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Nvidia CEO who adopted the new term, saying Nvidia is "putting advanced Super Intelligence in the hands of America's scientists."
- **[[US-China AI Race]]**: Trump argues that slowing domestic development so China can win is a bigger threat than the risks raised by [[Dario Amodei]], [[Sam Altman]], and [[Elon Musk]], who have called for global cooperation to pace AI development.

## Key Takeaways

- **Total commitment**: $2.4B in computing power for federal AI-driven science.
- **Largest donor**: Nvidia's $1B is the biggest single pledge.
- **Scope**: Genesis Mission now covers 14 agencies across energy, health, and space.
- **Rhetorical escalation**: Trump called anyone saying "Artificial Intelligence" "THE ENEMY."
- **Industry buy-in**: Jensen Huang used "Super Intelligence" in Nvidia's official statement.
- **Policy stance**: Administration sees losing to China as worse than moving too fast.
- **Context**: Came a week after a White House summit with tech titans.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Trump announce at the "Science: A New Golden Age" summit?
**A**: He announced $2.4 billion in computing power commitments from tech companies to bring the Genesis Mission, which uses AI to advance federal science, to 14 agencies across energy, health, and space.

**Q2**: How were the pledges split among the major contributors?
**A**: Nvidia pledged $1B, AMD $500M, OpenAI $200M, and Anthropic and Google $150M each. Smaller pledges came from AMP, Emerald AI, AWS, Armada, Crusoe, and Micron.

**Q3**: What does the pledge round show about the relationship between industry and the administration on AI?
**A**: Major AI firms are funding federal priorities, and some leaders, like Jensen Huang, are adopting Trump's "Super Intelligence" branding. This is happening even though some executives have publicly called for pacing AI development, which the administration rejects because of the China race.
