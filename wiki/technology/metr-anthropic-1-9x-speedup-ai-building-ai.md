## Summary

An independent METR assessment published November 5 estimates Anthropic is improving its models on well-specified tasks about 1.9× faster than a year earlier, mostly because AI is helping build AI rather than from faster compute scaling. The findings carry heavy caveats, including redacted evidence and possible overstatement. The report sharpens the tension Dario Amodei raised about AI progress outrunning safety work, though it does not establish that safeguards have fallen behind.

## Core Concepts

- **[[METR]]**: Nonprofit Model Evaluation and Threat Research; spent 11 weeks inside Anthropic (August 12 to October 29) with access to internal data.
- **[[Anthropic]]**: Frontier lab whose Claude models increasingly help develop newer versions of themselves.
- **[[Recursive Self-Improvement]]**: An AI system designing its own successor; Anthropic says it is "not there yet."
- **[[AI-Assisted AI Research]]**: The main driver of the acceleration, per METR, rather than compute scaling.
- **[[Dario Amodei]]**: CEO who urged the industry on September 12 to "slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models."
- **[[Embedded Evaluators]]**: Outside assessment with unusual access, though partly redacted.
- **[[Anthropic IPO]]**: The report lands as Anthropic prepares a possibly record Wall Street debut.

## Key Takeaways

- **Speedup Estimate**: 1.9× faster progress since October 2025 (95% CI 1.5× to 2.8×).
- **Compressed Timeline**: Roughly two years of earlier progress compressed into one.
- **Staff Productivity**: Staff using the best internal model self-reported about 12 times the productivity.
- **Source of Acceleration**: Mostly AI helping build AI, not faster compute scaling.
- **Code Output**: Anthropic says engineers ship eight times as much code per quarter as in 2021 to 2025.
- **Redactions**: Anthropic redacted much of the most informative evidence as confidential.
- **Scope Caveat**: Acceleration may be limited to measurable, well-specified tasks; METR may be overstating.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did METR find about Anthropic's rate of progress?
**A**: METR estimated a 1.9× speedup in Anthropic's progress on well-specified tasks since October 2025 (95% CI 1.5× to 2.8×), roughly two years of progress compressed into one.

**Q2**: Where does the acceleration come from, and how did METR assess it?
**A**: Mostly from AI helping build AI rather than compute scaling. METR spent 11 weeks inside Anthropic with access to internal data on pretraining, RL, compute and benchmarks, plus staff interviews.

**Q3**: What caveats should limit how far one reads the result?
**A**: Anthropic redacted much of the most informative evidence, METR acknowledged internal disagreement and possible overstatement, and the acceleration may only hold for measurable, well-specified tasks. The report also does not show safeguards have fallen behind.
