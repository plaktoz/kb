## Summary

[[Dario Amodei]] is set to have his first one-on-one meeting with President [[Trump]], an evening dinner at the White House, amid diverging views on AI oversight. Amodei has proposed slowing AI's development pace while Trump has dismissed safety concerns as politically motivated and floated renaming the technology "super intelligence." The meeting follows prior friction, including a Pentagon supply-chain-risk designation that [[Anthropic]] is contesting legally, though relations with some other officials have reportedly improved.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dario Amodei]]**: [[Anthropic]] CEO meeting [[Trump]] one-on-one for the first time, days after being the subject of a comedic sketch on a late-night show.
- **[[Trump]]**: Dismisses AI safety concerns as politically motivated and has floated renaming AI as "super intelligence."
- **AI Pacing Disagreement**: Amodei has pushed to slow AI's development pace, a stance the administration has resisted.
- **Pentagon Supply-Chain-Risk Designation**: A prior friction point between [[Anthropic]] and the administration that the company is contesting legally.
- **Improving Relations**: Despite friction over AI pacing and the Pentagon label, relations with some other administration officials have reportedly improved recently.

## Key Takeaways

- **First meeting**: Dinner marks Amodei and Trump's first one-on-one meeting.
- **Policy gap**: Amodei wants AI paced slower; Trump calls safety concerns politically motivated.
- **Rebranding floated**: Trump has floated renaming AI as "super intelligence."
- **Legal friction**: Anthropic is legally contesting the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation.
- **Mixed relations**: Some administration relations have reportedly improved despite the friction.
- **Sourcing**: Axios first reported the dinner plans; TechCrunch verified independently.
