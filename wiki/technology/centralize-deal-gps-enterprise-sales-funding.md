## Summary

Centralize, founded by ex-Meta engineer Rachit Kataria and ex-Slack engineer William Wang, raised a $15M Series A led by NEA (bringing total funding to $19M) to build a "deal GPS" that maps enterprise buying-committee relationships and flags stakeholder churn. Its AI assistant "Centra" analyzes calls, emails, and CRM data to answer relationship questions and surface missing or disengaged decision-makers, addressing the "multi-threading" problem in enterprise sales. Revenue has grown roughly 8x over the past year, with clients including CoreWeave, Cognition, and Brex.

## Core Concepts

- [[Centralize]] — startup building a relationship-mapping "deal GPS" for enterprise revenue teams
- [[Multi-Threading (Sales)]] — practice of identifying and engaging every stakeholder across a buying committee
- [[Rachit Kataria]] — co-founder and CEO, former Meta Facebook Shops founding engineer
- [[William Wang]] — co-founder and CTO, creator of Slack Huddles
- [[Buying Committee]] — the group of stakeholders (procurement, legal, C-suite) involved in enterprise purchase decisions
- [[Y Combinator]] — accelerator that backed Centralize's Winter 2024 batch

## Key Takeaways

- Centralize raised a $15M Series A led by NEA; total funding now $19M
- AI assistant "Centra" auto-builds org charts from calls, emails, and CRM data
- Flags deal risk: departed champions, new or unengaged decision-makers
- Revenue grew roughly 8x over the past year; clients include CoreWeave, Brex, LangChain
- Launching a free single-player tier to drive bottoms-up adoption
- Buying committees have nearly doubled in size over the past decade, widening the coordination gap
