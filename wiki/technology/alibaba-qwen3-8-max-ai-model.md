## Summary
[[Alibaba]] unveiled [[Qwen3.8-Max]], a 2.4-trillion-parameter model it calls its most powerful to date, claiming performance comparable to [[Anthropic]]'s Fable 5 as Chinese firms race to close the AI gap with the U.S. Alibaba's shares jumped on the news, and the model's open weights are set for public release next week.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Qwen3.8-Max]]** — Alibaba's flagship model, 2.4T parameters, 1M-token context window, ranking second in Vision Arena and fifth in Text Arena
- **[[Open-Weight Models]]** — Qwen3.8-Max's weights will be publicly released, intensifying the U.S.-China open-model competition alongside [[Moonshot AI]]'s Kimi K3 (2.8T parameters)
- **[[Autonomous Coding Agents]]** — the model reportedly coded independently for 16 days straight in an internal test, iterating on its own tool
- **[[Anthropic]]** — used as Alibaba's primary benchmark comparison point (Fable 5)

## Key Takeaways
- **2.4 trillion parameters**: makes Qwen3.8-Max one of the largest models in the Qwen family.
- **Stock reaction**: Alibaba shares rose 4.5% (NYSE) and 7% (Hong Kong) on the announcement.
- **1M-token context**: can process thousands of pages of text in a single session.
- **16-day autonomous coding run**: model built and refined a coding tool with minimal human input.
- **Competitive pressure**: follows Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, China's largest model at 2.8T parameters.

## 🃏 Review Questions
**Q1**: What is Qwen3.8-Max and how does it compare to Anthropic's models?
**A**: A 2.4-trillion-parameter Alibaba model that Alibaba says delivers comparable or better benchmark scores than Anthropic's Fable 5.
**Q2**: What specific capability demonstration did Alibaba highlight?
**A**: In an internal test, the model coded autonomously for 16 days, writing, testing, and refining a coding tool with little human input.
**Q3**: Why does this release matter for the broader AI industry?
**A**: It intensifies the U.S.-China race in large open-weight models, following Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, and signals China's AI labs closing the capability gap with U.S. frontier labs.
