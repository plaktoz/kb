## Summary
IBM announced a formal partnership with [[OpenAI]] to deliver AI models and tools to enterprise customers through IBM's global consulting arm, building a dedicated OpenAI practice within [[IBM Consulting]]. The deal extends an earlier cybersecurity-focused collaboration and follows a similar alliance IBM struck with [[Anthropic]] less than a year prior. For IBM, the move reinforces a model-agnostic strategy pairing its own [[Granite]] models with third-party offerings via the [[watsonx]] platform.

## Core Concepts

- [[IBM Consulting]] — the delivery vehicle; tens of thousands of consultants will be trained and certified on OpenAI technologies
- [[OpenAI]] — contributing GPT-5.6, [[Codex]], and [[ChatGPT Work]] to be integrated into IBM Consulting Advantage
- [[watsonx]] — IBM's AI platform; model-agnostic hub combining proprietary Granite models with third-party LLMs
- [[Enterprise AI Adoption]] — target sectors include financial services, government, telecom, and retail
- [[Systems Integrator Strategy]] — OpenAI's approach of partnering with large consulting firms (IBM, Infosys, TCS) to capture corporate customers at scale
- [[Forward Deployed Experts]] — specialized group being established via OpenAI's Partner Network to serve enterprise clients

## Key Takeaways

- **Dedicated practice**: IBM will build an OpenAI-certified consulting practice inside IBM Consulting.
- **Scale of training**: Tens of thousands of IBM consultants will receive OpenAI credentials.
- **Model-agnostic IBM**: IBM blends its own Granite models with OpenAI and Anthropic offerings via watsonx.
- **OpenAI's distribution play**: Large SIs (IBM, Infosys, TCS) are the go-to channel for enterprise AI sales.
- **Sector targets**: Financial services, government, telecom, and retail are the primary verticals.
- **Revenue context**: Partnership announced amid IBM lowering its 2026 revenue forecast.
- **Prior tie-up**: Extends a June 2026 cybersecurity-focused IBM–OpenAI collaboration.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Distribution as Moat]]**: OpenAI is using established systems integrators as a distribution layer — rather than building direct enterprise sales from scratch, it piggybacks on IBM's existing client relationships and trust, compressing the time to enterprise adoption.
