## Summary

[[OpenAI]] acquired [[Glass Imaging]], a Los Altos-based smartphone camera startup, for over $300 million. Founded in 2019 by former Apple engineers behind Portrait Mode, Glass Imaging uses neural networks trained on specific camera hardware to enhance image quality at capture time rather than in post-processing. The deal is part of OpenAI's expanding hardware ambitions alongside its $6.5 billion acquisition of [[Jony Ive]]'s device startup "io" in 2025.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Glass Imaging]]**: Smartphone camera startup founded in 2019 by [[Ziv Attar]] and [[Tom Bishop]], former Apple engineers who built [[Portrait Mode]].
- **Neural network-based image enhancement**: AI models trained on specific camera systems to improve quality at the moment of capture, not as post-processing edits.
- **[[OpenAI]] hardware strategy**: OpenAI is pursuing a consumer device portfolio including smartphones, earbuds, and screenless AI companions.
- **[[Jony Ive]] / io acquisition**: 2025 $6.5B acquisition of Ive's device startup, establishing design leadership at OpenAI.

## Key Takeaways

- **Acquisition price**: OpenAI paid over $300 million for Glass Imaging.
- **Founding team**: Ex-Apple engineers behind the original Portrait Mode feature.
- **Technology**: Neural nets trained per-camera-system — on-capture AI, not post-capture editing.
- **Prior funding**: Glass Imaging had raised ~$30 million before the deal.
- **Hardware trajectory**: OpenAI is building toward a full consumer device ecosystem.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: By acquiring camera AI talent and design leadership (Jony Ive), OpenAI is internalizing key layers of the hardware stack rather than relying on OEM partners — the same move Apple made to control its own silicon and camera pipeline.
