## Summary

Prentis is an AI research lab launched in April 2026 by [[Ritankar Das]], [[Reid Hoffman]], and [[Mark Pincus]], seeking $100M at a $1B valuation to build computer-use models that automate routine office workflows. The startup claims its Hive-32B model outperforms [[OpenAI]] GPT-5.4 and [[Anthropic]] Claude Opus 4.6 on computer-use benchmarks at roughly 10x lower cost per task. Prentis reports up to $50M in contracts with healthcare, manufacturing, and retail customers, projecting ~$75M annualized run rate by Q3 2026.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Computer-Use AI]]**: Models that autonomously operate software interfaces to handle tasks like insurance claims processing or customs paperwork without human intervention.
- **[[AI Lab Fundraising]]**: Prentis pursuing $100M at a $1B valuation just months after launch, reflecting continued investor appetite for applied AI infrastructure.
- **[[Hive-32B]]**: Prentis's flagship model, claimed to beat frontier APIs on computer-use benchmarks while offering ~10x cost efficiency.
- **[[Enterprise Automation]]**: Target verticals include healthcare, manufacturing, and retail — high-volume, document-heavy workflows.
- **[[Reid Hoffman]]** and **[[Mark Pincus]]**: Co-founders engaged as investors/advisors while maintaining primary ventures (LinkedIn/Inflection and Zynga respectively).
- **[[Ritankar Das]]**: CEO, age 31, previously founded Titan (self-funded AI health holding company); leads day-to-day operations.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Innovator's Dilemma]]**: Prentis targets the low-end of the compute market (10x cheaper) to displace incumbent frontier APIs — the classic foothold-then-expand trajectory that disrupts from below.
- **[[Signaling Theory]]**: Recruiting Hoffman and Pincus as co-founders provides credibility and network access disproportionate to their operational involvement, lowering fundraising friction for a 3-month-old lab.
