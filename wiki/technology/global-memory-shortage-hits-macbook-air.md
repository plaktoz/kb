## Summary

The global memory chip shortage, driven by chip-hungry AI companies, has spread from Apple's niche desktops to its best-selling MacBook Air, pushing wait times to late August or September. Apple is responding with price increases and new Chinese memory suppliers.

## Core Concepts

The [[AI Infrastructure Buildout]] is consuming so much [[Memory Chip Shortage|memory chip supply]] that it is now bottlenecking consumer electronics, not just data centers. [[Apple]] first saw the effect on the Mac mini and Mac Studio before it reached the [[MacBook Air]], its highest-volume laptop. Apple's response — raising prices and diversifying its supply chain toward Chinese suppliers — illustrates how [[Supply Chain Disruption]] from the AI boom is reshaping consumer hardware pricing and marketing strategy industry-wide.

## Key Takeaways

- **Consumer spillover**: AI datacenter demand for memory chips is now delaying and inflating prices on mainstream consumer laptops, not just niche desktops.
- **Availability crunch**: New MacBook Air orders face waits into late August or September 2026.
- **Apple's response**: Raising prices, sourcing memory from Chinese suppliers, and shifting back-to-school marketing toward the MacBook Pro base model instead.
