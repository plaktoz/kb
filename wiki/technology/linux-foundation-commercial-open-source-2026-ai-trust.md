## Summary
The Linux Foundation's second annual State of Commercial Open Source 2026 survey of 225 founders and enterprise leaders finds 72% see AI as net positive and nearly twice as many open more of their core product as close it. Brand and trust, not code, is the top competitive advantage (57%), monetized through layered revenue models, and building enterprise sales is the top scaling challenge.

## Core Concepts
- [[Commercial Open Source Software]] (COSS): businesses built on code they give away.
- [[Linux Foundation Research]] and [[Serena]]: publishers of the report, with sponsors [[AWS]], [[Red Hat]], [[Futurewei]] and [[CNCF]].
- [[Open Core]], [[Managed Cloud]], and support and services: the main revenue models.
- [[Product-Led Growth]] layered with [[Enterprise Sales]] as companies scale.
- [[Customer Acquisition Cost]]: 4 in 5 trackers recover it within 12 months.
- [[Hilary Carter]], [[Chris Aniszczyk]], [[Matthieu Lavergne]]: report author and commentators.

## Key Takeaways
- **AI Sentiment**: 72% report net positive AI impact versus 8% negative.
- **Internal Gains**: Productivity is the top benefit (73%), ahead of market expansion (46%).
- **Opening Up**: Companies adapting to AI are nearly twice as likely to open more.
- **Trust Moat**: 57% cite brand recognition and trust as their top advantage.
- **Layered Revenue**: 65% run two or more revenue models; managed cloud rises from 35% to 66%.
- **Two Personas**: Practitioners adopt; CTOs or VPs of Engineering approve 46% of purchases.
- **Sales Bottleneck**: Enterprise sales capability is the top scaling challenge (47%).

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. When the product is cheap to copy, trust becomes the durable advantage** — If code costs next to nothing, value shifts to assets that cannot be copied quickly, such as brand and community trust. · *model:* [[Economic Moat]]
→ explains: Trust Moat, Opening Up, Layered Revenue

**T2. The user and the buyer are different people** — Adoption by practitioners does not equal purchase by executives, so growth requires motions aimed at both. · *model:* [[Two-Sided Markets]]
→ explains: Two Personas, Sales Bottleneck

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the report's central finding about competitive advantage?
**A**: 57% of commercial open source companies name brand recognition and trust as their top advantage, ahead of features, integrations and community size.

**Q2**: How do companies monetize that trust as they grow?
**A**: They stack revenue models rather than switching: 65% run two or more, and managed cloud rises from 35% below $5 million ARR to 66% above $20 million.

**Q3**: What should an open source company prioritize as it scales?
**A**: Building mature enterprise sales on top of product-led growth, since executives approve purchases and this is the most cited scaling challenge.
