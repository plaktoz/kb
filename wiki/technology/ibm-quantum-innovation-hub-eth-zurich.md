## Summary

IBM is establishing a new quantum innovation hub at [[ETH Zurich]], powered by its "Quantum Nighthawk processor" and housed at Switzerland's National Supercomputing Centre in Lugano, in partnership with [[Lockheed Martin]]. The hub will support research in chemistry, materials science, optimization, and financial services under a Swiss defense procurement offset agreement. The initiative also extends IBM's relationship with ETH Zurich into a 10-year effort focused on foundational AI and quantum algorithms.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM Quantum Nighthawk Processor]]**: The quantum processor powering the new ETH Zurich hub, representing IBM's latest hardware milestone deployed in an academic and industry research setting.
- **[[ETH Zurich]]**: A leading Swiss technical university serving as the academic anchor for IBM's European quantum expansion; the hub is physically housed at Switzerland's [[National Supercomputing Centre]] in Lugano.
- **[[Lockheed Martin]]**: Aerospace and defense partner collaborating with IBM on quantum sensing for navigation and additive manufacturing improvements involving metallic alloys.
- **[[Quantum Sensing]]**: A key application area for the hub, particularly in aerospace and defense contexts — navigation systems and precision manufacturing.
- **[[IBM Quantum Education]]**: IBM plans to deliver courses, certifications, workshops, and hackathons through the hub to build the broader quantum ecosystem.
- **Competitive Landscape**: [[Microsoft]] (chemistry, materials, optimization) and [[Google Quantum AI]] (performance, university collaborations) are pursuing overlapping research verticals.

## Key Takeaways

- **New hub location**: ETH Zurich / National Supercomputing Centre, Lugano, Switzerland.
- **Defense partnership**: Lockheed Martin explores quantum sensing for navigation and metallic-alloy additive manufacturing.
- **10-year academic commitment**: ETH Zurich relationship extended to foundational AI and quantum algorithm research.
- **Education push**: IBM will offer courses, certifications, workshops, and hackathons via the hub.
- **Procurement framing**: Hub established under a Swiss defense procurement offset agreement.
