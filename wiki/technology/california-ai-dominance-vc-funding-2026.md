## Summary

California-based companies have drawn roughly $366 billion in venture capital since the start of 2026, more than triple the combined total for every other U.S. state and nearly double the state's own 2025 record. The AI boom in Silicon Valley is the primary driver, with New York a distant second at $27 billion.

## Core Concepts

The scale of [[Venture Capital]] concentration illustrates how the [[AI Industry]] boom is geographically lopsided rather than broadly distributed, reinforcing [[Silicon Valley]]'s position as the center of gravity for frontier AI investment despite talk of AI development globalizing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Extreme concentration**: California drew 3x more VC than all other 49 states combined in 2026.
- **Record-breaking pace**: $366B nearly doubles California's prior record set in 2025.
- **Distant second**: New York trails far behind at just $27 billion in deals.
