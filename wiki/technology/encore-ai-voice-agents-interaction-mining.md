## Summary

Encore AI (formerly Insait IO) raised a $30M Series A led by Team8 to build AI voice agents trained via "interaction mining" — analyzing call recordings, emails, and CRM data to identify which conversational tactics drove successful outcomes, then baking those playbooks into deployable agents. Founded in 2022 by CEO Dvir Ginzburg, the company has grown ARR more than 5x since its seed round and serves 40+ enterprise customers, mostly financial institutions. Ginzburg argues Encore's edge over CRM incumbents like Salesforce is that competitors don't treat historical conversation data as foundational to their agent-building process.

## Core Concepts

- [[Encore AI]] — startup (formerly Insait IO) building AI voice agents from mined customer interactions
- [[Interaction Mining]] — Ginzburg's term for analyzing calls, emails, and CRM data to extract winning conversational playbooks
- [[Dvir Ginzburg]] — founder and CEO of Encore AI
- [[AI Voice Agent]] — autonomous or assistive agent that communicates with customers by voice or text

## Key Takeaways

- Encore AI raised a $30M Series A led by Team8
- "Interaction mining" extracts winning playbooks from call, email, and CRM data
- ARR grew more than 5x since seed round; 40+ enterprise customers, mostly financial
- Agents can operate autonomously or assist employees with live recommendations
- Competitive moat claimed against Salesforce, SAP, Zoho, and HubSpot via data-first approach
- Proceeds will expand U.S. sales and deploy with more financial institutions
