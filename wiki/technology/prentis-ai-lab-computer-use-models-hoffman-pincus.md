## Summary

Prentis, an AI lab launched April 2026 by Ritankar Das, Reid Hoffman, and Mark Pincus, targets routine office workflow automation via computer-use models — processing insurance claims, customs forms, and similar paper-heavy tasks without human intervention. The company claims its Hive-32B model outperforms GPT-5.4 and Claude Opus 4.6 on computer-use benchmarks at 10x lower cost, and is seeking $100M at a $1B valuation.

## Core Concepts

- [[Computer-Use Models]] — AI systems that operate software interfaces directly to automate tasks
- [[Prentis]] — AI lab founded April 2026; CEO Ritankar Das (31); backers include [[Reid Hoffman]], [[Mark Pincus]]
- [[Hive-32B]] — Prentis's proprietary model; claimed to beat frontier APIs at 10x lower cost per task
- [[Agentic Automation]] — end-to-end document and workflow processing without human-in-the-loop

## Key Takeaways

- **Niche focus wins**: Prentis targets paper-heavy back-office workflows, not general reasoning.
- **10x cost advantage claimed**: Running specialized models instead of frontier APIs as the business model.
- **$75M ARR projected by Q3 2026** — performance-dependent; TechCrunch notes claims unverified.
- **Up to $50M in customer contracts**: Healthcare, manufacturing, retail already signed.
- **Team pedigree**: 25+ employees from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Tencent, Alibaba.
- **Founders split focus**: Hoffman and Pincus remain involved in other primary ventures.
