## Summary
NVIDIA's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as President, CEO and director, saying it was satisfied with his candor and, based on input from senior US government levels, sees no national security risk. The independent-director investigation into reports he was targeted by foreign intelligence will continue. The board also created a National Security and Export Control Committee and is searching for a Chief Compliance Officer.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Reinstated effective immediately as President, CEO and board member.
- **[[Nvidia]] Board of Directors**: Voted unanimously; Chair [[Harvey C. Jones]] remains chair.
- **[[Colette Kress]]**: Served as interim CEO while continuing as CFO.
- **[[Independent Committee Investigation]]**: Independent directors and outside counsel investigate the foreign intelligence reports; it proceeds to conclusion.
- **[[National Security and Export Control Committee]]**: New independent-director committee overseeing trade compliance and national security risk.
- **[[Chief Compliance Officer]]**: New position, reporting to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
- **[[US Government Input]]**: Cited as basis for confidence there is no national security risk.

## Key Takeaways
- **Unanimous Vote**: Board reinstated Huang after discussions confirming his candor and leadership ability.
- **Government Input**: Senior US government input informed the no-risk conclusion.
- **Investigation Continues**: The independent committee's probe will run to conclusion with Huang's cooperation.
- **New Oversight**: A National Security and Export Control Committee is staffed solely by independent directors.
- **Compliance Hire**: Nvidia is searching for a Chief Compliance Officer.
- **Interim Thanks**: Kress thanked for interim service, remaining CFO.
- **Ongoing Inquiries**: Nvidia will keep cooperating with government investigations.
