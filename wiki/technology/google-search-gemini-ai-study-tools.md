## Summary

[[Google]] announced a broad set of AI-powered study tools across [[Google Search]] and [[Gemini]], including interactive visuals, 3D simulations, customized practice quizzes, a Lens-based problem-solving experience, and a dedicated student hub, as it competes with [[OpenAI]] and education startups like Knowt and Gauth for student mindshare. The features aim to make Gemini the default AI assistant students turn to for learning and studying.

## Core Concepts

- **AI Overviews / AI Mode in Search** — generates interactive visuals and simulations (e.g., a pH scale) tailored to follow-up questions.
- **[[Search]] practice quizzes** — users can prompt Search to generate custom quizzes on any subject, including exam prep like the SAT.
- **[[Lens]]** — rolling out an interactive learning mode that lets students photograph a problem and get explanations, mistake identification, and guidance.
- **[[Gemini]] study hub** — a dedicated app hub combining study notebooks, flashcards, and practice quizzes.
- **Gemini Live multi-step research** — lets students launch background research reports and discuss findings conversationally afterward.
- **3D simulations in Gemini** — functional, interactive 3D models (e.g., DNA structure) generated from prompts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Google is bundling** visuals, quizzes, document-based study guides, and 3D simulations across both Search and Gemini.
- **Lens gains a tutoring mode** that identifies mistakes and offers guidance, not just answers.
- **Gemini can generate study documents** from uploaded PDFs, slides, and handwritten notes.
- **Gemini Live now supports** asynchronous multi-step research with follow-up voice discussion.
- **Competitive context**: Google is directly targeting OpenAI and dedicated study apps like Knowt and Gauth.
