## Summary
[[Sierra]], the enterprise customer-service AI startup co-founded by [[Bret Taylor]], raised $1.75B in a Series F at a $30B valuation, doubling its $15.8B valuation from under seven months earlier. ARR topped $400M in October, implying roughly 75x revenue. The round signals strong investor conviction in enterprise [[AI Agents]], alongside risk from high expectations and crowded competition.

## Core Concepts
- [[Sierra]]: Builds AI agents as a "digital front door" for companies, charging for outcomes rather than usage.
- [[Bret Taylor]]: Co-founder; former Salesforce co-CEO.
- [[Series F Funding]]: Led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, with Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global participating.
- [[Outcome-Based Pricing]]: Sierra's model of charging for results rather than usage.
- [[Annual Recurring Revenue]]: Topped $400M in October, up over $150M in Q3.
- [[Enterprise AI Agents]]: Category attracting heavy capital, including ElevenLabs, Wonderful, and Decagon.

## Key Takeaways
- **Valuation Jump**: $15.8B to $30B in under seven months.
- **Round Size**: $1.75B Series F, among largest ever for a US ML company.
- **Revenue Growth**: ARR above $400M in October; over $150M added in Q3.
- **Multiple**: Roughly 75x ARR, not audited full-year revenue.
- **Customers**: Nearly half of Fortune 50, including BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo, JPMorganChase.
- **Peer Valuations**: ElevenLabs $22B, Wonderful $5B, Decagon $4.5B.
- **Risk**: High bar to sustain growth and prove automated-service economics.
