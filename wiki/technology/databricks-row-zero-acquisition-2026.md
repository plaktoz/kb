## Summary

Databricks acquired Row Zero, a cloud spreadsheet startup capable of handling over 1 million live rows, integrating it with its AI agent Genie to let business analysts query data through a familiar spreadsheet interface. The deal is part of Databricks' active 2026 acquisition strategy, following purchases of Quotient AI, SiftD.ai, Panther, and Electric. CEO Ali Ghodsi has signaled the company intends to continue acquiring startups aggressively.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Databricks]]** — enterprise data and AI platform; acquirer; recently closed $5B funding at a $190B valuation
- **[[Row Zero]]** — cloud spreadsheet startup handling 1M+ live rows; founded by former AWS and Tableau engineers; raised $10M at ~$40M valuation in May 2025
- **[[Genie (Databricks)]]** — Databricks' AI agent that allows natural-language querying of business data
- **[[Lakebase]]** — Databricks' database product; broader platform context for acquisitions
- **[[Business Intelligence (BI)]]** — combining BI, agents, and spreadsheets is the stated thesis behind this deal
- **[[M&A Strategy]]** — Databricks' 2026 rollup covers evaluation (Quotient AI), notebooks (SiftD.ai), security (Panther), and lightweight databases (Electric)

## Key Takeaways

- **Row Zero acquired** to integrate familiar spreadsheet UX with Databricks' data platform.
- **Genie + spreadsheet combo** keeps data secure inside Databricks' cloud, eliminating insecure file exports.
- **Spreadsheets as interface**: Ghodsi sees them as the preferred analyst tool — logical to embed AI inside them.
- **Row Zero background**: ex-AWS and Tableau engineers; raised $10M at ~$40M valuation (May 2025).
- **2026 acquisition spree**: Quotient AI, SiftD.ai, Panther ($1.4B prior valuation), Electric (PGlite) already acquired.
- **Scale**: Databricks closed $5B funding, reached $7B annualized revenue; expects more acquisitions.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Platform Gravity]]**: Databricks is building lock-in by owning the full analyst workflow — from raw data to spreadsheet output — making switching costs prohibitive once each layer is integrated.
- **[[Adjacency Expansion]]**: Each acquisition (notebooks, security, lightweight DB, spreadsheets) targets an adjacent pain point for existing Databricks customers, reducing friction to upsell without requiring new personas.
