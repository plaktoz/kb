## Summary

At Meta's annual Connect 2026 event, [[Mark Zuckerberg]] unveiled a sweeping expansion of [[Muse]], Meta's personal AI agent, powered by the new **Muse Spark** multimodal model. New capabilities include a real-time digital avatar, hands-free smart glasses integration, autonomous Mac desktop control, a dedicated email address, and deep retail integrations with over a dozen commerce partners. Monetization will rely on small transaction fees rather than a subscription paywall, keeping Muse free for most users.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Muse Spark]]**: The new multimodal model powering Muse's expanded capabilities announced at Connect 2026, enabling real-time avatar generation and richer agentic task execution.
- **[[Muse Realtime Avatar]]**: A new model that lets users create a personalized visual avatar and conduct real-time video chat through it — the default is named "Jolly."
- **[[AI Computer Use]]**: Muse's forthcoming Mac desktop control feature, enabling autonomous operation of desktop apps in a manner comparable to [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] computer-use offerings.
- **[[AI Agent Monetization]]**: Meta's transaction-fee revenue model for Muse — free for most usage, with small fees on commerce actions — contrasts with subscription models used by competitors.
- **[[Meta Smart Glasses]]**: Meta's wearable hardware gaining Muse integration via wake word, enabling hands-free task automation such as meal logging and appointment booking.

## Key Takeaways

- **Digital avatar model**: Users can create a personalized avatar and video-chat with it in real time.
- **Smart glasses integration**: Muse accessible hands-free via wake word; arriving "in coming months."
- **Mac computer control**: Muse will autonomously operate desktop apps while the user steps away.
- **Own email address**: Muse can join email threads and manage correspondence independently.
- **14+ retail & productivity partners**: Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, Walmart, GitHub, Notion, Expedia, and more.
- **Transaction fee model**: Meta keeps Muse free for high-volume usage; monetizes via small commerce fees.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Platform Bundling]]**: By embedding Muse into smart glasses, email, desktop, and retail checkouts simultaneously, Meta is replicating the classic platform strategy — own the interface layer across contexts so the agent becomes infrastructure rather than an app users can easily substitute.
