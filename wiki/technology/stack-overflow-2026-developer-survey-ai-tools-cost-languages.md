## Summary
Stack Overflow's 2026 survey of 30,000+ developers shows AI coding tools are a daily habit (73%), yet only 20% use AI on production systems. The binding constraint is shifting from technology to cost, with employers capping usage and assigning cheaper models to routine work.

## Core Concepts
- [[Stack Overflow Developer Survey]]: 30,000+ respondents on AI habits, tools and technology choices.
- [[Claude Code]] vs [[GitHub Copilot]]: Claude Code named most often (66%), Copilot at 59% and growing from 26M to 50M users.
- [[Anthropic]] and [[OpenAI]]: lead model providers, with [[Google]] third; Anthropic has a small edge in retention.
- [[Coding Agents]] and chatbots: 66% of AI users work in agents, 63% in chatbots, so many use both.
- [[Chinese Open-Source Models]]: share of US company tokens on OpenRouter rose from 4.5% to over 30% weekly, driven by price.
- [[AI Spending Controls]]: usage caps and spend-limit features sold by AI makers.
- [[SQL]]: moved to second most-used technology, ahead of HTML/CSS.
- [[Cursor]]: grew annualized revenue from $4 million to $2 billion in 18 months.

## Key Takeaways
- **Daily Habit**: 73% of AI coding tool users use them daily; 62% of all developers feel positive.
- **Agent Competition**: Claude Code leads agents at 66%, but Copilot is growing fast.
- **Verifiable Work First**: 67% generate code in familiar areas; 61% debug with AI.
- **Production Line**: Only 20% use AI to deploy, operate or troubleshoot production systems.
- **Price Pressure**: Chinese models exceeded 30% of US company tokens on OpenRouter since Feb 2026.
- **Budget Limits**: 38% of developers' organizations set usage limits; Uber exhausted its annual AI budget in four months.
- **Model Routing**: 27% say employers assign separate models to separate kinds of work.
- **Quality vs Cost**: 75% of developers put quality first, while employers watch the bill.
- **Stable Languages**: Top-10 technologies barely changed apart from SQL passing HTML/CSS.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Delegation follows verifiability** — People hand work to an automated tool where they can still judge and reject its output, and withhold it where errors hit live systems. · *model:* [[Trust but Verify]]
→ explains: Verifiable Work First, Production Line

**T2. Cost constraints drive matching of capability to task** — When spending becomes the binding limit, buyers route routine work to cheaper substitutes and reserve the best option for jobs that need it. · *model:* [[Substitution Effect]]
→ explains: Price Pressure, Budget Limits, Model Routing, Quality vs Cost

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the central finding of Stack Overflow's 2026 survey regarding AI coding tools?
**A**: AI use is routine (73% of users go daily), but developers set their own limits on trust and spending. The constraint is increasingly the bill rather than the technology.

**Q2**: What evidence shows where developers draw their line of trust?
**A**: 67% use AI to generate code in areas they know and 61% to debug, but only 20% use it to deploy, operate and troubleshoot production systems.

**Q3**: How are companies responding to rising AI costs, and what does it imply for model choice?
**A**: 38% of developers' organizations set usage limits and 27% assign different models to different work, so the model a developer prefers and the one the employer allows increasingly differ.
