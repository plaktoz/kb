## Summary

Anthropic released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, two updated models up to 30% faster and 20% cheaper, as it prepares an IPO the week of Nov. 16 that could raise up to $100 billion. The listing, expected at a valuation above $1 trillion (down from a $2 trillion high), is seen as a gauge of how long the AI spending boom can last. Slowing sales growth, an operating loss, and political and regulatory risks weigh on the outlook.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anthropic]] IPO**: Targeted for the week of Nov. 16; up to $100B raised, which would be the largest IPO on record.
- **Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2**: Two models built on the same underlying model; Fable blocks most cybersecurity and biology tasks so it can go to all users.
- **Tiered access**: Mythos 5.2 has fewer restrictions and is limited to vetted [[Cybersecurity]] and biomedical research organizations.
- **Regulatory risk**: A Commerce Department order over [[National Security]] concerns pulled the first versions in June; Democrats regaining Congress raise fears of a tech crackdown.
- **[[Data Center]] restrictions**: Ballot measures limiting construction could hamper [[OpenAI]], [[Meta]], [[Microsoft]], [[Google]] and Anthropic.
- **AI spending boom**: Big tech's hundreds of billions in AI spending are a market worry after quarterly earnings spooked investors.

## Key Takeaways

- **Performance**: New models run up to 30% faster on complex tasks such as coding, at 20% lower cost.
- **Revenue**: Q3 revenue hit $17B, up from $1.4B a year earlier.
- **Growth slowing**: Competition from [[OpenAI]] and cheaper, often Chinese, models slowed sales growth.
- **Losses**: Q3 operating loss of $567M from paying more for short-term compute.
- **Valuation**: Expected above $1T, down from a $2T high in recent weeks.
- **Launch history**: Original models were pulled days after their June launch over foreign-national access controls.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Anthropic announce, and why does the timing matter?
**A**: It released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, up to 30% faster and 20% cheaper, just before an IPO planned for the week of Nov. 16. The IPO is expected to raise up to $100 billion and is seen as a gauge of the AI spending boom.

**Q2**: How do Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 differ?
**A**: Both are built on the same underlying model. Fable 5.2 has safeguards blocking most cybersecurity and biology tasks so all users can access it, while Mythos 5.2 has fewer restrictions and is limited to vetted cybersecurity and biomedical organizations.

**Q3**: What risks could weigh on Anthropic's valuation and growth plans?
**A**: Slowing sales growth amid competition, a $567M operating loss, a market pullback over AI spending, and possible regulatory crackdowns after Democrats regained Congress. Ballot measures restricting data center construction could also limit growth.
