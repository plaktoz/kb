## Summary
AI-assisted "vibe coding" has collapsed the technical moat that once protected software startups, since competitors can now assemble comparable apps in minutes. [[Fractl]] founder [[Rime Salmi]] argues the only defensible advantages left are proprietary data, which takes years to accumulate, and distribution, where founders who build owned audiences beat those renting attention. Global venture capital reflects the anxiety: $510B flowed into startups in H1 2026 — more than all of 2025 — with OpenAI and Anthropic alone capturing 43% of it.

## Core Concepts
- [[Vibe Coding]]: AI-assisted app assembly that lets anyone ship a working app in roughly ten seconds, collapsing the old engineering-effort moat.
- [[Distribution (Startup Moat)]]: Owning a direct audience or channel — becoming your own "media company" — rather than renting reach through ads or influencers.
- [[Proprietary Data]]: High-quality, in-depth datasets that take years to build and can't be replicated by copying code.
- [[Rime Salmi]]: Founder and CEO of [[Fractl]], an invite-only network running founder-and-investor events at conferences like Token2049 and Consensus.
- [[Venture Capital]]: Global VC hit $510B in H1 2026, more than all of 2025, heavily concentrated in OpenAI and Anthropic.

## Key Takeaways
- **Vibe Coding Collapse**: AI lets anyone assemble a working app in ~10 seconds, erasing the technical moat.
- **Two Moats Remain**: Salmi says only proprietary data and distribution still defend a startup.
- **Capital Concentration**: $510B global VC in H1 2026 (more than all of 2025); OpenAI + Anthropic took 43%.
- **Founder-as-Channel**: Owned audiences are "captive," unlike reach rented via ads or clipping farms.
- **Gatekeeping as Moat**: Fractl vets invite-only events to exclude "posers," itself a distribution/data play.
- **Media Company Mandate**: Founders are told every company must "turn into a media company" to stay relevant.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models
- **[[Economic Moat]]**: The article directly reframes Warren Buffett's economic moat concept for the AI era, arguing engineering execution no longer creates a durable moat — only data and distribution do.
