## Summary

[[Tesla]] formally launched the [[Cybercab]] — a two-seater, pedal-and-steering-wheel-free autonomous vehicle — in Austin, Texas, staging hundreds of units across multiple U.S. cities with 420 autonomous vehicles registered in Texas at launch. The rollout is framed as a decisive fork in the road: either Tesla begins reshaping urban transportation, or it confirms itself as fundamentally just an automaker. Challenges persist, including a geofenced-only operating model and a mid-2026 decline in paid robotaxi miles on the existing Model Y fleet.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Cybercab]]** — Tesla's purpose-built robotaxi: small, gold, two-seater, no steering wheel or pedals; grew from an effort to build a cheaper, lighter EV platform
- **[[Tesla Robotaxi]]** — Modified Model Y fleet operating since June 2025; paid miles were declining by mid-2026 despite the cumulative launch narrative
- **[[Geofencing]]** — Current Tesla AV operations are confined to geofenced areas, a constraint [[Elon Musk]] himself previously said disqualifies a system from being "real self-driving"
- **[[Ashok Elluswamy]]** — Tesla AI head who declared at launch: "streets won't be the same anymore"
- **[[Waymo]]** — Competitor benchmark; Tesla bulls argue Cybercab's cost-per-unit advantage over Waymo is decisive

## Key Takeaways

- **Launch scale**: 420 autonomous vehicles registered to Tesla in Texas at publication time.
- **No controls**: Cybercab has no steering wheel or pedals — hardware commits Tesla to full autonomy.
- **Cost thesis**: Smaller, lighter, less battery-intensive platform pitched as a structural cost edge over [[Waymo]].
- **Paid miles declining**: Model Y robotaxi paid miles were falling by mid-2026, before Cybercab launched.
- **Geofence problem**: Operations still geofenced — contradicting Musk's own prior standard for "real self-driving."
- **Binary outcome**: Launch either marks the start of urban transport transformation or confirms Tesla's automaker ceiling.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Innovator's Dilemma]]**: Tesla is betting a cheaper, purpose-built platform (Cybercab) disrupts incumbents from below — but only if it can escape the geofenced constraints that have kept its prior robotaxi miles in decline.
- **[[Crossing the Chasm]]**: The Cybercab moves from tech demo to formal commercial launch; the chasm is geofence-free, reliable operation at scale — which Tesla has not yet demonstrated.
