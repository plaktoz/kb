## Summary
Reflection AI co-founder Misha Laskin publicly called "AI agents" an overused, poorly-defined term — even as his own Nvidia- and Sequoia-backed startup builds autonomous AI coding agents. He remains bullish on the category's potential for reasoning and world models, illustrating a gap between hype-driven jargon and grounded engineering use.

## Core Concepts
- [[AI Agents]] as a contested, loosely-defined industry term.
- [[Reflection AI]], a $130M-funded startup founded by former [[Google DeepMind]] researchers building autonomous coding agents.
- [[World Models]] as an adjacent research direction Laskin connects to agent potential.

## Key Takeaways
- **Jargon Fatigue**: Even agent-startup founders admit "AI agents" is overused and ill-defined.
- **Funding Signal**: Reflection AI raised $130M from Nvidia's NVentures and Sequoia Capital.
- **Founder Pedigree**: Both co-founders are former Google DeepMind researchers.
