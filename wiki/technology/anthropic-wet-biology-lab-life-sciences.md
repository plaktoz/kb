## Summary

[[Anthropic]] has confirmed it operates a physical wet biology lab in the Bay Area, using its AI models to run real-world biological experiments focused on fundamental biology rather than drug discovery. The lab grew out of Anthropic's acquisition of stealth AI biotech [[Coefficient Bio]] in April, and is accompanied by a new Life Sciences Verification Program giving vetted researchers access to Anthropic's most powerful models. The announcement arrives amid internal tension over existential AI risk, with researchers and leadership publicly citing odds above 10% that AI could pose an extinction-level threat within a decade.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anthropic]]**: AI safety company now operating a physical wet biology lab in the Bay Area
- **[[Coefficient Bio]]**: Stealth AI biotech acquired by Anthropic in April, whose acquisition underpins the lab
- **[[Wet Lab]]**: A physical laboratory conducting hands-on biological experiments, used by Anthropic to validate AI-driven biology findings
- **[[Life Sciences Verification Program]]**: Anthropic initiative granting vetted researchers access to its most capable models for biological research
- **[[Protein Design]]**: Area of published research by Anthropic alongside the lab announcement, alongside biomolecular modeling
- **[[AI Existential Risk]]**: Internal and leadership-level concern at Anthropic that advanced AI could pose extinction-level danger within a decade
- **[[Novo Nordisk]]**: Pharmaceutical partner; Anthropic intentionally avoids drug discovery focus to not appear competitive with such partners
- **[[Eric Kauderer-Abrams]]**: Anthropic's Head of Life Sciences, who confirmed active wet lab experiments to Reuters
- **[[Dario Amodei]]**: Anthropic CEO who has cited bioterrorism as a top AI risk and called for industry self-regulation

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Dual-Use Dilemma]]**: An organization building potentially dangerous capabilities while simultaneously warning about those dangers embodies the classic dual-use tension — the same tools that accelerate beneficial biology research lower barriers to bioweapon design, making safety commitments and governance structures load-bearing rather than cosmetic.
- **[[Skin in the Game]]**: By operating a physical wet lab, Anthropic takes on direct liability and epistemic exposure to biology's ground truth, aligning incentives toward genuine safety rather than purely theoretical alignment research.
