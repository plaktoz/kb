## Summary

European startups raised €44.1B in H1 2026 (a 27% rebound) but deal count fell to a six-year low as AI absorbed 60.3% of all venture deal value, up from 37.9% in 2025. Capital is concentrating into fewer, larger mega-rounds while seed-stage deal count fell 44% year-over-year, a structural shift analysts warn will thin the founder pipeline reaching growth stage in 2027-2028.

## Core Concepts

- [[European Venture Capital]] — €44.1B raised in H1 2026 across just 1,740 deals, the lowest half-year deal count since 2020
- [[AI Funding Concentration]] — AI crossed a majority (60.3%) of European VC deal value for the first time in a six-month period
- [[Seed Funding Drought]] — seed deal count down 44% YoY as fund sizes grow and minimum check sizes rise
- [[Mega-Round]] — financings above €100M, now more than half of all H1 deal value
- [[Nscale]] — London AI infrastructure company, largest H1 2026 European deal (€2.9B)
- [[Isomorphic Labs]] — Alphabet-backed AI drug design company, $2.1B Series B

## Key Takeaways

- **AI majority**: AI absorbed 60.3% of European deal value, up from 37.9% in full-year 2025
- **Deal count at 6-year low**: just 1,740 deals in H1 2026, down from ~2,000 in H1 2024
- **Seed collapse**: seed-stage deal count fell 44% YoY in Q1 2026; Series A volumes down 30%
- **UK dominance**: UK companies raised €18.7B — 3x Germany's total — claiming 6 of the top 10 deals
- **Structural cause**: rising median fund size (€60M, up from €50M) pushes minimum viable check sizes above what seed startups need
- **Series A pricing up**: median rounds now ~$14M, up from $8-10M a few years ago

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Power Law Distribution]]**: Venture returns are famously power-law distributed, and this data shows capital allocation following the same pattern structurally — a few mega-deals now account for the majority of value, squeezing out the broad base of smaller bets that power laws actually depend on to find outliers.
