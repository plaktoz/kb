## Summary

Worker.ai acquired Prague-based real-time voice AI startup DeltaGen for an undisclosed sum, announced November 12, 2026 alongside the launch of Worker's company operating system for small businesses. DeltaGen's technology listens to live conversations, extracts decisions and commitments, and Worker turns them into assigned tasks. The deal is backed by Veeam founder Ratmir Timashev and reads as a new entrant buying technology and talent for a small-business platform.

## Core Concepts

- [[Worker.ai]]: Company operating system for small businesses, holding plans, responsibilities, key numbers, and processes where employees and [[AI Workers]] share work.
- [[DeltaGen]]: Prague startup (launched 2024, ~10 people, $1.2M pre-seed) building [[Real-Time Voice AI]] that captures decisions, commitments, and next steps.
- [[Ratmir Timashev]]: Veeam founder backing Worker to help manufacturers, service firms, and family businesses.
- [[John Roberts]]: Worker co-founder and CEO, a former private equity operator who had used DeltaGen's technology previously.
- [[Acqui-hire]]: All three DeltaGen founders join Worker; the Prague team stays and Worker plans to grow engineering there.
- [[Conversational AI Consolidation]]: Larger platforms are absorbing voice AI, e.g. Salesforce's pending purchase of [[Cresta]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal**: Worker.ai bought DeltaGen; terms undisclosed, announced November 12, 2026.
- **Product Fit**: DeltaGen converts live conversation into assigned tasks inside Worker.
- **Team**: All three DeltaGen founders join; roughly 10 staff remain in Prague.
- **Funding History**: DeltaGen previously raised a $1.2M pre-seed, backed by Forward Venture Capital and Techstars.
- **Thesis**: AI gives small businesses more capacity without longer owner workdays, per Timashev.
- **Market Signal**: Differs from Cresta's enterprise customer-service focus by targeting everyday business conversations.
