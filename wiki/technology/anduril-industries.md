## Summary

Anduril Industries is an American defense technology company founded in 2017 by Palmer Luckey and co-founders from Palantir and Oculus, applying Silicon Valley iteration methods to autonomous weapons systems. As of mid-2026, it is valued at $61 billion with ~7,000 employees and $2 billion in 2025 revenue, having grown through aggressive acquisitions and major US military contracts. Its core differentiator is the [[Lattice AI Platform]], which fuses heterogeneous sensor data into a unified battlefield operating picture.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Anduril Industries]]**: Autonomous defense startup disrupting traditional defense contractors by pre-emptively developing products before formal Pentagon requests.
- **[[Palmer Luckey]]**: Oculus Rift creator and co-founder; connection to [[Peter Thiel]] and [[Founders Fund]] through 2014 Founders Fund retreat.
- **[[Lattice AI Platform]]**: Core software fusing data from diverse sensors; powers ~290 CBP border surveillance towers along the US-Mexico border and used in ABMS military exercises.
- **[[Arsenal-1]]**: ~$1 billion hyperscale manufacturing facility under construction at Rickenbacker International Airport, Ohio, in partnership with Ohio State University.
- **[[Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)]]**: Air Force program in which Anduril's [[Fury (YFQ-44)]] drone competes against General Atomics' YFQ-42A.
- **[[JADC2]] / ABMS**: Joint All-Domain Command and Control — digital battlefield architecture connecting jets, drones, ships, and soldiers across incompatible data systems.
- **[[IVAS]]**: Integrated Visual Augmentation System; Anduril took over development from Microsoft in February 2025, with up to 121,000 units potentially ordered by the US Army.

## Key Takeaways

- **Founding**: Born from 2014 Founders Fund retreat; co-founders recruited from [[Palantir]] and [[Oculus]].
- **Valuation trajectory**: $1B (2019) → $4.6B (2021) → $14B (2024) → $30.5B (2025) → $61B (2026).
- **Revenue**: $2 billion in 2025 revenue; raised $5B in 2026 alone.
- **Lattice**: Powers ~290 CBP border towers; described as a "virtual border wall."
- **Fury drone**: Stealthy CCA drone with maiden flight Oct 31, 2025; derived from acquired Blue Force Technologies.
- **Ghost X**: Selected by US Army in Sept 2024 for Company-Level Small UAS; 75-min flight, 25 km range.
- **Ghost Shark**: Autonomous submarine for Royal Australian Navy; first delivery Nov 2025, ahead of schedule.
- **Arsenal-1**: Designed for hyperscale autonomous weapons production; ~$1B facility.
- **IVAS takeover**: Anduril assumed Microsoft's contract in Feb 2025; up to 121,000 Army units.
- **Controversy**: CBP Sentry Towers criticized for pushing migrants toward dangerous routes; China sanctioned Anduril over Taiwan arms sales.
- **UAE partnership**: Nov 2025 deal with Edge Group criticized over alleged Sudan arms links.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Pre-emptive Product Development]]**: Anduril builds products before Pentagon requests — inverting the traditional defense contractor model where requirements drive R&D — enabling faster iteration and creating proprietary leverage over procurement cycles.
- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: By acquiring firms across drones (Area-I), underwater vehicles (Dive Technologies), rocket motors (Adranos), radar (Numerica), and comms (Klas), Anduril replicates the Silicon Valley platform strategy in hardware-intensive defense, reducing supply chain dependency and capturing more margin.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Anduril's core strategic differentiator from traditional defense contractors?
**A**: Anduril pre-emptively develops products before formal Pentagon requests and applies Silicon Valley iteration methods to defense hardware and software, rather than building to slow government specifications.

**Q2**: What is the Lattice platform and where is it deployed at scale?
**A**: Lattice is Anduril's core AI software that fuses data from diverse sensors into a unified picture; as of 2023, approximately 290 CBP surveillance towers along the US-Mexico border operate using Lattice.

**Q3**: How does Anduril's Arsenal-1 facility fit into its long-term strategy?
**A**: Arsenal-1 is a ~$1 billion manufacturing facility under construction in Ohio designed for hyperscale autonomous weapons production, enabling Anduril to manufacture at volumes that traditional defense primes cannot match.
