## Summary
Workday announced on Nov. 23, 2026 that it completed its acquisition of C1.ai, an agentic identity platform for people and AI agents. Workday positions itself as the identity governance layer for a hybrid workforce, combining its employee data with C1.ai's access controls and real-time policy enforcement.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Workday]]**: Enterprise platform for HR, finance and IT, serving 11,500+ organisations including over 65% of the Fortune 500.
- **[[C1.ai]]**: Agentic identity platform offering scoped access, enforced policies, human approval and an audit record of every action.
- **[[Identity Governance]]**: Managing who and what can access business systems, from applications to cloud infrastructure.
- **[[AI Agents]]**: Autonomous workers that need permissioning, monitoring and security enforcement like human staff.
- **[[Hybrid Workforce]]**: People and AI agents managed across a full lifecycle.
- **[[Gerrit Kazmaier]]** and **[[Alex Bovee]]**: Workday product chief and C1.ai CEO quoted on the deal.

## Key Takeaways
- **Deal closed**: Workday completed the C1.ai acquisition on Nov. 23, 2026.
- **Positioning**: Workday aims to be the identity governance layer for people and agents.
- **Data advantage**: Every hire, job change and exit is already a Workday event.
- **Agent controls**: Permissioning, monitoring and real-time security enforcement across employees and agents.
- **Scale**: C1.ai reaches global scale on day one through Workday's customer base.
- **Adoption claim**: C1.ai says the fastest path to AI adoption at scale is the governed path.
