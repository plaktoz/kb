## Summary
Google is bringing computer use to its Gemini desktop app on macOS, letting the AI complete multistep tasks across native Mac apps, local files and websites in the background. TechCrunch notes Google is late versus OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta. The feature is U.S.- and English-only and requires a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Computer Use]]**: An agent takes actions directly on the user's computer across local files, apps and the web.
- **[[Google Gemini]]**: Desktop app for Mac gains agentic capability, alongside the broader rollout of Gemini "Tasks".
- **[[AI Agents]]**: Tasks include signing into sites, purchases and appointments; "CC" is Google's family-organizing agent in early testing.
- **[[Human-in-the-Loop Oversight]]**: Users can pause, stop and approve actions before they happen.
- **Competitors**: [[OpenAI]], [[Anthropic]] and [[Meta]] already offer agentic desktop features.

## Key Takeaways
- **Launch**: Gemini for Mac can navigate native apps and browse sites in the background.
- **Live Preview**: Users watch the agent in chat while their screen and cursor stay free.
- **Use Cases**: Form filling, batch file tasks, organizing folders, building slideshows from dashboards.
- **Late Entrant**: Rivals have offered agentic desktop features for some time.
- **Availability**: English, U.S. only; requires Gemini Mac app and AI Plus, Pro or Ultra.
- **Controls**: Pause, stop and pre-approval of actions are built in.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Google announce for Gemini?
**A**: Computer use in the Gemini desktop app on macOS, letting the AI complete tasks on the user's behalf across native Mac apps and websites in the background.

**Q2**: How does the feature work from the user's perspective?
**A**: After the user gives instructions, they see the agent working in a live preview in chat while keeping their screen and cursor free. They can pause, stop, and approve actions before they happen.

**Q3**: Who can use it now, and what tasks is it suggested for?
**A**: English-language U.S. users with the Mac app and a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription. Suggested tasks include form filling, repetitive batch file work, organizing files and creating slideshows.
