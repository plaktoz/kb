## Summary

[[Elon Musk]] publicly endorsed [[Dario Amodei]]'s call for AI companies to slow development and prioritize safety, posting "Dario is right" on X. However, analysts argue the endorsement is unlikely to alter Tesla's or xAI's actual AI trajectories, given massive financial commitments already underway. Musk personally favors peer review among leading AI firms over government regulation as the accountability mechanism.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dario Amodei]] Slowdown Call**: Anthropic's CEO published an essay urging AI companies to deliberately slow development pace to prioritize safety — endorsed publicly by [[Elon Musk]].
- **[[Tesla]] AI Roadmap**: Rolling out a semi-autonomous ride-share network and targeting 1 million [[Optimus]] humanoid robots by 2030, powered by [[Grok AI]].
- **[[SpaceX]] AI Expansion**: Plans orbital data center satellite demonstrations by late 2027; spent roughly $23.5 billion on AI capital expenditures in H1 2026.
- **Peer Review Preference**: Musk favors accountability via leading AI firms reviewing each other's work rather than government-imposed regulation.
- **Analyst Skepticism**: Any slowdown in Tesla or SpaceX timelines would more likely stem from overly ambitious planning than deliberate deceleration driven by safety concerns.

## Key Takeaways

- Musk endorsed Amodei's safety call publicly, but financial commitments make an actual slowdown implausible.
- Tesla targets 1 million [[Optimus]] humanoid robots deployed by 2030.
- SpaceX spent ~$23.5 billion on AI capex in just the first half of 2026.
- SpaceX plans orbital data center satellite demonstrations by late 2027.
- Musk prefers peer review among AI labs over government regulation as the safety backstop.
- Analysts see overambitious planning — not safety restraint — as the more likely cause of any timeline slippage.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Revealed Preference]]**: Musk's verbal endorsement of slowing AI conflicts with Tesla's and SpaceX's actual capital deployment — $23.5B in AI capex in a single half-year is not the behavior of an entity slowing down, revealing where the true priorities lie.
- **[[Cheap Talk]]**: Without enforceable commitments or operational definitions of "slowing down," public endorsements of AI safety by executives with conflicting financial incentives carry minimal informational content about future behavior.
