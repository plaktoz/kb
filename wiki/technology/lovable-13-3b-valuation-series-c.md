## Summary

[[Lovable]], the Stockholm "vibe-coding" startup, confirmed a $13.3 billion valuation after raising $400 million in a Series C led by [[Menlo Ventures]] and the Scaleup Europe Fund — doubling its valuation from $6.6 billion just eight months earlier. By September 2026, annualized run-rate revenue had climbed to $600M (up from $500M in June), with two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies now using the platform. Co-founder [[Fabian Hedin]] frames Lovable not as a code-output tool but as a platform that produces "a product, and increasingly so, a business."

## Core Concepts

- **[[Vibe Coding]]**: The category of AI tools, including Lovable and competitor [[Replit]], that let non-engineers generate working software from plain-language prompts.
- **[[Series C Funding]]**: Lovable's third major round, following a $300M raise in December 2025 at a $6.6B valuation — also led by Menlo Ventures; total raised exceeds $700M across two rounds in ~8 months.
- **[[In-House Model Strategy]]**: Lovable offers its own trained AI model alongside frontier options, reducing dependence on any single foundation-model provider.
- **[[Enterprise Adoption]]**: Microsoft, Nvidia, and Deutsche Telekom are named customers; apps on the platform collectively draw nearly 1 billion monthly views.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: Lovable hosts 60 million projects; combined app traffic nears 1 billion monthly views.
- **Revenue growth**: ARR grew from $500M (June 2026) to $600M+ (September 2026).
- **Enterprise penetration**: Two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies now use the platform.
- **Positioning shift**: Hedin explicitly positions Lovable's output as a "product" or "business," not code.
- **Infrastructure**: Signed a multiyear deal with Google Cloud in June for a 5x usage increase.
- **Competitive position**: Lovable is now Menlo Ventures' largest single investment after Anthropic; rival Replit reached a $9B valuation in March 2026.
