## Summary

[[Lovable]], the Stockholm "vibe-coding" startup, confirmed a $13.3 billion valuation after raising $400 million in a Series C led by [[Menlo Ventures]] and the Scaleup Europe Fund — doubling its valuation from $6.6 billion just eight months earlier. The round underscores continued investor appetite for AI tools that turn natural-language instructions into working software.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Vibe Coding]]**: The category of AI tools, including Lovable and competitor [[Replit]], that let non-engineers generate working software from plain-language prompts.
- **[[Series C Funding]]**: Lovable's third major round, following a $330M raise in December 2025 at a $6.6B valuation — also led by Menlo Ventures.
- **[[In-House Model Strategy]]**: Lovable offers its own trained AI model alongside frontier options, reducing dependence on any single foundation-model provider.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: Lovable hosts 60 million projects attracting 900 million monthly visitors.
- **Revenue**: Hit $500M in annualized run-rate revenue in June 2026.
- **Infrastructure**: Signed a multiyear deal with Google Cloud in June for a 5x usage increase.
- **Portfolio play**: Backed Danish hardware-design startup Atech, extending vibe-coding beyond software.
- **Competitive position**: Lovable is now Menlo Ventures' largest single investment after Anthropic; rival Replit reached a $9B valuation in March 2026.
