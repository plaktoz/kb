## Summary

IBM and Yotta Data Services announced general availability of a Sovereign Agentic AI Platform for Indian organisations, pairing IBM watsonx Orchestrate with Yotta's Shakti Cloud so enterprises can deploy and govern AI agents while keeping data, compute, and operations within India. The platform is live across Yotta's Panvel and Greater Noida data centre regions and targets workflows like security operations, document processing, and HR automation. Both companies frame the launch around India's evolving AI regulation, where the priority has shifted from what AI can do to how securely and compliantly it can be deployed.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM]] watsonx Orchestrate**: The agentic control plane in the stack — used to secure, govern, and manage AI agents as adoption scales across an enterprise.
- **[[Yotta Data Services]]**: Indian sovereign cloud infrastructure provider operating hyperscale data centre parks in Panvel (Navi Mumbai) and Greater Noida (Delhi NCR); also runs the MeitY-empanelled Yntraa cloud.
- **[[Shakti Cloud]]**: Yotta's GPU/compute layer providing the networking, security, and operational infrastructure for enterprise AI workloads.
- **[[Shakti Studio]]**: Yotta's AI platform for exploring, fine-tuning, deploying, and running open-source and proprietary models.
- **[[Sovereign AI]]**: The organizing principle of the platform — anchoring data, inference, models, and governance controls within national borders rather than on foreign infrastructure.
- **[[Agentic AI]]**: AI agents deployed at enterprise scale with built-in governance, security, and compliance controls.

## Key Takeaways

- **General availability**: Sovereign Agentic AI Platform launched for Indian organisations on September 29, 2026.
- **Stack**: [[IBM]] watsonx Orchestrate (control plane) + [[Shakti Cloud]] (GPU infra) + [[Shakti Studio]] (model dev/inference).
- **Data residency**: Data, operations, and governance controls stay within India.
- **Regions**: Live in Yotta's Panvel and Greater Noida cloud regions.
- **Use cases**: Security operations, document processing, HR automation, and more.
- **NVIDIA ties**: Yotta is an NVIDIA Cloud Partner and Exemplar cloud initiative member — one of only six global Reference Architecture Platform NCPs.
