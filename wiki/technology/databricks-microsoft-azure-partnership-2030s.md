## Summary

Databricks and Microsoft have extended their strategic partnership into the 2030s, deepening Databricks' commitment to the [[Azure]] cloud platform, including running its own core business operations on [[Azure Databricks]]. The deal expands use of Microsoft's [[Azure Cobalt]] Arm-based processors, with the Cobalt 200 delivering up to 50% better performance for data-intensive and agentic AI workloads. Databricks' AI tools — [[Genie]] and [[Unity AI Gateway]] — will be integrated across Microsoft's product ecosystem including Microsoft 365, Teams, Copilot, Power BI, and Microsoft Foundry.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Databricks]]**: Data and AI platform used by 20,000+ organizations globally, including 70% of Fortune 500; recently valued at $188 billion.
- **[[Azure Databricks]]**: The joint product Databricks will now use to run its own business operations — signaling eat-your-own-dogfood confidence.
- **[[Azure Cobalt]]**: Microsoft's Arm-based custom processors; Cobalt 200 offers 50% better performance over Cobalt 100 plus default memory encryption.
- **[[Genie]]**: Databricks' conversational analytics tool being integrated into Microsoft's product suite.
- **[[Unity AI Gateway]]**: Databricks' governance layer for models, agents, and costs; also entering the Microsoft ecosystem.
- **[[Microsoft Foundry]]**: Microsoft's platform for building and deploying AI models and agents, now a home for third-party AI integrations.
- **[[Enterprise AI]]**: The partnership aims to help enterprises unify data and ground AI in business knowledge at scale.

## Key Takeaways

- Databricks will run its own operations on Azure Databricks, validating the platform at enterprise scale.
- Azure Cobalt 200 delivers up to 50% better performance than Cobalt 100 for AI workloads.
- Genie and Unity AI Gateway will embed into Microsoft 365, Teams, Copilot, Power BI, and Foundry.
- Partnership covers thousands of existing Azure Databricks customers including Banco Bradesco and Unilever.
- Databricks $188B funding round finalized last week, expected to close before summer ends.
- Microsoft is on a cloud/AI partnership spree: AMD Helios rack-scale deal Monday, Mistral expansion Tuesday.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Eat Your Own Dog Food]]**: Databricks running its own business on Azure Databricks is a credibility signal — product confidence is most convincing when it's financially costly to fake.
- **[[Platform Lock-In]]**: Extending the deal into the 2030s and embedding tools deep into Microsoft's ecosystem creates bilateral switching costs, reinforcing the partnership's durability.
