## Summary
Swedish legal AI startup [[Legora]] is in early talks to raise capital at a valuation of at least $10 billion — nearly double the $5.6 billion it fetched just four months ago — driven by 50% quarter-over-quarter ARR growth to $150 million. The surge reflects both Legora's own traction and a broader boom in Swedish [[AI Startups]] like Lovable.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Legal AI]]**: Software that assists lawyers with document review, drafting, due diligence, and regulatory research without removing them from the decision loop.
- **[[Decacorn]]**: A startup valued above $10 billion; Legora's jump from $1.7B (Dec 2025) to $5.6B to a possible $10-12B in eight months illustrates how fast AI-sector valuations are compounding.
- **[[Reverse Innovation Hub]] (Stockholm)**: Legora, Lovable ($13.3B), and other Swedish AI startups are drawing increasing attention from Silicon Valley VCs like Sequoia and Andreessen Horowitz.
- **[[Build vs. Buy]]**: Law firms like Kirkland & Ellis are choosing to build proprietary AI platforms rather than rely solely on vendors like Legora — a tension founder Max Junestrand argues is a false binary.

## Key Takeaways
- **Valuation multiple**: At $10-12B, Legora trades at 67-80x its current ARR run rate.
- **Growth metrics**: ARR grew 50% QoQ to $150M; customer base grew 25% in three months to ~1,500 firms.
- **Competitive landscape**: Rival Harvey is separately seeking a ~$15B valuation, making the two companies a bellwether for whether specialized legal-AI platforms can outlast foundation-model commoditization.
- **Scaling plan**: Legora intends to more than double headcount from 700 to 1,500 employees by year-end.
