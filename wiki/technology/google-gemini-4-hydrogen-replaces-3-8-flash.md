## Summary

Google released [[Gemini 4 Hydrogen]], the model that replaces [[Gemini 3.8 Flash]] across its products, with immediate and relaxed access unlike the staged [[Gemini 4 Argon]] and [[Gemini 4 Carbon]] rollouts. Google claims it is 2.5x faster than Flash 3.8 in high mode, cuts fabricated claims by more than 5x, and more than triples Flash 3.8's Terminal-Bench score. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores 54 in xhigh mode versus 50 for Argon.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Gemini 4 Hydrogen]]**: The new fast, economical tier replacing the Flash line most Google users rely on.
- **[[Gemini 3.8 Flash]]**: The predecessor model, now replaced across Google's products.
- **[[Gemini 4 Argon]] and [[Gemini 4 Carbon]]**: Earlier Gemini 4 releases with staged rollouts that began with cybersecurity researchers and were limited to the priciest subscription.
- **Elemental naming scheme**: Google's move from Pro/Flash labels to element names, which are less descriptive of each model's role.
- **Thinking levels**: Four reasoning settings (low, medium, high, and the new xhigh).
- **[[Terminal-Bench]]**: Code-generation benchmark on which Hydrogen more than triples Flash 3.8.
- **[[Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index]]**: Third-party index where Hydrogen xhigh (54) edges Argon (50).

## Key Takeaways

- **Access**: Available immediately and broadly, unlike gated Argon and Carbon rollouts.
- **Replacement**: Hydrogen has replaced Gemini 3.8 Flash across Google's products.
- **Speed**: 2.5x faster than Flash 3.8 even in high thinking mode.
- **Reliability**: Google reports over 5x fewer "fabricated claims" than the prior Flash.
- **Coding**: Terminal-Bench score more than triples that of Flash 3.8.
- **Benchmark parity**: Scores 54 versus Argon's 50 on the Artificial Analysis index.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Gemini 4 Hydrogen and what does it replace?
**A**: It is Google's new fast, economical Gemini model, and it replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash across the company's products. It is the model most people in Google's ecosystem will use.

**Q2**: What evidence does Google give that Hydrogen improves on Flash 3.8?
**A**: In high mode it is 2.5 times faster, it reduces fabricated claims by more than 5x, and it more than triples the Terminal-Bench score. It also scores 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index in xhigh mode versus 50 for Argon.

**Q3**: How does Hydrogen's rollout differ from Argon and Carbon, and why does it matter to users?
**A**: Argon and Carbon were staged, starting with cybersecurity researchers and limited to the most expensive subscription, while Hydrogen's limits are relaxed and access begins immediately. Most users therefore get the upgrade without paying for the top tier.
