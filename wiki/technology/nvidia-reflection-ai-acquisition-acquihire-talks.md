## Summary
[[Nvidia]] is in early talks to acquire, acquihire or invest more in open-weight model developer [[Reflection AI]], according to the [[Financial Times]], with a deal possible within weeks though talks could still collapse. Options include an outright purchase, a larger stake bundled with more chips and compute, or an acquihire that hires staff and licenses technology without taking control, likely avoiding lengthy regulatory review. The report lands five days after Reflection launched Beam, its first open-weight model, and follows Nvidia's recent Groq acquihire and Hugging Face purchase.

## Core Concepts
- **Deal structures**: Outright acquisition, increased stake (possibly with more compute supply), or an [[Acquihire]] that avoids control and likely sidesteps long [[Antitrust]] review.
- **Precedents**: [[Groq]] licensed its technology to Nvidia in December in a deal reported at $20 billion, with founder [[Jonathan Ross]] and senior executives moving to Nvidia while Groq kept its cloud business; Nvidia agreed Sept. 3 to buy [[Hugging Face]] for $12.9 billion.
- **Existing stake**: Nvidia led Reflection's $2 billion round in October 2025 and has invested $800 million in total; the latest round closed at a $25 billion pre-money valuation per CEO [[Misha Laskin]].
- **Founders**: Misha Laskin and [[Ioannis Antonoglou]], both ex-[[Google DeepMind]] researchers, founded Reflection in 2024.
- **Beam model**: A 501-billion-parameter [[Mixture of Experts]] open-weight model, pitched against [[DeepSeek]] and [[Kimi]] on coding and agentic work; [[Coding Agents]] were Reflection's first focus.
- **Compute supply**: Beam trained on Nvidia GB300 systems rented from [[SpaceX]] under a June deal worth up to $6.3 billion ($150 million/month at [[Colossus 2]] in Memphis through 2029); [[Nebius]] agreed in July to sell another $1 billion of capacity.
- **Other investors**: [[Sequoia Capital]], [[Lightspeed Venture Partners]] and [[DST Global]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Deal Talks**: Nvidia weighing buy, acquihire or larger investment in Reflection AI.
- **Timing**: Agreement could come within weeks; talks may still collapse.
- **Acquihire Advantage**: Hiring staff and licensing tech likely avoids lengthy regulatory review.
- **Groq Precedent**: Groq licensing deal reported at $20B; leadership moved to Nvidia.
- **Hugging Face Precedent**: Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B.
- **Existing Stake**: Nvidia has invested $800M total, led the $2B round.
- **Valuation**: Latest round closed at $25B pre-money valuation.
- **Beam Launch**: 501B-parameter MoE open-weight model targeting coding and agentic tasks.
- **Compute Commitments**: SpaceX deal up to $6.3B plus $1B from Nebius.
