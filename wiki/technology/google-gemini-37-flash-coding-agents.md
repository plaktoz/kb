## Summary

Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash, a lower-cost model aimed at coding and autonomous AI agents, while still withholding a release date for its flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro — a gap investors are watching as a test of whether DeepMind can keep pace with Anthropic and OpenAI.

## Core Concepts

- [[Gemini 3.7 Flash]] — Google's new lower-cost model for agentic, multi-step coding and business workflows, priced at half of Gemini 3.6 Flash.
- [[DeepMind Leadership Overhaul]] — Demis Hassabis stepped aside as DeepMind chief in favor of deputy Koray Kavukcuoglu; two Gemini technical co-leads also departed to found a startup.
- [[Model Cadence as Competitive Signal]] — Gemini 3.7 Flash ships just three weeks after 3.6 Flash, a fast release cadence used to signal momentum while the flagship Pro model lags.

## Key Takeaways

- **Pricing**: 75 cents per million input tokens, $3.75 per million output tokens through year-end — half of Gemini 3.6 Flash's cost.
- **Positioning**: Aimed at businesses building autonomous agents that plan tasks and complete multi-step workflows with less human intervention.
- **Distribution**: Rolled out immediately to Gemini Spark, Google's subscription AI agent service in 160+ countries.
- **Strategic pressure**: Co-founder Sergey Brin has reportedly urged AI staff to focus fully on Gemini as Alphabet tries to close the gap with rivals.
