## Summary

Apple released [[iOS 27]], [[iPadOS 27]], [[macOS 27]], [[watchOS 27]], [[visionOS 27]], and [[tvOS 27]] on September 14, 2026, marking a platform-wide update cycle. The flagship additions are a rebuilt [[Siri]] with personal context and web knowledge, photorealistic [[Image Playground]] generation, and sweeping performance improvements. Child safety and [[Screen Time]] also received significant redesigns.

## Core Concepts

- **[[iOS 27]] / Platform Release**: Simultaneous shipping of six OS versions across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch, Vision Pro, and Apple TV
- **[[Siri]] Rebuild**: Personal context awareness, onscreen understanding, broad web knowledge, and cross-device conversation history via [[iCloud]]; English beta at launch, five additional languages in October
- **[[Apple Intelligence]]**: Suite of on-device AI features including Spatial Reframing, Extend, and upgraded Clean Up in Photos; photorealistic [[Image Playground]]; [[Call Context]] for business calls
- **Child Safety / [[Screen Time]]**: Streamlined child-account setup, "Ask to Browse" parental approval gate, Communication Safety blocks violent content, redesigned Screen Time with daily app schedules
- **[[Liquid Glass UI]]**: Updated design language now features a user-adjustable tint slider
- **Performance Benchmarks**: App launch up to 30% faster, photo load up to 70% faster, [[AirDrop]] transfers up to 80% faster, [[Apple Vision Pro]] Wi-Fi connection up to 3× faster

## Key Takeaways

- **Siri Beta**: English-only at launch; five more languages arrive October 2026.
- **Dedicated Siri App**: Syncs conversation history across devices via [[iCloud]].
- **Photorealistic Images**: [[Image Playground]] now generates photorealistic output.
- **Safari AI**: Auto tab organization, website change alerts ("Notify Me"), AI-described extensions.
- **30/70/80 Rule**: Apps, photos, and AirDrop each hit double-digit speed gains.
- **Health Additions**: Perimenopause/menopause cycle tracking added to [[Apple Health]].
- **Vision Pro**: Wi-Fi connection speed tripled; full-resolution [[iCloud]] Shared Albums.
- **AirPods**: Custom EQ support added.
- **Apple Watch**: Single-handed widget opening and offline Workout Buddy.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Platform Leverage]]**: By shipping all six OS versions simultaneously, Apple compounds network effects — every hardware category gains AI features at once, raising the switching cost for users embedded across iPhone, Mac, and Watch.
- **[[Beta-to-GA Rollout]]**: Shipping Siri in English-only beta and expanding languages in October reflects a staged deployment model that limits support surface while gathering real-world signal before broader rollout.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core new capability of the rebuilt Siri in iOS 27?
**A**: The rebuilt Siri features personal context awareness, onscreen understanding, and broad web knowledge, launching as an English beta with cross-device conversation history synced via iCloud.

**Q2**: What specific performance improvements ship with iOS 27?

**Q3**: How does the new "Ask to Browse" feature change parental controls?
