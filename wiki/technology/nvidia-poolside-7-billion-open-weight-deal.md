## Summary

Nvidia is paying $6B in technology licensing fees to AI startup [[Poolside]], investing an additional $1B at a $12B pre-money valuation, and absorbing 109 of Poolside's engineers into its [[Nemotron]] open-weight model project — a strategic pivot into open-weight models driven by competitive pressure from Chinese open-source AI and a defensive play to protect GPU demand as major customers develop custom chips.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nemotron Coalition]]**: Formed by Nvidia in March 2026 with open-model developers including Mistral, Thinking Machines Lab, and Perplexity, sharing data, expertise, and compute resources.
- **["Soft Acquisition"]]**: The deal structure — large licensing fee plus minority investment plus engineer absorption — functions like an acquisition without Nvidia taking full ownership or triggering the same regulatory scrutiny.
- **[[Model Factory]]**: Poolside's internal system for developing large models, the core asset licensed to Nvidia on a non-exclusive basis, meaning Poolside retains rights to sublicense elsewhere.
- **[[Reflection AI]]**: Another Nvidia-backed startup, positioned in reporting as the "American version of DeepSeek," which completed large-scale financing around the same period.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal structure**: $6B in licensing fees + $1B equity investment at $12B pre-money valuation + 109 engineers absorbed into Nvidia's Nemotron project.
- **Non-exclusive license**: Poolside keeps the right to sublicense its Model Factory system to other parties.
- **Strategic driver**: Competitive pressure from Chinese open-source AI models is pushing Nvidia toward open-weight strategy rather than staying purely a hardware seller.
- **Defensive logic**: Backing open models helps secure GPU demand as Nvidia's largest customers (hyperscalers) develop their own custom AI chips.
- **Coalition context**: The Poolside deal extends a six-month pattern of Nvidia deals (Nemotron Coalition formation, Reflection AI financing) rather than being an isolated move.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Vertical Integration Defense]]**: By funding and absorbing talent from open-model developers, Nvidia hedges against being commoditized to "just the chip layer" if hyperscalers succeed in building competitive custom silicon — owning a stake in the model layer preserves optionality.
