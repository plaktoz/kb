## Summary

Scale AI is an American AI infrastructure company founded in 2016 that evolved from a data annotation firm into a full-stack provider of RLHF services, LLM evaluation, and enterprise AI software. It reached a $14 billion valuation after a 2024 funding round backed by Meta and Amazon, and in 2025 Meta purchased a 49% non-voting stake for approximately $14.8 billion. Its work underpins major LLM pipelines, including a named partnership with OpenAI to fine-tune GPT-3.5 for ChatGPT.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Scale AI]]** — AI infrastructure company providing data labeling, [[RLHF]] pipelines, and [[LLM]] evaluation services to government and enterprise clients.
- **[[Alexandr Wang]]** — Co-founder (with Lucy Guo) and former CEO; moved to a senior role at Meta following the 2025 stake acquisition.
- **[[RLHF]] (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)** — Core service offering; Scale is a preferred partner for fine-tuning frontier models including [[GPT-3.5]].
- **[[Humanity's Last Exam]]** — Benchmark co-created with the [[Center for AI Safety]] to evaluate frontier model capabilities, released January 2025.
- **[[Remotasks]]** — Crowdworking subsidiary for labeled data creation targeting computer vision and autonomous vehicles; operates in Southeast Asia and Africa.
- **[[Outlier]]** — Separate platform for generative AI data work requiring contributors with advanced degrees or specialized expertise.
- **[[Scale Labs]]** — Expanded research division launched March 2026, focusing on AI capabilities research, post-training evaluation, and public benchmarks (SWE-Atlas, Voice Showdown).
- **[[Thunderforge]]** — Pentagon AI project using Scale's technology to coordinate military asset movements (March 2025 deal).
- **[[Meta Platforms]]** — Major owner holding a 49% non-voting stake; one of several major investors alongside Amazon.

## Key Takeaways

- **Founded 2016** via Y Combinator by Alexandr Wang and Lucy Guo.
- **Revenue $870M (2024)**; ~1,200 employees as of 2025.
- **Unicorn status** reached in August 2019 after $100M Founders Fund investment.
- **Defense contracts**: U.S. DoD client since 2020; first AI company to deploy LLM on a classified Army network (2023).
- **OpenAI partnership**: Named preferred partner for GPT-3.5 fine-tuning; contributed to ChatGPT's development.
- **Meta deal (2025)**: 49% non-voting stake for ~$14.8B; Scale remains independent.
- **Remotasks controversy**: Oxford study found fair work compliance in only 1 of 10 criteria; terminated Kenya, Nigeria, Pakistan operations in early 2024.
- **Valuation trajectory**: $1B (2019) → $7B (2021) → $13B (March 2024) → $14B (May 2024).

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Vertical Integration]]**: Scale moved up the AI value chain from commodity data labeling to RLHF, evaluation, and enterprise software — capturing more margin and strategic leverage at each layer as the market matured.
- **[[Platform Lock-in]]**: By embedding itself in the fine-tuning pipelines of OpenAI, the U.S. government, and Meta simultaneously, Scale created switching costs that explain why Meta chose investment over building a competing capability internally.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Scale AI's core business and how has it evolved since founding?
**A**: Founded as a data annotation firm in 2016, Scale AI expanded into RLHF services, LLM evaluation, and enterprise AI software, becoming a critical infrastructure layer for frontier model development and government AI projects.

**Q2**: What makes Scale AI's government work notable, and what specific contract illustrates this?
**A**: In May 2023, Scale became the first AI company to deploy an LLM ("Donovan") on a classified U.S. Army network; it has also secured a multimillion-dollar Pentagon deal for Thunderforge, an AI system to coordinate military asset movements.

**Q3**: What does the Meta 49% stake acquisition imply for Scale AI's strategic position?
**A**: Meta acquiring a non-voting stake for ~$14.8B while leaving Scale independent suggests Meta values Scale's data and evaluation infrastructure enough to secure preferred access without absorbing the operational complexity — Scale gains capital and a captive major client while retaining independence.
