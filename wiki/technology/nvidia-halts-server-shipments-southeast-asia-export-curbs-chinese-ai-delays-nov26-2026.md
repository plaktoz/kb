## Summary
The Financial Times reported that [[Nvidia]] told manufacturing partners such as Dell and Supermicro to halt AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia after new U.S. export rules took effect. The curbs threaten Chinese AI firms' access to advanced chips, delaying models like GLM-5.5 and Kimi K4. Nvidia still raised its fiscal 2028 growth forecast to about 75%, and it is unclear whether the freeze affects it.

## Core Concepts
- [[Nvidia]] — halted server shipments; stock fell 3.91% to $149.30 on Wednesday
- [[US Export Controls]] — licenses now required for advanced AI chips to four Southeast Asian countries and Brazil
- [[Jensen Huang]] — reinstated as CEO; board created an independent National Security and Export Control Committee
- [[Z.AI]] — GLM-5.5 launch could slip to Q1 2027
- [[Moonshot AI]] — Kimi K3 launched in July; K4 could slip to mid-2027
- [[Model Distillation]] — training on outputs of advanced models; OpenAI and Anthropic are trying to prevent it
- [[Colette Kress]] — Nvidia CFO who said demand far exceeds supply

## Key Takeaways
- **Shipment freeze**: Dell and Supermicro told customers in four countries servers cannot ship.
- **Temporary?**: Unclear whether the suspension is a short-term compliance pause.
- **Financing pause**: Lenders paused billions for Asian data centers tied to ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent.
- **Chinese model delays**: Z.AI's GLM-5.5 may slip to Q1 2027.
- **Longer cycles**: Moonshot says six-month development cycles could double to a year.
- **Guidance intact**: Nvidia raised fiscal 2028 growth forecast from 70% to about 75%.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Nvidia reportedly do, and why?
**A**: Nvidia told server makers to halt shipments of its AI-chip servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, a week after new U.S. export rules required licenses. It is unclear if the halt is temporary.

**Q2**: How are the curbs affecting Chinese AI companies?
**A**: Z.AI's GLM-5.5 could slip to Q1 2027, and Moonshot says its six-month model cycle could double to a year or more, pushing K4 to mid-2027. Lenders also paused billions in data center financing.

**Q3**: Does this threaten Nvidia's revenue outlook?
**A**: Nvidia raised its fiscal 2028 growth forecast to about 75% and said demand far exceeds supply. It is unclear whether the shipment freeze could affect that projection.
