## Summary

[[Anthropic]] signed a $10 billion, six-year compute deal with AI cloud startup [[Volta]], which will build a 133-megawatt Nvidia Vera Rubin data center in Norway with mining company [[Bitdeer]]. The deal is part of Anthropic's aggressive push to lock up compute capacity amid competition with rival AI labs.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Volta]]**: An AI cloud startup founded in 2026 by former [[Brookfield Asset Management]] executives, part of Nvidia's Cloud Partner program; it leases AI capacity and helps clients finance chip purchases.
- **[[Compute Scarcity]]**: Anthropic's pattern of signing large, multi-year cloud deals (also recently with SpaceX and Amazon) reflects an industry-wide race to secure GPU capacity years in advance.
- **[[Bitdeer]]**: A crypto-mining company partnering with Volta to physically develop the data center, illustrating how mining infrastructure and expertise are being repurposed for AI compute.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal size**: $10 billion over six years for dedicated cloud compute.
- **Location**: A 133-megawatt facility in Norway, fueled by Nvidia's Vera Rubin chip architecture.
- **Pattern**: This is one of several recent Anthropic compute deals (also SpaceX, Amazon), signaling sustained aggressive capacity expansion.
- **Volta's traction**: The startup separately raised $300 million in venture funding at a $2.4 billion valuation around the same announcement.
