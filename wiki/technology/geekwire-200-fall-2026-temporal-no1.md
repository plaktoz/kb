## Summary

The Fall 2026 GeekWire 200 crowned [[Temporal]] — durable-execution infrastructure for AI agents — as the new No. 1 Pacific Northwest startup, displacing fusion company [[Helion]], as AI software and advanced hardware (space, energy, defense) dominate the region's top rankings. Six of the top 10 companies now build advanced machinery, while [[Agility Robotics]] exits the list ahead of a planned SPAC merger.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Temporal]]**: Bellevue startup whose durable-execution software keeps AI agents running reliably; raised $550M at a $12.55B valuation (more than double its prior value) with annualized revenue past $250M, up 200%+ in a year; customers include OpenAI, Nvidia and Netflix.
- **[[Helion]]**: Everett fusion-power developer, now No. 2; grew its Series G to $500M and opened a Seattle office, but faces questions about its 2028 fusion-power delivery timeline to Microsoft.
- **[[GeekWire 200]]**: The ranking of the Pacific Northwest's top privately held tech startups, combining objective data, editorial insight and AI analysis; companies leave upon acquisition or IPO.
- **[[Agentic AI]] Infrastructure**: Temporal's rise reflects demand for infrastructure that makes autonomous AI agents reliable at scale.
- **[[Agility Robotics]]**: Humanoid robot maker exiting the list ahead of a $2.5B SPAC merger; unveiled Digit 5, which lifts 50 pounds and works alongside people without safety barriers.
- **[[Stoke Space]]**, **[[Chainguard]]**, **[[Overland AI]]**, **[[Carbon Robotics]]**, **[[Echodyne]]**, **[[Dropzone]]**: Rest of the Fall 2026 top 10, spanning reusable rockets, software supply-chain security, military ground-vehicle autonomy, agricultural laser weeding, radar manufacturing, and autonomous security agents.

## Key Takeaways

- **New No. 1**: Temporal overtakes Helion on $550M raise, $12.55B valuation.
- **Hardware surge**: 6 of the top 10 companies build physical/advanced machinery.
- **Fusion timeline risk**: Helion's 2028 Microsoft power-delivery commitment faces scrutiny.
- **Notable debut**: Entire, ex-GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke's distributed Git network for AI coding agents.
- **Big exits**: Agility Robotics (SPAC merger) and Common Room (Zoom acquisition) leave the list.
- **Orbital data centers**: Starcloud raised $250M at $2.3B with Nvidia as an investor.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Creative Destruction]]**: The rapid reshuffling at the top — Temporal unseating Helion, Agility Robotics exiting via SPAC, 12+ new debuts — illustrates Schumpeter's dynamic where new AI- and hardware-driven ventures continuously displace prior incumbents rather than settling into a stable hierarchy.
