## Summary

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet so its "dots" agents can help users decide what to buy and complete purchases, starting with a limited group of U.S. Pro subscribers. Users review items, total, and card, then approve with Face ID or a fingerprint, while a payment token keeps real card details from the agent and merchant. OpenAI plans spending limits and broader retailer support, but retailers such as Amazon block AI agents.

## Core Concepts

- [[ChatGPT Wallet]]: Feature letting dots agents charge a stored card via a payment token.
- [[OpenAI Dots Agents]]: OpenAI's ChatGPT agents that help decide on and complete purchases.
- [[Agentic Commerce]]: AI agents shopping and checking out for users; OpenAI published a Commerce Partner Playbook for retailers.
- [[Payment Tokenization]]: A substitute for the actual card details, so neither agent nor merchant sees them.
- [[Meta Muse]]: Rival agent with over 10 million users where AI-assisted shopping is a top activity.
- [[Instinct]]: Another personal AI agent that supports purchases on users' behalf.
- [[Amazon]]: Largest retailer banning AI agents, steering users to its own Alexa agent.
- [[DoorDash]] and [[Uber]]: Report low numbers of orders placed by AI agents.

## Key Takeaways

- **Limited Launch**: U.S. Pro subscribers 18+ with a U.S. Visa, Mastercard, or Amex.
- **User Control**: Users review items, total, and card, then approve via biometrics, creating a record.
- **Token Security**: A payment token replaces real card details for the agent and merchant.
- **Card Benefits Kept**: Rewards and dispute protections still apply.
- **Planned Autonomy**: Future spending limits and category or time rules enable pre-approved purchases.
- **Retailer Friction**: Amazon blocks AI agents to steer users toward Alexa.
- **Mixed Demand**: DoorDash and Uber see few agent orders; Muse sees shopping as a top activity.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What does ChatGPT Wallet let users do?
**A**: It lets OpenAI's dots agents help decide what to buy and complete the purchase on the user's behalf. It launches for a limited number of U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

**Q2**: How does the Wallet protect payment details and keep users in control?
**A**: It charges the selected card using a payment token, so the agent and merchant never see the real card details. Users review the items, total, and card, then approve with Face ID or a fingerprint.

**Q3**: What obstacles and next steps does OpenAI face for agentic shopping?
**A**: Some retailers block AI agents, with Amazon the largest, so OpenAI wants broader retailer support and published a Commerce Partner Playbook. It also plans spending limits and parameters so users can pre-approve purchases.
