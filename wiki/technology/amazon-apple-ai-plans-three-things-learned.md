## Summary

Major tech companies — [[Microsoft]], [[Meta]], [[Google]], [[Apple]], and [[Amazon]] — collectively signaled continued trillion-dollar AI investment despite AI chatbots not yet generating meaningful standalone revenue. Wall Street's patience is thinning, rewarding companies that show concrete AI adoption returns (Microsoft, Amazon) and punishing those offering only future promises (Meta). Consumer demand for new tech remains strong, with [[Google Gemini]] reaching 950 million monthly users and Apple facing chip supply constraints due to unexpectedly high device demand.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Capex Cycle]]**: Big tech is pouring hundreds of billions into [[Data Centers]], [[AI Chips]], and talent with no clear near-term payback timeline.
- **[[Free Cash Flow]] pressure**: [[Alphabet]] reported negative free cash flow on $118bn revenue — historically unprecedented — due to AI spending. [[Meta]] generated just $784m on $61bn revenue.
- **[[Microsoft]] as AI ROI benchmark**: Microsoft's stock hit a six-month high after demonstrating that AI investments are "beginning to deliver returns" via Azure and [[GitHub Copilot]] adoption.
- **[[Amazon]] AI strategy**: Despite negative cash flow and $220bn planned AI spend, strong core business performance drove its stock to a two-month high.
- **[[Apple]] Siri overhaul**: Apple is integrating [[Google Gemini]] into a revamped [[Siri]], with plans to monetize heavy usage; CEO [[Tim Cook]] cited off-the-charts excitement and chip supply constraints from high device demand.
- **[[Meta AI]] and agent ambitions**: [[Mark Zuckerberg]] announced an AI agent and enterprise AI tool with no timeline, triggering a stock selloff; Meta's [[Reality Labs]] lost ~$9bn in H1 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- AI chatbots do not yet generate meaningful revenue for any major tech company.
- [[Alphabet]] free cash flow went negative for the first time as a public company.
- [[Meta]] stock fell after Zuckerberg offered vague AI plans with no revenue timeline.
- [[Microsoft]] stock hit six-month high — seen as best-in-class AI ROI evidence.
- [[Amazon]] plans to spend $220bn on AI in 2026 despite negative cash flow.
- [[Google Gemini]] reached 950M monthly users, tripling year-over-year.
- [[Apple]] faces chip supply shortages due to stronger-than-expected device sales.
- Apple plans to charge for heavy [[Siri]] AI usage based on user testing feedback.
- [[Meta]] Reality Labs lost ~$9bn in H1 2026 alone.
- Markets now discriminate: execution rewarded, promises penalized.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Show Don't Tell]]**: Markets are repricing AI investments based on demonstrated revenue rather than executive narratives — companies with tangible adoption metrics (Microsoft, Amazon) outperformed those citing future potential (Meta), illustrating that credibility requires evidence, not assertions.
- **[[Opportunity Cost]]**: Alphabet's negative free cash flow signals that AI capex is consuming capital that would otherwise compound in proven businesses, forcing investors to re-evaluate whether the AI bet justifies the foregone returns.
