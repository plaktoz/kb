## Summary

Wikidata is a free, open knowledge base containing over 122 million data entities, editable by both humans and machines under the CC0 license. It serves as the central structured data repository for Wikimedia sister projects including Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wiktionary, and Wikisource. Data can be exported in standard formats and interlinked with other open datasets.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Wikidata]]** — a collaboratively edited [[Knowledge Graph]] operated by the Wikimedia Foundation
- **[[Structured Data]]** — machine-readable facts stored as subject-predicate-object triples (e.g., Douglas Adams Q42)
- **[[CC0 License]]** — public domain dedication allowing unrestricted reuse of all content
- **[[Wikimedia Projects]]** — Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wiktionary, and Wikisource all draw structured data from Wikidata
- **[[WikiProjects]]** — community working groups focused on specific domains (e.g., WikiProject Women for data representation of women)
- **Temporary accounts** — unregistered editors now receive temporary accounts instead of IP-based identification (deployed 2025-11-12)

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: Over 122 million data entities in a single open knowledge base.
- **Machine-readable**: Data queryable via SPARQL and exportable in standard formats.
- **CC0 license**: All data is public domain — no attribution required for reuse.
- **Centralized storage**: Single source of truth for structured data across all Wikimedia projects.
- **Mobile editing**: All datatypes now editable in mobile view.
- **Privacy update**: Temporary accounts replace IP tracking for unregistered editors.
- **Community-driven**: WikiProjects coordinate data modeling decisions for specific domains.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Wikidata's core purpose within the Wikimedia ecosystem?
**A**: Wikidata acts as the central structured data repository for all Wikimedia sister projects, storing over 122 million data entities readable and editable by both humans and machines.

**Q2**: Under what license is Wikidata content released, and what does that allow?
**A**: All Wikidata content is released under CC0 (public domain), meaning anyone can use, share, or build on the data without restriction or attribution.

**Q3**: How might a developer or researcher leverage Wikidata?
**A**: Wikidata data can be exported in standard formats or queried directly, and interlinked with other open datasets — making it a foundation for knowledge graph applications and structured research pipelines.
