## Summary

Korean AI chip startup FuriosaAI closed an 820 billion won Series D at a valuation of about 3.8 trillion won, above its initial 450-750 billion won target. It will use the money to commercialize its inference-focused RNGD chip globally and to develop a third-generation accelerator with Broadcom, aiming to challenge NVIDIA's dominance.

## Core Concepts

- [[FuriosaAI]]: Korean AI semiconductor startup moving from R&D to full-scale global supply.
- [[RNGD]]: second-generation chip specialized for [[AI Inference]], designed to cut power use and cost.
- [[Series D Funding]]: 820 billion won raised, valued at about 3.8 trillion won.
- [[National Growth Fund]]: largest investor, with 370 billion won.
- [[Broadcom]]: US partner co-developing the third-generation AI accelerator.
- [[NVIDIA]]: incumbent whose dominance in AI semiconductors FuriosaAI targets.
- [[June Paik]]: FuriosaAI CEO who frames the chip as a new option based on energy efficiency.

## Key Takeaways

- **Round Size**: 820 billion won raised, above the 450-750 billion won target.
- **Valuation**: About 3.8 trillion Korean won.
- **Lead Investor**: National Growth Fund put in 370 billion won.
- **Other Backers**: Infinitum, Cisco Investments, QUDA Capital Partners, Naver, DSC, Korea Development Bank.
- **Policy Bank**: Export-Import Bank of Korea made its first direct venture investment.
- **Product Focus**: RNGD targets inference with lower power consumption and cost.
- **Next Generation**: Third-gen accelerator is being developed with Broadcom.
