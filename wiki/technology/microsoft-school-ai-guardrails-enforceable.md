## Summary

[[Microsoft]] committed to ten legally enforceable principles governing AI use in schools, reaching agreements with two major teachers unions. Key provisions prohibit using student and educator data to train AI models, require plain-language explanations to families, and mandate human review for consequential decisions. Microsoft shares rose ~0.7% to $495.93 on the news.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Microsoft]]** — tech giant entering legally binding commitments with teachers unions over AI use
- **[[AI Guardrails]]** — ten enforceable principles limiting data use, AI companions, and automated decisions in schools
- **[[Student Data Privacy]]** — prohibition on using classroom data to train AI models; limited data collection
- **[[AI in Education]]** — context of growing EdTech AI adoption following temporary bans in NYC and LA
- **[[Teachers Unions]]** — counterparties to the agreement, acting as institutional safeguards for school AI deployment
- **[[Human-in-the-Loop]]** — requirement that decisions with serious consequences receive human review

## Key Takeaways

- **10 enforceable principles**: student/educator data cannot train AI models.
- **Limited data collection**: guardrails restrict what schools share with Microsoft's AI systems.
- **Plain-language transparency**: families must receive understandable AI explanations.
- **AI companions banned**: companion-style AI applications are prohibited in school settings.
- **Human review required**: consequential decisions must have a human checkpoint.
- **Opt-in by November**: districts can add provisions to existing contracts without full renegotiation.
- **Market signal**: shares +0.7% to $495.93; stock ~15% below GF Value of $583.64.
- **Context**: follows temporary student-facing AI bans by NYC and Los Angeles school districts.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Precautionary Principle]]**: NYC and LA pre-emptively restricted AI tools before harms materialized — Microsoft's binding commitments respond to this institutional caution by making accountability explicit rather than assumed.
- **[[Trust as Infrastructure]]**: Legally enforceable guardrails function as trust infrastructure — they lower the adoption barrier for risk-averse institutions (schools, unions) who need credible commitments before deploying new technology.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Microsoft's core commitment under the school AI guardrails agreement?
**A**: Microsoft agreed to ten legally enforceable principles, most critically that student and educator data will not be used to train AI models, with additional limits on data collection and mandatory human review for high-stakes decisions.

**Q2**: How can school districts adopt these guardrails without disrupting existing contracts?
**A**: Starting in November, districts can add the ten provisions to new or existing Microsoft contracts without needing to renegotiate entire agreements.

**Q3**: What does Microsoft's share price movement and GF Value gap suggest about the market's view of this agreement?
**A**: The ~0.7% share gain signals near-term positive sentiment, while the ~15% discount to GF Value ($583.64 vs. $495.93) suggests the market sees meaningful upside if these commitments translate into broader classroom adoption.
