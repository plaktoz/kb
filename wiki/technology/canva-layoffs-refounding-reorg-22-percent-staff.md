## Summary

Canva is restructuring, with 1,271 employees (22% of its workforce) facing layoffs, role changes, or relocation requests. CEO Melanie Perkins said growth slowed for the first time in a decade and that small changes won't suffice, so Canva must "refound" around small, empowered teams. The memo does not mention AI or cite financial pressure, though it nods to the pace of technological change.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Canva]]**: Australian design software maker and challenger to [[Adobe]]; privately held, with 260 million monthly users and $3.5 billion revenue reported last year.
- **[[Melanie Perkins]]**: Canva CEO whose staff memo "An important update on our team" announced the changes.
- **[[Refounding]]**: Perkins' plan to return to "small, empowered teams" with freedom to decide and take ownership.
- **[[Organizational Restructuring]]**: Product, growth, and platform teams merge into a single "Product Org" to simplify layers and consolidate overlapping responsibilities.
- **[[Tech Industry Layoffs]]**: [[Atlassian]] has also announced job cuts this year, alongside Canva.
- **[[Severance Packages]]**: At least six months' pay, a year of health insurance, keeping company MacBooks, and one year of LinkedIn Premium.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale of Cuts**: 1,271 staff, 22% of the workforce, face layoffs, role changes, or relocation.
- **Growth Slowdown**: Perkins said growth slowed for the first time in a decade.
- **Stated Rationale**: Inefficiency and the need to "refound"; no mention of AI or finances.
- **Structural Change**: Product, growth, and platform teams combine into one Product Org.
- **Relocation**: More product teams move to Sydney headquarters; some staff asked to relocate.
- **Departure Terms**: Minimum six months' pay, a year of health insurance, keep MacBooks.
- **Australian Tech Trend**: Canva and Atlassian, both celebrated Australian firms, have each announced cuts this year.
