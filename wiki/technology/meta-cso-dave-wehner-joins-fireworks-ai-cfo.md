## Summary

[[Dave Wehner]], Meta's chief strategy officer and former CFO, is leaving to become CFO of [[Fireworks AI]], an AI inference and model-training platform valued at $17.5 billion in its July Series D. He will scale the startup's finances and operations, citing its technical talent and customer focus. Fireworks is reportedly in early talks to raise at a $30 billion to $40 billion valuation.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Dave Wehner]]** — Joined Meta (then Facebook) in 2012; CFO from 2014 to 2022; most recently chief strategy officer.
- **[[Fireworks AI]]** — Four-year-old platform for customizing and running open models, founded in 2022 by former Meta and Google employees.
- **[[Lin Qiao]]** — Fireworks CEO; says Wehner wants to build toward big impact at a smaller company.
- **[[Meta]]** — About $1.56 trillion market capitalization as of Friday's close; COO [[Javier Olivan]] praised Wehner's disciplined thinking.
- **[[Open Models]]** — Fireworks' focus; competitors include [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]].
- **[[Series D Funding]]** — Led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures and TCV; also backed by [[Nvidia]] and [[Sequoia Capital]].
- **[[Security Incident]]** — October incident where an unauthorized party accessed environment variables in a limited number of customer accounts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Move**: Wehner leaves Meta after 14 years to become Fireworks CFO.
- **Mandate**: He will scale Fireworks' finances and operations.
- **Valuation**: $17.5B at Series D; early talks reportedly at $30B to $40B.
- **Size contrast**: Fireworks is far smaller than Meta's roughly $1.56 trillion market cap.
- **Motive**: Wehner wants a smaller company and sees AI as his lifetime's defining shift.
- **Risk**: October credentials incident exposed environment variables; no evidence customer models or data were accessed.
