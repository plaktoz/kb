## Summary
Walmart has partnered with Meta so shoppers can browse its Black Friday deals, compare prices and build carts through the Muse AI agent. The experience runs on Walmart's Agent Gateway, which lets outside AI assistants connect to its online store. The move contrasts with Amazon, which has taken steps to stop third-party AI agents from buying for customers.

## Core Concepts
- [[Walmart]]: Retailer opening its online store to outside AI agents.
- [[Meta Muse]]: Meta's AI agent, now able to shop Walmart deals. [[Instacart]] also recently joined Muse.
- [[Agent Gateway]]: Walmart's system, launched in October, letting outside AI assistants connect directly to its store. Walmart plans to open it to more developers and platforms.
- [[Agentic Commerce]]: Customers tell an AI what they want, and it finds products, compares prices and builds the cart.
- [[Sparky]]: Walmart's own shopping assistant, now with handwritten-list-to-cart, photo and voice search, and in-store navigation.
- [[Amazon]]: Takes the opposite stance by blocking third-party agents from purchasing.

## Key Takeaways
- **Partnership**: Announced Tuesday, Walmart Black Friday deals are shoppable through Muse.
- **Flow**: Users sign in to Walmart in the Muse app, then ask the assistant to shop.
- **Privacy**: Muse gets no access to Walmart shopping history or personal information.
- **User Controls**: Shoppers can set spending limits, require purchase approval, review orders and delete the agent profile.
- **Open Infrastructure**: [[Agent Gateway]] launched in October; Muse may become one of several assistants.
- **Divergent Strategies**: Walmart embraces third-party agents while Amazon restricts them.
- **Sparky Upgrades**: Handwritten lists to carts, photo and voice search, and a "Flash Price Tag" shelf-label light.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Walmart and Meta announce?
**A**: Customers can shop Walmart's Black Friday deals through Meta's AI agent Muse, which finds products, compares prices and helps build carts.

**Q2**: How does the integration work and protect privacy?
**A**: Muse sends requests to Walmart through its Agent Gateway, and Walmart returns products and helps build the cart. Muse cannot see shopping history or personal information, and shoppers can set spending limits and require approval.

**Q3**: What does this imply about retailers' approaches to AI shopping agents?
**A**: Retailers are splitting: Walmart opens its store to outside agents and plans to add more platforms, while Amazon has moved to stop third-party agents from purchasing.
