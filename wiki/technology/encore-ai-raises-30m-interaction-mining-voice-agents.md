## Summary

Encore AI (formerly Insait IO) raised a $30M Series A to build AI voice agents that learn from a company's own customer interactions via a process called "interaction mining." The platform analyzes call recordings, emails, and CRM data to identify which employee behaviors led to successful outcomes, then trains agents to replicate those playbooks. The company targets financial institutions and claims 5x ARR growth since its seed round 18 months ago.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Interaction Mining]]**: Encore's core method — collecting and analyzing conversations (calls, emails, texts) to identify which tactics move deals or support cases forward, then encoding those findings into AI agent behavior.
- **[[AI Voice Agents]]**: Agents that can communicate with customers autonomously by voice or text, or act as real-time assistants recommending responses to human employees mid-conversation.
- **[[Playbook Distillation]]**: The idea that the best employee behaviors across a large organization can be surfaced, ranked, and bundled into a replicable AI model — essentially crowdsourcing best practices from internal data.
- **[[CRM Integration]]**: Encore connects to existing CRM systems to correlate conversation history with outcomes, providing a richer signal than structured CRM fields alone.
- **[[Dvir Ginzburg]]**: CEO and co-founder of Encore AI; previously built recommendation software for financial advisers before pivoting to interaction-driven agent training.
- **[[Team8]]**: Lead investor in the Series A; a venture group with strong ties to enterprise cybersecurity and data infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways

- **$30M Series A** led by Team8; banks and insurers co-invested after using the product first.
- **Interaction mining** turns historical customer conversations into agent training data.
- **5x ARR growth** in under 18 months since seed round; 40+ enterprise customers globally.
- **Primary market**: Financial institutions — wealth managers, banks, insurers.
- **Competitive moat claim**: Incumbents (Salesforce, SAP, HubSpot) don't treat conversational history as a core data signal and would need full stack overhauls to replicate.
- **Dual-mode agents**: Can run autonomously with customers or act as live co-pilots for employees.
- **Use of proceeds**: U.S. sales expansion and deployment with larger financial institutions.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Tacit Knowledge Codification]]**: Encore's core bet is that the tacit know-how of top performers — the jokes, anecdotes, and timing that close deals — can be extracted from conversation data and transferred into an AI system, which is precisely what Michael Polanyi's concept of tacit knowledge suggests is hard but high-value when achieved.
- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: Each new enterprise customer adds more interaction data, which improves agent quality, which attracts more customers — a data-network flywheel that raises switching costs and makes the platform harder to displace over time.
