## Summary

Globant launched [[Glob.AI]], an AI-native enterprise services model built around "AI Pods" — agentic service units supervised by human experts — priced by validated output or consumption rather than by hours or seats. The launch reflects a broader shift in enterprise tech services away from months-long RFP cycles toward instant, continuously visible, agent-driven delivery.

## Core Concepts

- [[AI Pods]] — specialized units of parallel AI agents supervised by human experts, delivering a specific task or industry outcome
- [[Consumption-Based Pricing]] — customers pay for expert-validated output or real usage, not hours, seats, or wasted AI retries
- [[Token Vault]] — a client's proprietary record of token consumption, providing traceability and compounding institutional knowledge
- [[Agentic Service Delivery]] — treating enterprise software delivery as a live, continuous service rather than a fixed-term project

## Key Takeaways

- **Pricing shift**: charged per validated output/consumption, not per hour or seat — explicitly designed to eliminate paying for AI hallucinations or retries.
- **Claimed productivity gain**: at least 30% more productive than the typical "engineer plus AI" approach.
- **Early results**: FIFA (+20% throughput), YPF (40% shorter contract timelines), a bank's COBOL migration cut from 14 months to 2.
- **Partnership breadth**: AI Pods integrate with Anthropic, AWS, OpenAI, Google Cloud, Azure, SAP, and Salesforce.
- **Governance emphasis**: Globant pairs AI-native speed with 23 years of enterprise delivery discipline and explicit client-data sovereignty guarantees.
