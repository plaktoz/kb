## Summary

Jeff Su outlines six data-backed AI trends for 2026, drawing on reports from McKinsey, Stanford, OpenAI, and Epoch AI. The central thesis is that AI is commoditizing at the model layer, shifting competition toward applications, workflows, and context management. Individuals who learn to embed AI into structured workflows and manage context well will gain a compounding advantage.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Model Commoditization]]**: The gap between frontier models ([[OpenAI]], [[Google Gemini]], [[Anthropic Claude]], [[DeepSeek]], [[Llama]]) is shrinking; raw model capability is becoming less differentiating.
- **[[AI Workflows]] vs [[AI Agents]]**: McKinsey data shows true agents are deployed at scale by under 10% of organizations; workflow-level AI (custom GPTs, structured pipelines) delivers most current enterprise value.
- **[[Context Engineering]]**: The shift from prompt wording to providing the right files, documents, and data — whoever controls your context controls your AI productivity.
- **[[Technical Divide]]**: AI is closing the skill gap between technical and non-technical workers; 75% of enterprise users (OpenAI report) report completing tasks they previously could not.
- **[[AI Advertising]]**: Ads are confirmed coming to ChatGPT, likely as display banners separate from answers, enabling broader access for non-paying users.
- **[[Physical AI]] / Robotics**: Autonomous systems ([[Waymo]], Amazon warehouse robots) are already deployed at scale; humanoid robots remain 15+ years away per MIT's Rodney Brooks.
- **[[Jagged Frontier]]**: Wharton's [[Ethan Mollick]] concept — AI resets expertise thresholds, creating a window where fast learners outpace established experts.
- **[[Andrej Karpathy]]** on agent hype: frames 2026 as part of a "decade of agents," not a single breakthrough year.

## Key Takeaways

- **Model Parity**: Top models cluster in quality; choose by ecosystem fit, not benchmark scores.
- **Workflow Value**: McKinsey projects $3T in economic value from AI workflow redesign by 2030.
- **App Layer Wins**: Competition shifted from model power to reach, integration, and trust.
- **Non-Technical Empowerment**: Coding-related messages from non-technical employees grew 36% in 6 months.
- **Context Over Prompting**: File organization and consolidating information into one platform is now a productivity requirement.
- **Robotics Reality Check**: Waymo logged 100M autonomous miles; Amazon robots cut order-to-ship time by 78%; humanoids still far off.
- **China Robotics Lead**: China deployed more industrial robots than the US and rest of world combined by 2023.
- **Energy Efficiency**: Nvidia chips use 105,000x less energy per token than 10 years ago, driving cost collapse.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Commoditization]]**: When competing products converge in quality and price, differentiation shifts upstream to distribution and integration — exactly the dynamic playing out as model performance equalizes across frontier labs.
- **[[Jagged Frontier]]**: AI resets what expertise means, meaning early adopters who experiment now gain durable advantages before the field stabilizes around new norms.
