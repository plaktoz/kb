## Summary

[[Anthropic]] has expanded Claude Startups, a program it launched in May, so the package for founders is now worth more than $50,000. New members get a free year of [[Claude Team]], API credits, and up to $45,000 in discounts on partner AI tools. Anthropic says the goal is to lower the barriers that hold founders back as they scale: token costs, access to agentic tools, and limited technical expertise.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Claude Startups]]**: Started in May with Claude credits and a founder community. It already has thousands of startups and is open to new members at no cost.
- **Claude products and credits (up to $7,500)**: A free year of [[Claude Team]] with five Premium seats, which have higher usage limits than standard seats, plus $1,000 in [[Claude API]] credits.
- **[[Claude Startup Stack]]**: Up to 18 partner AI tools with discounts and credits worth up to $45,000. They include [[Attention]], [[ElevenLabs]], [[Granola]], and [[Lovable]], which cover revenue tracking, customer-support agents, meeting notes, and building software.
- **Barriers to scaling**: [[Beth Robertson]], Anthropic's head of Startups, names token costs, access to [[Agentic AI]] tools, and lack of technical expertise.
- **Solo-founder thesis**: Robertson says "an idea that used to need a team and a funding round to prove can now be built by a founder with a laptop and conviction."
- **[[Claude Code]] adoption**: A 2026 [[Supabase]] survey of about 2,000 startup builders found 63% used Claude Code to build their products. [[HumanLayer]], [[Ambral]], and [[Vulcan Technologies]] are examples.

## Key Takeaways

- **Total package**: More than $50,000 in perks per eligible startup.
- **Claude Team**: One free year with five Premium seats.
- **API credits**: $1,000 to integrate Claude and test ideas.
- **Startup Stack**: Up to 18 tools worth up to $45,000.
- **Cost**: Free to join; already has thousands of startups.
- **Survey**: 63% of builders used Claude Code; 10% used ChatGPT or Codex.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Switching Costs]]**: Giving founders free seats, credits, and partner discounts early builds Claude into their workflows from day one, which makes them more likely to keep using it as their companies grow.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Anthropic add to Claude Startups in this expansion?
**A**: A free year of Claude Team and up to $45,000 in discounts and credits on other AI tools through the Claude Startup Stack. That brings the total package to more than $50,000.

**Q2**: What do the Claude-specific perks include?
**A**: Up to $7,500 in total: a free year of Claude Team with five Premium seats that have higher usage limits, plus $1,000 in API credits.

**Q3**: Why might an AI-first founder join the program?
**A**: It lowers upfront costs for model usage and tooling and adds technical support. Those are the barriers Anthropic says stop founders from scaling, and joining is free.
