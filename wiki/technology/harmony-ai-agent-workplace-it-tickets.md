## Summary

[[Harmony]], founded by second-time founders Nitzan Shapira and Ran Ribenzaft (who previously sold Epsagon to [[Cisco]] for $500M), raised a $34 million seed round led by [[Lightspeed]] to build an AI agent that resolves employee IT, HR, and onboarding requests inside Slack and Microsoft Teams. The round is unusually large for a seed stage, reflecting investor confidence in repeat founders and giving the company runway to scale without returning to market soon.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Enterprise Service Management]]** — Harmony replaces traditional IT-ticket workflows with an AI agent that can take permissioned action directly inside chat tools
- **[[Role-Based AI Permissions]]** — the agent uses an employee's role and access level to tailor responses, with optional human approval gates before it takes action
- **[[Second-Time Founder Premium]]** — investors backed Harmony at above-typical seed size specifically because the founders had a prior successful exit

## Key Takeaways

- **Round size**: $34M seed, led by Lightspeed, which also backed the founders' first company
- **Integration surface**: works with 100+ applications inside Slack and Microsoft Teams
- **Early traction**: customers include n8n, eToro, Cyera, and Kith after only a few months of selling
- **Team**: 60 employees, roughly two-thirds based in Israel, the rest in New York
