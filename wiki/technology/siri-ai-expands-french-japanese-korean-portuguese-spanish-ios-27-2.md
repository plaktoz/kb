## Summary

With the 27.2 software updates released October 26, 2026, [[Apple]] expanded [[Siri AI]] to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Chinese (traditional), Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese are due by year-end. Siri AI remains in beta, is unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, and server-model features face daily usage limits, with expanded access to be offered for a fee in the future.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Siri AI]]**: A reimagined, [[Apple Intelligence]]-based version of [[Siri]] that is more conversational and personal while preserving user privacy.
- **Personal context understanding**: Helps users find content across messages, emails, photos and more.
- **Systemwide app actions and onscreen awareness**: Tasks such as drafting an email or editing and sharing photos, and answering questions about on-screen content.
- **Broad world knowledge**: Retrieves up-to-date information on virtually any topic.
- **[[iOS 27]] 27.2 releases**: Beta availability across [[iOS 27]], [[iPadOS 27]], [[macOS 27]], [[watchOS 27]] and [[visionOS 27]].
- **[[European Union]] availability**: Siri AI is unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS; Apple says it is working constructively with the EU on a path forward.
- **Usage limits**: Server-side features, including Siri AI, [[Image Playground]] and AFM 3 Cloud models in [[Shortcuts]], have daily limits.

## Key Takeaways

- **New Languages**: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish added with 27.2 updates.
- **Coming Soon**: Chinese (traditional), Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese by year-end.
- **Beta Status**: Siri AI is in beta and needs an Apple Intelligence-enabled device.
- **EU Gap**: Unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS.
- **Age Limit**: Not available to users under 13.
- **Daily Caps**: Server-model features are subject to daily usage limits.
- **Future Fee**: Expanded access to limited features will be available for a fee.
