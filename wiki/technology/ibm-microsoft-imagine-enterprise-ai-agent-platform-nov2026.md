## Summary
IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise, an agentic platform for building, running and governing AI agents that execute industry-specific processes, especially in regulated industries. It pairs IBM Consulting's 6,000+ pre-built agents and assets with Microsoft's AI, data, cloud and security stack, plus forward-deployed engineers. Initial frameworks cover financial services, insurance and retail, with SEI named as exploring the platform.

## Core Concepts
- [[Imagine Enterprise]]: the joint agentic platform for building, running and governing AI agents.
- [[IBM Consulting]]: supplies 6,000+ agents and assets built on [[IBM Consulting Advantage]] (Process Studio, Context Studio, Advantage Core).
- [[Microsoft]]: provides Foundry, Copilot Studio, Fabric, Entra, Purview, Defender and Azure; with [[GitHub Copilot]] for agent coordination.
- [[AI Agent Governance]]: controls, observability and AI-compliance support built in.
- [[Forward-Deployed Engineering]]: engineers from both companies help move customers from experimentation to production.
- [[SEI]]: financial technology provider exploring the platform with IBM Consulting and Microsoft.

## Key Takeaways
- **Instant Expertise**: More than 6,000 pre-built AI agents and assets from day one.
- **Fits the Enterprise**: Connects to existing data, processes and systems without replacing platforms.
- **Intelligence Multiplier**: Connects enterprise knowledge and context behind people and agents.
- **Industry Focus**: Starts with financial services, insurance and retail; manufacturing, energy and more later.
- **Delivery Model**: Deployed and operated by IBM Consulting, co-innovation, or self-managed.
- **Positioning**: IBM frames it as turning AI from productivity aid into competitive advantage.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Imagine Enterprise and who built it?
**A**: It is an agentic platform from IBM and Microsoft to help enterprises build, run and govern AI agents for industry-specific processes. IBM Consulting contributes expertise and agents, while Microsoft provides the AI, data, cloud and security foundation.

**Q2**: What are the three advantages the platform is designed around?
**A**: Instant Expertise (6,000+ pre-built agents and assets), Fits the Enterprise (adapts to existing systems with built-in security and governance), and Intelligence Multiplier (puts business knowledge and context behind people and agents).

**Q3**: How can enterprises adopt the platform, and where does it apply first?
**A**: Clients can engage IBM Consulting to deploy and operate it, co-innovate on large implementations, or self-manage. Initial industry frameworks cover financial services, insurance and retail, intended as a repeatable blueprint for other sectors.
