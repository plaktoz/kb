## Summary

[[Google]] unveiled TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max on November 9, the first time it has introduced four TPU systems in a single year. The 8i Max pairs two 8i chips in one package, doubling HBM bandwidth from 8.6 TB/s to 17.2 TB/s to attack the AI memory wall, while the 8tx adds hardware-level data encryption using [[AMD]] processors. Suppliers [[TSMC]], [[Samsung]] and [[SK hynix]] are expected to benefit.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Tensor Processing Unit]] 8tx**: Pairs Google's chip technology with AMD processors for hardware-level encryption of data in use, up to twice the speed of its predecessor with little performance penalty.
- **TPU 8i Max**: Two 8i chips in one package; HBM capacity reported rising from 288 GB to 384 GB, SRAM to 768 MB, with twice the compute density per rack.
- **[[Memory Wall]]**: The AI memory bottleneck addressed through more [[High Bandwidth Memory]] bandwidth and on-chip SRAM for [[KV Cache]].
- **[[Axion]]**: Google's Arm-based CPUs replace x86 hosts in TPU 8i servers, paired with DDR5.
- **HBM supply chain**: Samsung and SK hynix reportedly split Google's HBM3E orders about 50/50; [[Broadcom]] is Google's TPU development partner.
- **[[Google Cloud]]**: Revenue more than doubled to $30.71 billion, with TPU sales contributing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Cadence**: First year Google introduced four TPU systems.
- **Bandwidth**: 8i Max doubles HBM bandwidth from 8.6 to 17.2 TB/s.
- **Caveat**: Google has not officially confirmed the 8i Max total HBM capacity.
- **Security**: 8tx encrypts data during processing with virtually no performance penalty.
- **Earlier Gains**: April's 8t and 8i offered 2.7x training performance and 80% better inference price-performance.
- **Suppliers**: TSMC, Samsung and SK hynix are positioned as beneficiaries.
- **Custom HBM**: SK hynix is pursuing joint development of custom HBM for Google's TPUs.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Google announce, and why is it notable?
**A**: Google unveiled TPU 8tx and 8i Max, expanding its eighth-generation lineup. It is the first time Google has introduced four TPU systems in one year.

**Q2**: How does the TPU 8i Max address the memory wall?
**A**: It combines two 8i chips in one package, doubling HBM bandwidth from 8.6 TB/s to 17.2 TB/s and SRAM to 768 MB, without increasing rack space. HBM capacity is reported to rise from 288 GB to 384 GB but is unconfirmed by Google.

**Q3**: Who in the supply chain stands to gain from Google's TPU expansion?
**A**: TSMC is a key beneficiary through its process and packaging ecosystem. Samsung and SK hynix each supply roughly half of Google's HBM3E, and both are deepening collaboration around TPUs.
