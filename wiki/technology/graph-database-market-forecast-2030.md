## Summary

The graph database market is projected to reach USD 2.14 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 27.1%, driven by demand for connected-data applications. Adjacent markets — knowledge graphs and vector databases — are growing even faster, with CAGRs of 31.6% and 27.5% respectively. Key enterprise players include IBM, Oracle, Microsoft, AWS, and Neo4j alongside specialized vendors like TigerGraph, ArangoDB, and Memgraph.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Graph Database]]**: A database using graph structures (nodes, edges, properties) to represent and store data, enabling efficient traversal of relationships.
- **[[Knowledge Graph]]**: A structured representation of entities and their semantic relationships; projected to reach USD 9.88 billion by 2032.
- **[[Vector Database]]**: A database optimized for storing and querying high-dimensional vector embeddings; projected to reach USD 8.95 billion by 2030.
- **[[Graph Analytics]]**: Analytical workloads applied to graph-structured data; an earlier forecast projected USD 2.52 billion by 2024 at 34% CAGR.
- **[[Neo4j]]**: Leading native graph database vendor, consistently cited as a key player.
- **[[TigerGraph]]**: High-performance graph analytics platform for enterprise-scale workloads.
- **[[CAGR]]**: Compound Annual Growth Rate — the metric used across all four market segments to express sustained growth trajectories.
