## Summary

[[Jensen Huang]] resigned as [[Nvidia]] CEO and from the board after admitting to infidelity and facing reports of a relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence-linked executive and with a senior Nvidia executive who reported to him. CFO [[Colette Kress]] is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor. Nvidia shares fell more than 14% at one point on Thursday before recovering some ground.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Jensen Huang]]**: Co-founder and 33-year CEO who resigned; had campaigned for years against restrictions on chip sales to China.
- **[[Colette Kress]]**: CFO since September 2013, now interim CEO; revenue rose from $4.13bn (fiscal 2014) to $215.9bn (fiscal 2026) on her watch.
- **[[Nvidia Board Investigation]]**: Independent probe into reports Huang was targeted by Chinese intelligence; the board says it has no reason to believe national security was compromised.
- **[[AI Chip Export Controls]]**: Trump approved H200 exports to China in December 2025; Chinese firms reportedly ordered over two million chips, potentially worth $54bn.
- **[[CEO Succession]]**: Candidates discussed include Kress, Ian Buck, Rene Haas, Lisa Su, Sanjay Mehrotra and Tim Cook; Harvey Jones became first board chair.
- **[[Megaspeed]]**: Singapore-based firm where Alice Huang, alleged to have worked for Chinese intelligence, was an executive.

## Key Takeaways

- **Resignation**: Huang quit as CEO and director after several days of scrutiny; no reason was given.
- **Interim Leadership**: Kress takes over while the board weighs internal and external candidates.
- **National Security**: No established evidence Huang knowingly assisted Beijing; US lawmakers are still examining implications.
- **Conflict of Interest**: The New York Times reported an affair with Kimberly Powell, a direct report.
- **Market Impact**: Shares fell over 14% intraday; investors reportedly rotated into other tech stocks.
- **Political Reaction**: Trump defended Huang and floated Tim Cook as successor; no sign Cook is interested.
- **Industry Exposure**: Nvidia plans about $10bn investment in Anthropic's expected listing.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Jensen Huang resign as Nvidia CEO?
**A**: He resigned after he admitted to being unfaithful and after reports of a relationship with an alleged Chinese intelligence-linked executive and with a senior executive who reported to him. Nvidia gave no formal reason for his departure.

**Q2**: Who is Colette Kress and what is her track record?
**A**: She is Nvidia's CFO since September 2013 and now interim CEO. Annual revenue grew from $4.13bn in fiscal 2014 to $215.9bn in fiscal 2026 during her tenure.

**Q3**: What are the wider implications of the leadership change?
**A**: Analysts say it creates uncertainty for employees and customers, and it adds political scrutiny in Washington over chip exports to China. Nvidia's roles as a chip supplier and investor in AI infrastructure firms make the effects reach the wider AI industry.
