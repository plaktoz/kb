## Summary
Arab News's weekly roundup shows fintech, health tech, and AI startups across the Middle East and North Africa raising fresh financing, mostly at pre-seed and seed stage. The week's highlight was Egyptian fintech MNT-Halan listing on the Egyptian Exchange, with its retail tranche 43.6 times oversubscribed. Most deals fund regional expansion, product development, or regulatory licensing.

## Core Concepts
- [[MNT-Halan]]: Egyptian fintech listed as HALN.CA after an IPO of 320 million shares (20% of capital); 7.9 million customers and over EGP178 billion disbursed by June 30.
- [[Pre-Seed Funding]]: theQA (over $550k), CapQuest ($1M), Vrtx ($3.6M), Popcorn Studio AI ($1M), and INVE ($300k).
- [[Health Tech]]: Vitalls ($1.5M seed commitment, AI health passport) and Nabta Health (second close of $3M pre-series A, women's health).
- [[Fintech]] and [[Embedded Finance]]: Vrtx builds wallet, card, and payout infrastructure and has in-principle Saudi Central Bank approval for an e-money license.
- [[Agentic AI]]: Tactful AI builds agentic customer-experience infrastructure, adding former e& Group CEO [[Hatem Dowidar]] as an investor.
- [[Y Combinator]]: invested $500,000 in Databuddy Analytics, a Palestinian-founded AI analytics platform.
- [[Proptech]]: Aqar Exit raised $200k at about a $2M post-money valuation.

## Key Takeaways
- **IPO Demand**: MNT-Halan's institutional tranche was 11.2x oversubscribed; retail 43.6x.
- **Sector Mix**: Fintech, health tech, AI, proptech, and event tech all raised capital.
- **Stage Skew**: Most rounds were pre-seed or seed, ranging from $200k to $3.6M.
- **Regional Expansion**: Funds target Gulf, Saudi, UAE, and Egypt market growth.
- **Regulatory Milestone**: Vrtx has in-principle approval for a Saudi e-money institution license.
- **Investor Base**: Family offices, angels, VCs, and Y Combinator backed the rounds.
- **Event-Tech Niche**: INVE targets Saudi Arabia's meetings and exhibitions sector, which it projects at $5.65B by 2031.
