## Summary

IBM, the University of Chicago, the State of Illinois and IQMP unveiled an IBM Quantum System Two at Hyde Park Labs, IBM's first quantum computer in Illinois and its most advanced system available. Powered by the Nighthawk chip, it will anchor the National Quantum Algorithm Center and later move to the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park. The release (an IBM press release) frames it as a step toward making Chicago a global quantum hub.

## Core Concepts

- **[[IBM Quantum System Two]]**: IBM's most advanced available quantum system, hosted by the [[University of Chicago]] at Hyde Park Labs.
- **[[IBM Quantum Nighthawk]]**: The most powerful quantum chip IBM has built, powering the system.
- **[[Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park]] (IQMP)**: Campus under construction in Chicago where the system will ultimately be moved.
- **[[National Quantum Algorithm Center]] (NQAC)**: Established in 2024 by Governor [[JB Pritzker]] with IBM as a leading member; the new system is its anchor.
- **[[Arvind Krishna]]**: IBM Chairman and CEO, who says the system can deliver scientifically useful results today.
- **[[Quantum Advantage]]**: IBM and the University of Chicago recently demonstrated advantage over leading classical methods using an approach with guaranteed correctness.
- **[[Public-Private Partnership]]**: Collaboration among government, academia, national labs and industry.

## Key Takeaways

- **First in Illinois**: IBM's first quantum computer in the state, and its most advanced available system.
- **Hardware**: Powered by IBM Quantum Nighthawk, IBM's most powerful quantum chip to date.
- **Relocation Plan**: Currently at Hyde Park Labs; will move to IQMP once construction finishes.
- **Industry Grand Challenges**: Deloitte and AbbVie work with IBM and Northwestern on quantum molecular simulation for drug discovery.
- **Energy Use Case**: EPRI and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign explore quantum optimization for power grids.
- **Physics Research**: Fermilab works with IBM on high-energy physics and distributed quantum computing.
- **Workforce**: The FutureNow Chicago center with City Colleges of Chicago is slated to bring 750 jobs.
