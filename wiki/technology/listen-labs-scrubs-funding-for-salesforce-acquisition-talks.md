## Summary

[[Listen Labs]], a three-year-old voice AI market research startup, walked away from a signed $125M Series C term sheet at a $1.5B valuation to pursue acquisition discussions with [[Salesforce]] reportedly valued at approximately $2 billion. The company generates ~$30M in annualized revenue and counts Microsoft, Canva, Anthropic, and Sweetgreen among its customers. If the Salesforce deal collapses, VCs expect Listen Labs to seek a $2B+ valuation in a renewed fundraise.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Listen Labs]]**: Voice AI startup founded in 2023 by [[Florian Jüngermann]] and [[Alfred Wahlforss]] (Harvard meetup); its AI conducts audio/video customer interviews and converts them into reports and presentations.
- **[[Salesforce]] acquisition talks**: Acquisition reportedly valued at ~$2B — higher than the $1.5B funding round valuation — which drove the decision to pull the term sheet.
- **[[Menlo Ventures]]**: Was set to lead the $125M Series C before the deal was scrubbed.
- **[[Ribbit Capital]]**: Led the prior $69M Series B at a $500M valuation.
- **[[Simile]]**: Competing voice AI / research startup that recently closed a $200M Series B at a $2B valuation, influencing Listen Labs' valuation expectations.
- **Revenue multiple**: At $30M ARR and a ~$2B acquisition price, the deal implies a ~67x revenue multiple — a figure one source flagged as a potential dealbreaker for Salesforce.

## Key Takeaways

- Listen Labs signed then scrubbed a $1.5B Series C to pursue a ~$2B Salesforce acquisition.
- Company is ~3 years old with $30M annualized revenue and enterprise customers.
- Competitor Simile's $2B valuation set a market benchmark that shaped Listen Labs' thinking.
- Deal remains unfinalized; a 67x revenue multiple may deter Salesforce.
- If acquisition falls through, VCs expect a renewed raise targeting $2B+ valuation.
- Product: AI-powered audio/video interviews auto-converted to reports and decks.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[BATNA]]**: Listen Labs' willingness to walk away from a signed term sheet demonstrates a strong Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement — the perceived Salesforce upside raised their floor, giving them leverage to demand more.
- **[[Anchoring]]**: Simile's $2B Series B valuation served as a market anchor that recalibrated Listen Labs' own valuation expectations upward, illustrating how comparable transactions set negotiation reference points.
