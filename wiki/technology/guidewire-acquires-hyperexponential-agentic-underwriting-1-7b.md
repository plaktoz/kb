## Summary

Insurance software company [[Guidewire]] announced it will acquire the UK-based agentic underwriting startup [[Hyperexponential]] for $1.7 billion in cash, with up to $400 million more tied to revenue targets. The deal aims to bring [[AI Agents]] to work alongside human underwriters on analysis and pricing decisions, and is expected to close next year. It marks a significant return for Hyperexponential's investors, who backed a company that had raised nearly $90 million.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Guidewire]]**: Insurance tech company acquiring Hyperexponential.
- **[[Hyperexponential]]**: UK-based, founded in 2017, builds software for agentic underwriting; used by nearly 600 insurers in 40 countries.
- **[[Agentic Underwriting]]**: [[AI Agents]] working alongside humans to underwrite, analyze, and price insurance decisions.
- **[[Andreessen Horowitz]] and [[Battery Ventures]]**: Hyperexponential's venture backers.
- **[[Earnout]] structure**: $400 million of additional capital is reserved if revenue targets are hit, bringing total deal value to about $2 billion.
- **[[Insurtech]]**: The sector context, with incumbents buying AI-native startups.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal price**: $1.7 billion in cash, about $2 billion including contingent capital.
- **Earnout**: $400 million is reserved, payable if revenue targets are met.
- **Timeline**: Closing expected sometime next year.
- **Purpose**: AI agents to work alongside humans on underwriting and pricing.
- **Investor return**: Startup raised nearly $90 million per PitchBook; exit is a big multiple.
- **Customer base**: Nearly 600 insurers across 40 countries use the software.
