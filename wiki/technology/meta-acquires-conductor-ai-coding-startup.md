## Summary
Meta has acquired Conductor, a 2024-founded AI coding startup that lets users run multiple agents in parallel workspaces. Conductor's agents come from major labs rather than being built in-house, and its cloud service shuts down on November 20. The deal fits Meta's push to catch up in AI, including its Muse Code coding agent.

## Core Concepts
- [[Conductor]]: AI coding startup founded in 2024; core product launched last summer; founder and CEO [[Charlie Holtz]].
- [[Parallel Coding Agents]]: Agentic teams deployed in the cloud, locally, or via phone, working on separate, compartmentalized parts of a project in concert.
- [[Meta Superintelligence Labs]]: Holtz will work with [[Alexandr Wang]] (chief AI officer) and [[Nat Friedman]] (head of product at MSL).
- [[Muse Code]]: Meta's own coding agent, launched in August; Muse agents reportedly passed 10 million users.
- [[Anthropic]] and [[OpenAI]]: Source of the agents Conductor deploys.
- [[Moltbook]]: Viral agent platform, among Meta's other AI-related acquisitions over the past year.

## Key Takeaways
- **Acquisition**: Meta bought Conductor; reported by Axios, confirmed to TechCrunch without further details.
- **Product**: Multiple agents run in parallel workspaces on separate parts of a project.
- **Third-party agents**: Conductor's agents come from labs like Anthropic and OpenAI, not in-house.
- **Shutdown**: Cloud service ends November 20; user cloud data then deleted.
- **Roadmap**: Core features keep working until early next year; "something new" promised then.
- **Meta context**: Meta, previously lagging in AI, launched Muse Code in August and made several AI acquisitions.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What did Meta acquire, and what does the company do?
**A**: Meta acquired Conductor, an AI coding startup that lets users spin up software projects by deploying multiple agents into parallel workspaces.

**Q2**: Where do Conductor's agents come from, and what happens to its services?
**A**: The agents come from major labs like Anthropic and OpenAI, not in-house. Core features keep working until early next year, but the cloud service shuts down November 20 and user cloud data is then deleted.

**Q3**: How does the deal fit Meta's broader AI strategy?
**A**: Meta, which previously lagged in AI services, has spent months catching up, launching Muse Code in August and making several AI-related acquisitions, including Moltbook. Holtz wrote that the team has "big, crazy plans for the next interface" and promised "something new" by early next year.
