## Summary

[[Meta]] launched the [[Muse Wonder]] model family, built for agentic workflows, just six weeks after [[Muse Spark]] 1.3. Independent benchmarker [[Artificial Analysis]] ranks Muse Wonder Full second only to Claude Opus 5.5 Max on its Intelligence Index, tied with Claude Sonnet 5.5 Max, which suggests Meta has reached the AI frontier. Muse Wonder is available to developers now, and will later power the [[Muse]] agent for paying subscribers.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Muse Wonder]] / Muse Wonder Full**: Meta's most capable models, designed for personal and professional agentic tasks such as insurance claims, leases and building apps.
- **[[Artificial Analysis]] Intelligence Index**: Independent weighted composite score; Muse Wonder Full is top three on six of ten evaluations.
- **Active Focus**: Ability to keep working toward a goal on hard tasks, recover from errors and check in before important actions.
- **Active Memory**: Ability to carry relevant details across conversations into the current task.
- **[[Alexandr Wang]]**: Meta's Chief AI Officer, hired after Meta paid $14.3 billion for [[Scale AI]]; codename for the models was "watermelon".
- **[[Muse Code]] and [[Meta Model API]]**: Distribution channels; Wonder Full is limited to Muse Code subscribers.
- **Privacy and safety**: Meta says the models avoid sharing private information without permission and refuse harmful requests.

## Key Takeaways

- **Frontier arrival**: Independent benchmarks place Muse Wonder Full behind only Claude Opus 5.5 Max.
- **Outranks rivals**: Leapfrogs OpenAI's GPT-Astra, Anthropic's Fable 5 and Google's Gemini 4.
- **Fast cadence**: Launched six weeks after Muse Spark 1.3; Muse debuted in April.
- **Agent adoption**: The Muse agent has surpassed 5 million app downloads.
- **Paid access**: Muse access to Wonder costs $20 or $100 per month.
- **Pending safety report**: Meta says a full safety evaluation report is coming soon.
