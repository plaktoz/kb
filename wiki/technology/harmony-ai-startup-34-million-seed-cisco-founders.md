## Summary

[[Harmony]], an AI startup founded by [[Nitzan Shapira]] and [[Ran Ribenzaft]] — who previously sold [[Epsagon]] to [[Cisco]] for $500 million — has raised $34 million in seed funding led by [[Lightspeed Venture Partners]]. Harmony operates inside [[Slack]] and [[Microsoft Teams]] to autonomously handle employee IT and HR requests such as onboarding, software access, and password resets, integrating with over 100 applications. The oversized seed round reflects the founders' track record and their stated ambition to build a much larger company than their last exit.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Harmony]]**: AI agent that handles workplace requests inside Slack and Microsoft Teams, using employee role and permissions to tailor responses.
- **[[Nitzan Shapira]] and [[Ran Ribenzaft]]**: Serial founders; previously built [[Epsagon]] (cloud software monitoring), acquired by [[Cisco]] in 2021.
- **[[Agentic AI]]**: Harmony goes beyond Q&A to take action — handling tasks autonomously with optional human-approval gates.
- **[[IT Helpdesk Automation]]**: Core use case; eliminating tickets for onboarding, access requests, and password resets.
- **[[Lightspeed Venture Partners]]**: Lead investor in both Epsagon and Harmony, backing the same founding team twice.
- **Seed Round Scale**: $34 million — well above typical seed; justified by founder pedigree and desire to scale fast before needing follow-on capital.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prior Exit**: Epsagon sold to [[Cisco]] for $500M in 2021; same founders now building Harmony.
- **Product**: AI agent inside Slack/Teams that resolves IT and HR requests autonomously.
- **Integrations**: Works with 100+ enterprise applications; respects role-based permissions.
- **Customers**: [[n8n]], [[eToro]], [[Cyera]], [[Kith]] already using the product.
- **Funding**: $34M seed led by Lightspeed; Hitachi Ventures, Fin Capital, Mercer Ventures, Operator Partners also joined.
- **Team**: 60 employees, ~two-thirds in Israel, rest in New York.
- **Strategy**: Founders explicitly want a larger outcome this time — no early acquisition.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Founder-Market Fit]]**: Shapira and Ribenzaft built enterprise monitoring tooling at Epsagon inside Cisco's stack — experience operating inside the enterprise gives them direct insight into where IT friction lives, making their conviction in Harmony structurally grounded rather than opportunistic.
- **[[Optionality Preservation]]**: Raising an oversized seed buys the founders runway to defer the next fundraise, keeping dilution and investor pressure at bay while the product matures — a deliberate trade of capital efficiency for strategic flexibility.
