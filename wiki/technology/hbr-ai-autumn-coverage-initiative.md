## Summary

HBR's managing editor Gretchen Gavett launches "AI Autumn," a month-long editorial initiative dedicated to examining what is actually happening with [[Generative AI]] in business and society. The series will feature new insights from the creators of [[Blue Ocean Strategy]] and address the gap between AI hype and grounded reality. The central question driving the initiative: *"What's actually happening with AI?"*

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Autumn]]**: HBR's framing of September–October 2026 as a critical inflection point for sober, evidence-based AI analysis — a counterpoint to years of hype cycles.
- **[[Blue Ocean Strategy]]**: Strategic framework by [[W. Chan Kim]] and [[Renée Mauborgne]] focused on creating uncontested market space; new insights from its creators are featured in the AI Autumn series.
- **[[Generative AI]]**: The primary technology under examination — its real-world adoption, productivity impact, and strategic implications for organizations.
- **[[HBR Insider]]**: HBR's editorial newsletter format used to announce and contextualize coverage themes for subscribers.
