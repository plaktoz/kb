## Summary

[[Town]], a two-year-old San Francisco startup founded by former [[Plaid]] CTO Jean-Denis Greze, reached a unicorn valuation with a $108 million Series B co-led by [[Forerunner Ventures]] and [[Menlo Ventures]]. It launched publicly four months ago and says it hit millions in annual recurring revenue within weeks. Its business-focused AI assistants, called Townies, connect to work tools and can now interact with outside customers.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AI Assistants]]**: Town gives each employee a Townie that connects to email, calendars, and core work systems.
- **Inter-assistant sharing**: Townies talk to each other and share approved information.
- **Shared workflow**: New feature auto-updates all Townies with new business information instead of per-worker updates.
- **External work**: Townies can now work with customers and others outside the company.
- **Funding**: $108M Series B, $55M Series A in June, at least $160M raised total.
- **Investors**: Forerunner, Menlo, Andreessen Horowitz, Serena Ventures, First Round Capital.
- **Founder**: [[Jean-Denis Greze]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Fast Unicorn**: Valuation above $1B four months after public launch.
- **Quick Revenue**: Millions in ARR within weeks of launch.
- **Funding Pace**: $55M Series A in June, then $108M Series B.
- **Crowded Market**: Town is one of many AI assistants flooding the market.
- **Enterprise Target**: Product aimed at businesses, one assistant per employee.
- **Shared Context**: Shared workflow removes per-employee Townie updating.
