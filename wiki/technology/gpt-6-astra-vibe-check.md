## Summary

Every's review of [[OpenAI]]'s [[GPT-6 Astra]] finds the model impressive across writing, software operation, and visual design tasks — notably, the model drafted the article itself from a single prompt and the piece was indistinguishable from the human author's work to Every's CEO [[Dan Shipper]]. Despite this, the review concludes that [[Anthropic]]'s [[Fable]] model retains a meaningful edge in product-building judgment tasks.

## Core Concepts

- **[[GPT-6 Astra]]** — [[OpenAI]]'s latest frontier model; the first publicly reviewed version following the earlier [[Astra]] security pause period
- **[[Vibe check methodology]]** — real-world task-based evaluation (writing, software operation, visual design) rather than formal benchmarks
- **[[AI writing indistinguishability]]** — GPT-6 Astra's draft was mistaken for human-authored work by [[Dan Shipper]], Every's CEO; a qualitative capability milestone
- **[[Fable]]** — [[Anthropic]]'s competing model that the reviewer judges superior for product-building tasks despite GPT-6 Astra's gains

## Key Takeaways

- **Self-authoring**: GPT-6 Astra drafted this review article from a single prompt.
- **Indistinguishability**: Every's CEO Dan Shipper could not tell the AI draft from the human's.
- **Strengths**: Writing, operating software, and visual design tasks.
- **Weakness vs. Anthropic**: Fable still outperforms on product-building judgment.
