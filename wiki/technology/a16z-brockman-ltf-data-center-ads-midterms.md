## Summary

A pro-AI super PAC called *Leading the Future* (LTF), backed by [[Marc Andreessen]], [[Ben Horowitz]], and [[Greg Brockman]], plans to spend millions on data center ads targeting voters in Kansas, Ohio, and Wisconsin ahead of the midterm elections. [[Andreessen Horowitz]] has become the largest disclosed donor to the midterms with over $115 million in federal contributions, also backing pro-crypto efforts through the [[Fairshake]] super PAC. [[OpenAI]] publicly distanced itself from LTF, condemning "astroturfing" tactics, while [[Anthropic]] supports the rival pro-regulation group Public First Action.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Leading the Future]] (LTF)** — pro-AI super PAC with over $50 million in funding; runs affiliated advocacy group *Build American AI*
- **[[Andreessen Horowitz]] (a16z)** — largest disclosed midterm donor at $115M+; also backs [[Fairshake]] for pro-crypto candidates
- **[[Greg Brockman]]** — OpenAI President and personal donor to LTF
- **[[Astroturfing]]** — making organized campaigns appear grassroots; tactic OpenAI explicitly condemned
- **[[Data Centers]]** — central message of LTF's ad campaign, framing them as economic and national-security assets
- **[[Anthropic]]** — backs Public First Action, a competing group supporting AI regulation
- **[[Midterm Elections 2026]]** — target political context; Kansas, Ohio, and Wisconsin as battleground states

## Key Takeaways

- **LTF ad strategy**: Run millions in ads promoting data center benefits in three swing states.
- **Funding scale**: a16z has contributed over $115M to midterm campaigns, the largest disclosed total.
- **OpenAI split**: OpenAI disavowed LTF and condemned astroturfing despite Brockman's personal involvement.
- **Crypto overlap**: Fairshake super PAC channels a16z funds toward pro-crypto congressional candidates.
- **Regulatory divide**: Anthropic's Public First Action backs AI regulation; LTF opposes AI skeptics.
- **LTF launch**: Super PAC launched with $50M+ and targets candidates' positions on AI.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Regulatory Capture]]**: Tech incumbents funding political campaigns to shape AI and data-center policy directly mirrors the classic dynamic where regulated industries redirect government outcomes in their favor rather than competing in the marketplace of ideas.
- **[[Astroturfing vs. Grassroots]]**: By branding the effort as *Build American AI* — a community-sounding name — LTF illustrates how organized money manufactures the appearance of organic public support, which is why OpenAI's explicit distancing is politically significant.
