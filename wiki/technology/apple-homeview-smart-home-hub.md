## Summary

[[Apple]] is reportedly set to announce the HomeView, a Siri-powered smart home hub with a display and an A18 chip, according to Bloomberg's [[Mark Gurman]]. It would be Apple's most serious smart home bet yet, following limited traction for [[HomeKit]] and the [[HomePod]]. The launch is expected alongside refreshed Apple TV and HomePod mini devices and an LG partnership on connected home accessories.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Apple HomeView]]**: A display-equipped smart home controller powered by [[Siri]] and an [[Apple A18 Chip]], used to control other smart home devices.
- **Two form factors**: One model for countertops and one designed to be wall-mounted.
- **Prior smart home efforts**: [[HomeKit]] software framework and the [[HomePod]] smart speaker, both of which failed to gain much traction.
- **Companion launches**: Updated [[Apple TV]] set-top box and [[HomePod mini]].
- **[[LG]] partnership**: Reported plan to co-create a smart lock, thermostat, and doorbell.
- **Source**: Reporting from [[Mark Gurman]] at [[Bloomberg]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Launch Timing**: HomeView reportedly announced Tuesday after years of rumored development.
- **Hardware Specs**: Siri-powered, includes a display and an A18 chip.
- **Form Factors**: Countertop model and wall-mounted model.
- **Track Record**: HomeKit and HomePod failed to gain much traction.
- **Broader Refresh**: Apple TV and HomePod mini updates expected the same day.
