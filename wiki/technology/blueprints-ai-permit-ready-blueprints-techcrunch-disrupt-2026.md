## Summary

[[Blueprints AI]], a Santa Monica startup founded by architect [[Al Dram]] and data scientist [[Coco Cao]], uses AI to turn a 2D sketch, PDF, or description into code-compliant, permit-ready construction blueprints in minutes. Its second-generation software, launching at [[TechCrunch Disrupt]] 2026, generates every building discipline at once and propagates any design change across the whole project while checking it against local building codes. The founders pitch it as a way to flip architects' time from 80% drafting / 20% design to the reverse, while licensed professionals still review and seal the documents.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Blueprints AI]]**: Incorporated in 2025 in Santa Monica; selected for the 2026 [[Startup Battlefield 200]] cohort at [[TechCrunch Disrupt]] (Oct 13–15, San Francisco).
- **Founders**: [[Al Dram]] (architecture degrees from the [[University of Tehran]], PhD coursework at the [[European Graduate School]], founder of Drams Design-Build) and [[Coco Cao]] (chief commercial officer, sales and go-to-market).
- **Origin**: Dram's decades-old thesis idea became feasible after [[ChatGPT]] and [[Large Language Models]] arrived in November 2022; he launched the concept within six months.
- **Proprietary training data**: Models trained on eight years of approved drawing sets from Dram's own design and construction firm.
- **Version 1**: Produces architectural construction documents; priced $3,200–$8,500/month, with custom enterprise pricing.
- **Version 2**: Generates architecture, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and interior design together; one change updates the entire project and is checked against [[Building Codes]].
- **Version 3 (in development)**: Adds structural analysis and electrical panel design.
- **[[Human-in-the-Loop]]**: Licensed architects still review and seal documents before permit submission — "the judgment stays human."
- **Target customers**: Architecture, engineering, and design firms; developers and real estate owners. Subscription model, possibly a one-time flat fee per project.

## Key Takeaways

- **Speed Claim**: Permit-ready blueprints generated in minutes from sketches or PDFs.
- **Time Flip**: Architects spend ~80% drafting, 20% designing; goal is to reverse it.
- **Integrated Disciplines**: V2 produces all building disciplines simultaneously from one input.
- **Change Propagation**: One design edit updates the whole project, code-checked.
- **Data Advantage**: Trained on eight years of approved drawings from founder's firm.
- **Pricing**: V1 costs $3,200–$8,500 per month; enterprise pricing available.
- **Human Sign-off**: Licensed professionals still review and seal permit documents.
- **Roadmap**: V3 adds structural analysis and electrical panel design.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Automating repetitive work reallocates expert time to judgment** — When the repetitive, rule-bound portion of skilled work is automated, the expert's scarce time shifts toward the creative, judgment-heavy portion that drives value.
→ explains: Time Flip, Speed Claim, Integrated Disciplines

**T2. Accountability for consequential output stays with a licensed human** — Automated systems can produce the work, but where errors carry legal or safety consequences, a qualified human must review and take responsibility for it. · *model:* [[Human-in-the-Loop]]
→ explains: Human Sign-off, Change Propagation
