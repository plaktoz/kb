## Summary

[[OpenAI]] expanded its [[GPT-6]] lineup by releasing Sol and Luna, two new models following the earlier [[GPT-6 Astra]] launch. Sol is optimized for complex tasks like coding while Luna targets high-volume clerical work such as summarization, and both are priced at half the cost of their 5.6 series predecessors. The release intensifies competition with [[Anthropic]], which released an updated [[Opus 5.5]] just 90 minutes earlier.

## Core Concepts

- **[[GPT-6 Sol]]** — [[OpenAI]]'s new model targeting complex tasks like coding; achieves Astra-level reliability at roughly half the cost
- **[[GPT-6 Luna]]** — companion model aimed at high-volume clerical work such as summarization and quick Q&A; accessible to Free and Go users
- **[[GPT-6 Astra]]** — the earlier flagship release whose intelligence Sol and Luna make "more efficient and accessible"
- **[[Inference cost reduction]]** — caching and inference improvements enabled both models to be priced at 50% of their 5.6 series predecessors
- **[[Anthropic]]** — rival lab that released [[Opus 5.5]] 90 minutes before OpenAI's announcement, highlighting the intensifying race between the two labs

## Key Takeaways

- **Pricing**: Both Sol and Luna cost half their 5.6 series predecessors.
- **Accuracy**: GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many mistakes as its predecessor.
- **Reliability parity**: Sol reaches Astra-level reliability at much lower cost.
- **Target use cases**: Sol → coding/complex tasks; Luna → summarization/Q&A.
- **Availability**: Rolls out to ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API for paid users.
- **Luna access**: Also available to Free and Go users via the desktop app.
- **Rivalry**: Anthropic launched Opus 5.5 90 minutes before this announcement.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Cost-capability frontier]]**: Sol and Luna demonstrate that inference optimization (caching, efficiency gains) can push the capability-per-dollar curve outward — matching a prior frontier model's reliability at half the price, rather than needing a new architectural breakthrough.
