# IBM and the USTA Introduce New AI-Powered Fan Experiences for 2026 US Open

## Summary

IBM and the USTA unveiled three new AI-powered features for USOpen.org and the US Open app at the 2026 tournament: a personalized Live Updates homepage, a limb-tracking Serve Quality metric, and an enhanced conversational Match Chat. The rollout responds to survey data showing high fan trust in AI-powered sports content, and leans on IBM technologies like [[IBM Confluent]], [[IBM Bob]], and [[watsonx Orchestrate]] to process roughly 1.2 billion serve data points across the tournament.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Serve Quality]]**: An AI-powered metric using limb-tracking technology from [[IBM Bob]], capturing 21 data points across the body and racquet at 50 times per second to score serve mechanics in near real-time.
- **[[IBM Confluent]]**: Manages the continuous live data stream (efficiency, accuracy, consistency, ball toss) that feeds the Serve Quality score.
- **[[Key Moments]]**: An extension of the existing Likelihood to Win feature that summarizes match swings, turning points, and momentum shifts.
- **[[Match Chat]]**: A conversational AI companion powered by [[watsonx Orchestrate]], using a mix of AI agents and fit-for-purpose models trained on USTA editorial style, now able to surface photos and video alongside text answers.
- **[[Live Updates homepage]]**: A new personalized homepage letting fans prioritize favorite players and surface relevant matches, insights, and stories.
- **[[United States Tennis Association]]** (USTA) and **[[IBM]]** partnership dates back to 1992, serving more than 14 million global tennis fans annually.

## Key Takeaways

- **Fan trust in AI**: 91% of surveyed tennis fans use sports apps during events; 64% trust AI-powered sports content.
- **Serve Quality scale**: Generates roughly 1.2 billion data points across all 254 singles matches in the tournament.
- **Data granularity**: 21 data points tracked per serve, 50 times per second, covering wrist flex through leg-to-torso energy transfer.
- **Match Chat upgrade**: Now returns photos and video, not just text, drawing on live and historical match data.
- **Tournament context**: The 2026 US Open runs August 23 – September 13, with features available via USOpen.org and the mobile app.
