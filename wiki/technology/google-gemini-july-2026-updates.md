## Summary
Google's July 2026 AI recap introduced three new efficiency-focused [[Gemini]] models for scaling AI agents, launched [[Gemini Robotics ER 2]] for embodied reasoning, and expanded creative and productivity tools including Gemini Notebook, Google Vids, and Lyria 3.5 music generation.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Gemini]]**: Google's model family; Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber target the efficiency/latency sweet spot for scaling production [[AI Agents]].
- **[[Gemini Robotics ER 2]]**: Google's most capable "embodied reasoning" model, letting robots converse naturally, interpret surroundings, and execute multi-step tasks.
- **[[AlphaEvolve]]**: Google's Gemini-powered AI code-optimization agent, now generally available on Google Cloud, which searches for better algorithm implementations given a baseline and stated goals.
- **AI & Economy ATLAS**: A large-scale, de-identified Google study tracking how AI is actually used at work and in daily life.

## Key Takeaways
- **Three new Gemini models** ship specifically for efficient, scalable agentic workflows rather than raw capability gains.
- **Gemini Robotics ER 2** bridges digital reasoning and physical-world robot tasks.
- **AlphaEvolve going GA** signals Google pushing AI-driven algorithm optimization into mainstream enterprise cloud tooling.
- **Infrastructure-scale AI use**: Google partnered with NOAA to run weather models on Cloud H4D VMs and launched FireSat satellites for early wildfire detection.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What was the main purpose of the three new Gemini models announced in July 2026?
**A**: To give developers higher token efficiency, lower latency, and more reliable performance for scaling production AI agent workflows.

**Q2**: What does Gemini Robotics ER 2 add over prior robotics models?
**A**: It's Google's most capable "embodied reasoning" model, letting robots converse naturally with people, interpret their surroundings, and complete complex multi-step tasks.

**Q3**: How is Google applying AI beyond consumer products in this update?
**A**: It made AlphaEvolve generally available for enterprise algorithm optimization and partnered with NOAA to run weather forecasting models on Google Cloud infrastructure.
