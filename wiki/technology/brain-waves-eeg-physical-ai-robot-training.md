## Summary

[[Encord]] is experimenting with [[EEG]] brain wave signals as a novel source of training data for physical AI and robotics. The core problem is a data shortage: physical AI systems need far more real-world interaction data than current collection methods supply, and synthetic data has limits. Brain waves offer a way to capture human perceptual judgments at scale without requiring physical robot deployment.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Physical AI]]** — AI systems that perceive and act in the physical world; requires rich sensorimotor training data
- **[[EEG]] (electroencephalography)** — brain wave recording technology being repurposed as a training data source
- **[[Encord]]** — data annotation and AI training platform exploring brain-wave-based data collection
- **[[Training Data Shortage]]** — the bottleneck limiting physical AI progress; why novel data sources are being explored
- **[[Robot Training Data]]** — the data pipeline challenge distinguishing software AI from physical AI development

## Key Takeaways

- **Data bottleneck**: physical AI progress is limited by quantity and diversity of real-world interaction data
- **Brain wave signal**: EEG captures human perceptual responses without requiring physical robot involvement
- **Encord's approach**: using brain waves to generate implicit human judgments as training signal
- **Synthetic data limits**: simulated environments can't fully substitute for real sensorimotor experience
