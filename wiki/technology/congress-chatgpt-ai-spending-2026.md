## Summary

House spending records show [[ChatGPT]] dominates paid AI tool use on Capitol Hill: [[OpenAI]] captured roughly 90% of all AI tool spending by House offices during the year ending March 31, 2026, far outpacing [[Anthropic]]'s Claude. Congressional staffers use these tools for memos, legislation summaries, constituent replies, and hearing prep.

## Core Concepts

- **[[OpenAI]]**: Received ~$100,580 across 798 transactions (out of $113,740 total AI spend) from House offices, committees, and institutional accounts.
- **[[Anthropic]]**: Claude trailed far behind at $13,160 across 37 transactions.
- **Partisan AI adoption gap**: Democratic offices spent $54,165 on AI tools — roughly 3x the $15,782 spent by Republican offices.
- **Government AI use cases**: Drafting memos, summarizing/analyzing legislation, responding to constituents, preparing hearing materials, and drafting social media posts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Market concentration**: ChatGPT holds ~90% of paid Congressional AI spend, a striking real-world enterprise-adoption data point.
- **Data scope limits**: Figures exclude free-tier usage and AI bundled into broader software contracts, so actual usage is likely understated.
- **Partisan divide**: Democratic offices outspent Republican offices roughly 3-to-1 on AI tools during the measured period.
- **Use-case breadth**: Adoption spans legislative research, constituent services, and communications — not just drafting.
