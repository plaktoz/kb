## Summary
Corporate-spending platform Ramp's July data shows Anthropic still leads OpenAI among U.S. businesses (43.5% vs. 39.7% adoption), but OpenAI is growing faster early in Q3 2026, partly credited to its July GPT-5.6 model family launch, narrowing a lead that first opened in spring 2026.

## Core Concepts

- [[Ramp AI Index]] — built from aggregated, anonymized transactions on Ramp cards and Bill Pay across 70,000+ American businesses, skewed somewhat more tech-oriented than the general market
- [[Enterprise AI Adoption Race]] — the ongoing, non-exclusive competition between [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] for U.S. business customers, where companies increasingly pay for both rather than choosing one
- [[GPT-5.6 Model Family]] — OpenAI's July 2026 release (flagship "Sol," lower tiers "Terra" and "Luna," later discounted 80% and 20% respectively), credited with OpenAI's early-Q3 momentum
- [[Fable 5]] — Anthropic's higher-end model, which saw weaker adoption tied to price and a 30-day customer-data retention requirement for safety monitoring (data not used for training, deleted after 30 days in nearly all cases)

## Key Takeaways

- **July adoption split**: Anthropic 43.5% of businesses (+1.1 pts from June) vs. OpenAI 39.7% (+0.23 pts) — Anthropic still ahead by 3.8 points but growing more slowly.
- **Trend history**: Anthropic first overtook OpenAI in spring 2026 (34.4% vs. 32.3% in April, widening to 41% vs. 39% by May).
- **Overall market growth**: share of businesses paying for any AI product rose from just over 50% in March to nearly 56% in July — both companies can gain customers simultaneously.
- **Token-spend detail**: Fable 5 accounted for only 6% of tokens but 11.4% of dollars spent on Anthropic models in its first month, reflecting its premium pricing and narrower adoption.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the core claim of this article?
**A**: OpenAI is closing the enterprise-adoption gap with Anthropic in Q3 2026 even though Anthropic still leads on Ramp's July business-adoption reading.

**Q2**: What specific product change is credited with OpenAI's early-Q3 momentum?
**A**: The July launch of the GPT-5.6 model family (Sol, Terra, Luna), with Terra and Luna later discounted 80% and 20% respectively, reportedly appealing strongly to developers per Ramp's economist.

**Q3**: Why shouldn't Ramp's adoption percentages be read as a zero-sum market-share split?
**A**: The figures aren't mutually exclusive — many businesses pay for both OpenAI and Anthropic simultaneously, and overall AI-spending adoption across all businesses is still rising, so both companies can add customers while their relative share shifts.
