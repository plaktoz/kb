## Summary
[[OpenAI]] is adding visual display ads to [[ChatGPT]], placed alongside images that users ask it to generate. The ads start later this month in the US only, with a small test group of advertisers. OpenAI is also expanding its ad measurement partners and testing brand-suitability evaluation, as it works to make ChatGPT's free and low-cost tiers a viable ad-supported business.

## Core Concepts
- **Visual display ads**: Image-based ads shown next to ChatGPT's generated images. They are clearly labeled, and OpenAI says they won't influence ChatGPT's answers.
- **Ad rollout path**: OpenAI introduced ads earlier in 2026 to support its free and low-cost tiers, expanded them to India in August, and plans to eventually reach ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users globally.
- **Competitive pressure**: The launch follows [[Meta Muse]], Meta's free agentic AI app, which is a growing rival to leading chatbots like ChatGPT.
- **Measurement ecosystem**: Click attribution partners include [[AppsFlyer]], Triple Whale, Adjust, DV Rockerbox, Northbeam, Branch, Singular, Kochava, Airbridge and Tenjin. Full-funnel measurement partners are Fospha, Measured and INCRMNTAL.
- **Geo-based experiments**: Haus, Measured and WorkMagic will help advertisers run geography-based tests to measure ad lift.
- **Brand suitability pilots**: [[DoubleVerify]] and [[Integral Ad Science]] will help advertisers assess the ChatGPT ads environment.
- **[[Advertising in AI]]**: Bringing the display-ad model into a generative AI interface carries a risk of disrupting the user experience.

## Key Takeaways
- **Placement**: Ads appear alongside user-requested image generations in ChatGPT.
- **Scope**: US-only at launch, with an initial test group of advertisers.
- **Trust guardrail**: Ads are labeled and said not to influence ChatGPT's answers.
- **Scale target**: Global rollout would reach 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users.
- **Trigger**: Follows the launch of [[Meta Muse]], a free competing agentic AI app.
- **Measurement push**: 13+ attribution and measurement partners, plus geo-experiment tools.
- **Possible upsell lever**: Disruptive ads may push casual users toward paid subscriptions.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Freemium Model]]**: Ads both monetize free users and make the free tier less pleasant, so an ad-free subscription becomes the upgrade. That is the dynamic the article suggests may be "partly the point."
