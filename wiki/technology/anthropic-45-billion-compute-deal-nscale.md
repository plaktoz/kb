## Summary

[[Anthropic]] has agreed to rent roughly $45 billion in AI compute from [[Nscale]], a British AI infrastructure firm founded in 2024, using [[Nvidia]] Vera Rubin chips from a West Virginia data center over a six-year term starting late 2027. The deal is the latest in a series of aggressive compute-expansion moves Anthropic has made over the past eight months, totaling tens of billions of dollars across multiple partners.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Nscale]]**: A British AI infrastructure firm founded in 2024, hosting [[Nvidia]] Vera Rubin chips in a West Virginia data center; Anthropic's newest large-scale compute partner.
- **[[Nvidia Vera Rubin]]**: A six-chip system representing the current frontier of chip design, the hardware underpinning the Nscale deal and other recent Anthropic contracts.
- **[[Compute Scarcity]]**: Anthropic's ongoing pattern of signing multi-billion, multi-year deals to lock up GPU capacity years in advance, mirroring broader AI industry dynamics.
- **[[AI Infrastructure Race]]**: Google, OpenAI, and Meta are similarly racing to secure compute capacity, suggesting an industry-wide structural shift toward long-horizon infrastructure commitments.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal size**: $45 billion over six years — Anthropic's largest disclosed compute contract.
- **Hardware**: Nvidia Vera Rubin chips (six-chip system, current frontier) from Nscale's West Virginia facility.
- **Timeline**: Service starts late 2027; six-year term.
- **Recent compute spree**: Anthropic has signed deals with Volta ($10B, August 2026), AMD ($5B, July 2026), SpaceX (~$1.25B/month, May 2026), and expanded Amazon/Google/Broadcom ties (April 2026).
- **Industry context**: Google, OpenAI, and Meta are pursuing the same strategy, normalizing mega-scale compute lock-in.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Competitive Moat via Resource Acquisition]]**: Securing scarce compute years ahead of need is a form of supply-side moat — the lab that controls the most GPU capacity constrains rivals' ability to train and serve frontier models.
- **[[Arms Race Dynamics]]**: Each incremental commitment by one lab raises the stakes for all others, creating a self-reinforcing escalation loop that is rational at the individual level but costly in aggregate.
