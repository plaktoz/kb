## Summary
Phase launched a Layer 1 blockchain mainnet backed by $45 million from founder Kirill Eves, aiming to be the operating layer for AI agents. The network runs on everyday devices such as smartphones and bundles web access, model routing, distributed compute and payments for agents. Services pay in stablecoins or the PHASE token, with device owners earning PHASE for validating and supplying resources.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Phase]]**: a [[Layer 1 Blockchain]] positioned as the infrastructure for the [[AI Agent Economy]].
- **[[Kirill Eves]]**: founder, also founder of Unlimit.
- **[[Decentralized Physical Infrastructure]]**: smartphones and other devices secure the network and supply connectivity and compute.
- **[[Reputation-Based Consensus]]**: rewards reliable participation and randomly selects validators.
- **[[Phase Proxy]]**: routes agent requests through mobile and residential connections to reach blocked sites.
- **[[Phase Router]] and [[Phase Compute]]**: due November 2026 for model routing and decentralized inference.
- **[[x402]] and [[Stablecoins]]**: pay-as-you-go on-chain agent payments.

## Key Takeaways
- **Funding**: $45 million from founder Kirill Eves, not outside investors.
- **Low Barrier**: Join the network by downloading an app; no extra hardware or staking.
- **Proxy Pricing**: From 0.5 USDT per 1,000 requests and 1 GB, versus $5+ per GB typical.
- **Roadmap**: Router and Compute launch November 2026; own AI model planned.
- **Tokenomics**: Revenue funds open-market PHASE purchases distributed to node operators.
- **Founder Thesis**: Agent infrastructure should not belong to any single company.
