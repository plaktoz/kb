## Summary

Digital threat detection startup [[Overwatch]] raised a $15 million Series A led by Mouro Capital, as its CEO argues AI has already made cybercrime faster, cheaper and more interconnected. He says cyberattacks, fraud and money laundering are no longer separate silos and must be defended against holistically. Customers include Visa, Capital One, Cash App, SoFi, Stripe and DoorDash.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Overwatch]]**: New York startup, founded 2022 by Arjun Bisen and Zara Perumal, building AI agents to help companies scale defense against digital threats.
- **[[Arjun Bisen]]**: Cofounder and CEO, former Australian diplomat who shaped national cyber engagement strategy.
- **[[Cybercrime]] convergence**: Attackers blend exploits, fraud and financial crime into one "smash and grab, move money away" operation.
- **[[AI-Enabled Fraud]]**: Impersonation and automated attacks scale quickly at near-zero cost.
- **[[Mouro Capital]]**: Series A lead, with Visa Ventures, Capital One Ventures, PruVen Capital, New Era Capital Partners and [[Y Combinator]].
- **Illicit-economy SaaS**: Specialist tools for sending scam texts and harvesting stolen logins.

## Key Takeaways

- **Funding**: $15 million Series A, announced exclusively via Fortune.
- **Collapsing silos**: Cybersecurity, money laundering and fraud are no longer separate problems.
- **Cost of attack**: Mastercard cites under $3 per attack, effectively zero barrier to entry.
- **Scale example**: One actor ran 100+ campaigns in five days, compromised 27 payment sites.
- **Card theft**: Roughly 600,000 card details captured by skimming behind those sites.
- **Front businesses**: Bookshop and repair sites linked to gambling and illegal cash movement.
- **Illicit SaaS**: Criminal economy has its own enterprise software.

## 🧠 Core Principles

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Overwatch's central claim about modern cybercrime?
**A**: AI has already changed cybercrime for good by blending cyberattacks, fraud and financial crime into a single operation. Defending against them in separate silos therefore no longer works and they must be addressed together.

**Q2**: What evidence shows how fast AI lets attacks scale?
**A**: Mastercard puts the cost at under $3 per attack, and one threat actor launched over 100 campaigns in about five days, compromising 27 payment sites and capturing details of over 600,000 cards.

**Q3**: How is Overwatch positioned to respond, and who is backing it?
**A**: It builds AI agents to help companies scale defense against digital threats, backed by a $15 million Series A led by Mouro Capital with Visa Ventures and Capital One Ventures. Customers like Visa, Stripe and DoorDash are preparing for anyone-can-do-it cybercrime.
