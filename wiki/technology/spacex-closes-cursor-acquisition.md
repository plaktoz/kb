## Summary

SpaceX has officially closed its $60 billion all-stock acquisition of AI coding startup Cursor, folding it into the xAI division and giving Cursor access to what it calls the largest GPU fleet in the world.

## Core Concepts

- [[Cursor]] — AI coding assistant startup, now fully owned by SpaceX after a deal first struck in April 2026.
- [[SpaceX]] / [[xAI]] — Elon Musk's space company, which also owns xAI and has been renting out compute to Anthropic and Google.
- [[Vertical AI Infrastructure Consolidation]] — the broader trend of infrastructure owners (compute, satellites) acquiring application-layer AI companies rather than just partnering with them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Deal size**: $60 billion all-stock acquisition, officially closed as of August 15, 2026.
- **Origin**: Began as an April 2026 technology partnership with an option to acquire; SpaceX exercised it after going public in June.
- **Rationale**: Cursor gains "the largest fleet of GPUs in the world"; SpaceX gains a flagship AI application and 50,000+ enterprise customers.
- **Context**: SpaceX has separately been renting compute to rivals Anthropic and Google, and faces a lawsuit over gas-turbine data center pollution.
