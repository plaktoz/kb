## Summary
SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) launched Grok Bot, a beta feature offering always-on AI agents that work in the background as a "highly capable teammate," can run in parallel or manage each other in teams, and communicate in group chats to complete multi-step tasks with minimal human input.

## Core Concepts

- [[Grok Bot]] — SpaceXAI's persistent background AI agent; available in beta to SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium subscribers
- [[Persistent AI Agents]] — the broader category Grok Bot belongs to, alongside similar always-on background agents from [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]]
- [[Multi-Agent Coordination]] — Grok Bot's ability to run several bots in parallel, have one manage a team of bots, and message each other independently while working toward a shared task

## Key Takeaways

- **Beta access**: live now for SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium on desktop and iOS; Enterprise users can join a waitlist.
- **Delegation model**: bots "pass work, assign ownership, and only pull you in for judgment calls."
- **Real examples cited**: one bot collects NDA signatures via invites/follow-ups; another finds and follows up on open invoices across portals and email, escalating only disputed cases.
- **Competitive context**: follows OpenAI and Anthropic's own persistent background-agent offerings — always-on, team-capable agents are becoming a standard feature across major AI labs.
