## Summary

[[Alphabet]] posted Q3 2026 revenue of almost $128 billion, up 25%, yet its stock fell about 12% in one of its largest post-earnings drops because EPS of $2.12 missed the $3.02 consensus. The company admitted that AI Mode queries earn less than comparable web searches, with fewer and cheaper ads, while search volume growth slowed and search revenue was flat. Google also expects to spend even more on AI next year.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Alphabet]] Q3 2026 results**: Net income above $26 billion but almost $9 billion lower year over year.
- **[[Google Cloud]]**: Crossed $30 billion in revenue, about a billion above analyst guesses.
- **[[AI Mode]]**: Gemini-powered AI search platform where each query earns less than a similar web search.
- **[[Google Search]]**: Query growth slowed to 12% from 17% in Q2 and 19% in Q1; search revenue was flat.
- **[[Philipp Schindler]]**: Chief Business Officer who discussed search volume and AI Mode economics on the earnings call.
- **[[Gemini]]**: Model family behind Google's shift to AI search and AI features across products.
- **[[AI Capital Expenditure]]**: Google expects to spend even more on AI next year.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stock Drop**: Shares fell almost 12%, one of the largest post-earnings drops in company history.
- **EPS Miss**: $2.12 earnings per share versus $3.02 consensus.
- **Slowing Search**: Growth of 12% versus 17% in Q2 and 19% in Q1.
- **Flat Revenue**: Search revenue was flat in Q3.
- **Lower AI Yield**: Each AI Mode query earns less, with fewer ads that cost less.
- **Cloud Beat**: Google Cloud revenue topped $30 billion, above analyst estimates.
- **More Spending**: Google expects higher AI spending next year despite weaker monetization.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. A new product that monetizes less can erode the profits of the one it replaces** — When users shift from a higher-earning product to a lower-earning substitute, volume can grow while revenue stalls. The article illustrates this with AI Mode queries earning less than web search, alongside flat search revenue.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: Why did Alphabet's stock fall despite 25% revenue growth?
**A**: EPS of $2.12 missed the $3.02 consensus, net income was almost $9 billion lower year over year, search revenue was flat, and Google plans even more AI spending next year.

**Q2**: What did Google disclose about AI Mode economics?
**A**: Per Philipp Schindler, each AI Mode query earns less than a similar web search because there are fewer ads, and they cost less. Search query growth also slowed to 12% from 17% in Q2 and 19% in Q1.

**Q3**: What does this imply about the shift to AI search?
**A**: Growth in AI search usage does not automatically translate into search revenue, so Google must find ways to monetize it, such as adding more ads. Investors are weighing that against rising AI spending.
