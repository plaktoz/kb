## Summary

On Nov 16, 2026, [[Serena Williams]] and [[Beth Ferreira]] launched Starfire Ventures, a New York early-stage firm targeting a $200M debut fund for AI-native startups with $5M-$10M checks. It succeeds Serena Ventures, which raised $111M in 2022 and has finished writing checks. The launch is framed as building an institution that outlasts its founders.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Starfire Ventures]]**: New early-stage firm targeting fintech, health and wellness, and experience-economy startups.
- **[[Serena Ventures]]**: Williams' 2022 firm with a $111M fund; now managing its existing portfolio.
- **[[Beth Ferreira]]**: Managing partner; formerly FirstMark Capital, Etsy and Fab.com.
- **[[AI-Native Startups]]**: Companies built on autonomous workflows and proprietary datasets as moats.
- **[[Application Layer AI]]**: Thesis targeting consumer uses rather than infrastructure, compute or developer tooling.
- **[[University Spinouts]]**: Check sizes fit academic AI ventures moving past SBIR-style grants.

## Key Takeaways

- **Fund Size**: $200M target, 80% above Serena Ventures' $111M.
- **Check Size**: $5M-$10M, lead or co-lead for post-seed and Series A.
- **Track Record**: 100+ companies, five exits and 16 unicorns across the partners' portfolio.
- **Founder Mix**: About 70% of prior backed companies had women or founders of color.
- **Institution Building**: Rebrand removes the founder-name limit on syndication and succession.
- **Market Context**: Generic SaaS multiples contracted while domain-specific AI funding stayed robust.
- **Timeline**: First portfolio announcements expected in early 2027.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Starfire Ventures and why was it created?
**A**: A $200M-target early-stage fund launched by Serena Williams and Beth Ferreira for AI-native startups. It is meant to be an enduring institution rather than a namesake vehicle, succeeding Serena Ventures.

**Q2**: What are the fund's key parameters?
**A**: $5M-$10M checks into post-seed and Series A companies across AI-native platforms, fintech, health and wellness, and the experience economy. The target is 80% larger than the $111M Serena Ventures fund.

**Q3**: What does the launch imply for academic founders?
**A**: Its check sizes match what university spinouts need after translational grants, but founders must clear IP ownership and conflict-of-interest disclosure before venture financing.
