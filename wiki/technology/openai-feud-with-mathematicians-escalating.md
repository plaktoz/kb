## Summary

Twenty-five [[Fields Medal]] recipients co-signed an open letter warning that AI labs are undermining mathematical intellectual work through competitive proof-racing and inadequate attribution. NYU professor [[Tristan Buckmaster]] accused [[OpenAI]] of pressuring him to exclude an [[Anthropic]] collaborator from credit on a significant math problem, and raised suspicions that his Codex work may have informed OpenAI's own unverified [[Navier-Stokes]] proof. OpenAI's subsequent withdrawal from sponsoring a CalTech math event further inflamed tensions.

## Core Concepts

- **[[OpenAI]] vs. the mathematical community**: A growing public conflict over attribution, credit, and the ethics of AI-driven proofs
- **[[Navier-Stokes]] proof controversy**: OpenAI published an unverified proof; attribution integrity questioned by academic collaborators
- **[[Tristan Buckmaster]] accusation**: Alleged pressure from OpenAI to exclude an Anthropic collaborator from credit; raised suspicions about prior Codex work informing OpenAI's proof
- **Open letter at [[mathandai.org]]**: Signed by 25 Fields Medal recipients; warns of proof-racing undermining proper writeups, attribution, and integration of prior work
- **[[Leiden Declaration]]** (June 2026): Related guidance for mathematicians, institutions, and policymakers on AI use in research
- **Academia vs. big labs resource asymmetry**: Labs spending tens of millions risk pushing academia toward secrecy and competitive isolation

## Key Takeaways

- **25 Fields Medalists signed**: Open letter at mathandai.org condemns AI-driven proof-racing.
- **Attribution dispute**: Buckmaster says OpenAI pressured him to exclude an Anthropic collaborator.
- **Unverified proof**: OpenAI's Navier-Stokes result has not been independently confirmed.
- **Sponsorship withdrawal**: OpenAI pulled funding from a CalTech math event amid criticism.
- **Leiden Declaration**: June 2026 guidance addresses AI's role in academic mathematics.
- **Resource gap**: Labs' multi-million dollar budgets may force academia into secrecy.
- **Canary in the coal mine**: Mathematicians warn other fields will face the same pressures next.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Tragedy of the Commons]]**: Competitive proof-racing by well-funded AI labs depletes the shared resource of open mathematical collaboration — each lab optimises for proprietary credit at the expense of the cumulative, attributable knowledge base that makes mathematics work.
- **[[Goodhart's Law]]**: When "solving hard math problems" becomes a benchmark for AI capability, labs optimise for published proofs rather than verified, well-attributed mathematical progress — the metric displaces the underlying goal.
