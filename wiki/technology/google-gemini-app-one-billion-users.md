## Summary

Google's [[Gemini]] app surpassed 1 billion monthly active users as of August 11, 2026, becoming the 14th Google product to hit that mark and matching [[ChatGPT]]'s growth pace. The milestone follows a Q2 2026 earnings call where Gemini had 950M+ monthly users, with daily actives tripling over the past year.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Gemini]] app**: Google's standalone AI assistant app, distinct from [[AI Mode]] in [[Google Search]] (which separately exceeds 1 billion monthly users) — the two are not counted together.
- **[[ChatGPT]]**: Reached 1 billion monthly active users in June 2026, giving Google a direct pace comparison point.
- **[[Gemini 3.5 Flash]]**: Newer model Google is rolling out, aimed at improving coding and autonomous AI-agent tasks within the app.

## Key Takeaways

- **Growth pace**: Gemini crossed 1B MAU roughly two months after ChatGPT did, per Google's own framing.
- **Usage pattern**: 63% of Gemini users interact via voice; the app generates 150M+ images per day.
- **Platform reach**: Gemini now has 100M+ active users on iOS alone, beyond Google's own Android/Workspace integration.
- **Timing**: The announcement lands just ahead of Google's "Made by Google" event, where more Gemini-powered Pixel features are expected.
