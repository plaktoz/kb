## Summary

[[OpenAI]] announced a new independent advisory body — the [[AGMAI|Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence]] — hosted at the [[Institute for Advanced Study]] in Princeton, designed to give mathematicians greater input into OpenAI's math research. The announcement accompanied a disclosure that OpenAI's internal model has resolved over 100 open mathematical problems across various fields, following its earlier claimed solution to the [[Navier-Stokes]] Millennium Prize problem. The move comes amid significant pushback from the mathematical community, including an open letter signed by 25 [[Fields Medal]] recipients warning that AI labs are endangering mathematicians' intellectual work.

## Core Concepts

- **[[AGMAI]] formation**: Advisory body hosted at the [[Institute for Advanced Study]], Princeton; intended as "a bridge to the mathematical community" for OpenAI's math research.
- **100+ open problems resolved**: OpenAI's internal model reportedly solved over 100 open mathematical problems across various fields, on top of the [[Navier-Stokes]] Millennium Prize claim.
- **[[Fields Medal]] recipients' letter**: 25 Fields Medalists signed an open letter arguing AI labs are endangering mathematicians' intellectual work through competitive problem-solving races.
- **Advisory group mandate limits**: Members are unpaid, hold no decision-making authority, and OpenAI explicitly stated the group would not advise on pacing internal research progress.
- **Community alignment gap**: Of the nine founding members, only one also signed the Fields Medalists' open letter — suggesting limited overlap between advisory membership and the critical academic faction.

## Key Takeaways

- **100+ problems solved**: OpenAI's model resolved over 100 open math problems, not just [[Navier-Stokes]].
- **No authority**: AGMAI members are unpaid and hold no decision-making power.
- **Restricted scope**: OpenAI was explicit the group cannot advise on research pacing.
- **IAS clarification**: Institute for Advanced Study noted responsibility for company decisions "will rest with that company."
- **Limited critical representation**: Only 1 of 9 founding AGMAI members also signed the Fields Medalists' protest letter.
- **Mathematicians' concern**: 25 Fields Medalists warned AI labs are harming mathematical intellectual work.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Legitimacy Without Authority]]**: OpenAI gains reputational cover from an advisory board while structurally excluding it from any influence over research pacing — the advisory role is symbolic rather than substantive, a classic pattern when incumbents seek perceived accountability without surrendering control.
- **[[Goodhart's Law]]**: When "resolving open problems" becomes an AI benchmark, labs optimise for the count of resolved problems rather than verified, community-validated mathematical progress — the 100+ claim exemplifies this dynamic.
