## Summary

[[Meta Superintelligence Labs]] released [[Muse Wonder]] and Muse Wonder Full, the company's first attempt at a frontier model, with benchmark scores Meta says are comparable to [[Fable]] 5.1 and [[GPT-6]] Astra. The models are larger and more capable than the lightweight [[Muse Spark]], and they emphasize "active memory", the ability to retain and synthesize user information over long periods, on which Meta claims leading [[MemoryBench]] scores. Wonder is priced at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens through the [[Meta Model API]], while Wonder Full is limited to [[Muse Code]] subscribers.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Muse Wonder]] / Muse Wonder Full**: Meta's larger, more capable models, built for complex tasks like coding, unlike the low-latency [[Muse Spark]].
- **Active memory**: Meta's name for a model's ability to retain and synthesize information the user gives it over days, weeks and months. This supports Meta's goal of long-running personal assistants.
- **[[MemoryBench]]**: A benchmark that tests continual learning and memory construction frameworks. Meta says Wonder beats Fable, Opus and Astra on it.
- **Distribution split**: Wonder is sold through the [[Meta Model API]]. Wonder Full is only for [[Muse Code]] subscribers and is not on the API. Both power the [[Muse]] agent.
- **[[Alexandr Wang]] restructuring**: This is the first frontier release since Wang, the Scale AI founder, restructured Meta's AI efforts in August of last year.
- **[[Prometheus Data Center]]**: The models were trained at Meta's central Ohio facility, which came online earlier this year.
- **Personal superintelligence**: Meta calls the models "the next step on our scaling ladder."

## Key Takeaways

- **First frontier attempt**: Muse Wonder is Meta's first state-of-the-art frontier model release.
- **Benchmark parity**: Meta's model card claims scores comparable to Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra.
- **Pricing**: $4 input / $20 output per million tokens, similar to GPT-6 Sol and Opus 5.5.
- **Gated Full model**: Wonder Full is only for Muse Code subscribers, with no API access.
- **Memory focus**: Meta claims MemoryBench scores higher than Fable, Opus or Astra.
- **Agent integration**: Both models power the Muse agent.
- **Training site**: Trained at the Prometheus data center in Ohio.
- **Strategic shift**: Meta had previously avoided frontier releases of the kind OpenAI and Anthropic make.
