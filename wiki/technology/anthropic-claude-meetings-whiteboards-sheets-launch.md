## Summary
Anthropic launched Claude Meetings, Claude Whiteboards, and Claude Sheets, bringing Claude into video calls and shared workspaces to turn discussions into plans and tasks in real time. It also made Claude Data generally available and added interactive connectors for apps like Adobe, Atlassian, and HubSpot. The launch is part of a race with OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft, and Google to become the AI office suite.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Claude Meetings]]**: Claude joins a video call as a visible participant, drafts plans, maps timelines, answers chat questions, and pulls data from connected apps.
- **[[Claude Whiteboards]]**: a collaborative board where Claude turns team discussion into timelines and deadlines, and can convert it into a doc or slide deck.
- **[[Claude Sheets]]**: collaboratively edited spreadsheets with multiple tabs, formulas, formatting, and dropdowns, with Claude's cell edits outlined.
- **[[Claude Data]]**: formerly Claude Dashboards; connects to data warehouses or CRMs and answers with dashboards, data stories, or narrated videos.
- **Interactive connectors**: open full apps such as Adobe, Atlassian, and HubSpot inside Claude.
- **[[Anthropic]]** vs. [[OpenAI]], [[Meta]], [[Microsoft]], and [[Google]] in [[AI Productivity Suites]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Real-Time Work**: Claude starts tasks while participants are still talking in the meeting.
- **Post-Call Output**: Notes, action items, and created materials appear in claude.ai.
- **Guest Safeguards**: Claude leaves automatically when an external guest joins, unless an admin authorizes it.
- **Availability**: Meetings in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, initially Google Meet only.
- **Whiteboards and Sheets**: Beta on paid plans across web, desktop, and mobile.
- **Claude Data**: Now generally available, renamed from Claude Dashboards.
- **Competition**: Follows OpenAI's workplace tools weeks earlier and Meta's Muse agent.
