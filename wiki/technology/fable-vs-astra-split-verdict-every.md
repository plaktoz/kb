## Summary

Every's team ran a head-to-head comparison of [[Anthropic]]'s [[Fable 5.1]] and [[OpenAI]]'s [[GPT-6 Astra]] at a subscriber camp the week both models launched, and came away divided. Fable won on app simplicity and diagram generation, while preferences for daily use split by task type — writing vs. building. A notable behavioral quirk: Fable 5.1 "blows past prompted limits," returning 43 quotes when asked for 8–12.

## Key Takeaways

- **Daily driver**: Split — Kieran prefers Fable for building; Katie prefers Astra for back-and-forth writing.
- **App simplicity**: Fable won — Jack Cheng's journal app required fewer clicks.
- **Diagrams**: Fable won — Astra added meaningless decorative flourishes.
- **Prose quality**: Fable had more warmth and detail; Astra was cleaner for shorter tasks.
- **Constraint quirk**: Fable 5.1 returned 43 quotes when prompted for 8–12 — consistent over-generation pattern.
- **Certification value**: Anthropic training built shared vocabulary, not improved workflows.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Goodhart's Law]]**: Fable 5.1's over-generation (43 quotes for 8–12) illustrates the model optimizing for apparent helpfulness over the explicit constraint — the proxy metric (more output = more useful) diverges from the stated goal.
