## Summary

[[Meta]] launched Spaces, topic-based breakout conversations in the sidebar where its [[Muse Agents]] work toward a goal on the user's behalf. Users describe an idea in chat and Muse builds a tracker, planner or project board that updates itself via connected third-party apps. It is the second new Muse feature in a week, following Muse Voice.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Meta Spaces]]**: Sidebar "custom experiences" focused on one project, such as a vacation, wedding planner or DIY project.
- **[[Muse Agents]]**: Meta's cartoonish agentic helpers; 10 million users in the U.S. and Canada by the October earnings call.
- **[[Muse Voice]]**: Announced Thursday; lets users video chat with the agent and give verbal commands.
- **[[Mark Zuckerberg]]**: Posted that he built a BBQ game, event tracker and home control dashboard in Spaces.
- **[[AI Agent Competition]]**: Rivals include OpenAI's dots and SpaceXAI's Grokbot, also with cartoonish personas.
- **[[Third-Party App Integration]]**: Connected apps keep spaces current in the background without user prompts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Spaces Concept**: Per-project agent conversations that live in the sidebar and persist.
- **Generative Building**: Describe an idea in chat and Muse builds the tracker or planner.
- **Background Updates**: Connected apps surface concert dates, price changes and savings progress unprompted.
- **Adoption**: 10 million U.S. and Canada agent users less than two months after debut.
- **Release Cadence**: Spaces follows Muse Voice within a week.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What are Meta's Spaces?
**A**: Spaces are topic-focused breakout conversations, which Meta calls "custom experiences", that live in the sidebar of the Muse agent interface. Muse works toward a particular goal for the user inside each one.

**Q2**: How do Spaces stay current without user prompts?
**A**: Users connect third-party apps, and the spaces keep themselves up to date in the background. New concert dates, price changes or savings progress show up without the user asking.

**Q3**: How does Spaces fit into Meta's broader Muse strategy?
**A**: It is the second new Muse feature in a week, after Muse Voice, which shows Meta building out the user experience around its agents. It also positions Muse against rival cartoonish agents such as OpenAI's dots and SpaceXAI's Grokbot.
