## Summary

A Columbia University study found adults who lost 90 minutes of sleep nightly for six weeks gained about one pound on average versus periods of adequate rest, with no significant change in diet or exercise tracked. An OB-GYN not involved in the study warned that women in menopause may be especially vulnerable, since sleep disruption often coincides with slowing metabolism at that life stage.
