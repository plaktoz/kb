## Summary

The FDA approved centanafadine (Simtriyo), the first nonstimulant triple reuptake inhibitor (norepinephrine, dopamine, serotonin) for ADHD in patients 6 and older, based on four phase 3 trials showing significant symptom and executive-function improvements. The drug has low reported potential for dependence and abuse, with decreased appetite and headache the most common side effects in adults.

## Core Concepts

- **[[ADHD]]** — attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, the treatment target
- **[[Centanafadine]]** — first-in-class NDSRI, brand name Simtriyo
- **[[Otsuka Pharmaceutical]]** — developer of centanafadine
- **[[FDA]]** — approving body, based on four phase 3 trials
- **[[Triple Reuptake Inhibitor]]** — mechanism of action (norepinephrine, dopamine, serotonin)

## Key Takeaways

- **First-in-class nonstimulant NDSRI** approved for ADHD ages 6+.
- **Improved executive function**: time management, planning, working memory.
- **Low reported dependence and abuse potential** versus stimulant alternatives.
- **Common side effects**: decreased appetite and headache in adults.
