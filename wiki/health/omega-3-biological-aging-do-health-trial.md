## Summary
The DO-HEALTH RCT (n=~2,000, age 70+, 5 European countries, 3-year follow-up) found that 1g/day algae-based omega-3 supplementation made participants biologically ~3 months younger per epigenetic clock measurements. The triple combination of omega-3 + vitamin D + exercise showed the strongest effect on cellular aging — consistent with the hypothesis that anti-inflammatory interventions compound when combined. Effect sizes are modest per year but meaningful as a long-term compound protocol.

## Core Concepts

- [[DO-HEALTH Trial]] — multi-center RCT; 2x2x2 factorial design testing omega-3, vitamin D3, and home exercise in adults 70+
- [[Epigenetic Clocks]] — DNA methylation-based biological age measurements; validated predictors of all-cause mortality and functional decline
- [[Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)]] — polyunsaturated fatty acids; anti-inflammatory; 1g/day algae-based was the studied dose
- [[Inflammaging]] — chronic low-grade inflammation as a primary driver of biological aging; omega-3 targets this mechanism
- [[Biological Age vs Chronological Age]] — epigenetic clocks measure the rate of cellular aging; can diverge from calendar age
- [[Triple Combination Protocol]] — omega-3 + vitamin D3 + exercise showed greater biological age benefit than any single intervention

## Key Takeaways

- **Primary finding**: 1g/day omega-3 for 3 years → biologically ~3 months younger per epigenetic clocks
- **Strongest effect**: Triple combination (omega-3 + vitamin D + exercise) outperforms individual interventions
- **Mechanism**: Omega-3 EPA/DHA reduces systemic inflammation ("inflammaging") — a primary driver of accelerated biological aging
- **Preferred form**: Algae-based omega-3 (EPA+DHA) for sustainability and purity vs. fish oil
- **Dose studied**: 1g/day
- **Effect size**: Modest (3 months over 3 years) but consistent with long-term compounding
- **Practical protocol**: Omega-3 + Vitamin D + regular exercise is the evidence-backed combination for cellular longevity

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Compounding (Biology)]]**: 3 months of biological age reduction over 3 years sounds modest — but compounded across decades of a consistent protocol, the divergence between treated and untreated biological age grows nonlinearly, especially when combined with exercise and vitamin D.
- **[[Anti-Inflammatory as Aging Lever]]**: Inflammaging — chronic low-grade inflammation — is one of the most tractable first-principles targets for slowing biological aging. Omega-3's mechanism (reducing pro-inflammatory eicosanoids) directly addresses this root cause rather than downstream symptoms.
