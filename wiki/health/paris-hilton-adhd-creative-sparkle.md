## Summary

Paris Hilton publicly shared her adult ADHD diagnosis, describing it as a relief that explained years of misunderstood behavior and school struggles. She uses JORNAY PM, a night-taken stimulant that activates roughly 10 hours later, to manage morning symptoms and be more present as a mother and entrepreneur. Hilton advocates reframing ADHD as a source of creativity rather than a deficit.

## Core Concepts

- **[[ADHD]] (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder)**: A neurodevelopmental condition affecting focus, impulse control, and executive function; often misdiagnosed or undiagnosed in adults.
- **[[JORNAY PM]]**: An extended-release stimulant taken at bedtime that activates ~10 hours later, designed to address morning symptom spikes common in ADHD.
- **[[Neurodiversity]]**: The framework of viewing neurological differences such as ADHD as natural variation rather than deficits, emphasizing strengths.
- **[[Cognitive Behavioral Therapy]] (CBT)**: A therapeutic approach that complements medication by training executive functioning, organization, and time-management skills.
- **[[Paris Hilton]]**: Entrepreneur and media personality who became a public advocate for adult ADHD awareness through her partnership with Collegium Pharmaceutical.

## Key Takeaways

- **Late Diagnosis Relief**: Hilton called her ADHD diagnosis "a huge relief" after years of punishment for attention struggles.
- **Night-Time Stimulant**: JORNAY PM is taken at night and activates ~10 hours later, targeting morning symptom peaks.
- **Medication + Therapy**: Extended-release stimulants paired with CBT offer more consistent symptom management throughout the day.
- **Creativity Framing**: Hilton credits ADHD with fueling her creativity and entrepreneurial innovation — her "creative sparkle."
- **Advocacy Message**: "There's nothing wrong with you, [it's] just the way that your brain works."

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Reframing]]**: Hilton's approach treats ADHD not as a deficit to overcome but as a trait to leverage — a classic reframe that shifts identity from broken to differently wired, unlocking engagement with strengths rather than fixation on limitations.
