## Summary

A study presented at ADLM 2026 found that a highly sensitive blood test measuring the ESAT-6 protein — secreted directly by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* — can accurately distinguish active pulmonary TB from latent infection and other lung diseases. The test addresses a critical gap where sputum-based diagnostics fail, particularly in patients with early or low-burden disease. Validation in a prospective real-world study is underway before clinical adoption.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Tuberculosis]]** — caused by *[[Mycobacterium tuberculosis]]*; infects ~25% of the global population, with 5–10% progressing to active disease
- **[[ESAT-6]] (6-kDa Early Secreted Antigenic Target)** — a protein biomarker secreted directly by TB bacteria, measured in blood to detect infection stage
- **[[Biosensor]]** — novel high-sensitivity sensor used to quantify circulating ESAT-6 at levels undetectable by prior instruments
- **[[Latent TB Infection]]** — asymptomatic bacterial presence; distinguishable from active TB by ESAT-6 concentration gradient
- **[[ADLM 2026]]** — Association for Diagnostics & Laboratory Medicine annual meeting where findings were presented
- **[[Sputum Culture]]** — current gold standard for TB diagnosis; limited by patients' inability to produce sputum in early disease

## Key Takeaways

- **Global burden**: TB is the top global infectious-disease killer; 25% of the world infected.
- **Diagnostic gap**: Many patients cannot produce sputum, especially in early-stage disease.
- **ESAT-6 gradient**: Blood levels increase stepwise — uninfected < latent TB < active TB.
- **Bacterial origin**: ESAT-6 is pathogen-derived, not immune-response-based — explains high specificity.
- **Study size**: 217 patients across six disease/exposure categories evaluated.
- **Outperforms competitors**: ESAT-6 beat two existing blood-based TB biomarkers in discrimination.
- **Next step**: Prospective study planned to validate real-time predictive performance.
- **Triage role**: Positioned as a practical triage tool when sputum diagnostics are unavailable.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Signal vs. Noise]]**: ESAT-6's direct bacterial origin eliminates immune-response variability — measuring the pathogen itself rather than the host's reaction improves the signal-to-noise ratio in diagnosis.
