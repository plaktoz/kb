## Summary
Compass Pathways has completed its New Drug Application to the FDA for COMP360, a synthetic psilocybin formulation for treatment-resistant depression (TRD). If approved, it would be the first classic psychedelic medicine approved by the FDA. A National Priority Voucher sets a 1- to 2-month review target, with launch expected in the first half of 2027.

## Core Concepts
- [[Compass Pathways]]: the company, led by CEO [[Kabir Nath]], that submitted the NDA.
- [[COMP360 Psilocybin]]: investigational synthetic, proprietary psilocybin formulation.
- [[Treatment-Resistant Depression]]: depression that has not responded to available treatments.
- [[New Drug Application]]: the formal FDA submission seeking approval.
- [[FDA National Priority Voucher]]: grants accelerated review with a 1- to 2-month target.
- [[Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy]]: responses were anchored by psychotherapy and trained therapist supervision.
- [[MADRS]]: the depression scale used to define clinically meaningful response.

## Key Takeaways
- **First of Its Class**: Approval would make COMP360 the first FDA-approved classic psychedelic medicine.
- **Trial Base**: NDA rests on two positive phase 3 trials, COMP005 and COMP006, with over 1,000 participants.
- **COMP006 Result**: 39% of the 25 mg arm had at least 25% MADRS reduction by week 6.
- **Durability**: Response was maintained through at least week 26.
- **Second Dose**: COMP005's single dose gave 25%; two doses in COMP006 gave 39%.
- **Chronic Population**: Participants averaged over 3 years in the current episode and 6+ lifetime episodes.
- **Therapy Required**: An investigator stressed responses needed psychotherapy and trained therapist supervision.
- **Fast Review**: Priority voucher sets a 1- to 2-month review target.
- **Launch Timeline**: Expected launch in the first half of 2027 if approved.
