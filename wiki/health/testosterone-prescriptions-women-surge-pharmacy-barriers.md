## Summary

Testosterone prescriptions for females rose 146% between January 2023 and July 2026, yet no FDA-approved female-specific testosterone products exist, creating dosing and pharmacy access complications. The FDA responded in September 2026 by calling for more data and urging manufacturers to pursue female-specific studies. The [[Menopause Society]] endorses testosterone only for [[Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder]] (HSDD) in postmenopausal women, cautioning against off-label use for fatigue, mood, or muscle mass.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Testosterone Therapy]] for women** — 146% prescription increase (Jan 2023–Jul 2026) per Truveta/Reuters analysis
- **[[Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder]] (HSDD)** — affects ~1 in 8 females aged 45–65; the only FDA-endorsed indication for female testosterone use
- **[[Menopause]] care gap** — no FDA-approved female-specific testosterone product forces use of male-formulated products at reduced doses
- **[[FDA]] regulatory response** — September 17, 2026 call for more data; encouragement of female-specific clinical trials
- **[[Avivo Bio]]** — company developing a female-specific testosterone therapy and studying breast tissue effects
- **[[Off-label Prescribing]]** — using male-formulated testosterone at lower doses for women due to absence of approved alternatives

## Key Takeaways

- **Prescription surge**: Female testosterone prescriptions up 146% from Jan 2023 to Jul 2026.
- **No approved product**: No FDA-approved testosterone therapy exists specifically for females.
- **Only endorsed use**: [[Menopause Society]] supports testosterone only for HSDD in postmenopausal women.
- **Dosing risk**: Male-formulated products prescribed at lower doses create pharmacy fulfillment issues.
- **Evidence gaps**: Evidence does not support testosterone for fatigue, bone density, muscle, or mood.
- **Normal range guidance**: Hormone levels should stay within premenopausal female normal range.
- **Research pipeline**: 30+ trials currently studying testosterone for various conditions in females.
- **Advocacy shift**: Clinicians urging testosterone to be treated as a standard part of menopause care.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Supply-Demand Mismatch]]**: Rising clinical demand (146% prescription surge) without a corresponding approved supply creates systemic access failure — pharmacies reject prescriptions because male-formulated products don't map cleanly to female dosing needs.
- **[[Regulatory Lag]]**: The FDA's approval pipeline lags real-world clinical adoption, leaving practitioners relying on off-label workarounds for years while evidence accumulates.

**Q2**: What is HSDD and why is it relevant here?
