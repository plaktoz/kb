## Summary

The FDA withdrew publication of studies tracking the safety of COVID-19 and shingles vaccines, with HHS claiming the authors drew conclusions unsupported by the underlying data. The move fits a broader pattern under HHS Secretary [[Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] of reshaping U.S. vaccine policy, including removing all 17 members of the CDC's vaccine advisory committee and canceling mRNA vaccine funding. Critics, including a former CDC official, say HHS is now blocking high-quality taxpayer-funded science that contradicts its anti-vaccine narrative.

## Core Concepts

- **[[FDA]]**: Withdrew publication of the COVID-19 and shingles vaccine safety studies at HHS's direction.
- **[[HHS]]**: Said the withdrawn studies drew "broad conclusions... not supported by the underlying data."
- **[[Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]**: HHS Secretary who has repeatedly cast doubt on vaccine safety and efficacy, including calling the COVID-19 vaccine "the deadliest vaccine ever made."
- **[[CDC]]**: Subject of Kennedy's claim that it approved COVID-19 vaccines "without any scientific basis."
- **[[Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]]**: Kennedy removed all 17 members and replaced them with vaccine-skeptic appointees; a federal judge later stayed their votes.
- **[[mRNA Vaccine]]**: Kennedy canceled at least $500 million in federally funded mRNA vaccine development in August.
- **[[Childhood Vaccine Schedule]]**: A federal judge temporarily blocked Kennedy's reduction of recommended childhood shots from 17 to 11.

## Key Takeaways

- **Studies Withdrawn**: FDA pulled COVID-19 and shingles vaccine safety research from publication.
- **HHS Justification**: Claimed authors' conclusions weren't supported by underlying data.
- **Evidence Base Intact**: COVID vaccines remain among the most studied, benefits outweigh risks.
- **Shingles Vaccine Cleared**: CDC states it is "not associated with serious adverse events."
- **Critic's Warning**: Ex-CDC official calls it a "pattern of blocking high-quality studies."
- **ACIP Overhaul**: All 17 advisory committee members replaced with skeptic-leaning appointees.
- **Judicial Pushback**: Federal judge stayed votes and blocked schedule changes made by new ACIP.
- **Funding Cuts**: $500 million in mRNA vaccine development funding canceled in August.

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Motivated Reasoning]]**: Kennedy's history of asserting vaccine harm before withdrawing studies that contradict it shows conclusions fixed first, evidence filtered second.
- **[[Institutional Capture]]**: Controlling appointments to agencies like the FDA and ACIP lets a single actor reshape official "scientific consensus" without new data emerging.
