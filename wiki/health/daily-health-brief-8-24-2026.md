## Summary

A daily wellness brief flags that a small fortified-orange-juice supplementation trial didn't hold up under continuous monitoring, using it as a reminder to weigh short-term small trials cautiously against longer-run, real-world evidence before changing daily habits.

## Core Concepts

- **Fortified food trials**: Short-term supplementation studies (like fortified orange juice) can show effects that don't persist under sustained, continuous monitoring.
- **Evidence hierarchy caution**: The brief implicitly argues for weighting longer-duration, real-world evidence over single small trials when deciding on habit changes.

## Key Takeaways

- **Core finding**: A fortified orange juice supplementation study didn't hold up when monitored continuously over a longer period.
- **Practical lesson**: Single small nutrition trials should be weighed cautiously against longer-run evidence before prompting a change in daily habits.
