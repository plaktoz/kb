# FDA Clears PrecivityAD2 and Elecsys pTau217 Alzheimer's Blood Tests

## Summary

The FDA cleared two new blood tests in August 2026 to help clinicians assess Alzheimer's-related brain changes, bringing the total number of FDA-cleared Alzheimer's blood tests to four. The PrecivityAD2 (C2N Diagnostics) is notable as the first FDA-cleared test indicated for symptomatic adults as young as 40, while the Elecsys pTau217 (Roche/Eli Lilly) targets adults 55 and older. Neither test is a standalone diagnostic — both are designed to inform comprehensive clinical evaluation.

## Core Concepts

- **[[PrecivityAD2]]**: First FDA-cleared [[Alzheimer's Disease]] blood test for adults 40+; measures amyloid-beta 42/40 ratio and p-tau217/np-tau217 ratio, producing an Amyloid Probability Score 2 (APS2).
- **[[Amyloid Probability Score 2 (APS2)]]**: A composite score combining two biomarker ratios in the PrecivityAD2 test; validated against PET scans and CSF biomarkers.
- **[[C2N Diagnostics]]**: Developer of PrecivityAD2; the FDA-cleared version was expected to launch in late 2026.
- **[[Elecsys pTau217]]**: [[Roche]]/[[Eli Lilly]] test measuring a single biomarker ([[pTau217]]) for symptomatic adults 55+; runs on Roche's existing cobas infrastructure already in 4,500+ U.S. labs.
- **[[Blood-Based Biomarker Testing]]**: Growing category of minimally invasive tests replacing or supplementing PET scans and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) analysis for Alzheimer's screening.
- **[[Amyloid Pathology]]**: The hallmark brain change detected by these tests — buildup of amyloid plaques historically requiring expensive imaging or lumbar puncture to confirm.

## Key Takeaways

- **Younger cohort**: PrecivityAD2 extends eligibility to adults 40+, a first among FDA-cleared Alzheimer's tests.
- **Strong accuracy**: PrecivityAD2 showed 97.6% positive predictive value and 93.1% negative predictive value vs. PET/CSF in 1,142-person validation study.
- **Broad infrastructure**: Elecsys pTau217 runs on 4,500+ already-installed cobas instruments across U.S. labs, enabling rapid rollout.
- **Agreement rate**: Elecsys pTau217 showed ~88–91% agreement with amyloid PET results in a 2,100+ participant study.
- **Not diagnostic alone**: Both tests complement — not replace — neurological exams, cognitive testing, and imaging.
- **High-stakes results**: Positive findings "can have major implications for work, family, finances, and emotional well-being."
- **Four tests total**: These join two earlier approvals (Lumipulse G pTau217/ß-Amyloid 1-42 Plasma Ratio in May 2025; Elecsys pTau181 in October 2025).

## 🧠 First Principles & Mental Models

- **[[Triage Heuristic]]**: The Elecsys test enables meaningful biomarker assessment in primary care before specialist referral — applying the principle of filtering out clear negatives early to focus scarce specialist capacity on ambiguous or positive cases.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the key distinction of the PrecivityAD2 test among FDA-cleared Alzheimer's tests?
**A**: It is the first FDA-cleared Alzheimer's blood test indicated for symptomatic adults as young as 40, expanding access to earlier-career patients who may face cognitive symptoms.

**Q2**: What two biomarker ratios does PrecivityAD2 measure, and how does it report results?
**A**: It measures the amyloid-beta 42/40 ratio and the p-tau217/np-tau217 ratio, combining them into a single composite Amyloid Probability Score 2 (APS2) validated against PET scans and CSF biomarkers.

**Q3**: How do these blood tests change Alzheimer's clinical workflows in practice?
**A**: They allow physicians to perform minimally invasive biomarker triage in primary care — reducing reliance on costly PET scans and lumbar punctures — and help identify patients who need specialist referral or confirmatory testing sooner.
