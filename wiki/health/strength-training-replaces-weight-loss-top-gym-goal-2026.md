## Summary

A 2026 Life Time wellness survey finds strength training has overtaken weight loss as Americans' top fitness priority (42.3%), driven by GLP-1 medications changing how people lose weight and a broader cultural shift toward longevity and functional capability over aesthetics.

## Core Concepts

- [[Functional Strength]] — training for real-world capability (lifting, carrying, mobility) rather than aesthetic goals; framed as central to independence with age.
- [[GLP-1 Fitness Shift]] — as GLP-1 drugs handle weight loss pharmacologically, gym culture is reorienting around muscle, mobility and metabolic health instead.
- [[Consistency Over Intensity]] — trainers consistently emphasize regular, manageable sessions over sporadic high-intensity workouts that lead to burnout.

## Key Takeaways

- **Top priority shift**: 42.3% now name getting stronger as their top health goal, ahead of weight loss.
- **Longevity framing**: 82% of respondents plan to prioritize overall wellbeing in 2026, up 7 points year-over-year.
- **Sleep tradeoff**: 69% would choose 8 hours of sleep over unlimited snacks without weight gain.
- **Age-related decline**: Without resistance training, adults lose muscle and bone density each decade, raising injury risk and limiting independence.
- **Tech adoption**: Over a third of respondents already use AI tools for workouts or nutrition; wearables are a top predicted 2026 trend.
