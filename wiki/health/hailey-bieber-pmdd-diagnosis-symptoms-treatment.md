## Summary
Hailey Bieber disclosed her PMDD diagnosis and announced a $5 million, three-year partnership with the Foundation for Women's Health to address the lack of research and awareness. The article explains that PMDD is a severe, cycle-linked form of premenstrual disorder affecting about 1.6% of women and girls worldwide. Diagnosis requires symptom tracking, and treatment spans SSRIs, a birth control pill, CBT and lifestyle changes.

## Core Concepts
- [[Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD)]]: severe physical and psychological symptoms in the days or weeks before menstruation, distinct from [[Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)]] by severity and interference with daily functioning.
- [[Hormonal Fluctuation]]: PMDD stems from sensitivity to cyclic hormone and neurotransmitter changes; postpartum, weaning and [[Perimenopause]] can trigger it.
- [[Hailey Bieber]]: Rhode Beauty founder; struggled with PMDD after giving birth and is funding research via the [[Foundation for Women's Health]].
- [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs)]]: sertraline, fluoxetine and paroxetine are FDA-approved for PMDD.
- [[Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)]]: a non-drug option for managing symptoms.
- [[Women's Health Research Gap]]: Bieber cites a lack of research, awareness and conversation.

## Key Takeaways
- **Prevalence**: About 1.6% of women and girls (about 31 million) meet strict criteria; 3.2% have provisional diagnoses.
- **Distinguishing Feature**: Severity and timing separate PMDD from PMS: worse before a period, gone after.
- **Diagnosis**: Track symptoms over at least two cycles; at least five symptoms, one mood-related.
- **Risk Factors**: Personal or family history of depression, anxiety or PMDD, plus trauma or chronic stress.
- **Medication**: Three SSRIs and the drospirenone/ethinyl estradiol pill (Yaz) are FDA-approved.
- **Lifestyle**: Consistent sleep, whole-foods diet, less sugar and caffeine, exercise, no smoking.
- **When to See a Doctor**: When symptoms interfere with work, school or relationships.
- **Advocacy**: Bieber's $5M partnership targets the women's health research gap.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What distinguishes PMDD from ordinary PMS?
**A**: Severity and functional impact: PMDD symptoms significantly interfere with daily life. They also follow a clear timing pattern, worsening a week or two before a period and fading within days of its start.

**Q2**: How is PMDD diagnosed, and how common is it?
**A**: Clinicians review history and ask patients to track symptoms over at least two cycles, requiring at least five symptoms including one mood-related. About 1.6% of women and girls meet the criteria, roughly 31 million people.

**Q3**: What management options does the article list for someone with severe cycle-linked symptoms?
**A**: See a doctor if symptoms disrupt work, school or relationships. Options include FDA-approved SSRIs or the Yaz pill, CBT, and lifestyle changes such as consistent sleep, a whole-foods diet and exercise.
