## Summary
The [[NAIS]] (National Association of Independent Schools) 2026 report argues that AI has already moved to the center of K–12 education, so the question for school leaders is no longer whether AI will enter their communities but how schools evolve in an increasingly AI-driven environment. Because adoption has outpaced understanding of AI's effects on students, the report aims to help leaders act on evidence rather than anecdote, balancing caution with optimism. It combines multi-year snapshot survey data from teachers, department heads, and division heads with in-depth interviews of teachers and school leaders nationwide.

## Core Concepts
- **[[AI in Education]]**: AI is now a routine feature of academic life for students and teachers, regardless of a school's stated position.
- **Adoption–evidence gap**: The pace of adoption has outrun the field's understanding of AI's effects on students; early evidence urges caution alongside optimism.
- **Mixed-method design**: Multi-year [[NAIS]] snapshot surveys plus qualitative interviews across the country.
- **Survey dimensions**: Policy formalization, adoption differences across roles, training development, and evolving perceptions.
- **Interview themes**: How and why schools make choices, from [[Developmental Calibration]] to [[Assessment Redesign]].
- **Report sections**: Snapshot Survey Analysis, Interview Themes, [[AI Policy]] Exemplars, Methodology, plus a Quick View summary.
- Related: [[ai-us-schools-learning-evidence-student-privacy]], [[ibm-k12-ai-readiness-gap-study-2026]], [[should-schools-use-ai-tools-student-learning]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Already arrived**: The question is how, not whether, AI enters schools.
- **Evidence lag**: Adoption speed exceeds understanding of AI's effects on students.
- **Balanced stance**: Neither dismisses AI's risks nor ignores its possibilities.
- **Policies formalizing**: Survey tracks how school AI policies have become more formal.
- **Role differences**: Adoption varies across teachers, department heads, and division heads.
- **Design choices**: Schools calibrate AI use developmentally and redesign assessments.
- **Source limit**: Scraped page is an overview; detailed figures are not included.
