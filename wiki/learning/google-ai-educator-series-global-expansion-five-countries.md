## Summary

[[Google]] is expanding its free, on-demand [[Google AI Educator Series]] (GES) to Spain, Brazil, Mexico, Australia, and sub-Saharan Africa, after earlier launches in the U.S., India, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines, and the U.K. and Ireland. The post highlights how teachers in each market use [[Gemini]] to save time and make lessons engaging. It also announces new November modules on assessments, game-based learning, and stronger assignments.

## Core Concepts

- [[Google AI Educator Series]]: no-cost, on-demand training giving educators practical AI skills; content updated monthly from educator feedback.
- [[Gemini]] and [[Gemini Notebook]]: tools teachers use to build comics, podcasts, infographics, lesson plans, and assessments.
- [[Train-the-Trainer]]: bootcamps and workshops with local partners to scale training in Malaysia and the U.K.
- [[Government Partnerships]]: Philippine education bodies, [[Seoul National University of Education]], and aligned national curricula and frameworks.
- [[AI Literacy]]: November modules teach data accuracy, model performance, and human oversight.

## Key Takeaways

- **New Markets**: Spain, Brazil, Mexico, Australia, and sub-Saharan Africa are coming.
- **India**: September sessions trained 80,000 educators; modules in English and six local languages.
- **Philippines**: Partnership targets over one million educators; national memorandum endorses GES.
- **Malaysia**: Teachers report saving three to five hours of prep per topic.
- **UK**: All 650,000 teachers can join virtual or in-person training.
- **Korea**: Aligned with the 2022 Revised Curriculum and teacher AI competency framework.
- **November Modules**: Interactive assessments, game-based learning, and stronger academic assignments.
- **Critical Thinking**: One module helps spot questions that invite shortcuts.
