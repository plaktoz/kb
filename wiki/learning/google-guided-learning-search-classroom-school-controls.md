## Summary
Google is building Guided Learning, a Gemini study tool built around "productive struggle", into Google Search and Google Classroom. It is also changing school controls so that Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom, and Search AI are off for K-12 student accounts until administrators turn them on.

## Core Concepts
- [[Guided Learning]]: a Gemini study tool that coaches students through problems with open-ended questions and step-by-step breakdowns.
- [[Productive Struggle]]: students are challenged without being so overwhelmed that they give up.
- [[Google Search]] for student accounts: when it detects homework help, it walks the student through the problem and does not give the answer directly.
- [[Google Classroom]]: teacher-assigned Guided Learning activities act as a 1-1 study tool anchored in class material, with real-time insights for teachers.
- [[School Controls]]: administrator opt-in for AI products on K-12 student accounts.
- [[Gemini]], [[Gemini Notebook]]: the AI products covered by the new default-off policy.

## Key Takeaways
- **Design Philosophy**: AI products should support [[Productive Struggle]] and not shortcut straight to answers.
- **Search Pilot**: Guided Learning in Search starts with a pilot in select U.S. schools this month.
- **Classroom Rollout**: Guided Learning is globally available in Google Classroom starting today.
- **Teacher Insight**: Real-time insights show teachers where the class needs extra support.
- **Default Off**: Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Search AI are off for K-12 students until admins enable them.
- **Educator Input**: Google says it tested with schools and educators before launching in Classroom.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Learning needs calibrated effort** — Students learn when they are actively challenged but not overwhelmed, so a tool that hands over answers removes the effort that produces learning. · *model:* [[Desirable Difficulty]]

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What philosophy underlies Google's Guided Learning?
**A**: AI products should support "productive struggle", which challenges students actively without overwhelming them so they give up. Guided Learning coaches with open-ended questions and step-by-step breakdowns.

**Q2**: How will Guided Learning in Google Search behave for students?
**A**: When Search detects a student seeking certain homework help, it walks through the topic or problem step by step, with prompts to think critically instead of giving the answer directly. It starts as a pilot in select U.S. schools.

**Q3**: What is changing about school controls, and what does it mean for administrators?
**A**: Access to Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search AI will be off for K-12 student accounts until administrators explicitly turn them on. Schools get time to prepare before the change rolls out.
