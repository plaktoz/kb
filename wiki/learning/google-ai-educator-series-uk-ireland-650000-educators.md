## Summary

[[Google]] is expanding its free [[Google AI Educator Series]] to around 650,000 educators across the UK and Ireland, covering primary, secondary and higher education with short practical modules built around [[Gemini]]. The program aims to build practical [[AI Literacy]] and confidence for lesson planning, adapting materials, student support and administrative work, following earlier rollouts to six million US educators and extensions to India and Korea. It builds on Google's UK "AI Works" pilot with multi-academy trusts and is paired with an [[AI Policy Toolkit]] for school governors developed with the [[National Governance Association]].

## Core Concepts

- **[[Google AI Educator Series]]**: Free training delivered via teacher-led virtual/in-person events or self-paced sessions in the [[Google Learning Centre]]; modules run 15–45 minutes and award [[Micro-credentials]] and badges.
- **Learning pathway**: Foundational [[AI Literacy]] → teaching and learning → reducing administrative workload, plus [[Inclusive Design]], student support, [[Agentic Workflows]] and institutional use.
- **Educator-developed curriculum**: Designed by educators so teachers can apply skills immediately without lengthy technical training.
- **[[Public First]] research**: Found 74% of UK education-sector roles have potential to be enhanced by AI — a potential, not measured, productivity figure.
- **[[AI Works Pilot]]**: Earlier pilot with [[Lift Schools]] and [[LEO Academy Trust]] — 150 staff from 14 schools in hands-on workshops and one-to-one training, testing how confidence and trust affect AI adoption.
- **Augment, not automate**: [[James Browning]] (COO, Lift Schools) framed AI as assisting teachers with resource creation and planning while teachers continue to interpret, inspire and guide.
- **Governance support**: An [[AI Policy Toolkit]] with the [[National Governance Association]] helps governors and trustees decide on AI policy, tools and training.
- **Policy alignment**: Completions count toward the UK government's goal of training 10 million workers in digital skills by 2030.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: Roughly 650,000 UK and Ireland educators eligible for free training.
- **Format**: 15–45 minute modules; online, facilitated events, or self-paced.
- **Credentials**: Completers earn micro-credentials and badges.
- **Prior rollout**: Six million US educators first, then India and Korea.
- **Practical focus**: Lesson plans, differentiated materials, student support, admin streamlining.
- **Pilot evidence base**: 150 staff across 14 schools in the AI Works pilot.
- **Headline stat caveat**: 74% figure reflects potential AI enhancement, not measured gains.
- **National target**: Feeds UK goal of 10 million digitally skilled workers by 2030.
- **Distribution channel**: Google for Education Gemini Roadshow hosts upcoming UK/Ireland events.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Google's AI Educator Series expansion and who does it target?
**A**: It is free AI training for about 650,000 primary, secondary and higher education educators in the UK and Ireland, designed to build practical AI literacy and confidence with tools like Gemini.

**Q2**: What did the AI Works pilot involve, and what was it testing?
**A**: It involved 150 education professionals from 14 schools across Lift Schools and LEO Academy Trust in workshops and one-to-one training, testing whether greater AI confidence helps staff use AI for lesson planning, adapting materials and admin.

**Q3**: How should the 74% Public First statistic be interpreted when evaluating the program's case?
**A**: It measures the potential for AI to enhance UK education roles, not realized productivity gains, so it supports the rationale for training rather than proving outcomes.
