## Summary
Lynda Gratton analyzes a self-assessment of 550 people across eight "threads" of a working life and finds that people report strong capability but inconsistent renewal. She points to two obstacles, too little space to reflect (Calm) and too little unfamiliar experience (Adventure). Organizations share responsibility for protecting time for reflection and valuing lateral moves.

## Core Concepts
- **Eight threads**: [[Mastery]], [[Knowing]], [[Cooperation]], and [[Amplification]] (how we contribute), plus [[Friendship]], [[Intimacy]], [[Calm]], and [[Adventure]] (what sustains us). Source: [[Lynda Gratton]]'s book *Living the 100-Year Life*.
- **Capability vs. renewal**: Skill and curiosity do not automatically become a consistent practice of renewal.
- **[[Reflection]] and novelty**: Calm gives space to notice what experience teaches; Adventure supplies the unfamiliar.
- **[[AI]] as amplifier**: Using technology to extend thinking and judgment, not just output.
- **Organizational responsibility**: Protecting reflection time and valuing lateral moves.

## Key Takeaways
- **Strong Threads**: Mastery, Knowing, and Amplification were the strongest; 77% experiment with new AI tools.
- **Capability Without Renewal**: 72% develop skills intentionally but not consistently; 39% say growth has slowed.
- **Comfort Zone**: 38% build on what they do well rather than stretch.
- **Reflection Gap**: 37% intend to reflect on values but often do not follow through.
- **Calm Is Weakest**: 18% rank Calm as fragile; many know what sustains energy but do not act.
- **Adventure Needs Renewal**: 73% say Adventure needs renewal, the highest of any thread.
- **Technology Habits**: 74% use technology inconsistently; almost half default to easy uses.
- **Organizational Role**: Protect reflection time, value lateral moves, and expand judgment rather than output.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Capability decays into narrowness without renewal** — Expertise that is only exploited and never stretched stops growing, even while current performance stays high. Renewal needs both space to reflect and exposure to the unfamiliar. · *model:* [[Exploration vs. Exploitation]]
→ explains: Capability Without Renewal, Comfort Zone, Adventure Needs Renewal

**T2. Insight does not become practice without space** — Intention and curiosity convert to action only when there is steadiness and time to notice what experience is teaching. · *model:* [[Deliberate Practice]]
→ explains: Reflection Gap, Calm Is Weakest

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Gratton's central claim about continuous personal growth?
**A**: Many people have strong capability, but renewal is held back by two absences: too little space to reflect (Calm) and too little unfamiliar experience (Adventure).

**Q2**: What data point shows capability is not the same as renewal?
**A**: In the 550-person diagnostic, 72% said they develop skills intentionally but not consistently, and 39% said development had slowed despite good performance. 73% said Adventure needs renewal, the highest of any thread.

**Q3**: How should individuals and organizations apply this?
**A**: Individuals should ask what they are still learning and seek unfamiliar projects or lateral moves. Organizations should protect reflection time, value lateral moves, and use technology to expand judgment rather than just output.
