## Summary
A Lumina Foundation and Gallup survey of 2,500 US hiring decision-makers finds 60% have great or quite a lot of confidence in higher education and 80% would prefer a candidate with a degree even when a role does not require one. Nearly half (48%) expect [[AI]] to make college degrees more important over the next five years, versus 26% who expect them to matter less. Confidence and degree preference rise with organization size and vary by industry.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Lumina Foundation]] and [[Gallup]] employer study**: second annual opt-in online survey, Aug. 5-18, 2026.
- **[[Confidence in Higher Education]]**: 26% great deal, 34% quite a lot among employers; just 38% of US adults in June.
- **[[Degree Preference in Hiring]]**: preference for degree holders independent of role requirements.
- **[[AI and Credential Value]]**: employers' expectations about how AI changes the worth of a degree.
- **[[Organization Size Effects]]**: the smallest firms are the least confident and least likely to require degrees.

## Key Takeaways
- **Confidence**: 60% of employers have great deal or quite a lot of confidence.
- **AI Outlook**: 48% expect AI to make degrees more important; 26% less.
- **Degree Preference**: 80% prefer degree holders, 31% strongly, even when not required.
- **Size Gradient**: Preference runs 62% under 10 employees, 84%+ at 100 or more.
- **Industry Spread**: Information sector leads at 91% preference; leisure and hospitality lowest at 66%.
- **Durable Value**: 76% say a degree will matter as much or more for good jobs.
- **Public Gap**: Employer confidence (60%) far exceeds public confidence (38%).

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the study's central finding about employers and higher education?
**A**: Despite AI uncertainty, most employers retain confidence in higher education (60%) and prefer degree holders (80%), and more expect AI to raise degree value than lower it.

**Q2**: How does degree preference vary by organization size?
**A**: It is lowest at firms with fewer than 10 employees (62%), rises to 78% at 10 to 99 employees, and reaches 84% or higher at 100 or more.

**Q3**: What does the finding imply for the value of a degree in the age of AI?
**A**: For now, hiring decision-makers are not turning away from the degree; 76% say it will be as important as today or more so for getting a good job at their organization.
