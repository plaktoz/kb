# Anthropic AI Fluency Framework — Introduction

**Source:** [Lesson 1: Introduction to AI Fluency (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpGtOfSgR-c)
**Course:** AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Anthropic, Ringling College of Art and Design, University College Cork

## What Is AI Fluency?

AI fluency is our ability to engage with AI systems in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**. It is not primarily about understanding AI as a technology — it is about how humans interact and collaborate with AI systems.

The goal: treat AI not as a glorified spell-checker, but as a **trusted partner for creative and innovative problem-solving**.

## Why a Framework Matters

The AI landscape changes too fast for tactical tips to keep up. Learning the latest prompting tricks risks constant obsolescence. A grounding framework survives model generations and interface changes; tactics don't.

Three gaps this course addresses:
- Organizations adopting AI without clear strategies
- Users frustrated with systems they don't understand
- A widening gap between what's possible and what feels intuitive

## The Four Ds (Overview)

The course structures AI collaboration around four core competencies:

| Competency | Core Question |
|------------|--------------|
| **Delegation** | When should humans do the work, and when should AI? |
| **Description** | How do we communicate clearly with AI systems? |
| **Discernment** | How do we evaluate what AI gives us? |
| **Diligence** | How do we ensure AI interaction is responsible, transparent, and accountable? |

See [[ai-fluency-4d-framework]] for detailed breakdowns of each competency.

## Key Takeaways

- AI fluency is a **durable competency**, not a short-lived skill set tied to specific tools.
- Shifting from "learning about AI" to "learning to think *with* AI" is the central reframe.
- By building these competencies, practitioners are prepared not just for today's AI but for future change.
