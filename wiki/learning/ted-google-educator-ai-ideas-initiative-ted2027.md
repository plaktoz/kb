## Summary

[[TED]] and [[Google]] launched an initiative inviting elementary, secondary and higher-education educators who use [[Artificial Intelligence|AI]] in instruction or curriculum development to propose ideas about AI in teaching and learning, with five selected to speak at TED2027 in San Diego. It starts with a free, self-paced online Idea Workshop (launched Oct. 5), and proposals are due Nov. 15. The initiative frames educators, rather than outsiders, as the people who should lead public conversation about [[AI in Education]].

## Core Concepts

- [[TED Idea Workshop]] — free, hourlong, self-paced professional development with three lessons: identify a compelling topic, draw on professional experience, and organize ideas into a presentation.
- [[Educator Voice in AI Policy]] — the initiative's premise that educators should lead the AI-in-education conversation; [[Logan Smalley]], TED's head of learning and education, calls outsider-led discussion "backwards."
- [[Google AI Educator Series]] — Google educational resources given to workshop completers, along with TED's public speaking course.
- [[Student Voice in AI Decisions]] — at the Bridges conference in July, district leaders and students urged involving students in AI policy rather than top-down approaches.
- [[AI Teacher Training]] — contrast: the July 2025 $23 million [[American Federation of Teachers]], [[Microsoft]], [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthology]] effort to train and certify 400,000 educators by 2030 focuses on skill-building, not idea-sharing.
- [[TED2027]] — five educators will give community-stage talks in March, later published on TED's global platform.

## Key Takeaways

- **Five speakers**: five educators selected to present at TED2027 in San Diego next March.
- **Open eligibility**: K-12 and higher-ed educators using AI in instruction or curriculum.
- **Deadline**: proposals accepted through Nov. 15 for TED's selection team.
- **Free workshop**: hourlong self-paced Idea Workshop launched Oct. 5, three lessons.
- **Completion perks**: TED public speaking course plus Google's AI Educator Series resources.
- **Educator-led framing**: Smalley says outsiders leading AI-in-education talk is "backwards."
- **Different emphasis**: sharing educators' ideas, versus AFT-led $23M training for 400,000 educators.
- **Reach**: TED content was viewed or heard over 1.7 billion times in 2025.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the TED and Google initiative trying to change about the AI-in-education conversation?
**A**: It aims to put educators at the center of the conversation, since, per TED's Logan Smalley, much of it is currently led by people outside the field, which he calls "backwards."

**Q2**: How does the initiative's process work?
**A**: Educators complete a free, self-paced, hourlong Idea Workshop with three lessons, submit proposals by Nov. 15, and five selected educators deliver community-stage talks at TED2027 that are then published on TED's platform.

**Q3**: How does this initiative differ from other educator-AI programs, and why does that matter?
**A**: Unlike the AFT, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthology $23 million program focused on building educators' AI skills, it emphasizes sharing educators' experiences with TED's broad global audience, extending their influence into wider public debate about technology and society.
