## Summary

UNESCO's Institute for Lifelong Learning, with the Global Alliance for Literacy and Huawei, has trained around 10,000 literacy educators worldwide since December 2025 through a multilingual online course on digital competencies, teaching educators to use digital tools, evaluate online content critically, and integrate AI responsibly into instruction.

## Core Concepts

The course reframes **[[Digital Literacy]]** for educators as more than technical tool use — it emphasizes critical evaluation of online information and responsible AI use, positioning digital competence as a form of **[[Educator Empowerment]]** that enables more learner-centered, collaborative teaching.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale**: ~10,000 educators trained across Arabic, English, French, and Spanish since December 2025.
- **Format**: 11 self-paced sessions tied directly to real teaching practice.
- **Reported shift**: Educators report designing more learner-centered lessons using digital tools for collaboration and assessment.
- **Critical framing**: The course stresses critically evaluating online content and using AI "more consciously," not just adopting tools.
