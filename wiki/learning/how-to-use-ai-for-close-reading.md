## Summary

LLMs used as interactive reading companions — asking questions, pursuing tangents, building personalised context — transform close reading from a solitary discipline into a collaborative one. Rachel Thomas documents the fast.ai workflow: PDF-to-Markdown conversion, chapter summary generation for LLM context, question-driven reading, and optional Anki flashcard creation. The key distinction from passive AI summarization is that the human remains the active thinker; the AI extends reach without replacing judgment.

## Key Takeaways

- **Jeremy Howard's rabbit hole**: Four Jack Welch mentees became Boeing CEOs — discovered via LLM-assisted reading of *Incorruptible*.
- **"Absolute best reading experience"** — Howard's assessment; highlights depth possible with interactive reading.
- **Workflow**: PDF → Markdown → chapter summaries → question-driven reading → Anki cards.
- **Grounding is essential**: Hallucination risk managed by keeping LLM anchored to the actual source text.
- **Setup barrier**: Requires coding ability; tooling still rough as of early 2026.
- **Architect's pencils**: Johno Whitaker analogy — small upfront investment transforms the experience.
