## Summary

Free AI fluency courses built by [[Anthropic]] with [[Ringling College of Art and Design]] and [[University College Cork]] have been used by more than 1.1 million people. They rest on the [[4D Framework for AI Fluency]], which teaches decisions about when to use AI, how to evaluate it, and how to take responsibility rather than just prompting. Institutions such as the [[University of Texas at Austin]] are adopting it, though no completion rates or measured outcomes were provided.

## Core Concepts

- **[[4D Framework for AI Fluency]]**: Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence.
- **[[Rick Dakan]] and [[Joseph Feller]]**: Co-developers of the framework and courses.
- **[[AI Fluency Tutor]]**: University of Texas at Austin tutor trained on the framework, assignable through [[Canvas]].
- **[[Claude Academy]]**: Free, openly licensed course hosting alongside Ringling College Online.
- **[[Ringling College of Art and Design]]**: AI Task Force since 2023, an AI certificate, and an annual AI Symposium since 2024.

## Key Takeaways

- **Reach**: More than 1.1 million people have used the course series.
- **Beyond Prompting**: Framework teaches when to use AI, evaluating outputs, and owning results.
- **Four Areas**: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.
- **Start Without AI**: Participants reflect on existing practices and values before introducing AI.
- **UT Austin Adoption**: AI tutor trained on the framework, assigned via Canvas.
- **Caveat**: Figures reflect uptake across courses, not completions or learning outcomes.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Effective AI use depends on human judgment and accountability, not just technique** — Skill with prompts is insufficient; users must decide when AI fits, assess output reliability, and remain responsible for results. The 4D Framework is built on this, covering Delegation, Discernment and Diligence beyond Description.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What does the 4D Framework teach beyond prompting?
**A**: It teaches people to decide when to use AI, evaluate its outputs and take responsibility for the results, organized as Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence.

**Q2**: What caveat applies to the 1.1 million figure?
**A**: It represents uptake across the wider course series, not completions of one course. Ringling College has not provided completion rates or independently measured learning outcomes.

**Q3**: How is the University of Texas at Austin applying the framework?
**A**: It developed an AI Fluency Tutor trained on the 4D Framework that faculty can build into course designs and assign through Canvas, supporting faculty, staff and students outside scheduled sessions.
