## Summary
MapleLMS argues 2026 corporate [[Learning and Development]] is shifting from measuring course completion to measuring real-world capability, with AI evolving from content recommender to full learning "[[Orchestration]]" — deciding when learning should occur and who needs intervention based on live performance signals.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Skills-Based Learning]]**: With ~39% of workforce skills expected to change by 2030, the article argues organizations must replace static job-role-based training with living skill taxonomies and real-time capability mapping.
- **[[Learning in the Flow of Work]]**: Embedding capability-building directly into daily tasks (AI-guided decision frameworks, workflow-based coaching prompts) rather than treating training as a separate activity.
- **[[AI Orchestration vs. Personalization]]**: The article distinguishes earlier AI personalization (recommending courses) from 2026's orchestration model, where AI actively decides intervention timing based on productivity dips, workflow changes, or risk exposure signals.

## Key Takeaways
- **Adoption gap**: 92% of companies are increasing AI investment, but workforce readiness remains the primary bottleneck.
- **Measurement shift**: Static annual skill assessments are being replaced by live performance signals (error reduction, decision speed, productivity recovery).
- **Manager role change**: Managers shift from performance reviewers to real-time "skill enablers" using AI-generated coaching insights.
- **Ecosystem consolidation**: The biggest 2026 shift is platforms becoming integrated ecosystems connecting skills intelligence, manager coaching, performance data, and learning interventions rather than standalone course libraries.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the article's core claim?
**A**: Corporate learning and development in 2026 is shifting from tracking course completion to building continuous, workflow-embedded capability, orchestrated in real time by AI.

**Q2**: What specific data point motivates the shift to skills-based (vs. role-based) learning?
**A**: Nearly 39% of workforce skills are expected to change by 2030, making static job-description-based training obsolete.

**Q3**: How does "AI orchestration" differ from earlier AI-driven personalization in L&D?
**A**: Personalization recommended content to learners; orchestration actively decides *when* learning should happen and *who* needs intervention, triggered by real-time signals like productivity dips or workflow changes.
