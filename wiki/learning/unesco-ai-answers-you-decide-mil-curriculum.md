## Summary
UNESCO launched "AI Answers. You Decide.", a companion guide to its Media and Information Literacy (MIL) curriculum for teaching in AI-mediated information environments. It targets educators, teacher-training institutions and curriculum developers, focusing on how AI shapes information, knowledge, behaviour and society rather than on specific technologies. Its author argues AI changes the environment in which MIL is applied, not MIL's foundations.

## Core Concepts
- [[Media and Information Literacy]]: UNESCO's framework for critically engaging with information, extended here to AI.
- [[UNESCO]]: publisher of the guide, introduced during Global Media and Information Literacy Week 2026 (24-31 October).
- [[Critical Thinking]]: the central skill for questioning AI outputs and making informed choices.
- [[Generative AI]]: can create convincing text, images, audio and video, raising reliability and authenticity questions.
- [[Algorithmic Bias]]: one of the transparency, privacy and power issues the guide examines.
- [[Informed Trust]]: ability to evaluate AI-generated information.
- [[Anne Leppäjärvi]]: author of the guide.
- [[Mariya Gabriel]]: UNESCO Assistant-Director General for Communication and Information.

## Key Takeaways
- **Companion Guide**: Builds on the 2021 MIL curriculum, piloted and adapted in over 40 countries.
- **Expert Review**: Developed with eight international experts and peer-reviewed by ten more.
- **Audience**: Educators, teacher-training institutions and curriculum developers.
- **Five Areas**: AI in everyday life, media, mind and knowledge, power and human rights, future.
- **Beyond Tools**: Focuses on systems and interests behind AI, not technical skills.
- **Practical Format**: Combines concepts with discussion questions, activities and adaptable examples.
- **Stable Foundations**: AI changes the environment where MIL applies, not MIL's foundations.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Core literacy outlasts the technology** — Critical thinking and media literacy skills stay constant, while the information environment they are applied to keeps changing.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the central purpose of UNESCO's "AI Answers. You Decide." guide?
**A**: It helps educators teach Media and Information Literacy in AI-mediated information environments so learners keep the ability to think critically and make informed choices. It examines how AI influences information, knowledge, behaviour and society rather than teaching specific technologies.

**Q2**: What five areas does the guide cover?
**A**: AI in everyday life, AI and the media, AI and the human mind and knowledge, AI power and human rights, and AI and the future. Each combines conceptual explanations with discussion questions and practical activities.

**Q3**: How should educators approach keeping up with AI change, according to the guide's author?
**A**: They should not try to keep up with every technological change. Instead they should equip learners with critical thinking skills for an ever-evolving information environment, since AI changes the environment, not the foundations of MIL.
