## Summary

[[Kyndryl]] and [[Skillsoft]] announced a collaboration to provide no-cost AI education to eligible U.S. high school students (grades 9-12) and educators through the Skillsoft Percipio Platform. The program, aligned with Kyndryl's commitment to the White House AI education initiative, is available to up to 25,000 student and educator users across eligible school districts through May 29, 2027.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Skillsoft Percipio Platform]]**: An AI-native skills management platform delivering the curriculum's foundational AI concepts, prompt engineering techniques, and everyday AI-tool use cases.
- **[[White House AI Education Initiative]]**: A federal commitment that Kyndryl's program supports, aimed at preparing students for an AI-driven workforce.
- **[[Workforce Readiness]]**: The program frames AI literacy as a fundamental skill for all students, not just future technologists, to prepare them for a changing job market.
- **[[Digital Credentials]]**: The curriculum offers hands-on learning and credentialing opportunities tied to future career pathways.

## Key Takeaways

- **No-cost access**: Eligible U.S. high school students and educators (grades 9-12) get free AI-focused learning content.
- **Scale capped at 25,000 users**: The agreement covers up to 25,000 aggregated student and educator users across participating districts.
- **Program runs through May 29, 2027**: A defined term for the current agreement.
- **Curriculum spans basics to application**: Covers foundational AI concepts, prompt engineering, and using AI tools in everyday tasks.
- **Framed as universal skill**: Kyndryl positions AI literacy as necessary "not just for future technologists, but for every student."
