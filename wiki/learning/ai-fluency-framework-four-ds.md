## Overview

The AI Fluency Framework defines what it means to work with AI **effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely**. It is built on four core competencies — the Four Ds — that apply across all modes of AI interaction and remain relevant as AI systems continue to evolve.

## The Four Core Competencies

### 1. Delegation
**What work should be done by/with AI vs. humans alone?**

Combines your understanding of the problem, awareness of AI capabilities, and skill in distributing work effectively between you and your AI assistants.

- Your expertise and judgment remain the foundation of effective AI use.
- The most powerful outcomes emerge when you identify opportunities for iterative human–AI collaboration — creating outcomes neither could achieve alone.

### 2. Description
**How do we communicate with AI systems?**

Clear articulation of what you want across three dimensions — *product, process, and performance* — guides AI to produce the outputs you need.

- Clear communication bridges your intentions and AI capabilities.
- Effective instructions aren't always elaborate prompts; thoughtful conversations providing context, examples, and feedback are often more powerful.

### 3. Discernment
**Critically evaluating AI outputs and behaviors**

Assessing quality and appropriateness across *product, process, and performance* dimensions.

- Critical evaluation of AI outputs is a non-negotiable responsibility.
- Discernment protects against AI limitations and enables results neither you nor the AI could easily create alone.

### 4. Diligence
**Ensuring AI interactions are responsible**

Covering thoughtful selection of AI systems, transparency about AI's role, and accountability for the final product — via *creation, transparency, and deployment* diligence.

- You remain accountable for what you do with AI and its impact on others.
- Being transparent about AI's role builds trust and integrity.

See [[AI Fluency Diligence Three Types]] for deeper coverage.

## Three Modes of AI Interaction

The Four Ds apply across all three modes:

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Automation** | AI performs specific tasks following your instructions |
| **Augmentation** | You and AI collaborate as thinking partners |
| **Agency** | AI is configured to act independently on your behalf |

## Developing AI Fluency

- These competencies are not mastered overnight — they develop through deliberate practice.
- The goal is not perfection, but continual development of awareness, commitment, and intentional practice.
- The framework is designed to be applicable to a broad range of generative AI assistants and remain relevant as AI evolves.

## Key Insight

AI systems are powerful but not silver bullets. They are only as useful and safe as we enable them to be. Effective AI fluency means:
1. Investing in your own expertise so you can delegate appropriately.
2. Practicing description and discernment — the thoughtful back-and-forth that transforms AI from simple automation into a genuine thinking partner.
3. Taking responsibility for how you work with AI and what you create together.
