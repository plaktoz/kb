## Summary
WashU's Jason Jabbari argues that education must combine up-to-date technical training with durable skills like adaptability and critical thinking as AI erodes entry-level tech pathways. LaunchCode and Per Scholas adapt by partnering with employers and upskilling alumni, shortening the feedback loop between employer needs and curriculum. Higher education can pair its scale with that agility so AI expands opportunity rather than inequality.

## Core Concepts
- [[Jason Jabbari]]: Brown School associate professor and co-author of "Rethinking Human Capital Development in the Age of AI."

## Key Takeaways
- **Entry-Level Squeeze**: Experienced workers use AI for tasks once given to new workers.
- **Faster Skilling**: Economic mobility requires learning advanced skills earlier and more quickly.
- **Employer Signals**: Employer partnerships get in-demand skill signals into programs faster.
- **Alumni Upskilling**: Per Scholas now offers alumni courses to keep graduates current.
- **Meta-Skill**: Students learn how to learn how to code, not just code.
- **Durable Skills**: Adaptability and growth mindset enable learning the next technical skills.
- **Hybrid Model**: Higher education's scale plus agile providers' methods can broaden opportunity.

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is Jabbari's central argument about preparing workers for AI?
**A**: Education must combine current technical training with durable skills such as critical thinking, adaptability and collaboration. This helps workers succeed in an AI-driven economy.

**Q2**: How do LaunchCode and Per Scholas keep pace with the labor market?
**A**: They work directly with employers so skill-demand signals reach programs quickly, and Per Scholas offers alumni upskilling courses. Both shorten the feedback loop between employer needs and what students learn.

**Q3**: How can higher education make sure AI expands opportunity rather than inequality?
**A**: By combining its scale with the quick adaptation of providers like LaunchCode and Per Scholas, including AI-personalized learning tied to career goals. This helps avoid AI gains going mainly to the already advantaged.
