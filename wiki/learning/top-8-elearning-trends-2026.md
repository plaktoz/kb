## Summary

The global eLearning market is projected to hit $842.64 billion by 2030, driven by three converging forces: agentic AI, immersive tech, and skills validation. Eight trends will define 2026 — from self-directed AI tutors and LXPs replacing LMSs, to microlearning, data-driven analytics, hybrid/cohort learning, micro-credentials, and subscription-based "learning-as-a-service" models.

## Core Concepts

- [[Agentic AI]] powering self-directed, adaptive digital tutors that reason and personalize instruction in real time.
- [[Intelligent Tutoring Systems]] evolving from static chatbots into emotionally aware, conversational tutors.
- [[Learning Experience Platforms]] (LXPs) replacing traditional [[Learning Management Systems]] as "Netflix of learning" style curators.
- [[Microlearning]] and nanolearning delivering just-in-time, context-aware content inside workflow tools.
- [[Experience API]] (xAPI) and [[Learning Record Store]] (LRS) infrastructure connecting learning data to business KPIs.
- [[HyFlex Learning]] and [[Cohort-Based Learning]] blending synchronous, asynchronous, and community-driven formats.
- [[Micro-Credentials]] and digital badges validating stackable, job-ready skills over traditional degrees.
- [[Subscription Economy]] applied to education via continuous, "learning-as-a-service" platforms.

## Key Takeaways

- **Agentic AI tutors**: 79% of businesses already use AI agents; 66% report productivity gains.
- **LXPs overtaking LMSs**: 71% of enterprises are replacing legacy LMS with LXP systems.
- **Microlearning efficiency**: Completion rates up to 80% vs. 20% for long-form courses.
- **Analytics gap**: 95% of L&D orgs struggle to link learning to business objectives.
- **Hybrid preference**: 82% of learners now prefer hybrid over traditional learning.
- **Cohort engagement**: Cohort-based courses see completion rates over 90%.
- **Micro-credential hiring**: 87% of employers hired someone with a micro-credential in the past year.
- **Subscription shift**: Learning platforms adopt recurring, subscription-based access models.
