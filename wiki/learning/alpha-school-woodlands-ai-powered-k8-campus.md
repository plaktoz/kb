## Summary
[[Alpha School]] will open a new K-8 campus in [[The Woodlands]], Texas for the 2026-2027 school year, bringing its AI-powered, mastery-based learning model to the community. The roughly 8,000-square-foot campus pairs adaptive, technology-driven academic instruction with life-skills workshops covering communication, critical thinking, financial literacy, and leadership.

## Core Concepts
- **[[Alpha School]]** — founded 2014, combines adaptive technology-driven instruction with immersive life-skills workshops
- **[[The Woodlands]]** — Howard Hughes master-planned community in Texas hosting the new campus
- **[[Mastery-Based Learning]]** — core-subject learning model emphasizing mastery over seat time
- **[[Howard Hughes Communities]]** — real estate developer partnering with Alpha School on the expansion

## Key Takeaways
- **New campus** opens for the 2026-2027 school year at 2000 Woodlands Parkway.
- **Serves kindergarten through eighth grade** in an approximately 8,000-square-foot facility.
- **Model combines** adaptive AI-driven academics with real-world life-skills workshops.
- **Skills focus** includes communication, critical thinking, financial literacy, leadership.
- **Applications open** via alpha.school/the-woodlands, with limited space.
