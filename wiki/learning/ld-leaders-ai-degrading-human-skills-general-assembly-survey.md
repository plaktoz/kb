## Summary

A General Assembly survey of 510 enterprise L&D leaders finds 52% believe AI use is degrading employees' human skills such as communication and collaboration. Protecting those skills tops 2027 challenges while teams are pressured to deliver AI upskilling and prove business impact. Leaders tying learning strategy to documented business priorities are more likely to expect bigger budgets.

## Core Concepts

- [[General Assembly]]: AI skills training provider that ran the survey; CEO [[Daniele Grassi]] is quoted.
- [[Learning and Development]]: Enterprise L&D leaders at companies with 1,000+ employees across six countries.
- [[Soft Skills]]: Critical thinking, communication, conflict resolution and collaboration.
- [[AI Upskilling]]: Role-specific, hands-on, personalized training replacing generic literacy.
- [[Learning ROI]]: Linking training to business outcomes rather than course completions.
- [[SHRM]]: Found median L&D spending per full-time employee fell 28% in 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Skill Erosion**: 52% of L&D leaders say AI is degrading human skills.
- **Top 2027 Challenge**: 41% cite retaining soft skills despite AI usage.
- **Upskilling Pressure**: 64% say their team is under pressure to deliver AI upskilling.
- **Training Shift**: 57% expect role-specific AI training demand to grow.
- **Budget Link**: Strategy tied to business priorities doubles odds of expecting bigger budgets (23% vs 11%).
- **Measurement Gap**: Only 47% rank business outcomes top three; 36% count completions.
- **Completion vs Competency**: 26% struggle to distinguish AI course completions from competency.

## 🧠 Core Principles

**T1. Activity metrics are weak proxies for capability** — Counting course completions measures participation, not behavior change or competency, so impact must be measured on outcomes. · *model:* [[Goodhart's Law]]
→ explains: Measurement Gap, Completion vs Competency

## 🃏 Review Questions

**Q1**: What is the survey's central finding?
**A**: 52% of enterprise L&D leaders believe AI use is degrading employees' human skills like communication, conflict resolution and collaboration. Retaining those skills is a top 2027 challenge for 41%.

**Q2**: What evidence shows L&D struggles to prove impact?
**A**: Only 47% rank business outcomes among their top three success metrics, while 36% count course completions. 26% say separating AI course completions from competency is a top challenge.

**Q3**: How should L&D respond, according to the article?
**A**: Integrate AI and soft-skills training with hands-on practice and real-time feedback, and measure behavior change and competencies tied to business outcomes. Leaders who link strategy to business priorities are more likely to expect budget increases.
