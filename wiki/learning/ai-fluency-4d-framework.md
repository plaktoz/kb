# AI Fluency: The 4D Framework

**Source:** [Lesson 2B: The 4D Framework (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4Ua6XFfX9w)
**Course:** AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Anthropic, Ringling College of Art and Design, University College Cork

## Overview

The 4D Framework defines four core competencies that make AI collaboration effective, efficient, ethical, and safe — regardless of which AI system or workflow is being used. These competencies apply across all interaction modes (automation, augmentation, and agency).

---

## 1. Delegation — Deciding What to Hand Off

**Core question:** What work should you handle yourself? Where does AI add value?

Effective delegation requires:
- Clarity on your goal and the problem you're solving
- Realistic understanding of what AI can and cannot do well
- Thoughtful division of labor between human and AI

**Example:** On a research project, assign the AI to review lengthy documents and surface data patterns; reserve critical analysis and final conclusions for yourself.

Delegation is not about offloading tasks — it is about **strategic task allocation** with a clear vision of the overall goal.

---

## 2. Description — Communicating Clearly with AI

**Core question:** How precisely have you communicated what you need?

Description goes beyond writing prompts. It involves establishing:
- What you want to achieve and the format of the output
- How you want the AI to approach the task
- Relevant context and background the AI needs
- Tone, style, and constraints

**Example:** Instead of "Make me a logo," specify the company's values, target audience, preferred colors, and style references. When using AI as a tutor: "Don't give me the answer — help me work through the problem step by step."

Effective description sets up both you and the AI for maximum collaborative success.

---

## 3. Discernment — Evaluating AI Outputs

**Core question:** Is this output accurate, useful, and aligned with your goals?

Discernment draws on domain expertise and critical judgment to:
- Verify factual accuracy
- Assess whether reasoning holds up
- Check alignment with your values, audience, and objectives
- Decide when output needs refinement vs. should be set aside entirely

**The description–discernment loop:** Most AI interactions cycle between describing what you need → evaluating what you get → refining the request. This loop is the practical core of AI collaboration.

---

## 4. Diligence — Responsible, Accountable AI Use

**Core question:** Are you willing to stand behind the AI-assisted work you produce?

Diligence covers:
- Controlling for potential biases (e.g., in hiring tools or application reviews)
- Verifying accuracy before important decisions
- Protecting sensitive data
- Being transparent about AI involvement
- Taking full accountability for AI-assisted outputs

Diligence is not optional — it is the ethical foundation that makes AI collaboration trustworthy.

---

## Why the 4Ds Are Durable

Unlike specific prompting techniques or tool-specific settings, these four competencies are **not tied to particular AI systems**. They are fundamental skills that remain relevant as AI technology evolves.
